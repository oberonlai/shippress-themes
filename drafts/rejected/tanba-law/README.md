# Tanba Law Office

A law office theme in old purple: a formal white top header with an ink side rail; the first screen sets a tall old-purple banner with the firm line and a low-key office photograph beside a narrow centred column with a matter search, six filter chips and the first rows of a numbered practice index, with a cut-out desk lamp breaking over it; then the full eighteen-row index in four groups, three ways to engage, sectors, four partners and a contact strip. English sample content.

- Design plan row b6-2: horizontal-strip (hairline index rows and a four-partner strip), vertical-banner (a tall old-purple banner with the firm line and a low-key office photograph at its foot), side-rail nav (a 200px ink rail with lettered entries under the formal top header), real-photography (low-key: 50mm f/1.8, single warm window light, deep shadow, fine grain, graded toward old purple; a cut-out desk lamp breaks over the index column, cut-out partner portraits rise out of purple blocks), old purple #895B8A, Shippori Mincho + Hanken Grotesk; first screen centered-narrow-column, page structure service-index.
- Front page blocks: hero-practice-index, service-index, how-to-engage, sectors, partners, contact-strip.
- Sample content language: en (English-only theme).
- Contrast: white on old purple #895B8A 5.35:1 (banner and engage band text); ink #21152A on white 17.42:1; deep plum #5E3B60 on white 9.25:1 (labels, links); white on ink 17.42:1 (rail, contact strip); brass #C9A24A on ink 7.26:1 (rail letters, contact labels only); white on deep plum 9.25:1 (button hover).
