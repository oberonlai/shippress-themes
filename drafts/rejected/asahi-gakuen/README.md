# Asahi Gakuen

A private high school theme in forget-me-not blue: a giant type-poster motto on a pale blue field with three facts and the admissions date, then a collage index of high-key cut-out portraits of students and teachers, a long interview, the school year, people in figures, admissions dates and the latest notices. English sample content.

- Design plan row b5-16: poster-typographic, centered-giant-type (a giant three-line motto filling the first screen), split nav (school name left, numbered menu right), collage, 2d-flat (rendered as high-key photographs under the 2026-10-18 photo rule: 85mm f/2.8, softbox fill, near-white ground, graded toward forget-me-not, cut out and laid on flat colour blocks), forget-me-not #7DB9DE, Zen Old Mincho + Figtree; first screen type-poster, page structure people-story.
- Front page blocks: hero-type-poster, portrait-index, interview, school-year, people-figures, admissions, notices.
- Sample content language: en (English-only theme).
- Contrast: ink #14273A on main #7DB9DE 7.1:1; deep #1D5277 on white 8.3:1; white on deep #1D5277 8.3:1; quiet #4A5866 on white 7.3:1; deep #1D5277 on mist #EAF4FA 7.5:1; deep #1D5277 on main (labels) 3.9:1; white on main 2.2:1 is never used for text.
- Break-frame: transparent cut-out portraits (WebP with alpha) rise above the top of their flat colour blocks in the portrait index and the interview.
