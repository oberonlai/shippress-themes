# Nami Staffing - brand brief

People-first recruitment and staffing agency sample site for employers and job seekers in Leeds. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market.
All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Nami Staffing.
- **Location:** second floor, 14 Wharf Approach, Leeds LS1 4BR. A canal-side open-plan office with a reception, a meeting room where candidates can sit down and talk, and the consultants' desks. Company no. 00000000 (sample).
- **History:** founded in 2012 by Sofia Marin (written "Sofia Marín"), previously an HR manager at a logistics firm. Two desks above a cafe, placing people in warehouses. Daniel Owusu joined in 2015 and opened the healthcare desk; Mei Lin joined in 2019 for offices and hospitality. The day-30 follow-up visit became standard for every placement in 2017. Move to the canal-side office on Wharf Approach in 2023.
- **Today:** independent, three consultants and a small office team.
- **Audience (who it serves):** employers (warehouses and logistics, clinics and care, offices, kitchens and hospitality) who need the right person, and job seekers who want honest advice and a consultant who stays in touch.
- **Tone:** warm, plain, practical, British English. Short honest sentences. Meet people in person, say what it costs. No match rates, success rates, client names, guarantees of employment or awards.

## People
- **Sofia Marín** - founder, consultant for logistics and warehousing.
- **Daniel Owusu** - consultant for healthcare and clinics; joined 2015.
- **Mei Lin** - consultant for offices and hospitality; joined 2019.
- A small office team handles payroll, right-to-work checks and the diary.

## What we do
Permanent recruitment; temporary staffing (agency workers); temp-to-perm. Sectors: warehousing and logistics, clinics and care, offices, kitchens and hospitality.

## How a placement works (shown as photographed chapters, not a numbered grid)
1. **The brief** - we visit the site and meet the team.
2. **The shortlist** - we meet every candidate in person; usually three names.
3. **The interview** - arranged and followed up by the consultant.
4. **The first morning** - the consultant meets the new hire at the door.
5. **Week one** - a call to the manager and the new hire.
6. **The day-30 visit** - a sit-down with both.

## Fees (the only figures used anywhere)
- Job seekers never pay anything.
- Permanent placement: 15% of the first-year base salary, invoiced only when the person starts; if they leave within the first eight weeks we search again at no charge.
- Temporary staff: billed by the hour at the worker's pay rate plus a 26% margin (holiday pay, employer costs and our time).
- Temp-to-perm: no transfer fee after 12 weeks.
- No currency amounts are shown anywhere.

## A day at the agency
- 08:00 the office opens; overnight messages from supervisors and early-shift workers are read, short shifts sorted first.
- 10:00 briefs and phone calls; consultants visit sites.
- 12:00 candidate meetings over a cup of tea.
- 14:30 shortlists written and sent.
- 17:30 follow-up calls to people who started this week, and the next day-30 visits booked.
- Office open Mon-Fri 8:00-18:00.
