# Nami Staffing - S5-Style references

Researched on S5-Style (https://www.s5-style.com/) on 2026-10-20 among recruit / corporate entries. Only structure, techniques and colour relationships are borrowed; no copy, photographs, logos or whole-page layouts. Proof image: `reference-compare.png` in the run output (one row per technique plus a palette row).

## Signature techniques used

| Technique | Source | On the reference | In Nami Staffing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Split first screen: two photographs either side of a narrow centre title column, flat solid corner labels overlapping the photo corners, a scroll cue and a line starting under the title | Salad Bowl recruit | Two square tomato photographs left and right, the vertical title in the middle, a green label on the left photo's top-left corner and an orange one on the right photo's top-right corner, "SCROLL" over a thin green line | Home / `front-cover`: first-morning photograph left with a green "First mornings" label, interview photograph right (lower) with a terracotta "Every placement" label, the opening line in the centre column, "Scroll" over the green line. In ja and zh-TW the centre title is set top to bottom like the reference (Latin text never is). Info bar below (free for job seekers, hours, phone, address). |
| One thin centre line running down the page, heading + eyebrow + pill button left of it, text right of it, alternating pale grey and white bands | Salad Bowl recruit | A 1px green line through every section; "Topics" / "Message" headings with a dark green pill "MORE ->" on the left, text or a ruled news list on the right | Home / `front-intro`, `front-paths`, `front-consultants`, `fees-plain`, `front-journal`, ending in `front-close`: the same 1px field-green line, eyebrow + heading + green pill left, narrative or a ruled post list with terracotta category pills right. |
| Photographs of very different sizes at staggered, overlapping positions | Cominka recruit | Vision section: a large portrait and a small offset photo placed diagonally on white, no grid | Home / `front-placement`: from the brief to day thirty as five captioned photographs of different sizes (4:3, square, 4:5 overlapping the first, 3:2, a 21:9 strip), not a step grid or timeline. |
| Convex arc closing a band | Cominka recruit | The deep green first screen ends in a large convex arc | Home and Fees page / `fees-plain`: the fees paragraph on a deep ink-green band (#1E2E26) whose lower edge is a large convex arc. |

The consultants row whose third card runs past the right edge (scrollable) also follows the Salad Bowl photo slider with a cropped last card.

## Palette

Source: Salad Bowl recruit (measured on its 1440x900 first screen: white #FFFFFF 46%, photo orange #D5521B 15%, sage #9CB269 12%; UI greys #F3F3F1; deep green #0B5D3A on text, labels, buttons and the centre line; 39% saturated pixels, mostly the tomato photographs).

| Role | Salad Bowl | Nami Staffing | Share in Nami first screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | #FFFFFF | #FFFFFF white | about 55% |
| Band | #F3F3F1 | #F3F3F0 band grey | below the first screen |
| Main (line, labels, pills) | #0B5D3A | #0B5D3A field green | about 1% |
| Accent label | #D5521B | #C0603A terracotta (softened) | under 1% |
| Secondary | #9CB269 sage | #BFD2BF light sage (rules), #1E2E26 deep ink green (fees band, footer) | - |

First-screen saturated pixels: reference 39%, limit min(30%, 39%) = 30%; Nami Staffing 4.2-4.7% in all five languages (the documentary photographs are muted). Not blue, not beige: the green comes straight from the reference.

Not reused from earlier themes: no mountain overlay, white tag chips or rotated watermark text (Kagi), no polygon crops with (001) numerals, shape strip or deep blue bottom band with centred brand (Hatsumei).
