# Reference — Shikii Mats (draft; "our counterpart" column filled in later steps)

Reference: https://biz.duskin.jp/ (business home + mat rental page https://biz.duskin.jp/item/mat/)
Secondary: https://www.duskin.jp/item/mat/

Why: the planned reference (a large cleaning-service company) leads its mat pages with an entrance mat going down at a doorway and one enquiry. The business site shows it best: a flat colour band with staff photos (one person kneeling at a door laying a mat), a four-up pick-up card rail with arrows and a numbered pager, a 4-week exchange idea ("swap every four weeks"), trial price next to standard price, and an "indispensable at the entrance" lead.

## Palette relation
Reference: pale ground + one deep teal band + one mid blue bar + one tiny yellow rule. Ours: pale cool-grey ground + charcoal (mat) band + slate #7D8B96 mid + one tiny ochre rule; saturation kept low.
