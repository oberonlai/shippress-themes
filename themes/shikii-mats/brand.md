# Shikii Mats - brand brief

Entrance-mat hire for offices, shops, clinics and showrooms, run from a depot in Easton. A fitter lays a clean charcoal mat just inside the door and swaps it for a fresh one every four weeks. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shikii Mats. "Shikii" is the Japanese word for a threshold or door sill; the business itself is international and plain.
- **Location:** depot and wash room at Unit 4, Canal Yard, Easton (fictional). Phone +00 20 7000 4410, email round@shikiimats.example. Prices in euros in every language, no converted prices.
- **History:** founded in 2014 by Mara Okafor, who laid the first mats herself on a Saturday round of six shops. The Monday-to-Saturday round, the Canal Yard depot and the four-week swap came over the following years; the hire is still one mat at the door, swapped on a fixed round.
- **Today:** independent; six people at the depot and on the van. Offices, shops, clinics and showrooms on the same round.
- **Audience:** facilities managers, shop owners, clinic managers and showroom managers who want a clean entrance without washing, storing or shaking mats themselves.
- **Tone:** calm, specific and practical, the way a depot desk would explain a round to a facilities manager. Short sentences, named people, fixed prices. No awards, press, customer counts, guarantees or outcome claims.

## The day (a daily routine on the round)
Installs start at 06:30, before most sites open. A fitter loads the van at the depot, drives the Monday-to-Saturday round, measures new doorways, lays fresh mats and rolls the used ones into the van. Used mats return to Canal Yard in the afternoon, where Liesel Brandt washes and inspects pile, edges and size before a mat goes out again. The depot desk (Ana Ferreira) is open Monday to Friday 07:00-17:00 and Saturday 07:00-12:00, and answers trial bookings, size changes and stop requests.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Round: every **4 weeks**; install takes about **20 minutes**; installs start **06:30**; rounds Monday to Saturday.
- Depot desk: Mon-Fri 07:00-17:00, Sat 07:00-12:00, closed Sunday.
- Trial: **one site, one month** (install + one swap visit), **EUR 19** for one 85 x 150 cm mat; then the size price if the mat is kept; no fee to stop.
- Prices per mat, per 4-week round: Standard 85 x 150 cm EUR 24; Large 115 x 180 cm EUR 34; Lobby 150 x 240 cm EUR 52; Logo mat 85 x 150 cm EUR 39. Extra swap visit in a wet week EUR 12.
- Founded 2014.

## Process (4 steps)
1. Book a trial. 2. Measure and install. 3. Week-4 swap. 4. Keep, resize or stop (no fee).

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. **Threshold split collage:** four abutting photo tiles of unequal width cut by one 4 px charcoal threshold line with a short yellow segment; the headline sits below the photographs. Inner pages use a two-tile version.
2. **Four-week round track:** a four-cell hairline track (Wk 1, Wk 2, Wk 3, Wk 4 swap) with a dark swap cell and a yellow marker; mini on cards, large in the round section.
3. **Dashed rows and ribbed edge:** every H2 sits on a 1 px yellow rule, list rows are separated by dashed rules, and a 14 px ribbed strip (fine vertical grooves like a mat pile) closes the top of dark bands.
Reference: a cleaning-service business site's mat page (quiet white ground, one teal main colour, yellow hairline accent, abutting people photographs, dashed link rows, a "swapped every four weeks" badge).
