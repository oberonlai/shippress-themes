# Shikii Mats

A landing-page block theme for an entrance-mat hire service: a fitter lays a clean mat inside the door of an office, shop, clinic or showroom and swaps it every four weeks. One long conversion page with an anchor menu and one repeated call to book a trial install.

- Home: white top bar and one teal anchor bar, a four-tile threshold split photo collage (outside above the dark threshold line, inside below it) with the headline below the photographs and a booking bar, four square site cards with a mini four-week track, three narrative paragraphs beside a photograph, the large round track with two counted figures, a parallax doorway band, dashed trial rows on a ribbed charcoal band, the price table, customer notes, questions and a closing booking band.
- Fonts: Manrope and Public Sans (bundled, OFL); Noto Sans TC and Noto Sans JP for Chinese and Japanese headings.
- Scroll effects: sp-stagger and sp-reveal (up, left), sp-count and two sp-parallax layers; all off with reduced motion and visible without JavaScript.
- Forms: `shippress_form_slot` newsletter in the footer; works without plugins (mailto fallback with a visible notice).
- Design notes: `brand.md`, `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md`.
- Photography: AI-generated low-key documentary photographs.
