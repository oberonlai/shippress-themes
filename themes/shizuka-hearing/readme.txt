Shizuka Hearing is a WordPress block theme for a calm audiology clinic serving adults who strain to follow conversation in noise. The fictional practice was founded in 2014 at 18 Harbour Lane, Northgate, and publishes its hearing check, real-ear fitting, tinnitus care, and review fees in euros. The copy follows a visit from the missed sentence through measurement, a 14-day home trial, and later review.

== Description ==

Shizuka Hearing opens on a white field of rounded photographs with the statement set in white highlight strips, then stacks three journey cards (the missed sentence, the fitting, the review visit) over a blue-gray band. Manrope for headings, Public Sans for reading, Noto Sans TC and JP for Chinese and Japanese. Works without WooCommerce.
