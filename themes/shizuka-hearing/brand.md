# Shizuka Hearing - brand brief

Audiology clinic for adults who strain to follow conversation in noise. All names, places and people are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shizuka Hearing ("shizuka" is the Japanese word for quiet; the practice is otherwise international).
- **Location:** 18 Harbour Lane, Northgate, 2nd floor, lift access (fictional). Currency EUR (€) in every language, no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Dr. Elena Varga.
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday 9:00–18:00, Thursday until 20:00, Saturday 9:00–13:00. Closed Sunday and public holidays. Open listening evening Thursday 18:30, monthly and free.
- **Audience:** Adults who strain to follow conversation when the room is not quiet. The clinic's clients are adults, not children.
- **Voice:** The voice is calm, concrete, and adult, with short sentences, real visit lengths, and prices in euros, as if the coordinator were explaining the day at the desk. It does not boast, does not name a hearing-aid manufacturer, and does not turn one person's story into an expected result for the next.

## History and practice
Shizuka Hearing was founded in 2014 by Dr. Elena Varga, a clinical audiologist who left a hospital ENT department to open a clinic where a fitting takes the time it needs. The rooms are on the second floor of 18 Harbour Lane, Northgate, with lift access from the street. She had watched adults leave hospital appointments still unsure how a hearing aid would behave at a noisy table. The clinic was built so that the first conversation, the measures, the fitting, and the weeks that follow could happen in one place, with the same people.

The practice sees adults who strain to follow conversation when there is background sound. A hearing check takes 60 minutes and covers conversation, otoscopy, and pure-tone audiometry. A diagnostic assessment takes 90 minutes and includes a speech-in-noise test. Fitting uses real-ear measurement, with a thin probe tube at the ear, so the aid is verified against what arrives rather than assumed from another room. Two consulting rooms and one sound booth keep that work unhurried. Devices are chosen without a preferred manufacturer, then tried at home for 14 days on loaner devices before the decision is settled.

After the trial, review visits are booked at 2 weeks, 6 weeks, and 6 months, then yearly. Each review lasts 45 minutes. The first two are included with every fitting; later visits are €60. Cleaning and a re-tune can be done in 30 minutes for €35. Tinnitus care is a separate 75-minute consultation with Mei Lin Hartmann, with follow-up at 4 weeks. There is no cure on offer, and many people find the sound becomes less intrusive. The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00, Thursday until 20:00, and Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. A free open listening evening is held on Thursday at 18:30, once a month.

## Process (narrative, not a step grid)
1. A first conversation and a hearing check, to learn which sentences are slipping away and to measure hearing over 60 minutes.
2. A speech-in-noise test within the 90-minute diagnostic assessment, to see how speech holds up when the room is not quiet.
3. A fitting verified with real-ear measurement, using a thin probe tube at the ear across two visits.
4. A 14-day home trial with loaner devices, worn where the missed sentences actually happen.
5. Review visits at 2 weeks, 6 weeks, and 6 months, then yearly, each lasting 45 minutes.
