Denki Components — a business theme for a passive-components maker, built as a ledger of numbers. A cool pale ground with blue-grey hairlines, indexed product rows with 3:2 photographs at 54 percent and a text column from 63 percent, outline pills with a filled round arrow disc, and a fixed navy badge with a rotating ring of tracked text. The home page opens on a headline over a band of four figures and a framed reel-line photograph with scroll parallax; it continues with a right-hand company introduction, four ruled product families, a monthly ledger beside a sticky photograph, a staggered plant photo field with hash tags, three case rows beside an offset portrait, a sample-kit price list and a request-and-visit block. IBM Plex Serif for headings, IBM Plex Sans for reading. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; scroll effects respect reduced motion.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download denki-components.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/denki-components.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Denki Components.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates the home page with all its text and images, the menu, and sets the front page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. WooCommerce support is declared but not needed; the theme works as a plain site.

Scroll effects (assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js, ShipPress sp-scroll): reveal on scroll, a left-to-right photograph wipe and parallax on photographs. They are off for visitors who prefer reduced motion and show the final state without JavaScript and in the editor.

The company, its people, figures, prices and address are fictional.
