# Denki Components - brand brief

Maker of passive electronic components (multilayer ceramic capacitors, thick-film chip resistors, power inductors, ferrite chip beads) in Eindhoven, Netherlands. Sample content in English first; Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German follow in stage two. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Denki Components B.V. A brand name only; no Japanese motifs, scripts or scenes.
- **Location:** plant, lab and office at Kanaalweg Oost 18, 5644 RK Eindhoven, Netherlands (fictional street number and postcode). EUR prices in every language, prices identical.
- **History:** founded 1987 with 14 people making disc capacitors. 1996 first MLCC line and ISO 9001; 2008 IATF 16949 and inductors; 2016 ferrite bead line; 2024 taping line 3.
- **Today:** 420 employees, plant 24/7 in three shifts, 3 taping lines, kiln at 1,250 °C, reel store with 41,000 reels, 1,240 catalogue part numbers across 13 series.
- **Audience:** design and purchasing engineers in hearing devices, e-bike drives, metering and industrial electronics who need the same part every month.
- **Tone:** calm, exact, plain. Numbers first, "we measured" never "guaranteed". No awards, press, real customers or real brands.
