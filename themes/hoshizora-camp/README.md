# Hoshizora Camp

Hoshizora Camp — a glamping-site theme laid out like an app: under a full top header, a night-indigo sidebar holds the synced camp board (tonight's sky, free tents, the fire circle, quiet hours and a Book a site button) beside the scrolling page. Tsutsuji azalea pink (#E95295) is the main colour: the header, the hero, every page head and the azalea band are pink fields with night-indigo type. The header splits the menu around the centred camp name, with the Book a site button at its end. The hero is a scene map: a watercolour map of the camp in a white frame with numbered pins for the lodge, tents, domes, star deck, fire circle and creek, and a cut-out bell tent that steps out of the frame and over the hero edge; a cut-out camp lantern hangs over the night band. Every picture is a watercolour illustration. Klee One for display, Gothic A1 for reading. The sample content comes in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German; vertical labels only in Chinese and Japanese. WCAG AA colours; no entrance animations.

- Design plan row b3-21: sidebar-app, scene-map hero, split nav, full-illustration, watercolour, tsutsuji #E95295, Klee One + Gothic A1.
- Sample content languages: en, zh-TW, ja, fr, de.
- Contrast: night indigo #1E1433 on tsutsuji #E95295 5.1:1 (all text on the pink header, hero, page heads and azalea band); night on blush paper #FFF5F8 16.4:1; deep azalea #9B1F57 on paper 7.2:1 (links, labels); paper on deep azalea 7.7:1; dusk violet #5B4A63 on paper 7.6:1 (secondary text); glow pink #F28AB8 on night 7.6:1 (labels, times, prices on the sidebar and night band); pale #F7C8DC on night 11.8:1; paper on night 16.4:1; night on blush #F9D3E3 12.9:1; tsutsuji on night 5.1:1 (sidebar button, footer rule); white on tsutsuji only 3.4:1, never used for text.

## Templates and patterns

Every template is the app shell: the header, the page column and the night sidebar with the synced camp board, then the footer. Page bodies come from patterns (home-hero with the site-map, home-stays, home-meadow, home-night, home-journal, rooms-*, about-*, letter-*, contact-*); template headings come from tpl-* patterns so they follow the content language.
