## Story
Suji Sports Medicine Clinic opened in 2014 at 48 Millrace Road, Easthaven, for club athletes and amateur runners who needed somewhere to look past the sore spot. Dr Ingrid Solheim, a sports physician, started the rooms after seeing the same pattern repeat: pain settled with rest, then came back as soon as training did. Assessment, hands-on treatment and a plan for the return were meant to live in one place, so a runner was not taped, sent away and left to guess.

The work still follows three stages. Assess is the first visit, sixty minutes, used to test the irritable tissue and the load around it. Rebuild usually runs through weeks one to six, in sessions that are typically weekly and always adjusted to the injury rather than to a fixed calendar. Return is a graded plan that finishes with the return-to-run test: twenty-five single-leg calf raises on each side, a single-leg hop within ten percent from side to side, and thirty minutes of easy running with pain no higher than two out of ten. Those figures are the clinic's checks for calling a plan complete. They are not a forecast of a race or a season.

Hana Lindgren keeps the diary: Monday, Wednesday and Friday 9:00–19:00, Tuesday and Thursday 7:30–19:00, Saturday 9:00–13:00, closed Sunday. The early assessment slots are Tuesday and Thursday at 7:30. Booking is by phone on +00 555 0148 or by email at hello@sujiclinic.example, and cancellation is free up to 24 hours before.

## Method (3 stages)
- Assess: the first visit is 60 minutes, to test the painful place and the load around it, then name a plan.
- Rebuild: weeks 1–6, typically weekly sessions, with the work adjusted to the injury rather than held to a fixed timetable.
- Return: a graded plan back to club training or running, ending with the 45-minute return-to-run test and the clinic's three checks.
- Return checks (clinic criteria, not outcome claims): single-leg calf raises, 25 on each side; single-leg hop distance within 10% side to side; 30 minutes of easy running with pain no higher than 2 out of 10.
