## Story
Kosame Hot Pot opened in 2016 on Harbour Row in Wellmouth, founded by Mara Lindqvist and Daniel Cho. It rained steadily on the first evening of service, giving the restaurant its name. The dining room began with just nine tables, each built with its own dedicated burner so that guests could cook at their own pace over clear, fragrant broth.

From the beginning, the kitchen set its schedule around the rhythm of the stockpots. Broths were put on at half past five each morning, so chicken, kelp, pork bones and mushrooms could simmer undisturbed before the morning delivery arrived. As regular guests filled the room, the restaurant expanded in 2019, doubling the dining room to eighteen tables and sixty-four seats.

In 2023 three private rooms opened upstairs for celebrations, family meals and longer dinners with several pots on one table. The discipline has not changed: four broths finished at dawn, ingredients prepared cold each morning, and one table burner waiting for every party that walks in.

## How to book (the single way)
1. Choose the date, time, number of guests and the dining room or a private room.
2. Send the booking request (the form on Book a table, or the email it opens).
3. We confirm by email within 3 hours during desk hours (requests after 22:00 are answered from 14:00 on the next opening day).
4. Arrive. The table is held for 15 minutes and your pot is on the burner as you sit.
Free cancellation until 24 hours before (private rooms 72 hours). Parties of 7 or more go to a private room.
