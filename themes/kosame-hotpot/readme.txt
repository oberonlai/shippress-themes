Kosame Hot Pot is a quiet block theme for hot pot, shabu-shabu and sukiyaki restaurants that take table and private-room bookings. A sticky half-screen low-key photo stage sits beside one narrow paper column of vertical section headers, with a four-line booking frame, link boxes, price and info tables, a parallax photograph band and a newsletter slot.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (Latin headings), DM Sans (Latin reading text), Noto Sans TC (Traditional Chinese headings) and Noto Sans JP (Japanese headings), all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Chinese and Japanese body text uses the system face for that language.

== Installation ==

1. Download kosame-hotpot.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kosame-hotpot.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kosame Hot Pot.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with their text and images, three journal posts with a category and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Optional forms: The Pot Letter box uses a small dynamic block. With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active, their form is shown; without a plugin it shows an email field that opens your mail app (you are not on the list until you send the message). Booking and contact pages use Contact Form 7 or WPForms when present, and a mail button otherwise. Recommended Contact Form 7 booking fields: name, email, phone, date, time, guests, dining room or private room, broth pair, dietary notes, consent.

Scroll effects use the bundled sp-scroll files (assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js); they respect prefers-reduced-motion. The photo stage cross-fade is CSS only.

The restaurant, its people, places and prices are fictional.
