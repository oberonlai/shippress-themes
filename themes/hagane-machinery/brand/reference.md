# Hagane Machinery: S5-Style references

Note: sites 2 and 3 are scroll-animation driven; the static full-page capture shows only the image stream, so their notes are partly from the first-screen crop and the gallery description.

## Daiko Seisakusho (quartz glass maker) — https://www.daico.co.jp/ (gallery entry: https://www.s5-style.com/11954/)
- Home block order: hero (huge friendly headline + floating product renders + news card) -> giant ghost English tagline strip -> intro with funnel diagram (units / parts / materials) + lead copy -> photo strip with hexagon/bubble cut-outs -> "Products" white rounded card with 4 product tiles + custom-order callout -> Technology / Quartz glass (two offset text blocks beside a faceted photo) -> Strengths (text left, 2-col photo mosaic right) -> Company / Recruit / Contact cards -> dark-navy footer with link grid and group-site banners.
- Image / text ratio, whitespace, type scale, navigation: about 55 / 45; generous vertical rhythm (120-200px between blocks); hero H1 roughly 80-90px, section H2 about 32-40px, body 15-16px with small English eyebrow labels above H2; two-row header (utility links on top, pill-shaped main nav with dropdowns below); pale blue tonal palette, rounded cards.
- What we borrow (structure/technique only): product-category card block with a custom-order callout inside; English eyebrow + H2 pairing; offset two-text-block section beside an image; mosaic photo grid for strengths; pill nav container; oversized ghost tagline band.

## Koei Kasei Yamanashi Factory (resin pipe clamps, online store) — https://koueikasei.jp/ (gallery entry: https://www.s5-style.com/12067/)
- Home block order: full-bleed product macro photography on a flat teal field with a centred asterisk logo and a hamburger-only header -> long vertical stream of oversized product shots (clamps, saddle bands, nuts) with the factory name overlaid centre -> store entry.
- Image / text ratio, whitespace, type scale, navigation: roughly 95 / 5; almost no text, products used as graphic shapes; minimal header (hamburger left, logo centre, cart/login right, small 13px type).
- What we borrow (structure/technique only): treating industrial parts as flat-colour graphic compositions; ultra-minimal three-slot header; one flat accent field behind product cut-outs.

## Nishizaki Kogei Finishing Dept. (furniture finishing / painting) — https://tosou.nishizaki.co.jp/ (gallery entry: https://www.s5-style.com/11837/)
- Home block order: full-viewport photo hero with huge two-line company name top-left and small English label above -> tall scrolling process photography (spray gun, sanding, finished panel) -> small thumbnail "Scroll Down" cue and copyright line at the bottom.
- Image / text ratio, whitespace, type scale, navigation: about 90 / 10; one very large light-weight heading (about 70px), tiny 14px English caption; navigation collapsed into a square black "..." button at top right; thin white frame around the viewport.
- What we borrow (structure/technique only): oversized single heading over a work-floor photo; square icon menu button; thumbnail scroll cue.

## Sun Bosai (fire and disaster-prevention equipment installer) — https://sunbousai.jp/ (gallery entry: https://www.s5-style.com/11934/)
- Home block order: illustrated isometric town hero with chunky black English headline -> lead statement with two red square link tiles (strengths / company) -> Business cards (two cream rounded cards with illustrations) -> Products band on a warm gradient blob -> Works carousel of rounded cards with tags -> Info/news row -> footer.
- Image / text ratio, whitespace, type scale, navigation: about 60 / 40; large wavy organic section dividers instead of straight edges; H2 about 36px bold centred with tiny English sub-label; body 14-15px with loose line height; single-row compact header with right-aligned text nav and a red contact button.
- What we borrow (structure/technique only): red CTA tiles beside the lead copy; tagged work-case cards in a carousel; section dividers that change shape; compact header with one accent CTA button.

## Mix used for Hagane
Only structure and technique were borrowed; no copy, photographs, logos or page layouts. No machine-tool maker appeared in the gallery search, so the nearest manufacturing and industrial sites were used.
- **First screen in two halves** (from Nishizaki Kogei's work-floor photograph under one heading, made calmer): a framed low-key photograph of the machining hall over a lower half with one sentence and three figures. Their oversized two-line name and full-viewport photo were not used; our heading stays left-aligned at 4.5rem.
- **Product-category index** (from Daiko Seisakusho's Products block with four tiles): our four lines as a numbered, ruled index with small photographs, instead of rounded cards.
- **English eyebrow label over every heading** (Daiko, Sun Bosai): small uppercase iron blue-grey labels.
- **Tagged work cases** (from Sun Bosai's works carousel): three delivered-machine cases tagged by industry, as a static three-column grid instead of a carousel.
- **One machine explained with numbered markers** and **a horizontal timeline**: our own additions for a machinery maker, so the page carries narrative and specifications without a table.
- **Compact single-row header with one accent button** (Sun Bosai): one top menu and a "Request a quotation" button.
- Not taken: illustrated heroes, wavy dividers, flat colour fields, product cut-outs, hamburger-only navigation, scroll-driven image streams.
