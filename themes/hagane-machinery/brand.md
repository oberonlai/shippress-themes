# Hagane Machinery — brand brief

Design plan row: b6-6 (industrial machinery maker, manufacturing). Built in English first and localised into zh-TW, ja, fr and de (decision of 2026-10-20, the same mechanism as Hokuto Driving School 1.1.0 and Kasumi Mortgage). The brand world stays the same in every language; the wording of machines, service and quotations follows each market (a Taiwanese machine-tool builder, a Japanese industrial machinery maker, a French fabricant de machines industrielles, a German Maschinenbau company).
All names, places, people, prices and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hagane Machinery. *Hagane* is the founder's word for tempered steel: hard enough to cut, tough enough not to crack. His line: "A machine should still hold its tolerance on the day the customer has forgotten who built it."
- **Location:** 2-14 Riverside Works Road, Ostmere, a fictional river town with an old foundry quarter, a rail freight yard and a technical college. One plant: a machining hall, an assembly bay with two 40-tonne cranes, a temperature-controlled inspection room at 20 degrees, a design office over the gatehouse.
- **Founded:** 1961, by **Kenji Hagane**, a toolmaker, with four lathes and a rented shed. Built its first horizontal machining centre in 1988; third generation since 2016.
- **Today:** 212 people, 140 machines built a year, 4,200 Hagane machines in service in 38 countries, six service bases.
- **Products:** four lines. Horizontal machining centres (HM series, flagship HM-630), five-axis machining centres (HV series), cylindrical grinders (HG series), hydraulic press brakes (HP series). Every machine is built to order; lead time 7 to 9 months.
- **Audience:** production engineers and plant managers at automotive, aerospace, medical-device, energy and general subcontract machining companies; purchasing teams comparing quotations; machine-tool dealers.
- **Tone:** plain, exact and unhurried. Microns, tonnes and days before adjectives. Never "cutting-edge", "world-class" or "revolutionary". We would rather say "scraped by hand to 3 microns over 1.2 metres" than "ultimate precision".

## People
- **Ren Hagane** — president since 2016, the founder's grandson. Trained as a mechanical engineer; spent six years in the service department before running the company. Visits two customer plants every month.
- **Mika Oda** — chief engineer, spindles and thermal design, joined 1999. Led the HM-630; insists every new machine runs 72 hours in the inspection room before it ships.
- **Tomas Lindqvist** — head of service, Europe, based at the Ostmere plant and the Gothenburg base. Former customer: ran a machining line before joining.
- **Aiko Mori** — head of the scraping and assembly bay, 31 years at the bench.
