# Komorebi Cabins - brand brief

A cabin village that rents twelve whole self-catering cabins under cedar and beech: guests take the whole cabin, cook for themselves and hold a weekend through one request. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Komorebi Cabins. "Komorebi" is the Japanese word for sunlight that falls through leaves; the village is otherwise a plain, international, modern place of timber cabins.
- **Location:** Larch Gate, Gravel Lane, Hollin Vale (fictional). By train to Hollin Vale station, then bus 32 to "Larch Gate" and a ten-minute walk; by car the last 400 m are gravel, with parking beside each cabin. Phone +44 1632 960 418 (calls daily 08:30-19:00). Email stay@komorebi-cabins.example.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Ilse Marten and Ruben Okafor on 38 hectares of mixed cedar and beech woodland. The first four cabins that summer; eight more in 2017 (twelve in total); the woodshed office in 2022.
- **People (seven):** Ilse Marten (co-founder, host), Ruben Okafor (co-founder, woodsman and repairs), Sofia Navarro (housekeeping lead), Amira Haddad (bookings and the office), Tobias Klein (carpentry), Nora Adeyemi (weekend host), Jonah Pereira (forest walks and trails).
- **Audience:** couples, families and small groups who want a whole cabin for a weekend or two midweek nights; owners of rental cabin sites and forest-stay operators who take direct bookings are the theme buyers.
- **Tone:** calm, plain, practical, warm. Specific about sizes, hours, prices and who does what. No awards, no "best", no guarantees.

## A day in the village (the daily routine)
08:30 the woodshed office opens - 10:00 the firewood round reaches every porch - 11:00 check-out - 11:00-15:00 the cabins are turned and aired - 15:00 check-in - 19:00 the office closes - 22:00 quiet hours begin. The routine has the same shape every day of the week.

## Hold a weekend (the single way, four steps)
1 choose a cabin and dates (Friday to Sunday, or two midweek nights) - 2 send the hold request (the form on Reserve, or email) - 3 we hold the cabin for 48 hours and email a confirmation with a 30% deposit link - 4 arrive from 15:00; keys at the woodshed office. Free cancellation until 14 days before arrival; after that the deposit is kept.

## The Woodshed Letter
One short monthly note on the first Thursday: open dates, the season in the woods, one recipe for the stove.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Gutter tagline: a dark forest photograph flush to the left edge at 86% width on a greige ground, with the season chip, a vertical serif tagline and a Scroll label in the right gutter (reference: Kajima no Mori first screen).
2. Mosaic over canopy: overlapping mixed-size photographs over one blurred green canopy photograph, with a small vertical text column.
3. Split header: nav, a thin divider, a terracotta reserve button and a white boxed Menu with three dots.
Supporting: the Inside row (a large interior with a second photograph peeking from the right edge and three small portraits), slow 0.8 s fades and parallax.

## Pages and contents
Cabins (comparison), Inside, The Forest, Reserve, About, People, Getting here, Contact, The Woodshed Letter, Journal (three stories in "From the woods"), Privacy.

## Photography
Dark documentary film photographs: soft natural forest light, fine grain, muted greens and warm timber, no text, no logos, no cut-outs.
