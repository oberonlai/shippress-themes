# Komorebi Cabins

A block theme for a self-catering forest cabin village. Design notes: `brand/design-spec.md`, brand brief: `brand.md`.

- **Signatures:** a photograph flush left with a vertical two-line tagline, season chip and scroll label in the right gutter; a free-overlap mosaic of the four cabin sizes over a blurred beech canopy (with parallax); a split header with a terracotta reserve button and a white Menu box.
- **Plan comparison:** Fern, Beech, Cedar and Hollow side by side in a table, stacked into one card per cabin below 782px.
- **Inside row:** an edge-peek photograph with a stepping strip of three portraits.
- **Fonts:** Lora (display) and Work Sans (text), plus Noto Serif TC and Noto Serif JP subsets, bundled under `assets/fonts` with their OFL licences.
- **Status:** home page complete; inner pages and languages follow.
- **Home sections (patterns):** `front-hero`, `front-intro`, `front-mosaic`, `front-compare`, `front-inside`, `front-weekend`, `front-hosts`, `front-here`, `front-band`, `front-journal`, `front-letter`.
- **Parts:** `header` (split header, one menu), `footer`.
- **Scroll effects:** `assets/sp-scroll.css|js` (reveal, stagger, parallax).
- **Forms:** `inc/shippress-forms.php` (shared form slot, works without plugins).
