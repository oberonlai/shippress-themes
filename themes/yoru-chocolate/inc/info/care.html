Warm-weather pause

From 15 June to 15 September we do not ship bonbons; tablets ship in cool packs, and the shop is open as usual.

Keeping

Store bonbons cool and dry, between 15 and 18 °C, away from strong smells. Not in the refrigerator unless the room is warm.

Best within

Bonbons two weeks from the date on the box, tablets six months. The ganache is fresh and made without preservatives.

If it turns grey

A pale bloom on the surface is cocoa butter or sugar rising after a change in temperature. It is harmless and the taste is the same.

To serve

Take the box out twenty minutes before eating, so the ganache softens and the scent opens.
