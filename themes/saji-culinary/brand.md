# Saji Culinary School — brand

## History
In 2011, chefs Marta Ilić and Owen Achterberg founded Saji Culinary School inside a former ship's-chandler warehouse along the Veerkade canal in Rotterdam. They sought to establish a calm, disciplined academy where practical technique takes precedence over demonstration, grounding every student in classical European kitchen standards from their very first morning.

The school draws its name from saji, the Japanese tasting spoon. On day one, every incoming student receives an unadorned steel tasting spoon that remains in their pocket throughout the forty-week curriculum, reinforcing the essential discipline of tasting stocks, sauces, and seasoning at every stage of preparation.

By week three, students step onto the line to cook midday service for the public at The Pass, our on-site dining room. Marta and Owen built the curriculum around daily live lunch service, ensuring that timing, temperature, teamwork, and accountability become instinctive habits long before graduation.

## Voice
Calm, precise, and understated, the voice speaks with the quiet authority of an active commercial kitchen brigade. It prioritises technical accuracy, concrete routines, and documentary honesty over promotional superlatives or empty marketing claims.
