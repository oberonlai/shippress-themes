# Saji Culinary School: design spec (stage 1, binding for implementation)

Slug `saji-culinary`, professional culinary school, type `business` (declare WooCommerce support, nothing needs it). Design-plan row b18-2: archetype editorial-magazine, first screen `timeline-line`, page structure `day-schedule`, palette 鉄紺 `#1E2C30`, fonts Newsreader + Source Sans 3, font_direction **serif**.
Main reference: **menma.jp** (https://menma.jp/, food maker, collected on S5-Style). No secondary. Measurements are in `reference-tokens.md` (cited below as `tok(a)`…`tok(g)`). Screens: `ref/menma-first.png`, `ref/scroll-menma-before-unknown.png`, `ref/scroll-menma-after-cards.png`, `ref/menma-news.png`.
We borrow structure, techniques, colour relationships and the hero concept. We do **not** copy menma's photos, illustrations, mascot, logo, hanzi stamps or wording.

Imagery: **photo** (from stage-a IMAGERY: photo). Documentary, candid, high-key kitchen photography, generated by us (section 8).

Fixed brand facts (every page, all five languages): Saji Culinary School, founded **2011**, teaching kitchens at **Veerkade 18, 3016 DE Rotterdam, Netherlands** (fictional address). The name comes from *saji*, the tasting spoon: every student gets a steel tasting spoon on day one and carries it all course. Currency **EUR** in every language. Full list in section 9.

## 1. Signature techniques (three, all obvious at a glance)

### T1. Fixed thin ink frame with four round corner stamps (menma red page frame + hanzi corner stamps, `ref/menma-first.png`, `tok(c)` `tok(f)`)
- **Reference:** an 8 px solid red `#DA0000` border is fixed to the viewport edge on every page, so the content always sits inside a frame. Each corner holds a ø32 red disc with one black glyph in a thin ring. The frame stays put while the page scrolls under it.
- **Saji:** an 8 px `#1E2C30` frame fixed to the viewport (`position: fixed; inset: 0; border: 8px solid #1E2C30; pointer-events: none; z-index: 90`), on **every** template. Four ø32 stamps in the corners, centred on the frame's corner point (so they overlap the frame and run 12 px into the page, as in the reference): disc `#1E2C30`, inner ring 1 px `#A8432F` inset 3 px, roman numeral in Newsreader 600 0.875rem `#F6F5F2`: top-left **I**, top-right **II**, bottom-right **III**, bottom-left **IV** (the four terms, clockwise like a training year).
- Markup: one `<div class="saji-frame" aria-hidden="true">` in `parts/header.html` with four `<span class="saji-stamp saji-stamp--1…4">`. The numerals are CSS `::before { content: "I" }` text, not DOM text (keeps them out of text-overlap gates and screen readers).
- Body gets `padding: 8px` so nothing hides under the frame; `html { scroll-padding-top: 96px }`.
- <782 px: frame 4 px, stamps ø22, numeral 0.625rem. Header content is inset ≥ 36 px from the corners, so it never touches a stamp.
- On Programmes the stamp for the term you are reading turns brick (`#A8432F` disc, white numeral) through `body.saji-term-1…4` classes on the four term anchors' pages (see 4.2). That is the only place a stamp changes.
- **Do not dilute:** the frame must stay solid, 8 px, dark ink (not a hairline, not grey, not inset into the content). All four stamps are always visible, including on inner pages and while scrolling.

### T2. Left stacked nav + vertically set headline over a full-bleed photo; after the hero the nav becomes filled pills (menma first screen + pinned pill header, `ref/menma-first.png`, `ref/scroll-menma-before-unknown.png`, `tok(b)` `tok(g)`)
- **Reference:** the logo sits top-left as a stacked lockup (mark / name / "SINCE 1941"); under it, from about 57% of the height down, six text links stacked at 46 px pitch and an outline "ONLINE SHOP" pill. A two-line handwritten headline runs vertically top-to-bottom in the right quarter, starting about 12% from the top. Once the photo scrolls away the same links show as filled red pills (47 px high, radius 9999) in a row at the top right, plus an off-white shop pill and a ø44 menu disc.
- **Saji home hero (desktop ≥1024):**
  - Lockup at left 80 px, top 80 px: spoon mark (our own simple line drawing, 1.5 px stroke, 40×56 px, white) / "Saji" Newsreader 500 2rem / "CULINARY SCHOOL · EST. 2011" Source Sans 3 600 0.6875rem, tracking 0.24em, all white.
  - Stacked nav at left 88 px, its top at 57% of the hero height: seven links (The Day, Programmes, The Pass, Instructors, Graduates, Tuition, Contact), Source Sans 3 500 0.875rem white, 46 px pitch (line-height 1, gap 30 px), no bullets. Under it, 32 px gap, an outline pill "Apply" (1 px white border, 47 px high, padding 0 28px, radius 9999, Source Sans 3 600 0.8125rem, tracking 0.16em uppercase, small spoon icon left).
  - Vertical headline in the right quarter: left edge at 78% of the width, top at 12%, `writing-mode: vertical-rl; text-orientation: mixed`, Newsreader 400 2.75rem, line-height 1.5, letter-spacing 0.08em, white, `text-shadow: 0 1px 18px rgba(0,0,0,.25)`. Two lines in en: "Cooks are made" / "at the pass." In zh-TW/ja the CJK runs upright, which is natural; fr/de keep two short lines (≤ 18 characters each, see 9).
- **After the hero / inner pages:** the header becomes a sticky bar inside the frame (`top: 8px`, transparent background), lockup reduced to the spoon mark + "Saji" (1.375rem, ink) at left 48 px; right side a row of filled ink pills: each `#1E2C30` ground, `#F6F5F2` text, Source Sans 3 500 0.8125rem, height 47 px, padding 0 20px, radius 9999, gap 6 px; then the "Apply" pill on `#F6F5F2` with ink text and a 1 px `#A8432F` ring; then a ø44 `#F6F5F2` disc holding a two-bar menu icon (opens the overlay; on desktop the overlay lists the same links plus Admissions, About, Notices).
- Switch from hero layout to pill layout: the home hero carries a 1 px sentinel at its bottom; a tiny inline script uses **IntersectionObserver** (no scroll listener) to toggle `body.saji-past-hero`. With no JS the pill bar is simply shown from the start above the hero (still valid).
- Only **one** Navigation block (`parts/header.html`); the stacked and pill looks are the same menu restyled by CSS.
- Inner page heads (T2 continued, 4 pages+): a 64vh (min 460 px) full-bleed photo with the vertical title in the right quarter (same type, 2.75rem) and a small label under the pill bar, top-left: Source Sans 3 600 0.75rem tracking 0.24em caps `#F6F5F2`.
- **Mobile (<782):** no vertical text and no absolutely positioned labels on the first screen. Header = lockup (mark + "Saji" 1.5rem white) left and the ø44 menu disc right, in normal flow. The headline is horizontal, Newsreader 2.25rem/1.15 white, in flow at the bottom of the hero above the timeline, on a bottom scrim `linear-gradient(to top, rgba(30,44,48,.62), rgba(30,44,48,0) 55%)`. The stacked nav is not shown (menu overlay instead); "Apply" pill sits under the headline.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen (menma.jp)
- **What it shows:** one candid, full-bleed photograph (100% × 100vh, no mask, `tok(e)`) of a young woman in a small, warm local eatery, eyes closed, savouring a bite held in chopsticks, a plate of the product in her other hand. The whole screen is the photograph, framed by the 8 px red page border with a ø32 glyph stamp in each corner. Logo lockup top-left, a stack of six text links lower-left, an outline shop pill bottom-left, a two-line handwritten vertical tagline in the right quarter, a red pill bottom-right.
- **Story:** "eating this makes people happy": the product is shown at the moment of tasting, in an everyday place; the brand stays quietly at the edge.
- **Signature motion:** the photo is pinned while the pill header slides in over it as you scroll (ScrollTrigger pin, scrub 1); below, groups of text and cards fade up in stagger (y 50 → 0, .5 s, stagger .25, power1.out) and section words rise per character (y 20 → 0, .6 s, stagger .05, power3.out). `tok(g)`

### (2) Our translation (same composition, same motion idea, same image type: documentary photo)
The moment of tasting becomes the moment at **the pass**: a full-bleed candid photograph (H1) of two students plating on a brushed steel pass under a heat lamp while their chef-instructor, behind them, lifts a tasting spoon. The pass is our table; the instructor's spoon is the bite. The ink frame with term stamps I–IV holds the screen as menma's red frame does; our lockup (spoon mark, Saji, EST. 2011) sits top-left; the seven links stack lower-left with an "Apply" pill; the two-line vertical serif headline "Cooks are made / at the pass." sits in the right quarter. Where menma has a pill at the bottom right, we run the **timeline line** of the row's first screen: a 1 px white line across the bottom of the photo with five ticks marking a training day, 06:30 → 15:00. Motion: the photo moves slower than the page (sp-parallax), the translation of menma's pinned photo under a sliding header; the header turns into ink pills after the hero (T2).

## 3. Colour (source: menma.jp `tok(a)`; row hex `#1E2C30`)

| Role | Hex | Source / change | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Main ink (鉄紺) | `#1E2C30` | row hex; takes the role of menma's red frame/pill colour `#DA0000` | frame, stamps, nav pills, section words, footer, visit band, buttons | ~30% of a page |
| Ground | `#F6F5F2` | menma off-white `#F9F7F5`, −1.5% lightness | page ground, cards, pill text | ~40% |
| Secondary (steel mist) | `#D9E0E0` | replaces menma paper `#EFE8DB` | day-schedule band, halftone disc ground, note rules | ~15% |
| Text | `#262629` | menma text `#262629` (unchanged) | body text | — |
| Accent (brick) | `#A8432F` | menma red `#DA0000`, same hue family, chroma lowered | subtitles under words, seal ring, card rules 1 px, stamp inner ring, first tick, halftone lower band, active stamp | ≤ 4% (never a background except the active stamp and the halftone band) |
| Muted text | `#5D6669` | derived from ink | dates, meta, captions | — |
| Rule (light) | `#C9D0D0` | menma tan rule `#D9C9A8` role, cooled | notices list rules, form borders | — |

Why the two changes: (1) menma's pure red `#DA0000` frame and green `#05A954` giant words read as packaged-food retail; the row requires a quiet 鉄紺 palette, so the frame/words take the ink and the red survives only as the brick accent in the same roles (subtitle, seal, card rules). (2) menma's paper `#EFE8DB` is cream, which common rules tell us to avoid; steel mist keeps the "second ground" role and matches the steel pass. Every other relationship (dark frame around a photo, off-white ground, accent on subtitles/seal/rules, ≈3% saturated on the first screen) is kept.

- **First-screen saturated cap:** `check-first-screen.py` gate **15%**, target ≤ 8% (row note allows up to 30%; reference ≈ 3%). Only saturated items on the first screen: brick stamp rings, first tick, whatever colour the photo has. The H1 grade is muted cool-neutral (steel, white jackets, pale tiles), with greens/herbs small.
- Contrast: `#262629` on `#F6F5F2` 14.6:1; white on `#1E2C30` 14.9:1; `#A8432F` on `#F6F5F2` 5.6:1 (subtitles fine at any size).

## 4. Typography (font_direction: serif)

- **Latin display:** Newsreader (variable, opsz 6–72, wght 400–600, normal + italic). **Latin body/UI:** Source Sans 3 (variable wght 400–700).
- **CJK (明體, both headings and body, no 黑體 anywhere):** Noto Serif TC (zh-TW), Noto Serif JP (ja), weights 400 and 600, subset files.
- Stacks (theme.json `fontFamilies`):
  - `display`: `"Newsreader", "Noto Serif TC", "Noto Serif JP", Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`
  - `body`: `"Source Sans 3", "Noto Serif TC", "Noto Serif JP", system-ui, sans-serif`
  - `:lang(ja)` swaps the order to `"Noto Serif JP", "Noto Serif TC"` in both stacks; `:lang(zh-TW)` keeps TC first. Because style.css will have `:lang(` rules, copy `shared/inc/editor-lang.php` to `inc/editor-lang.php` and `require_once` it in functions.php.
- All faces load through theme.json `fontFace` (woff2 in `assets/fonts/`), so the block editor iframe and the front end show the same fonts, including the zh-TW and ja subsets (use the Hokuto/Hada subset method; the shared CJK subset script is broken). No `wp_enqueue_style` font CSS, no Google CDN.
- Size presets (theme.json):
  | slug | value | use | source |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | x-small | 0.75rem | labels, caps tracking 0.24em (≈2.9 px at 12 px) | tok(b) Lexend Giga 12 px / 2.9 px |
  | small | 0.8125rem | nav pills, meta | tok(b) nav 13 px |
  | medium | 1rem | body (line-height 1.7) | tok(b) body 14 px/1.5, raised for long reading |
  | large | 1.375rem | card titles, instructor names | — |
  | x-large | 1.75rem | subtitles under section words, pull lines | tok(b) JP subtitle ~28 px |
  | xx-large | 2.75rem | page/section headings without a giant word, vertical headline | tok(b) hero tagline scale |
  | xxx-large | clamp(3.5rem, 7.2vw, 6.5rem) | giant section words (max = 6.5rem ≥ 4.25rem) | tok(b) 90–120 px |
- Weights: Newsreader 400 (headings, vertical heads), 600 (section words, stamps); Source Sans 3 400 body, 500 nav, 600 labels/buttons. Line heights: body 1.7, headings 1.2, section words 0.98, vertical heads 1.5.
- CJK: headings ≈ 0.75× English size (section words in zh/ja `clamp(2.75rem, 5.4vw, 4.875rem)`, letter-spacing 0.12em), body line-height 1.85. fr/de: `hyphens: auto` on body and headings ≤ 2.75rem; section words get `overflow-wrap: anywhere` fallback and shorter words (section 9 lists them).
