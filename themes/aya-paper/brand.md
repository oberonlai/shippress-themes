# Aya Paper - brand brief

Stationery shop in Ghent, Belgium, selling six papers, plain envelopes and a stitched notebook, with a WooCommerce shop for orders. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Aya Paper.
- **Location:** 17 Papierstraat, 9000 Ghent, Belgium (fictional address). hello@ayapaper.example. EUR prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2011. Aya Mensah opened the shop and still works the counter.
- **History:** the range stopped at six papers: Airmail Bank, Everyday Bond, Laid Writing, Soft Wove, Grey Card and Cotton Rag. A sheet is chosen by holding it up to the window. The shop did not add a seventh paper, so the comparison stays in the hand.
- **Audience:** people who write, draw or print at a desk every day, students, small studios, and anyone sending a letter.
- **Tone:** plain, exact and a little dry. It names the paper, the weight, the size and the price, and never calls a sheet the best or the finest. No awards, press, ratings or customer counts.

## A day at the shop (the daily routine)
09:00 deliveries are unpacked. 10:00 the door opens (Tuesday to Saturday, until 18:00). Samples are cut through the morning. 14:00 the notebooks are stitched. 16:30 the parcels leave, wrapped in kraft paper and tied with cotton tape. On Thursday the paper clinic runs from 16:00 to 18:00.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Founded 2011. Open Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00-18:00. Thursday paper clinic 16:00-18:00.
- Cutting to size at the counter and the kraft wrap are free.
- Delivery is free over EUR 60; otherwise flat EUR 7 in the EU and EUR 16 elsewhere. Dispatched in 2 working days. EU delivery 3-5 working days, elsewhere 6-10.
- Returns within 30 days when unopened. Paper cut to size cannot be returned.
- The counter process has three steps: Feel (hold the sheets to the window), Cut (cut to size at the mat), Wrap (kraft paper, tied with cotton tape).

## Signature techniques (carried to inner pages)
1. T1 hairline frame: a 1 px ink frame split into equal photograph columns by 1 px rules, with own contour rings and a torn-edge line crossing them (mud.co.jp first screen).
2. T2 spec rows: ruled rows with a 5rem grammage figure, the paper name and a four-cell mono spec strip (mud.co.jp index rows, andmade.jp meta).
3. T3 catalogue: three columns of underlined titles with grey meta lines and a mono line, rising in with a stagger (andmade.jp).
