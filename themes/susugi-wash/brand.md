# Susugi Wash - brand brief

Preorder landing page for an undercounter commercial dishwasher built for small, ten-seat kitchens. Sample content is in English; zh-TW, ja, fr and de follow in a later stage. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Susugi Wash (susugi: to rinse). Product: Susugi Wash 45 (448 mm wide) and Susugi Wash 60 (598 mm wide).
- **Location:** Susugi Wash B.V., Werfstraat 12, 3011 Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional). EUR prices in every language.
- **History:** founded 2023 in Rotterdam by Ines Aalders and Tomás Reyes.
- **Hours:** Support Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00 CET; fitting slots Mon–Sat 06:30–11:00 (CET).
- **Audience:** owners and cooks of counter bistros, grill counters and small bars with about ten seats.
- **Tone:** Calm, precise, and understated, focusing strictly on practical kitchen routines and physical specifications. It speaks with quiet confidence without hype, superlative claims, or marketing jargon.

## Story
Susugi Wash began in 2023 in Rotterdam after founders Ines Aalders and Tomás Reyes spent years running a ten-seat dining counter. During every late service, the bottleneck was never the cooking or the plating, but the final mountain of glassware and small plates piling up beside a narrow domestic sink. Commercial dishwashers were built for banquet halls and large brasseries, demanding dedicated utility rooms, noisy extraction hoods, and three-phase power lines that compact kitchens simply do not have.

Working from their workshop on Werfstraat, Ines and Tomás set out to engineer an undercounter machine tailored specifically for dining rooms with ten seats. They settled on a compact 448 mm width that slides beneath standard 820 mm worktops, alongside a wider 598 mm companion. Every millimeter was calculated around service flow: racks sized for twenty-five wine glasses, clearance for thirty-centimeter dinner plates, an integrated drain pump and water softener, and a quiet fifty-two decibel operating volume.

Today, Susugi Wash builds compact commercial dishwashers designed to clear the closing rush in ninety-second cycles. Supported by head fitter Grace Mensah and fitter Jun Park, the company coordinates site checks and four-hour morning installations across Europe before lunch service begins. From independent wine bars to tight counter bistros, the focus remains unchanged: helping small kitchen teams finish the last rack sooner so everyone goes home on time.
