# Kaoru Cocoa: design spec (stage 1 spec, binding for implementation)

Slug `kaoru-cocoa`. A neighbourhood chocolatier and cocoa cafe that **tempers small batches in the shop every morning and serves them the same afternoon**. Row b40-3: archetype **sticky-media**, hero **framed-window**, nav **centered**, page structure **booking-led**, first screen **centered-narrow-column**, type **business** (category food-drink; WooCommerce declared compatible, not required; no shop page in the demo), photo direction **high-key**, font direction **serif** (Fraunces / Source Serif 4, CJK Noto Serif TC / Noto Serif JP, Mincho/Ming), palette "smoked bamboo" (susutake) `#6B5A4E`. Five languages: en, zh-TW, ja, fr, de.

Primary reference (ref-check passed): **Recit** https://recit-tokyo.studio.site/ (S5-Style entry; patisserie-chocolaterie one-pager). Secondary (structure of the menu cards only, never its colour): **Parade** https://parade.kyoto/. Measurements in `brand/reference-tokens.md`, quoted below as **RT(a)…RT(g)**. Screens: `brand/ref/recit-first.png` (split: fixed overhead cake plate left, white column right with centred serif menu, orbit mark, hours), `scroll-recit-900.png` (three dotted vertical phrases hanging from the menu line, 72 px "News", dotted rows with 100 px hairline thumbnails), `scroll-recit-1800.png` (plate has swapped photo; pale closed-days panel with month numerals; 36 px accordion rows with black disc chevrons), `scroll-recit-2700.png`, `scroll-recit-3400.png`, `parade-first.png`, `scroll-parade-900/1800/2700.png` (staggered three-column outline card wall with tag chips, ruled price row and inset photo).

**Imagery: photo** (stage-a IMAGERY = photo; both references are photographic still-life). Every photo is generated by us with one STYLE string (§6). We never copy Recit or Parade photos, copy, logo/wordmark (the orbit mark and script logo are not reproduced) or full-page layout. Our scribble mark, strips, cards and panels are our own SVG/CSS/markup.

## Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion
Recit's first screen is a calm split. **Left:** a high-key overhead photograph of glazed chocolate-and-red entremets lined up on a white table, cut by the frame at top, bottom and right, cool white daylight, colour only in the product. **Right:** a pure white column with a centred serif menu (Top, News, Menu, Shop Info), a circular cart button top right, a thin hand-drawn orbit mark with one rust loop above a script wordmark, and the opening hours in two lines ("Open … / Closed on Mondays"). Just under the menu hang **three dotted-edged vertical phrases** — a three-line motto about meeting ingredients, refining and telling a story. **Story:** one small pastry kitchen; every cake is a finished object; the white table is the stage, the shop stays quiet. **Signature motion:** the photo plate **stays still while the page scrolls past it, and its photograph changes section by section**; content fades up softly (Studio "appear", 0.3 s).

### (2) Our translation (same idea "the making surface is the stage", same motion family, image type photo; composition forced to framed-window / below-photo / logo-row)
Our first screen is the shop at ten in the morning. The overhead still-life becomes **an overhead high-key photograph of a maker's hands drawing a ribbon of dark tempered chocolate across a white marble slab, beside a row of filled polycarbonate moulds**, cut by the frame on three sides — the same table-as-stage, our trade instead of finished cakes. Because the row requires a **framed window**, the photograph sits as a centred window on a white page (not a half-viewport split); the window itself carries the reference motion: the image drifts inside its frame on scroll (parallax — the photo stays while the page moves), and from the end of the hero the **reference's fixed half plate takes over** (T1), swapping the photograph hour by hour as the making schedule scrolls. Under the window, the white column's elements come back in order: our scribble mark (one rust loop), the headline, one lede, the hours ("closed Mondays", as in the reference), and at the foot a logo row of the five growers whose beans are on the slab this season. The three dotted strips hang from the menu line beside the window with our three-line motto, the hours of the day: temper at ten, mould at noon, glaze at three. Concept kept: one small kitchen, the slab is the stage, the shop stays quiet and white.
