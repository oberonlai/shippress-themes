# Tsukuri Design Office

Portfolio theme for an industrial and product design studio. Home: a flat pale mauve-grey title strip with the heading at the left and a high-key photograph crossing the seam, a foam-model row cut by the fold, the problem and the method as caption rows under centred object photographs, a full-bleed model shop band, equal twin proof photographs, product rows, a dark statement band, one way to start a project and the journal. Fonts: Inter Tight and IBM Plex Sans. Brand and design notes in `brand.md` and `brand/`.

## Status
The English home page and all inner pages (Work, Method, Medical devices, Consumer products, Model shop, Studio, Fees, Start a project, Contact, Newsletter, Journal and three posts) are built; the zh-TW, ja, fr and de localisations follow. `screenshot.png` is a 1200x900 capture of the home first screen.
