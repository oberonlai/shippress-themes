## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://ishigami.studio.site/)
- **What it draws:** a flat pale warm-grey title strip across the top with a tracked name and a role line at the left, a thin burger at the top right; below the seam a quiet white stage with a centred wide-tracked label over a hairline, and a pale designed object photographed on white rising into the bottom of the screen.
- **Story:** a designer shows one finished object and the small observations from the people who use it that shaped it; calm, evidence first, the product is the hero, the designer stays in the margin.
- **Signature motion:** each text and photo block fades in and rises 20px over 0.6s, ease, one-shot when it enters the viewport; on the secondary site the hero photograph veils into the paper colour as you scroll (scrubbed 900–2700px).

### (2) Our translation (same top–bottom halves, same motion idea, same image type: high-key studio photograph with hands in frame)
Same two halves. **Top:** the pale mauve-grey strip with our H1 "From foam model to factory floor" left and the role line under it. **Right, crossing the seam:** a high-key studio photograph of **Ines Varga's hands and forearm turning a hand-sanded pale foam nurse-call handset prototype between her fingers against a white sweep** (P01) — the reference's "pale object on white" with the designer's hands that this studio sells. **Bottom stage:** the centred wide-tracked label "FOAM TO FACTORY" over a 30px mauve hairline, then a **row of seven sanded white and pale-grey foam models of the same handset in sequence** (P02), cut by the fold like the reference's rising object. Motion: the photo layer drifts slower than the page (parallax, our version of the IMT veil); everything below the hero rises 20px over 0.6s.
