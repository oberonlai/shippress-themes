# Senbatsu Partners - brand brief

A retained executive search firm in a fictional Amsterdam office, built as a landing theme: one long conversion page with an anchor menu and one repeated next step, "Open a search". Sample content is in English (stage 1) and is localised later for Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German. All names, places, people and figures are fictional; client names are never published.

## Concept
- **Name:** Senbatsu Partners. A partnership that fills one senior seat by a retained search, starting from a written role brief and ending with a signed offer.
- **Location:** Oostelijke Handelskade 1210, 1019 BL Amsterdam (fictional address). One office only.
- **Founded:** 2012 by founding partner Helena Marsh.
- **Audience:** boards, founders and chief executives who need to fill one senior seat: a CFO, COO, general counsel, CTO, chief people officer, managing director, board chair or head of plant operations.
- **Tone:** The firm communicates in a calm, precise, and understated voice that presents executive search as an exacting professional discipline. Copy relies on concrete house rules, fixed timelines, and transparent fee schedules rather than promotional superlatives or recruitment jargon.
- **Contact:** search@senbatsu-partners.example, +31 20 555 0142 (fictional). Hours Mon–Fri 08:30–18:00 CET; brief sessions are held on Tuesdays and Thursdays. A partner replies within two working days.
- **House rules (stated as rules, never as results):** one partner leads each search from start to finish; a partner runs at most 6 searches at a time; the shortlist is 3 people, presented by week 6; brief to signed offer usually takes 12–16 weeks; about 60 names are reviewed; follow-up calls at 3, 6 and 12 months after the start date.

## Story
Senbatsu Partners was founded in 2012 at Oostelijke Handelskade 1210 in Amsterdam to provide boards, founders, and chief executives with an undivided, disciplined approach to retained executive search. Established by founding partner Helena Marsh, the firm was built on the conviction that senior leadership appointments require personal ownership rather than delegated agency processes. From its single Dutch office, the firm focuses exclusively on critical executive mandates where organizations must appoint one senior seat with complete discretion and rigorous market alignment.

To preserve quality and depth on every assignment, the partnership operates under strict, binding house rules. A single partner leads each retained search from the initial brief to the signed offer, managing at most six searches at any given time. Rather than relying on public advertisements or contingent databases, the research team reviews a comprehensive market map of about 60 qualified names, presenting an exact shortlist of three vetted candidates by week six. Each shortlisted candidate is interviewed in person and referenced twice before presentation.

A standard search progresses from the half-day brief session to a signed employment agreement within 12 to 16 weeks. The firm remains actively engaged through structured follow-up review calls at 3, 6, and 12 months after the start date to ensure seamless executive integration. Backed by transparent fixed and percentage-based fees, Senbatsu Partners maintains a clear commitment: if a placed candidate leaves within 12 months, the team executes the search again for expenses only.

## A day at the firm (daily routine)
The daily routine at Oostelijke Handelskade is fixed on purpose. Tuesdays and Thursdays are brief-session days: two partners travel to the client for a half day and come back with a written role brief. On other days the research lead keeps the market maps, the associate runs referencing and assessment calls, the partners meet shortlisted candidates in person, and the client office manager schedules every interview and invoice. The office answers enquiries Mon–Fri 08:30–18:00 CET, and no candidate is approached before the client has signed off the brief.

## Process (one flow, the same words on every page)
1. Brief session (week 0, half day, two partners, at the client) → written role brief incl. the line we will not cross.
2. Market map (weeks 1–4): about 60 names reviewed, nobody approached before the brief is signed off.
3. Shortlist of 3 (by week 6): each met in person, referenced twice, still interested.
4. Interviews and references (weeks 7–11).
5. Offer and start (weeks 12–16), partner present at the offer conversation.
6. Follow-up calls at 3, 6 and 12 months.

## Fees (EUR, excl. VAT, identical on every page and language)
- Brief session — half day, two partners, written role brief; credited against the search fee if a search opens — **€4,800**
- Retained search — full process to signed offer, paid in three equal instalments (signing, shortlist, start) — **30% of first-year base + target bonus**
- Minimum search fee — applies when 30% would be lower — **€48,000**
- Board / non-executive seat — fixed fee, same process — **€36,000**
- Market map only — weeks 1–4 as a report, no approaches — **€18,000**
- Internal candidate assessment — one structured assessment + two references — **€6,500 per person**
- Expenses — travel and assessment tools at cost — **capped at 8% of fee**
- Term: if the person we placed leaves within 12 months, we run the search again for expenses only.
