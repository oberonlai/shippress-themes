## Signature candidates borrowed
1. Grey-framed CTA holder: pale box with a primary button over a white secondary button; the single next step repeated at each section.
2. Eyebrow framed by two hairlines + serif-weight headline + 80x2 accent rule (section opener, centred narrow column).
