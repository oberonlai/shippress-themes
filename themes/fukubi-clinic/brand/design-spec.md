# Fukubi Clinic: design spec (stage 1, binding for implementation)

Slug `fukubi-clinic`, gastroenterology clinic, type **business** (WooCommerce-compatible, not required). Design-plan row b26-1: font direction **sans** (Figtree / Public Sans, CJK Noto Sans TC / Noto Sans JP), palette family slate (row hex `#5E6A72`), IMAGERY **photo**, page structure narrative-problem-method-proof, five languages (en, zh-TW, ja, fr, de).

References (S5-Style, education/healthcare):
- Main: https://shinagawa-asada.jp/ (Asada). Values below are taken from `reference-tokens.md`; a value marked `tok(a)` … `tok(g)` cites that file's section.
- Secondary: https://taizankai.org/ (Taizankai). Structure only: the sweeping thin curve under the hero and the coloured top edge of facility cards. Its saturated multicolour blobs are not used.

We borrow composition, technique, colour relations, measurements and motion. We never copy their photos, copy, logos, icons or full page layout. All photos are generated by us; all copy is ours.

Fixed brand facts (identical everywhere, all five languages):

- Phone **+1 604 555 0148**, email **hello@fukubi-clinic.example**.

## 1. Signature techniques (three, each obvious at a glance)

### T1. Circle triptych on a pale wash with a blurred halo (Asada first screen, `ref/asada-first.png`)
- **Reference:** the whole first screen is a pale wash (`#E8EDE6`, ~55% of the screen). A row of round photos: a large centre circle (~560 px) with a smiling physician at a desk, two smaller circles (~316 px) at left and right showing bright interiors, and the next circles cut by the viewport edges (~17vw peeking). The centre circle sits ~40 px lower than the flanks. A large soft glowing disc (gradient, 30 px blur) sits behind the centre circle. On autoplay the row slides sideways and the next photo grows into the centre.
- **Ours, home block 1 (first screen):** exactly this composition, see §3 for geometry. Centre circle = P01 (physician turning a printed scope report toward a patient), left = P02 (nurse writing the fasting window), right = P03 (daylight consulting room), edge-cut neighbours = P04 (reception) and P05 (corridor).
- **Shape, ratio, position, contrast:** all photos masked to perfect circles (`aspect-ratio:1; border-radius:50%; object-fit:cover`), no border, no shadow. Sizes at 1440: centre 560 px, flanks 316 px, edge circles 316 px with only ~17vw visible (they start at x = −250 px and x = 1440 − 66 px). Centre top = flanks top + 40 px. Halo disc behind the centre: 38.8vw (≈ 560 px at 1440 = the centre circle + 15% margin each side), see §2 for colours.
- **Must not be diluted:** at least three full circles plus two edge-cut circles must be visible at 1280 and 1440; the centre circle must be ≥ 1.6× the flank diameter; the halo must be visibly lighter-glowing (not a flat grey disc, not removed); no rectangle photo on the first screen; the wash must fill the whole first screen behind the header.
- **Inner pages (page head, all 10 inner pages):** "mini triptych" head: wash band (`#E4EAED`, height 420 px desktop / auto mobile), page label + H1 + 1-line lead at left (6/12), at right one 320 px circle photo (page's head photo, listed in §5) with a 160 px circle cut by the right viewport edge (second photo) and the halo disc 420 px behind the big circle. Static (no cycle) on inner pages.

### T2. Dot-matrix week card docked over the centre circle (Asada hours card)
- **Reference:** a white card (505 × 190 at 1440, radius 10, no shadow) docked at the lower right of the hero, overlapping the bottom right of the centre circle. Left: clock line icon + label; 8 columns (Mon–Sun + holidays) × 2 time-band rows; filled round dot = open, short diagonal slash = closed; 1 px `#CCCCCA` lines between rows; a small note line underneath.
- **Ours, home block 1:** the **week card**: label "Clinic hours" with a clock line icon (slate stroke 1.5 px); columns Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun Hol; rows **8:00–12:30** and **14:00–18:30**. Three glyphs:
  - filled dot 14 px `#5E6A72` = open (reference: filled green dot),
  - **ring** 14 px, 2 px `#5E6A72` stroke, white centre = **scope-list morning** (our gastro addition: fasting required),
  - short slash 14 × 2 px `#B9C3C8` rotated −55° = closed.
  - Data (fixed, §6): morning Mon ● Tue ○ Wed ● Thu ○ Fri ● Sat ○ Sun / Hol /; afternoon Mon ● Tue ● Wed / Thu ● Fri ● Sat / Sun / Hol /.
  - Note line 0.8125rem: "○ Scope mornings: nothing to eat after midnight, clear fluids until 6:00."
- **Geometry:** card 505 × 190 (auto height allowed up to 220 with the note), white `#FFFFFF`, radius 10 px (tok d), padding 24/28 px, placed with its right edge at the content right (x = 1440 − 32 px) and its top at 64% of the hero; it must overlap the centre circle's lower-right edge by 60–90 px horizontally. Markup: a real `<table>` is **not** used (first screen must have no table): build as a Group of rows (Columns/Group with flex) and dots as inline SVG or spans with `role="img"` + `aria-label` ("Monday morning: open").
- **Must not be diluted:** card is white on the wash, overlaps the circle, shows all 8 columns × 2 rows with three distinct glyphs.
- **Inner pages:** the same card component (pattern `fukubi-clinic/week-card`) appears on **First visit** (right column), **Tests & endoscopy** (beside the scope section, ring glyph emphasised), **Preparation** (opening, as "your fasting window"), **Contact & hours** (full width 1100 px version, larger dots 18 px), and in the **footer** (compact 3-column-less version: text only). Same data everywhere.

### T3. Bowl-topped white cards with square chevron box (Asada "for whom" cards, `ref/scroll-asada-900.png`)
- **Reference:** white cards ~255 × 170, radius 10 (5 on phones), no shadow, on a pale ground; the top ~45% is a tinted gradient shape whose bottom edge is a downward arc (a "bowl") holding a white line icon; a centred two-line label below; a 22 px square chevron box (1 px tinted border, radius 3) at bottom right. ## Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion
- **What it draws:** a pale green wash; a row of round photos: a smiling physician at his desk in the large centre circle, the bright reception hall and the laboratory corridor in the flanking circles, more circles cut by the screen edges; a blurred glowing disc behind the centre; a light, oversized-quote-mark headline at the lower left ("“Pregnancy as a result” …"); a white dot-matrix hours card docked at the lower right overlapping the centre circle; a floating header with an outline FAQ pill and a round orange booking pill.
- **Story:** a calm, competent physician in a calm bright place, the result as the promise, and when you can come, readable at a glance.
- **Signature motion:** centred Swiper loop; every 2000 ms the row slides sideways over 1500 ms, the next photo moves into the centre and grows (active slide 56.6% → 84.6% width), the previous one shrinks out to the side (tok g).

### (2) Our transformation (gastroenterology clinic, same composition, same motion idea, same image type: high-key documentary photograph)
- **Scene:** a digestive complaint is explained before any test is booked. The centre circle is the signature photograph **P01**: a physician turning a printed scope report toward a patient across a white desk, a nurse soft in the background. Left circle **P02**: the nurse writing the next fasting window in the margin of a printed preparation sheet. Right circle **P03**: the empty daylight consulting room with two chairs and a desk. Edge circles **P04** (bright reception) and **P05** (pale corridor with frosted doors), exactly like the reference interiors.
- **Story:** "Understand it before a test is booked." The result (the printed report) is what the physician is showing; the week card tells you which mornings are scope mornings and when to stop eating.
- **Motion (same concept, CSS only):** the five circles glide one slot to the left in a loop: hold 2.5 s, glide 1.5 s (`cubic-bezier(.25,.1,.25,1)`, Swiper default ease), the photo entering the centre scales from 316 px to 560 px and drops 40 px; the one leaving shrinks to 316 px; the circle wrapping round fades out (opacity 0 during the off-screen move). Cycle 12 s for the three main photos (P04 and P05 stay as the edge circles and only shift ±20 px with the glide). Desktop ≥ 1024 px only; on phones, `prefers-reduced-motion`, `?sp-static`, and in the editor the row is static in its initial state (P01 centre). Implement with `@keyframes` on transform/opacity in the theme CSS, gated by `html.sp-js:not(.sp-no-motion)` and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference) and (min-width: 1024px)`; no JS, no slider library. The halo disc moves with `sp-parallax` (§9).
- **Headline:** lower left, Figtree 300, with oversized quote marks in slate: “**Understand it**” (slate `#5E6A72`) / "before a test is booked." (ink `#404040`). Under it one line Public Sans 1rem: "Digestive and liver care in Vancouver since 2014." and the two buttons (white, §2 button spec): "Book a first visit" and "Which symptoms we see".

## 2. Colour, type and components (reference values, cited)

### Colour
Reference relation (tok a): wash ~55%, pale page, one ink, one small accent (<2% saturated), one tiny warm point (~1%), measured saturated share **1.2%**.

| Role | Reference (tok a) | Ours | Change | Use | Share (first screen) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wash | `#E8EDE6` | `#E4EAED` | hue → slate, lightness equal (L≈92%) | hero ground, inner page heads, "who reads" panel, close panel | ~55% |
| Pale page | `#F7F7F7` / `#F3F5F0` | `#F7F7F7` / `#F2F5F6` | first kept exactly; second hue → slate | section grounds after the hero, T4 rows ground | — |
| Card / header | `#FFFFFF` | `#FFFFFF` | none | cards, week card, header bar, buttons | ~8% |
| Ink | `#404040` | `#404040` | none | body and headline second line | text |
| Main accent | `#86B812` | **`#5E6A72`** (row hex) | **hue changed** (reason below) | quote words, dots, chevron boxes, section label squares, links | ~3% |
| Accent gradient (bowls, label squares) | `#A9CE52` → `#EAEA67`, 245deg | `#7E8F98` → `#B9CCD3`, 245deg | hue changed, same lightness steps | T3 bowls, 14 px label square, chevron box fill on buttons | ~1% |
| Halo gradient | `#A9CE52` → `#EAEA67` | `#9FB4BE` → `#E6F0F2` (+ warm rim `rgba(242,178,48,.10)` at 85% radius) | hue changed; the faint warm rim keeps the reference's yellow glow edge | halo disc | ~6% (low saturation) |
| Point | `#FF9D00` / `#F2B230` | `#FF9D00` → `#F2B230` (90deg) | none | one booking pill in the header, booking button in the close block | ~0.6% |
| Line | `#CCCCCA` | `#CCCCCA` | none | 1 px dividers, week card row lines | — |
| Closed slash | (reference light green) | `#B9C3C8` | derived from wash | week card | — |
| Foot curve | (Taizankai orange line) | `#D9B98C` | point at 40% on white | separator curve | <0.3% |

- **Why the accent hue changed:** the design-plan row assigns this theme the slate family ("ai-nezumi" blue-grey, `#5E6A72`) so the catalogue keeps distinct palettes; the reference's lime green is also a fertility/fresh-produce signal that, on gastro-intestinal photos, reads bilious. Every relation is kept: same lightness of wash and page, the accent stays under 3% of the first screen, gradient direction (245deg) and step sizes are the reference's, the point stays orange in one pill only.
- Booking pill label: ink `#2A3236` Figtree 700 1rem (white on `#FF9D00` is 2.1:1; we keep the reference colour and darken the label instead).
- Contrast: `#5E6A72` on `#E4EAED` 4.6:1, `#404040` on `#E4EAED` 8.6:1, `#404040` on white 10.4:1.
- **First-screen saturated share:** target ≤ 5% (reference 1.2%), hard cap for `scripts/check-first-screen.py` **15%** (common rule; the row note's 30% is superseded by the stricter 15%). Achieved by the cool high-key grade of P01–P05, slate accent and one small orange pill.
- Not beige-led: the wash is cool slate-grey; no cream, no beige anywhere.
