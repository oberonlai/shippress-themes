# Fukubi Clinic

Fukubi Clinic is a clean, accessible block theme tailored for outpatient gastroenterology, digestive health, and medical specialist practices. Built around calm slate tones and precise typography, it features dot-matrix timetable cards, multi-circle hero compositions, and structured symptom indexes. Every layout prioritises patient clarity, transparent fee disclosures, and unhurried clinical storytelling.

## Highlights

- Home: a row of five circular photographs on a pale slate wash with a docked dot-matrix clinic-hours card, bowl-topped symptom cards, a first-visit timeline in minute rows, a parallax scope-room photograph and dated news rows.
- Fonts: Figtree (headings) and Public Sans (reading), bundled locally; CJK fallbacks Noto Sans TC and Noto Sans JP.
- Motion: short scroll reveals and one parallax photograph; everything is static under `prefers-reduced-motion`, in the editor, and without JavaScript.
- Works without WooCommerce. Sample content ships in English; Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow.

See readme.txt for installation, fonts and image credits.
