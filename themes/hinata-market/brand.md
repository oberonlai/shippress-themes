# Hinata Market: brand

## Name and location
**Hinata Market**, a weekend farmers' market. Location: Linden Station Square, Easthaven (a fictional town), between the station's east exit and the tram stop. Market office: Linden Station Square, tel. 020 448 6130, hello@hinata-market.example. Sub-line: Station Square · Saturdays.

## Founded
Spring 2011, when nine local growers and the station's community council set up the first trestle tables on the square. Rows C and D were added in 2015, which brought the market to forty pitches in four rows of ten. The weekly list started with issue No. 1 on Saturday 5 November 2022; the front page in the sample content shows Issue No. 212, Saturday 21 November 2026.

## Audience
Visitors who shop in the town centre on Saturday mornings; growers, bakers and cheesemakers within sixty kilometres who want a pitch; and the organisers of farmers' markets, town-centre management bodies and producer cooperatives who need a clear weekly front page.

## Voice
Neighbourly, plain and specific. Numbers instead of adjectives: forty pitches, 08:00–14:00, sixty kilometres, the 1st of each month. No promises about prices, organic certification or what will be on a stall.

## Story
Hinata Market was established in the spring of 2011 when nine local growers partnered with the Linden Station community council to create a regular food market at Linden Station Square in Easthaven. In those first seasons, growers traded from simple trestle tables beneath plain canvas awnings, with a weekly routine anchored by morning harvests and direct conversation with shoppers.

In 2015 two more rows were chalked on the square, adding rows C and D for farmhouse cheese, heritage fruit, honey and bread alongside the founding vegetable and egg stalls. Every grower sells only what they grow, rear or make within sixty kilometres of the square, and there is no resale.

In November 2022 the market launched the weekly list, sent every Thursday at 18:00 with the next Saturday's stalls and notices. Now in its fifteenth year, the market runs every Saturday from 08:00 to 14:00 (December to February 09:00 to 13:00).

## A day (daily routine)
05:30 Tomas chalks the pitches; 06:00 the vans come in through the east gate; 07:40 crates come out; 07:55 the bell; 08:00 the market opens; 11:00 soup or coffee round; 14:00 close; 14:30 the square is swept. The Saturday routine is the same every week; only the stalls and the season change.

## Market facts
40 pitches in four rows A to D (ten each). Hours: every Saturday 08:00–14:00; December to February 09:00–13:00; growers arrive from 05:30; closed on the Saturday after 25 December. Market office Tuesday to Thursday 10:00–16:00. Saturday visitor desk at pitch A1. Payment: card and cash at all forty stalls. Bring your own bags; paper or own bags only. Dogs on short leads.

## How a grower applies
1. Apply. Form or email; applications are read on the 1st of each month and answered within 10 working days.
2. Farm visit. Amara visits within 3 weeks to confirm what you grow and that it is within 60 km.
3. Two trial Saturdays at €19 each.
4. Your pitch. A pitch number and an offer of a season pass.

## Grower rules
Grow, rear or make it yourself. Within 60 km of the square. Someone from the farm at the stall all morning. A clear price on everything. No resale of bought-in goods. Paper or own bags only.

## Weekly list
One email every Thursday at 18:00: next Saturday's stalls, what is in season, market notices and a note from one grower.
