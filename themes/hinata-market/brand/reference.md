# Reference notes (counterpart column completed in the stage 1 design spec, `design-spec.md`)

Main: https://nosson.jp/ - community organisation front page set as a newspaper: masthead strip, three ruled columns, a stadium lead photo with corner diamond badges, a giant date numeral with weather, dotted scissor-cut rows. Sub-selection S5-Style (community / events / local-business gallery).
Secondary: https://hitomachi.iwamiginzan.jp/ - cooperative site: peach ground + deep-green ink, one tall centred paper column, sticker badge, fixed side slideshow, curtain + underline motion.

## Trick candidates (shape, composition, place)
1. Gazette frame: 2px frame inset 16px, masthead strip (issue no / date / pill) with a rule, three columns divided by 1px rules (430 / 650 / 318).
2. Date stamp card: giant date numeral at the head of the right column, vertical weekday and month, arrowed rule, two stadium-outline cells (weather / temperature).
3. Scissor-cut dotted rows: notice rows with 121x74 thumbnail at the right; dotted divider ending in a scissor glyph.
4. Stadium lead photo with four diamond badges (support).
5. Secondary: fixed side photo slide beside a narrow centred column; rotating badge.
