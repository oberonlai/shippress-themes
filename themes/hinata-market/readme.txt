Hinata Market is a gazette-inspired WordPress block theme for weekend farmers' markets, community food hubs and local grower collectives. The whole page sits in a ruled frame: a masthead strip, three ruled columns, a date stamp card, dotted notice rows, a stall ticker and a stall strip.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

Fonts: Hanken Grotesk (headings) and Albert Sans (reading text), both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Chinese headings use a Noto Sans TC subset (OFL 1.1). Japanese headings use a Noto Sans JP subset (OFL 1.1).

== Installation ==

1. Download hinata-market.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/hinata-market.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Hinata Market.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with their text and images, four notices with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Optional forms: the weekly-list box uses a small dynamic block. With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active, their form is shown; without a plugin it shows an email field that opens your mail app (you are not on the list until you send the request). The contact and pitch application pages use Contact Form 7 or WPForms when present, and a mail button otherwise.

Inner pages: about, notices, stalls and growers, in season, visit, apply for a pitch, pitch fees, weekly list, contact and privacy, plus one notice, each with a ruled mini-gazette head and a date card. Scroll effects use the bundled sp-scroll files (assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js); they respect prefers-reduced-motion.

The market, its people, places and prices are fictional.
