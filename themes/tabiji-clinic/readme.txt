Tabiji Clinic is a calm, photographic theme for travel health and vaccination clinics. An inset rounded photo frame with a notch card cut into its corner opens the home page, followed by a statement flanked by stepped photo pairs, white journey cards on a pale band with ghost line drawings, feather-edged appointment rows, a slate-mist record band and fees with dotted rules. Figtree and Public Sans. Sample content in English.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). Fonts: Figtree for headings and Public Sans for reading (both OFL-1.1, bundled in assets/fonts). Traditional Chinese headings use Noto Sans TC; Japanese headings use Noto Sans JP (both OFL-1.1, subset files in assets/fonts).

== Installation ==

1. Download tabiji-clinic.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/tabiji-clinic.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Tabiji Clinic.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, sample notes with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. The theme works without WooCommerce.

The clinic, its people, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
