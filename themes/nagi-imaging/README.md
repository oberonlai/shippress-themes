# Nagi Imaging

A calm block theme (type health) for a diagnostic imaging centre. Home page, inner pages, news and one article (English) in this release; other languages follow.

## Signature
- Scattered field of seven rounded low-key photographs, each drifting at its own speed (`sp-parallax`).
- Headline on pure white strips crossing a photograph's edge.
- Pinned statement whose ground turns white to slate mist, thin white type with a hairline.

## Patterns

- `front-hero`
- `front-people`

## Fonts
Digits are drawn in Public Sans (a `Nagi Digits` unicode-range face in style.css) because Atkinson's slashed zero reads badly in times and prices.

Public Sans and Atkinson Hyperlegible (SIL OFL 1.1, licences in assets/fonts).
