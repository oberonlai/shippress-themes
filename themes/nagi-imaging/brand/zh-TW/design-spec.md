Design row b16-1: archetype split-screen, hero photo-left-text-right, first screen top-bottom halves, nav top-bar, real photography, photo direction **low-key**, page structure **narrative → problem → method → proof**, font direction **sans**.

## 1. Signature techniques (three, obvious at a glance, never "softened")

### T1. Scattered rounded photo field with different drift speeds (mstage KV, `tok (e)`, `tok (g)`)
- **Reference (mstage-group.jp first screen, `ref/ms-fold.png`)**: a plain white viewport with 8+ rounded rectangles of photography scattered with no grid, sizes 87–548 px wide, aspect ≈ 1.5:1, the largest 548×309 (38% of the width) near the centre; several are cut by the viewport edges (left −232, right 1410+, top −200). Radius 10.5 px, no border, no shadow, no overlap between photos. While scrolling each photo drifts upward at its own slow speed (≈ 0.06–0.08 of scroll; measured −100 / −70 / −90 px over 1200 px of scroll).
- **Nagi home**: block 1 (hero). Seven frames (one large + six small) on the white/light-grey field, exact coordinates in section 3.1. Each frame is its own `sp-parallax` with a different `--sp-par` (4–10%), so the pictures drift at visibly different speeds inside their frames.
- **Shape rules**: `border-radius: 10px` (`tok (d)` 10.5 → 10 px) on every frame, `overflow: clip`, no border, no shadow, no mask, no clip-path. Aspect 1.78:1 for the large frame (ref 548×309), 1.5–1.6:1 for small frames (ref 279×187, 255×162, 248×165, 154×113). At least three frames must be cut by a viewport edge on desktop (top, left, right). Frames never overlap each other; only text strips (T2) overlap a frame.
- **Must not be diluted**: no grid alignment, no equal sizes, no rounding above 12 px or below 8 px, no drop shadows, no "collage" overlap of photos. The contrast between low-key (near-black) photos and the white field is the point — do not lighten the photos to blend in.
- **Mobile 390**: large frame in flow (full width − 32 px), then a row of three small frames at 30% width each with different `margin-top` (0 / 28 / 10 px) so the scatter is still read; the other three small frames `display:none` below 782 px. No absolutely positioned items on mobile.

### T2. Headline on white highlight strips crossing a photo edge (mstage H1, `tok (f)`)
- **Reference**: the H1 is set line by line on separate white strips (padding ≈ 8 px, white ground, thin grey type); the strips start over the left part of the central photo and run out onto the white page, so the strip edge visibly cuts across the photo.
- **Nagi home**: hero H1 crosses the **right** edge of the large console photo (photo-left-text-right). Also used on the parallax band (block 6, strips over the full-bleed photo) and on the method block (block 4, strips crossing P10's right edge).
- **Spec**: each line is its own `<span class="nagi-strip">` inside the H1 (`display:inline` with `box-decoration-break: clone; -webkit-box-decoration-break: clone`, or one block span per line with `display:table`), background `#FFFFFF`, padding `8px 14px`, Public Sans 300, ink `#49565E`, line-height 1.0, gap between strips 14 px (ref pitch 66 px at 33.7 px font → ours 2.625rem font with 14 px gap). No shadow, no radius (strips are square-cornered as in the reference).
- **Must not be diluted**: the strips must physically overlap the photo by ≥ 56 px desktop (≥ 24 px mobile), they must be pure white (not translucent), and type must stay thin (300) and ink-grey, never bold, never white-on-dark.

### T3. Pinned statement on a section that turns white → slate mist, white thin type with a 1 px hairline (mstage PURPOSE/message section, `tok (a)`, `tok (g)`)

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen — concept, story, signature motion
- **Scene**: a white field with about eight rounded documentary photographs of medical life — a hand holding an infant's finger (largest, centre), staff on a rooftop, a doctor talking with a patient, a hospital corridor with a nurse walking away, office people, blurred interiors, plants — floating at irregular sizes and positions, some cut by the screen edges. The headline sits on thin white strips that cross the central photo. A quiet news ticker runs along the bottom.
- **Story**: "everything for a sustainable medical future" — many small human moments of care, each in its own frame, together making one purpose.
- **Signature motion**: on scroll each frame drifts upward at its own slow speed (0.06–0.08 of scroll, smooth-scrolled), then the page pins a statement while the ground turns from white to blue-grey `#C5D1D5`.

### (2) Our translation — the same scene in a diagnostic imaging room
- **Scene (same composition, same image type = documentary photographs)**: the white field becomes the outside of a dark reading room. Each frame is one low-key moment on a study's path from a nearby clinic to a written report: the **largest frame** (same role as the infant-hand photo) is the radiographer at a darkened console, monitor glow on their face and hands; around it smaller frames show the CT gantry edge in dim light, greyscale light on a reading monitor, the corridor with a lit door, hands on a keyboard with a dictation microphone, a printed report on a desk, a waiting chair by a dusk window.
- **Story**: "Scans from your clinic, read and reported the same day" — many small, quiet steps (scan, read, second read, sign, send) joined into one same-day report.
- **Motion (same concept)**: every frame drifts at its own slow speed (T1, `sp-parallax` 4–10%); just below the fold the statement pins and the ground turns white → slate mist (T3).
- **Row constraints applied**: split-screen + photo-left-text-right = the large console photo sits left, the strip headline runs to the right; first screen top-bottom halves = the field is divided at 60% height: top half white `#FFFFFF` (photo field + headline), bottom half light grey `#F2F2F2` (`tok (a)` light section) carrying the lede, two text links and the ticker; one frame straddles the boundary so the halves still read as one field.
