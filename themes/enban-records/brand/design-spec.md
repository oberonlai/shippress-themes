# Enban Records: design spec (stage 1 spec, binding for implementation)

Slug `enban-records`. An independent record shop in one room where **people listen before they buy**: one turntable on a wooden counter, a listening desk, bins of sleeves, and a crate of new arrivals refilled every opening day at 17:00. Row b40-4: archetype **editorial-magazine**, hero **text-left-photo-right**, nav **overlay**, first screen **horizontal-scroll-strip**, page structure **audience-gateway**, type **store** (category shops-hobbies; **WooCommerce required, checkout must work**: the crate records are real products), photo direction **film-grain**, font direction **serif** (Newsreader / Literata, CJK Noto Serif TC / Noto Serif JP, Ming/Mincho), palette "grape grey" (budou-nezumi) `#65586A`. Five languages: en, zh-TW, ja, fr, de.

Primary reference (ref-check passed): **Disk Union online shop** https://diskunion.net/ (record / CD retail). No secondary site (see stage-a.md). The live site geo-blocks the build machine; measurements come from the archived render of 2026-10-08 in `brand/reference-tokens.md`, quoted below as **RT(a)…RT(g)**. Screens: `brand/ref/du-first.png` (black keyword bar, centre slide with neighbours cut by both viewport edges, black square arrow tabs, square pager dots on a grey strip), `du-900.png` (5-up news cards, grey category bars), `scroll-du-1800.png` (bar docked at the top while scrolling), `scroll-du-2700.png` (5-up sleeve cards with format disc, bold price + small tax suffix, full-width outline status box; 330 × 55 outline "see more" button with chevron; full-width 1 px separator; centred title over grey caps sub-label).

**Imagery: photo** (stage-a IMAGERY = photo; the reference is photographic packshots). Every photo is generated by us with one STYLE string (§6). We never copy Disk Union images, sleeves, copy, logo/wordmark or full-page layout; the bar, cards, glyphs, buttons and timeline are our own markup/CSS/SVG.

Shop: **7 Weir Lane, Halden Row, Easterby** (fictional, international-neutral). Email `hello@enban-records.example`. Phone **+00 41 555 0147** (fictional format). One room, **about 6,000 records** in the bins, **one listening desk** with one turntable and two pairs of headphones.

## Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion
Disk Union's first screen is a shop window for this week: a **black keyword bar** with "Recommended keywords" and a slash list of artists and events, then a **grey stage strip** carrying a **wide centre slide** (a framed announcement, ~900 × 450, 2:1) whose **neighbours are cut by both viewport edges** (previous release art at left, next at right, ~255 px each visible), **black square arrow tabs** sitting on the neighbour edges and a row of **square pager dots** on the grey strip underneath. The story: "this week's releases at a glance — and what to hunt for". The signature motion: the slide strip advances sideways (Slick carousel, centre mode, ~500 ms default ease), while the keyword bar stays docked on scroll.

### (2) Our translation (same idea "this week, at a glance, in a strip", same image type photo; composition forced to inset-card / beside-photo / timeline)
Our first screen is the shop at one in the afternoon, when the desk opens. The announcement slide becomes **an inset film-grain photograph of a browser at the listening desk** — one person in headphones at a wooden counter, a turntable, a record spinning, two sleeves stood against the plaster wall behind — set as a card with margin on every side at the right of the screen. The neighbour slide survives as **the next photograph peeking 120 px at the right viewport edge**, cut by the edge exactly as Disk Union's next release is. The black square arrow tabs and square dots stay on the card. The headline the reference puts inside the slide moves **beside** the photo (hero_text = beside-photo): "Listen first. Then decide." The grey pager strip under the reference stage becomes our **timeline strip** (hero_footer = timeline): the shop day's four stops on a pale band, with the listening-desk hours marked. Motion: the keyword bar is docked (sticky, as the reference); the strip advances sideways only on click (500 ms ease, no autoplay); the photo drifts inside its card on scroll (parallax); the desk segment of the timeline draws once on load.
