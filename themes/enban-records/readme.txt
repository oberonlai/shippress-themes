Enban Records — a WooCommerce store theme for an independent record shop where people listen before they buy. A docked black keyword bar under a transparent overlay header; a hero with the headline beside an inset film-grain photograph card with a peeking next photo, square arrow tabs and square dots, and the shop day as a timeline band; centred section heads between full-width hairlines, an outline box button with a chevron, and five-up square sleeve cards with a format disc, a bold price and an outline status box. Newsreader for headings, Literata for reading. Sample content and products in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German. Works without WooCommerce.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download enban-records.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/enban-records.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Enban Records.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, journal entries with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. With WooCommerce active it also adds five sample crate products (inc/woo-import.php); without WooCommerce the theme works as a plain site and the crate on the home page shows the sleeves.

Forms: the contact page and the newsletter sections use a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With Contact Form 7 or WPForms (contact) and MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP (newsletter) active, their forms are shown and styled; without a plugin the contact page keeps a mailto button and the newsletter shows a real email field and submit button that opens your email app, with a clear note that you are not subscribed until the message is sent.

The shop, its people, records, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
