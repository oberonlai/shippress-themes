# Enban Records - brand brief

Independent record shop in Easterby, one room, one turntable, one listening desk. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people, records and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Enban Records. "Enban" is the round disc itself: the shop sells only what has been played on its own counter.
- **Location:** 7 Weir Lane, Halden Row, Easterby (fictional, international-neutral). Email hello@enban-records.example, phone +00 41 555 0147 (fictional format). Prices in euros including VAT in every language.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Ines Valdane, with the conviction that music should be heard before it is bought.
- **People (all fictional):** Ines Valdane (founder, buyer: jazz and soul); Kofi Asante-Lind (buyer: dub, reggae, Ethio-groove); Mira Lindqvist (listening desk, pulls the records for appointments); Rafael Ortega (cleaning and grading, fills the crate at 17:00); June Park (Saturday counter, writes the Friday letter); Tomasz Wierzba (turntable set-ups, Wednesdays and Thursdays). Customers in the stories: Aurelio Bassi, Delphine Roux, and the Thursday 13:00 regulars.
- **Today:** about 6,000 records in the bins, one listening desk with one turntable and two pairs of headphones, a crate by the door refilled every opening day.
- **Audience:** diggers who know what they want, first-time buyers with no turntable yet, and people selling a parent's shelf or clearing one before a move.
- **Tone:** calm, unhurried and precise, grounded in the physical routine of the shop. Informative and understated; no hype, superlatives, awards, press, ratings, guarantees or real artists, labels or brands.

## The shop day (daily routine, the same four stops everywhere)
- **11:00** Doors open: the bins are restocked from yesterday's buying.
- **13:00** The listening desk opens: appointments on the hour until 16:00.
- **17:00** Today's crate goes out: the day's cleaned and graded arrivals go into the crate by the door.
- **19:00** Doors close.
Open Wednesday to Sunday 11:00-19:00; closed Monday and Tuesday. Listening appointments Wednesday to Sunday at 13:00, 14:00, 15:00 and 16:00, 45 minutes, one or two people, up to 12 records pulled in advance. Replies to booking and selling requests within one working day.

## Prices (EUR including VAT; identical on the home page, the Prices page and the product pages)
Listening appointment EUR 6 (credited to any purchase that day) - Used LP from EUR 14 (graded VG+ or better, cleaned) - New LP from EUR 29 - 7-inch single from EUR 9 - Record cleaning EUR 4 per disc - Shelf valuation at home EUR 40 (within 15 km, up to 300 records, credited if we buy) - Turntable set-up EUR 25 (30 minutes at the desk) - Gift card from EUR 20 (valid two years).

## How a listening appointment works
1. Tell us what you like: three records you love, or a mood, by form, email or at the counter. 2. We pull up to twelve: Mira pulls and cleans them the morning of your slot. 3. Listen at the desk: 45 minutes, your hour, one turntable, two pairs of headphones. 4. Take one home, or none: the EUR 6 is credited to anything you buy; records you did not choose go back in the bins.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. A docked black 40 px keyword bar under the header: a bold label "On the desk this week" and a slash-separated list of genre links (stays put while the page scrolls).
2. Five-up square sleeve cards: 1:1 sleeve with no frame, a round format disc glyph, a bold price with a small "incl. VAT" suffix and a full-width 1 px outline status box.
3. Centred section heads with a grey caps sub-label between full-width hairlines, and a 330 x 55 outline box button with a chevron.
Supporting: an inset photo card with a peeking next photograph, square arrow tabs and square dots; the shop day as a timeline band.
