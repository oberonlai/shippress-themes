# Shirabe Research - brand brief

Independent qualitative market research practice. Sample content in English first; Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German follow in stage two, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shirabe Research. "Shirabe" is a Japanese word for investigation; the practice itself is international and modern.
- **Location:** 4F, 27 Calder Street, Northbank (fictional), with interview studios A and B.
- **History:** founded in 2012 by Clara Wendt.

Shirabe Research was founded in 2012 by Clara Wendt as an independent, qualitative-first market research practice. Established on Calder Street in Northbank, the studio was created around a straightforward premise: organizational decision-makers need direct, unfiltered contact with the people who use their products and services. Rather than relying entirely on high-level surveys or detached telemetry, Shirabe Research built its reputation by sitting across the table from participants and listening to their unvarnished experiences.

Over the years, the practice expanded its facilities to include dedicated interview studios A and B at 4F, 27 Calder Street, accommodating both individual depth interviews and collaborative analysis sessions. As the team grew to seven specialized practitioners working across five languages, the firm refined its hallmark fieldwork approach: conducting in-person moderated interviews, undertaking contextual visits to homes and workplaces, and organizing rigorous paper-charting sessions with client teams.

Today, Shirabe Research delivers tightly focused five-week studies for organizations navigating product shifts, membership changes, and complex customer journeys. By maintaining small sample sizes, disciplined recruitment, and transparent reporting grounded in direct fieldwork, the practice provides clear qualitative foundations that allow leadership teams to make informed decisions with confidence.

## Process (5 stages, typical study 5 weeks)
- Stage 1: Brief (week 0, 90-minute meeting)
- Stage 2: Recruit (weeks 1–2)
- Stage 3: Interview (weeks 2–3)
- Stage 4: Chart on paper (week 4, team + client)
- Stage 5: Readout and written report (week 5)
