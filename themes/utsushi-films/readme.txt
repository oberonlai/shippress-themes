A calm, low-key documentary portfolio theme for independent film directors, producers, and small studios. Built with Space Grotesk and Inter Tight typography, featuring an asymmetric case wall, a centred framed film window crossed by works indices, and full site editing patterns. Designed for showcasing commissioned broadcast documentaries, institutional projects, and festival cinema. The home page opens on a charcoal screen with a centred 16:9 film window holding a low-key frame of the director at a fishing-boat door, crossed by a six-row film index, then continues with a masonry case wall, the method panel, three phases, a parallax harbour band, commission rate rows, the crew index and the single way to propose a film. Space Grotesk for headings, Inter Tight for reading. Works without WooCommerce. Sample content in English; Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download utsushi-films.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/utsushi-films.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Utsushi Films.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with their text and images, a journal entry with a category and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. Languages: the sample content is in demo-content.json (English); other languages use the `shippress_demo_lang` option.

Forms: the newsletter section uses a dynamic form block (inc/shippress-forms.php). Without a form plugin it shows an email field and a submit button that opens your mail program with the address filled in (the visitor is not subscribed until the message is sent). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP it shows that form.

The studio, its films, broadcasters, festivals, people, address and prices are fictional.
