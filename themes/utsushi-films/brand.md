# Utsushi Films - brand brief

An independent documentary director's small studio: commissioned documentaries for public broadcasters, NGOs and institutions, plus self-initiated festival films. One action everywhere: "Propose a film". Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people, festivals, broadcasters and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Utsushi Films. A quiet, dark, index-like site: the work speaks first, picked from a list of films.
- **Location:** Studio 3, Ropewalk Yard, 12 Netmakers' Lane, Port Alder (fictional). Phone 010 482 7716. Email propose@utsushi-films.example (every mailto).
- **Founded:** 2013 by Ines Calder, a former local-radio producer who listened to harbour workers, mechanics and townspeople before she ever held a camera. A seven-person crew since the late 2010s.
- **People (seven):** Ines Calder (director, since 2013), Tomasz Wren (producer, 2014), Adaeze Morrow (cinematographer, 2016), Luca Ferreira (sound recordist, 2017), Hana Sørlie (editor, 2018), Priya Natarajan (research and access, 2019), Owen Achterberg (colourist and post, 2020).
- **Audience:** commissioning editors at broadcasters, NGO communications leads, foundations and institutions, festival programmers.
- **Tone:** calm, direct and unadorned; patient, observational, respectful of real working lives. Specific about months, weeks, fees and who does what. No promotional hyperbole, no awards, no guarantees, no real media or people.

## A day in the field (the daily routine)
05:30 on the quay while the boats leave - film until the work stops - logs and sound notes at night. The routine follows the people in the film: field days are whenever they work. The studio itself keeps office days Monday to Thursday.

## Hours (identical everywhere)
- Studio Mon-Thu 10:00-18:00; Friday by appointment.
- Proposals are read every Monday; reply within 5 working days.
- Field days: whenever the people in the film work.

## How a commission is shaped (five steps)
01 First conversation (free, 45 min) - 02 Research and access (up to 6 weeks) - 03 Living alongside (shoot blocks over 3-9 months) - 04 Edit (10-16 weeks, two viewing rounds) - 05 Delivery and screenings. Home groups them as Listen (01-02), Stay (03), Shape (04-05).

## Field letters
One letter on the first Monday of each month: a still, a field note, open months. Subscribe on /letters/ (and the home page).

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Film index crossing a centred framed window (T1): a dark ground, a 16:9 window and a full-width index of rows that passes across it.
2. Asymmetric case wall in index-row voice (T2): three masonry columns at different offsets, captions like index rows.
3. The single white "Propose a film" box and the page-turn line heads (T3).

## Photography
Real low-key documentary photographs (generated for this theme): available light, deep shadows, muted colour, one practical light per frame; no text, no logos, no cut-outs.
