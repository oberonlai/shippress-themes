# Shitsurae Interiors: brand

- **Name:** Shitsurae Interiors. An interior design studio for flats and small shops (renovations and fit-outs). The business is fictional.
- **Location:** Unit 3, Kiln Yard, 21 Harwell Row, Lindmere (fictional). Phone +00 20 555 0193. Email studio@shitsurae.example.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Mara Ellison. Seven people. At most 14 projects a year, never more than 5 on site at once.
- **Hours:** Studio Monday to Friday 09:00–18:00. Site visits Tuesday to Saturday 09:00–17:00, by appointment. Closed Sundays and public holidays.
- **Audience:** owners of city flats and small shops who want a room drawn around how they live or sell; independent interior designers and small fit-out studios who buy this theme.
- **Tone:** calm, specific and unhurried. It speaks plainly about materials, schedules and daily routines, without slogans, superlatives or promised results. Budgets and weeks on site are published.

## A day in the studio (daily routine)
The day starts at 09:00 at Kiln Yard with a walk through the sample library and the week's site list. Ayo checks schedules and budgets, Tomás is in the workshop fitting oak, Priya prepares sample boards, Hanna and Mara draw. Jonas is on a site most days; Lucie answers every booking enquiry within two working days. Site visits run Tuesday to Saturday, 09:00–17:00, by appointment.

## Process (five steps)
01 Survey (week 1): the 90-minute visit, measured survey within 7 days. 02 Brief and budget (weeks 2–3): one written page, one number. 03 Design and samples (weeks 3–7): drawings and a sample board to hold against your wall. 04 Works on site (shops 3–9 weeks, flats 5–14): Jonas on site every week. 05 Handover: one walkthrough, care notes, a return visit after 6 months.

## Fees (EUR)
Consultation visit €180 (90 minutes on site, notes within 3 working days; deducted from the design package if you go ahead). Measured survey and brief €650. Design package, flat (up to 70 m²) €3,400. Design package, shop (up to 120 m²) €4,800. Site supervision €520 per week of works. Joinery drawings €380 per piece. Building works are billed by the contractor at cost, never marked up.

## The single way to book
A consultation visit (€180, 90 minutes). Write to studio@shitsurae.example or call; Lucie replies within 2 working days with a date, Tuesday to Saturday.
