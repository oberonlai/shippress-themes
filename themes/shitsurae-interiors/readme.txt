Shitsurae Interiors is a portfolio and studio theme for interior designers, architects, and bespoke fit-out practices. Designed around high-key documentary photography and structured case studies, it presents finished rooms, transparent budgets, and hand-crafted material details with calm clarity. Built with block patterns, responsive typography, and full site editing support, it delivers an unhurried, tactile editorial experience.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The home page opens on a full-bleed room photograph with a white caption strip under it, then runs through case rows with briefs and budgets, a sticky works index beside a two-column grid, a strip of tall material photographs, five ruled process rows, one booking band and the journal. See README.md for the design notes.

== Installation ==

1. Download shitsurae-interiors.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/shitsurae-interiors.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Shitsurae Interiors.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, journal entries with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The scroll effects (assets/sp-scroll.css and sp-scroll.js) respect prefers-reduced-motion and show the final state without JavaScript. The newsletter and contact slots use Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when active; without a plugin they fall back to an email link.

The studio, its people, address, telephone number, email and prices are fictional.
