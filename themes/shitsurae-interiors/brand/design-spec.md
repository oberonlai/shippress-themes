## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://niwahouzing.com/)
- **What it draws:** one whole finished living-dining room photographed at eye height with a wide lens: high white ceiling lit by cove light, a black arc lamp and a potted tree in front, a long dark sofa, oak floor running away to the right toward the kitchen. Transparent caps nav sits on the bright ceiling; one solid black CONTACT button at the top right. Under the photo, a white strip: tiny two-line descriptor left, model name and a loop-ring timer right.
- **Story:** "the finished room is the portfolio" — no slogan, no claim; every image is a real, named, dated project, and the visitor steps straight into it.
- **Signature motion:** slides change every 4s with a 1.1s sliding parallax (image layer slower than the frame); the ring fills over the 4s timer and the name swaps; below the fold paragraphs and photos rise in over 1.2s; section words open with a slanted mask over 1.4s.

### (2) Our translation (same composition, same motion idea, same image type: high-key documentary photograph)
One whole just-finished flat, photographed at eye height with a wide lens in bright daylight: **Flat 4, Marlow Street. A pale, freshly plastered warm-white wall on the left third; Mara Ellison, seen from behind in three-quarter view, holds a linen fabric swatch flat against the plaster; right of centre Tomás Reyes, kneeling and turned away, slides the last oak board into a built-in alcove shelf; an oak floor runs away to the right toward a tall window; the top 15% is bright ceiling.** It is the reference's "finished room as portfolio", with the work's final minute still in it — the designer's choice and the carpenter's hands — which is what this studio sells. Header floats on the bright ceiling; one solid ink "Book a consultation" button top right; the white caption strip under the photo names the project and its size, and the ring fills while two more finished projects (the bakery, the family bedroom) take their turn. Scroll: the case wall follows directly with briefs and budgets.
