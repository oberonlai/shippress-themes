# Tsubasa College

An editorial block theme for an aircraft maintenance training college: licence-track programme, hangar teaching, cohort intakes and a published fee ledger. Quiet iron-slate palette with a single red accent, Fraunces headings over Source Sans 3 text.

## Home page

- Focus-pull hero: a documentary hangar photograph with a sharp centre window, pale blurred flanks, the headline on a light frosted panel and two rotated work-point pills (flat row under 1024px).
- Slate cohort strip with a white notice card for the next intake.
- Intro with chevron module links and four count-up facts.
- Isometric field of eight module tiles on a wash-to-slate band.
- Horizontal 18-month ruler, certification ledger (core table block), parallax hangar band.
- Instructors, fees table, journal and newsletter slot.

## Notes

- Block theme (templates, parts, patterns). Patterns hold literal block markup; copy is translatable through `inc/i18n`.
- Scroll effects use the bundled `sp-scroll` assets (reveal, stagger, count, parallax) and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Contact and newsletter pages use the shared `shippress_form_slot` block (Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet, Mailchimp for WP, or a mailto fallback).
- Fonts (Fraunces, Source Sans 3, SIL OFL 1.1) are bundled in `assets/fonts` and loaded through `theme.json`.
- Photographs in `assets/images` are AI-generated for the demo; replace them with your own.
- Sample content is English; further languages are added in later releases.
