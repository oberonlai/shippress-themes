# Otonoha Acoustics: brand

## Name, place, founding
- **Name:** Otonoha Acoustics. "Otonoha" is Japanese for "leaves of sound": the studio reads each room leaf by leaf — reverberation, background noise, sound insulation, speech. An independent architectural acoustic consultancy of 7 people that measures offices, classrooms, lecture rooms and small halls before finishes are specified.
- **Location:** Studio 4, 18 Ferrow Lane, Calder Wharf (fictional, international-neutral).
- **Founded:** 2011, by Mira Halvorsen.
- **Line:** Hear the room before it is finished.

## Story
Otonoha Acoustics was founded in 2011 by Mira Halvorsen in Calder Wharf to provide independent architectural acoustic testing before interior finishes and treatment schedules are finalised. Operating from Studio 4 at 18 Ferrow Lane, the practice began with a straightforward observation: room acoustics are routinely compromised when surface specifications rely on theoretical calculations rather than physical on-site measurements.

The name Otonoha translates to "leaves of sound", reflecting a diagnostic method of reading each space leaf by leaf. Since 2011 the practice has grown into a focused team of 7 people — consultants and field engineers measuring offices, classrooms, lecture halls and small recital spaces. They assess physical volume, reverberation decay, mechanical background noise, sound insulation and speech intelligibility in empty rooms before finishes are permanently installed.

As an independent consultancy, Otonoha Acoustics neither manufactures nor sells acoustic panels or interior products. It delivers unbiased technical data and practical finish proposals within 10 working days of every site visit, so architects, contractors and building operators can refine room acoustics while construction drawings and material procurement can still change.

## Audience
Architects, interior designers, contractors, school and university estates teams, office fit-out managers and the operators of small halls who need measured facts about a room before they specify wall, ceiling and floor finishes.

## Tone
Calm, precise and understated. The voice speaks directly to architects and builders in plain English about measurements and findings, without promotional hype, guaranteed outcomes or decorative language. Posts say "what the report proposed", never "we fixed".
