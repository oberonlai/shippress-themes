# Yui Credit Union - brand brief

Member-owned credit union in the fictional riverside city of Lindenport. Stage 1 covers the home page in English; inner pages and the other languages follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional, and every rate is a sample for demonstration.

## Concept
- **Name:** Yui Credit Union. Founded 1961 by mill and market workers.
- **Status:** not-for-profit cooperative, 18,420 members, 3 branches, 7 volunteer directors elected each March. No real deposit insurer, regulator, award or media is claimed.
- **Audience:** households and small shop owners in Lindenport County who want plain terms and a say in how their money is used.
- **Tone:** calm, plain, specific. Short sentences, real numbers, no hype.

## Fixed numbers (home and every future page)
- Sample rates, as of 1 October 2026: Share savings 0.75% APY, Money market 2.10% APY, 6-month certificate 3.40% APY, 12-month certificate 3.90% APY, 24-month certificate 3.70% APY, New auto loan from 5.49% APR (up to 72 months), Used auto loan from 5.99% APR, 30-year fixed mortgage from 6.25% APR, 15-year fixed mortgage from 5.60% APR, Personal loan from 8.90% APR (12 to 60 months).
- Membership: one-time $5 fee, $25 minimum in share savings, approval within 2 business days, loan decisions within 1 business day.
- Calculator default: $24,000, 60 months, 5.49% APR, about $458 a month.
- Branches: Riverside Main, 120 Mill Street (Mon-Fri 9:00-17:00, Thu until 19:00, Sat 9:00-13:00); Market Street, 48 Market Street (Mon-Fri 9:00-17:00); Northgate, 7 Orchard Road (Mon-Fri 9:30-17:30, Sat 9:00-12:00). Phone (555) 014-1961.
- Directors: Helen Marsh (chair), Samuel Adeyemi (vice chair), Lucia Ferreira (treasurer), Daniel Kim (secretary), Grace Whitfield, Rafael Ortiz, Mei Tanaka-Brooks.
- Member stories: Okoye family (30-year fixed mortgage, member since 2014), Ana Ruiz (bakery owner, personal loan, 2019), Tomas Lee (used auto loan, 2021), Walter Brenner (certificates, 1978).

## Signature techniques
1. Floating white rounded header bar with two outlined pills and one solid pine pill (also the button system).
2. Ruled ledger head: 1 px rule, numbered capital label at the left, heading with one pine italic word.
3. Uniform 3:2 pickup row with captions under a rule.

## Colour and type
