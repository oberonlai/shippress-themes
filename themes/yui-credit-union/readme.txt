Yui Credit Union — a business theme for a member-owned credit union. A floating white rounded header bar with outlined pills and one solid pine pill, ruled ledger heads (a hairline, a numbered capital label and one pine italic word) above every section, and a uniform 3:2 pickup row with captions under a rule. A stone ground with white panels, ink text and one small pine accent (#5E7350). The home page opens on an inset branch photograph with the headline below, then continues with ownership, a sample-rate table, a loan calculator, what members use, a member story, the join steps, governance, branches and the journal. Merriweather for headings, Public Sans for reading. Works without any plugin. WCAG AA colours; no entrance animations.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress. See README.md for the design notes.

== Installation ==

1. Download yui-credit-union.zip from the ShipPress themes releases.
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Yui Credit Union.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates the home page and ten inner pages with all their text and images, six journal entries and the menu, and sets the front page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote.

The credit union, its people, branches, telephone number and rates are fictional; all rates are samples for demonstration.
