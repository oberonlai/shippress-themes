# Yanagi Hygiene — brand

## Name
Yanagi Hygiene (the name comes from the willow, yanagi, that bends and stays rooted).

## Concept
Yanagi Hygiene is a commercial pest-control firm that keeps buildings open through the year. Its home page is one long landing page with an anchor menu and one repeated call to action: "Request an inspection hour". Reference: Asante (a Japanese termite and pest-control company); we borrow the skyline-cut photograph foot, plus-pill hotspots on a drawn building, the pentagon request tab, the arc-edged wash bands and the light title lockups.

2011 by Hana Yanagi, formerly a hospital infection-control officer.

## History
Yanagi Hygiene was founded in 2011 by Hana Yanagi, formerly a hospital infection-control officer. Drawing on years of managing sterile boundaries and transmission pathways in healthcare settings, Hana recognised that commercial pest control suffered from superficial treatments and generic chemical applications. She established the company at 41 Wellington Street in Collingwood to bring clinical observation, systematic building inspection, and rigorous hygiene practices to commercial facilities across Melbourne.

From the beginning, the company focused on sites within 60 km of the Collingwood depot, working primarily with commercial kitchens, food production facilities, warehouses, and multi-storey buildings. Instead of speculative interventions or unprovable promises, Yanagi Hygiene structured its service around an initial sixty-minute inspection hour and a dependable four-visit annual treatment calendar aligned with the seasons in February, May, August, and November.

Over the past decade, the business has grown into a dedicated team of fourteen specialists, including field technicians, reporting leads, and an in-house entomologist. Every inspection produces immediate written findings on site, followed by comprehensive reports that are archived for the life of the plan plus three years. Today, Yanagi Hygiene continues to provide calm, methodical pest prevention that keeps commercial buildings compliant, safe, and open for trade.

## Audience
Facility managers, restaurant and café owners, hotel operations leads, warehouse and cold-store supervisors and office building managers who need an auditable pest-prevention routine.

## Voice
Our voice is calm, precise, and practical, speaking with the measured authority of experienced building practitioners rather than sales representatives. We avoid alarmist language and unprovable promises, focusing entirely on methodical physical inspection, transparent reporting, and consistent seasonal maintenance.

## A day at the depot
A routine day starts at 07:00 when the desk opens, with the first technicians already on site from 05:30. Daily work is inspection hours and seasonal visits booked around trading hours, a written findings sheet left on site each time, and a records review so every full report is emailed within two working days. The desk closes at 18:00 on weekdays and 12:00 on Saturday.

## How the inspection hour is spent
- 00–10 min: Walk the floor plan with the facilities manager to review where deliveries arrive, where food and waste sit, and what has been seen.
- 10–35 min: Look where nobody looks, inspecting ceiling voids, under kitchen lines, drains, risers, dock seals, and plant rooms.
- 35–50 min: Map the points by placing numbered monitors and stations directly onto your site floor plan.
- 50–60 min: Write it down on site, producing a one-page findings sheet signed by both parties with our recommended plan.
