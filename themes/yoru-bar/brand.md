# Yoru Bar - brand brief

Reservation-only cocktail bar in Ghent, Belgium. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Yoru Bar. "Yoru" is the Japanese word for night; the bar is otherwise European and modern.
- **Location:** the basement of Maanstraat 7, 9000 Ghent, Belgium (fictional), six minutes on foot from the Korenmarkt tram stop.
- **History:** founded in 2017 by Margit Vos in the cellar of a former print shop. One curved walnut counter, twelve stools, brass lamps, an ink-blue tiled back wall.
- **Audience:** adults who want a quiet, unhurried evening at one counter: couples, small groups of up to four, people who like to watch a drink being built. Guests are 18 and over.
- **Tone:** calm, exact, first person plural, no superlatives. Describe what happens at the counter. No awards, press, ratings, health claims or real brands (spirits are generic: "a Belgian genever", "a rye whiskey").
- **Day:** open Wed-Sun 19:00-01:00 (last order 00:30). Ice is cut every Tuesday; prep in the afternoon; three seatings at 19:00, 21:00 and 23:00; Mon-Tue closed except for private hire.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Opened 2017. 12 counter seats (1-8 facing the bartender, 9-12 window end). Three seatings 19:00-21:00, 21:00-23:00, 23:00-01:00. Max 4 guests per booking.
- Bookings open 14 days ahead at 12:00; EUR 10 deposit per guest, taken off the bill; free cancellation up to 24 hours before; seat held 15 minutes; confirmation by email within 2 hours.
- Process: ice cut every Tuesday from a 25 kg block into 5 cm cubes, measured by jigger, stirred 40 turns (about 30 seconds), strained into a coupe kept at -18 C, peel expressed and discarded.
- Price list (EUR): Night Martini 14, Counter Old Fashioned 15, Cask Negroni 15, Smoke & Pear 14, Milk Punch No. 3 16, Garden Tonic (zero-proof) 9, The Short List 42, Counter Board 12.
- Private Counter: Mon or Tue 19:00-23:00, up to 12 guests, minimum spend EUR 900.

## Signature techniques (from Karuizawa Whisky)
1. Framed full-bleed cover: two big foreground objects, a centred moon ring and wordmark that fade in, the photo slowly settling, vertical cue, bottom info bar.
2. Full-viewport 50/50 panel with a display word struck by a 1px hairline and a tan sub-head.
3. Circle photograph with an equal tan gradient disc offset below it; ghost-disc link with a long thin arrow.
Supporting: white wash section with a tile strip bleeding off both edges.
