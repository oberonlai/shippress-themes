# Kesho College — brand

- **Name:** Kesho College (fictional)
- **Location:** 14 Harbour Lane, Lindmoor (fictional)
- **Founded:** 2009 by Amara Lindqvist
- **What it is:** a hairdressing and beauty college for future salon juniors. Programmes: Hairdressing Diploma (12 months, full-time), Barbering Certificate (6 months, full-time), Makeup Artistry Certificate (4 months, part-time), Nail Technology Certificate (3 months, part-time), Evening Colour Course (10 weeks, 2 evenings), Taster Saturday (1 day).
- **Method:** demonstrate, practise on blocks, work on real clients. Cohorts of at most 14; a floor ratio of 1 instructor to 7 students.

## Audience
Applicants from age 16, no prior salon experience needed, who want to learn a craft by hand and join a salon team as dependable juniors. Career changers and school leavers alike.

## Tone
Calm, grounded and deliberate: the understated confidence of working craftspeople. Precise descriptions of floor practice, technical discipline and transparent schedules. No promotional hyperbole, no guaranteed outcomes.

## A day
Classes Mon–Fri 08:45–17:00. 08:45 floor opens & set-up · 09:00 demonstration · 10:15 block practice on mannequins · 12:30 lunch · 13:30 Student Salon with real clients · 16:15 critique & clean-down. Office Mon–Fri 09:00–17:30. Student Salon Tue–Sat 10:00–16:00. Open mornings: first Saturday of each month, 10:00.
