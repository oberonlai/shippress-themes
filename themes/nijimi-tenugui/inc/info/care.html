The dye

Pour-dyed by hand with indigo and fast cotton dyes. The colour is fixed, but it softens a little with every wash: that is the cloth growing old well.

First wash

Wash it alone, by hand, in cold water before the first use. Some colour comes out; rinse until the water runs nearly clear.

Every wash

Cold or lukewarm water and a little mild soap, no bleach. For the first months wash it with dark colours, never with whites.

Drying

Pull it straight while wet and hang it in the shade. It dries in about an hour; iron on low only if you like it crisp.

The cut ends

The short ends are cut, not hemmed, so the cloth dries quickly. A few threads fray after the first washes, then stop; trim them with scissors.
