How to measure

Run a soft tape round your head, a finger's width above the eyebrows and ears, where the band will sit. Read it snug, not tight.

Small

54–55 cm. Most cloches and the pillbox are blocked a touch closer, so they stay put without pins.

Medium

56–57 cm. Our most common size, and the one every shape is first blocked in.

Large

58–59 cm. Available in every shape; wide brims are balanced so the brim still sits level.

Between sizes

Choose the larger size: a fine sizing ribbon inside the band takes up the difference, and we adjust it free at the atelier.
