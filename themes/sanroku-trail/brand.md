## Tone
The tone is calm, unhurried, and grounded in the physical reality of mountain stewardship. We speak plainly about course distances, cut-offs, weather decisions, and the quiet discipline of leaving trails untouched.

## Story
Founded in 2014 in Hollin Village within the Upper Arden Valley, the Sanroku Trail Series began with a quiet commitment to low-impact mountain running. Rather than constructing permanent race infrastructure across fragile ground, the founders chose to stage seasonal footraces that borrow the high alpine ridges for a single day before returning the landscape to stillness.

Over subsequent seasons, the series grew into four distinct mountain courses that follow the natural rhythm of the year, from early spring snowmelt to the high autumn ridge. Every course is marked entirely by hand with fabric ribbons, and every marker is gathered back within twenty-four hours of the final runner finishing, guided by sixty local volunteers and community trail maintenance crews.

Today, operations remain rooted in Hollin Village Hall on Mill Lane, welcoming runners across distances from twenty-one to sixty kilometres. With thorough mandatory kit inspections, strict intermediate cut-offs, and an unhurried respect for changing mountain weather, the series preserves an enduring balance between personal endurance and collective environmental stewardship.

## Mandatory kit
Waterproof jacket with taped seams, 1 L water, foil blanket, charged phone with the race office number, whistle, reusable cup. Gorge 45 and Autumn Ridge 60 add a headlamp with spare batteries and a warm layer. Kit check: Friday before each race 15:00-19:00 and race morning from one hour before the start.
