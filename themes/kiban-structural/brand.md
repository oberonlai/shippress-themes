# Kiban Structural - brand brief

Structural engineering practice for architects and developers at Calder Quay. English sample content in stage 1; Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kiban Structural. "Kiban" is the Japanese word for foundation; the firm is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** 3F, 14 Harbour Row, Calder Quay (fictional). Prices in euros, VAT excluded.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 08:30–17:30, site visits by arrangement, closed weekends & public holidays.
- **Email and phone:** studio@kiban-structural.example, +00 20 7000 2009.
- **History:**

Kiban Structural was founded in 2009 by Mara Lindqvist after twelve years working inside contractor design teams. Having witnessed how misalignments between technical drawings and physical construction sites cause avoidable complications, Lindqvist established an independent engineering practice grounded in direct site engagement and methodical technical scrutiny. The practice took the name Kiban, the Japanese word for foundation, establishing the firm as the reliable ground beneath every structural proposal. Operating from Calder Quay, the studio was structured to provide calm, rigorous engineering design, independent checks, and practical site reviews.

From its inception, the studio developed an approach based on clarity, collaboration, and personal accountability. Rather than passing projects between detached departments, Kiban Structural assigns a single named engineer to lead each commission from initial feasibility through to final certification. The team works alongside architects and developers from the earliest project stages, drafting concise structural scheme notes that resolve load paths before formal modelling begins. This direct working relationship ensures that technical decisions remain aligned with spatial intentions and constructability.

Today, the seven-person practice operates from 14 Harbour Row, managing a structured six-stage workflow that spans surveys, load take-downs, computational modelling, independent checking, drawing production, and signed calculations. Over the past decade and a half, Kiban Structural has engineered civic landmarks, residential housing, bridges, and complex retrofits, including the Calder Quay Library, Northfield Row Housing, Weir Lane Footbridge, and Mill Street Warehouse Retrofit. Across all materials—whether reinforced concrete, structural steel, or mass timber—the firm upholds its core conviction that every calculation must be checked on the drawing and verified on the wall.

- **Tone:** Calm, specific, and understated, the voice communicates with the quiet authority of an experienced technical practice. It relies on precise engineering facts and transparent process rather than promotional claims or marketing superlatives.

## Process (six stages, identical everywhere)
- 01 Survey (1–2 weeks)
- 02 Load take-down (1 week)
- 03 Model (2–4 weeks)
- 04 Independent check (1–2 weeks)
- 05 Drawings (2–3 weeks)
- 06 Signed calculations (3 working days)

## Fees (from-prices, euros, VAT excluded)
- Feasibility & structural scheme note: from €1,800. Early stage appraisal outlining framing options, load paths, and structural viability before detailed design.
- Site survey & condition report: €950 per site day. Dimensional verification, structural defect logging, and condition assessment on existing buildings.
- Structural design, residential up to 4 storeys: from €9,500. Complete structural analysis, gravity framing, core design, foundation engineering, and production drawings.
- Independent design check: from €3,200. Rigorous third-party review of models, load take-downs, member sizing, and calculation packages.
- Retrofit assessment & strengthening design: from €4,800. Load capacity evaluation and structural steel strengthening design for historic and adaptive reuse structures.
- Site inspection visit with written report: €640 per visit. On-site reinforcement and connection verification with a formal written inspection report.

Payment stages: 30% on the scheme note, 50% on the calculations, 20% on sign-off. Fixed quotes follow the scheme note.
