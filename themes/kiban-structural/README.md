# Kiban Structural

A WordPress block theme for a structural engineering practice at Calder Quay (founded 2009). The practice is fictional; people, places and fees are written down in [`brand.md`](brand.md). Stage 1 ships the English home page; inner pages are placeholders.

## Concept: checked on the drawing, signed on the wall

- **Palette.** White ground, ink `#0D0D0D`, mid grey `#6A6A68`; a 6px green dot `#47C94A` only on the Contact pill. Photographs carry the cool concrete greys.
- **Type.** Albert Sans (headings, Black 900 wordmark) and Instrument Sans (reading), OFL-1.1, Latin WOFF2 bundled.
- **Signature.** Stadium photo window over a 250px cropped wordmark marquee; letter-spread tagline and a fixed 8-column hairline grid with ticks; a dark full-bleed band with rounded top corners and parallax; process beside sticky media.
- **Motion.** `sp-scroll` (parallax, reveal, stagger, count, marquee); respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, content visible without JavaScript.
