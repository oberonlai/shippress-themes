# Satei Adjusters

A quiet WordPress landing theme for an independent loss-adjusting desk that takes requests for insurer panel places (waitlist). Palette: grape-mouse mauve `#6B5E66` on a cool-light ground, with a mauve-grey `#AFA3A3` band. Type: Onest and Source Sans 3 (Noto Sans TC / JP for Chinese and Japanese).

## Home page patterns
`front-hero` (SATEI wordmark over a parallax photograph, statement bottom-right), `front-why` (hairline opener, numbered underlined points, chips), `front-calls` (strip of four photographs), `front-visit` (colour-flooded sticky 50/50 split with contents list and 48 h count), `front-adjusters` (ruled name list), `front-cases` (three case rows), `front-fees` (ruled fee table), `front-faq` (mauve-grey band), `front-panel` (single call to action).

## Motion
sp-scroll: `sp-parallax` on the hero photograph, `sp-reveal--wipe` (1.3s) on photographs, `sp-reveal--up` on text columns and rows, `sp-count` on the 48 h numeral.

## Pages and menu
The demo import creates Home, Waitlist (request a panel place), Thank you, Privacy and Terms, and an anchor menu on the home page (Why, Calls, A visit, Adjusters, Cases, Fees, FAQ). The footer links to Waitlist, Privacy and Terms.
