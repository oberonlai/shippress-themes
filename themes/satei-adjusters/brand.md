# Satei Adjusters - brand brief

Independent loss-adjusting desk in Bristol, taking requests for insurer panel places (waitlist landing page). Sample content in English first; zh-TW, ja, fr and de follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Satei Adjusters. "Satei" is the Japanese word for assessment; the desk is otherwise a plain UK practice.
- **Location:** Unit 3, 41 Canal Wharf, Bristol BS1 6XN, United Kingdom (fictional). GBP prices in every language.
- **History:** founded 2014 by chartered loss adjuster Helen Marsh.
- **Hours:** Phone lines Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00 (UK time). Site visits Mon–Sat 08:00–17:00.
- **Audience:** claims teams at insurers who appoint an adjuster after water or fire damage.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, precise, and understated, relying on specific operational facts rather than promotional claims. Sentences remain direct and factual, avoiding speculative promises, cost-saving claims, or industry jargon.

## Story
Satei Adjusters was founded in 2014 by chartered loss adjuster Helen Marsh at Unit 3, 41 Canal Wharf in Bristol. The desk was established as an independent loss-adjusting practice, completely free from ownership by insurance companies or building repair contractors. From the outset, the founding principle was straightforward: provide instructing insurers with calm, objective, and meticulous on-site damage investigations across South West England and South Wales, reachable within a ninety-minute drive of Bristol.

Over the following years, the practice expanded carefully to assemble a dedicated team of seven specialists covering water escape, drying verification, fire and smoke damage, building surveying, contents inventory, and report editing. Because our adjusters are appointed directly by insurers, policyholders never pay our desk. Every instruction receives immediate attention, with appointments confirmed the same working day, adjusters on site within forty-eight working hours, and clear, evidence-based reports delivered within five working days of the visit.

To protect the thoroughness of our fieldwork and preserve rapid response times, the desk limits its active working relationships to at most twelve insurers at any given time. We operate transparently on fixed fees agreed before attendance, without contingent incentives, percentage-based pricing, or repair-network ties. As panel places open, insurers join our waitlist in the strict order requests are received, ensuring every client receives our full, unhurried attention on site.

## How a visit runs (4 stages)
1. The call: Ruth confirms the appointment with the insurer the same working day.
2. The visit: An adjuster is on site within 48 working hours of the appointment.
3. The record: Photographs, moisture readings, a room-by-room damage sheet, and a contents list, before anything is stripped out.
4. The report: A written report to the insurer within 5 working days of the visit.
