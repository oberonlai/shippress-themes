# Renkei Forum - brand brief

Landing site for **Renkei Forum 2027**, a two-day regional procurement forum where buyers meet suppliers. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Renkei Forum ("renkei" is the Japanese word for cooperation; the forum is otherwise international and neutral). 2027 is the **6th edition**.
- **Organiser:** the Renkei Forum Association, an independent non-profit founded in **2019** that matches regional buyers with regional suppliers.
- **Dates:** Thursday 13 - Friday 14 May 2027. Day 1 doors 08:30, close 18:30 (supplier reception 17:30-18:30). Day 2 doors 08:30, close 16:30.
- **Venue:** Calder Hall, 12 Wharf Road, Northport (fictional, a converted wharf hall, 8 minutes on foot from Northport Central). Hall A = talks (420 seats), Hall B = 60 meeting tables, Foyer = 40 supplier tables.
- **Numbers:** 12 sessions, 8 speakers, 4 meeting rounds (two a day, 20-minute tables), places for 160 buyers and 220 suppliers. Ticket requests close 16 April 2027; meeting matching opens 19 April 2027; every request is answered within 5 working days.
- **Office:** Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00, hello@renkeiforum.example. Currency EUR in every language.
- **Story:** Most regional trade falters on the difficulty of making first contact. The association was founded in 2019 to bring commercial procurement back into direct regional relationships: one hall, two days, pre-booked tables, reviewed profiles. Over six editions it grew from a modest regional meeting into a structured two-day exchange across packaging, textiles, food and drink, logistics, components and facilities.
- **Audience:** purchasing managers, category leads and public procurement officers; owners of small regional workshops, producers and logistics firms.
- **Tone:** calm, precise, understated. Operational facts and realistic timelines; no marketing hyperbole, no awards, press, ratings or guaranteed outcomes beyond the facts below.

## Stories from 2026 (no figures beyond these)
Case 01: a bakery cooperative found a flour mill 60 km away and moved to monthly deliveries. Case 02: the city kitchen split one catering tender into 4 lots so two local growers could bid. Case 03: a contract textiles workshop and a hospital laundry agreed a 2-year uniform trial.

## Flow (4 steps)
Request a ticket -> Profile review (up to 5 working days) -> Meeting matching (opens 19 April 2027) -> Forum days 13-14 May 2027. The one repeated button everywhere is "Request a ticket".
