# Hada Dermatology — brand brief

Design plan row: b6-15 (community dermatology clinic, type health). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other languages follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional. Medical copy never promises a cure or an outcome.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hada Dermatology, a community skin clinic. "Hada" is the Japanese word for skin; the founder used it as a quiet reminder to look at the skin itself, not only the label on the problem.
- **Location:** 418 Willow Street, Madison, Wisconsin 53703; phone (608) 555-0142. A low brick-and-glass building on a tree-lined street.
- **Founded:** 2011 by Dr. Naomi Reyes with two exam rooms; four doctors today.
- **Services:** general (medical) dermatology, children's skin, skin-cancer screening (full-body skin checks, biopsies, minor skin surgery), acne and scarring, cosmetic dermatology (consultation, light peels).
- **Audience:** families and working adults in the neighbourhood who have a rash, a changing mole or a long-running skin condition.
- **Tone:** calm, plain, specific. Times, steps and names before adjectives. Never "best", "guaranteed", "cure" or before-and-after claims.

## Fees (self-pay; insured medical visits billed to the plan)
New patient consultation (30 min) $185; Follow-up visit (15 min) $110; Full-body skin check $160; Child consultation (under 16) $150; Skin biopsy, incl. lab $195; Mole or lesion removal (minor surgery, single) from $240; Patch testing series (3 visits) $340; Acne plan review $120; Cosmetic consultation (credited to treatment) $60; Light chemical peel from $150.
Insurance: most major plans accepted for medical visits, no referral needed; cosmetic treatments are self-pay; coverage is confirmed before the first visit.
