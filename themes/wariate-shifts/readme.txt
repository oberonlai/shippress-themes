Wariate Shifts — a landing-page theme for shop rota software. White ground, grape-mouse folder tabs with numbered steps, four shop photographs edge to edge under one shallow arch, slash-flanked captions over one pill call to action, reason cards with hanging numerals, a sixty-minute time bar and a drifting row of shop kinds. Inter Tight and Albert Sans. Demo: Wariate Shifts is rota software for independent shops with 1 to 6 locations and 5 to 60 staff. Lay out the week, collect availability, swap shifts, cover a sick day, and export hours for payroll. Book a free 60-minute demo; the first month on any plan is free, and nothing is charged automatically.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download wariate-shifts.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/wariate-shifts.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Wariate Shifts.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, three news entries with two categories and four tags, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. The sample content comes in English (demo-content.json). The importer uses the `shippress_demo_lang` option, else the site language.

Scroll effects: assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js (a small script loaded in the head) add the drifting row of shop kinds, a slow parallax on the photographs and a soft entrance for the cards. They are switched off for visitors who prefer reduced motion, and the content is fully visible without JavaScript.

The company, its people, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
