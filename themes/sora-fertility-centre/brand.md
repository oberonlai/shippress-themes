# Sora Fertility Centre — brand

- **Name**: Sora Fertility Centre (wordmark "Sora"). Fertility clinic, founded 2011 by Dr. Helena Marsh. 14 Harbour Lane, Lindenport (fictional), second floor, lift access. Currency EUR in every language.
- **Tone**: calm, plain, answers first. No outcome promises, no success rates, no awards, no real media, people or brands.
- **Hours**: Mon–Fri 7:30–18:00, Sat 8:00–12:00 (scans and collections), Sun closed; phone line Mon–Fri 8:00–17:00; on-call nurse for patients in a cycle.
- **Fees (EUR)**: first consultation, both partners, 60 min €180 · fertility assessment €420 · IUI cycle €950 · IVF cycle €4,800 · ICSI add-on €1,100 · frozen embryo transfer €1,450 · egg freezing cycle €3,900 · storage per year €350. Medication usually €1,200–2,500 per IVF cycle, billed by the pharmacy. Payment in two parts.
- **People**: Dr. Helena Marsh (Medical Director), Dr. Tomas Varga, Dr. Amara Osei, Lina Bergström (lead embryologist), Rafael Duarte (andrology lab), Nadia Haddad (nurse coordinator), Grace Lin (fertility counsellor).
- **Stories**: Mei & Jonas (IVF, second cycle); Priya (egg freezing at 36); Claire & Sam (tests, then IUI).
- **First 90 days**: first consultation (60 min) → assessment (cycle day 2–5, results in 5 working days) → plan review (30 min, included) → treatment or watchful waiting.
- **IVF timings**: stimulation 10–12 days; 3–4 monitoring scans; egg collection about 20 min under light sedation; 5 days in the lab; transfer about 15 min; pregnancy test 12 days later.
- **Pages (stage 2)**: Treatments, IVF step by step, Fertility tests, Fees & funding, FAQ (28 questions), About, Care team, Patient stories, First consultation & contact, Privacy.

- **Audience**: couples, single parents by choice and same-sex couples who are thinking about or starting fertility care; most arrive with questions rather than a plan. Location: Lindenport harbour district, where the clinic is based.
