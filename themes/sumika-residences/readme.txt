Sumika Residences is a serviced-apartment house in Calder Harbour, founded in 2014 by Inés Aldana. Three houses — Ashgrove House, Canal Row, and Linden Court — hold fifty-four apartments for stays of thirty nights or more. Monthly rent includes bills, Wi-Fi, and weekly linen, with the front desk open from 08:00 to 20:00 and a night line through the night.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download sumika-residences.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/sumika-residences.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Sumika Residences.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates the home page and eleven inner pages (Houses, A day, Rates, How a stay is arranged, For companies, Neighbourhoods, Stories, People, FAQ, About, Enquire), the Journal with one article, all with their text and images, the menu, and sets the front page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The residence, its houses, people, prices and telephone number are fictional.
