# Sumika Residences — brand

## Story

The name is Sumika Residences. It was founded in 2014 by Inés Aldana in Calder Harbour. The audience is people stationed in a city for months: a project team, a visiting musician, or a family that needs a kitchen of its own. It is a quiet house inside the city for people stationed somewhere for months: a project, a season with an orchestra, a posting, a family that needs a kitchen of its own. The stay is an apartment, not a hotel night. You settle in, and you belong to the street you walk every day.

The three houses were already part of the city. Ashgrove House, at 12 Ashgrove Lane, is an 1898 brick building of five floors and fourteen apartments. Canal Row, at 40 Canal Row, is a 1931 warehouse of six floors and twenty-two apartments. Linden Court, at 7 Linden Place, is a 1962 courtyard block of four floors and eighteen apartments, with birch trees and benches in the court. Together they hold fifty-four apartments, from a studio of 28 m² to a two-bedroom of 64 m².

Rent includes bills, Wi-Fi, and a weekly linen change and clean. A stay is at least thirty nights, with thirty days' notice and a deposit of one month's rent. The front desk is open from 08:00 to 20:00 every day, and the night line answers around the clock. Tomasz Wierzbicki, Amara Okafor, and Lena Hartmann manage the three houses. Residents walk to the tram, the market, and the park, then come back to a desk, a kitchen, and a street they know.

## Voice

The voice is calm and concrete, the way a house manager speaks at the desk to someone who will still be here next month. It names the room, the walk, and the rent, and it leaves slogans out.

## Services and rates

- **Studio** — €2,150 / month. 28 m². Bills, Wi-Fi, and weekly linen and clean included.
- **Desk studio** — €2,460 / month. 34 m². Bills, Wi-Fi, and weekly linen and clean included.
- **One-bedroom** — €2,780 / month. 42 m². Bills, Wi-Fi, and weekly linen and clean included.
- **Two-bedroom** — €3,640 / month. 64 m². Bills, Wi-Fi, and weekly linen and clean included.
- **Extra mid-week clean** — €45 per visit. On top of the weekly linen and clean included in the rent.
- **Parking (Canal Row only)** — €160 / month. Offered only at Canal Row, billed by the month.
