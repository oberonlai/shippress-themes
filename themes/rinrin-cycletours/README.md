# Rinrin Cycle Tours

Theme for a guided coast cycling operator. Home: full-bleed quay hero with a tall vertical headline, About on a blurred photograph with two edge-flush photos, a sideways scroll-snap strip of five tour spec cards, a framed "riding day" band, two stacked photograph strips, a grade ledger, one way to book and a journal. Fonts: Sora and Manrope. Brand and design notes in `brand.md` and `brand/`.

## Status
Stage 1 delivers the English home page. Inner pages (stubs for now), the journal and the zh-TW, ja, fr and de localisations follow in stage 2.
