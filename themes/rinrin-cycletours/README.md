# Rinrin Cycle Tours

Theme for a guided coast cycling operator. Home: full-bleed quay hero with a tall vertical headline, About on a blurred photograph with two edge-flush photos, a sideways scroll-snap strip of five tour spec cards, a framed "A riding day" band ("From quay to quay"), two stacked photograph strips, a grade ledger, one way to book and a journal. Fonts: Sora and Manrope. Brand and design notes in `brand.md` and `brand/`.

## Status
Stage 1 delivered the English home page; the stage 2 fix pass regenerated the hero photograph (pale sky across the top quarter, muted teal-green cast), added readability gradients, set the ghost numerals to 20% and made the footer Subscribe an outline pill. Inner pages, the journal and the zh-TW, ja, fr and de localisations follow in stage 2.
