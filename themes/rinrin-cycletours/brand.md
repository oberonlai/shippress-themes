## Story
Rinrin Cycle Tours was founded in 2014 by lead guide Ines Marlow at 4 Quay Street in the coastal harbour town of Saltmere. Beginning with modest local rides along the quay, the company was established around the principle that quiet coast roads are best experienced in small groups with full logistical backup and honest riding paces.

Between April and October each season, our departures explore the diverse shoreline, ranging from flat tidal causeways and working fishing harbours to exposed headland lighthouses and rugged cliff roads. Every departure is strictly limited to eight guests and accompanied by two dedicated guides and a support van driver, maintaining a personal and attentive atmosphere on the road.

From our Saltmere base, we take care of every mechanical and logistical detail, transferring all guest luggage directly to the next lodging by mid-afternoon. This seamless daily routine allows riders to travel unencumbered, taking in the coastal scenery, local cafés, and sea air with complete peace of mind from start to finish.
