# Design spec - Rinrin Cycle Tours (rinrin-cycletours)

Binding for the stage-1 build (home, en) and stage 2 (inner pages, five languages). Written by the design lead (opus). Where this file and `stage-a.md` disagree, this file wins. Numbers marked `[token …]` come from `brand/reference-tokens.md` (section letter in brackets).

- Row: design-plan b35-6. Type: business (WooCommerce compatible, not required). Archetype: horizontal-strip. First screen: full-bleed photo hero with overlay nav; the horizontal scroll strip of tour spec cards is the block directly under About. Page structure: spec-catalogue.
- Reference (primary): https://matsukawaura-guide.com/ - local guide collective of a lagoon harbour town.
- Secondary: https://mikurasima-portal.jp/ - island portal (structure of stacked photo strips only; never its sky-blue saturation).
- Imagery: **photo** (from stage-a.md IMAGERY). Documentary real photography, generated by us, one STYLE string (section 11). No illustration, no cut-outs (0).
- Font direction: **sans** - Latin Sora (display) + Manrope (body); CJK Noto Sans TC / Noto Sans JP (黑體 direction, never 明體).
- Similarity target: about 80% with the primary reference in colour, photo treatment, scroll motion, decoration and layout. Photos and copy are ours; no reference asset, text or logo is copied.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen - concept, story, signature motion
- **Draws:** a high-angle photograph of a lagoon harbour: a dense row of white fishing boats moored diagonally along a concrete quay from lower left to right, grey-teal water, a green far shore, a low cloudy pale sky across the top. Tall vertical headline lower right over the quay; a small catch over the water at left; link groups across the sky. Logo top-left.
- **Story:** "We are the local hosts - let us show you our shore." Local people who work the harbour guide visitors to what only locals know.
- **Signature motion:** smooth scrubbed parallax on the hero photo (image scaled 1.2, moving on Y from "top 80%" to "bottom top", scrub 2 s); rows fade in as they enter; the framed band photo scales while its centred card stays; hover 0.5 s cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1).

### (2) Our translation - guided coast cycling (same composition, same motion idea, same image type: documentary photo)
- **Scene:** a high-angle documentary photograph of a small harbour at 8 am: eight tour bikes racked in a neat diagonal row along the harbour wall from lower left to right (the row of boats becomes the row of bikes), the white support van parked at the end of the row with its rear door open and bags inside, a guide in a dark green jacket crouched at the near end adjusting a guest's saddle height, grey-teal water to the left, a green headland in the distance, pale overcast sky in the top quarter.
- **Story:** "We ride you along the coast; your bags are already at the next harbour." Locals who ride these roads every week lead eight guests at a time.
- **Motion:** hero photo moves with `sp-parallax` (scroll-linked, CSS scroll-driven) - our version of the scrub-2 parallax; framed band photo zooms on enter (`sp-reveal--zoom`); rows rise on enter (`sp-reveal--up` + `sp-stagger`); hovers 0.5 s cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1). No smooth-scroll library.

## 6. Type (sans direction; tokens [b])
- **Latin:** Sora (display: 300, 600) + Manrope (body: 400, 600). Self-hosted woff2 in `assets/fonts/`, loaded via `theme.json` `settings.typography.fontFamilies[].fontFace` (editor and front end identical).
- **CJK (黑體):** Noto Sans TC (zh-TW) and Noto Sans JP (ja) subset woff2 (display weights 400/500), also as `fontFace` in theme.json. Use the Hokuto/Hada subset workaround (the zh subset script is broken).
- Stacks:
  - `--display` en/fr/de: `Sora, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`
  - `--body` en/fr/de: `Manrope, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`
  - zh-TW display: `Sora, "Noto Sans TC", "PingFang TC", "Microsoft JhengHei", sans-serif`; zh-TW body (no bundled zh, no ja fonts): `Manrope, "PingFang TC", "Heiti TC", "Microsoft JhengHei", sans-serif`

  - `:lang()` rules exist → copy `shared/inc/editor-lang.php` to `inc/editor-lang.php` and require it.

## 8. Responsive rules
- **390px first screen:** the vertical headline becomes horizontal (`writing-mode: horizontal-tb`), Sora 300 2.75rem / 1.15, bottom-left at left 24px, bottom 96px, max-width 90vw; catch 0.9375rem above it; the menu is the outline "Menu" pill top-right (link groups hidden). **No vertical text and no absolutely positioned labels that overlap text on the mobile first screen.** The headline and catch are normal-flow blocks inside the Cover's inner container (Cover content position bottom-left), not absolutely positioned.
- 768-1023px: headline horizontal, 3.5rem; link groups collapse into the pill.
- About on mobile: photos stack (hands full-bleed 4:5, then text, then guests 340px flush right); stepped indent becomes 0 / 24px / 0.
- Departures strip on mobile: cards 78vw, swipe; no reveal on strip children.
- Strips on mobile: 70svh, caption horizontal 1.75rem centred, link stack under the caption (static flow).
- **Price table (Departures) at ≥ 782px: full container width (100%), `table-layout:auto`, price column `white-space:nowrap; text-align:right`, never clipped (no `overflow:hidden` on its wrapper).** Grade table, sizes table and dates table same rules.
- **Every table on ≤ 781px becomes stacked:** each row a block, each cell `display:grid; grid-template-columns: 9rem 1fr` with the header text from `data-label` in markup (written in the block HTML, not CSS content).
- Overflow: no horizontal overflow at 390 or 1280 (the strip scrolls inside its own container with `overscroll-behavior-x: contain`).
