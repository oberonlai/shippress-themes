# Futari Journeys

A calm, bespoke travel-planner block theme for couples: an inset documentary photo that dissolves into white over three question doors, a square and wide photo pair across a pale band, and itinerary cards with an offset caption sheet.

## Design notes
- Palette: white, a moss-tinted pale band (#F2F4EF), ink (#222222), bamboo moss (#769164) for small marks and deep moss (#3F5236) for the single booking button.
- Type: Manrope headings, Public Sans body (bundled WOFF2, loaded through theme.json).
- Motion (sp-scroll): fade-up, shutter wipe on photos, one slow parallax band; none in the hero, forms or tables.
- Forms: the Route Letter slot works without plugins (mailto with a clear notice) and uses MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when present.

## Patterns
`front-hero`, `front-pair`, `front-budget`, `front-timing`, `front-band`, `front-documents`, `front-how`, `front-planners`, `front-book`, `front-letter`, plus `header-main` and `footer-main`.
