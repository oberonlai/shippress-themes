# Futari Journeys - brand brief

A small bespoke travel studio that plans honeymoons and anniversary trips for couples, one trip at a time. One action everywhere: "Book a planning call". Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Futari Journeys (wordmark "futari journeys"; *futari* means "the two of us"). The site answers the questions couples ask first: what will it cost, when should we go, which documents do we need.
- **Location:** 2nd floor, 8 Linden Row, Valmere (fictional harbour town). Phone +00 0000 0000. Email hello@futari-journeys.example (every mailto).
- **Founded:** 2014 by Aiko Brandt, after planning her sister's honeymoon around a kitchen table. The studio opened on Linden Row in 2017, the travel-day line began in 2020, the 300th trip was planned in 2024. Today seven people, about forty trips a year.
- **People (seven):** Aiko Brandt (founder and lead planner), Mateus Okafor (planner, islands and coasts), Lina Haddad (planner, mountains and rail), Priya Nair (documents and entry requirements), Jonas Weber (bookings and suppliers), Camille Laurent (the travel-day line), Ren Ishida (routes and maps).
- **Audience:** couples planning a honeymoon, an anniversary or a surprise trip, and sometimes the friends and family who give a trip as a gift.
- **Tone:** calm, specific and kind; answers before selling. No "best", no awards, no guarantees about weather, prices or visas, no real brands, hotels or people.

## A day at the studio (the daily routine)
08:30 the travel-day line hands over from night to day - 09:00 first planning calls - 11:00 route reviews at the long table - 13:00 lunch - 14:00 supplier calls and bookings - 16:00 documents check with Priya - 18:00 the studio closes - 19:00 last planning call. The daily routine is the same on every trip: one planner stays with each couple from the first call to the flight home.

## Hours (identical everywhere)
- Studio: Tuesday to Saturday 10:00-18:00, visits by appointment.
- Planning calls by video: Monday to Saturday 09:00-19:00 CET, 45 minutes, free.
- Travel-day line: answers 24 hours a day while a couple is travelling.

## Prices (EUR, planning fee per couple; travel is paid separately to hotels, airlines and guides)
1 Planning call (45 minutes by video) free - 2 Anniversary weekend (2-4 nights, one place) 290 - 3 Short escape (5-7 nights, up to 2 stops) 450 - 4 Honeymoon (8-16 nights, up to 3 stops) 780 - 5 Long journey (17-30 nights, two or more countries) 1,150 - 6 Surprise planning (add-on; one partner plans, we keep the secret) +120.
Most honeymoons we plan cost 6,000-14,000 for two, flights included (a range we see, not a promise). The fee is paid 50% after the first route, 50% when bookings are confirmed. No commission is taken from hotels. Cancelling the planning fee is free until the first route is sent.

## How a trip is built (five steps)
1 the planning call (45 min, free) - 2 a first route and a fee within 7 days - 3 two rounds of changes - 4 bookings confirmed, deposits paid to suppliers - 5 the travel kit 14 days before departure (route map, day sheets, bookings folder) and the travel-day line during the trip. Start 4-9 months ahead for a honeymoon; 6 weeks is enough for an anniversary weekend.

## The Route Letter
Four letters a year (March, June, September, December): where couples went that season and which months open next. Subscribe on /letter/ and on the home page.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Three questions on a white dissolve: the inset hero photo fades into white and three question links, divided by 1px vertical hairlines with a small circle arrow each, are the three doors of the site.
2. A square and a wide photograph with tops aligned and a pale band that begins 120px below their tops, then a 55/45 text split.
3. Itinerary cards: two-up photographs with an offset white caption sheet over the lower edge and a small ink place chip on the photo's right edge.
Supporting: eyebrow with a 76px rule, dash-flanked labels, a 1px line dropping into the text, fade-up and shutter-wipe reveals, one slow parallax band.

## Photography
Real documentary colour photographs of couples travelling (generated for this theme): natural light, muted greens, shallow depth of field, foreground leaf blur; no text, no logos, no cut-outs.
