# Kazu Data Course - brand brief

Solo-instructor online course in practical data analysis. Lessons are online; the studio in Rotterdam is where they are recorded. Sample content in English; zh-TW, ja, fr, de follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kazu Data Course. Founded **2019**. Studio: Kadekamer 14, 3011 AB Rotterdam, Netherlands.
- **Instructor:** Kazu Moreno (she/her), 11 years as a working analyst in logistics and public-health reporting before teaching. No awards, media or employer names.
- **Tone:** plain, warm, exact; "you" voice; no promises of jobs, salaries or results.

## The course
Practical Data Analysis: 6 modules, 48 lessons, 14 hours of video, 5 reviewed projects, access 24 months, captions in five languages. Modules: 1 Asking a good question (8 lessons) · 2 Cleaning messy data (8) · 3 SQL for analysts (9) · 4 Python with pandas (10) · 5 Charts people actually read (7) · 6 Final project: a report for a real decision (6). Free preview: lesson 1.3 "Where good questions come from", 11 min. Refund within 14 days if less than a quarter of the course has been watched.
