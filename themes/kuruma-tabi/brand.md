## Story
Founded in 2014 by Mika Sato-Lund and Daniel Ferreira, Kuruma Tabi began at 12 Quay Road in Alderwick Harbour. Having guided travelers throughout the region, Mika and Daniel recognized that international visitors longed for unhurried journeys shaped around their own rhythm rather than rigid tour bus timetables. They started with a single comfortable vehicle, offering private chauffeured sightseeing tours dedicated to one family at a time across the quiet roads of the Alder Hills and Lake Morrow.

From the beginning, the company established an uncompromising standard of hospitality where the day is drawn entirely around the guests. A dedicated, multilingual driver-guide handles every logistical demand—including navigation, parking, highway tolls, and fuel—giving families the freedom to explore at ease. Whether taking extra time for photos at an overlook or pausing for an unexpected lunch stop, guests travel without the pressure of fixed group schedules.

Today, Kuruma Tabi continues to operate from Alderwick Harbour, remaining true to its founding promise of personal, unhurried travel. Daniel coordinates the experienced driving team while Mika personally crafts customized routes within twenty-four hours of every inquiry. Every journey remains a private, tranquil experience designed to give each family a seamless and memorable encounter with the landscape.

## A day (the daily routine, in five stops)
A typical full day runs as a calm routine, drawn around the guests: 08:30 hotel pickup, 10:15 ridge viewpoint, 12:30 harbour lunch, 15:00 lakeside walk, 18:00 drop-off. Every day is redrawn for the family that books it; the stops can move, shrink or stretch with the weather, the children and the length of lunch.
