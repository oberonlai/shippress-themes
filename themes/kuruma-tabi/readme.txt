Kuruma Tabi is a refined WordPress theme designed for private chauffeured sightseeing tours and bespoke travel services. Built around documentary photography and flexible itineraries, it provides dedicated templates for daily timelines, fleet specifications, multilingual guides, and transparent fares. Ideal for family-focused transfer and private driving services seeking an unhurried, trustworthy online presence.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The home page opens on a split hero: a documentary photograph of a driver-guide holding a van door open at a mountain viewpoint in the right half, the title beside it, and a white tab with an evergreen day-timeline bar flush along the bottom. It continues with a welcome, one horizontal line for the day with five stops, three mirrored route rows with line drawings crossing the seam, a fare table on a pale evergreen band, a lead guide portrait with a docked caption tab beside a thumbnail list, three circular vehicle photographs, guest stories under a parallax photograph, questions and one itinerary request. See README.md for the design notes.

== Installation ==

1. Download kuruma-tabi.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kuruma-tabi.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kuruma Tabi.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, a journal entry with a category and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The scroll effects (assets/sp-scroll.css and sp-scroll.js) respect prefers-reduced-motion and show the final state without JavaScript. The newsletter and contact slots use Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when active; without a plugin they fall back to an email link (the newsletter form opens your email app and says so; it does not subscribe anyone by itself).

The company, its people, address, email and fares are fictional.
