# Ayumi Podiatry - brand brief

Podiatry (foot) clinic for people who are on their feet all day. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Ayumi Podiatry. "Ayumi" is a Japanese word for a step or a way of walking; the clinic is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** 1F, 22 Mercer Yard, Eastgate (fictional). **Phone** +00 20 5180 3360, **email** hello@ayumipodiatry.example. USD prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 07:30–19:30, Sat 08:00–14:00; closed Sunday and public holidays. Early and late hours exist so people can come before or after a shift.
- **Founded:** 2015.
- **Audience:** nurses, cooks, carers, drivers, shop and warehouse workers, hairdressers and anyone else whose work is done standing.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, clear, and clinical, addressing working people with quiet respect and practical specificity rather than medical jargon. It avoids exaggerated promises or claims of permanent cure, focusing plainly on daily work demands, footwear realities, and measurable mechanical relief.

## History
Ayumi Podiatry was founded in 2015 by podiatrists Lena Marsh and Tomás Ribeiro at 1F, 22 Mercer Yard in Eastgate. Having treated countless workers who spent their shifts on unyielding concrete, hospital vinyl, and retail floors, Lena and Tomás recognised that conventional clinic schedules and generic shoe advice failed the people who needed foot care most. They established the clinic with early morning and late evening opening hours, running from 07:30 to 19:30 on weekdays and 08:00 to 14:00 on Saturdays, so patients could attend appointments before or immediately after their shifts.

From the beginning, the clinic was structured around a simple principle: to understand why a person hurts, you must examine how they move in the footwear they actually wear for work. Rather than relying solely on static examination tables, the practice installed a dedicated ten-metre walkway in Room 2, allowing clinicians to watch each patient walk both barefoot and in their everyday work boots, clogs, or trainers. This direct mechanical observation formed the foundation of the clinic's diagnostic method.

Over the years, Lena and Tomás expanded the Eastgate practice into a comprehensive foot clinic, bringing in orthotics technician Priya Raman to run an on-site insole workshop, foot-care nurse Grace Mensah to handle skin and nail health, Jonas Becker at reception, and practice manager Ana Kovač. Ayumi Podiatry continues to operate under the same ethos it began with in 2015: listen carefully to the patient's routine, explain findings in plain language, and recommend fewer, more targeted visits rather than prolonged treatment cycles.

## Services and fees (USD, identical on every page and in every language)
- **First visit & gait assessment** ($120): 60 min. Discussion of shifts and shoes, 10-metre walkway assessment barefoot and in work shoes, and an initial treatment or written plan.
- **Follow-up treatment** ($70): 30 min. Targeted hands-on care and progression of your treatment plan for ongoing foot, heel, or arch discomfort.
- **Nail & skin care** ($60): 30 min. Routine treatment for ingrown toenails, calluses, corns, and cracked heels using sterile clinic instruments.
- **Diabetic foot check** ($55): 30 min. Sensation, circulation, and vascular foot check with documentation for your health records.
- **Custom orthotics, one pair (scan, making, fitting)** ($380): 2 visits. Full foot scan, fabrication in our on-site workshop within 7 working days, and a dedicated 20-minute fitting.
- **Orthotic review & adjustment** ($40): 20 min. Scheduled review at week 6 to evaluate fit and wear patterns, with workshop adjustments included.

Card or cash. Itemised receipts for your insurer. Please give 24 hours' notice to cancel.

## First visit (prose rows, never a step grid)
1. Book: Reserve your appointment online or by phone at a time that suits your work schedule.
2. Talk (15 min): A seated conversation about your shifts, your workplace flooring, your work shoes, and where it hurts.
3. Walk (20 min): Walking along our 10-metre indoor clinic walkway barefoot and in your work shoes while we observe your gait.
4. Treat or plan (20 min): Receive your initial hands-on treatment or a clear written management plan tailored to your daily work demands.
5. Follow up: Your complete treatment plan is emailed within 2 working days, with a follow-up review scheduled in 4–6 weeks.

## People (six, all fictional)
- **Lena Marsh**, Podiatrist, co-founder. Lena co-founded the clinic in 2015 and focuses on heel and arch pain, nail conditions, and diabetic foot health. She leads initial gait assessments and works with patients to relieve chronic strain from standing shifts.
- **Tomás Ribeiro**, Podiatrist, co-founder. Tomás specialises in gait analysis, sport and work-boot strain, and prescribing custom orthotics. He works closely with workers across various trades to correct gait alignment and alleviate load.
- **Priya Raman**, Orthotics technician. Priya manages the clinic's on-site insole workshop, crafting each pair of custom orthotics to exact clinician prescriptions within seven working days.
- **Grace Mensah**, Foot-care nurse. Grace provides dedicated nail and skin care, managing calluses, corns, and regular diabetic foot health checks with gentle clinical precision.
- **Jonas Becker**, Reception and bookings. Jonas coordinates appointments and answers questions at the front desk, helping patients schedule visits conveniently around their work shifts.
- **Ana Kovač**, Practice manager. Ana oversees clinic administration, handles payment queries, and prepares detailed itemised receipts for private health insurance claims.
