## Concept

Hikari Lighting was founded in 2016 by Ines Varga as an independent lighting showroom at Steenkade 14 in Rotterdam. Frustrated by the glare of mass-produced fixtures and impersonal retail catalogues, Ines established a quiet waterfront space dedicated exclusively to lighting homes and small dining rooms. From the beginning, our guiding principle has been simple: every room deserves its own hour, and light should be treated as the quietest furniture in the space.

Hikari Lighting communicates in a calm, restrained, and specific voice that avoids marketing superlatives, hollow claims, or decorative adjectives. The language focuses purely on architectural space, material facts, warm 2700K light, and the practical craft of lighting one room at a time.

## Story

Hikari Lighting was founded in 2016 by Ines Varga as an independent lighting showroom at Steenkade 14 in Rotterdam. Frustrated by the glare of mass-produced fixtures and impersonal retail catalogues, Ines established a quiet waterfront space dedicated exclusively to lighting homes and small dining rooms. From the beginning, our guiding principle has been simple: every room deserves its own hour, and light should be treated as the quietest furniture in the space.

Rather than filling warehouse shelves, we collaborate directly with independent European workshops known for honest materials and enduring craftsmanship. Our collection brings together spun aluminium from Norrland Spun Metal, stoneware from Casa Trevi Ceramica, hand-turned timber from Oakhurst Turnery, pleated linen and paper shades from Lindgren Paper Shades and Lucía Ferraz, and custom metalwork from Atelier Vermeul and Studio Pellan. On the first Wednesday of every month, we re-set the showroom floor with exactly six lamps, allowing visitors to experience each piece in unhurried stillness.

Over the past decade, our practice has expanded from our Steenkade 14 showroom into a complete residential service. Lighting planner Tomasz Rehn devises written room plans and conducts home consultations, while Sam Okoro maintains our workshop bench, rewiring and restoring vintage lamps for ongoing life. Together with partner electrician Joris van Dalen, who handles installations across the Rotterdam area, we ensure that every lamp fits its room seamlessly, accompanied by our signature seven-day home trial and calibrated 2700K warm white bulbs.
