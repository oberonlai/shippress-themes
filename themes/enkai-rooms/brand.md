# Enkai Rooms - brand brief

A restaurant on the harbour of Calder that keeps three private rooms for company dinners, family celebrations and wedding parties. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Enkai Rooms. "Enkai" is a banquet; the house is otherwise a plain, international, modern restaurant with candles and oak.
- **Location:** 14 Harbour Steps, Calder (fictional). Step-free ground floor; public parking about 100 metres along the harbour.
- **Founded:** 2011, by Marguerite Okafor, in a former ropemaker's hall.
- **People (seven):** Marguerite Okafor (owner), Teodor Brandt (head chef), Lucía Navarro (floor manager, sets every name card herself), Aiyana Reyes (events coordinator, answers every "Ask about a date"), Samir Haddad (sommelier), Ingrid Solberg (pastry chef), Kwame Mensah (maître d' for wedding parties and the Long Room).
- **Audience:** people who book an evening for others: office managers, families marking a birthday, couples planning a wedding party; parties from 6 to 60.
- **Tone:** calm, welcoming and precise, as an experienced dining house speaks. Unadorned sentences, specific numbers, no boasting, no awards, no superlatives.

## An evening in one room (the daily routine)
Morning deliveries - 15:00 briefing in front of the bar - 17:30 name cards and tables set - 18:00 doors open for dinner - the first course arrives at the pace of the conversation - 23:00 the room closes (a late room is available by the hour). Private lunches run Saturday and Sunday from 12:00. The routine is the same shape most weeks.

## Hours (identical everywhere)
Dinner Tue-Sat 18:00-23:00 - Private lunches Sat-Sun 12:00-15:30 - Closed Monday. Events office Tue-Sat 10:00-18:00. Every enquiry is answered within one working day. Phone +00 31 555 0186. Email rooms@enkai-rooms.example.

Deposit 30 % to confirm; final numbers 10 days before. Cancellation: full refund 30 days or more ahead, 50 % at 10-29 days, none under 10 days.

## The single way to book
1. Ask about a date (the form on the Ask page, or email). 2. Aiyana replies within one working day and holds the room for 7 days. 3. Choose the menu (a tasting for wedding parties of 30 or more) and pay the 30 % deposit. 4. Final numbers 10 days before; on the night the name cards are set by 17:30. One repeated call to action everywhere: "Ask about a date".

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Hanging paper tag over a russet strip and a black bar (reference: kyoto-kitcho.com header): an 80 x 174 pale tag with the stacked wordmark hangs from the top-left onto the page below a 38 px russet announcement strip and a 70 px black bar with a centred menu and one outline button.
2. Two-by-two abutting photo tiles (kyoto-kitcho.com restaurant page): four photographs 4 px apart, a dark scrim, a centred serif label and a short arrow, the whole tile a link.
3. Seasonal caption on a dimmed photograph (kikunoi.jp first screen): a gold hairline, the month's first course in two lines, centred on the hero seam and returned at the head of inner pages.
Supporting: gold-label ledger rows on alternating dark rows, a 168 px title band with an arc hairline pattern, 1 px hairline lists.
