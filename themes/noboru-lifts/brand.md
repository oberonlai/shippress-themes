# Noboru Lifts - brand brief

Lift inspection and maintenance contractor for office buildings, with a landing page built to book one inspection hour. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people, buildings and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Noboru Lifts. A small contractor that inspects and services lifts in office buildings of 4 to 12 floors.
- **Location:** 14 Wharf Row, Easterhaven (fictional). Phone +1 555 0142, email book@noboru-lifts.example. USD prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2011, by Ines Varga, who still leads inspections.
- **Audience:** facilities managers and owners of office buildings of 4 to 12 floors.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, specific and spoken by someone who has done this work for years. It names the stop, the minute and the paper, and it records only what the visit actually did.
- **Office hours:** Mon-Fri 07:00-17:00. Inspection slots Mon-Fri, start 07:00 or 18:00, one hour, outside the building's busy times. Fault call-outs Mon-Fri 07:00-19:00. Next free slot (notice card): Tuesday, 07:00.

## History
Noboru Lifts began in 2011 at 14 Wharf Row, Easterhaven. The work is inspection and maintenance for facilities managers and owners of office buildings of 4 to 12 floors. Ines Varga founded the company and is still the lead inspector. The team is 7 people, and they do the visits themselves. The day starts at 06:30 at Wharf Row. Vans are out by 06:40. The first lift is taken out of service at 07:00, while the building is still quiet.

An inspection hour is 60 minutes, with 2 technicians and 5 stops: the landing, the car, the pit, the machine and the lobby. They work a 32-point checklist on paper. The hour has three parts. From minute 0 to minute 10 they meet the building manager, sign in, stand the notice at the landing and take the lift out of service. From minute 10 to minute 50 they make the five stops and fill the checklist. From minute 50 to minute 60 they return the lift to service and go through the findings with the manager on paper. The written report follows in 2 working days.

Slots run Monday to Friday and start at 07:00 or 18:00, one hour, outside the building's busy times. Fault call-outs run Monday to Friday from 07:00 to 19:00. Office hours at Wharf Row are Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 17:00. Reports are written in the afternoon, and the second round of visits begins at 18:00. A reply to a booking comes within 1 working day. An inspection hour is not a statutory examination. It is a careful look at one lift, written down, and handed back to the person who runs the building.
