Noboru Lifts is a block theme for an office lift inspection and maintenance contractor at 14 Wharf Row, Easterhaven, founded in 2011. The home page is built to book one inspection hour: 60 minutes, 2 technicians, a 32-point paper checklist, and a written report in 2 working days. It speaks to facilities managers and owners of office buildings of 4 to 12 floors.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download noboru-lifts.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/noboru-lifts.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Noboru Lifts.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, three news posts with two categories and three tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The company, its people, buildings, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
