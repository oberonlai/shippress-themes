# Akari Hotel - brand brief

Boutique city hotel, wordmark AKARI (HOTEL). Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German. All names, places, people and prices are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Akari Hotel (wordmark "AKARI (HOTEL)", line under it "Tessel Street").
- **Location / address:** 18 Tessel Street, Wrenfield (fictional city), a 6-minute walk from Wrenfield Central station.
- **Founded:** 2019, in a converted 1931 printing house. 42 rooms: Studio x24, Corner x12, Loft x6.
- **Audience (who it serves):** guests on short work trips, a night between trains, and quiet weekends; long stays of five nights or more.
- **Tone / voice:** Calm, concrete, and paced by the clock: room sizes, hours and names carry the sentence. The hotel states what it does and does not reach for praise, awards or promises it has not printed.
- **The day:** Check-in from 15:00; check-out by 11:00; front desk 24 hours. Breakfast 07:00–10:30 (Saturday and Sunday until 11:00). Bar 17:00–00:30 daily. Quiet hours 23:00–07:00. The site is told as one guest day: 15:00 Arrival, 19:00 Evening, 23:00 Night, 07:00 Breakfast.

## Story
Akari Hotel opened in 2019 in a printing house that had stood at 18 Tessel Street since 1931. Wrenfield is the city, and the door is a six-minute walk from Wrenfield Central station. The conversion gave the building 42 rooms and left the address as the introduction: the wordmark reads AKARI (HOTEL), and the line under it is Tessel Street. Guests come for the night between trains, for a week of work, or for a Saturday with nothing planned. The house is small on purpose, and the day inside it has a fixed shape.

Check-in is from 15:00. The evening is marked at 19:00, after the bar has opened at 17:00. Night begins at 23:00, which is also when quiet hours start, and they hold until 07:00. Breakfast runs from 07:00 to 10:30, and on Saturday and Sunday until 11:00, included in every rate. Check-out is by 11:00. The front desk stays open 24 hours, so a late arrival is still a normal arrival. The bar closes at 00:30. Smoking is not allowed, and pets are not accepted.

Seven people run the house, and their names are on the door in the staff sense of the word. Mara Lindqvist is general manager. Tomás Ferreira leads the front office. Aiyana Brooks is night manager. Jonas Weber is breakfast chef. Priya Raman leads the bar. Lucie Morel leads housekeeping. Daniel Okafor looks after guest relations for work stays, including the reading room, a meeting room for six, taken by the half day of four hours. A phone call to +00 (0)20 4180 1931, or a note to hello@akari-hotel.example, is answered within one working day. Every rate on the site is in euro.

## Enquiry flow

2. We reply within one working day with availability and the rate.
3. Confirm with a card. Free cancellation until 48 hours before arrival.
