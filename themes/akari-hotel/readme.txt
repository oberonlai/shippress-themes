== Description ==

Akari Hotel is a boutique city hotel at 18 Tessel Street, Wrenfield, opened in 2019 in a converted 1931 printing house, a six-minute walk from Wrenfield Central station. The site follows one guest day, from check-in at 15:00 through evening, night and breakfast, across 42 rooms with rates in euro. Enquiries are answered within one working day by phone or by email.

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond and Nunito Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1), bundled. Photographs were generated for this theme. The scroll effects (sp-scroll) respect the visitor's reduced-motion setting and stay visible without JavaScript.
