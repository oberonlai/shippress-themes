# Akari Hotel

A block theme (type business) for a boutique city hotel. Home page (English) in this release; inner pages and the other languages follow.

## Signature
- An ink wordmark screen opens the home page: a two-line serif name, a small arrival photograph and an info bar of the four times of the guest day.
- Giant solid serif chapter words (Arrival, Evening, Breakfast, Enquire) cross the edges of photographs and invert against whatever is behind them; a 1 px hairline grows left to right before each chapter.
- The three room types stack flush right on an ink band while their text stays sticky on the left.

## Patterns
- `front-arrival`
- `front-breakfast`
- `front-dusk`
- `front-enquire`
- `front-evening`
- `front-hero`
- `front-night`
- `front-notes`
- `front-rates`
- `front-rooms`

## Fonts
Cormorant Garamond and Nunito Sans (SIL OFL 1.1, licences in assets/fonts).
