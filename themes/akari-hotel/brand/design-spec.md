References (S5-Style, measurements in `reference-tokens.md`, screenshots in `ref/`):
- **R1 (main, 80% similarity target): TRUNK (HOTEL) CAT STREET** — https://wedding.trunk-hotel.com/catstreet

Borrow structure, technique, colour relationships, the key-visual concept and motion. Do **not** copy R1/R2 photos, copy, logo (the vertical TRUNK mark) or the whole page layout. All photos are generated by us; all copy is ours.

## 1. Signature techniques (three, obvious at a glance; never diluted)

### T1. Giant serif chapter word overlapping a photo edge, with "( 01 )" label (R1 "Ceremony" / "Banquet")
- **Source**: R1 https://wedding.trunk-hotel.com/catstreet — Ceremony section (`ref/scroll-trunk-ceremony-before-after.png`, `ref/scroll-trunk-banquet-sticky-before-after.png` left half). A serif word "Ceremony" at 164.6 px (11.43vw), line-height 0.8, letter-spacing −0.05em, ink on white, starts at the left gutter and runs **across the left edge of a portrait photo** so the last letters sit on top of the picture. A tiny "( 01 )" label in Inter 11 px sits directly above the first letter.
- **Ours, home**: chapters **Arrival (01), Rooms (02), Evening (03), Night (04), Breakfast (05)**; full T1 overlap on Arrival, Evening, Breakfast.
- **Geometry (1440)**: word `font-size: clamp(4.5rem, 11.43vw, 10.3rem)` (=164 px at 1440, R1 b), Cormorant Garamond 400, `line-height: .8`, `letter-spacing: -0.05em`, colour `#121212` on white. Photo: portrait 3:4, width 32vw (≈460 px, R1 photo 463 px wide at x 248), left edge at x = 17vw. Word baseline sits at 62% of photo height; the word starts at the gutter (x 20 px) so roughly the last **35–45%** of the letters overlap the photo. Word is a sibling **above** the photo in z-order (`position: relative; z-index: 2`), photo `z-index: 1`. Implement with a CSS grid (word and figure in the same grid area, `margin-left` on the figure) — **not** `position:absolute` (mobile overlap gate).
- Label "( 01 )": Nunito Sans 0.75rem/1.3, 500, ink, 0.6rem above the word; parentheses with a space each side.
- **Must not be diluted**: word ≥ 10rem at 1440 (≥ 4.5rem at 390), real overlap of the photo (at least 3 letters over the image), ink (not grey, not accent, not outlined/ghost). It is **solid ink on white** (this is what differs from Shokutaku's pale ghost words).
- **Mobile 390**: word 4.5rem, photo full width minus gutters, word placed **above** the photo with a negative margin-bottom of −0.35em so only the descender zone touches the photo's top edge; normal flow, no absolute positioning, no vertical text.

### T2. Ink band with sticky stacked near-square photos, flush right (R1 "Banquet")
- **Source**: R1 Banquet: an ink `#121212` band 3600 px tall; left 616 px column holds a centred "( 02 )" + white "Banquet" (same giant word) + centred grey text + "View More"; the right column (x 616→1439, flush to the window edge) holds four **823×900** photos (≈0.91:1) that are `position: sticky; top: 0` and stack on top of each other while scrolling (previous one scales to 0.7 and fades).
- **Ours, home "Rooms" (02)**: band `#121212` full-bleed, height = 3 × 100vh. Left column 42.85% (616/1440): sticky (`top: 18vh`) text block centred, max 26rem: "( 02 )" white, word **Rooms** white at the T1 size, 2 short paragraphs `#A0A0A0`, then three room lines (Studio 19 m² · from €148 / Corner 26 m² · from €186 / Loft 34 m² · from €238) separated by 1 px `#808080` hairlines, then underline link "See the rooms" (Cormorant 1.25rem, white, underline offset 4px). Right column 57.15% flush right (no gutter, touches the viewport edge): three figures **P05, P06, P07**, each `aspect-ratio: 823/900`, `position: sticky; top: 0`, radius 0, each one a room type; each has a small caption below-left inside the column "( Studio )" white 0.75rem.
- Stack effect: pure CSS sticky (later figure slides over the earlier one). The R1 shrink-fade is approximated with `sp-reveal sp-reveal--zoom` on figures 2 and 3 (scale 1.06→1 fade in). No scroll listeners.
- **Must not be diluted**: the band is true ink `#121212` (not grey, not purple), photos are flush to the right viewport edge and ≥ 55% of the width, ≥ 3 photos really stack (sticky works: no `overflow:hidden` on ancestors, use `overflow: clip`).
- **< 782 px**: no sticky; text first, then the three photos full-bleed one after another (aspect 823/900), captions under each.

### T3. Wide photo that opens from a slightly inset frame + 1 px line grow before each chapter (R1 Ceremony wide photo)
- **Source**: R1 Ceremony wide photo (16:10, `ref/scroll-trunk-ceremony-before-after.png` left): `clip-path: inset(0 1.5%)` → `inset(0)` scrubbed on scroll; inner image yPercent 0→16; and a 1 px `#808080` line that grows from width 0 to 100% (1 s power1.inOut) above each section; numbering "( 01 )" rises 1.2 s.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen (R1, `ref/trunk-first.png` + `ref/trunk-sheet.png` frame 1)
- **What it shows**: a near-black ink screen (#121212, ≈90% of pixels). Top centre a small logo mark; then a **two-line giant serif wordmark** "TRUNK (HOTEL) / CAT STREET" (≈90 px caps, centred, the second line's last letters still rising — load animation caught mid-flight); under it a **small centred portrait photo** (174×213, ≈4:5) of the couple standing at a lit wooden entrance in greenery; on the same horizontal line as the photo, the **five stacked link columns** at the left and a **white pill** "Bridal Fair" at the right with a hamburger; below, a **parenthesised label "(Beyond The Wedding)"** and a centred grey paragraph. At the very bottom edge (y≈870) the **next full-bleed photo** already peeks in; scrolling reveals a full-bleed portrait photo in green light.
- **Story**: "beyond the wedding" — the day is only the doorway; the site walks through it chapter by chapter (01 Ceremony, 02 Banquet…).
- **Signature motion**: on load the wordmark letters rise (y 150% → 0, 1.2 s power2.out, chars staggered), a bar grows (width 1 s power2.inOut), text follows with 0.3 s delay; then the full-bleed photo scrolls up with an inner parallax (yPercent 0→16).

### (2) Our transposition (boutique city hotel, same composition, same motion idea, same image type = photographs)
"**Beyond the check-in**": the stay is a doorway too; the site walks a guest through one day, 15:00 arrival to 07:00 breakfast. Same ink screen, same centred two-line serif wordmark, same small centred portrait photo — but the photo is **a guest arriving at our lit doorway at dusk with a small suitcase** (same "people at the entrance" scene, our industry). The R1 link-columns row becomes an **info bar of the four day times** (row first_screen "full-bleed-photo-info-bar"), the pill becomes **Enquire**, the label becomes "(Beyond The Check-in)". The peeking full-bleed photo becomes **the guest room at dusk** (row signature), which then scrolls with parallax.

## 4. Type (font_direction = serif)
- Latin display: **Cormorant Garamond** (OFL) 400, 500 (R1 adobe-text-pro 400, Minion-like). Latin body/labels: **Nunito Sans** (OFL) 400, 600 (R1 Inter 400–500). Verify OFL when building.
- CJK: headings **Noto Serif TC / Noto Serif JP** (明體); body Noto Sans TC / JP (R1 JP body is a gothic, Zen Kaku Gothic New). Stacks:
  - heading en/fr/de: `"Cormorant Garamond", "Noto Serif TC", "Noto Serif JP", Georgia, serif`
  - heading zh-TW: `"Cormorant Garamond", "Noto Serif TC", serif`; ja: `"Cormorant Garamond", "Noto Serif JP", serif`
