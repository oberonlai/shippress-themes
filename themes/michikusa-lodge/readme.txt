== Description ==

Michikusa Lodge is a quiet, editorial WordPress theme designed for roadside motor lodges, motels, and independent travellers' stays. Built around film-grain photography, warm tungsten tones, and refined serif typography, it highlights late-arrival logistics, direct booking, and practical amenities. Features include responsive double-exposure hero windows, daily routine ledgers, and clear room rates.

The home page opens on an inset photo window holding two film-grain photographs laid over each other, the menu and a round amber Book tonight disc on the photo, a booking bar docked on its foot and the headline below on paper. It continues with a centred concept paragraph, the arrival hour with stacked time lines and an amber line drawing, the rooms on a hairline ledger, what a stay includes with the full price table, a dawn breakfast parallax band, a scroll-snap road strip, the night desk, three stories, questions and a single way to book. Newsletter and contact forms use the optional ShipPress form slot and need no plugin.
