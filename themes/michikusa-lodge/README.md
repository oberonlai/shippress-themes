# Michikusa Lodge

A quiet block theme (type hospitality) for a trunk-road motor lodge. Home page plus eleven inner pages and one article in English; other languages follow.

## Signature
- Framed-window hero: an inset window holding two film-grain photographs laid over each other (double exposure), the menu and a round amber "Book tonight" disc on the photo, a booking bar docked on the window foot and the headline below on paper.
- Double-hairline heading bands on every section heading.
- Arrival hour: a 4:3 photograph with stacked time lines, a circle photograph and an amber single-stroke line drawing of the lodge.
- Scroll-snap road strip, dawn breakfast band, hairline room ledger and price table.

## Fonts
EB Garamond and Karla (SIL OFL 1.1), with Noto Serif TC and Noto Serif JP subsets for the Chinese and Japanese headings; licences in assets/fonts.
