## Story
Tomasz Wierzbicki opened the yard in 2004 with a handful of compact excavators and a single low-loader, to supply dependable plant for scheduled groundworks. The fleet grew to 43 machines, all kept, serviced and dispatched from the one yard at Gate 3. Nothing is sub-contracted: the people who inspect the pins also sign the hire sheet.

## Audience
Small and mid-size contractors, groundworkers, landscapers and site managers who plan work week by week and need a machine on the gate on Monday morning.

## A day at the yard (the daily routine)
06:00 the yard opens and the fitter walks the machines due out. 06:30 the low-loader leaves for the Monday deliveries with key and hire sheet. Through the day the hire desk takes calls and extensions. 14:00 on Friday collections start; every machine comes back to Gate 3 to be washed, greased and checked before it goes out again.

## The hire week
Mon 06:30 delivery and handover with the hire sheet. Tue first check call. Wed fitter visit if needed. Thu extend or call off by 15:00. Fri collection from 14:00, sheet signed off.

## Voice
Calm, practical and specific. Short sentences, real numbers, no hype and no guarantees. It sounds like a yard coordinator on the phone: clear about dates, weights and what is included.
