# Kashidashi Hire

Block theme for construction plant hire yards. Slant-cut pine tab and wedge, inset photo hero with a two-cell hire dock, tabbed fleet finder, horizontal hire-week strip. See `brand.md` for the fictional yard's facts and `brand/design-spec.md` for the design rules.

## Notes

- Fonts: Schibsted Grotesk (display, variable axis starts at 400, so headings render at weight 400) and Source Sans 3 (text), both OFL-1.1 and bundled under `assets/fonts`.
- Menu: the single top menu links to the real inner pages (`/fleet/`, `/hire-week/`, `/rates/`, `/yard/`, `/faq/`, `/request/`); the home page keeps its anchors for the dock and notice links.
- Home photos are low-key documentary images; the hero photo keeps its subjects in the left third so the headline strips stay clear of them.
