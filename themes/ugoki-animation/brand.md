# Ugoki Animation - brand brief

Independent 2D animation studio in Port Arden (fictional) that makes commercials, title sequences and short films, drawn by hand on paper and finished on screens. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, clients and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Ugoki Animation. "Ugoki" is the Japanese word for movement; the studio is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** Unit 4, 27 Canal Yard, Port Arden (fictional). Canal Yard tram stop, 4 minutes on foot; step-free entrance; visits by appointment.
- **History:** founded in 2014 by director Ines Marlow and animation director Tomasz Reyes with two light tables. In 2018 the studio moved to Canal Yard and grew to six; in 2023 it reached eight people and made its first title sequence.
- **Today:** eight people, eight light tables, one line-test screen. Hand-drawn 2D on paper, scanned, cleaned up, coloured and composited on screens.
- **Audience:** agency producers, broadcasters' promo teams and directors who need a small, reliable 2D studio. Theme buyers are small 2D and stop-motion animation studios pitching to agencies and broadcasters.
- **Tone:** plain, precise, warm. Short sentences, studio vocabulary explained once. No awards, no "best", no guarantees, no real brands or broadcasters.
