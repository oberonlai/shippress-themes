## Concept
Kosei Architects was founded in 2008 by Anna Kosei, an architect who still works at the same long table in the studio at Quai des Charbonnages 12, 1080 Brussels. The practice began with drawings, a timber model and a question from a client, and that order has not changed. Rui Almeida joined as partner and architect in 2011. The studio now has eight people and remains open Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00, with Saturday and Sunday closed and a first meeting only by appointment. A normal day starts with the timber model open on the long table and ends when that day's drawings are filed.

The work is workplaces, housing, renovation and the studies that come before a line is fixed. Finished buildings are the record: Harbour Workshops in Ostend (2023, office, 4,200 m², 180 desks in a 1960s warehouse, steel frame kept), Linden Court in Leuven (2022, 24 co-housing apartments in timber, deck access, shared garden), Old Mill studios in Ghent (2024, 1,600 m² flour mill turned into 14 workshops) and the Rivière school annex in Namur (2021, six classrooms, 900 m² of timber in eleven months). The model stays on the table until the building is in use.

## Fees (EUR, excl. VAT)
- First meeting (60 min, studio or site, written note of the brief): Free
- Feasibility study (site and planning check, 2 massing options, area schedule, cost range): €3,800
- Sketch design (plans, sections, 1:100 model, cost estimate; 6–8 weeks): from €9,500
- Permit drawings (full permit set, consultants coordinated, submission; 10–12 weeks): from €14,000
- Full service, design to handover (all stages, site meetings, handover file): 9 % of build cost
- Interior workplace fit-out (layout, finishes, furniture plan): €38 per m²
A percentage fee moves with the build cost; a "from" fee is the start of that stage.

## Process
1. Brief and site visit (2 weeks): 60 minutes at the studio or on site, a written note of the brief.
2. Sketch design (6–8 weeks): plans, sections, a 1:100 model and a cost estimate.
3. Permit drawings (10–12 weeks): full permit set, consultants coordinated, submission.
4. Construction stage (per contract): site meetings through to a handover file.

## Cases
1. Harbour Workshops, Ostend — 2023, office, 4,200 m²: a logistics firm needed one floor for 180 desks in a 1960s warehouse; timber mezzanine and roof lights, steel frame kept; in use since 2023, daylight at every desk.
2. Linden Court housing, Leuven — 2022, co-housing, 24 apartments; timber frame, deck access, shared garden.
3. Old Mill studios, Ghent — 2024, renovation, 1,600 m²; flour mill turned into 14 workshops.
4. Rivière school annex, Namur — 2021, 900 m², six classrooms, timber, built in 11 months.
