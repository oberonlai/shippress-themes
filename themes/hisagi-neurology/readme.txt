Hisagi Neurology is a block theme for a small outpatient neurology clinic that explains a symptom before it orders a scan. The front page carries the clinic's story, the problems that bring adults in, the examination, one case, fees and a visit to 14 Linden Row, Westmere. The theme is WooCommerce-compatible and works fully without WooCommerce.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download hisagi-neurology.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/hisagi-neurology.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Hisagi Neurology.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Forms: the newsletter section uses a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP configured it shows that form; without a plugin it shows a real email field and a button that opens your email app with the address (nothing is subscribed until the email is sent).

The clinic, its people, address, telephone number, cases and fees are fictional.
