# Haru Allergy - brand brief

Allergy and immunology clinic in Ghent. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Haru Allergy.
- **Location:** Linden Quay 14, 9000 Ghent, Belgium (a fictional address). Email hello@haru-allergy.example, phone +32 9 000 41 20. EUR (€) prices in every language.
- **History:** founded in 2015 by Dr Ines Marlow, allergist and clinical immunologist, who wanted a quiet clinic where a history is taken properly before anything is tested. It began with a single consulting room and has grown into a team of seven.
- **Today:** adults on weekdays, children on Wednesday afternoons, testing only on Saturdays. Every new patient leaves with a written one-page avoidance plan and a review booked at 12 weeks.
- **Audience:** adults and parents with a rash, a wheeze, a seasonal cough or a food or drug reaction they cannot place, who are tired of guessing and avoiding too much.
- **Tone:** calm, clear and practical. No cures, guarantees, statistics, awards, press or real brands; case notes are anonymised and written with permission, details changed.

## People (7, named the same everywhere)
Dr Ines Marlow (founder, allergist and clinical immunologist, adults) - Dr Tomasz Weber (paediatric allergist, children's clinic) - Dr Amara Okoye (clinical immunologist, immunotherapy and drug allergy) - Leah Fontaine (nurse lead, skin-prick and patch testing) - Samir Haddad (respiratory physiologist, lung function and FeNO) - Noor Aziz (allergy dietitian, avoidance plans) - Grace Mendes (patient coordinator, bookings and reviews).

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Hours: Mon-Fri 8:00-18:00 (Wednesday late clinic until 19:30), Sat 9:00-12:30 (testing only), Sun closed. New-patient first visits Tuesday and Thursday mornings. Children's clinic Wednesday 13:00-17:00.
- Process, one flow: History (60 min), Test (skin-prick read after 15-20 minutes, same day or within one week), Plan (a written one-page avoidance plan), Review (booked at 12 weeks; immunotherapy every 6 months).
- Fees (EUR): first consultation with full history, 60 min, €145; skin-prick test panel (up to 20 allergens), 40 min, €90; patch test series (3 visits in one week), €210; lung function and FeNO, 30 min, €75; supervised oral food challenge, half day, €260; follow-up review, 30 min, €80; immunotherapy start visit (60 min observation), 2 h, €120. Itemised receipt for your insurer (reimbursement depends on the insurer); cancel at least 24 hours ahead or a €40 fee applies. The written plan is included in the consultation.

## A day at the clinic (daily routine)
8:00 the clinic opens and Leah prepares the skin-prick trays; Tuesday and Thursday mornings are new-patient histories of sixty minutes; late morning the test panels are read after twenty quiet minutes; on Wednesday the children's clinic runs 13:00-17:00 and a late adult clinic runs until 19:30; Saturday 9:00-12:30 is testing only. Grace books every review at twelve weeks before the patient leaves.

2. Tall portraits flush to the left and right viewport edges at different heights with a narrow heading between them and a veiled third photo (nuset.jp About).
3. A rounded-40 paper card with a ruled footer and dot pager beside a tall 2:3 photo (nuset.jp Voice).
4. Supporting: a line-up band with a huge light heading, hairline circles, 2:3 photo rows, chips and vertical side notes; a slate band of translucent case-note cards with a progress line.
