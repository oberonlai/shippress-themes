Haru Allergy is a calm, minimal WordPress theme for allergy and immunology clinics, consulting practices, and specialised health centres. Designed with refined sans-serif typography, airy high-key layouts, and quiet paper tones, it presents diagnostic services, structured practitioner bios, and patient case notes with clarity and warmth. Fully block-based and responsive, the theme provides dedicated templates for initial consultations, diagnostic testing panels, and clinic hours.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download haru-allergy.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/haru-allergy.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Haru Allergy.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the case-note posts with their categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. The theme works as a plain site without WooCommerce.

Forms: the newsletter slot in the footer uses a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active it shows your form; without a plugin it shows an email field and a button that opens your email app with the address filled in (you are not subscribed until you send that email). The theme sends nothing itself.

The clinic, its people, its address, telephone number and fees are fictional.
