Packing

Every piece is wrapped in recycled paper, nested in rice-husk padding and packed in a small wooden crate. We pack by hand, one order at a time.

Breakage

If anything arrives broken, send us a photograph within seven days and we will make and send a new piece at no cost. No need to return the pieces.

Delivery

Sent within three working days. Within the country 2–4 days, $12, free over $150. Abroad 7–14 days, from $28, with tracking.

Returns

Unused pieces can be returned within 30 days in their crate. We refund on arrival; return postage is yours.
