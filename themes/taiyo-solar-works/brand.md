# Taiyo Solar Works: brand brief

Design plan row: b6-16 (regional solar installation company, type business). Home page plus eleven inner pages and a journal, in five languages (en, zh-TW, ja, fr, de). All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Brand name:** Taiyo Solar Works. **Location:** Marlow, Alder Valley (fictional). **Audience:** homeowners with a pitched roof, small landlords and owners of commercial buildings within about 60 miles.
- **Company:** Taiyo Solar Works, founded 2011, workshop and office at 214 Foundry Lane, Marlow, in the fictional Alder Valley. Phone (555) 014-2290, hello@taiyosolar.example.
- **Founders:** Ruth Calloway (electrical engineer, design and permits) and Tomas Ibarra (roofer, runs installs).
- **Installers:** Marcus Bell (lead, 14 years on roofs), Priya Natarajan (electrician, 9), Jonah Kessler (commercial, 11), Sofia Reyes (assessments, 7).
- **Service area:** about 60 miles of Marlow: Marlow, Kenning, Ashby, Halden, Pell Harbor, Westbrook.
- **Hours:** Mon-Fri 7:30-17:00; Sat 9:00-13:00 roof assessments by appointment; Sun closed.
- **Track record:** more than 600 roofs since 2011. No awards, media or guarantees. All savings are labelled est.; basis: modelled yield and $0.20/kWh household ($0.15 commercial).
- **A typical day:** crews load the vans at 7:30, are on the first roof by 8:30 and clear the site each evening; assessments run in the afternoon routine.
- **Tone:** plain, specific, numbers with their assumptions.

## Prices (USD, installed, before incentives)
Home solar 4-6 kW $11,800-$16,900; 7-10 kW $18,500-$26,000; battery 13.5 kWh from $10,900; EV charger $1,450-$2,300; commercial $2.10-$2.60 per watt; maintenance $190; roof assessment free. Warranty: 15-year workmanship.

## Signature
Split first screen (roof photo | graphite gradient panel with inset portrait), two overlapping hairline circles with case figures, white numeral cards on a grey band, plus a shallow roof-pitch edge. Sources: BEST HOME EP and URBAN HOME (S5-Style), see brand/reference.md.

## Palette
Ground #F1F1EF, band #E6E6E3, graphite #43474B, mid grey #6E7275, sun ochre #B9822E (dots, bars and est. figures only).

## Inner pages
Installations (case wall, seven cases C1-C7 with kW, estimated output and saving), Kenning case (C2, 8.8 kW + 13.5 kWh battery, est. 11,000 kWh and $2,150 a year), Residential Solar, Battery Storage and EV Charging, Commercial Roofs (C6 Westbrook 96 kW, C7 Ashby 32 kW), How It Works (the four steps: Book, Visit, Proposal, Install), Maintenance and Warranty (15-year workmanship, 25-year panel performance, 10-year battery, $190 visit), Pricing and Financing (full price table; pay on completion or 10, 15, 20-year loans through the fictional Alder Valley Credit Union; 10% deposit), Free Roof Assessment (plain HTML form with a mailto action), About (founders, installers, safety), Contact. Journal: six posts.

## Languages
Copy is rewritten locally, not translated word for word. Prices, system sizes, estimates and warranty terms are identical in every language; currency stays USD.
