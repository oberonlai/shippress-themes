# Hakari Course

A calm WordPress **block theme** for an **online food-safety supervisor course** (landing page; Tutor LMS and WooCommerce compatible, neither required). White ground with a pastel pink-mint-cream wash, rust-teal (#5F8884) display words and numbers, signal-red enrol buttons and mint tabs. **Onest** headings over **Public Sans**; Noto Sans TC / JP for Chinese and Japanese. All names, the Rotterdam address and the prices are fictional.

## Signature

- **Rounded-bottom full-bleed hero**: 88px bottom corners, white copy across the lower middle, a #FOODSAFETY #SUPERVISOR hashtag pair straddling the photo edge, two round badges and two vertical fixed edge tabs (a bottom bar on phones). Load motion: frame and photo zoom, copy bounce, badges pop.
- **Tilted rounded photo rows**: 88px photos rotated five degrees with a big number, a two-line headline and an outlined pill button.
- **White rounded panels on the wash**: inset 55px with 88px corners, holding 33px photo cards, 22px outlined link cards, the syllabus accordion, cohort dates and FAQ.
- **Scroll effects** (sp-scroll): parallax on the hero and the photo band, reveal and stagger on cards and rows.

## Who it is for

- **Industry:** online courses and training schools (food safety, hospitality, vocational).
- **Site type:** landing page with one repeated call to action.

## Pages

Home (eleven sections, anchor menu: Course, Syllabus, Instructor, Dates, Fee, FAQ), plus Contact, Privacy notice and Terms of enrolment sample pages. More inner pages follow in the next release step.

## Photography

Fourteen AI-generated high-key photographs (one STYLE string): a bright teaching kitchen for the hero and course cards, a home kitchen and a home desk for the kit and exam rows, and a different setting for each learner story (bakery, school canteen, food truck).
