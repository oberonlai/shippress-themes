# Hakari Course - brand brief

Online food-safety supervisor course by the Hakari food-safety school, with a studio teaching kitchen in Rotterdam. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hakari Course. "Hakari" (the school) comes from the Japanese word for measuring; the course itself is international and plain.
- **Location:** studio teaching kitchen at Havenkade 14, 3024 Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional). Prices in EUR in every language, VAT included, never converted.
- **History:** Hakari Course was established in 2016 at Havenkade 14 in Rotterdam to provide practical food hygiene education for hospitality supervisors. Founded by kitchen auditors Ines Vermeulen and Tomás Brandt, the school was created to bridge the gap between regulatory hygiene paperwork and daily commercial kitchen routines. Starting from a studio teaching kitchen in the port district, Hakari focused specifically on the operational realities faced by sous-chefs, shift leads, canteen cooks, and mobile caterers.
- **Today:** Today, Hakari Course delivers a structured six-week online programme accompanied by professional temperature-monitoring kits shipped directly to learners. Combining weekly live sessions, tutor-reviewed practical kitchen logs, and proctored examinations, the school prepares working kitchen leads to maintain consistent safety controls across commercial prep stations, deliveries, and service lines.
- **Audience:** people who supervise food handling: shift leads, sous-chefs, canteen and bakery leads, food-truck owners.
- **Tone:** The tone is calm, practical, and precise, focused on daily kitchen routines and verifiable controls. It addresses working kitchen supervisors in clear, factual, and reassuring professional English. No promised pass rates, jobs, legal compliance, awards or press. Allowed: "prepares you for", "built on", "designed with working supervisors".

## The course (fixed numbers, identical on every page and in every language)
- 6 weeks, 24 lessons (4 a week, 10-20 minutes each, about 18 hours with practice), 6 live Q&A sessions on Tuesdays 18:30-19:30 CET (recorded), lessons open for 12 months.
- Practical log: 5 recorded probe checks in your own kitchen, reviewed by a tutor.
- Exam: online, proctored, 40 questions, 60 minutes, pass mark 75 %, one free resit within 30 days. PDF certificate within 2 working days, valid 3 years.
- Kit (full fee): digital probe thermometer, calibration cup, 50 probe wipes, temperature log pad; ships within 5 working days of enrolment.
- Cohorts (40 seats each, Monday start, Sunday end): 14 = 11 Jan - 21 Feb 2027 (Open); 15 = 1 Mar - 11 Apr 2027 (Open); 16 = 3 May - 13 Jun 2027 (waitlist opens 1 Feb 2027); 17 = 6 Sep - 17 Oct 2027 (Waitlist).
- Fees (EUR, VAT included): full course 390 - course without kit 340 - team seats of 5 or more 330 per seat - extra exam resit 60 - certificate re-issue or second-language copy 20.
- Refunds: full refund up to 14 days before the cohort starts; after the start, full refund within 14 days if fewer than 25 % of the lessons are opened.
- Support: Mon-Fri 9:00-17:00 CET, reply within 1 working day, hello@hakari-course.example.
- Repeated call to action: "Enrol in the next cohort" (short label "Enrol").
