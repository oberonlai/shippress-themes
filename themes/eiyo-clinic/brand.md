# Eiyo Clinic - brand brief

Nutrition and dietetics clinic for adults in Seattle, Washington. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Eiyo Clinic. "Eiyo" is the Japanese word for nutrition; the clinic is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** one floor of a small building at 418 Alder Row, Suite 2, Seattle, WA 98103 (fictional). Three consult rooms and one teaching kitchen. USD prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** founded in 2016 by registered dietitian Ingrid Solberg around one wooden table.
- **Audience:** adults who want plainer everyday meals instead of rigid diets: breakfast skippers, night-shift workers, households with different schedules.
- **Tone:** Our tone is calm, plain, and warm, describing everyday meals and consultation routines without hype or clinical jargon. We focus on realistic domestic routines built steadily over time, speaking with honest clarity and never forecasting outcomes.
- **Rules:** says "dietitian", never "doctor"; works alongside the client's doctor; no guarantees, weight-loss figures, cure claims, awards, press or real people.

## Story
Eiyo Clinic was founded in 2016 by registered dietitian Ingrid Solberg. Having spent years listening to adults struggle with rigid diets and contradictory nutrition rules, Ingrid opened a small practice centered around a single wooden table in Seattle. Her premise was straightforward: lasting nutritional health is built from ordinary, repeatable meals that people actually enjoy preparing, rather than strict meal plans that collapse under everyday stress.

Over the following years, the practice expanded to occupy one floor of a modest building at 418 Alder Row, Suite 2, Seattle, WA 98103. Today, our space includes three quiet consult rooms designed for focused, unhurried conversations and one dedicated teaching kitchen for hands-on cooking classes. The clinic remains deliberately grounded in practical, domestic routines, ensuring our guidance reflects the real physical constraints of household pantries and busy workdays.

Our team has grown to include seven practitioners, including registered dietitians with distinct areas of focus, a dedicated kitchen instructor, and a welcoming front desk coordinator. We do not prescribe medications or offer dramatic cures; instead, we work alongside each client's primary doctor to provide steady, evidence-informed dietary support. Every plan we create is drafted by hand together in the room, keeping everyday eating unpretentious, sustainable, and humane.
