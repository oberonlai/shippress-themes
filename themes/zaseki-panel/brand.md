# Zaseki Panel: brand

## Name
Zaseki Panel (company and product share the name), a small hardware maker (fictional) that sells one matte wall panel for shared offices and coworking floors. The panel shows which desks are free, held or taken, so nobody has to walk the floor. Industry: shared-office desk-booking hardware, pre-order landing page.

## Story
Zaseki Panel was founded in 2022 by Mara Lindqvist and Kwame Asante at Ferrow Yard, Unit 4, 18 Canal Street in Norrbridge. Working from their workshop, Mara led product design while Kwame developed the core electronics and the wireless desk tags. They set out to solve a persistent friction in shared offices: members and visitors walking the whole floor each morning just to find an open desk.

Rather than building another app that needs downloads, accounts and constant screen checking, they made a physical wall panel. It measures 55 by 38 centimetres, is 14 mm deep, weighs 2.1 kg and mounts in the lobby, showing a floor plan of up to 120 desks in three states: sage for free, clay for held, stone for taken. A small round tag on every desk confirms who is sitting where with a single tap.

Pilot installs at Quayside Works, Lindenhof Commons and Oriel House showed that checking the floor at the door replaced the morning lap and the front-desk queue. Today the Norrbridge team includes install lead Priya Raman, surveyor Daniel Okafor and Élodie Marchand at the pre-order desk, preparing each panel and tag crate for a single-day install.

## Audience
Front-desk and operations managers of shared offices, coworking floors and studio buildings with 40 to 300 desks, and the members and visitors who arrive without an app.

## Tone
Calm, specific and understated: dimensions, hours and plain steps instead of slogans. No guarantees, no invented numbers, no awards. Short sentences, concrete nouns.

## A day at the workshop and on the floor (daily routine)
Daily routine on a floor with a panel: 08:10 Rosa switches on the lobby lights and the panel already shows yesterday's tags released; 08:40 the first wave taps in; 10:00 held desks that nobody claimed let go; 13:00 lunch, taken desks stay taken; 17:30 tags tap out and the floor goes back to sage overnight.

## The product
One wall panel per floor, 55 × 38 cm, 14 mm deep, 2.1 kg, matte pale face, floor plan of up to 120 desks, one power socket (wall adapter, 3 m cable). Desk tags: one per desk, adhesive pad, replaceable coin cell. Desk states: free = sage, held = clay, taken = stone.

## How a desk is held
1. Tap a free desk on the lobby panel; it turns clay and is held for 15 minutes.
2. At the desk, tap the desk tag; the desk turns stone (taken) until you tap out or leave for the day.
3. If nobody taps in within 15 minutes, the hold lets go by itself and the desk turns sage again.
