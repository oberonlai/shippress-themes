# Zaseki Panel

A calm, single-column landing theme for Zaseki Panel, a small maker of matte lobby wall panels that show which desks are free in shared offices and coworking spaces. The home page opens on three equal columns (a lobby photograph, a solid clay panel with a tilted russet desk grid and the one pre-order button, and a second photograph) with an information bar, then the floor story, three numbered rows, a parallax photo band, a coloured tile directory, an install table, a grey price band, dated lists, question rows and a closing russet band with wide diagonal stripes. Outfit and Nunito Sans, a cool white ground, russet lines and flat sage, clay and sand tiles. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German. WCAG AA colours.

Reference: KOKUYO (https://www.kokuyo.com/): the tilted grid sheet, the coloured action tile directory and the half-field striped footer, translated into a one-product hardware landing page. See `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md` and `brand/reference-tokens.md`.

## Home blocks
Notice strip and top bar, hero (photo | clay grid panel | photo) with an information bar, the floor story with a sticky label rail, three numbered rows, a parallax band, the tile directory, the install table, the price band, pilot notes and dated lists, question rows and the closing striped band (the footer on every page).

## Patterns
`front-hero`, `front-floor`, `front-hold`, `front-band`, `front-box`, `front-install`, `front-price`, `front-pilots`, `front-questions`, `tpl-home` (posts list), `header-main` and `footer-main`.

## Fonts
Outfit and Nunito Sans (OFL-1.1), loaded through theme.json.
