# Zaseki Panel: design spec (stage 1 spec, binding for implementation)

Slug `zaseki-panel`. A small manufacturer selling one product: a matte wall panel for shared offices that shows which desks on the floor are free, held or taken, so nobody has to walk the floor. Row b38-6: archetype **single-column-longform**, hero **product-grid-hero**, nav **top-bar**, page structure **landing-longform**, first screen **three-equal-columns**, type **landing** (home = one long conversion page, anchor menu, **one repeated CTA "Pre-order a panel"**; newsletter page = waitlist; plus privacy, terms, thanks), font direction **sans** (Outfit / Nunito Sans, CJK Noto Sans TC / JP), palette "kabazome" (birch-red) russet `#8C5648`, category business-services. Five languages: en, zh-TW, ja, fr, de.

Primary reference (ref-check passed, not in `used-refs.txt`): **KOKUYO** https://www.kokuyo.com/ (S5-Style entry, office products and workplace maker). No secondary reference. Measurements in `brand/reference-tokens.md`, quoted below as **RT(a)…RT(g)**. Screens: `brand/ref/kokuyo-first.png` (hero slide 1), `scroll-kokuyo-1150-after.png` (slide 2 grid sheet + first tile), `scroll-kokuyo-1800.png` (tile directory), `scroll-kokuyo-2700.png` (news lists), `scroll-kokuyo-3600.png` (footer stripes).

**Imagery: photo** (stage-a IMAGERY = photo; KOKUYO's tile directory and people shots are photographs; its hero slide 1 is a flat-vector diorama whose *concept* we translate into photographs, see §2). All photos are generated by us with one STYLE string (§6). We never copy KOKUYO's photos, illustrations, copy, logo, wordmark or full-page layout. The tilted desk grid, floor-plan lines, triple-slash glyph, circle arrows and stripes are our own SVG/CSS.

## 1. Signature techniques (three, from KOKUYO; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

### T1. Tilted desk-grid sheet on a solid panel (KOKUYO hero slide 2, https://www.kokuyo.com/, `ref/scroll-kokuyo-1150-after.png`, RT(e)(f)(g))
- **Reference:** a white sheet crossed by a heavy cyan grid (≈12 px lines, rotated ≈ −15°), cells ≈ 300 px, headline bottom-left in black over the sheet, "2 / 2" counter right. It reads as a notebook page blown up to a whole screen.
- **Ours:** inside the solid clay hero panel (home, col 2) a rotated grid of **russet `#8C5648` lines 10 px**, cell **128 px**, rotated **−9°**, own inline SVG `.zp-grid` (decorative, `aria-hidden`). It occupies the **top 56%** of the panel and is clipped there by the panel's overflow (hard edge, no fade); the headline sits on plain clay below it. Three cells are filled: one **sage `#C3D3C6`** (free), one **clay-deep `#D9B3A4`** (held), one **stone `#CFCBC1`** (taken) — this is the "panel" drawn large: the floor seen as a grid.
- **Not to be diluted:** lines stay ≥ 8 px and russet (not a pale tint, not 1 px); the rotation stays visible (−9° ± 1°); the grid crosses the panel edge to edge (clipped, not inset); at least the three filled cells.
- **Inner pages:** every inner page head (T1 head, §4) repeats a smaller version: a clay panel 1/2 width × 360 px with the same grid (cell 96 px, lines 8 px, −9°), page title lower-left on plain clay.

### T2. Action-pair tiles with status swatches (KOKUYO tile directory, `ref/scroll-kokuyo-1800.png`, RT(c)(d)(f))
- **Reference:** flat full-colour tiles 408 × 144 with 16 px gutters, title ~32 px top-left, two-line one-liner right-aligned top-right, foot "circle-arrow | plus", and under the tile edge two pairs of tiny 16 × 3 swatches; a 832 × 288 wide documentary photo tile above; a grey 832 × 112 strip tile below; right column a card + link rows topped by rules with a small colour tick.
- **Ours (home block 5 `#box`):** left area 832 px: wide photo tile X1 832 × 288 → 2 × 2 tint tiles 408 × 144 → grey strip tile 832 × 112; right column 408 px (56 px gap): photo card X2 408 × 192 → four link rows. Tile fills: **russet `#8C5648`** (white text), **sage `#C3D3C6`**, **clay `#E2C3B6`**, **sand `#DDDACB`** (RT(a) tile sand, exact); strip **`#CCCCCC`** (RT(a) exact). Under every tile: swatch pair `16×3 + 4 gap + 16×3`, 8 px gap, second pair — colours sage/clay then stone/russet (the three desk states + brand). Radius 0, no bevel (the cut corner is another theme's; not used).
- **Not to be diluted:** tiles must be visibly coloured fields (no tile lighter than `#E2C3B6`; Tsumugi lesson); title-left / one-liner-right tension; circle-arrow | plus foot; swatch pairs below the edge.
- **Inner pages:** Panel (4 parts tiles), Install (4 "what your building provides" tiles), Pricing (4 line-item tiles linking to table rows), Pre-order (4 "what happens next" tiles, above the form, not around it).

### T3. Half-field diagonal stripe close (KOKUYO footer, `ref/scroll-kokuyo-3600.png`, RT(a)(f))
- **Reference:** a saturated footer field; left half link columns each topped by a 1 px rule with a 16 px two-colour tick; right half filled by wide two-tone diagonal stripes (~190 px, ~58°) with the slogan + big wordmark bottom-right.
- **Ours:** the closing pre-order band, used as the **site footer template part on every page** (home and all inner pages). Ground **russet `#8C5648`**; right half (x ≥ 720 at 1440) stripes `repeating-linear-gradient(58deg, #8C5648 0 190px, #F1E4DD 190px 380px)`; bottom-right: slogan Outfit 400 32 px + wordmark "ZASEKI" Outfit 600 **6rem** uppercase, ink `#2A2624` on a pale stripe — wordmark placed so it sits over the pale stripe region (white-ish `#F1E4DD` box behind if needed for contrast, no box visible in practice). Left half (white text): H2 "Hold a desk from the door." Outfit 400 2.75rem, one line of terms, **the CTA "Pre-order a panel"** (white fill, russet text), then 3 link columns (Pre-order · The panel · Company), each topped by a 1 px `rgba(255,255,255,.5)` rule with a 16 × 3 tick (8 px sage + 8 px clay).
- **Not to be diluted:** stripes stay ≥ 160 px wide, ≈ 58°, and occupy the whole right half for the full band height (≥ 560 px desktop); the field is russet, not a pale tint.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion
KOKUYO opens on a full-viewport, pale-green "pop-up book" office: desks, shelves, chairs, plants and a ladder stacked on several floating levels, small people working at different heights (one at a raised desk, one crouched by a chair, two at a bookshelf), all in flat vector shapes over faint line sketches of a globe, a camera and a dinosaur. Headline bottom-left in white ("Curiosity is Life"), a circle-arrow link, counter "1 / 2" with white edge ticks. Slide 2 is the white sheet with the thick tilted cyan grid. **Story:** a workplace maker shows *the whole working world in one picture* — many people, many places to work, all visible at once. **Signature motion:** full-screen sections that snap one per screen with an "n / 2" counter and edge ticks; the diorama's pieces move gently while the section is current; slash/line elements draw in (showslash 1.2 s, cubic-bezier(.3,0,.3,1)).

### (2) Our translation (same idea "the whole floor in one picture, seen at once", same motion family, image type photo; composition forced to collage / on-solid-panel / info-bar)
A bright lobby of a shared office at 08:40. **Column 1:** Rosa at the front desk touches the Zaseki panel on the wall — the floor's desks shown as a small grid of coloured cells (photo H1). **Column 2:** the "floor seen at once" itself: KOKUYO's grid sheet becomes a solid clay panel crossed by the tilted russet desk grid with free / held / taken cells, carrying the headline and the one CTA. **Column 3:** a visitor with a laptop bag waits on a bench by the lobby wall, looking left toward the panel (photo H2) — the person who no longer has to walk the floor. Under the columns the info bar is KOKUYO's counter + edge tick turned into pre-order facts. The diorama's "pieces move while current" becomes: one grid cell slowly turning from free to held and back (the panel is live), plus both photos drifting at different parallax speeds as the page leaves the hero (KOKUYO's layered depth). The concept is kept, not dropped: many desks, one view, before anyone walks the floor.
