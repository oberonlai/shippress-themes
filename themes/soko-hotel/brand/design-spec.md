## 1. Signature techniques (three; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

### T1. Letterbox stage: one dim 2.4:1 still held between two solid black bars, nav living inside the frame (Tawaraya first screen, RT(c)(e)(f))
- Reference: https://the-tawaraya.jp/ — first screen 1440×900 is a pure black stage; a 1440×600 dim film still sits between **150px black bars** top and bottom; wordmark tiny and tracked in the top bar; three tracked Latin links stacked inside the photo at x=141; three vertical-set links inside the right edge; a rotated "RESERVATION" label at the far-left edge.
- Ours: home hero (block H1). **Bars 150px each, warm black `#0E0B08`; photo 1440×600 (2.4:1), edge to edge, radius 0, no overlay except a left-side gradient** (`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(14,11,8,.72) 0%, rgba(14,11,8,.35) 34%, rgba(14,11,8,0) 55%)`) under the H1 only.
- Not to dilute: the bars must be solid and ≥140px at 1440×900 (never a gradient, never paper); the photo must stay dim (mean luminance ≤ 30%); nav text inside the frame stays tiny (12px) and tracked; no button boxes in the photo.
- Continues to inner pages: every inner page opens with a **short letterbox** (black bar 110px with header, still 1440×520 at 2.75:1 cropped, black bar 90px holding the page title in Gloock). Rooms, The Building, People, Canteen, Visit, Reserve, Contact, Newsletter all use it with their own photo (see §6). Mid-page the home night band (H5) repeats the letterbox at 120px bars.

### T2. Vertical title over three equal serif columns (Tawaraya essay, RT(b))
- Reference: a three-character title set `vertical-rl`, centred (40px, ls .12em, #333), above three equal text columns ≈345px wide, gap ≈62px, lh ≈2.2, ls .08em, 7 lines each; small caption (12px, #B2B2B2, ls .1em) and author line centred beneath.
- Ours: home H3 "Essay". Title **"No. 7"** set `writing-mode: vertical-rl; text-orientation: upright` (desktop ≥1100px; upright letters stacked, Gloock 2.5rem, ls .12em, colour #333333, centred, block height ≈150px). For zh-TW/ja the title is native CJK (e.g. 七號倉庫 / 七番倉庫), naturally upright. Below: **three columns, each 345px, gap 62px, total 1159px, centred**; Source Sans 3 300, 1rem, lh 2.2, ls .035em (CJK: ls .08em, system Mincho body). Caption above columns centred: "An essay on the building" 12px #B2B2B2 ls .1em; author line beneath: "Text by Ines Varga, founder" 13px #666 ls .08em centred.
- Not to dilute: three columns of equal width (never 2, never a card), vertical title strictly centred above the middle column, generous lh 2.2, no images inside the columns.
- Continues to inner pages: The Building (history essay, title "1924"), People (title "Hands", three columns = three people), Canteen (title "Table", three columns: morning / evening / suppliers), Journal single posts (post title horizontal, but the post lede uses the three-column layout at ≥1100px for the first 3 paragraphs via a `is-style-essay-3col` group style).

### T3. Ground inversion black → paper → black, with the photograph returning under the black footer (Tawaraya scroll sequence, RT(e)(g))
- Reference: as the stage photo scrolls away the ground becomes white; the next photo sits **inset 90px each side (1260×525–560)**; at the end the ground returns to black under the footer text and a full-bleed photo is revealed beneath it.
- Ours: section grounds alternate **black hero → paper (H2–H4) → black night band (H5) → paper (H6–H8) → black footer**. H2 is the inset still: **x=90–1350, 1260×525 (2.4:1)**, radius 0, no shadow. The footer ends with a full-bleed photo band (P10, 1440×560, 2.57:1) beneath the black footer text, with `sp-parallax`.
- Same-page rule: the reference re-uses the same photograph; we may not repeat an image on a page, so we keep the *idea* (the same scene returns) with a **reverse shot**: hero P01 = the door seen from the corridor; H2 inset P02 = the same door seen from inside the loft suite at the same minute; footer P10 = the whole warehouse seen from the water. Same light, same palette, read as one film sequence.
- Not to dilute: the inversion must be hard edges (no gradient transitions between grounds); paper is near-white (#FAFAF8), never cream; the inset must keep the 90px margins at 1440 (scale as 6.25vw).
- Continues to inner pages: every inner page = black letterbox header → paper body → black footer with P10 returning (footer is shared). Rooms page inserts one black band (rates) to repeat the inversion.

### Supporting (required, not counted)
- **S1 Fixed edge labels** (RT(f)): left edge rotated "Reserve" (11px, ls .2em, uppercase, x=41 centre, vertical centre of viewport, `transform: rotate(-90deg)`) and right edge vertical label "Door 7" (11px, vertical-rl, x≈1396). Plain text links to /reserve/; colour umber #8C7042 (readable on both black and paper; no mix-blend-mode). **Only ≥1100px.** Hidden below 1100px (Reserve appears in the header instead).
- **S2 Bay stamp**: our own 1px line drawing, a stencil "No. 7" inside a narrow arched bay outline, 95×135, stroke #888888, no fill. Used on the splash, small (40×57) at top-left of the top bar, and 60×85 at footer top-left.
- **G1 (secondary, Gora Kadan)**: heading rise only (blur skipped, sp-scroll has none) — mapped to `sp-reveal--up`.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://the-tawaraya.jp/)
- **What it draws:** a pure black stage. A line-drawn seal appears alone; then a dim film still — a lit interior seen through an open sliding screen, maple leaves in the courtyard, lanterns, dark timber floor — fades in between black bars. Nav is tiny and tracked; vertical links stand at the right edge; "RESERVATION" lies rotated at the far left.
- **Story:** an inn whose rooms are lived-in art. Evening, stillness, the threshold between corridor and room; nothing is sold loudly; the single booking label waits at the edge and the photograph does the persuading.
- **Signature motion:** splash (seal) → still fades in over 1.8s while scaling 1.3 → 1 over 2s (expo-out); as you scroll the ground flips black → white → black, the photograph returning under the footer.

### (2) Our translation (same composition, same motion idea, same image type: low-key documentary photograph)
A black stage. Our hairline bay stamp "No. 7" appears alone for a moment. Then the hero still fades in between the black bars: **inside the 1924 brick warehouse, head concierge Mirela Costa (small, three-quarter back view, unposed) slides open a tall steel-framed glazed door; through it a loft suite glows — a timber floor, a low bed, one warm lamp — while evening light rakes across the bare brick wall on the left.** Threshold between corridor and room, as in the reference; brick/steel/timber instead of paper/lacquer. The left third (dark brick, shadowed) carries the H1; the door and the lit room sit in the right 60%. The photo eases from scale 1.3 to 1. Scroll: ground flips to paper and the same moment returns from the other side of the door (P02), then the essay, the rooms, the night harbour, the people, and the footer where the whole warehouse returns from the water.
