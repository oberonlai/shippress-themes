# Soko Hotel — brand

## Name and location
Soko Hotel ("soko" is the harbour-side word for warehouse) is a fictional design hotel of 32 rooms inside Warehouse No. 7, a 1924 brick harbour warehouse at Pier 7, Kessel Quay 14, Vessa Harbour. Phone +00 20 555 0147. Email stay@sokohotel.example. Reception 24 hours; reservations line daily 09:00–19:00; check-in 15:00; check-out 11:00. The Canteen: breakfast daily 07:00–10:30; dinner Wednesday to Sunday 18:00–22:00.

## Founded
Built in 1924 for coffee and rope; closed in 1987. Restored 2016–2019 under founder and architect Ines Varga; the hotel opened in 2019.

## Audience
Travellers who choose a stay for its building and its people rather than for its facilities: architects, photographers, couples, small groups and anyone who wants a quiet harbour room. Buyers of the theme: independent design hotels and heritage-building conversions that sell a story-led stay and take reservations directly.

## Tone
Calm, unhurried and specific. It names bricks, beams, doors and hours instead of using promotional adjectives, and treats guests as observant visitors who appreciate honest materials. No guarantees, no invented awards, no superlatives.

## Story
Warehouse No. 7 was raised on Kessel Quay in 1924 to store raw coffee and ship cordage. Freight steamers tied up alongside its red brick bays for over sixty years, until trade moved down the bay in 1987 and the doors were locked.

Between 2016 and 2019 Ines Varga's team converted it one arched bay at a time. Henrik Lund repaired the façade with bricks salvaged from the collapsed loading shed, the iron crane beam stayed in place above the two top-floor suites, and new glazed doors were framed in blackened steel. The hotel opened in 2019 and is run by the people who rebuilt it.

Three stories are told on the home page and in the journal: the brick count (the façade repaired bay by bay), the crane beam (kept in the Crane Suites) and the night log (Pavel Novak's handwritten record).

## Process (the single way to reserve)
1. Write to stay@sokohotel.example or call the reservations line (daily 09:00–19:00).
2. We reply within one working day, with the room held for 48 hours.
3. Confirm, and arrive at Door 7 on the quay; reception is behind it.
There is no booking engine and no third-party widget. Every "Reserve" goes to /reserve/.
