# Misora Links - brand brief

A members' golf club on the dune road at Westerby Point (fictional coastal town, no country named). Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Misora Links.
- **Location:** 1 Starter's Lane, Westerby Point.
- **Founded:** 1961. Eighteen holes, par 70, 5,980 m, laid along a dune line with old pines. Membership capped at 640. Currency USD in every language.
- **Audience:** local golfers who want an early round on a quiet, walkable course; prospective members and their guests.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, understated, and specific, reflecting a traditional seaside members' golf club. It speaks with unhurried authority about local routines, tee sheets, and course care without promotional rhetoric.

## History
Misora Links was founded in 1961 along the dune road at Westerby Point. The founders laid out an eighteen-hole, par-70 course measuring 5,980 metres through coastal sand ridges and stands of mature pines. From the earliest seasons, the club was conceived as a modest coastal home for local golfers, maintaining a strict membership cap of 640 to preserve quiet fairways and an unhurried pace of play.

Over six decades, the routing has remained faithful to the natural seaside terrain, with fairways shaped by prevailing sea winds and firm turf. The greens were carefully rebuilt in 2019 to safeguard drainage while retaining their original contours, and Monday closures have continued without interruption for dedicated turf maintenance. Life at the club has always centered around the simple routine of walking games, carry bags, and personal introductions at the starter's box.

Today, the morning sheet continues to open at 06:40 Tuesday through Sunday, sending four-balls out into the lifting dawn mist at ten-minute intervals. The clubhouse veranda on Starter's Lane remains a calm meeting ground where players return their cards and watch afternoon rounds depart along the dunes. Westerby Point preserves this quiet golf tradition unchanged, dedicated solely to the community living beside the course.

## How a season is booked
1. Write by 31 January to submit an application to the club office.
2. Walk the course with a member on scheduled Saturdays in February.
3. Meet with the committee on the first Tuesday of March for review.
4. Begin playing when the season opens 1 April, with the first sheet opening Thursday at 18:00.
