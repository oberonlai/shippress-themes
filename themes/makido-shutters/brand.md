# Makido Shutters: brand

## Name
Makido Shutters. A door and shutter contractor that measures the bay before anything is made.

## Location
Unit 4, Brannock Works, 22 Kestle Road, Wrenfield (fictional). Service area: within 60 km of Wrenfield.

## Founded
2009, by Aaron Makido, a shutter fitter who had spent a decade fitting other people's quotes.

## Story
Makido Shutters was founded in 2009 by Aaron Makido, a shutter fitter who had spent a decade fitting other people's quotes. He kept meeting openings the paperwork had not really described. A width written on a sheet was not the width of the bay, and the drum, the guide rails and the power supply were often left off the note. He started the firm on the line he still uses: nobody had measured the bay. The early work was roller shutters and shopfront grilles within reach of Wrenfield, and nothing was written down until he had stood in the opening.

In 2013 the workshop opened at Unit 4, Brannock Works, 22 Kestle Road, Wrenfield, and Joel Asante joined as lead fitter. Priya Venn had joined in 2012 to write the quotes and keep the schedule. In 2017 a fire-rated team was added, with Marta Kowal fitting fire-rated shutters. Sami Haddad joined in 2019 for motors and controls, and Lena Brandt joined in 2021 for aftercare and the service desk. In 2022 a second van went on the road. The company is still six people, two vans and one workshop.

The work has stayed the same. A job begins with a 45-minute site measure within 60 km of Wrenfield. The opening is measured, the guide rails, the drum and the power supply are photographed, and a written quote follows within three working days. Nothing is ordered until that quote has been read, and the person who measured the bay is on the crew that fits it. Vans are loaded at Brannock Works at 7:00, the first survey is at 7:30, and at 13:00 Priya sends that day's written quotes. A standard bay, up to 5 m wide, is fitted in one day. Fire-rated shutters take 20 working days to make and two days to fit, and an aftercare visit is booked at 12 months.

## Process
1. Request a site measure.
2. Site measure, 45 minutes.
3. Written quote within 3 working days.
4. Manufacture in 10–15 working days, or 20 for a fire-rated shutter.
5. Fitting in 1 day per standard bay up to 5 m wide, or 2 days if the opening is wider or fire-rated.
6. Aftercare visit at 12 months.

## A day at Makido Shutters
A normal working day follows the same routine. At 7:00 the two vans are loaded at Brannock Works. At 7:30 the first site measure starts. Late in the morning a fitting crew is on site, working one bay at a time. At 13:00 Priya sends the day's written quotes. In the afternoon there is a second visit or aftercare calls. At 16:30 the vans are back and tomorrow's doors are checked against the measure sheets.
