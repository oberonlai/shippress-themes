Makido Shutters measures, makes and fits industrial doors, roller shutters and shopfront grilles within 60 km of Wrenfield. Every job starts with a 45-minute site measure and a written quote within three working days, from a workshop at Brannock Works. The site records the doors, the lead times, the prices and the six people who do the work.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). A diagonal-split hero with a docked booking pill, a deep lens band that slides over the pinned hero, hairline notice boxes, a peeking door row, a parallax crew photograph and a price table. Fonts: Schibsted Grotesk, Albert Sans, Noto Sans TC and Noto Sans JP (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

== Installation ==

1. Download makido-shutters.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/makido-shutters.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Makido Shutters.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, sample posts with category and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Optional forms: the survey-day list section uses a dynamic form block (inc/shippress-forms.php). Without a form plugin it shows an email field with a submit button that opens the visitor's email app with the request ready to send (the theme sends nothing itself). With MailPoet (explicit form ID) or Mailchimp for WP, that plugin's form is shown instead.

Scroll effects: assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js (reveal, number count, parallax). They respect prefers-reduced-motion and show everything without JavaScript.

WooCommerce: support is declared; the theme works fully without it.
