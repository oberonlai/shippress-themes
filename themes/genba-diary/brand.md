# Genba Diary - brand brief

A site-diary app for construction crews, made in Rotterdam. Sample content in English first; Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German follow, localised for each market. All names, places, people, customers and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Genba Diary (the app), made by Genba Diary B.V. "Genba" is the place where the work happens, the site itself; the diary keeps the whole working day of a crew in one record.
- **Location:** Havenkade 41, 3024 AB Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional address). Prices in USD in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** founded in 2021 by Marta Kowalczyk, a former site manager who kept paper site diaries for years, and Samuel Adeyemi, an engineer. She had watched morning boards get wiped before the office could read them; they built one diary per site, written once at the gate and read by everyone.
- **Today:** a small team in Rotterdam, supporting active building projects. Pilots start every Monday; support runs Mon-Fri 06:30-18:00 CET and Sat 07:00-12:00, closed Sundays and public holidays.
- **Audience:** site managers, trade foremen and the commercial offices behind them, on concrete-frame, fit-out and roofing and facade crews working standard day shifts.
- **Tone:** calm, disciplined, practical. Plain, factual English about the daily routine on site; no promotional jargon. We never promise savings, percentages, awards or results, and we name no real media, people or brands.

## A day in the diary (the daily routine)
Every diary day has the same 6 sections: Morning board (a photo of the gate briefing board before the 07:00 shift), Crew & hours, Deliveries, Photos & marks, Weather & delays and Sign-off. The site manager signs off at 17:00; the rollout specialist reads each pilot day by 18:00 and leaves one note.

## Rollout (7-day pilot, identical on every page)
1. Day 1 - Request and call: request form, 30-minute call with the rollout lead.
2. Day 2 - Gate briefing: 45 minutes at the hoist gate before the 07:00 shift; the crew is added to the diary.
3. Days 3-6 - Diary days: the crew writes the six sections; the rollout specialist reads each day by 18:00 and leaves one note.
4. Day 7 - Review: one hour with the site manager; keep, extend or stop. Nothing is billed for the pilot.
