# Genba Diary

Genba Diary is a single-column longform landing theme for a construction site-diary app and its crew rollouts. A white ground with a light grey band, steel-blue ink (#2B3440 / #3A4654) and a small signal red (#EF3340 / #C8102E) used only on tiny blocks: a slanted three-slice photo triptych with a round badge and a white italic banner strip, a flush logo tile and two flush request cells in the header, rectangle-wipe labels with a short growing rule, and crew and diary-day cards with square icon tiles.

## Structure
- `patterns/front-*.php`: the home sections (hero, intro, crews, diary day, gate band, rollout, stories, plans, questions, request); `head-bar.php` and `foot-main.php` are the header and footer.
- `templates/`: block templates (front page, page, single, archive, category, tag, search, 404, posts page).
- `inc/i18n/<lang>.php`: theme strings; `demo-content*.json`: sample pages, posts, menu and screen URLs.
- Photos: `assets/images/` (AI-generated documentary photographs, high-key, GPL-2.0-or-later / CC0). See `readme.txt`.
- Motion: `assets/sp-scroll.css` and `assets/sp-scroll.js` (reveal, stagger and one parallax gate band; reduced-motion and no-JS show the final state).
- Fonts: Albert Sans (headings) and Inter Tight (body), both OFL-1.1, loaded through `theme.json`.

Design notes: `brand.md`, `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md`.

License: GPL-2.0-or-later.
