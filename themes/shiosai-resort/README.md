# Shiosai Resort

A block theme for a family seaside resort hotel. Design notes: `brand/design-spec.md`, brand brief: `brand.md`.

- **Signatures:** the beach-flag bar docked across the hero foot, the fixed tide-gauge chapter pager, the roman-numeral week grid with sea-tint marker phrases.
- **Hero:** a film-grain photograph of an adult lifeguard and a child on the tide line below the hotel; the headline and header use the ink colour on the pale sky for legibility (no text shadow).
- **Journal:** the latest stories as three 3:2 photo cards.
- **Fonts:** Figtree (display) and Nunito Sans (text), bundled under `assets/fonts` with their OFL licences; CJK fallback Noto Sans TC / Noto Sans JP is added with the language files.
- **Status:** home page complete; inner pages and languages follow.
- **Home sections (patterns):** `front-hero`, `front-welcome`, `front-rooms`, `front-week`, `front-packages`, `front-beach`, `front-families`, `front-checkdates`, `front-letter`, `front-journal`, plus `pager-home`.
- **Parts:** `header-over` (home, transparent over the photo), `header` (inner pages, white), `footer`.
- **Scroll effects:** `assets/sp-scroll.css|js` (reveal, stagger, parallax); the hero settle and the pager are theme CSS.
- **Forms:** `inc/shippress-forms.php` (newsletter slot works without plugins).
