Shiosai Resort is a calm, direct-booking family seaside resort theme designed for coastal hotel stays. Built with a high-key film-grain aesthetic, it features beachfront room showcases, seasonal packages, daily tide schedules, and dedicated family amenities. Every room and chapter faces the sea, offering clear pricing and a straightforward reservation process. A high-key film-grain tide-line photograph opens the home page under a thin lowercase headline, with a white four-cell beach-flag bar docked on its foot, a fixed tide-gauge chapter pager at the left, a sticky-media room list and a roman-numeral week. Figtree for headings, Nunito Sans for reading. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; scroll effects respect reduced motion.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download shiosai-resort.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/shiosai-resort.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Shiosai Resort.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, three stories with two categories and five tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Forms (optional): the Tide Letter section shows an email field and a submit button even without plugins (it opens your email app with the address; it is not a subscription until you send the message). MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP replace it when active. The contact form on the Check dates page uses Contact Form 7 or WPForms when active and falls back to a mailto button.

Languages: the sample content comes in English (demo-content.json); further languages follow in demo-content.<lang>.json. The importer uses the `shippress_demo_lang` option (set by ShipPress from its interface language), else the site language.

The resort, its people, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
