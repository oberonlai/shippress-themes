# Shiosai Resort - brand brief

A family seaside resort hotel on the Merrow coast with beachfront rooms and summer packages, selling direct instead of through booking platforms. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shiosai Resort (wordmark "shiosai"; "shiosai" is the sound of the tide coming in).
- **Location:** 1 Tideline Road, Port Merrow, on the Merrow coast (fictional, international-neutral). Four hundred metres of sand lie directly below the terrace. Port Merrow station is 12 minutes away by the hotel shuttle (09:30, 12:30, 15:30, 18:30); 62 parking spaces (EUR 12 per day) and 4 EV chargers.
- **Founded:** 1964 as a timber bathing pavilion; run by the Lund family since 1991; rebuilt in 2012 so that every one of the 48 rooms faces the sea.
- **Audience:** families with children from babies to teenagers who want to be by the water all week, and regional guests who book direct for a long weekend.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, warm, and practical, describing seaside days and daily resort routines in plain English without exaggeration or promotional claims. It avoids boastful phrasing and superlative promises, focusing instead on honest observations, daily coastal schedules, and thoughtful family hospitality. No awards, no "best", no guarantees about weather or the sea.
- **Contact:** +00 0000 0000 (bookings 08:00-20:00 in season), stay@shiosai-resort.example.

## History
In 1964, the property began as a simple timber bathing pavilion on the Merrow coast, offering shelter, changing cabins, and tea to families visiting the wide sands of Port Merrow. The pavilion stood at the edge of the dunes, serving swimmers and walkers who arrived by train to spend long summer days along the four hundred metres of open beach.

The Lund family took over the house in 1991, establishing it as a dedicated family seaside resort hotel. Through decades of welcoming returning guests across the summer months, the house developed its quiet rhythm of morning tide walks, family dinners on the terrace, and children's activities sheltered beneath the awnings.

In 2012, the hotel was carefully rebuilt from the ground up so that every one of its forty-eight rooms faces directly toward the sea. Today, second-generation owners Marisol and Tobias Lund manage the resort, preserving the founding spirit of straightforward hospitality where families can step straight from their doors onto the sand.
