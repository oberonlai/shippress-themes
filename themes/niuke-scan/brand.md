# Niuke Scan - brand brief

Handheld goods-in scanner and receiving app for small warehouse docks, sold as a monthly subscription. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Niuke Scan B.V. (fictional). Product: the **N1 scanner** and the **Count Sheet** app (phone, tablet, browser).
- **Location:** Dock Lane 12, 3089 Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional). Prices in EUR, excl. VAT, in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2021.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 06:00–18:00 CET (dock hours); closed weekends and Dutch public holidays.
- **Voice:** The voice is calm, practical, and grounded in the everyday reality of small warehouse docks. It avoids marketing hyperbole and outcome percentages, focusing on clear, specific descriptions of receiving work.

## History
Ilse Verhoeven spent nine years working as a dock supervisor at a parts distributor in Rotterdam. Every morning brought the same persistent friction: paper delivery notes marked with pencil ticks, delivery drivers waiting impatiently by the bay, and missing cartons discovered days later when stock lists failed to reconcile. Ilse recognized that once an uncounted pallet moved into the warehouse racking, resolving supplier discrepancies turned into an exhausting week of phone calls and disputed invoices.

In 2021, Ilse partnered with hardware engineer Tomás Reyes in Rotterdam to create a dedicated handheld receiving system designed specifically for small goods-in docks. Tomás engineered the rugged N1 handheld scanner to endure cold dock floors and repeated concrete drops, while software lead Amara Nwosu built the Count Sheet app to convert forwarded purchase orders into straightforward checklist counts across mobile, tablet, and browser interfaces. The goal was never bloated enterprise software, but a dependable tool to verify deliveries carton by carton before the driver departed.

Today, Niuke Scan B.V. operates from Dock Lane 12 in Rotterdam, supported by onboarding and customer support lead Lukas Brandt. From bicycle parts distributors in Ghent to tea importers in Hamburg and lighting wholesalers in Lyon, dock teams rely on the N1 scanner and Count Sheet every shift. Operating during dock hours from 06:00 to 18:00 CET, the company remains dedicated to making warehouse goods-in calm, legible, and accurate.

## N1 scanner (spec band)
Weight 280 g; battery 12-hour shift, 2.5 h to full; reading range 5 cm - 1.2 m; reads 1D / 2D barcodes, QR and GS1 carton labels; offline queue 2,000 scans; drop 1.5 m onto concrete; IP54; Wi-Fi 6 + Bluetooth 5.3; photo note camera for damage.

## How a delivery is counted
1. Forward the purchase order (email or CSV) so it becomes the expected list in Count Sheet.
2. Scan every carton at the door as the count fills in, carton by carton.
3. Flag short, extra or damaged cartons with a photo before the driver leaves.
4. Send the receiving report to the supplier and to your stock list at the end of the shift.
