# Akane House - brand brief

A neighbourhood gastropub that keeps one room open from the lunchtime pie to last orders. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Akane House. "Akane" is madder red, the colour of the old brick; the pub is otherwise a plain, international, modern room.
- **Location:** 3 Tanner's Row, Eastbridge (fictional). Tram line 2, stop Dye Works, three minutes' walk. Six bike stands, step-free entrance and toilet. Dogs welcome in the bar; children until 20:00.
- **Founded:** 2014, in the 1890s lodge of the old madder dye works, by Ruth Okafor and Tomas Brandt. In 2016 the back room became the Private Room.
- **People (seven):** Ruth Okafor (co-founder, licensee, front of house), Tomas Brandt (co-founder, head cook), Ines Varga (sous chef, the pies), Priya Sen (afternoons and pastry), Callum Reid (cellar and bar manager), Jonah Whitfield (bar, evenings), Ana Ferreira (bookings and the private room).
- **Audience:** neighbours, people working nearby at lunch, small groups and families at the weekend.
- **Tone:** plain, warm, unhurried, as if the writer also works the room. Specific about sittings, prices and who does what. No awards, no superlatives, no claim to be the best.

## A day in one room (the daily routine)
08:30 bread and stock - 11:00 the pie of the day goes in - 12:00 doors open - 15:00 lunch ends, the afternoon begins - 17:30 staff meal - 18:00 dinner - 21:30 kitchen closes - 22:45 last orders - 23:00 doors close - 23:30 the cellar is checked. The routine is the same shape most weeks.

## Hours and sittings (identical everywhere)
- Tue-Sat 12:00-23:00, Sun 12:00-18:00, Mon closed.
- 01 Lunch 12:00-15:00 (book) - 02 Afternoon 15:00-18:00 (walk-in) - 03 Dinner 18:00-21:30 (book) - 04 Bar 21:30-23:00, last orders 22:45 (walk-in). Sunday roast 12:00-17:00 in two booked seatings, 12:30 and 15:00; the Sunday bar ends at 18:00.
- Booking is one way: the Book page form or tables@akane-house.example. Tables are held 15 minutes. Groups over 8 use the Private Room. Phone 020 7946 0318, same-day requests only, 15:00-17:00.

## Prices (GBP, identical everywhere)
Pie of the day with mash, greens and gravy 16 - Soup, bread and butter 9 - Cheese on toast with pickles and a half pint 12 - Dinner two courses 32 / three courses 38 - Sunday roast 24 - House cask bitter, pint 5.40 - Private room set menu per person 45 / 55. A 12.5% service charge only for tables of eight or more.

## Private room
Seats 8-14. No hire fee Tuesday-Thursday; 150 minimum spend Friday-Saturday. Ask through the Book page.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Slash-numbered chapter heads ("02/ The sittings") with the heading in the left column and the text starting at exactly 50% (reference: Coffee Shimeno).
2. A ragged four-up tile row: photographs of different ratios, top-aligned, captions above, radius 0.
3. A text-free letterbox parallax band between chapters, then a near-black reservation band with one white link.
Supporting: a static grain overlay and arrow text links instead of buttons. The home first screen translates the reference's dark intro layer into a dark low-key photograph of the bar with a small centred wordmark and the four sittings along its foot.

## Photography
AI-generated photographs (Grok Build), one style: low-key documentary, dark interior, single warm practical light, aubergine and charcoal shadows, brass, glass and food, shallow depth of field, film grain, diverse adults, no text or logos. No cut-outs.

## Inner pages (all English slugs, identical in every locale)
- **The Day** (the-day): the four sittings as ragged tiles, one chapter per sitting, Sunday in three columns, "Can I book?" list. Band: morning prep.
- **Menu** (menu): dish rows per sitting, the full seven-item price table, allergen and service notes. Band: dinner pass.
- **The Bar** (bar): cask, taps, wine and soft, the six lines of the bar, walk-in always. Band: last orders.
- **About** (about): the building, 2014, the daily routine, four photographs. Band: the long room.
- **People** (people): seven people as ruled rows in three chapters. Band: the staff meal.
- **Private Room** (private-room): seats 8-14, set menus 45 / 55, no hire fee Tuesday-Thursday, 150 minimum spend Friday-Saturday, ask through the Book page. Band: the back room.
- **Visit** (visit): address, tram, bike stands, step-free access, hours table. Band: the front door.
- **Book a table** (book): the single way to book, the contact form slot with an email fallback, same-day calls 15:00-17:00. No reservation band.
- **The House Letter** (letter): what the monthly letter holds (first Thursday), newsletter form on the near-black panel; without a plugin the form opens the reader's email app and says they are not subscribed yet. Band: produce at the kitchen door.
- **Journal** (journal, posts page) with three stories: The Tuesday table, The pie that stayed, Last orders at 22:45; categories Stories and Kitchen.
- **Privacy** (privacy), linked from the footer.
