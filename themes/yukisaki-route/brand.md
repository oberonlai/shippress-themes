# Yukisaki Route: brand brief

Design plan row: b20-5 (delivery-route software for regional couriers, type landing). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other languages follow. The company, its people, address and figures are fictional. Copy never promises savings, time or fuel guarantees, awards or integrations.

## Concept
- **Name:** Yukisaki Route, a software desk that lays out the morning delivery round for small courier firms. One printed round sheet per driver, also on the phone.
- **Location:** Noorderhaven 18, 9000 Ghent, Belgium (fictional address). hello@yukisakiroute.example.
- **Founded:** 2019.
- **Audience:** dispatchers and owners of courier firms with 3 to 25 vans and 40 to 600 stops a day.
- **Tone:** A calm, matter-of-fact business voice that speaks with practical respect for daily courier operations and yard routines. It avoids marketing hyperbole, technical buzzwords, and unsubstantiated claims, focusing purely on clear routing, local dispatcher knowledge, and dependable morning round sheets.

## Story
Yukisaki Route was founded in 2019 in Ghent by Ines Vermeulen and Tomasz Zielinski. Ines had spent eight years running courier dispatch desks, managing the morning rush where parcels arrived in bulk and drivers needed their routes before dawn. She knew firsthand that regional courier firms did not need complicated enterprise platforms; they needed an accurate, dependable way to turn daily address lists into clear delivery sequences.

Tomasz Zielinski brought the software engineering experience needed to build a dedicated desk for small fleets. Together, they focused on the specific needs of depots operating between 3 and 25 vans handling 40 to 600 stops each day. They built a system that puts stops into road order while leaving the final decisions with the dispatcher, who understands local roadworks, tight delivery windows, and customer gate codes better than any automated algorithm.

Based at Noorderhaven 18 in Ghent, Yukisaki Route BV supports courier desks across Europe with early-morning support starting at 05:30 CET. The company continues to champion paper-first dispatch, delivering a calm morning workflow where every driver leaves the yard with a clear printed round sheet in hand and the same route accessible on their phone.

## Fixed facts (identical on every page and language)
- Trial: 14 days, no card, up to 25 vans, ends by itself, data export on request.
- Fit: 3 to 25 vans, 40 to 600 stops a day. Not yet for instant same-day dispatch, long-haul trunking or more than 25 vans.
- Prices (EUR, excl. VAT):
- Trial, 14 days: €0. Fourteen days of full access for up to 25 vans, with no credit card required and automatic expiration.
- Yard plan, 3–5 vans: €24 per van / month. Standard monthly plan for fleets with 3 to 5 vans, excl. VAT.
- Depot plan, 6–25 vans: €19 per van / month. Standard monthly plan for regional fleets with 6 to 25 vans, excl. VAT.
- Extra depot: €40 per depot / month. Additional depot location under the same courier account, excl. VAT.
- Guided set-up morning (optional): €180 once. Optional one-time assisted onboarding morning with our set-up team, excl. VAT.
- Flow:
- Day 1: Import addresses and print your first round sheet.
- Days 2–3: Run the printed sheet beside your usual delivery plan.
- Days 4–7: Rely on the round sheets alone across your yard.
- Days 8–14: Evaluate the workflow and decide on a subscription.
- Stories (fictional firms): Kade Couriers (Ghent, 6 vans, 210 stops); Valmara Express (Porto, 11 vans, 380 stops); Norrby Bud (Malmö, 4 vans, 90 stops); Lindenhof Kurier (Freiburg, 14 vans, 460 stops).
