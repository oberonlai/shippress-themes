Family workshops crossing the audit threshold, charities with restricted funds, and groups facing a first consolidation needed a firm that would still go to the site. The office is at Wapping Quay Studios, 3 Gaol Ferry Steps, Bristol BS1, United Kingdom. The sentence the firm still works to is the one on the door of every engagement: we go and count what is there, then we sign.

Independent, here, means the partner the client meets is the partner who signs the opinion. The file is not passed to a department the client has never seen. Two rules apply on every appointment. We attend every material stock count, any day by arrangement, because a count we did not observe is not a count we will rely on. A partner signs every opinion personally, and the management letter goes out with that signature. The firm is 26 people, three of them partners.

The practice covers company statutory audit, group audit, charity independent examination, charity statutory audit, and grant assurance reports written to a funder's procedures. Fees are in pounds sterling, exclusive of VAT, and fixed in the engagement letter, with 40 percent due on planning and 60 percent on signing. When records arrive by week 4, the opinion is signed within 12 weeks of year-end. We reply within 2 working days. A planning call is 30 minutes, and it is free.

## Fees (GBP, ex VAT; identical on every page and in every language)

2. Charity statutory audit: from £4,800. ex VAT, fixed in the engagement letter. For a charity over the audit threshold, including restricted funds and the trustees' report.
3. Company statutory audit (turnover up to £15m): from £6,500. ex VAT, fixed in the engagement letter. Statutory audit for an owner-managed company with turnover up to £15m.
4. Group audit, parent + up to 3 subsidiaries: from £11,800. ex VAT, fixed in the engagement letter. A parent and up to 3 subsidiaries, including a first consolidation.
5. Grant assurance report (per funder): from £1,900. ex VAT, fixed in the engagement letter. One report per funder, timed to the grant claim and the year-end.
6. Stock count attendance (per site, per day): from £780. ex VAT, fixed in the engagement letter. Per site, per day, any day by arrangement.
7. Pre-audit readiness review: from £950. ex VAT, fixed in the engagement letter. A review before appointment, so the first audit starts from a known file.

## Process (5 stages; the home page shows four chapters that map onto them)
1. Appointment: the engagement letter is agreed two weeks before year-end, naming the opinion, the sites and the fee.
2. Planning: a risk meeting, then the document list the fieldwork will use.
3. Observation: we attend the stock count at year-end wherever the balance is material.
4. Testing and findings: fieldwork runs in weeks 4 to 9, and closes with a findings meeting.
5. Signed opinion: a partner signs, the management letter goes with the opinion, and both are issued by week 12.
