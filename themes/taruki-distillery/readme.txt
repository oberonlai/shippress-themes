Taruki Distillery is an artisanal WordPress theme crafted for independent distilleries, tasting rooms, and craft beverage workshops. Rooted in calm, editorial typography and warm documentary imagery, it features an unhurried process timeline, transparent price ledgers, and direct visit booking. Two flat colour strips over a charcoal navigation row, a photograph flush left beside the headline with a six-dot batch timeline, a sticky still photograph beside six ruled process chapters, an inset still-room photograph with stage dots and a frosted botanicals photograph. DM Serif Display for headings, Work Sans for reading. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download taruki-distillery.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/taruki-distillery.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Taruki Distillery.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, journal entries with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Forms: the contact and newsletter sections use a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php) that shows Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP forms when one is active, and otherwise a plain email field or a mailto button. No plugin is required.

The distillery, its people, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
