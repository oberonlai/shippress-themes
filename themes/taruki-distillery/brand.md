# Taruki Distillery - brand brief

Craft gin distillery and tasting room in Larchmere, a fictional harbour town with citrus groves on the hill above it. Tours, tastings and bottle sales. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Taruki Distillery. A small independent distillery that steams local citrus peel through a copper still.
- **Location:** 14 Quarry Lane, Larchmere (fictional). Email hello@taruki-distillery.example, phone +00 1234 5678 (placeholder). Euro prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2016, by Mira Okafor, around a single 300-litre copper pot still named Ottilie.
- **People:** Mira Okafor (founder and head distiller), Tomasz Wren (still operator), Inès Dubreuil (botanical forager), Kenji Halvorsen (tasting room lead), Ana Lucía Ferro (tour guide), Ruth Abernathy (bottling and labels), Samir Haddad (citrus grower, Hillside Grove partner).
- **Audience:** adults who want to see how a craft spirit is made and taste it at the source: couples, small groups, visiting friends, gin drinkers who read labels. Also craft distilleries and small breweries with tasting rooms looking for a site of their own.
- **Tone:** calm, measured, documentary. Quiet specifics about copper, steam and botanicals. No awards, press, ratings, visitor counts or health claims.
- **A day (routine):** the still house starts at 8:00 when Tomasz lights the steam; Mira tastes the run at the spout through the morning; the tasting room opens at 11:00 (Wed-Sun), tours leave at 11:30, 14:00 and 16:00, and Ruth labels and corks bottles on the bench until closing at 18:00. The still keeps running on Monday and Tuesday while the room is closed.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- One batch = 9 days, 420 bottles, one 300-litre copper pot still named Ottilie.
- Six batch stages, always in this order: 01 Botanicals, 02 Steep, 03 Still, 04 Cut, 05 Rest, 06 Bottle.
- Tasting room Wed-Sun 11:00-18:00 (Mon-Tue closed). Tours Wed-Sun at 11:30, 14:00, 16:00; 60 minutes; max 12 guests; age 18+.
- Prices: Still-Room Tour + three-gin tasting (60 min) EUR 32; Tour + Cut Tasting at the spout, with G&T (90 min) EUR 48; Botanical Blending Workshop, take home 50 cl (3 h) EUR 115; Private group tour, up to 10 guests EUR 390; Larchmere Citrus Peel Gin 70 cl, 43% EUR 44; Navy Cut Gin 50 cl, 57% EUR 49.
- Booking has one route only: the /book/ page (form slot with a mailto fallback). Cancellations 48 h ahead.
- Nine botanicals: juniper, Larchmere lemon peel, bitter orange, coriander, angelica, orris, bay, pink pepper, sea fennel.

## Stories

- "Peel from the Hillside Grove" (2019): partnering with Samir Haddad for hand-harvested citrus peel.
- "Why Ottilie runs slowly" (2023): gentle copper temperatures keep the delicate oils.

## Signature techniques (from the reference, carried to inner pages)
1. Stacked flat strips (copper next-tour strip, caramel batch strip) above a centred charcoal nav with a 4px grey underline on the current item.
2. A wide inset photograph at 87% of the width on charcoal with a six-dot pager (the six batch stages).
3. A frosted, desaturated photograph fading in from the right behind a bottle photograph and long-leading text, with a caps link.
4. Support: a 20px charcoal rule under every full-bleed photo band.
First screen concept: a film-grain photograph of the distiller tasting at the still's spout, botanicals drying behind her, beside the headline; a horizontal batch timeline with six dots along the foot, the copper dot marking today's stage.
