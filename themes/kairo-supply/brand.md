## History

Kairo Supply was founded in 1987 as an independent family trade counter at Unit 4, Northgate Trade Park on Mill Lane in Northbridge. From the beginning, the counter was organized around a clear working rule: stock on the shelf matters more than catalogue promises. The business was established to give local electricians immediate access to essential cable, conduit fittings, and distribution gear before heading to site each morning.

In 2015, the founder's son and daughter took over management of the counter, keeping the second-generation business focused on trade contractors. Rather than expanding into retail showrooms, the counter doubled down on trade essentials, keeping 4,200 product lines stocked in depth and maintaining 180 drums of cable ready to be cut to length while customers wait.

Today, the counter opens at 06:30 every weekday morning to serve domestic firms, commercial maintenance teams, solar installers, and panel builders. With same-day collections, scheduled morning deliveries, and secure after-hours collection lockers, Kairo Supply continues to ensure contractors have the right materials on hand before the van is loaded.

## How an account opens

- 1 Apply: Complete a 10-minute application form online or at the counter with your company registration and one trade reference.
- 2 Check: Our credit team reviews your application and returns a credit decision within 2 working days.
- 3 Trade: Receive your account number by e-mail with an opening credit limit of €2,500 and terms of 30 days from end of month.

Up to 5 named card holders per account, each with their own PIN. Same-day cut-off 14:00; orders confirmed by 17:00 delivered next morning. Stock: 4,200 lines, 180 cable drums.
