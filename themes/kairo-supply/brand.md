# Kairo Supply - brand brief

Electrical wholesaler trade counter in Ostwick (fictional): a trade-account landing page theme. Sample content in English first; zh-TW, ja, fr and de follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kairo Supply. International and modern; the wordmark stays in Latin capitals in every language.
- **Location:** Unit 3, Brennan Yard, 41 Mill Road, Ostwick (fictional). EUR prices, shown ex VAT, in every language.
- **History:** Ruth Abernethy opened Kairo Supply in 2004 inside a former tyre depot on Mill Road in Ostwick. Recognising that local electrical contractors were losing hours each morning waiting at broad builders' merchants, she established a dedicated wholesale counter focused strictly on speed, stocked essentials, and trade service. From the start, the counter opened its doors at 06:30 so electricians could pick up cable and accessories before arriving on site.
- **Today:** independent electrical wholesaler trade counter with a seven-person team, founded 2004.
- **Audience:** electrical contractors, shopfitters and sole traders who need cable and fittings early in the morning, cut and picked for the job.
- **Tone:** Calm, specific, and professional English focused entirely on practical trade details and counter efficiency. Direct and grounded tone without marketing slogans, artificial superlatives, or exaggerated claims.

## Account (identical on every page and language)
- Opened in two working days; credit up to €5,000 at opening on 30-day terms from statement; review after 3 months; up to 5 named buyers per account.
1. Apply online or at the counter in 10 minutes with your company name, VAT number, and one trade reference.
2. Credit check completed the same day.
3. Counter card and up to 5 named buyers set up by the next working day.
4. First order placed on account.

## Price list (EUR, ex VAT)
- Trade account: 30-day terms, credit up to €5,000, up to 5 named buyers — **No fee**
- Cable cut to length: Any length from 5 m, cut while you wait (≤ 10 min) — **€0 per cut**
- Job pack: Picked and labelled by job; pick list by 15:00, ready 06:30 — **€8 per pack**
- Van delivery: Within 20 km, same day if ordered by 11:00 — **€15 per drop**
- Cable drum deposit: Refunded in full when the drum comes back — **€40 per drum**
- Special order: Non-stock lines, 3–5 working days — **€0 · 20% deposit**

## Range (6 families)
Cable & flex - Containment & trunking - Wiring accessories - Distribution & protection - Lighting & controls - EV charging & renewables. Cable in standard sizes 1.0-35 mm². Stocked lines for immediate collection; special orders 3-5 working days.
