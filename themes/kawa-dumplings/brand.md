# Kawa Dumpling House — brand

## Name and place
Kawa Dumpling House ("kawa" is the house's short name) is a fictional soup dumpling house with three branches in Port Aldren, a fictional harbour city. Head kitchen: 12 Harbour Street, Port Aldren. Phone +00 555 0140. Email hello@kawa-dumplings.example.

## Founded
2014, by Lena Kowal and Samuel Adeyemi, with one glass-walled kitchen on Harbour Street.

## Audience
Guests who want a quick, exact meal folded in front of them (dine in, takeaway or a frozen box for home) and who plan ahead for lunch or dinner pickup. Buyers of the theme: dumpling houses and small multi-branch restaurants with takeaway and frozen sales.

## Tone
The voice is calm, plain and exact. It names the branch, the hour, the basket and the gram weight, and avoids superlatives, awards or promises. Quiet warmth, short sentences, one idea per line.

## Story
Kawa Dumpling House was founded in 2014 by Lena Kowal and Samuel Adeyemi on Harbour Street in Port Aldren. Lena focused on the dough wrappers and the rhythm of the eighteen folds, while Samuel developed the broth that turns to liquid inside the steamer. From the first day of service, the folding bench stood behind a clear glass wall, so guests queuing on the pavement could watch the flour, the rolling and the pleats.

In 2016 the glass wall was widened across the whole Harbour Street kitchen. In 2018 Kawa opened a second counter at Stall 21 in Linden Covered Market, with fourteen seats for market shoppers. Frozen packs followed in 2020, for steaming at home, and in 2022 a takeaway window opened at Unit 4 in Station Arcade for morning commuters. In 2024 the Order ahead page replaced the phone queue.

Across the three branches every soup dumpling follows the same discipline set in 2014. Each wrapper weighs six grams, the filling with broth jelly weighs fifteen grams, and the dumpling receives eighteen folds before seven minutes in the bamboo steamer: 21 g in all. Guests eat in, collect an order ahead at the window, or take frozen boxes home on Friday.

## Process (the single way to order ahead)
1. Choose from the menu or the frozen list.
2. Send it on the Order ahead page by 11:00 for lunch pickup 12:00–14:00, or by 16:00 for dinner pickup 17:30–20:00.
3. Collect and pay at the window of the branch you picked. For 100 or more dumplings, give 48 hours' notice.
