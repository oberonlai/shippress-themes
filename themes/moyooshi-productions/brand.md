Studio and warehouse: Unit 4, Harbour Works, 18 Quay Road, Port Ellery (fictional, international neutral). Email brief@moyooshi.example (the recipient of every brief and sign-up request).

Port Ellery. The studio and the cable-and-case warehouse share one building; crews load out from there at 06:00 on show days and work in halls across the region.

Calm, disciplined and understated: the practical authority of seasoned technical producers who prefer operational precision to marketing rhetoric. Concrete timelines, named people and plain logistics, never superlatives.

## Story
Moyooshi Productions was founded in 2014 by executive producer Mara Lindqvist with a commitment to calm, disciplined stage and space production for live corporate gatherings. Operating from Unit 4 at Harbour Works on 18 Quay Road in Port Ellery, the studio was established to bring structural rigour and technical clarity to temporary event halls. From the outset the team rejected last-minute improvisation, anchoring every production in detailed advance planning, exhaustive line checks and transparent technical documentation.

Over the past decade the company has produced 214 events, from sales kick-offs and award ceremonies to technical product reveals. To support productions of varying scale without compromising oversight, Moyooshi keeps a team of 38 crew on call, spanning rigging, lighting design, sound engineering and stage management. Whether configuring a product reveal for 250 guests or plenary staging for a 1,600-seat hall, the studio applies the same standards for structural safety and cue precision.

Today Moyooshi delivers end-to-end production across a typical six-week timeline from discovery brief to show night: concept and budget, build and rehearsal, show day, overnight strike and a written report.

## A day (the routine on show day)
Every show day follows the same daily routine, written on the run sheet: 06:00 Load-in, 10:00 Line check, 14:00 Rehearsal, 18:30 Doors, 19:00 Show, 23:30 Strike. Flight cases arrive and rigging begins at 06:00; sound, lighting and signal paths are verified by 10:00; presenters rehearse against the real cues at 14:00; the room is set to entry levels for doors at 18:30; the show is called live from 19:00 and the strike crew clears and hands the hall back by 23:30.

## Process
Brief (week 0) → Concept and budget (weeks 1–2) → Build and rehearse (weeks 3–5) → Show day → Strike and report (+5 days).
