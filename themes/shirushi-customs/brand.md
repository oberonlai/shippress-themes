# Shirushi Customs - brand brief

A small customs broker at a fictional port. Sample content in English (stage 1: home page and notes); Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German follow in stage 2, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shirushi Customs. "Shirushi" is the Japanese word for a mark or a stamp, the mark a declaration receives when it is lodged and released; the desk is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** Shed 11, North Quay, Port Ardale (fictional). Originals and samples are taken in at Door 4. USD prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** founded in 2009 by Emiko Hart, a licensed customs broker who spent twelve years at a freight forwarder. She opened the desk for workshops and small factories that import components and samples a few times a month and cannot justify a customs department.
- **Today:** seven people, one named broker per shipment, four kinds of declaration, five stages from documents to release.
- **Audience:** owners and buyers at small manufacturers (joinery, bicycle frames, skin care, audio) who import occasionally and want to be told plainly what is needed and what it costs.
- **Tone:** calm, exact, plain. Short sentences; say what is checked, by whom and when. No guarantees of clearance times, no savings figures, no awards, no press, no real people or brands.

01 Documents - 02 Classification - 03 Declaration - 04 Duty - 05 Release.
