# Kura Wine - brand brief

Independent wine merchant and cellar in Rotterdam, Netherlands. Sample content in English at stage 1 (Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow). All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kura Wine. A small merchant that brings in few shipments and sells each with the grower's story.
- **Location:** shop and cellar at Kelderstraat 9, 3011 KD Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional). Phone +31 10 555 0142, cellar@kurawine.example. EUR in every language.
- **History:** founded in 2014 by buyer Ines Varga in a quiet cellar space. The premise: stay small and independent, avoid distributor catalogues, buy directly from growers whose vineyards and cellar practice we know first-hand. Four shipments a year (Feb, May, Sept, Nov) instead of hundreds of permanent references.
- **Today:** a team of four plus the growers in the Jura, Douro and Burgenland. Every case arrives sealed in straw, rests ten days at 13 °C on the rack, is tasted by three (Ines, Tomas, Ruth) and is opened on a Friday at 18:00 at the long wooden table.
- **Audience:** adults who want to know who made what is in the glass: home cooks, small restaurants, people building a cellar of 20 to 60 bottles.
- **Tone:** calm, unhurried and precise, like an experienced merchant who respects both grower and drinker. Grounded in cellar work, harvest and provenance; no marketing flourish, no awards, no ratings, no press.

## People (fictional)
