# Kaname Watches - brand brief

Small mechanical-watch shop in Ghent, Belgium with one glass counter and a short list. Sample content in English first (Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow in stage 2). All names, places, people and prices are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kaname Watches (wordmark `KANAME`, sub-line `WATCHES`). "Kaname" is the pin that holds a folding fan together; the shop is the one fixed point around which a short list turns.
- **Location:** Klokstraat 12, 9000 Ghent, Belgium (fictional). EUR prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2012 by Ilse Verhoeven.
- **Hours:** Tue–Fri 11:00–18:00 (Thu until 20:00), Sat 10:00–17:00, Sun–Mon closed.
- **Voice:** Our tone is calm, precise, and understated, speaking with the measured clarity of an experienced independent watchmaker. We present mechanical specifications and workshop standards honestly, avoiding marketing superlatives, hollow prestige claims, or sales pressure.

## Story

Kaname Watches was founded in 2012 by Ilse Verhoeven in Ghent, Belgium. Operating from Klokstraat 12, the shop was established as an antidote to crowded showrooms and endless display cases: a single room with a single glass counter, focused entirely on the quiet appreciation of mechanical timepieces. From the beginning, Ilse set the rule of the short list—never presenting more than twelve watches at any one time, allowing every case, dial, and caliber to be understood in full detail.

Every watch in our collection is chosen one by one and thoroughly tested before it reaches the counter. At our workshop bench, watchmaker Tomasz Wrona inspects movements, performs precise regulation, and manages full overhauls with meticulous care. Rather than chasing transient trends or mass manufacturing, we collaborate with independent workshops and partner artisans, including leather strap maker Rafael Ortega in Porto, ensuring each watch is built to be worn, maintained, and treasured for decades.

Today, the shop lists five carefully vetted mechanical watches. We welcome visitors by appointment for forty-five unhurried minutes, presenting three watches on a dark felt tray tailored to the wearer's wrist and preferences. There is never pressure to purchase; once a client selects a piece, we hold it for seven days to allow for calm reflection. Supported by insured European delivery, two-year warranties on new timepieces, and twelve months on pre-owned and serviced watches, Kaname Watches remains dedicated to honest, enduring mechanical horology.

## Audience
People who want one good mechanical watch and prefer to try it on their own wrist, in a quiet room, with a person who knows the movement; first-time mechanical-watch buyers, owners looking for a service bench, and collectors of small cases.

## A day at the shop
Tomasz opens the bench at 10:00 and checks the overnight rates on the timing machine. Amara sets out three watches on the felt tray for the 11:00 visit. Jonas packs the insured parcels before the courier at 16:00; on Thursday the counter stays open until 20:00.
