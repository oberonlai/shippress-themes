# Hoshi Wealth Partners — brand brief

Design plan row: b7-10 (wealth management firm; type business). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other four languages follow. All names, places and people are fictional.

## Concept
Hoshi Wealth Partners opened in Seattle in 2009, named for founding partner Naomi Hoshi-Clarke. She wanted families to sit with an adviser who was paid only by them. The office is at 1418 Western Avenue, Suite 600, and it has stayed small on purpose: seven people, one table, and about one hundred forty households. The firm does planning, retirement income, investment management, and tax and estate coordination. The work is written down before money moves.

Fee-only means the invoice is the only payment. There are no commissions, no product fees, and no performance fees. The firm does not manufacture products, and it does not take a fee that rises when a portfolio rises. Portfolios are built from low-cost index funds and rebalanced by a written rule. Each household has a written investment policy that says what the money is for and what would make the firm change course. Custody stays at an independent custodian, named in the engagement and never owned by the firm.

Naomi works with three-generation families and retirees. Tomás Ferreira writes investment policy. Priya Raman coordinates tax and estate work. Henrik Aalto sits with business owners. Grace Mwangi, Samuel Ortiz-Bell, and Lena Fischer complete the table. A first meeting is sixty minutes, free, on Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday, at the office or by video. Discovery takes about two weeks. A written plan is presented within six weeks of that first meeting. After that, the household receives a quarterly report, two review meetings a year, and a yearly update of the written policy. The office is open Monday to Friday, 8:30 to 17:30 Pacific, with Thursday meetings until 19:00 by appointment.

## Fees (USD)
- Introductory meeting (60 minutes): $0. Tuesday to Thursday, at the Seattle office or by video. No pitch.
- Written financial plan (one-off, three meetings): $4,800. Presented within six weeks of the first meeting.
- Ongoing planning retainer (billed quarterly): $7,200 / year. Quarterly report and two review meetings a year. Billed quarterly in arrears.
- Investment management, first $2M: 0.80% / year. No commissions, no product fees, and no performance fees. Custody is at an independent custodian.
- Investment management, above $2M: 0.50% / year (minimum $9,600 / year). The same written investment policy applies above and below $2M. The minimum annual fee is $9,600.
- Hourly project advice: $380 / hour. For a defined piece of work outside a retainer.
No commissions, no product fees, no performance fees; custody at an independent custodian.

## Process
1. Introductory meeting: 60 minutes, free, Tuesday to Thursday, at the Seattle office or by video.
2. Discovery: about two weeks, documents gathered, and one working session.
3. Written plan: presented within six weeks of the first meeting.
4. Ongoing: a quarterly report, two review meetings a year, and the written investment policy updated yearly.
