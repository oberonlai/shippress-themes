== Description ==

Hoshi Wealth Partners is a block theme for a fee-only wealth-management firm. The home page is told through its advisers: a mixed-type headline over two framed portraits, ruled partner rows, family stories, and a private introductory meeting. It declares WooCommerce support and works fully without WooCommerce installed. The home page opens on a three-line headline that mixes EB Garamond and Heebo capitals and overlaps a landscape photograph and a square portrait bleeding off the right edge. A letters list, a prologue with a sea-pine tile, ruled partner rows on a dark band with a sticky chapter card, a parallax strip, a framed family story and the introductory meeting panel follow. EB Garamond for headings, Heebo for reading. Works without WooCommerce.
