# Kohaku Brasserie

A block theme for a city brasserie that keeps one room working from lunch to the late sitting. Low-key photography, Fraunces and Literata, one stone-and-espresso palette with a single lamp-amber accent.

Signature techniques (every page): the service-day hero with a timeline hairline, the pinned letterbox window, the plate-rim ellipse. Dated hairline ledger for notes.

See `brand.md` for the brief and `brand/design-spec.md` for the design notes. Sample content: `demo-content.json`. All names, places and prices are fictional.

## Pages

Home, The Room, The Day, Menus & Prices, The Kitchen, Book a Table, Visit, The Monday letter (newsletter), and Notes (posts page). The main menu links to Home, The Room, The Day, Menus, The Kitchen and Notes; Book is the single call to action in the header, and Visit and the newsletter are linked from the footer. Booking and the newsletter use the shared form slot: a form plugin when one is active, otherwise a disclosed mailto fallback.

## Fonts

Fraunces (display) and Literata (text), both OFL-1.1, bundled in `assets/fonts`.
