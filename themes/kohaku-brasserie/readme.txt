A serene WordPress theme designed for Kohaku Brasserie, capturing the calm rhythm of a working city dining room from lunch to last orders. Features low-key documentary photography, refined serif typography, and signature pacing devices including a service-day timeline hairline and pinned letterbox windows. Built for booking-led hospitality with full-width price ledgers, multi-sitting schedules, and clean responsive layouts across five locales.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download kohaku-brasserie.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kohaku-brasserie.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kohaku Brasserie.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the notes with their categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. The theme works as a plain site without WooCommerce.

Forms: the newsletter sections use a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active it shows your form; without a plugin it shows an email field and a button that opens your email app with the address filled in (you are not subscribed until you send that email). Booking and contact pages show a Contact Form 7 or WPForms form when one is active, otherwise a button that opens your email app. The theme sends nothing itself.

The brasserie, its people, its address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
