# Kohaku Brasserie - brand brief

A city brasserie that keeps one room working from lunch through the late sitting. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kohaku Brasserie.
- **Location:** 14 Arcade Row, Old Exchange Quarter, Lowmarket (a fictional city). Email bookings@kohaku-brasserie.example, phone +00 20 7000 0414. EUR (€) prices in every language, service included.
- **Founded:** 2014.
- **History:** opened in 2014 by Inès Marchetti (front of house) and Tomasz Lindqvist (head chef) as a single unhurried room that does not close between lunch and dinner. In 2019 eight counter seats were added at the pass, so guests can watch the plating.
- **Today:** 64 seats in the main room, 8 counter seats at the pass, 8 bar seats, and the Lantern Room (14 seated / 20 standing) for groups.
- **Audience:** neighbours and office colleagues who want a dependable set lunch, people who work or meet in the quiet afternoon hours, and couples and small groups for the late sitting.
- **Tone:** calm, unhurried, precise. Light, times, plates and prices rather than promotion. No guarantees, statistics, awards, press or real brands.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Hours: Tue-Sat 11:30-00:00, Sun 11:30-17:00, Mon closed.
- Service day: Lunch 11:30-14:30; the quiet hours (afternoon plates, coffee) 14:30-18:00; Dinner 18:00-22:00; the late sitting 22:00-00:00 (kitchen last orders 23:15).
- Seats: 64 main room, 8 counter at the pass, 8 bar, Lantern Room 14 seated / 20 standing.
- Prices (EUR, service included): Set lunch, two courses €28; Set lunch, three courses €34; Afternoon plate €16; Dinner à la carte, mains from €24; Late-sitting menu, three courses €52; Counter menu at the pass, six courses €78. Lantern Room group menu €62 (Private Room page only); wine by the glass from €7 (Wine & Bar page only).
- Booking: up to 60 days ahead; confirmation by email within 4 hours during opening times; table held 15 minutes; groups of 7 or more book the Lantern Room; no booking needed for 4 or fewer in the quiet hours.

## A day in the room (daily routine)
Morning: the kitchen starts at 07:00 with stocks and bread, front of house sets linen at 10:30. 11:30 the doors open for lunch and the daily routine of two- and three-course set menus. 14:30 the room turns quiet: afternoon plates, coffee, tables for work or a slow meeting. 18:00 dinner service begins, 22:00 the late sitting starts under candlelight, kitchen last orders at 23:15, closing at midnight.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Service-day hero: a full-bleed low-key photograph of one corner table that cross-fades through lunch, afternoon and late sitting, with a bracketed counter and a one-pixel service-day hairline whose amber marker steps along the three times (sasa first screen).
2. Pinned letterbox window: a wide 2.4:1 photograph stays under the header while ruled chapter rows scroll past and each chapter brings its own photograph (osteria-ao course block, sasa pinned scenes).
3. Plate-rim ellipse: a large pale ellipse wider than the viewport crosses the stone ground like the rim of a plate, with a centred statement and one photograph offset below it (osteria-ao about block).
4. Supporting: a dated hairline ledger for notes and archives.
