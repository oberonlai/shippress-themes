# Awa Taproom - brand brief

Small brewery taproom in Bellingham, Washington. Sample content in English (stage 1: home); Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Awa Taproom. A five-barrel brewery with a room built around its taps; the beer is made in the room you drink it in.
- **Location:** 214 Water Street, Bellingham, WA 98225, USA (fictional). USD prices in every language.
- **History:** founded in 2019. The voice is calm, unhurried, and grounded in the physical reality of a working small-scale brewhouse. It speaks with specific, practical clarity, avoiding marketing hype, awards, or ornate embellishments.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, unhurried, and grounded in the physical reality of a working small-scale brewhouse. It speaks with specific, practical clarity, avoiding marketing hype, awards, or ornate embellishments.
- **Audience:** neighbours and after-work regulars, small groups booking a table, visitors who want a short, honest tap list.

## Story
Ines Halvorsen and Tomas Achebe founded Awa Taproom in 2019 at 214 Water Street in Bellingham, Washington. After years of working in commercial production cellars, they set out to establish a small, self-contained neighborhood brewery where beer could be brewed and enjoyed in the exact same room. They installed a compact five-barrel brewhouse and six temperature-controlled fermentation tanks directly behind the bar rail, creating an open brewhouse where guests can see the kettle and cold cellar from their stools.

From the beginning, the taproom was designed around four draft lines and a dining area of thirty-eight seats, dividing the space between twelve bar stools and twenty-six seats across seven wooden tables. Ines and Tomas chose to brew exclusively on Tuesday and Thursday mornings, producing unhurried batches that transfer directly into dedicated serving tanks behind the bar. By keeping the operation compact, the brewery avoids delivery trucks, distribution warehouses, and compromised freshness, pouring beer just metres from where it fermented.

Today, the taproom remains grounded in calm hospitality, daily draught line maintenance, and steady cellar routines. The tap list rotates only when a tank runs empty, offering clean lagers, farmhouse saisons, aromatic pale ales, and roasty dark porters brewed from regional grain and whole hops. Whether guests stop by for a single pour after work or reserve a table for the evening, Awa Taproom serves its Bellingham community with quiet patience and honest craftsmanship.
