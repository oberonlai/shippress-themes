# Nuno Workwear: brand

## Who

Phone +31 10 555 0142, email shop@nunoworkwear.example. Currency EUR. Hours: Mon–Fri 07:00–18:00, Sat 08:00–13:00, Sun closed.

## History
Nuno Workwear began in 2009 as an independent repair counter at Rijnhaven Works in Rotterdam, mending torn work trousers, replacing broken zips, and re-proofing weather-beaten canvas jackets for dockers and local tradespeople. From the start, our focus was keeping working garments in daily service rather than replacing them when seams or knees wore thin under hard shifts.

In 2014, we expanded beyond bench repairs to introduce our own trade catalogue of durable garments, stocking hard-wearing pieces in cotton duck, waxed cotton, and heavy twill. We selected each fabric and pattern specifically for daily trades, offering dependable fits across sizes XS to 4XL and multiple leg lengths so crews could work comfortably without binding or tearing.

In 2021, we set up our dedicated back-room embroidery workshop on Kadeweg to stitch crew names and trade marks directly onto garments before delivery. Today, our workshop and shop continue to fit, finish, and mend clothing for working teams across Rotterdam, opening early every weekday morning before the first shift begins.
