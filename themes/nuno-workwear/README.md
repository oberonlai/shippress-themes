# Nuno Workwear

Nuno Workwear is a documentary, sans-serif WordPress block theme designed for workwear and safety clothing shops, repair workshops, and trade outfitters. Built around a split-navigation layout, dashed column guides, and high-contrast ink-and-ground section rhythms, it operates smoothly as a standalone business catalogue or alongside WooCommerce. The design provides structured templates for trade-specific workwear specs, alteration price lists, and crew fitting services.

## Structure
- `patterns/front-*.php`: the nine home sections (hero, shop, trades, floor, fit, prices, crews, order, visit).
- `templates/`: front page, page, index, archive, single, search, 404. `parts/`: split-nav header and ink footer.
- `assets/sp-scroll.*`: shared scroll effects (reveal, wipe, mask, stagger, parallax).
- Design notes: `brand.md`, `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md`.
