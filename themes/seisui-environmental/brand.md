## History
Seisui Environmental opened in 2009 at 14 Millrace Lane, Alder Bridge, a river town on the River Wend. Dr Ilse Varga, a hydrologist, started the practice so landowners and municipal clients could see what the water and the soil were doing before a bank, a field or a building plot was changed. The first work was desk study, site walkover and sampling, written up from the same office that still keeps sample cool boxes by the door.

The monitoring ledger grew with the river and the ground around it. Surface water at Wend River, Mill Weir (WR-01) has been on the round since 2012, and Wend River, Ferry Steps (WR-04) since 2015. The Alder Bridge mill pond (PD-05) joined in 2018, the Copse Farm borehole (GW-09) in 2019, and Station yard soil (SL-06) in 2022. Habitat work at Wend Meadow (WM-03) began in 2021. Soil investigation at the Fennick Road lot (SL-02) began in 2023, and groundwater at the Hollin wells (GW-07) in 2024. On a monitored site the standing rhythm is four rounds a year.

Sixteen years on, the practice monitors 38 sites for councils and private landowners. In 2021 Alder Bridge council commissioned a floodplain meadow restoration baseline at the Wend Meadow reach, then three years of monitoring. In 2023 a private landowner commissioned Phase I and Phase II work on the former tannery lot at Fennick Road before a resale. In 2024 Hollin parish council commissioned a groundwater quality programme for five wells. The office is still at 14 Millrace Lane, open Monday to Friday from 08:30 to 17:30, with field days by arrangement and the door shut at weekends and on public holidays.

## Process
- 01 Scoping call. Tell us the site and the decision in front of you, and we will say whether a walkover, a Phase I or a sampling round is the right first step. The call is free, lasts 20 minutes, and is offered within 2 working days.
- 02 Desk study & walkover. We read the maps and records, then walk the ground with you, and you receive that reading before any sample is taken. The desk study and walkover are completed within 10 working days.
- 03 Sampling & laboratory. Sample points are agreed first, then water or soil is taken and sent to the laboratory against that plan. Results are reported in 15 working days.
- 04 Report & next steps. You receive a written report of what was done and what the results show, and we sit down to talk through what comes next. The review meeting lasts 45 minutes.
