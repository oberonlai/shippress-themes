# Shoko Archives

Shoko Archives is a commercial records-management theme for a collection-request landing page: boxed storage, barcode indexing and next-working-day retrieval from a numbered aisle.

Part of [ShipPress themes](https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). Design brief: `brand.md`; design notes: `brand/`.

## Features

- Photo window hero: three high-key photographs cross-fade (dock, aisle, desk) with a diagonal bottom edge; the headline and two sub-lines sit in outlined white boxes; a square pause button.
- Ruled headings with striped square-bullet lists, icon rows and tabbed photo cards.
- Aisle band with slow parallax, a docked white panel and counted figures; a striped price table that stacks on phones.
- Waitlist and contact forms use the shared form slot (MailPoet, Mailchimp for WP, Contact Form 7 or WPForms when active; a mailto fallback otherwise).
- Fonts: Schibsted Grotesk (display) and Instrument Sans (body), loaded through theme.json; the block editor shows the same fonts as the front end.
- Languages: en, with zh-TW, ja, fr and de dictionaries to follow in the next build step.
