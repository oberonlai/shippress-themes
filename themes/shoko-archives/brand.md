# Shoko Archives - brand brief

A commercial records firm that takes a company's paper into a numbered aisle and brings it back on request. Collections leave client offices on Tuesday and Thursday rounds; each standard box is barcoded, given an aisle-bay-shelf address, shelved at Unit 7 and returned the next working day when asked for by box number. One way to book, one price list, six named people.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shoko Archives - commercial records management. Sub-line wherever the brand is introduced: "Records management, since 2004".
- **Location:** Unit 7, Harlow Mead Trading Estate, Lindenport (a fictional address). Email hello@shoko-archives.example, phone +00 20 7000 1404. Prices in euros (EUR), excluding VAT, in every language.
- **Founded:** 2004 by Mara Okonkwo, who started the service for nearby offices that had outgrown their filing rooms. The unit was a plain light-industrial shed with a roller door and room for a first run of steel shelving.
- **Audience:** office managers, practice managers and finance leads at small and mid-size firms (law offices, clinics, architects, accountants) who must keep paper for years but no longer have the room, and who want a box back on their desk without a phone chase.
- **Tone:** calm, concrete and close to the working day. Name the box, the aisle, the round and the price in plain sentences. No guaranteed outcomes, no invented statistics, no awards, no real media, people or brands.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Hours: Mon-Fri 07:30-18:00; Sat 08:00-12:00 (retrievals only); closed Sunday. Pickup rounds Tuesday and Thursday.
- Facility: 14 aisles, capacity 38,000 standard boxes. Standard retrieval returns the next working day; an urgent retrieval requested before 11:00 returns the same day.
- Process: Book, Collect (Tue/Thu), Index (barcode, aisle-bay-shelf), Shelve, Request by box number, Return the next working day.
- Prices (EUR, excl. VAT): collection visit (up to 40 boxes) EUR 45 per visit; standard archive box with barcode EUR 3.50 each; storage EUR 0.42 per box per month; retrieval, next working day, EUR 6 per box; urgent same-day retrieval EUR 18 per box; secure destruction with certificate EUR 2.20 per box. Invoices are sent monthly.
- Cases (composite, names withheld): a 6-partner law office (220 boxes of closed matters); a family clinic (90 boxes on a retention schedule); an architects' studio (140 boxes of drawings, scan on demand).
- Waitlist: Hall Two, the second hall, opens in spring; early access by email.

## A day at Unit 7 (daily routine)
07:30 the desk opens and the day's request list is printed; the Tuesday and Thursday round leaves for client offices after the first coffee; boxes arrive at the dock, are counted against the booking and go to the indexing desk, where each receives a barcode and an address; Elena Sousa shelves them in the afternoon; requests received before 11:00 can be pulled and returned the same day; the desk closes at 18:00. The routine is the same every working day, so no box waits without an address.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page; reference: a large Japanese printing and information company's corporate top and service pages)
- **Outline-boxed headline strips:** the page headline and its sub-lines each sit in their own white box hugging the text, with a 3px ink outline on the headline and 1px on the sub-lines, stacked flush-left over a photograph. Inner pages repeat the boxed headline on a pale band.
- **Ruled heading with striped square-bullet list and icon rows:** every H2 has a 1px moss rule under it; lists alternate pale and white 54px rows with a 10px square bullet; retrieval steps use 112px line icons.
- **Flush top-left tab on photo cards:** three-up 16:9 photographs with a pale tab fused to the top-left corner (Collect, Index, Shelve; Law office, Clinic, Architects), the caption below with a small chevron link.
- Supporting: a docked white panel over a full-bleed aisle band with slow parallax, a 40px square pause control on the hero, a quiet info bar of three centred links, a 40px square back-to-top button.
