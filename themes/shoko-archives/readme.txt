Shoko Archives is a commercial records-management theme for a collection-request landing page. It sets out boxed storage, barcode indexing and next-working-day retrieval from Unit 7, Harlow Mead Trading Estate, Lindenport, founded in 2004. The front page follows one box from the loading dock to a numbered aisle and back to the desk.

== Description ==
A block theme for a records-management firm with one way to book. The front page opens on a 600px photograph window that cross-fades between a loading dock, a numbered aisle and a desk, cut on a shallow diagonal at its foot; the headline and its two sub-lines each sit in their own outlined white box, and a square pause button stops the slideshow. A quiet info bar, a ruled heading with a striped square-bullet list, three tabbed photo cards, a full-bleed aisle band that drifts as you scroll with a docked white panel and counted figures, four icon rows, a striped price table, the people, three collections and a single booking band follow, closed by a waitlist strip with a newsletter form and a white footer.

= Fonts =
* Schibsted Grotesk (headings, wordmark, navigation, figures), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
* Noto Sans TC and Noto Sans JP (zh-TW and ja headings, bold subsets), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
* Instrument Sans (body text, labels, tables, forms), SIL Open Font License 1.1.

= Images =
The sample photographs were generated for this theme. They contain no text, logos or real people.

= Forms =
The newsletter and contact forms use a dynamic block that shows a Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP form when one is active. Without a plugin the waitlist form still has an email field and a submit button that opens your email app with the address filled in; nothing is stored by the theme.
