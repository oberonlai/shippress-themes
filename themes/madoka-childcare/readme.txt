Madoka Childcare is a childcare centre for babies and toddlers of working parents, founded in 2015 at 12 Linden Row, Ranelagh, Dublin 6. The house has 39 places in three rooms, Nest, Meadow and Grove, and is open Monday to Friday from 07:30 to 18:30. These pages set out the care day, the rooms, the monthly fees and how to visit, in plain English. It ships as a block theme with Albert Sans and Public Sans set in theme.json.
