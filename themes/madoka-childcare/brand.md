# Madoka Childcare — brand

Madoka Childcare opened in 2015 in a two-storey brick house at 12 Linden Row, Ranelagh, Dublin 6. Madoka Quinlan, an early-years teacher, founded it after twelve years as a room leader. She wanted a house where a child would see the same adults each day, and a back garden that belonged to the ordinary day. The centre is for babies and toddlers of working parents, from 4 months to 4 years.

The house holds 39 places in three rooms. Nest takes babies from 4 to 15 months, 9 places, with one educator to three children. Meadow takes children from 15 to 30 months, 12 places, at 1:4. Grove takes children from 2½ to 4 years, 18 places, at 1:6. Each child has a key person in their room. Samuel Osei leads Nest, Aoife Brennan leads Meadow, and Clara Moreau leads Grove. Room educators are hired with a QQI Level 6 early-childhood award or higher. That is our staffing rule, stated as policy.

The doors open Monday to Friday, 07:30 to 18:30. The house is closed at weekends, on Irish public holidays, and from 24 December to 1 January. Tomás Rivera cooks in the kitchen each morning, and the day moves from a hello at the door to stories and a last pickup at 18:30. Families come to visit on Tuesday and Thursday at 10:00 and at 16:00. Hanna Kowalski arranges those visits and the enrolment that follows. A place is offered after a visit, then a child settles in over two weeks before a first full day.

## Process
1. Visit on Tuesday or Thursday at 10:00 or 16:00, for 45 minutes.
2. Place offer and €150 registration, paid once when you accept a place.
3. Family meeting with your key person, covering routines, food and sleep.
4. Settling-in fortnight, with short days growing to full days. Included in the first month's fee.
5. First full day.
