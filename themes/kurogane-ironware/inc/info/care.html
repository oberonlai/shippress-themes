First use

Rinse with warm water only, no soap. Fill to two-thirds, boil, pour away. Repeat twice; the third boil is ready to drink.

Every day

Boil fresh water and use it while it is hot. Never boil a kettle dry, and keep the flame no wider than its base.

After use

Empty it while it is still warm, lid off, and let the heat dry the inside. Never leave water standing overnight.

If rust appears

A little red inside is harmless iron. Boil once with a used tea bag in the water; the tannin seals it again. Do not scrub.

The outside

Wipe with a dry cloth while warm. Water marks fade with use; if the outside dulls, rub it once with a cloth damp with brewed tea.
