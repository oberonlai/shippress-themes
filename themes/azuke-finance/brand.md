# Azuke Finance — brand brief

Design plan row: b15-6 (small-supplier invoice factoring, type landing). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other languages follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional. Copy never promises approval, speed beyond the stated terms, or an outcome.

## Concept
- **Name:** Azuke Finance B.V. "Azuke" means to entrust something for safekeeping: the supplier hands over one invoice and gets cash back while the customer's payment is pending.
- **Location:** Haringvliet 92, 3011 TG Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional office); phone +31 10 555 0192; apply@azuke.example, hello@azuke.example.
- **Founded:** 2019 by Ines Vercauteren and Tomás Aldana.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 8:30–17:30 CET.
- **Services:** single-invoice funding for EU small suppliers (makers, wholesalers, service firms) with B2B invoices of €1,000–€150,000. Currency EUR in every language.
- **Audience:** owners of small workshops and wholesale businesses who delivered on time and wait 30, 45 or 60 days for payment.
- **Tone:** Quiet, direct, and measured, reflecting the practical perspective of an experienced commercial partner. The language avoids hype and financial jargon, focusing entirely on verifiable operational facts, clear timeframes, and transparent terms.

## Story
Azuke Finance B.V. was founded in Rotterdam in 2019 by Ines Vercauteren and Tomás Aldana to solve a persistent operational challenge faced by European suppliers. Small manufacturing workshops, wholesale distributors, and specialized service firms routinely deliver goods and complete project milestones on schedule, only to wait thirty, forty-five, or sixty days for their business customers to settle approved invoices.

This structural payment gap frequently ties up working capital when businesses need it most to pay staff, replenish essential inventory, and purchase raw materials for incoming orders. Traditional bank facilities often require extensive asset security or pledges of personal assets, while conventional factoring services demand complex multi-year contracts, fixed monthly subscription fees, and all-turnover volume commitments that small suppliers do not need.

Operating from Haringvliet 92 in Rotterdam, Azuke Finance was established with a singular, transparent approach to small-supplier factoring. Suppliers select individual B2B invoices between €1,000 and €150,000 to advance ninety percent of the funds on the next working day, with a single initial fee of 1.4 percent. By entrusting their receivables for safekeeping, independent businesses maintain steady cash flow and continue trading on their own terms.

## Process
1. Apply online, ~3 minutes (company number, IBAN, contact)
2. Upload one invoice with its delivery note or signed proof
3. We confirm the invoice with your customer by email within one working day
4. 90% paid; the remainder minus the fee when your customer pays

## Fees (same on home, Pricing, FAQ and all five languages)
- Account and application — no setup, no monthly fee, no minimum volume — €0
- Advance — paid the next working day after approval — 90% of invoice
- Funding fee, first 30 days — charged once, shown before you accept — 1.4% of invoice
- Each further 15 days — if your customer pays later than 30 days — 0.7% of invoice
- Fee cap — the most we charge on one invoice — 4.2% of invoice
- Same-day payout — approved before 13:00 CET — €25 per invoice
- Invoice size — per invoice, B2B customers in the EU — €1,000–€150,000
Worked example: invoice €12,400 on 30-day terms → €11,160 next working day → fee €173.60 → €1,066.40 paid when the customer pays.
