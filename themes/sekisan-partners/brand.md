# Sekisan Partners - brand brief

Independent building cost consultancy in Rotterdam (fictional): a proposal-request landing page theme. Sample content in English first; zh-TW, ja, fr and de follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Sekisan Partners. International and neutral; the wordmark stays in Latin letters in every language.
- **Location:** Maaskade 41, 3071 NB Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional). Fees in EUR excl. VAT in every language.
- **History:** Sekisan Partners was founded in 2011 in Rotterdam by chartered quantity surveyor Ilse van Damme. Having spent years managing estimating desks within major commercial contracting firms, she observed a recurring disconnect in the building sector: project owners and design teams were committing to complex architectural schemes without independent financial verification. Determining that construction pricing should serve clients before contracts are tendered, she established an independent cost consultancy dedicated to transparent, pre-construction financial planning.
  From the studio on the Rotterdam waterfront the practice grew methodically to advise property developers, public institutions and leading design practices across the Netherlands, expanding its estimating team to cover every stage of capital delivery, from feasibility massing checks to detailed bills of quantities and post-contract cost monitoring.
  Today it is a close-knit partnership of seven specialists, entirely independent of contractors and supply chains, committed to one principle: buildings must be priced before they are built.
- **Audience:** property developers, public institutions, architects and design teams who need a dependable budget before contractors are asked.
- **Tone:** calm, precise and practical, the measured clarity of an experienced cost consultancy. Sentences stay grounded in project figures; no promotional rhetoric, no guarantees, no unverified claims.

## Process (identical on every page and language)
1. Send drawings, project brief and site address.
2. Fixed-fee proposal issued within 2 working days.
3. 30-minute call with the partner who will price the project.
4. Concept cost plan delivered in 10 working days (detailed cost plan in 20 working days). Payment 30 days from invoice.
