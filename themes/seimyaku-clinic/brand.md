# Seimyaku Clinic - brand brief

A nephrology and blood-pressure clinic in Wrenfield that follows adults with kidney disease and high blood pressure over years, not single visits. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Seimyaku Clinic, nephrology and blood pressure. "Seimyaku" stands for the quiet line that runs through years of care: the clinic keeps the same doctor, the same numbers and the same room across a patient's whole follow-up.
- **Location:** 2nd floor, 41 Alder Row, Wrenfield (above the Alder Row pharmacy; step-free lift). Fictional, international-neutral. Email care@seimyaku-clinic.example, phone +00 31 555 0148 (fictional format). Fees in euros in every language.
- **Founded:** 2013 by Dr Ilse Marchetti, after years in a hospital renal unit where she saw people only once something had already gone wrong. The clinic opened in a single room on Alder Row.
- **People (all fictional):** Dr Ilse Marchetti (founder, nephrologist); Dr Kwame Asante (nephrologist); Dr Hana Reyes (hypertension physician); Tomas Lindqvist (lead nurse); Priya Nair (renal dietitian); Elena Varga (sonographer); Samuel Obi (patient coordinator). People followed in the stories: Ruth (64), Daniel (47), Amara (38).
- **Today:** a team of seven in rooms on one floor: consulting rooms, an ultrasound room, a sample room and a quiet waiting area. 2018 added the home blood-pressure diary programme. The clinic does not run dialysis or emergency care; stage 5 care is shared with the regional hospital renal unit.
- **Audience:** adults 18+ with chronic kidney disease (stages 1-4), high blood pressure including resistant hypertension, protein or blood in the urine, a past kidney stone, or kidney questions before pregnancy. A referral is not needed.
- **Tone:** calm, unhurried and precise. Plain words, no medical jargon dumps, no urgency, no promotional claims. No guarantees, outcomes, awards, press, ratings or real brands. In an emergency: call your local emergency number.

## The clinic day (daily routine, the same everywhere)
- **08:30** Doors open; fasting blood and urine samples are taken from 08:30.
- **Morning and afternoon** First consultations (90 minutes) and follow-up visits (30 minutes) with the same doctor.
- **Wednesday** The evening clinic runs until 19:30.
- **17:30** Doors close (Monday to Friday). Saturday 09:00-12:30. Sunday and public holidays closed.
Every result is read by the doctor who knows the patient; results calls happen within 5 working days of a first consultation.

## Fees (EUR, self-paying; insured patients are billed under their plan; identical on the home page, Fees page and everywhere else)
First consultation, 90 min (incl. blood and urine panel) EUR 180 - Follow-up visit, 30 min EUR 75 - Blood and urine panel (eGFR, electrolytes, urine ACR) EUR 48 - Kidney ultrasound EUR 120 - 24-hour blood pressure monitor EUR 95 - Dietitian session, 45 min EUR 70. Results calls and the home blood-pressure diary are included. Cancellations up to 24 hours before are free.

## Monitoring pace (set with the patient, reviewed every year)
Stages 1-2, stable: once a year. Stage 3a: every 6 months. Stage 3b: every 3 months. Stage 4: every 6-8 weeks, shared with the hospital renal unit. Everyone has a yearly review in the month they joined.

## How a first consultation works
1. Send letters, past lab sheets and a medicine list beforehand. 2. Samples at 08:30 that morning. 3. 90 minutes with the nephrologist. 4. A results call within 5 working days. 5. Your monitoring pace agreed.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Rising veil and docked headline (after Takeda Hospital Group): a pale blue-grey wave-edged veil lifts over a full-bleed photograph, and the headline sits below the photograph, then docks small beside the narrative.
2. Scale-back stack of three chapter sheets (after the Takeda philosophy page): sticky sheets that stack with width and tone steps, photographs drifting 10 % inside.
3. Medallion-split slashed menu (after the Naganonaika clinic): a round medallion docked on the photograph edge between three links and three links, separated by thin diagonal slashes.
Supporting: a dot, a chip and a giant light word as section heads (FOLLOW-UP, FOLLOWED, FEES).
