Seimyaku Clinic is a calm, single-column WordPress theme tailored for nephrology practices, hypertension specialists, and outpatient medical clinics. Built with an unhurried, editorial aesthetic, it features signature wave veil transitions, sticky chapter stacks, and structured clinical fee tables. Designed for full site editing, the theme delivers responsive typography and quiet elegance to showcase long-term patient care. Outfit for headings, Figtree for reading. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download seimyaku-clinic.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/seimyaku-clinic.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Seimyaku Clinic.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, news entries with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Forms: the contact page and the newsletter sections use a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With Contact Form 7 or WPForms (contact) and MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP (newsletter) active, their forms are shown and styled; without a plugin the contact page keeps a mailto button and the newsletter shows a real email field and submit button that opens your email app, with a clear note that you are not subscribed until the message is sent. Suggested booking form fields: full name, email, phone, date of birth, reason for visit, referred by a doctor, preferred days, message and consent.

The clinic, its people, address, telephone number and fees are fictional. The theme does not give medical advice.
