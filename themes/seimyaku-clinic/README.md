# Seimyaku Clinic

A block theme for a nephrology and blood-pressure clinic that follows people over years, not single visits.

- **Signature techniques:** (1) a pale blue-grey wave veil that rises over a full-bleed photograph, with the headline below the photograph and a docked headline beside the narrative; (2) three sticky chapter sheets that stack with width and tone steps while their photographs drift; (3) a medallion-split single menu with thin diagonal slashes (reused in cards, lists, the footer and breadcrumbs). Supporting: a dot, a chip and a giant light word as section heads.
- **Home:** header with the medallion, hero (photograph, arc, scroll pill, headline, four cards), message cover with the docked headline, how care is paced (three sheets), what we watch (ruled rows and photographs to the edge), three people followed, the team, fees, visit, notes, the clinic letter.
- **Type:** Outfit (headings), Figtree (text); CJK headings Noto Sans TC / JP.
- **Colour:** ground `#F3F6F6`, mist `#E6EDEE`, veil `#BFD0D6`, main `#51686B` (aonibi), ink `#1F2D30`, one small accent `#8A4940` for arrows.
- **Motion:** sp-scroll (parallax, reveal up with stagger, wipe) and a one-off arc draw and headline fade; all respect reduced motion.
- **Forms:** `shippress/form` dynamic block (contact and newsletter) with plugin detection and a mailto fallback.
- Design notes: `brand/design-spec.md`, brand brief: `brand.md`.
