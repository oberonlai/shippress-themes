## Story
Katachi Ergonomics was founded in 2014 in Northvale by Ilse Varga. She had worked for twelve years as a physiotherapist in hospital rehabilitation, and she kept seeing the same shoulders in clinic. The cause was a desk. She started the practice so that someone would watch the desk before recommending a change, and that is still the only thing the firm sells: a readiness review, booked before an office move, a furniture purchase, or a return to the office.

The work follows three habits. Watch first: a consultant spends the visit on the floor, looking at the chair, the desk height, the screen, the keyboard and the laptop, then at light, noise and how people move, before anyone talks about new furniture. Change the least: adjust what is already there, and write a purchase list only when something is missing. Write it down: every change goes into a plan the team can keep, one page per desk and a floor summary, delivered within five working days, with a check on site six weeks later.

In 2017 the practice added remote desk reviews, for one person at a time by video. In 2020 hybrid and home-desk reviews were added, for teams who work both on an office floor and at a desk at home. In 2023 the Next openings list started: a note on the first Monday of each month, about three minutes to read, with that month's open visit days and one desk tip. Seven people now work from Unit 4, 18 Foundry Row, Northvale. Visits are on site within forty kilometres; remote desk reviews go anywhere. Bookings come from office and operations managers, and from HR and facilities leads, at teams of five to sixty people.

## A day (daily routine)
A visit day runs on site on a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday. The consultants arrive at 08:30 and walk the floor until 10:00: light, noise, routes and how the room is used. Then each person gives twenty minutes at their own desk, up to 15 desks per consultant in a day, with small fixes made on the spot. At 16:30 the consultant talks through the findings with the manager. Friday is for reports: the written plan, one page per desk and a floor summary, is delivered within 5 working days, and the 6-week check follows on site for 90 minutes. The daily routine at the studio is quieter: planning, floor plans and questionnaires before a visit, and report editing after it.
