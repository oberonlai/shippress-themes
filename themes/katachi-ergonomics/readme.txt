Katachi Ergonomics is a landing theme for a workplace ergonomics consultancy that watches a working desk before it recommends a change. The home page is one long page toward a single booking, the readiness review, for teams preparing an office move, a furniture purchase or a return to the office. WooCommerce is compatible but not required.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

Fonts: Hanken Grotesk (headings), Public Sans (reading text) and Noto Sans TC (Traditional Chinese headings); all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

== Installation ==

1. Download katachi-ergonomics.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/katachi-ergonomics.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Katachi Ergonomics.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with their text and images, three journal entries with two categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Optional forms: the Next openings section uses a small dynamic block. With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active, their form is shown; without a plugin it shows an email field that opens your mail app (you are not on the list until you send the request).

Scroll effects use the bundled sp-scroll files (assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js); they respect prefers-reduced-motion.

The consultancy, its people, places and prices are fictional.
