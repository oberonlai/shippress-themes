# Teate Clinic

Teate Clinic is a WordPress theme for a two-clinician osteopathy practice in Halbrook, opened in 2014 by Maren Holt and Kofi Mensah. It sets out hands-on care for desk workers and amateur athletes: the first hour, the three usual complaints, the clinicians’ days and a six-line price list in euros. The voice stays calm and specific, and the clinic makes no promise about an outcome.

Reference: Ito Hospital (https://www.ito-hospital.jp/): the split first screen of one rounded photograph beside a solid identity card and two outline entry boxes, the shadowed five-cell icon sheet with dotted dividers, and the ruled hours ledger, translated into a two-clinician osteopathy clinic. See `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md` and `brand/reference-tokens.md`.

## Home blocks
Header with a celadon stripe and one menu; split hero (photo left, info card, headline, two outline boxes) with a five-cell sheet; notices beside the hours ledger; three complaints; why hands, not machines (two text columns and a 3:1 photograph); the first hour as a minutes ledger; the room parallax band; two clinicians; three story rows; prices; the tile grid; footer.
