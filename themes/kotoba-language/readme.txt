Kotoba Language is an adult language school at Scheepmakerspassage 14, Rotterdam, founded in 2016. It teaches English, Dutch, French, German, Mandarin and Japanese to new arrivals and cross-border teams, in classes of at most 8. This theme presents the pathways, the CEFR placement, the 2026 intakes and the EUR fee list.
