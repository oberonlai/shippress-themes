# Kotoba Language — brand

Kotoba Language opened in 2016 at Scheepmakerspassage 14, 3011 VH Rotterdam, in the Netherlands. It is an adult language school for new arrivals and for teams whose work crosses borders. Classes are kept to a maximum of 8, so the room stays a class and not a hall. Noor Abadi, director of studies, has taught English at the school since that first year.

The timetable holds six languages: English, Dutch, French, German, Mandarin and Japanese. Teaching is arranged as three pathways. Work and cross-border teams covers a shared working language, including an on-site workplace group. Settlement and daily life covers appointments, housing and schools. Exam preparation works to a CEFR level from A1 to C2 and makes no claim that a learner will pass. A free 45-minute placement interview, in person or online, sets the level before enrolment.

The year has four intakes: 12 January, 13 April, 7 September and 9 November 2026. A placement evening runs on the first Thursday of each month at 18:30. After the interview, the school sends a written level and track proposal. Enrolment includes a registration fee of €45, paid once. The first class follows, and a level review falls at week 5 of each term. Opening hours are Monday to Thursday 09:00–21:00, Friday 09:00–17:00, and Saturday 10:00–14:00 for placement interviews only. Sunday is closed.

## Process
1. Send an enquiry to hello@kotobalanguage.example or call +31 10 555 0142. You may also come to the placement evening on the first Thursday of each month at 18:30.
2. Sit the free 45-minute placement interview, either in person at the school or online. No course fee is due at this step.
3. Read the written level and track proposal. It names a CEFR level from A1 to C2 and one pathway: work, settlement or exam preparation.
4. Enrol and pay the registration fee of €45 once. Course fees are separate and are listed in EUR.
5. Begin the first class. A level review takes place at week 5 of the term, while there is still time to adjust the plan.
