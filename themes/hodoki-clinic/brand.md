# Hodoki Clinic — brand

Hodoki Clinic is a physiotherapy practice in Fernhill, opened in 2015 by Clara Mendes. The rooms are on the second floor at 41 Alder Street: five treatment bays and one small exercise studio, with reception at the front for bookings. Clara is founder and clinical lead, and she treats neck and shoulder problems. The clinic is for desk workers and for people after surgery, two groups who often arrive holding a joint still and wanting ordinary movement back.

Hodoki means untying. Before strength work, we find what is being held: a shoulder raised toward the ear, a neck that aches after laptop hours, a lower back stiff from sitting, a knee or hip still protecting itself after an operation. We listen, then test how you move, and only then use our hands. Loosening comes before loading. The work is aimed at ordinary movement: the desk, the stairs, and the walk from the tram.

A first visit is 60 minutes and costs $95. It covers your history, movement tests, and the first hands-on treatment, and a written plan is sent the same day. Follow-ups run every one to two weeks with a home programme, and we review at week six. No referral is required. You receive a receipt with treatment codes for your insurer, and you may cancel up to 24 hours before at no charge. The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 20:00 and Saturday from 09:00 to 14:00. Sunday and public holidays are closed. The last appointment is one hour before closing. Call +1 555 0142 300 or write to hello@hodoki-clinic.example.
