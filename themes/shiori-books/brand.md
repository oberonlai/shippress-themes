# Shiori Books - brand brief

Independent neighbourhood bookshop in Edinburgh, Scotland. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shiori Books. "Shiori" is the Japanese word for a bookmark; the shop is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** 27 Calder Lane, Edinburgh EH9 1PQ, Scotland (fictional street). Two rooms: the front room (new books, the front table) and the back room (second-hand and rare, the bench, the Saturday reading). About 9,000 books. Phone +44 131 555 0127, hello@shioribooks.example, letters@shioribooks.example.
- **Founded:** 2011.
- **Hours:** Tue–Fri 10:00–19:00, Sat 10:00–21:00, Sun 12:00–17:00, Mon closed.
- **Voice:** Calm, specific and unhurried, as if a bookseller were speaking across the front table. Facts stay concrete — a price, a day, a room — and the sentence ends when the fact is said.
- **Currency:** GBP (£) in every language; no converted prices.

## Audience
Neighbourhood readers in Edinburgh: people who live within a bus ride of Calder Lane, buy a few books a month, come for the Saturday reading, give books as gifts and want a bookseller to choose for them. Second audience: collectors and people clearing a shelf who bring books for a valuation or a repair.

## History
Shiori Books opened in 2011 at 27 Calder Lane, Edinburgh, when Hanna Moreau and Callum Reid took one room and began filling it with books they would hand to someone on the street. Hanna bought the new fiction, poetry and essays. Callum took the second-hand and rare stock and kept notes on the condition of each copy. The front table was the point of the room from the first week: a short row chosen by a bookseller, cleared and set again when the week asked for something else. Special orders were free from the start. If a title was in print, the shop fetched it, usually in two to four working days, and sent a text when it reached the counter.

In 2015 the shop took the room behind and made it the back room. Callum's second-hand and rare books moved there, and Amara Osei began the Saturday reading: doors at 19:00, the reading from 19:30 to 20:30, thirty seats at £5, and that £5 taken off any book bought the same evening. Tomasz Wilk set up the bindery bench in 2016. Spine re-backing is done in the back room, about ten working days a book, at £18. In 2018 Lucía Ferrer built the children's shelves and started story hour on the second Saturday of the month at 11:00, free. In 2019 Ravi Menon took the orders and the shelf subscription: one book a month, chosen by Hanna or Ravi by the 5th, wrapped and ready by the 10th, £24 a month, with a swap inside 30 days if the subscriber already owned it.

Elin Sørensen joined in 2021 for Saturdays and the poetry shelves. The two rooms now hold about 9,000 books. The front room is still new books and the front table. The back room is still second-hand and rare stock, the bench, and the chairs for Saturday. A book is still chosen by someone who works here. The reading note is still three books loved and one that was not, and the parcel is still plain paper, cotton string, and a card.

## Shelf subscription, how it works
1. Fill in a short reading note at the counter or by email: three books you loved, and one you didn't.
2. Hanna Moreau or Ravi Menon choose your book by the 5th of each month.
3. It is wrapped and ready by the 10th, to collect or posted in the UK.
4. Already own it? Swap it within 30 days.

## Saturday reading
Every Saturday: doors 19:00, reading 19:30-20:30, back room, 30 seats, £5 a seat, the £5 comes back off any book bought that evening. Story hour on the second Saturday of the month at 11:00 (free).

## Signature techniques (carried to inner pages)
1. T1 split entrance: two equal full-height halves, light and dark warm grey, small centred caps title, four-line blurb and bracket label (inner pages: a 360 px split head).
2. T2 hairline index list: title | name | date at 14/21, past rows dimmed, a sticky 3:2 thumbnail in the left column that follows the hovered row.
3. T3 photograph flush to the viewport edge at 41.9 % width (1.43:1) with a #E3E3E3 grain panel flush to the opposite edge.
Supporting: dark #44413C field, ghost ring text and a white 12 px card.
