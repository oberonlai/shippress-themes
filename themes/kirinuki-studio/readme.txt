Kirinuki Studio is a calm, single-column WordPress theme designed for commercial product photography studios and batch catalogue shoots. Built with a floating overlay header, chamfered product grids, and grid-ruled specification sheets, it presents transparent day rates, crew profiles, and straightforward booking workflows. Features balanced grotesque typography, subtle parallax motion, and full responsiveness without extraneous layouts.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The home page opens on a grained stone mist: a large headline at the left, three high-key product photographs fused into one chamfered polygon at the right and a foot row of five client wordmarks. It continues with a pale statement sheet over a ghost text wall, a near-black band of chamfered work tiles that settle from a slight tilt, a grid-ruled white sheet of outline chips for the kinds of shoot, the day rate with a six-line price table, a parallax studio floor band, a shoot day in four prose rows, the people, three case notes, the questions and the one way to book. See brand.md for the studio facts used on every page.

== Installation ==

1. Download kirinuki-studio.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kirinuki-studio.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kirinuki Studio.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, a journal entry with a category and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The scroll effects (assets/sp-scroll.css and sp-scroll.js) respect prefers-reduced-motion and show the final state without JavaScript. The newsletter and contact slots use Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when active; without a plugin they fall back to an email link.

The studio, its people, address, telephone number, email, clients and prices are fictional.
