## 1. Signature techniques (three; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

### T1. Chamfered product triptych beside a big dark headline on a grain mist (amana first screen, https://amana.jp/, `ref/amana-first-0.png`, RT(a)(b)(e)(f)(g))
- **Reference:** a soft mint (`#B2D3CC`) to blush (`#CBBEBA`) gradient with film grain fills ~90% of the first screen; a dark plum-grey 88 px headline "Co-Creation" sits at centre with a small light "with" tucked at its end, and against it a cluster of **three equal photographs side by side inside one polygon** whose top-left and bottom-right corners are cut at 25% (RT(e) clip-path). Floating white r16 header bar, inset 8 px.
- **Ours (home hero):** same ground (stone-sage → stone-blush, grain), the headline at left, and the **triptych enlarged into the product grid** (row's hero_composition): three equal columns of high-key studio photos in one chamfered polygon at right, the light "in a day" tucked against the headline's last word where the polygon starts. Geometry in §3.
- **Inner pages:** every inner page head = the same mist ground, height 520 px desktop (not full screen), page title (3.5rem) left, a **smaller triptych** (480 × 300, same polygon, same 3 equal columns) right, breadcrumb-style 12 px sub-label under the title. On About, Shoots, Work, Day rates, Booking, FAQ, Newsletter, Contact (8 pages).
- **Not to be diluted:** the polygon must show both 25% cuts clearly (cut legs ≥ 100 px on the home hero); the three photos are equal width with a 2 px mist seam; the headline is dark (`#47393F`) and ≥ 5rem at 1440; the mist must read as a two-hue gradient (sage left, blush right), not a flat grey; no photo behind the text.

### T2. Dark band of chamfered work tiles that settle from a tilt (amana Works, `ref/amana-first-900.png`, `ref/scroll-amana-1350.png`, RT(c)(e)(g))
- **Reference:** near-black `#131313` band, padding 160 px top / 96 px bottom, grid 1248 px (x 96–1344), gap 16 px; mixed-ratio tiles (1.5:1 wide, 0.83:1 tall, 1.6:1 landscape) each clipped by the same chamfer polygon; white caption bottom-left over a dark scrim on some tiles; tiles enter from `rotate(5deg) translateY(40px)` to rest, 1.5 s `cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)`; a white pill button below.
- **Ours (home #work):** band `#151614`, same paddings and grid; section title with dot cluster in white; **6 tiles** in the reference rhythm: row 1 = W1 wide (span 2 of 3 columns, 1.5:1) + W2 tall (0.83:1); row 2 = W3, W4, W5 (1.6:1 each); row 3 = W6 wide (span 3, 2.4:1). Captions bottom-left over the scrim on W1, W4, W6 (client + one line, e.g. "Alder & Moss — 48 bottles, one sweep"). Under the grid, the white pill "Book a shoot" (§5 button spec) centred.
- **Inner pages:** Work page (full wall, 9 tiles incl. the 3 case notes), Shoots page (one dark band with 4 tiles, one per kind of shoot), About page (3-tile band "Inside the studio"), Booking page (2-tile band "What arrives after the day").
- **Not to be diluted:** band must be near-black (not grey); tiles must have the chamfer (no rounded rectangles); mixed ratios (at least one wide and one tall); the tilt-settle motion on scroll (§7); the pill is white on dark.

### T3. Grid-ruled white sheet with dotted-heading outline chips (amana Solutions, `ref/amana-first-2700.png`, RT(c)(d)(f))
- **Reference:** grey `#EEEEEE` ground, a white r16 sheet (1248 wide, padding ~64 px) ruled by 1 px `#EAEAEA` lines every ~130 px; title with dot-cluster glyph + 12 px grey sub-label; four groups in 2 columns, each with a two-dot coloured heading (purple, orange, magenta, green) and 1 px outline chips r4 (padding 4 px 12 px, 12–13 px text, chip colour = group colour) ending in three dots.
- **Ours (home #shoots, "Kinds of shoot"):** identical geometry; four groups: **Packshot** (plum) · **Lifestyle** (ochre) · **Detail & flat lay** (rose) · **Apparel & motion** (green); 5–7 chips each (list in §3 block 4). Chips are links to anchors on the Shoots page.
- **Inner pages:** Shoots (full chip sheet + detail per group), Day rates (the 7 "includes" lines as one chip group + price table inside the same ruled sheet), FAQ (topic chips as in-page anchors), Booking ("What to send us" chip group: product list, sizes, reference images, marketplace specs…), Journal archive (categories as chips).
- **Not to be diluted:** grid lines must be visible (1 px `#E6E6E2`, 128 px cell) across the whole sheet behind content; chips outline-only (no fill) with the trailing three-dot mark; four distinct muted chip colours; two-dot heading markers.

### Supporting (required, not counted)
- Floating white header bar r16, inset 8 px, 64 px tall, separate dark CTA cell (RT(d)).
- Dot-cluster glyph (5 dots, 14 px, inline SVG, `aria-hidden`) before every section title, with the 12 px grey sub-label on the same baseline (RT(b)(f)).
- Ghost text wall behind the statement sheet (RT(f)): 10 lines of repeated shoot words at 4% contrast; one line animated with `sp-marquee`.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion
amana.jp opens on an almost empty, softly grained mist (mint to blush). In the middle one big dark word "Co-Creation" runs into a small light "with", and right against it three street photographs of a person with a camera are fused into one chamfered polygon. **Story:** a production company that makes pictures *with* its clients — the camera and the people are the message, the rest is quiet air. **Signature motion:** the hero is pinned; on scroll the headline wipes, the photo cluster slides and scales while the next white sheet rises over it; further down, work tiles settle from a 5° tilt.

### (2) Our translation (same composition, same motion idea, same image type: photo)
A quiet stone-mist studio screen. At left: "We shoot your whole range" running into a light "in a day". Right against it, the **product grid**: three equal high-key photographs fused in one chamfered polygon — **H1** photographer Dev adjusting a large softbox over a row of glass bottles on a white sweep while Marta checks the tether screen; **H2** close-up of the bottle row on the sweep under the softbox; **H3** the finished packshot on the studio monitor beside a hand holding a colour card. This is the row signature scene and the direct counterpart of amana's "people with a camera" triptych: the camera, the people and the product together. Story: "send us the range, we shoot it with you on one sweep, you approve as we go." Motion: on scroll the triptych columns drift (parallax) while the white statement sheet rises over the hero foot; then the dark work tiles settle from a tilt.

## 3. Home page

### First screen composition
**hero_composition: product-grid · hero_text: beside-photo · hero_footer: logo-row · hero_fingerprint: product-grid/beside-photo/logo-row**

- **product-grid:** the photos are a product grid, not a full-bleed backdrop: one Group `.kr-triptych` (x 760–1344 at 1440 = 584 × 460, y 190–650) with `clip-path: polygon(25% 0, 100% 0, 100% 75%, 75% 100%, 0 100%, 0 25%)` (RT(e) shape; the tiny rounded ends of the reference may be added with the extra points `100% 73.3%, 99.8% 74.7%, 99% 75.9%` / `0.2% 25.3%, 1% 24.1%`). Inside: three columns, each 193.3 px wide, gap 2 px (the mist shows through as a seam), each column a `figure.wp-block-image.sp-parallax` whose `img` is a direct child (`object-fit: cover`, focal points: H1 55% 40% — softbox and photographer's hands in frame; H2 50% 60% — bottle row; H3 50% 45% — monitor). The clip-path is on `.kr-triptych` (a non-observed wrapper), never on the `.sp-parallax` figures. At 1280: 520 × 410. Columns never collapse to one photo at ≥782.
- **beside-photo:** text block in the left column on the mist, never over the photos: x 96–720. Top to bottom: eyebrow (dot cluster + "Product photography studio · Harwick", Albert Sans 500 12 px, letter-spacing .04em, `#4F4A40`); H1 "We shoot your whole range" Schibsted Grotesk 600, `clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)` (88 px at 1440), line-height 1.0, letter-spacing −0.02em, colour `#47393F`, 3 lines; "in a day" Schibsted 300, 2.5rem, same colour, inline after "range" (baseline-aligned, it sits at the polygon's left edge like amana's "with"); lede Albert Sans 18 px / 1.6, `#2C2A26`, max 34ch ("Catalogue and online-shop photography in batches: one sweep, one light, up to 60 products a day, every first frame approved on screen while you watch."); then a row: dark pill "Book a shoot" (§5) + text link "See the day rate ↓" (`#rate`, underline 1 px). Vertical centre of the text block ≈ centre of the triptych.
- **logo-row:** at the hero foot, y 790–860, x 96–1344: 1 px rule `rgba(21,22,20,.14)` on top; left label "Recently on our sweep" Albert Sans 500 12 px `#4F4A40`; then 5 invented wordmarks (§0) typeset in Schibsted Grotesk 600 15 px caps, letter-spacing .14em, `#5E594E` (opacity feel of client logos), evenly spaced (`justify-content: space-between`). Text, not images, editable in the editor. Not links.
- Ground: `linear-gradient(100deg, #BFC9BD 0%, #C8CBBE 45%, #CFC4B4 100%)` + grain: inline SVG `feTurbulence` (baseFrequency .9, 2 octaves) as `background-image` layer at opacity .16, `mix-blend-mode: multiply` on a pseudo-free extra div `.kr-grain` (decorative, `aria-hidden`). Separate cell x 1280–1432 (152 × 64) "Book a shoot", dark `#151614` r16, white 14 px 600 text, 8 px gap from the bar. Exactly one top menu.
- Bottom of the first screen at 900: the statement sheet edge (white, r16 top corners) peeks at y ≈ 872.
- Saturated share of the first screen: mist S ≈ 6–13% (below the 0.5 threshold) → 0%; photos high-key with clear/amber glass and stone-grey sweep: **target ≤ 3%** (hard max 15%, row 30%). No saturated product colours larger than one bottle.
