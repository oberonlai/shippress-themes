## Story
Ines Calloway founded Kirinuki Studio in 2014 after years inside high-volume commercial catalogue houses. She wanted to build a quieter, more disciplined studio environment focused entirely on product photography in calibrated batches, replacing hurried multi-set churn with steady sweep workflows, repeatable lighting, and direct collaboration between client and crew on the studio floor.

Operating from Unit 4 at Wharf Yard on Lantern Street in Harwick, the studio has expanded into two dedicated bays. Bay A houses a 6-metre seamless white sweep beneath an overhead strobe and softbox rig engineered for packshot runs and ghost-mannequin apparel. Bay B provides a generous daylight set room for lifestyle tableware, natural textures, and styled morning light.

Over more than a decade of daily shooting, our working standard has remained transparent and fixed. Clients review every initial frame live at the digital tech station as each batch begins, followed by an online proofing gallery within three working days and retouched, marketplace-ready master files delivered within five working days.

## A day at the studio (the daily routine)
The daily routine of a shoot day: load-in from 08:30, a shot-list check, first frames on the tether screen at 09:00 and your yes before the batch begins, steady shooting through the approved list on the white sweep or in daylight, then the files: an online gallery within 3 working days and retouched finals within 5. Before the day, Theo hosts a shot-list call two working days ahead.
