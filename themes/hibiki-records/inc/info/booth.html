Booth hours

Every day the shop is open, from opening until one hour before closing.

Slots

45 minutes, starting on the hour. The last slot starts an hour before closing.

People

Up to two people. Children are welcome with a grown-up.

Fee

Free with any record bought that day; otherwise $6 a slot, taken off anything you buy within the week.

In the booth

A belt-drive turntable with a fresh stylus, a pair of open-back headphones (a second pair on request) and a pair of small speakers, a chair each and a lamp.

How to book

Send the request form on the Listening booth page, ring the shop or ask at the counter. Bring a list, or ask us to pull a stack for you.
