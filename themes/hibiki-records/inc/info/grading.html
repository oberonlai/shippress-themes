Grading

Mint · Near Mint · VG+ · VG — graded by eye under the lamp and by ear on the booth turntable.

Mint (M)

Sealed, or never played. New records are always Mint.

Near Mint (NM)

Played a handful of times. No marks you can see in the light or hear in the quiet passages.

Very Good Plus (VG+)

Light signs of play: a few faint hairlines, a little sleeve wear. Plays quietly from start to finish.

Very Good (VG)

Surface noise you will hear between tracks, never a skip. Priced to match, and marked on the sleeve.

Played through

Every used record is cleaned on the machine and played through, both sides, before it is graded.

Packing

The disc travels outside its sleeve, in a fresh inner sleeve, between two boards in a stiff mailer, so seams never split.

Shipping

Sent within two working days. A flat $6 nationwide, free over $80. If a record does not play as graded, send it back within 14 days.
