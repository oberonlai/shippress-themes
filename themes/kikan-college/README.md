# Kikan College

Kikan College is a block theme for a plumbing and heating apprenticeship college for adults: a two-year course, one year at the bench and one year placed with a working firm. The home page opens on a rounded framed window with a navy panel, vertical labels, a copper slash and a low-key photograph, then a stats row, tinted tiles with white plates, a proportional day strip, the two years, a parallax hall band, placements, instructors, apprentice stories, a fee table and a taster Saturday band. Copy and photography are the college's own; quiet navy and cool grey with one copper accent.

- Home: announcement strip, split header, framed window hero with stats row, 3 x 2 plate tiles, who the course is for, the day strip, the two years, parallax hall band, placements ledger, instructors, story tiles, fee table, taster Saturday band, footer with the college letter slot.
- Patterns: `patterns/front-*.php` (home), `header-main`, `footer-main`, `page-contact`, `page-newsletter`, `tpl-home` (posts list).
- Scroll: `sp-reveal--wipe` on numbered heads, `sp-reveal--zoom` in `sp-stagger` on tiles, `sp-reveal--up` on prose and cards, `sp-count` on stats and placement numbers, `sp-parallax` on the hero photo and the hall band.
- Fonts: Instrument Sans, Source Sans 3, Noto Sans TC, Noto Sans JP (OFL-1.1).
- Palette: katsu-iro `#3C4F62`, panel `#34404C`, copper `#B8683A`, ground `#F4F6F8`.
