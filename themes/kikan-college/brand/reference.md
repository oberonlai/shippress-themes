## Key visual
Reference first screen: a rounded window holding a low-key photograph of a ground-crew worker signalling a plane at dusk, flanked by two bright panels with vertical text (intake date, course name) and cut by a red diagonal slash. Story: "there is a job that keeps things safe on the ground." Signature motion: video loop, characters rising in headings, pill rule growing.
Ours: a low-key photograph of an apprentice in navy overalls checking a soldered copper joint on a practice rig under a task lamp, in the same r40 window, beside a katsu-iro panel with vertical intake / course labels and the headline "Warm homes start at the bench.", a copper slash on the join, stats row (2 years / 4 + 1 days / 24 places / 18 partners) at the foot. Story: a trade that keeps buildings warm, learned at the bench. Motion: the window video becomes slow photo parallax; stats count up. Full KV checklist in design-spec.md section 2.
