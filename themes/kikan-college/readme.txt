Kikan College is a block theme for a plumbing and heating apprenticeship college for adults: a two-year course, one year at the bench and one year placed with a working firm. The home page opens on a rounded framed window with a navy panel, vertical labels, a copper slash and a low-key photograph, then a stats row, tinted tiles with white plates, a proportional day strip, the two years, a parallax hall band, placements, instructors, apprentice stories, a fee table and a taster Saturday band. Copy and photography are the college's own; quiet navy and cool grey with one copper accent.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns. Fonts: Instrument Sans, Source Sans 3, Noto Sans TC and Noto Sans JP (all SIL Open Font License 1.1).

== Installation ==

1. Download kikan-college.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kikan-college.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kikan College.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, a post with category and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Languages: the sample content comes in English (demo-content.json), with Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German following. The importer uses the `shippress_demo_lang` option (set by ShipPress from its interface language), else the site language.

Optional forms: the contact and college letter pages and the footer subscribe area use a dynamic form block (inc/shippress-forms.php). Without a form plugin the contact page keeps an email button; the college letter shows an email field with a submit button that opens the visitor's email app with the request ready to send (the theme sends nothing itself). With Contact Form 7 or WPForms (contact), or MailPoet (explicit form ID) or Mailchimp for WP (newsletter), that plugin's form is shown instead.

Scroll effects: assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js (reveal, stagger, count and parallax). They respect prefers-reduced-motion and show everything without JavaScript.

WooCommerce: support is declared; the theme works fully without it.
