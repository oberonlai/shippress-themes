# Oto Audio - brand brief

Online hi-fi shop and listening room in Rotterdam (headphones, amplifiers, a DAC and speakers). Sample content is planned in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German; stage 1 ships the English home page. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Oto Audio.
- **Location:** shop and listening room at Pelgrimsgang 14, 3011 Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional address). EUR (€) prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2012.
- **Hours:** Tuesday–Friday 11:00–19:00, Saturday 10:00–17:00; closed Sunday and Monday. Listening hours by appointment, walk-ins welcome when the room is free. Online orders leave Monday–Friday, within 1 working day before 15:00.
- **Voice:** Calm and concrete, the way a shop sounds when it would rather you hear a product than read a claim about it. Prices, times and sound are given as fixed figures and plain words, with no flourish around them.

## History
Oto Audio opened in 2012, when Ines Vermeulen and Tomasz Wójcik took a former print shop at Pelgrimsgang 14 and made it a hi-fi shop with a listening room beside the bench. The address is still Pelgrimsgang 14, 3011 Rotterdam, Netherlands. Ines buys the range and has hosted the listening room since that first year. Tomasz is co-founder and bench technician. He designs the two house amplifiers: the A10 for the desk and the A40 for the room.

The listening room is 28 m², with treated walls, and it is where the range is chosen. A listening hour is 60 minutes, free, and held by appointment. The room is open Tuesday to Friday, 11:00–19:00, and Saturday, 10:00–17:00, and it is closed Sunday and Monday. You bring your own music. We play it at the same volume and compare up to three headphones or two speaker pairs, then write the sound in ordinary words before we measure. A headphone can also go home for 10 days. That trial is €25, and the fee is credited when you buy.

The house range is eight products, tuned in that room: the H1 Open at €349, the H2 Closed at €299, the P7 Planar at €890, the A10 at €420, the A40 at €1,290, the D2 at €260, the S5 at €780 and the S8 at €2,150. Seven people work here. Amara Nwosu specialises in headphones, Lucas Moreau makes speaker placement visits, Daniel Reyes sets up DACs and streaming, Sofie Brandt repairs, re-cables and fits pads, and Yara Haddad looks after orders, shipping and returns. Orders placed before 15:00 on a weekday leave within one working day. EU shipping is free over €150, returns run to 30 days, and the eight house products carry a 2-year warranty.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. **Photo-left bleed** (CAMITA, https://camitabox.com): one photograph flush to the left window edge at 75.5% width, with the heading straddling its right edge; vertical caption on the far right; seven plain nav items spread across the full width.
2. **Product trio** (Luci KIWA, https://luci.co.jp/led/en/pickup/kiwa): three 3:4 cards with a 20 px gap, name at the foot, a round outline plus button, bracketed name and an outline pill below, on a white band between ink sections.
3. **Crosshair frame** (Luci KIWA): comparison tables on ink `#16160E` inside 1 px `#5D5D58` corner brackets with plus marks at the column boundaries.
Supporting: hollow-circle section labels, line-drawn category icons with ink numeral chips, a 480 px parallax band, fade-up and wipe reveals.
