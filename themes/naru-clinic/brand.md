## History
Naru Clinic was founded in 2012 by occupational physician Dr. Helena Varga at 2F, 41 Foundry Lane, Westmere. Established to support local manufacturing plants, logistics hubs, and corporate offices, the clinic emerged from a clear observation: work-related physical strain was routinely left unaddressed until it resulted in prolonged sick leave. Dr. Varga sought to create a medical practice dedicated entirely to early occupational intervention, where clinical expertise meets practical workplace realities.

Over the following years, the practice expanded to assemble an interdisciplinary team of occupational health nurses, physiotherapists, ergonomists, and screening technicians. By stepping directly onto factory floors and visiting office workstations, the clinic moved occupational health beyond passive compliance and into active prevention. The team introduced structured walk-throughs, movement assessments, and evidence-based rehabilitation schedules that allow employers and employees to address physical strain together.

Today, Naru Clinic delivers comprehensive workplace health assessments, gradual return-to-work programmes, and statutory medical screenings across the region. With dedicated facilities for physical assessments, audiometry, and vision testing, alongside on-site workplace visits on Tuesdays and Thursdays, the clinic remains committed to a practical, calm, and collaborative standard of workplace care that keeps workforces active, safe, and working comfortably.

## Assessment process
- 1 Talk: A 20-minute clinical history to understand daily tasks, pain patterns, and previous injury.
- 2 Look: A movement and posture check examining the shoulder, lower back, and wrist.
- 3 Visit: An evaluation of the work station conducted on site or reviewed by photo and video.
- 4 Plan: A practical, written action plan delivered to worker and, with consent, employer within 3 working days.
