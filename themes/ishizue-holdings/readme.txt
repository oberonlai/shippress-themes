Ishizue Holdings is a business theme for Ishizue Holdings B.V., the privately held group founded in Rotterdam in 1962 by Kenzo Ishizue and Anneke de Vries. It presents the six group companies, the long ownership record, investor notices and the shared-services fees in calm, specific English. Figures, dates and the head-office address at Maaskade 41 stay in euros and in the form the group publishes. A tagline sits beside a rounded photograph that bleeds off the right edge, with a curve-ended timeline line, bracket-phrase marquee rows and a sticky stack of six flat-tinted company cards. Noto Serif for headings, Noto Sans for reading. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; scroll effects stop under reduced motion.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download ishizue-holdings.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/ishizue-holdings.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Ishizue Holdings.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. The sample content is in English (demo-content.json); the importer uses the `shippress_demo_lang` option or the site language.

The group, its companies, people, address, figures and fees are fictional.
