# Ishizue Holdings - brand brief

## Concept
Ishizue Holdings B.V. is a privately held group of six companies in Northern Europe, founded in Rotterdam in 1962. "Ishizue" means foundation stone. The site presents the group through its history: a tagline beside a rounded photograph of the head office, a dated timeline line, the six companies in the order they joined, governance, investor notices and a route for owners who want to pass a company on. Fictional company; the people, addresses and figures are invented for the theme.

## Story
Ishizue Holdings B.V. was founded in 1962 in Rotterdam by Kenzo Ishizue and Anneke de Vries. They began as a stone and aggregates trader on the Maas, supplying material for building work in the delta. The word ishizue means foundation stone. That first trade is still in the group as Ishizue Materials, based in Rotterdam, and the holding company has owned its businesses for the long term ever since. The habit of the house was set there: buy something sound, and stay with it.

The group grew by taking whole companies and leaving their names and local management in place. Northquay Logistics joined from Antwerp in 1978, in warehousing and regional freight. In 1987 the head office moved to Maaskade 41, 3071 NE Rotterdam, and it has remained there. Lindholm Precision, a maker of machined components in Gothenburg, joined in 1994. In 2003 Ishizue Holdings B.V. was formed, the Ishizue Foundation became the majority owner, and the employee share plan began. Verda Facilities joined from Utrecht in 2009, Calder Food Partners from Hamburg in 2016, and Ishizue Renewables from Aarhus in 2021.

The group now employs 2,860 colleagues in nine countries: the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, Denmark, Sweden, Poland, France, Spain and Ireland. Fifty-eight of those colleagues work at the holding office on Maaskade 41. Six companies sit under one private owner. Reports are published voluntarily, including an annual report every March and the interim report for the first half of 2026, published on 28 August 2026. Companies that join keep their names, their sites and their local management, and the holding company takes the next decision only when the last one has settled.

## Voice
The voice is calm, concrete and unhurried, the way a privately held group speaks to owners, colleagues and readers of its reports. It names the company, the year and the place, and it stops when the fact has been said.
