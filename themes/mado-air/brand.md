## Story
Mado Home Studio was founded in 2021 by industrial designer Ines Albrecht and airflow engineer Tomás Reyes in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Working from their design studio and test room, they began with a simple observation: modern city apartments frequently trap stale air, yet opening windows often admits cold winter drafts, outdoor exhaust, and street noise. They set out to build a compact air purifier that would create the calm, refreshing feeling of an open window while keeping the glass firmly closed.

Rather than designing a typical plastic appliance that begs to be hidden in a dark corner, Ines Albrecht conceived Mado Air as a quiet piece of furniture tailored for deep windowsills and low shelves. In Rotterdam, acoustician Priya Raman refined the internal ducting and motor dampening to achieve a whisper-quiet 19 dB sound profile in Night mode. Meanwhile, textile designer Léa Moreau developed the light warm-grey woven fabric wrapping the lower intake, balancing airflow permeability with durable, tactile warmth.

Engineered around an H13-grade pleated filter medium and an activated carbon layer, Mado Air delivers 160 m³/h of clean air to rooms up to 22 m². Today, under the guidance of product lead Jonah Whitfield, test-room coordinator Hana Sato-Lindgren, and customer support lead Kwame Mensah, the Rotterdam team prepares for pre-order shipments in March 2027 with an enduring commitment to honest acoustic testing, deliberate materials, and calm domestic design.

## Voice
The voice of Mado Air is calm, precise, and understated, describing the lived feeling of a room without hyperbole or artificial urgency. It relies strictly on measured facts from our Rotterdam test room, avoiding medical claims, percentages, and aggressive marketing jargon.

Compact square air purifier, 22 × 22 × 38 cm, 2.6 kg. Chalk and Graphite. 160 m³/h clean air delivery, rooms up to 22 m², 19 dB (Night) to 46 dB (Boost), 3–28 W, H13-grade pleated medium + activated carbon layer (change every 6 months), 3-year warranty.

## People
- **Ines Albrecht**, Co-founder, industrial design. Ines directs the physical form, proportions, and presence of Mado Air. She focuses on creating quiet domestic objects that sit naturally on windowsills alongside books and everyday items.
- **Tomás Reyes**, Co-founder, airflow engineering. Tomás engineers the internal airflow geometry and low-centrifugal fan path in our Rotterdam test room. His work delivers 160 m³/h of balanced clean air delivery through a compact square frame.
- **Priya Raman**, Acoustics. Priya oversees sound tuning and vibration isolation for the motor and fan blades. Her acoustic engineering achieved the whisper-quiet 19 dB sound profile in Night mode.
- **Jonah Whitfield**, Product lead, pre-order and launch. Jonah coordinates product preparation, parts scheduling, and pre-order fulfilment. He ensures all pre-orders progress smoothly toward delivery in March 2027.
- **Léa Moreau**, Materials and fabric. Léa selects and tests the warm-grey woven textiles and matte finishes. Her work balances air intake permeability with durable, furniture-grade tactile qualities.
- **Kwame Mensah**, Customer support lead. Kwame manages customer support from Rotterdam, assisting customers Monday through Friday from 9:00 to 17:00 CET. He helps with setup enquiries, filter replacements, and product care.
- **Hana Sato-Lindgren**, Test-room coordinator. Hana coordinates ongoing trials in our Rotterdam test room and oversees the 60-day home trial programme. She gathers feedback from early testers living with units in diverse homes.
