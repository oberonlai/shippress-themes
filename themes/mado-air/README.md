# Mado Air

A WordPress block theme (landing track) for a fictional compact air purifier, by ShipPress. English home page, twelve inner pages and one journal article are included; other languages follow.

Signature techniques (reference: balmuda.com/jp/pure/): a two-tier header (dark brand bar + white sticky product bar with pills), a grey technology band with a tall line-art cutaway cropped by the band, and a dark full-bleed macro design band with parallax. Hero: crossfading window-sill photographs, lockup lower left, round play button lower right.

Patterns: `front-hero`, `front-info`, `front-why`, `front-how`, `front-design`, `front-night`, `front-gallery`, `front-specs`, `front-reviews`, `front-preorder`, `front-faq`, `front-close`. Scroll effects: `assets/sp-scroll.css|js` (reveal, stagger, count, wipe, parallax). Fonts: Inter Tight, Manrope. Brand notes: `brand.md`.

Palette: white, soft grey `#E9E9E9`, ink `#333333`, dark `#1F1F1F`, cyan `#4DC0DB` (pills and airflow only), mouse grey `#8E9194`. Fonts: Inter Tight and Manrope (OFL-1.1). Works without WooCommerce.
