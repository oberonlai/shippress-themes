# Toso Paints - brand brief

Paint and wallpaper shop with its own mixing counter in Rotterdam, Netherlands. All names, places, people and figures are fictional. English home page (stage 1); the other languages follow in stage 2.

## Concept
- **Name:** Toso Paints. Location: Verfstraat 18, 3011 Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional street). EUR prices.
- **Founded:** 2009 by Ines Valk, a former decorator, with one mixing counter and a tinting machine.
- **People:** Ines Valk (founder, mixing counter), Amara Osei (colour matcher, in-home visits), Lena Brandt (tinting machine and recipe file), Tomás Herrera (wallpaper lead), Yusuf Demir (trade counter, orders and delivery), Mei Lindgren (swatch table, Saturdays), Pieter de Wit (decorator in residence).
- **Audience:** people finishing a room: renters and owners repainting a wall, decorators buying for a job, anyone matching a colour to a photo.
- **Tone:** calm, unhurried and practical. Plain words about materials, drying times and wall preparation. No guarantees, awards, press or customer counts.
- **Story line:** "A colour is chosen against the room, not the chip."

## A day at the shop
The daily routine is quiet. The tinting machine is switched on at 09:00, drawdown cards from yesterday are checked by the window, deliveries for orders placed before 15:00 are loaded for the next working day, and Amara leaves for in-home visits. On Saturdays Mei sets up the swatch table from 10:00 to 13:00.

## Fixed numbers (identical everywhere)
- Hours: Tue-Fri 09:00-18:00, Sat 09:00-17:00 (swatch table 10:00-13:00), Sun and Mon closed.
- Mixed paint ready to collect in 2 hours; orders before 15:00 (Tue-Sat) delivered in Rotterdam the next working day; wallpaper not in stock ordered in 3-5 working days. Delivery in Rotterdam EUR 9, free over EUR 150; click and collect free. Returns of unopened stock within 14 days; mixed paint not returnable.
- Colour match at the counter free; in-home colour match visit EUR 45 (deducted from orders over EUR 200). A drawdown card dries 20 minutes.
- Range: House Matt EUR 58 (2.5 L), Wood & Metal Eggshell EUR 34 (1 L), Lime Wash EUR 46 (2.5 L), Sample Pot EUR 6 (100 ml), Linen-Weave Wallpaper EUR 72 (roll 10.05 x 0.52 m), Grasscloth Wallpaper EUR 138 (roll 5.5 x 0.91 m).

## Signature techniques
1. Three equal photo columns with a hairline room-elevation drawing across the seams and a stacked caps menu at the top left (from Open Rise).
2. Ruled statement band: headline, full-width rule, label with a slanted underline, paragraph from 50% (Open Rise).
3. Sticky full-bleed chapters Paint / Paper / Mix with a counter and tick pager (Open Rise).
4. Round-thumbnail range strip, caps marquee and offset caption rows (Fredrik).

## Pages
Home, Ranges (shop), Colour Mixing, Wallpaper, Order & Collection, About, Journal (posts page), Newsletter, Visit (contact). Stage 1 builds the home page; the other pages hold short text until stage 2.

## Photography
Low-key documentary photographs generated with an AI image tool: side light from a tall window on the left, moss green and charcoal shadows, matte surfaces, no text, logos or labels.
