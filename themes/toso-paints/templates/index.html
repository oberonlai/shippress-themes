What field notes are recorded from the mixing counter?

Our journal exists to record the daily lessons learned at our Verfstraat counter and on decorating sites across Rotterdam. Rather than discussing fleeting seasonal fashion, we write about durable materials, pigment behaviour, and how architectural light alters paint film. Here you will find detailed accounts of real projects, including our lime-wash application over old emulsion in a Noord rental flat and wallpaper quantity calculations for steep staircases in Delfshaven. We explain how our team approaches tricky architectural surfaces and why careful preparation saves time and paint.

What topics are covered in our core articles?

Our core archive features six foundational guides authored by our shop specialists. Mei Lindgren describes what happens at the Saturday swatch table (10:00–13:00), showing how homeowners paint sample boards side by side. We explain why we sell a €6 sample pot before a tin, and examine how one green behaves across north and south light. Tomás Herrera outlines hanging the first drop straight for linen and grasscloth papers.
