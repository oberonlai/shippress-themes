# Undo Ba

A restrained, elegant WordPress **block theme** for **small strength and conditioning studios**, boutique gyms and personal trainers. *Undo ba* is an old word for a training ground. The sample studio, **Undo Ba**, is a fictional small-group studio in Kiyosumi, Tokyo, with three fictional coaches (Ren Takamori, Mio Aranami, Daichi Kurose). Address, phone, email and prices are placeholders.

## Who it is for

- **Industry:** fitness-gym: strength studios, small gyms, personal training.
- **Site type:** health. Programs, coaches, memberships and prices, about, a journal, a monthly newsletter and trial booking.
- **Mood:** minimal, elegant; Japanese minimal, quiet and refined. No poster or sports-advert look.

## Design concept

| Axis | Choice |
|------|--------|
| Palette (neutral, light) | **Stone** `#EFECE6` ground, **paper** `#F8F6F2` bands and cards, **charcoal** `#1E1F1E` text and dark bands (14.0:1), **warm grey** `#77716A` hairlines and large numerals only (4.1:1, not used for small text), **deep warm grey** `#6A645D` quiet text (5.0:1), **oxblood** `#6E2B27` the single accent, used sparingly for the primary button, links and the timeline hover (8.7:1) |
| Type | **Bellefair** (display serif) for headings, the wordmark, timeline numerals and prices; **Inter** for text, labels, navigation and forms. Bundled Latin subsets, SIL OFL 1.1, no CDN |
| Header (H8 transparent) | Wordmark left, small spaced-capital navigation right, no background: it floats over the home photograph in paper white and sits on the stone ground elsewhere. Phones get the core overlay menu on stone |
| Footer (F7 CTA band) | A charcoal band: "Train with us, quietly and well." with Book a free trial / Memberships, then a hairline and the wordmark, the synced studio details and a small note |
| Home (L12 timeline) | Full-bleed photograph, an intro with three quiet figures, the training week as a numbered timeline (01-06) beside a sticky photo, three program cards, a charcoal coach quote, memberships (synced) and the latest journal posts |
| Signature | The **rep counter**: fine 01-06 numerals in thin serif marking the week (home) and the six phases of a session (Programs) |
| Motion | None on entry; only colour changes on hover |

Deviations from the roadmap plan (#F2F2F2/#111111/#E10600 red, Anton, "athletic, high-contrast"): at the user's request the theme is elegant and restrained instead; Marcellus and Gilda Display carry Reserved Font Names, so Bellefair (no RFN) was subset.

## Single sources

- `inc/info/studio.html` → synced pattern **Undo Ba: studio address, hours, phone and email** (footer, Contact).
- `inc/info/prices.html` → synced pattern **Undo Ba: memberships and prices** (Home, Pricing), including the editable "Most chosen" badge.

## Pages

Home, About the studio, Programs, Coaches, Memberships and prices, Journal (posts page; 6 posts in Training, Recovery, Studio notes with 6 tags), The monthly letter, Book a free trial (contact), plus search and 404 templates.
