# Noka Cheese

A calm, photographic block theme for an independent cheese counter (store type; WooCommerce for board reservations and pickup).

Home: sticky split header with a double-line menu glyph, a blur-to-focus hero (stacked "Named. Cut. Wrapped." beside our own wedge line drawing and a double-rim pebble photograph), a statement beside a sticky pebble, six style cards in a two-by-three mosaic beside a second sticky pebble, a parallax counter band, three dotted-rule steps, the monger's note, the board price list, three journal thumbnails, a willow counter-letter band with the newsletter form slot and a paper arch closing band.

Signature techniques: double-rim pebble photographs, three dotted guides with a fixed double-line glyph, arch closing band, blur-to-focus first screen. Fonts: Albert Sans and Instrument Sans. See brand.md and brand/design-spec.md.
