# Noka Cheese - brand brief

Independent cheese shop with one counter and a small ageing cellar under it, at Old Dairy Yard, Brookmere. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Noka Cheese. A cheese counter where each wedge is named, cut and wrapped for the person standing in front of it.
- **Location:** Old Dairy Yard, 9 Tannery Lane, Brookmere (fictional). Phone +00 21 4418 2290, email counter@nokacheese.example. Prices in euro.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Noa Kessler, who is still head monger and buys every wheel herself.
- **Hours:** Tue-Fri 10:00-19:00, Sat 09:00-17:00, Sun 10:00-14:00, Mon closed. Board pickup from 11:00; reserve by 18:00 the day before. Pickup only, no shipping.
- **Audience:** neighbours who cook and host at home, who want to taste before they buy and want to know what they are eating, and who reserve a board the day before for a table of two to eight.
- **Tone:** Speak the way the counter does: short sentences, the cheese named by style, the person named by the work they do. Stay with what is on the board, in the cellar and on the clock; no praise, promises, awards or comparison.
- **One call to action everywhere:** "Reserve a board" (the boards category in the shop). Secondary wording only: "Visit the counter".

## History
Noka Cheese opened in 2014 as a single counter with a small ageing cellar beneath it. Noa Kessler, the founder and head monger, set one rule from the first day: every wheel is opened when a customer asks for it, cut with a wire or a two-handled knife, weighed in front of them and wrapped in two layers of cheese paper with the name and the date written on the fold. The shop has never added a second counter; boards, wedges and the Saturday tasting all leave from this one.

About sixty cheeses are on the counter at any time, kept in six styles: fresh and lactic, bloomy, washed rind, alpine, blue, and goat and sheep. Henrik Dahl, who looks after sourcing, visits the four makers twice a year. Hollin Ridge Farm supplies goat's milk cheeses, fresh and as ash logs, with kidding in spring. Ostra Creamery, where two cheesemakers work, supplies small washed-rind wheels that are finished in the Noka cellar. Vell Alpine Cooperative supplies summer-pasture wheels kept for 18 months. Lowmere Sheep Dairy makes one blue, from October to May.

Under the counter the cellar is held at 11 °C and 92 % humidity. Elif Arslan turns, brushes and washes the wheels there, and the washed rinds are washed in brine twice a week. Saturday tastings are led by Grace Owusu at 10:30 and 15:00: 45 minutes, 6 cheeses, at most 8 people.

## How a wedge is chosen (3)
- 01 Taste - tell us what it is for; we cut a sliver of two or three on the knife tip.
- 02 Cut - washed and soft rinds with the wire, hard wheels with the two-handled knife; weighed in front of you, 150 g is a good wedge for two.
- 03 Wrap - two-layer cheese paper, name and date written on the fold, a line on how to keep it.
