Seseragi House is a calm, single-column WordPress theme designed for a four-room riverside bed and breakfast. Built with classic serif typography and documentary photography layouts, it presents quiet rooms, morning breakfast rituals, and walking paths along the weir. Includes dedicated templates for room reservations, seasonal journal notes, and unhurried hospitality storytelling.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The home page opens on a 24 px framed window holding a high-key breakfast photograph, with a vertical headline and a two-column vertical lede at the right and a centred sheet on the window edge. Below it runs one long column of text at 31% of the page: the couple who keep the house with photographs hung beside the text at different heights, a wide photograph bleeding off the left edge and a title set vertically in the right margin; how a stay runs as four prose rows; the breakfast table with a slow strip of small tray photographs; a full-bleed dim river band with a vertical title and white serif text; the four rooms as a vertical run; one way to ask about dates; house notes and a river letter. Scroll effects (rise, slow strip, parallax) use the small sp-scroll script in assets/ and respect prefers-reduced-motion. The optional newsletter and contact slots work without any plugin and use Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when present.

== Installation ==

1. Download seseragi-house.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/seseragi-house.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Seseragi House. On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): the home page, the house notes with sample posts and the menu. Use Tools > Import demo content to run it again.
4. Edit everything in the block editor; the sections are block patterns in the "Seseragi House" category.

The house, its people, addresses, telephone number and prices are fictional.

== Forms ==

Ask about dates and Contact: recommended Contact Form 7 fields are name (required), email (required), arrival date (required), nights (required, minimum 2 on Friday and Saturday), room (select: Weir, Willow, Loft, Boathouse, no preference), guests (required, 1 to 3), dietary needs and message. Without a plugin the page shows a button that opens your email app. The river letter shows an email field and a button that opens your email app with the request.
