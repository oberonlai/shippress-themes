# Seseragi House — brand

## Name and place
Seseragi House is a fictional bed and breakfast of four rooms in a 1908 mill-keeper's house on the bank of the river Lisle. Address: 3 Weir Lane, Millbrook (fictional). Phone +00 31 2045 7710 (fictional), answered 08:00–20:00. Email stay@seseragihouse.example.

## Location
Millbrook, a fictional village in a green valley on the river Lisle. A train to Millbrook plus a 12-minute walk; four parking places at the house.

## Founded
Marta and Jonas Lind bought the house in 2012, spent two years on floors and windows, and opened to guests in 2014.

## People
- **Marta Lind**, host. Bakes, sets the breakfast table and answers every request about dates herself, within 24 hours.
- **Jonas Lind**, host. Makes the coffee, looks after the house and walks guests along the river to the weir at 16:00.
- **Ana Ferreira**, the village baker. Her sourdough and rye loaves arrive at the kitchen door at 06:30.
- **Lotte Brandt**, neighbour with the plum and apricot orchard. Makes the jams (plum, apricot, rhubarb).
- **Samuel Osei**, housekeeping and linen, mornings 10:00–14:00.
- **Karim Haddad**, keeps the rowing boats at the mill pond and lends one to guests, May to September.
- **Noor Lind**, Marta and Jonas's daughter. Helps at weekends and with the summer breakfasts.

## Audience
Couples, friends and families who want a handful of quiet rooms, a real breakfast and a river outside the window, and who are happy to write one email to ask about dates. Buyers of the theme: family-run bed and breakfasts and guesthouses with a few rooms.

## Tone
Calm, specific and unhurried. Short sentences about bread, rooms, hours and water; names instead of adjectives; no superlatives, awards, ratings or promises. First person plural is rare; the hosts are named.

## Story
In 2012 Marta and Jonas Lind bought the 1908 mill-keeper's house on the quiet bank of the river Lisle. Two years went into uncovering the original pine floors, freeing the old sash windows and learning the sound of the weir. In 2014 the house opened to its first guests. From the beginning they kept only four rooms, so that everyone sits at one long window table for breakfast and the hosts can know every guest by name.

## A day at the house (daily routine)
06:30 Ana's loaf arrives at the kitchen door · 08:00 the window table is set, breakfast until 10:00 · 10:00–14:00 rooms and linen · 11:00 check-out · 15:00–19:00 check-in and tea on the river terrace · 16:00 Jonas walks the bank to the weir · dusk, the lamps are lit.

## Rooms (four, same order everywhere)
1. **Weir Room** — first floor, bay window over the weir, king bed, 2 guests, 22 m². €165 per night.
2. **Willow Room** — ground floor, garden door under the willow, queen bed, 2 guests, 18 m². €140 per night.
3. **Loft Room** — top floor under the beams, skylight onto the river, king or twin, 2 guests, 24 m². €150 per night.
4. **Boathouse Room** — the small timber boathouse at the water's edge, own deck, king bed and day bed, up to 3 guests, 28 m². €195 per night.

## Rates
Willow Room €140 · Loft Room €150 · Weir Room €165 · Boathouse Room €195 (two guests, per night, breakfast included) · third guest in the Boathouse Room €35 per night (day bed and breakfast) · river basket €22 (bread, cheese, fruit and a flask for the river path, order the evening before). Breakfast for every guest is included. Minimum 2 nights on Friday and Saturday. 30% deposit to confirm; free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival.

## Hours
House phone 08:00–20:00. Check-in 15:00–19:00, check-out 11:00. Breakfast 08:00–10:00. Open all year except 6–31 January, when the house rests.

## How a stay runs (the single way to ask)
Ask (one email or the form on the ask page: dates, room, guests; Marta replies within 24 hours with the deposit link) → Arrive (15:00–19:00, tea on the river terrace, Jonas walks you to the weir) → Breakfast (08:00–10:00 at the window table) → Leave (11:00, a jar of the week's jam goes home with you). Every "Ask about dates" goes to the ask page; there is no second booking path.

## Breakfast
Ana's sourdough and rye · Lotte's jams (plum, apricot, rhubarb) · butter and soft cheese from the valley · eggs cooked to order · yoghurt with stewed fruit · coffee by Jonas, tea, fresh juice. Dietary needs: tell Marta when you ask.
