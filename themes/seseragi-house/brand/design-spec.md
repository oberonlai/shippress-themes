## 0. Fixed brand facts (identical on every page and in all five languages)

- **Seseragi House** — a bed and breakfast of **4 rooms** in a 1908 mill-keeper's house on the bank of the river Lisle; Marta and Jonas Lind bought it in **2012** and opened in **2014**. Breakfast is cooked in the house kitchen and set at the long window table above the water.
- Address: **3 Weir Lane, Millbrook** (fictional, international-neutral; local address order per language in brand.md). Phone **+00 31 2045 7710** (fictional). Email `stay@seseragihouse.example` (the same address in every mailto, form fallback and footer).
- **Hours:** house phone **08:00–20:00**. Check-in **15:00–19:00**, check-out **11:00**. Breakfast **08:00–10:00** (the table is set at eight). Open all year except **6–31 January** (the house rests). Minimum **2 nights** on Friday and Saturday.
- **Rooms (4, each named once, same order everywhere):**
  1. **Weir Room** — first floor, bay window over the weir, king bed, 2 guests, 22 m².
  2. **Willow Room** — ground floor, garden door under the willow, queen bed, 2 guests, 18 m².
  3. **Loft Room** — top floor under the beams, skylight onto the river, king or twin, 2 guests, 24 m².
  4. **Boathouse Room** — the small timber boathouse at the water's edge, own deck, king + day bed, up to 3 guests, 28 m².
- **Rates (6 items, EUR in every language, no "from"; breakfast for every guest included):**

  | Loft Room | 2 guests, per night, breakfast included | €150 |

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (koti-okinawa.com)
**Draws:** a full-bleed daylight photograph of an old tiled-roof house: the pale roof across the top half, the timber lattice windows with small bulbs lit inside across the bottom half, garden leaves and blossoms blurred in the foreground (left and right edges). The wordmark (mark + name, white) sits top-left over the roof with a small tracked label above it; a short list of links stacks at the lower left; at the right edge a **tall vertical white headline** (~56 px) hangs from the top, with a **two-column vertical lede** (~16 px / 2.0) just to its left. A pale announcement strip runs above the photo.
**Story:** "An old house you step into; tea and sweets served as they always were here." The house itself is the product.
**Signature motion:** the film breathes slowly (the photo is a gently moving clip); headline characters rise one by one (translateY .5em + 7° rotation, .62 s, 0.027 s step); the nav flips from white to ink when the dark section passes.

### (2) Our translation (same composition, same motion idea, same image type: high-key documentary photography)
**Seseragi House from the river bank on a bright morning:** the timber-and-white house with its lit bay window (the Weir Room) above the water; through the open window we see the long window table — **Marta setting out a board of bread and three jars of jam while a guest pours coffee** — and the river running past in the lower foreground with reeds blurred at the edges. Story: **"Breakfast above the water"** — the house and its table are the product; the couple keep it. Same layout as the reference (house photo carrying the screen, wordmark top-left over the photo, link list lower-left as the overlay menu, tall vertical headline at the right with a two-column vertical lede beside it), same motion idea (the photo settles and drifts slowly; the headline arrives once on load), same image type (daylight documentary photograph). The row's framed window keeps the whole scene inside a 24 px frame; the centred narrow column (row first_screen) holds the one-sentence invitation at the base of the window. This is the reference scene moved to our river; it is not dropped.
