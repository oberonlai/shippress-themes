# Ito HR Partners - brand brief

HR and employment-law consultancy in Dublin, Ireland, for companies of 15 to 400 people. Sample content in English (stage 1: home page); Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

The work covers payroll compliance, policies and handbooks, workplace investigations and retainer support. Seven advisers carry it: employment law, HR policy, payroll, investigations, onboarding and contracts, and the management of retainers.
- **Audience:** founders, operations leads and finance directors of growing companies who want people matters settled early, in writing, at a fee agreed first.
- **Voice:** Sound like a careful adviser in a first meeting: calm, concrete, and willing to name the fee and the limit. Use short sentences, the published figures, and no promise the matter itself does not already make.

## Story
Ito HR Partners opened in Dublin in 2014. Erika Ito founded the practice after twelve years in employment law, wanting growing companies to settle people questions early, in writing, for a fee agreed before anyone starts. The office is at 24 Harcourt Lane, Dublin 2, D02 X940. Advisers are there Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:30 Irish time, and the same people also meet by video.

The practice works with companies of 15 to 400 people under Irish and EU employment law. The advice itself is limited to Irish law. Remote clients across the EU are welcome on that basis. When a question belongs in another jurisdiction, the firm says so and turns to partner firms, which are not named on this site. The work covers payroll compliance, policies and handbooks, workplace investigations and retainer support. Seven advisers carry it: employment law, HR policy, payroll, investigations, onboarding and contracts, and the management of retainers.

Fees are published in euro and exclude VAT at 23%. A scoping call of 30 minutes costs nothing. An HR health check of contracts, policies and payroll set-up is €1,450, with a written report in 10 working days. The path after that call does not change with the service. A written fixed quote arrives within two working days, the work proceeds with a weekly check-in, and the matter ends with a written handover plus 30 days of follow-up questions. Enquiries are answered within one working day.

## Services and fixed fees (EUR, excl. VAT 23%)
- Scoping call: €0. 30 minutes, by video or at the Dublin office.
- HR health check: €1,450 fixed. Contracts, policies and payroll set-up reviewed, with a written report in 10 working days.
- Contract set: €980 fixed. Templates for 3 role types.
- Payroll compliance review: €1,900 fixed. One payroll cycle, up to 150 staff. Advisory only; the firm does not run payroll.
- Employee handbook & policies: €2,400 fixed. Up to 12 policies.
- Workplace investigation: from €3,800. One complaint, up to 6 interviews, and a findings report.
- Retainer Essentials: €890 / month. Up to 6 hours a month, with a reply within 1 working day. Three months minimum, then monthly.
- Retainer Growth: €1,850 / month. Up to 14 hours a month, plus a quarterly review. Three months minimum, then monthly.

3. The work, with a weekly check-in.
4. A written handover, plus 30 days of follow-up questions included.
