Ito HR Partners is a business theme for a Dublin consultancy in HR and employment law, working with companies of 15 to 400 people. The sample copy publishes fixed fees for payroll compliance, handbooks and policies, workplace investigations and retainer support, and introduces the seven advisers and three anonymised client stories. Fees are in euro and exclude VAT at 23%. A one-line PT Serif headline with four tick-disc service tags sits over a full-bleed loop of tall rounded photo cards with a floating slate fixed-fee pill; white approach cards with vertical labels float over a blurred photograph that moves slower than the page. PT Serif for headings, PT Sans for reading. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; reveals and the photo loop stop under reduced motion.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download ito-hr-partners.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/ito-hr-partners.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Ito HR Partners.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, three client stories and a note with two categories, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. The sample content is in English (demo-content.json); the importer uses the `shippress_demo_lang` option or the site language.

The practice, its advisers, address, telephone number, fees and client stories are fictional.
