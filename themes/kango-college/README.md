# Kango College

A serif block theme for a nursing college. The home page opens on a film-grain skills-lab photograph with a flat wine notice card across the seam, then three glyph cells, a glow lead with a sideways quote strip, the teaching day in timed rows, ruled admissions and lab sections, a full-bleed building band and a short list of notes.

- Fonts: Fraunces (headings), Source Serif 4 (reading), Noto Serif TC / JP for zh-TW and ja headings. All OFL-1.1, bundled in `assets/fonts`.
- Palette: paper `#F7F6F5`, band `#EFEAE6`, ink `#070B0D`, wine `#941E57`, rose glow `#F3E6EA`, one lilac band `#E6E5EF`.
- Motion: `sp-reveal`, `sp-stagger` and a parallax on the hero photo and the building band; reduced motion shows the final state.
- Demo content: home, eight inner pages, a notes index and six sample posts, imported with the theme (`demo-content.json`).
- Images: AI-generated, CC0, listed in `readme.txt`.
- Brand and design notes: `brand.md`, `brand/`.
