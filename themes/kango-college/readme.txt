Kango College is a serif block theme for a nursing college in Wrenfield, founded in 1987 as a two-room teaching ward. Its pages follow the teaching day, three programmes, placement hours, admissions dates and fees in US dollars, set in quiet photographic type. Graduates may apply to the regional nursing register; registration is decided by the register, not by the college.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The home page opens on a skills-lab photograph with the headline beside it, a flat wine notice card across the seam and a day line of six times, then runs through three glyph cells, a glow lead with a sideways quote strip, the teaching day in timed rows, who the course admits, the lab hour on a lilac band, a full-bleed building, ruled note rows and an open lab morning.

The inner pages (about, programmes, teaching day, skills lab, admissions, fees, faculty, placements, contact), a notes index and six sample posts are imported with the theme.

== Installation ==

1. Download kango-college.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kango-college.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kango College.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images and the menu, and sets the front page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The college, its town, hospitals, people, address, telephone number and fees are fictional.
