# Kango College — brand

## Name and place
Kango College, a fictional college of nursing at 120 Infirmary Road, Wrenfield. Phone 555-0176. Email admissions@kangocollege.example. All fees are in US dollars.

## Founded
1987, as a two-room teaching ward. The address has not changed.

## People (all fictional)
- Dr. Ingrid Solheim, Dean. She keeps the teaching day in its order, from handover at 08:00 to debrief at 15:30.
- Tomás Arriaga RN, Head of the Skills Lab. He leads the lab hour at 10:30.
- Yuna Seo RN, simulation ward instructor.
- Nadia Rahimi RN, placements lead.
- Dr. Kwame Boateng, bioscience lecturer.
- Marguerite Dubois, admissions officer.
- Leila Haddad, second-year student. Noah Petrov, first-year student.

## Audience
Adults who are choosing nursing as a first course or a return to it: school leavers with biology or a science course, and returners to practice. Also the families and employers who ask how the course is run.

## Tone
Calm, specific and adult, like a college office explaining a day rather than selling a result. Name the hour, the place, the fee or the person. No rankings, rates, awards or promises about registration.

## The daily routine (a teaching day)
A teaching day runs Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:30, in six stations:
- 08:00 Handover practice (practice)
- 08:30 Bioscience lecture (theory)
- 10:30 The lab hour (practice): 6 students to 1 instructor, the same skill until it is steady
- 12:30 Lunch (break)
- 13:30 Ward simulation (practice)
- 15:30 Debrief, until 16:30 (reflection)

## Admissions process
1. Apply online. 2. Written statement. 3. Interview and lab morning. 4. Offer.
Entry: secondary school completion with biology or a science course; a health check and a background check before the first placement.
Next intake: Monday 6 September 2027. Applications Monday 15 February to Wednesday 31 March 2027, closing at 17:00 on the last day. Interviews in April 2027. Offers by Friday 14 May 2027.
