Himuro Gelato is a calm, editorial WordPress theme designed for an artisan gelateria in Larkhaven's Harbour Quarter. Built with refined serif typography and documentary film-grain imagery, it presents daily four-litre batches and tub set-asides through a measured single-column layout. The design features clear pacing, structured product viewers, and dedicated templates for independent makers.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download himuro-gelato.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/himuro-gelato.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Himuro Gelato.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the journal posts with their category and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. The theme works as a plain site without WooCommerce.

Forms: the newsletter sections use a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active it shows your form; without a plugin it shows an email field and a button that opens your email app with the address filled in (you are not subscribed until you send that email). The contact and tub pages show a Contact Form 7 or WPForms form when one is active, otherwise a button that opens your email app. The theme sends nothing itself.

The cafe, its people, its address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
