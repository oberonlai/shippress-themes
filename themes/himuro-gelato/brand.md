# Himuro Gelato - brand brief

A small-batch gelato cafe in a former cold-store. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Himuro Gelato.
- **Location:** 14 Quay Lane, Harbour Quarter, Larkhaven (a fictional address). Email hello@himurogelato.example, phone +00 10 4820 1907. EUR (€) prices in every language.
- **History:** founded in 2019 by Ines Varga inside a former cold-store on Quay Lane. She wanted a counter where nothing is hurried: a base that rests, a freezer that is never overfilled, a cup scooped when you ask for it.
- **Today:** six flavours churned on each open day, cups and tubs for the walk home, a hold shelf for set-aside tubs, 14 seats inside and 6 outside.
- **Audience:** neighbours and people walking home across the bridge who want a small, good cold thing rather than a big sweet one; families who collect a tub on Sundays.
- **Tone:** calm, unhurried, specific. Temperatures, times and ingredients rather than marketing. No guarantees, health claims, statistics, awards, press or real brands.

## People (6, named the same everywhere)
Ines Varga (founder, maker at the slab) - Tomas Lind (morning batch maker) - Amara Okafor (flavours and fruit buying) - Mei Han (front of the glass, counter lead) - Rafael Moreno (evenings and the hold shelf) - Sofie Brandt (wafers and cones).

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Hours: Wed-Sun 12:00-21:00; Mon-Tue closed (churning and cleaning days). Tub reservations by 15:00 the same day, held 3 hours on the -18 °C hold shelf.
- Batch: the base rests 12 h at 4 °C; churned 4 litres per batch for 11 minutes; hardened 2 h at -22 °C; served at -12 °C; 6 flavours each open day; 14 seats inside, 6 outside.
- Prices (EUR): single cup, one flavour €4.20; double cup, two flavours €5.60; affogato €6.20; walk-home tub 500 ml €12.00; family tub 1 L €21.00; insulated carry bag (reusable) €3.00.
- Flavours (6): Burnt Fig & Plum, Black Sesame Milk, Pear & Bay Leaf, Hazelnut Gianduja, Lemon Ricotta, Dark Cocoa Sorbet (vegan).
- Set-aside process: reserve by 15:00, we churn, hold shelf at -18 °C, collect by name.

## A day at the counter (daily routine)
Morning: Tomas starts the daily routine by checking the bases that rested overnight at 4 °C, and Sofie rolls cones on the warm iron. The first four-litre batch is churned for eleven minutes, hardened for two hours and brought up to -12 °C. 12:00 the counter opens and Mei greets the glass. Afternoon: reservations close at 15:00 and Rafael sets the tubs on the hold shelf. 21:00 the counter closes; whatever is left goes to the hold shelf or is discarded at close.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. A full-bleed film-grain photograph whose lower third melts into paper, with a pale full-width wordmark across its top (a-bite-coffee first screen).
2. A pinned product viewer: left index with an active dot, two stacked thumbnails, a tall main photograph and a bordered spec box with a dot meter (a-bite-coffee Products).
3. A lit-row band on dark grain: three ruled statements, one lit, a small photograph parked beside it (a-bite-coffee Brand Philosophy).
4. Supporting: rosette label with a hairline above every section; grain-wave footer.
