Shokutaku Dining is a hospitality theme for a neighbourhood restaurant on Havenstraat in Rotterdam, open since 2014 for weekday lunch and unhurried dinner. The sample copy gives forty seats around an open kitchen, three sittings, six priced menus in euro, a private room for eight to twelve guests, seven named people and four kitchen notes. A greige wall holds a lead title and a booking button beside an arch-topped photograph with an info bar of hours and address, then a 320px white ghost word over three scattered arch photographs, the sittings, a full-bleed room photograph with parallax, a price list, a pill-shaped dish strip that runs off the page, the people, the visit block and a booking close. A tall vertical reservation tab stays at the right edge. Fraunces for display, Source Sans 3 for reading. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; reveals and parallax stop under reduced motion.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download shokutaku-dining.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/shokutaku-dining.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Shokutaku Dining.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, four kitchen notes with a category, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. The sample content is in English (demo-content.json); the importer uses the `shippress_demo_lang` option or the site language.

The restaurant, its people, address, telephone number, prices and producers are fictional.
