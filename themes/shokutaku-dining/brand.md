# Shokutaku Dining - brand brief

Neighbourhood restaurant in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, open since 2014 for weekday lunch and unhurried dinner. Sample content in English (stage 1: home page); Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shokutaku Dining. A dining table in the middle of a room: forty seats around an open kitchen, a short menu and three sittings.
- **Location:** Havenstraat 41, 3024 Rotterdam, the Netherlands (fictional address). Euro prices in every language, no converted prices.
- **History:** Shokutaku Dining opened in 2014 on Havenstraat in Rotterdam, in a corner building that had been a bakery. Elena Vasquez, the chef-owner, and Tomasz Nowak, co-owner and host, kept the tall windows and the pale plaster and set an open kitchen in the middle of the room. The pass is steel, the tables are oak, and the room holds forty seats: thirty-two at tables and eight at the counter, where guests can watch the plating.
- **Today:** The room holds forty seats, thirty-two at tables and eight at the counter along the pass, with a private room for twelve guests (minimum eight). The menu is short and changes with the Tuesday delivery from three fictional producers: Polder Vegetable Co-op (vegetables, Tuesday), Molen Ten Wind (stone-milled flour, weekly) and Harbour Day Boats (fish, Wednesday and Friday).
- **Audience:** neighbours and office workers who want a weekday lunch they can plan, couples and small groups who want an unhurried dinner, and hosts of private dinners for seven to twelve.
- **Voice:** Calm, specific and unhurried, as if the host were explaining the room rather than selling it. Name the sitting, the price and the hour, and leave out praise, awards and promises.

## Story
Shokutaku Dining opened in 2014 on Havenstraat in Rotterdam, in a corner building that had been a bakery. Elena Vasquez, the chef-owner, and Tomasz Nowak, co-owner and host, kept the tall windows and the pale plaster and set an open kitchen in the middle of the room. The pass is steel, the tables are oak, and the room holds forty seats: thirty-two at tables and eight at the counter, where guests can watch the plating.

The menu stays short and changes with the Tuesday delivery. Vegetables come from Polder Vegetable Co-op, stone-milled flour from Molen Ten Wind each week, and fish from Harbour Day Boats on Wednesday and Friday. Weekday lunch runs Tuesday to Friday, from 11:30 to 14:30, last order at 13:45, in about seventy-five minutes. Dinner runs Tuesday to Saturday, from 17:30 to 22:30, with the last seating at 21:00. Sunday and Monday the restaurant is closed.

A private room seats twelve guests, with a minimum of eight. The dinner menu is €58 a guest and the kitchen tasting is €78; wine pairing is €38 for four glasses. Tables open thirty days ahead and are held for fifteen minutes. Please cancel by phone or email at least twenty-four hours ahead. Groups of seven to twelve use the private room. There is no deposit. Elena cooks at the pass, Tomasz hosts the room, and the short menu is the same idea they opened with in 2014.
