## History
Founded in 2009 at 14 Calder Lane in Wellmouth, Hazuki Instruments opened as a ground-floor shop guided by a quiet conviction: an instrument purchase should be a fitting, not a sale. Over the years, the shop expanded across all three floors of the harbour building. In 2013, the first floor opened to house upright and grand pianos alongside bowed strings, giving acoustic keyboard and string players a dedicated room to explore tone and touch.

In 2017, three sound booths were built on the second floor so players could test instruments in complete privacy without an audience or rush. The repair workbench moved upstairs in 2021, providing space for full setups, fretwork, and delicate restorations, such as reviving a vintage 1970s acoustic guitar. In 2024, a dedicated rentals desk was added on the ground floor to support local students and school band programmes.

Across seventeen years of operation, our daily focus has remained consistent and personal. From helping a retired teacher choose her first upright piano after forty years to fitting an entire school trumpet section in a single Saturday, Hazuki Instruments ensures every player leaves with an instrument that genuinely fits their posture and hands, backed by dependable workbench care for years to come.
