## Concept
- **Name:** Mugi Bakehouse.
- **Location:** 9 Spelt Yard, 9000 Ghent, Belgium (fictional address). Tram 4, Spelt Yard stop; bike rack for twelve; step-free door.

- **Tone:** The voice is calm, specific, and unhurried, reflecting the grounded daily routines of an artisan neighbourhood bakery. It relies on concrete descriptions of baking hours, regional grains, and street deliveries rather than promotional language.

## Story
Mugi Bakehouse was founded in 2019 by Ines Moreau and Tomasz Wierzbicki at 9 Spelt Yard in Ghent. The bakery was established around a single second-hand steel deck oven and one mother levain starter, with the quiet commitment to bake honest sourdough for the street outside. From the outset, the daily schedule was organized around slow overnight fermentation, regional stone-milled grains, and dawn baking.

The founders made an early decision to remain a small neighbourhood bakehouse rather than expanding production or chasing distant wholesale accounts. All wheat, rye, and spelt flours are delivered directly from three regional partners: Molen Ter Beke twenty-two kilometres away, Lindenhof mill thirty-eight kilometres away, and Haverveld farm fifty-four kilometres away. Sourcing exclusively from these independent mills ensures that every loaf carries the distinct taste and integrity of local agriculture.

Today, the bakehouse continues to run five mornings and one Sunday afternoon every week under the same disciplined rhythm. The single deck oven supplies fresh loaves directly to walk-in neighbours, while dedicated morning crates go out by cargo bike to local partners including Café Lune, Westpark Primary school kitchen, and Okafor Grocer. The bakery remains rooted in its neighbourhood, keeping craft, community, and daily nourishment at the center of each bake.

- 04:30 — Mix and autolyse regional flours, water, and levain starter (Lukas)
- 05:30 — Divide and shape country sourdough, rye, and tin loaves at the bench (Ama)
- 06:15 — First bake: country sourdough peels loaded into the deck oven (Ines)
- 07:00 — Shop doors open, warm loaves displayed on front counter shelves (Priya)
