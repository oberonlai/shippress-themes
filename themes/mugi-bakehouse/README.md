# Mugi Bakehouse

An editorial block theme for a **neighbourhood bakery** that supplies the street outside (ShipPress **store** theme). The demo bakery is fictional: a deck-oven bakehouse at 9 Spelt Yard, Ghent, run by Lukas, Ama and Ines. See `brand.md` for the people and the day, `brand/design-spec.md` for the design spec and `readme.txt` for fonts and image credits.

## Concept: a greige room, one russet line

- **Palette.** Greige-white ground `#F9F6F2`, ink `#1A1A1A`, band `#E7E5DD`, russet `#6B4540` for the outlined wordmark line, the emblem, links and the active tab, pink `#F9D4D8` and mint `#BFEBD9` marker chips, muted text `#5C5752`. The first screen is almost all photograph (about 2% saturated).
- **Type.** Fraunces (display, plus a static 500 outline instance for the stroked BAKE line so no variable-font contour overlaps show) over Source Serif 4 (text). Noto Serif TC and Noto Serif JP subsets for the Chinese and Japanese demo.
- **Photography.** Film-grain, high-key window light: one baker sliding a peel into the deck oven, a colleague dusting flour across the bench.

## Signature

- **Half-ring edge tabs.** OVEN (left) and BENCH (right) sit at mid-height on the window edge and swap the two hero photographs with a crossfade. On phones they become an in-flow "Oven | Bench" pill.
- **Outline capsule tabs** BREAD and NOTES fixed at the bottom corners, with a ring dot menu and a basket in the header.
- **Slanted one-pixel rule rows** with price, marker chip and a thin long arrow: loaves, the oven-day hours, standing-order facts, neighbours and opening hours.
- **Parallax** on the hero and the counter photograph (`sp-parallax`, reduced-motion safe) and a fade-up reveal.

## Pages and templates

Demo pages: Home, Loaves, The Oven Day, Standing Orders, Neighbours, About, Flour & Grain, Journal (posts page) and Visit; sample posts with categories and tags; six sample WooCommerce products.

Templates: front page, home, page, single, archive, category, tag, search, 404, plus WooCommerce shop and product templates. Without WooCommerce everything still works; with it, the basket in the header shows the cart count.

## Languages

Demo content in English, Traditional Chinese (zh-TW), Japanese (ja), French and German. The editor and the front end load the same fonts through `theme.json`.
