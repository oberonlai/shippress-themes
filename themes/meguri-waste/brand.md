# Meguri Waste - brand brief

A commercial waste collector on a fixed weekly schedule. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Meguri Waste.
- **Location:** Unit 4, Quay Road Yard, Port Alder (a fictional depot). Licence no. WC-2011-0418. Email desk@meguri-waste.example, missed-pickup line +00 0000 0418. EUR (€) prices in every language, excluding VAT.
- **History:** founded in 2011 by Mara Okafor at Port Alder harbour with a single truck. 2015 a food-waste route for the local food halls; 2019 weighbridge reports emailed after every round; 2024 a fifth stream, confidential paper, completes the week.
- **Today:** seven people run fixed weekly rounds from the Quay Road depot for offices, food halls and clinics.
- **Audience:** facilities managers and site managers who want a collection day they can plan around: a named driver, a fixed day per stream, a report the same morning.
- **Tone:** calm, understated, practical. Times, days, container sizes and prices rather than marketing. No guarantees, percentages, savings claims, awards, press or real brands.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Hours: collection window Mon-Fri 05:30-18:00, Sat 06:00-12:00; customer desk Mon-Fri 08:00-17:30; missed-pickup line answered Mon-Sat 06:00-18:00, re-collection the same or next working day. First lift within 10 working days of the site visit.
- Streams (5) and default days: General waste Mon/Thu; Mixed recycling (paper, card, cans, plastics) Tue; Glass Wed; Food and organics Mon/Wed/Fri; Confidential paper Fri, fortnightly.
- Weekly cover (EUR, ex VAT): Starter, one stream, one 660 L bin weekly, €48/week; Core, three streams, €96/week; Full week, five streams, €158/week; Food daily add-on (3 lifts a week) €34/week; Extra lift (any stream) €22/lift; Confidential console (240 L, fortnightly shred) €14/month. Monthly rolling terms, 30 days' notice, no setup fee for sites under 8 bins.
- Case notes (3): Alder Quay office tower (14 floors, 4 streams, Tue/Thu); Linden Street food hall (22 stalls, daily food lift 06:10); Northgate medical practice (confidential paper and glass, fortnightly).
- A collection day: 05:30 text notice, 06:10 bins out, 06:25 truck lift, 07:40 weigh-in, 09:00 report email (inner page adds 05:45 depot leave and 06:40 contamination photo).

## A collection day (daily routine)
Jonas checks the trucks before the 05:30 departure window and the customer desk sends the text notice to each site. The daily routine on the road: at 06:10 the driver checks the bins are out and the dock is open, at 06:25 the bin goes up the truck lift and comes back to its bay, and where something does not belong a photograph is taken at 06:40. At 07:40 the truck crosses the weighbridge at Quay Road, Priya checks the slip, and by 09:00 the report reaches the facilities manager's inbox. The customer desk opens at 08:00 and the missed-pickup line answers until 18:00.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. A full-bleed watercolour panorama of a loading dock in a sideways scroll-snap strip, with the headline panel on the picture and five paper day tags hanging from the bins (daiseki.co.jp home first screen).
2. Ruled section heads (1 px slate rule under every h2), a 24x3 teal dash before every h3, and a flat pictogram line joined by long slate arrows with teal markers and times (daiseki.co.jp service pages).
3. A wash band whose watercolour bleeds off the right edge and dissolves into white at its left, with a 3 px yellow-green to teal hairline under each page title (daiseki.co.jp /business/).
4. Supporting: teal-outline licence box and red-outline missed-pickup box above the menu; the current menu item is a filled slate tab.

## Imagery
Watercolour and gouache illustrations on lightly textured paper (AI-generated), one style: wet-edge washes, pale cyan-to-white sky, muted patina greens and slate blues, one hi-vis orange vest and a red lid tag as the only warm accents, faint pencil, small faceless people; no text, logos or brand marks. Process pictograms are separate flat SVGs drawn for the theme.
