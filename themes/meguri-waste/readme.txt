Meguri Waste is a clean, focused WordPress landing theme designed for commercial waste collection services on a fixed weekly schedule. Built with Hanken Grotesk and Instrument Sans typography, it features horizontal pan-strip illustration layouts, timed process flows, and clear weekly cover pricing. Structured block templates provide facilities teams and business districts with a calm, dependable schedule they can plan around.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download meguri-waste.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/meguri-waste.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Meguri Waste.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the site notes posts with their category and tags, the anchor menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. The theme works as a plain site without WooCommerce.

Forms: the collection-notes sections use a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active it shows your form; without a plugin it shows an email field and a button that opens your email app with the address filled in (you are not on the list until you send that email). The set-your-schedule page shows a Contact Form 7 or WPForms form when one is active, otherwise a button that opens your email app. The theme sends nothing itself.

The company, its people, its address, telephone numbers, licence number and prices are fictional.
