## Audience
Guests who travel with one or two dogs (up to 40 kg each) and want the answers before they book: sizes, rooms, the dog run, what the stay includes. Buyers of the theme: pet-friendly hotels and inns that need to answer owners' questions before booking.

## Tone
A calm, observant, and specific tone that speaks with quiet confidence and practical clarity to dog owners. It avoids emotional excess, marketing buzzwords, and exaggerated promises, focusing instead on precise routines, welcoming rituals, and honest care.

## Story
Ashiato Hotel began in 2016 within a quiet 1931 seaside guesthouse at 7 Linden Row in Alder Bay. Founder Ines Moreau envisioned a hotel created entirely around people travelling with their dogs, transforming the historic building into a dedicated fourteen-room retreat. Rather than treating four-legged companions as an afterthought or a reluctant exception, every corner of the property was reshaped to welcome them as true guests.

From the opening morning, the hotel established a calm, practical standard for canine hospitality. The front desk team learns every arriving dog's name, breed, and weight before they set foot on the property, greeting them at eye level in the lobby. The grounds were designed with purpose: an enclosed 1,200-square-metre dog run with double gates, dedicated paw-wash stations at the doors, and sunny outdoor terrace tables where dogs can relax at their owners' feet during morning breakfast.

Today, Ashiato remains a peaceful seaside haven offering four distinct room types, from ground-floor garden rooms with private fenced patios to spacious family suites. With clear rules, transparent rates, and a small team dedicated to daily care, grooming, and dog-sitting, the hotel provides a seamless travel experience where dogs and their owners share quiet coastal days together in complete comfort.

## Process (the single way to book)
1. Ask: Submit your dates, preferred room, and your dog's name, breed, and weight via the form on /book/ or email stay@ashiatohotel.example.
2. Confirm: Mei replies within 24 hours with your reservation details and a secure 20% deposit link to confirm your stay.
3. Arrive: Check in between 15:00 and 20:00; the desk greets your dog by name, with a paw wash at the door and a welcome kit in your room.
Every "Book a room" goes to /book/. There is no second booking path.
