## 0. Fixed brand facts (identical on every page and in all five languages)

- **Ashiato Hotel** — a small hotel of **14 rooms** built for guests who travel with their dogs. The building is a 1931 seaside guesthouse; Ashiato opened in it in **2016**.
- Address: **7 Linden Row, Alder Bay** (fictional, international-neutral; local address order per language in brand.md). Phone **+00 44 3100 2468** (fictional). Email `stay@ashiatohotel.example` (the same address in every mailto, form fallback and footer).
- Front desk **daily 07:00–23:00** (night bell after 23:00). Check-in **15:00–20:00**, check-out **11:00**. Breakfast **07:30–10:00** on the terrace (dogs welcome at the terrace tables). **Dog run open 06:00–22:00**, lit after dusk.
- **Dog rules (the numbers every page repeats):** dogs only (no cats); up to **2 dogs per room**; up to **40 kg** per dog; vaccination record (rabies + DHPP) shown at check-in; flea/tick treatment within the last **30 days**; dogs may stay alone in the room for up to **2 hours** with the "Dog in room" door card, otherwise the dog sitter.

## 1. Signature techniques (three; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

All three come from **Prince & Pet** (https://www.princehotels.co.jp/pet/), first screen and first scroll (`ref/prince-first.png`, `ref/prince-full.png`).

### T1. Rounded inset photo window with a huge light headline lying across its upper-left (RT(c)(d)(b))
- **Reference:** under a 100 px light header bar, one photograph sits in a window inset **32 px** from both edges, radius **18 px**, ~1.7:1, no border, no shadow; a huge **light geometric sans** wordmark (~110 px at 1440, white) lies over the photo's upper-left (x≈90 page / ~60 px inside the window, top ~45 px inside), with a semibold JP sub-line ~25 px, wide tracking, directly under it. The photo is slightly dimmed so white type reads.
- **Ours (home first screen, top half):** the window is the **left part of the split**: `x32 → x916` at 1440 (884 px wide), `y100 → y620` (520 px tall, **1.70:1**, RT(e)), radius **18 px**, `overflow:clip`. H1 inside: **Lexend 300, 5.5rem (88 px) / 1.02, tracking −0.01em, white**, 2 lines "Your dog is / on the list.", top-left at **60 px / 48 px** inside the window. Sub-line under it (16 px gap): **Public Sans 600, 1.5rem (24 px) / 1.4, tracking .12em, white**: "Rooms, a dog run and a front desk that knows the names." Dimming: overall overlay `rgba(28,36,27,.10)` + upper-left scrim `linear-gradient(160deg, rgba(28,36,27,.42) 0%, rgba(28,36,27,0) 55%)` (white H1 on scrim ≥ 4.5:1 measured on P01).
- **Must not be diluted:** the 32 px side inset, r18, the huge light (300) weight and size ≥ 5.5rem on desktop, white type **on** the photo (not beside it), the tracked semibold sub-line. Not a capsule, not an arch, no wordmark cropped by the frame.
- **Inner pages (continuation, every page):** page hero = the same inset window, full width `x32 → x1408`, height `clamp(420px, 56vh, 560px)`, r18, same scrim; H1 Lexend 300 **4.25rem** white at 60/48 inside; tracked sub-line Public Sans 600 1.125rem .12em. This is literally the reference composition at full width.

### T2. Pale sage paw-print trail stepping out of the window's lower right (RT(e)(f))
- **Reference:** 4 pale sage paw prints (#E4ECE2, ~70 px, rotated −20…+20°) step diagonally down-right just below the photo window's lower-right corner, leading the eye to the narrow intro text.
- **Ours:** our own paw SVG (4 toe ovals + heel pad, no outline), fill **#E4ECE2** (RT, unchanged). **Home first screen (≥1024 px):** 4 prints, **68 px** wide, positioned in the right half below the window corner, stepping down-right: centres at (x956, y660) −18°, (x1046, y716) +12°, (x1112, y790) −14°, (x1204, y846) +16° (left/right alternation like a walking dog). - **Continuation:** (a) every inner page: 3 prints (56 px) stepping down-right from the hero window's lower-right corner into the space beside the intro, exactly like RT; (b) home section dividers: a trail of 4 prints (48 px) crossing from one block to the next at blocks 3→4, 5→6 and 8→9 (positions in §3), the last trail ends at the "Book a room" button; (c) the FAQ accordion uses a 20 px paw as the open/close marker; (d) the footer has one 4-print trail on the sage ground in `#B9C6B4`.

- **390 px:** in the first screen the 4 prints are an **in-flow row** (36 px each, gap 14 px, alternating ±14°, slight 6/0/6/0 px vertical offsets via `translate`) placed after the booking button — no absolute positioning.

### T3. Four-moment slow cross-fade in the window with grey/sage pager dots (RT(e)(f)(g))
- **Reference:** the window is a slider: 4–5 full-frame photos (people with dogs on grass and balconies) cross-fade (slick fade, autoplay **3000 ms**); a row of **10 px** dots sits under the window at its right end (inactive grey #959595, active sage #769E6F).

Dots: 4 × **10 px** circles, gap 10 px, right-aligned to the window's right edge, 16 px under it (y≈636); inactive `#C8C8C8`, active `#769E6F`, active state driven by the same keyframe timing. ## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (princehotels.co.jp/pet)
**Draws:** a light header bar with sage nav; below it one big rounded photo window inset from the edges: a person sitting on summer grass, hand resting on a young dog, or a woman holding a small dog on a white balcony — high-key daylight, relaxed, the dog is the subject; a huge light white wordmark across the upper-left of the photo with a tracked sub-line ("a hotel you can stay at with your dog/cat"); under the window at right a row of pager dots; just below it a trail of pale sage paw prints stepping toward a narrow intro paragraph ("travel together, run in nature, stay in the same room").
**Story:** "Bring your dog — the whole stay is made for both of you."
**Signature motion:** the photos slowly cross-fade (fade, 3 s autoplay) through different moments of a stay; blocks fade in as you scroll; hover 0.5–0.6 s ease.

### (2) Our translation (same composition, same motion idea, same image type: high-key documentary photos)
Arrival at a small seaside hotel for people with dogs: **Ines, the receptionist, kneels on the lobby floor to greet a wire-haired terrier at the front desk while its owner signs the register** above them — bright morning light through tall windows, pale walls, a water bowl by the desk. The window then cross-fades through the rest of arrival day: the room with a dog bed at the foot of the bed, the dog run in morning light, breakfast on the terrace with a dog under the table. Story: **"Your dog is on the list."** — the hotel already knows the dog's name, size and room before you arrive; the questions owners ask are answered right under the photo (bottom half of the first screen), and there is one way to book. Same layout (inset rounded window, huge light white headline upper-left, tracked sub-line, dots, paw trail to the intro), same motion (slow 4-moment cross-fade), same image type as the reference; the scene and copy are ours. Not "adapted, not copied" in the sense of dropping the scene: the whole scene is kept and moved to the front desk.
