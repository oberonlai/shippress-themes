# Shizuku Filter — brand

## Story

Shizuku Filter was founded in 2023 in a small workshop at Harbour Lane 14 in Rotterdam, Netherlands. The studio began with a singular focus: to design and build one quiet, thoughtful object for the everyday kitchen table, known as the Shizuku Countertop Filter. Frustrated by bulky plastic appliances, the team set out to craft an unhurried water filter using honest materials that require no plumbing and no electricity.

Every filter pairs a slip-cast, matte stone-grey ceramic body with a 1.6-litre clear glass carafe and a custom four-layer cartridge. Rather than making exaggerated laboratory claims, the engineering emphasizes noticeable everyday improvements: clearer water, a noticeable reduction in chlorine smell, and a softer taste. Water flows gently through the cartridge, filtering a full 1.6 litres in about four minutes, while each cartridge lasts two months or approximately 150 litres.

Operating as a dedicated independent maker, Shizuku Filter is preparing for its initial production campaign to raise $40,000 for manufacturing tooling and a pilot run of 50 units. From the Rotterdam workshop, the team works directly with backers, delivering fortnightly updates from initial tooling in December 2026 through full production and delivery in April 2027.

## Voice

The brand speaks in a calm, specific, and grounded voice focused on the everyday ritual of drinking water without hyperbole or medical claims. Copy highlights physical materials, small-batch craftsmanship, and noticeable improvements in taste and clarity in clear, natural English.
