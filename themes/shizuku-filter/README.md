# Shizuku Filter

Block theme for a small maker's crowdfunding landing page (landing track). Home (en): a white band with a giant two-line wordmark and a 1px ink rule, a full-bleed photograph directly under it (10 s zoom-out plus parallax) with a slate pill badge and a fixed vertical "Back this" pill, a four-cell campaign info bar with a 79% funding bar, then sections on a 45 px grid ground with an 80 px left rail and vertical labels: why, four layers, six full-pill reward capsules, making schedule, makers, test households, questions and one repeated back call.

Signature techniques (from ampuleshot.jp): T1 wordmark band + rule, T2 full-pill capsule cards, T3 rail with vertical labels and crosshair marker leads.

Scroll effects: `assets/sp-scroll.css|js` (marker wipe left to right, capsule stagger, parallax on the hero and the closing photo) — off for reduced motion.

Photos: 12 high-key photographs in `assets/images` (one consistent spout-free ceramic filter body across the hero, rewards, schedule and stories). Languages: en only so far; inner pages and zh-TW, ja, fr, de are stage 2.
