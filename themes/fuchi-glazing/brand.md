# Fuchi Glazing: brand

## Name
Fuchi Glazing, a commercial glazing contractor (fictional). It replaces shopfront panes, entrance doors and office partitions for shops, clinics and offices, and it measures the opening twice before the glass is ordered. Industry: commercial glazing; landing type. The home page is one long conversion page with an anchor menu and a single repeated request: Book a survey.

## Story
Fuchi Glazing was founded in 2011 by Corin Abbott, a former shopfitter who experienced first-hand the costly delays caused when suppliers manufactured glass from hasty single measurements. After receiving two consecutive orders of wrong-size glass in one month on commercial fit-outs, Corin set out to build a trade glazing service dedicated entirely to dimensional accuracy. Starting with a single van in Lindenport, the company established a strict protocol: every opening would be surveyed thoroughly on site, verified a second time on acceptance, and fitted in a single visit without site alterations.

In 2014, operations expanded to a dedicated workshop at Unit 3, Calder Yard, 14 Weir Street, Lindenport. The facility provided space for A-frame storage racks, pre-assembly staging, and specialised handling equipment for large toughened and laminated panels. As commercial demand across the town developed, Fuchi Glazing established a dedicated interior partitions team in 2019, extending its dual-survey discipline to acoustic screens, glass doors, and clinic fit-outs.

By 2024, the addition of a second survey van allowed the team to protect its focused Tuesday-to-Thursday survey windows while keeping lead times predictable. Today, seven trade specialists operate from Calder Yard, replacing commercial shopfronts, entrance hardware, and office glass across Lindenport. The company remains committed to its founding principle: measured twice, checked before ordering, and glazed once.

## A day at Fuchi Glazing (daily routine)
Daily routine: the client desk opens at 08:00 and takes requests by form or email; survey visits run Tuesday to Thursday from 07:30, so a shop can be measured before it opens or at its quietest hour. A typical survey is 45 minutes on site: the surveyor photographs every opening, records three widths and three heights, checks both diagonals and the sill level, and walks the lifting route. Back at the workshop the written quote is prepared within 3 working days. After acceptance a second surveyor checks every dimension on a different day, and the glass is ordered the same day. Fitting teams start from 06:30 so most shopfront panes are in and sealed before trading hours.

## Process
1. Day 0: Request — Submit a photo of the opening, rough dimensions, and site address.
2. Day 1: First survey — A 45-minute on-site survey visit scheduled Tuesday to Thursday.
3. Day 3: Written quote — A fixed-price written quotation issued within 3 working days, valid for 30 days.
4. Day 4–14: Second check measure and order — On-site check measure conducted on acceptance day; glass ordered immediately with an 8–12 working day lead time.
5. Fitting day: Installation — Fitted on site from 06:30, with most shopfront panes completed in 1 day on site.
6. Week 6: Aftercare visit — A follow-up site visit to inspect perimeter seals, closer action, and frame alignment.
