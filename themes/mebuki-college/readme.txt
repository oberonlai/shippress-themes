== Description ==

Mebuki College is a calm, typography-focused WordPress theme designed for early-childhood teacher-training colleges, nurseries, and educational settings. Built with Fraunces serif headings, warm natural palettes, and documentary photography layouts, it presents day schedules, placement structures, and tutor profiles with quiet clarity. Features include custom chalk-line accents, notched photo cards, and accessible course and fee schedules.

The home page opens on a pale split hero with one documentary photograph and a tutor's white chalk marks, then notched photo cards, a placement day on one hairline, mirrored panel cards, three routes, a full-width fee sheet, admission steps, a tutor list, graduate quotes and the open-day letters sign-up. Contact and newsletter forms use the optional ShipPress form slot and need no plugin.
