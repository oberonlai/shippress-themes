## 1. Signature techniques (three; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

### T1. The tutor's chalk marks drawn over the photo (baika hero scribble, `bk-first.png`, RT(f))
- **Reference:** a **white hand-drawn chalk scribble** (loops of flowers, ~520px wide) lies over the upper centre-right of the hero photograph, line ≈ 5px, rough round caps, pure white on the photo; it reads as a child's drawing laid over real life.
- **Ours (Home H1, on P01):** a single inline SVG (no text, `aria-hidden="true"`) of **three white chalk marks** made by the tutor watching: (a) a **loose loop** (≈ 260 × 170) circling the two pairs of hands in the sand; (b) a **curved arrow** (≈ 220 long) from the loop up toward the tutor in the doorway; (c) a **tick** (≈ 70 × 54) in clay `#C9825B` beside the arrow head. The SVG covers the whole photo (`viewBox="0 0 800 740"`, `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"`, same box as the image) so the marks stay on the hands at every width. Stroke **5px** (`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`), `stroke-linecap: round`, `stroke-linejoin: round`, white `#FFFFFF` at 95% opacity, paths slightly wobbly (hand-drawn, 2–3 control points of jitter), overall mark area **≈ 520 × 300px at 1440** (RT: 520px wide), placed centre-right of the photo, upper half, not over faces.
- **Motion:** draws itself once on load: `stroke-dasharray: var(--len); stroke-dashoffset: var(--len) → 0`, **1.6s**, loop 0s, arrow 0.5s delay, tick 1.0s delay, `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)` (RT(g) ease-out-quint). The start state is only set under `html.sp-js:not(.sp-no-motion)` with `animation-fill-mode: backwards`, so no-JS / reduced motion / `?sp-static` / editor show the finished marks. This is a load animation, not a scroll reveal.
- **Inner pages (one small white chalk mark on the page-hero photo, 5px stroke, same draw-in):** A day on the course (clock-like loop round the children at the low table), Placements (arrow from tutor to trainee), Admissions (tick beside the trainee), Stories (loop round a graduate's hands at the easel), Open days (underline under the doorway) = **5 pages**.
- **Must not be diluted:** white, thick (5px), hand-drawn, visibly **on** the photograph, ≥ 400px total width on desktop; never a thin 1px line, never coloured except the one clay tick, never moved off the photo onto the pale ground.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://www.baika-kindergarten.ed.jp/)
- **What it draws:** a tall documentary photograph of **children at a low table, eating and making things, at close range** (faces, hands, soft daylight, shallow focus), a **white chalk scribble of flowers** drawn over the photo's upper centre-right, a two-line statement with a small Latin line, a pink dot at left, and a **white news card docked over the bottom-left of the photo** with three dated rows. White 74px header with a 4px pink top rule; pale cream/pink ground.
- **Story:** a child grows into someone who stands on their own through close, everyday contact with nature and people; the adults are near and watching, the child is the centre.
- **Signature motion:** the hero photos cross-fade slowly (1s, `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`); sections fade in with a ~30px rise (0.6s, `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`); card panels fade after their photos (≈0.15s later).

### (2) Our translation (same composition idea, same motion idea, same image type: documentary photograph)
**You learn the work by watching it closely.** One documentary photograph, close range, soft daylight: in a bright nursery room, **Leah (trainee) kneels at a low sand table with a four-year-old; both have their hands in the sand, shaping a small hill**; behind them, in the open doorway of the next room, **Ines (tutor) leans on the door frame writing in a notebook**, softly out of focus. The same low table and close hands as the reference, but now the adult is learning too, and the observer is visible. The reference's child-like scribble becomes **the tutor's chalk notes**: white marks drawn over the photo (a loop round the hands, an arrow to the doorway, a clay tick), as if the observation were written on the moment itself. The reference's docked news card becomes **the card row** of the next three things an applicant needs: open day, a placement day, applications. Motion: the marks draw themselves once (1.6s, ease-out-quint) the way the scribble sits on the reference photo, and the photo drifts gently slower than the page (`sp-parallax` 6%), standing in for the reference's slow cross-fade.
