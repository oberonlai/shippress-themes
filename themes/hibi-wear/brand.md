# Hibi Wear - brand brief

Everyday clothing label and online store in Portland, Oregon. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hibi Wear. "Hibi" is the Japanese word for everyday; the label is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** a studio and packing room at 1120 SE Division St, Portland, OR 97202 (fictional). USD prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** founded in 2018 by Mara Lindqvist (design, ten years at larger labels) and Daniel Okafor (fabric and making, worked with mills in Portugal and Belgium). First run: one tee in one colour, sold to friends and neighbours, sold out in a weekend; the second run added a shirt.
- **Today:** independent, two founders and a small packing team; nine pieces in the range, reordered in the same colours each year.
- **Audience:** adults who want plain, well-made clothes they can wear twice a week for years: commuters, people building a small wardrobe.
- **Tone:** calm, plain, practical. Short sentences, say what the cloth is and how it wears. No awards, press, ratings, customer counts or sustainability claims beyond the facts below.

## Makers and fabrics (fictional mills)
- **Ribeira Knit Mill**, Porto: organic cotton jersey (180 g) and 330 g loopback.
- **Flandre Linen Works**, Kortrijk: washed European linen.
- **Ashby Worsted**, Yorkshire: extra-fine merino (17.5 micron).

## Range (9 pieces, USD)

Categories: Tops, Shirts, Bottoms, Knitwear, Outerwear. Sizes XS-XL; two fits, relaxed (tees, sweatshirts, jacket, wide trouser) and regular.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Free shipping on orders over $120; otherwise flat $8 within the US. International flat $24.
- Returns and exchanges within 30 days of delivery, unworn with tags; the return label is free in the US.
- Free seam repairs for 1 year.
- US delivery 2-5 business days; international 7-14 business days.
- Founded 2018, Portland, Oregon.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Gold dot + spaced uppercase label above a big two-line heading (ReWEAR, "LINE UP").
2. A rounded 8 px portrait straddling a warm band (#EDEAE2) that starts at 55% of the photo, short statements bottom-aligned on the right, an outline circle arrow.
3. A before / after split photograph with two flat tabs (stone / teal) and a big serif number ("20 washes").
First screen mood from Kaneko Optical "Sabae to the World": hazy full-bleed photo, tiny captions at the left and right thirds, vertical scroll rule.

## Pages and contents
1. **Home** - front-cover, front-lineup, front-idea, front-wash, front-fabrics, front-city, front-promise, front-journal, front-close.
2. **Shop** (/shop/) - head with the clothing rail, category pills, 3-column grid (WooCommerce archive template; static line-up without WooCommerce).
3. **Single product** - gallery on the band, summary, care panel (small before/after split), tabs, related.
4. **About** (/about/) - Founded in Portland 2018; why a small range; how we choose mills.
5. **Makers & Fabrics** (/fabrics/) - the wash split and three fabric notes.
6. **Sizing** (/sizing/) - fits, measurement table on the band, how to measure.
7. **Shipping & Returns** (/shipping-returns/) - $120 / 30 / $8 on the band, delivery, returns, repairs.
8. **Journal** (/journal/) - posts page; six entries (Studio, Fabric notes, Care).
9. **Contact** (/contact/) - studio address, hours, mail.
Cart, checkout, account, order confirmation, search and 404 follow the same heads.
