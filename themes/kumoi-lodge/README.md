# Kumoi Lodge

A block theme (type hospitality) for a small mountain lodge. English in this release: the home page plus short inner pages (Rooms, The Stay, The Table, Rates & Booking, Walks & Weather, Getting here, Keepers, Notes) so that every menu and footer link opens a real page. More inner-page detail and the other languages follow.

## Signature
- A framed window first screen: an r30 photograph inset in the lichen ground with a centred serif wordmark, a Scroll cue and a hairline info bar carrying availability and an ember ring.
- A full-viewport out-of-focus opener with two small text columns.
- Room cards: 1.85:1 rounded photo with an outline circle arrow linking to the room, then a hairline split row.
- A 1 px bordered facility box with three outline icons and a schedule, 2-up rounded squares, a rate table and a dusk close with a tracked index row.

## Fonts
Fraunces and Nunito Sans (OFL), Noto Serif TC / JP subsets for Chinese and Japanese. Loaded through theme.json fontFace, so the editor and the front end match.

## Photos
Thirteen AI-generated film-grain photographs, each used once on the home page. No text, logos or brands.

## Patterns
`front-window`, `front-opener`, `front-rooms`, `front-stage`, `front-night`, `front-table`, `front-rates`, `front-access`, `head-main`, `foot-main`.
