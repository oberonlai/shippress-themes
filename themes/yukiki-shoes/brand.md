# Yukiki Shoes — brand

**Name:** Yukiki Shoes, an independent shoe shop with one fitting stool.
**Location:** 14 Lantern Row, Halden (fictional).
**Founded:** 2011.
**Audience:** adults who want a pair of leather shoes fitted properly: wide or narrow feet, one foot larger, long days on their feet, first good pairs, wedding and winter pairs, and people with a good pair that needs resoling.

## Story

Yukiki Shoes was founded in 2011 by Mara Lindqvist at 14 Lantern Row, Halden. It is an independent shoe shop with one fitting stool, two benches, and six hundred and forty boxes on open shelves. The work starts with the foot. A shoe is fitted before it leaves the shelf: both feet measured, a last tried at the stool, a walk taken on the shop floor, and a decision made at the end of forty-five minutes. Mara still leads those fittings as head fitter. The room has stayed small so that sequence can stay intact.

Six lasts cover the shelf. Arden is a round toe in a standard fit. Hollis is cut for a wide forefoot. Wren holds a narrow heel. Moor is shaped for a high instep. Fen has a soft square toe. Teal is the low-volume last. Sizes run from 35 to 47 and include half sizes. On the home shelf, the Arden oxford is €265, the Hollis derby is €245, the Wren loafer is €230, and the Moor Chelsea boot is €290, in oxblood calf or chestnut leather. A fitting appointment is €20, credited to any pair. The same stool sees a nurse on twelve-hour shifts into the Hollis, a groom with one foot larger into the Arden in split sizes, and a walker bringing a nine-year-old Moor back to be resoled.

The bench is small and named. Tomas Okafor and Inès Duval fit through the week. Leo Castillo fits on Saturday and Sunday. Hanna Weiss buys the lasts. June Park looks after the front of the shop. Ravi Mehta runs the repair bench: a full leather resole is €85, a heel block is €35, and a stretch and adjust is €20, with the first stretch free when it comes with a pair. Repairs take ten working days and are collected on Tuesday. The shop is closed on Monday. Other hours are Tuesday and Wednesday 10:00–18:00, Thursday 10:00–20:00, Friday and Saturday 10:00–18:00, and Sunday 11:00–16:00. Fitting slots are 10:00, 11:30, 14:00, 15:30 and 17:00, and Thursday also has 18:30.

## Process (one fitting)

1. 10:00 Arrive — come in the socks you usually wear, and bring the shoes you wear most.
2. 10:10 Measure — both feet, on the measuring device, before a box comes off the shelf.
3. 10:20 Try — the last goes on at the stool; heel, instep and toe are checked while you are seated.
4. 10:35 Walk — a turn on the shop-floor test strip in the pair.
5. 10:45 Decide — keep the pair, or leave it on the shelf. The €20 fitting is credited to any pair you take.
