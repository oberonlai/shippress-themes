# Nodoka Counselling — brand brief

Design plan row: b19-1 (psychotherapy practice, type business). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other languages follow. All names, places and people are fictional. Copy never promises a cure, an outcome, a success rate or an award.

## Concept
- **Name:** Nodoka Counselling, a psychotherapy practice. "Nodoka" is the Japanese word for calm, unhurried; the room is kept quiet so people can talk something through.
- **Location:** 2F, 18 Alder Row, Riverside District (fictional). Step-free entrance and a lift.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Mira Halden; six people today.
- **Services:** individual counselling and psychotherapy, couples work, grief support, transition guidance, online sessions.
- **Audience:** adults and couples who feel something has grown too heavy to carry alone.
- **Tone:** The tone is calm, unhurried, and grounded, speaking directly without therapeutic jargon or false promises. Sentences remain clear, thoughtful, and respectful of the reader's space and privacy.

## Story
Nodoka Counselling was founded in 2014 by clinical psychologist Mira Halden to provide an unhurried, thoughtful environment for psychotherapy and personal reflection. Situated on Alder Row in the Riverside District, the practice was designed from the beginning as a quiet alternative to sterile institutional clinics and rushed consultations. Mira envisioned a calm, physical setting where individuals could sit down, pause their week, and speak candidly about whatever felt heavy or unresolved.

Over the past decade, the practice has grown thoughtfully into a close team of six dedicated practitioners offering individual counselling, couples therapy, grief support, and transition guidance. Rather than adopting a rigid single methodology, the practice brings together integrative psychotherapy, cognitive-behavioural principles, and person-centred dialogue tailored to the specific rhythm of each person who walks through the door. The physical rooms were carefully arranged to preserve acoustic privacy, natural window light, and an enduring sense of ease.

Today, the practice continues its work from its second-floor rooms at 18 Alder Row, offering step-free lift access alongside secure online sessions for clients across the wider region. Whether meeting in person over tea or speaking remotely from home, the core principle remains unchanged: to provide a steady, confidential space where difficulties can be untangled at an honest pace with experienced, compassionate clinicians.
