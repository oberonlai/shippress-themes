# Mimamori College — brand

**What it is:** a caregiver training college for career-changers, returners and school leavers. Founded 2009, 18 Orchard Row, Lindmere (fictional). The name *mimamori* means watching over another person with attentive, respectful care.

**Location:** 18 Orchard Row, Lindmere (fictional town); the practice room, classroom and office are on one campus.
**Audience:** career-changers from any first job, returners to care, school leavers aged 18 and over, and care homes that host a student on placement.

## Voice
Our tone is calm, precise, and supportive, speaking plainly about the physical and emotional realities of adult care without sentimentality or jargon. We offer grounded, practical encouragement to career-changers, emphasizing safe technique, unhurried practice, and respectful human dignity.

## Story
Founded in 2009 at 18 Orchard Row in Lindmere, Mimamori College was established to provide thorough, unhurried caregiver training for adults seeking a new vocation. The name mimamori draws from the principle of watching over another person with attentive, respectful care. From the beginning, the college set out to replace rushed orientation courses with rigorous classroom instruction, calm repetition in a dedicated practice room, and sustained placements inside real residential communities.

Over the years, the college developed its hallmark Core Care Certificate alongside flexible evening pathways and focused short courses. Training centres on the physical and relational realities of daily support, emphasizing that safety begins when one person's steady hand supports another. Rather than teaching through distant demonstration, instructors work directly beside students at hospital beds, wheelchairs, and hoists, ensuring that basic transfers, hygiene routines, and communication practices become second nature before anyone enters a resident's living space.

Today, Mimamori College works in close partnership with 22 local residential care homes, including Larchfield House, Mill Lane Care Home, and The Orchards. Every cohort spends hundreds of hours learning on the floor under the guidance of named workplace mentors. The college remains committed to small group cohorts, an intentional one-to-eight teaching ratio, and a grounded, humane approach that prepares career-changers, returners, and school leavers for meaningful work in social care.

## How to apply
1. Come to an open morning or call our office to discuss courses and visit the practice room.

3. Attend a conversation with an instructor followed by a half-day session in the practice room.
4. Complete a standard reference check, after which a formal place is offered.
