# Hashira Partners - brand brief

Small-warehouse leasing brokerage in Port Merrin (fictional city). Landing-page theme: one long conversion page with an anchor menu and a single repeated action, "Request a viewing". Sample content in English now; Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hashira Partners, small-warehouse leasing brokers. "Hashira" is a pillar; the firm is otherwise international and plain.
- **Location:** Unit 4, 18 Tanner Row, Port Merrin (fictional; no country is named). USD and m² in every language, no conversions.
- **History:** founded in 2014 by Mika Hashira. An empty bay is a beginning, not a vacancy: the shutter is raised, the floor is walked and the owner is in the room when that can be arranged.
- **Today:** six buildings, 50 bays, 90-420 m², in Eastgate, Riverside and Mill Cross. Not handled: bays over 420 m², cold stores, hazardous storage.
- **Audience:** local firms (parts sellers, wholesalers, print finishers, restorers) that need one small bay and want it shown properly.
- **Tone:** a broker on the bay floor: name the building, the square metres, the person who does the step and the fee. Calm and specific. No awards, savings, ratings, guarantees or press.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. **Ruled two-line headline** (reference: a Japanese property reuse company's first screen): three full-width 2 px rules, one above, between and below a bold line over a lighter, larger, tight-tracked line; glyphs touch the rules and descenders cross the bottom one. Home hero, home close and every page head.
2. **Case panel with four caption columns:** near-full-height photograph, radius 0, 20 px gaps, bottom gradient and four columns (Project / Concept / Overview / Type).
3. **Stepped-bar archive:** three touching columns, each 25 px lower, a 24 px solid black bar above each title, a small 2:3 photograph and "View More".
Motion: headline lines rise 24 px and the rules draw on load; the hero photograph opens upward; photographs move slower than the page (sp-parallax); a 235 px line loops; archive cards and steps rise in turn.
