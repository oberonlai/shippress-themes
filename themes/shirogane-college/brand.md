## Story

Shirogane College is a veterinary nursing college at 14 Linden Yard, Ashbourne Vale. Dr Margit Halvorsen, a small-animal vet, founded it in 2004. She had been training new nurses on the job and wanted a ward built for teaching. The teaching ward opened that year with 8 kennels and now has 18. Animals here are calm, stable inpatients and post-operative patients, referred by partner clinics, with the owner's consent. A vet is on site through the teaching day, from 07:45 to 17:00. The college does not take emergencies.

The Diploma in Veterinary Nursing is two years, full-time, with 24 places in each intake. Intakes begin in February and in September. On the ward, 6 students work with one tutor on every shift. The diploma is taught in six parts: foundations, ward nursing, theatre assist, imaging and lab, pharmacy, and placement. Placement runs for 40 weeks across 22 partner clinics and animal hospitals, and each clinic takes 1 or 2 students in a block. Beside the diploma, the college offers a foundation certificate of 10 weeks, part-time on Mondays and Thursdays, and an evening course of 12 Tuesday evenings, 18:30 to 21:00, for people who already work in a clinic.

A training day runs from the 07:45 handover at the ward desk to the 17:00 handover to the late vet. The admissions office is open Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 17:00. Weekend feeds run from 08:00 to 11:00, and each student covers 1 weekend in 6. The college is closed on public holidays except for that feeds rota. For February 2027, applications close on Friday 8 January, kit fitting and induction run from 25 to 29 January, and term starts on Monday 1 February. Admissions is on +00 44 555 0172 and at admissions@shirogane-college.example. Bus 14 stops at Linden Yard, a 4-minute walk away. There are bike racks, and the building is step-free.

## A day (daily routine)

A training day (the daily routine) runs from 07:45 to 17:00: 07:45 ward handover; 08:30 morning rounds and observations; 10:00 practical class; 12:00 lunch; 13:00 rotation in theatre assist or imaging and lab; 15:00 lecture; 16:30 evening feeds, medication round and ward notes; 17:00 handover to the late vet.
