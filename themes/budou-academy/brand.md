# Budou Academy - brand brief

A sommelier school in Bordeaux, France, for people who already work in service. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Budou Academy ("budou" is the Japanese word for grape; the school itself is French and international).
- **Location:** 6 cour des Tonneliers, 33000 Bordeaux, France (fictional address). hello@budou-academy.example, +33 5 56 00 14 14 (fictional).
- **Founded:** 2014, by Hélène Marchetti after eighteen years as a head sommelier.
- **Audience:** waiters, bartenders and floor managers who pour wine every night and want to say why it tastes like that; restaurant owners booking a team day.
- **Tone / voice:** The tone is calm, direct, and unpretentious, addressing working service professionals as capable peers rather than novices. It relies on precise hospitality details and quiet confidence, strictly avoiding promotional hype, exaggerated claims, or academic detachment.
- **Hours:** Classes Mondays 09:30–16:30; Wednesday evening tastings 19:00–21:00; office Tue–Fri 10:00–18:00. Twelve seats per class, Mondays because most restaurants close that day.

## History
Budou Academy was founded in 2014 in Bordeaux, France, by Hélène Marchetti, following her eighteen years working as a head sommelier across busy dining room floors. She recognized that dedicated hospitality professionals—waiters, bartenders, and floor managers—rarely have access to structured sommelier education that fits the demanding schedule of service work. Because dining rooms traditionally close their doors on Mondays, the academy established its classes exclusively on Mondays, allowing working staff to develop their expertise without interrupting their employment.

Teaching takes place around a single long oak table limited strictly to twelve seats, preserving direct discussion between students and instructors. In this intimate environment, learning is rooted in the daily physical realities of hospitality rather than remote theory. Every session balances focused blind tastings with tableside mechanics, decanting technique, and cellar list economics, treating wine as an active language spoken on the restaurant floor.

The school deliberately avoids promotional marketing, exam promises, or artificial shortcuts, focusing entirely on practical discernment and disciplined tasting habits. All instructors remain active on dining room floors, ensuring that every lesson reflects current cellar practices and guest interactions. Over the years, Budou Academy has maintained this focused approach, preparing hospitality workers to taste with precision and speak about wine with quiet authority.
