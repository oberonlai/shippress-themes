# Kakeru Run - brand brief

Running shoe fitting shop and online store in Boulder, Colorado. Sample content in English first; Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German follow, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kakeru Run. "Kakeru" is the Japanese verb for running or dashing; the shop is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** 2140 Canyon Row, Boulder, Colorado 80302, USA (fictional street), five minutes from the creek path. USD prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** founded in 2014 by Noor Haddad (head fitter, years of club running and shop-floor fitting) and Tomas Varga (lasts and stock, works with the partner factory). Noor wanted a quiet shop that watches how a runner actually lands before it shows a single shoe; Tomas built a small house line across four lasts so the fit comes from the foot, not the shelf.
- **Today:** independent; seven people; four lasts (Narrow, Standard, Wide, Open-toe), six house pairs made with a partner factory, a treadmill gait session of 45 minutes.
- **Audience:** road and trail runners of any pace, from returning runners to first-marathon trainees, who want a fitted pair rather than a guess.
- **Tone:** calm, practical, precise. Short sentences, say what the shoe is and how it was fitted. No guaranteed results, health or performance claims, awards, press, ratings or customer counts.

Narrow 94 mm (low-volume feet, narrow heel) - Standard 98 mm (most feet) - Wide 103 mm (high-volume or wide midfoot) - Open-toe 108 mm (wide forefoot, toes that spread on landing).

## A day at the shop (daily routine)
09:30 treadmill belt check and camera charge; 10:00 doors open and the first session starts; noon restock from the back wall of lasts; 17:00 evening runners after work; 19:00 the day's online orders go to the carrier. The daily routine is the same on every open day: watch first, choose second.

## Gait session (four parts, 45 minutes)
Talk 10 min (what you run, how often, what your pair does well or badly) - Watch 10 min (your current shoes on the treadmill at an easy pace, filmed from the side and from behind) - Try 20 min (three pairs on two lasts, two minutes each, played back side by side) - Decide 5 min (buy now, order online later from your session notes, or special-order).

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. One continuous ruled grid with two outer rails; the hero is a hexagon-outline stage closed by diagonal rail lines, with an own "KR" outline monogram and the brand word stacked in the left rail.
2. Specimen cells: one pair per 1:1 cell on its own muted seamless paper, edge to edge, with breathing rows of empty ruled cells between photo rows.
3. 2+1 info rows: text cell with a label, a name and a big outline pill, a two-cell photograph, and a solid clay slash cell in the rail; the next row is mirrored.
Scroll: grid lines draw in as rows enter, cell content rises 24 px, and one full-width gait-lab photograph moves slowly behind its caption.
Reference moods: 4kake.com (ruled grid, specimen tiles) for structure; amprem.jp for the single deep brown ground with off-white type.
