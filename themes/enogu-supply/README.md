# Enogu Supply

A calm, photo-led WooCommerce block theme for an independent art-supply shop that sells paper, pigment, brushes and studio tools to illustrators and design students.

- Home: a promo strip and one top bar, three equal captioned photo columns that crossfade slowly (plan chest, brush counter, pigment jars) with the headline below the photos and a four-figure stats row, four abutting rounded range panels under one light title, product tiles on pale grey, a vertical tagline story, a mill-order band with a slow parallax drift, the shop week as a spec table, six line icons and a Thursday letter band.
- Inner pages: Shop, Paper, Pigment, Brushes, Studio tools, Mill orders, Price list, Visit, About, Contact, Newsletter and the Journal list; the one top menu links the ranges, mill orders and the visit page.
- Fonts: Manrope and Public Sans (bundled, OFL); Noto Sans TC and Noto Sans JP for Chinese and Japanese headings.
- Scroll effects: sp-reveal (up + stagger, zoom) and one sp-parallax band; all off with reduced motion and visible without JavaScript.
- Forms: `shippress_form_slot` newsletter on the home page and the Newsletter page; works without plugins (mailto fallback with a visible notice).
- WooCommerce: optional; sample products are added when it is active, and the home tiles link to them.
- Design notes: `brand.md`, `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md`.
- Photography: AI-generated high-key documentary photographs.
