# Enogu Supply: design spec (stage 1 spec, binding for implementation)

Slug `enogu-supply`, independent art-supply shop (paper, pigment, brushes, studio tools) for illustrators and design students. Row b38-1: archetype **card-mosaic**, hero **product-grid-hero**, nav **top-bar**, page structure **spec-catalogue**, first screen **three-equal-columns**, font direction **sans** (Manrope / Public Sans, CJK Noto Sans TC / JP), type **store** (WooCommerce required, checkout must work), category shops-hobbies, palette "ai-nezumi" (indigo-mouse grey) slate `#5E7380`.

Primary reference (not in `used-refs.txt`, ref-check passed): **Kakimori** https://kakimori.com/ (independent stationery shop, product-photo led). Secondary: **Itoya** https://www.ito-ya.co.jp/ (navigation structure only: two-line menu labels; its saturated hero is NOT borrowed). Measurements in `brand/reference-tokens.md`, quoted as **RT(a)…RT(g)**. Screens: `brand/ref/kak-first.png` (hero), `scroll-kak-900.png` (story + notice), `scroll-kak-1800.png` (new-arrivals band + picks), `scroll-kak-2700.png` (articles + category icons), `scroll-kak-3600.png` (banner), `ito-first.png` (nav).

**Imagery: photo** (stage-a IMAGERY = photo; Kakimori leads with macro product photographs). All photos generated by us with one STYLE string (§6). We never copy the reference's photos, line drawings, copy, logo or full-page layout. The six category icons are our own SVG line drawings.

## 1. Signature techniques (three, from Kakimori; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

### T1. Captioned photo slides with a flat "View more" tab, slow crossfade (Kakimori hero, https://kakimori.com/, `ref/kak-first.png`, RT(b)(e)(g))
- **Reference:** a full-bleed 1.6:1 macro photograph (brass desk pen on a pale reflective surface, desaturated grey-brass) fills the first screen; bottom-left a two-line caption — 16 px label "New Arrival" + 20 px item name "Desk pen", letter-spacing 0.8–1 px, dark text directly on the pale photo — and under it a flat grey `#8A8A8A` "View more" tab 150 × 32 with a white 13 px label; slides change by a slow crossfade (~1 s fade, ~5–6 s hold) while the caption keeps its place.
- **Ours (home hero, converted to the row's product grid):** three equal photo columns in one row under the header; **each column is its own Kakimori slide**: a two-still crossfade and its own bottom-left caption (13 px label "This week" + 20 px item name) and a flat slate tab "See the range" 150 × 32. Geometry in §3.
- **Inner pages (slim version, 8 pages):** Paper, Pigment & paint, Brushes, Studio tools, Mill orders, Price list, Visit, About, Newsletter, Contact open with **two equal photo columns** 1392 × 360 (gap 8 px) with the same caption + tab on each column (static, no crossfade — the crossfade is home-only), page title below the photos (below-photo lockup, 3.25rem).
- **Not to be diluted:** caption is bottom-left inside the photo at 24 px from the left and bottom edges, dark `#333333` text on a pale area of the photo (soft white scrim allowed, no dark box behind it); the tab is flat (no shadow, no outline, r2), 150 × 32, directly under the caption; every column has its own caption + tab; the three columns are equal width.

### T2. Four abutting photo panels in one r8 band with one light centred title across the seam (Kakimori "New arrivals", `ref/scroll-kak-1800.png`, RT(c)(d)(e))
- **Reference:** a 950 × 285 band (3.33:1) with radius 8 px made of four photographs touching edge to edge (no gutters), mixed tints (wood, indigo textile, blush stone, grey-blue); one light serif title "New arrivals" in white, letter-spaced, sits centred across the seam between panels 2 and 3.
- **Ours (home block 2 "The four ranges"):** same 950 × 285 band, r8, `overflow: hidden`, four panels 237.5 px wide each, no gap: **P1 paper drawer · P2 pigment tubes · P3 brushes on linen · P4 studio tools**; centred title "The four ranges" Manrope 300 36 px white, letter-spacing .08em, across the P2/P3 seam; each panel is a link to its range page with a small white 12 px uppercase label (letter-spacing .14em) 20 px from its bottom-left corner.
- **Inner pages:** Shop (WooCommerce archive head: same band as category navigation, title "Shop by range"), Mill orders ("What the mills make": H1, P1, S1, W1), About ("Inside the shop": H1, H3b, P3, W1).
- **Not to be diluted:** panels must abut (0 gap) inside one rounded band; ratio 3.33:1 at desktop; one title only, centred across the seam, light weight, white; uniform darkening overlay `rgba(40,48,56,.18)` so the title reads (no gradient boxes, no per-panel titles larger than the 12 px labels).

### T3. Vertical tagline between two short accent rules + centred section titles with a hugging 1 px underline (Kakimori story block and every section title, `ref/scroll-kak-900.png`, `scroll-kak-2700.png`, RT(b)(f))
- **Reference:** a short vertical tagline set between a 20 × 1 px orange rule above and below, centred on the page above a line drawing and the "Our Story" title; every section title (28 px, letter-spacing .05em, centred) has a 1 px orange underline exactly as wide as the text.
- **Ours:** every section title on every page uses the lockup: Manrope 400 28 px / 35 px, letter-spacing .05em (1.4 px), `#333333`, centred, `display: inline-block; border-bottom: 1px solid #FF5800; padding-bottom: 4px` (underline = text width). The vertical tagline appears in home block 4 (Our story) and as the intro mark of every inner page that has prose (About, Paper, Pigment, Brushes, Studio tools, Mill orders, Visit, Newsletter): rule 20 × 1 `#FF5800`, 12 px gap, tagline, 12 px gap, rule.
  - **zh-TW / ja:** real vertical text, `writing-mode: vertical-rl`, display stack, 18 px, letter-spacing .2em, 8–12 characters (meaning "paper, pigment, brushes and tools"; localised copy lives in the zh-TW / ja dictionaries only).
  - **en / fr / de:** the same vertical column as a stacked tagline, one word per line, centred, Manrope 400 15 px uppercase, letter-spacing .24em, line-height 2 (e.g. PAPER / PIGMENT / BRUSHES / TOOLS). Not rotated text.
- **Not to be diluted:** the rule colour stays the reference orange `#FF5800` (hairlines only, < 1% of the screen); the underline hugs the text (never full-width); titles centred.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion
Kakimori opens on one object, very close: a brass desk pen lying on a pale reflective surface, a second brass piece blurred at left, everything desaturated grey-brass. Bottom-left a quiet caption "New Arrival / Desk pen" and a flat grey "View more" tab; a thin transparent header and a 28 px promo strip above. **Story:** a small shop that shows one beautiful tool at a time, close up and unhurried — "this week, look at this". **Signature motion:** a slow crossfade between full-bleed slides while the caption stays bottom-left; on scroll, titles, cards and banners rise 30 px with a 75 ms stagger and the banner images zoom-fade.

### (2) Our translation (same idea "this week's tools, close up and unhurried", same motion, same image type: photo; composition forced to product-grid / below-photo / stats-row)
The shop counter at Enogu Supply, seen as three slides side by side. **Column 1 (paper):** H1 — Tomás drawing a large cotton sheet out of a wide, shallow plan-chest drawer, crossfading to H1b — macro of the deckled edge of a stack of cotton sheets. **Column 2 (brushes):** H2 — a design student at the counter comparing two round brushes, Amara holding a water pot and a test strip, crossfading to H2b — macro of round brushes standing in a pale ceramic jar. **Column 3 (pigment):** H3 — macro of glass pigment jars (ochre, umber, terre verte) on a pale stone shelf, crossfading to H3b — a hand painting a swatch strip with a fine round brush. ## 3. Home page

### First screen composition
**hero_composition: product-grid · hero_text: below-photo · hero_footer: stats-row · hero_fingerprint: product-grid/below-photo/stats-row**

- **product-grid:** the photos are a grid of three equal product/scene tiles, not a backdrop. Group `.en-hero-grid` (alignfull, padding 0 24 px), CSS grid `repeat(3, 1fr)`, gap 8 px, at 1440: each column 458.7 × 420 (≈ 1.09:1), y 116–536, radius 0 (RT(d) product tiles r0). Each column is a `figure.en-slide` (position relative, overflow hidden) holding **two direct `img`** (A then B, both `object-fit: cover`, width/height 100%); B is `position: absolute; inset: 0; opacity: 0` and fades with the keyframe `en-xfade` (0–43%: 0; 50–93%: 1; 100%: 0) over 14 s `ease` infinite, `animation-delay` 0 s / 2 s / 4 s per column. Keyframes only run under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` and `html:not(.sp-no-motion)`; editor (`.editor-styles-wrapper`) never animates. This is CSS autoplay, not a scroll listener and not an sp class. Focal points: H1 40% 45% (hands + drawer), H2 50% 40% (both brushes in frame), H3 50% 60%.
