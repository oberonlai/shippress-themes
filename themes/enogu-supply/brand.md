# Enogu Supply: brand

## Name
Enogu Supply, an independent art-supply shop for illustrators and design students (fictional). Industry: art supply, store (WooCommerce).

## Location and founding
Calder Row 9, Wrenfield (fictional, international-neutral town). Opened in 2011 by Hanna Weiss. Email hello@enogu-supply.example, phone +00 31 555 0148.

## Story
Hanna Weiss opened Enogu Supply on Calder Row in 2011 with a single plan chest and a small selection of specialist European papers she could not find anywhere in town. Working as a bookbinder and printmaker, she wanted an independent shop where artists could buy full sheets stored flat in drawers rather than pre-cut pads, keeping pure pigments alongside readymade tubes and treating studio tools as durable working equipment rather than decorative gifts.

Daily life at the counter follows an unhurried workshop routine. Tomás Ferreira slides open the wide plan chests to cut sheets to custom dimensions while customers wait, Amara Nwosu keeps water pots and testing strips ready so students can wet a brush before choosing, and Jonah Reyes cares for studio blades and cutting boards. On Mondays, while the shop doors remain closed to visitors, Leo Marchetti wraps stacked paper flat between heavy protective boards to send across Europe.

We keep our four ranges deliberate, narrow, and deep rather than filling shelves with duplicate brand names. By ordering paper directly from three historic mills—Aubel Mill, Hartsend Mill, and Vossberg Mill—we supply dependable cotton sheets, unadulterated earth pigments, and studio tools that reliably serve illustrators, painters, and design students through years of daily studio practice.

## Audience
Illustrators, printmakers, botanical painters and design students who buy paper by the sheet, pigment by the jar and brushes they can test at the counter; studios and colleges placing mill orders.

## Tone
Calm, specific, and unhurried, describing physical materials, paper weights, and workshop routines without marketing hyperbole. The language reflects an independent shop that values tactile experience, technical clarity, and dependable utility for working artists and students.

## The shop week
Mon closed, Leo packs and posts the week's orders. Tue mill crates arrive and are unpacked into the plan chests. Wed restock and brush testing at the counter. Thu late opening until 20:00, demo evening with Sun-hee 18:30-20:00, mill-order cut-off 17:00. Fri paper cut to size while you wait. Sat the busiest day, Mira's student desk. Sun closed.

## How a mill order runs
1. Ask at the counter or by email with sheet size, weight and surface
2. We confirm the mill's price and lead time within two working days (30% deposit)
3. Orders leave for the mill every Thursday at 17:00
4. About three weeks later the sheets arrive; collect them or we post them flat
Minimum 10 sheets of one paper; 30% deposit; orders go to the mill every Thursday at 17:00; about three weeks to arrive. Mills (fictional): Aubel Mill, Hartsend Mill, Vossberg Mill.
