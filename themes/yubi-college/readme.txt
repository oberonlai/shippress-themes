== Description ==

Yubi College is an editorial WordPress theme designed for sign-language interpreter training colleges and specialized adult education institutions. Built with split-screen hero layouts, diagonal grained accents, and overhanging cards, it presents part-time courses, weekly schedules, and partner placements with clarity and restraint. The home page carries the course routes, the teaching week, placements, graduate stories, fees and the taster evening sign-up.

The home page opens on a split hero with one documentary photograph, a grain-stippled diagonal ribbon and an eight-route link grid, then overhanging caption cards, the Saturday table told as a story, a horizontal teaching-week strip, a ribbon band of placement settings with counting figures, a parallax photo band, graduate rows, a full-width fee sheet, notices and the term-letters sign-up. Inner pages: about, courses, a teaching week, placements, tutors, graduate stories, fees and entry, taster evenings (booking), contact, term letters and privacy, each with a small split hero and a bordered jump grid. Contact and newsletter forms use the optional ShipPress form slot and need no plugin.
