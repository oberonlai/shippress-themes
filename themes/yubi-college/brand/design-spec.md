## 0. Fixed brand facts (identical on every page, in brand.md and in all five languages)

Founded **1996** by **Johanna Reiss** (a Deaf teacher) and **Tobias Mendel** (a hospital interpreter), who began with a Saturday class of eleven adults in the meeting room of the Calloway Street public library (historical figures, not in the people list).

## 1. Signature techniques (three; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

All three come from **https://www.o-hara.ac.jp/** (RT(d)(e)(f)(g)). Source shapes are kept: diagonal grained ribbons at 45°, a white panel overhanging a photo with a gradient corner triangle, a bordered link grid with a dark left stub.

### T1. Grain-stippled diagonal ribbons (o-hara hero + "About" band, `ohara-first.png`, `scroll-ohara-about-ribbons.png`, RT(f)(g))
- **Reference:** three broad ribbons at **45°** (bottom-left → top-right), **blue → transparent**, with a visible **grain/stipple** texture (fine noise, `mix-blend-mode: multiply`), crossing the hero corners and again the "About" band, where they start sharp at the band's top edge and dissolve toward the lower left. They slide in diagonally on load.
- **Ours, Home H1 (on P01):** **two** ribbons, each **150px wide** (perpendicular), rotated `-45deg`:
  - **Ribbon A** over the photo's **top-left corner**: centre line from (x −60, y 540) to (x 430, y 50) at 1440×900, i.e. it cuts the corner of the photo; dense end at the top right (`#0E3684`), fading to fully transparent at the lower-left end; clipped by the photo box (`overflow: clip`).
  - **Ribbon B** at the hero's **right edge, lower half**: enters at (x 1440, y 470) and runs down-left to the info-bar top at about (x 1200, y 730), dense at the viewport edge (`#4766B3` → `#0E3684`), fading out toward the lower left; z-index below the text column; it must not cross the h1 or lead (text column ends at y ≈ 640 and its buttons are left-aligned, so x > 1250 stays empty).
  - Build: an empty decorative element per ribbon (`<div class="yb-ribbon yb-ribbon--a" aria-hidden="true"></div>` in the pattern markup; `content: ""` is not used for text), `background: linear-gradient(45deg, rgba(71,102,179,0) 0%, rgba(71,102,179,.55) 45%, #0E3684 100%)`, grain = inline SVG `feTurbulence` (`baseFrequency .9`, `numOctaves 2`) used as `mask-image` together with the linear fade, `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, `opacity: .92`.
- **H5 "Where interpreters work" band:** two larger ribbons (**190px** wide) start **sharp at the band's top edge** at x 330–520 and x 1010–1200 and dissolve down-left over ~520px (exactly the reference About-band behaviour), behind the centred heading; H10 has one 120px ribbon in the band's top-right corner.
- **Motion (RT(g)):** on load, ribbons slide from `translate(-40%, 40%)` to `translate(8px, -8px)` and settle to `0` (the 8px settle), **1.5s `cubic-bezier(.83,0,.17,1)`**, delays **0s / .3s** (A / B). Theme CSS keyed to `html.sp-js:not(.sp-no-motion)` inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`; under reduced motion, `?sp-static`, no-JS and in the editor the ribbons sit in their final place. Ribbons are not `sp-reveal` elements and carry no text. H5 ribbons use `sp-reveal sp-reveal--wipe` on their wrapper (clip on the child, per sp-scroll §4) so they draw in when the band enters.
- **Not to dilute:** keep the 45° angle, the 150px+ width, the grain and the transparent tail. No flat solid stripes, no thin lines, no pastel replacement. Saturated share is controlled by the fade, not by lightening the colour.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://www.o-hara.ac.jp/)
- **What it draws:** a daylit documentary photograph of **a smiling student holding books by a big window**, soft blur, washed to white at the top; **three grained blue ribbons** crossing the hero diagonally from the corners; a centred serif statement (a small lead line above a large serif title, five short lines of text); a **4 × 2 grid of eight programme links** with dark stubs at the hero foot, standing on a **blue gradient floor**; a translucent white header with a burger.
- **Story:** one college group, many ways in. An adult learner stands at the door of learning, and the grid at her feet is the set of doors: pick the one that fits your life.
- **Signature motion:** ribbons slide in diagonally (1.5s `cubic-bezier(.83,0,.17,1)`, delays 0 / .3 / .15s, 8px settle); the photo settles slowly (scale 1.08 → 1 over 5.5s ease, delay 1s) and cross-fades to the next photo every 3.5s; link cells fill blue on hover (0.3s).

### (2) Our translation (same composition idea, same motion idea, same image type: documentary photograph)
**Adults learning by a big window, and the doors in at their feet.** One documentary photograph, daylight from a tall window: on a Saturday afternoon at the long table, **Noor (Deaf tutor) sits across from Tomasz and Adaeze; all three have their hands raised mid-sign**, faces turned to each other, **a closed notebook on the table in a patch of afternoon light**. The reference's lone student with books becomes the moment the course is about: three adults talking with their hands. The ribbons cross the photo's corner exactly as on the reference (our two ribbons, same angle, grain and fade), the photo settles slowly as the page opens (scale 1.06 → 1) and drifts slower than the page (`sp-parallax` 6%), replacing the cross-fade. The serif statement stands **beside** the photo (row composition), and the reference's eight-link grid becomes the **info bar** of eight routes into the college (five courses, a teaching week, taster evenings, fees & entry), standing on a pale aqua floor that keeps the reference's "wash to colour at the foot" without a saturated blue area.
