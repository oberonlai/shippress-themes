# Mame Coffee — brand brief

Design plan row: b15-4 (specialty coffee roastery and bar, hospitality). The English home page and ten inner pages are built; zh-TW, ja, fr, de follow (the brand world below stays the same in every language; prices keep their amount, currency EUR).
All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Mame Coffee.
- **Location:** Branderijstraat 12, 9000 Ghent, Belgium (fictional street): a roastery and coffee bar in one room, five minutes' walk from Gent-Dampoort station.
- **Founded:** 2019, by Ines Duarte (head roaster) and Tomas Varga (green buyer) after years of roasting for others. One 5 kg drum roaster.
- **Audience:** home brewers and café regulars in Ghent, plus restaurants and offices that buy beans wholesale and want to know exactly where their coffee comes from.
- **Idea:** buy few green lots (six a year), keep four on the bar at a time, roast on three mornings a week and pour every lot between day 5 and day 28 after roasting.
- **Hours:** Bar Wed–Sun 8:00–16:00; Monday and Tuesday closed to guests (roasting only). Roasting Monday, Tuesday, Thursday from 6:30; cupping every Thursday 11:00.
- **Voice:** Calm, specific, and documentary, focusing on practical roastery routines and measured brewing without exaggeration. The language is factual and unhurried, presenting clear origin details and honest workshop craft.

## Story
Mame Coffee was founded in 2019 by head roaster Ines Duarte and green buyer Tomas Varga after years of roasting coffee for others. Set in a quiet workshop at Branderijstraat 12 in Ghent, Belgium, the roastery was built around a single five-kilogram drum roaster and a deliberate intention: to purchase only what we can roast attentively and serve at its seasonal peak. Rather than stocking dozens of coffees, Tomas travels directly to origin farms to select just six green lots each year, ensuring we know the growers and harvests intimately.

Roasting takes place three mornings a week on Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday, starting at 6:30. Ines Duarte manages production profiles alongside Amara Okoye, while Tomas roasts whichever lot runs low. Every Thursday at 11:00, Mei Lin Ho cups every production batch from the week to evaluate development and consistency. We keep exactly four lots active on the bar at any given time, allowing each origin character room to be understood and appreciated.

Freshly roasted beans rest until day five, then pour at our coffee bar through day twenty-eight before their flavors fade. Head barista Lena Brandt and barista Rafael Souza extract each cup using calibrated water and precise pour-over recipes, served alongside fresh cardamom buns. From our workshop in Ghent, we also pack whole-bean bags for home brewing and dispatch wholesale orders across the city by bicycle, keeping our daily craft patient, focused, and grounded.

## Process
1. Select six green lots a year directly from partner producers at origin farms.
2. Roast in small batches on our 5 kg drum roaster Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday from 6:30.
3. Cup every roast batch together on Thursday at 11:00 to verify flavor development.
4. Rest roasted beans until day 5 and pour at the bar through day 28.

## Signature (from the S5-Style reference, adapted)
