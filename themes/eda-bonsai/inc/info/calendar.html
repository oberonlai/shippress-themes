Spring

March – May

Watering

Check daily as buds open; water when the surface turns pale.

Feeding

Begin slow feed once new leaves harden, every four weeks.

Pruning

Pinch juniper and pine shoots; let maples run, then cut to two leaves.

Repotting

The main season, as buds swell: young trees every two years, old ones every four or five.

Wiring

Take winter wire off before it marks the bark.

Summer

June – August

Watering

Once or twice a day; shade the pots from the afternoon sun.

Feeding

Continue, with a two-week pause in the hottest spell.

Pruning

Cut back long shoots; leaf-cut strong maples in early June only.

Repotting

None, except azaleas straight after flowering.

Wiring

Light wire on maples; check it every week.

Autumn

September – November

Watering

Taper as the nights cool; water in the morning.

Feeding

A low-nitrogen feed in September, none after November.

Pruning

Thin pine needles; tidy, but leave the tree its strength.

Repotting

Junipers and black pines, early in the season only.

Wiring

The main wiring weeks for conifers as growth slows.

Winter

December – February

Watering

Once a week or less; never water frozen soil.

Feeding

None. The tree is resting.

Pruning

Structural cuts on bare deciduous trees.

Repotting

Prepare soil, pots and wire for March.

Wiring

Wire bare maples; keep pots out of frost below −5 °C.
