Packing

Each tree travels upright in its own pot, the soil covered with moss and paper, the trunk tied to a bamboo frame inside a double-walled box.

Delivery

Nationwide by courier in two to four days, sent Tuesday to Thursday so no tree waits in a depot over a weekend. Free over $150; pots, tools and soil from $8.

Settling in

A 30-day settling-in promise: if a tree fails in its first month with you, send us a photograph and we replace it or refund it.

Care sheet

Every tree comes with a handwritten care sheet: its age, its soil, when it was last repotted and how to water it through the season.
