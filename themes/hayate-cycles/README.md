# Hayate Cycles

A block theme (type store) for a bicycle shop and workshop. Home page (English) with the menu linked to real inner pages (Services with anchored sections, Shop, Prices, Rides, About); the other languages follow.

## Signature
- A two-line "Ride true. / Ride on." heading beside three off-grid low-key photographs that bleed off the right and left viewport edges; an accent block wipes off each photograph on load.
- A hairline service index: edge-to-edge 1 px ink rules, 01-06 numbers, 60 px names, accent pills and 3:2 photographs at the right.
- Capsule photograph masks with a dashed road line, a parallax ride band with one card poking out, a euro price table that stacks on phones, and an ink contact band.

## Patterns
- `front-hero`, `front-index`, `front-shop-story`, `front-ride-band`, `front-process`, `front-prices`, `front-stories`, `front-floor`, `front-contact`, `head-main`, `foot-main`

## Fonts
Schibsted Grotesk and Inter (SIL OFL 1.1, licences in assets/fonts).

## Images
AI-generated for this theme, no text or logos (assets/images/, listed in readme.txt).

## Header and footer
The header carries the menu, a Cart link (WooCommerce cart, or the shop page when WooCommerce is off) and the Book a service pill; the footer shows an Open now spot dot.
