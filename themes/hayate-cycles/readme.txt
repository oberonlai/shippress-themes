== Description ==

Hayate Cycles is a block theme for a Ghent bicycle shop and workshop serving city commuters and weekend riders. The front page sets out the bench services, a workshop price list in euros, the Saturday ride and a small house line of bikes, wheels and lights. WooCommerce runs the shop, the cart and checkout.

Fonts: Schibsted Grotesk and Inter (SIL Open Font License 1.1), bundled. Photographs were generated for this theme. The scroll effects (sp-scroll) respect the visitor's reduced-motion setting and stay visible without JavaScript.
