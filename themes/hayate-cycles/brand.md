# Hayate Cycles - brand brief

Bicycle shop and workshop for city commuters and weekend riders. Sample content in English in this release (home page, ten inner pages and one article); the other languages follow. All names, places, people and prices are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hayate Cycles (wordmark "Hayate Cycles", micro-line "GHENT · SINCE 2012").
- **Location / address:** Kanaalstraat 27, 9000 Ghent, Belgium (fictional address; a former print shop with a tall street window and a long workbench at the back).
- **Founded:** 2012 by Lena Vos and Samir Haddad.
- **Audience:** city commuters and weekend riders.
- **Tone / voice:** The voice is calm and concrete, the way a workshop explains a job before it starts. Sentences name the person doing the work, then the time, the price and the rule that applies.
- **Hours:** Tue–Fri 09:00–18:30, Sat 09:00–17:00, Sun & Mon closed. Phone +32 9 555 01 27, hello@hayate-cycles.example.
- **Currency:** EUR in every language.

## Story
Hayate Cycles was founded in 2012 by mechanic Lena Vos and electrician-turned-mechanic Samir Haddad. They took a former print shop at Kanaalstraat 27, 9000 Ghent, Belgium, and made it a bicycle shop and workshop for city commuters and weekend riders. The tall street window still faces the Kanaalstraat. The long workbench still runs along the back of the room, and that is where wheels are laced, rims are trued and e-bike connectors are checked under a single task lamp.

Every job follows the same short list. Drop-off includes a free 15-minute safety check of the brakes, tyres, chain, bolts and lights, and you leave with a written note. Nothing starts without a written quote, and nothing is added beyond that quote without a phone call. Tune-ups are ready within 2 working days. A puncture or another small fix is done while you wait when the work stays under 30 minutes. Any adjustment is redone free within 30 days. A bike fit with Tomás Ferreira is a 90-minute session at €160, and it is included free with a new bike. New bikes are delivered free within 10 km of the shop. Parts and accessories ship to Belgium, the Netherlands and Luxembourg for a flat €7.50.

The door is open Tuesday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:30 and Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00. Sunday and Monday the shop is closed. Every Saturday at 07:30 a free no-drop ride of 45 km leaves from the shop, led by apprentice mechanic Elif Yıldız. On the first Wednesday of the month at 19:00 the commuter clinic takes 12 places, also free. Mei-Ling Chen runs the service desk and the bookings. Grace Okonkwo looks after new bikes on the floor, and Jonas Becker takes repairs and tune-ups beside Lena’s wheel bench. Call +32 9 555 01 27 or write to hello@hayate-cycles.example.

## Price list (EUR, identical on every page)
- Safety check: Free. Brakes, tyres, chain, bolts and lights, with a written note. 15 minutes at drop-off.
- Puncture repair: €15. Labour while you wait. Tube or tyre extra.
- Wheel true: €20. Per wheel, spoke tension balanced. Same day.
- Basic tune-up: €69. Gears, brakes, wheel true, chain clean and bolt check. Ready within 2 working days.
- E-bike service: €119. Basic tune-up plus motor and battery diagnostic, firmware and connectors. Ready within 2 working days.
- Full service: €149. Strip, clean, regrease bearings, new cables and a full tune-up. Ready within 2 working days.
- Bike fit: €160. 90-minute session for saddle, reach and cleats, with a written fit sheet. By appointment. Free with a new bike.
- Wheel build: €90. Labour per wheel, hand-laced and tensioned. 5 working days.

## Workshop flow
1. Book online or call.
2. Drop-off and a free 15-minute safety check.
3. Written quote, and nothing extra without a phone call.
4. Work and a test ride.
5. Pick-up with a short note of what was done.

## Workshop rules
Free 15-minute safety check at drop-off; written quote before any work; nothing beyond the quote without a phone call; tune-ups ready within 2 working days; punctures and small fixes while you wait when under 30 minutes; any adjustment redone free within 30 days.
