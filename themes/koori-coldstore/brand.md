## Audience
Shippers, food producers, importers and distributors who need chilled, frozen or deep-frozen storage with a logged temperature, and the planners and drivers who work with the dock.

## Voice
Plain, exact and calm, with a number where another site might use an adjective. Name the band, the room and the reading, and stay inside the ranges already published.

## Story
Koori Cold Stores opened in 1987, when Jonas Ekdahl fitted two rooms on Quay 3 at Northmere Port. The quay already moved fish and dairy, and the rooms were numbered from the first week, so a shipper knew which door, which band and which probe reading belonged to the goods. That habit is still the booking. A shipper is given a numbered room, the room is held inside a stated temperature band, and the weekly log follows that number.

In 2009 the store was rebuilt. Rooms 6 to 12 were added, together with an ammonia and CO₂ plant. Those rooms now hold the frozen band, −18 to −21 °C, and the deep-frozen band, −25 to −28 °C. Room 10 includes the blast-freezing tunnel, which takes a pallet to a −18 °C core in about 18 hours. Across all twelve rooms there are 6,400 pallet positions, and eight dock doors with seals serve Quay 3, 18 Saltmarsh Road. Drivers reach the gate from ring road exit 4.

Since 2016 Marit Ekdahl has run the store. A reading is taken every 10 minutes, day and night. If a room leaves its band, the customer is called within 15 minutes, and each week the log is sent as a PDF and a CSV. The dock is open Monday to Friday, 05:00 to 21:00, and Saturday, 06:00 to 14:00. Sunday and public holidays are closed. The office answers on 010 377 4120, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00, and replies to a capacity enquiry within one working day. A room offer is held for 48 hours.

## A day (daily routine)
05:00 the dock opens and the first truck backs onto door 3; 06:00 the log of all twelve rooms is reviewed; 10:30 the plant check (compressors, condensers, frost on the pipes); enquiries from the morning are answered with a room number before 12:00 and afternoon enquiries by 17:00; 20:30 the night lead walks every room with a torch and signs the night log. Readings are taken every 10 minutes, around the clock.

If a room leaves its band, the customer is called within 15 minutes. The weekly log is a PDF and a CSV.

## How a booking is confirmed
1. Ask. Send the band, the pallet count, the pallet type, the weeks, the first delivery date and a description of the goods, by form or on 010 377 4120. We acknowledge the enquiry the same day.
2. Room offer. Within one working day you receive a room number, the positions, the start date and the rate.
3. Confirm. The offer is held for 48 hours. Sign the storage order and book the first 30-minute dock slot.
4. First pallet in. At the dock seal we probe the carton, log the reading with the room number, and read the room every 10 minutes from then on.
