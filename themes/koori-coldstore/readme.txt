Koori Cold Stores is a regional cold-storage operator at Northmere Port. A shipper is given a numbered room in a chilled, frozen or deep-frozen band, and the room is held there with a reading every 10 minutes. The theme presents twelve rooms, 6,400 pallet positions and eight sealed dock doors on one long capacity-enquiry page.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

Fonts: Schibsted Grotesk (headings) and Onest (reading text), both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Chinese headings use a Noto Sans TC subset (OFL 1.1). Japanese headings use a Noto Sans JP subset (OFL 1.1).

== Installation ==

1. Download koori-coldstore.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/koori-coldstore.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Koori Cold Stores.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with their text and images, three journal entries with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Optional forms: the room-alerts section uses a small dynamic block. With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active, their form is shown; without a plugin it shows an email field that opens your mail app (you are not subscribed until you send the request).

Scroll effects use the bundled sp-scroll files (assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js); they respect prefers-reduced-motion. The hero and card slideshows are CSS only, with a small pause button script (assets/kc-pager.js).

The company, its people, places and prices are fictional.
