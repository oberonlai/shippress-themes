Kodou Charging is a calm, single-column WordPress theme designed for commercial EV charging installers and workplace facilities. It pairs documentary site photography with structured assessment workflows, clear service pricing tables, and accessible tabbed site qualification. Built with clean typography and dependable notice banners to communicate technical competency without marketing excess.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns. Fonts: Hanken Grotesk and Nunito Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

== Installation ==

1. Download kodou-charging.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kodou-charging.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kodou Charging.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, a sample post with category and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Optional forms: the contact and updates sections use a dynamic form block (inc/shippress-forms.php). Without a form plugin the contact page keeps an email button and the updates form shows an email field with a submit button that opens the visitor's email app with the request ready to send (the theme sends nothing itself). With Contact Form 7 or WPForms (contact), or MailPoet (explicit form ID) or Mailchimp for WP (newsletter), that plugin's form is shown instead.

Scroll effects: assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js (reveal, stagger, count and parallax). They respect prefers-reduced-motion and show everything without JavaScript.

WooCommerce: support is declared; the theme works fully without it.
