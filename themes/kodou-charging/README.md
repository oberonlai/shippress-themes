# Kodou Charging

Single-column landing theme for a workplace EV charging installer: split photo-left hero with an outlined booking bar, filled-cell site tabs (no JavaScript), a centred statement with two line-pictogram columns, assessment tiles, a price table, story rows and a flat FAQ. Home is one long conversion page with an anchor menu and one repeated request.

See `brand.md` and `brand/design-spec.md`.

## Notes (stage 2 fix)

- Photography: 12 documentary photographs in `assets/images/`; `story-offices` shows a single wall unit in an underground car park and `visit-walkthrough` has both people centred, so neither repeats another frame.
- The footer links to the newsletter page instead of repeating the home subscribe form. Footer and menu currently point at home anchors until the inner pages are added.
- Fonts: Hanken Grotesk and Nunito Sans (OFL-1.1), loaded through `theme.json` fontFace.
