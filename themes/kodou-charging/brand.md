# Kodou Charging: brand

It surveys office car parks, depot yards and staff parking, then fits wall units and twin-socket posts for staff and fleet use. ## Story
Kodou Charging was founded in 2017 by lead electrician Ines Halvorsen in Northfield, starting with commercial electrical installations across local business premises. As commercial vehicle adoption grew, businesses consistently reported difficulties finding contractors who understood both facility load constraints and daily parking operations. The company was established to address this precise gap, providing dedicated electrical surveys and dependable infrastructure tailored directly to existing commercial car parks.

From the beginning, the company chose to focus exclusively on workplace car parks, depot yards, and staff parking facilities rather than residential driveways or highway rapid-charging stations. This specialised focus allowed the technical team to develop disciplined installation practices for multi-vehicle environments, prioritising robust cable containment, switchboard protection, and dynamic load management. By treating the car park as an integral part of the building's electrical ecosystem, installations remain safe, tidy, and unobtrusive.

Operating from Unit 4 on Lantern Street, Kodou Charging today employs a team of seven technical and operational specialists managing every stage from initial site assessment to annual maintenance. Every project begins with a comprehensive ninety-minute survey and a transparent fixed quote, ensuring facility managers have complete clarity before commissioning hardware. The team delivers durable, compliant charging facilities that integrate smoothly into everyday workplace routines.

## A day at Kodou Charging (daily routine)
Daily routine: the office desk opens at 08:00 and takes requests by form or email; site visits run Monday to Saturday from 07:30 so a car park can be walked before the first shift arrives. A typical visit is 90 minutes on site: the assessor meets the facilities manager at the car park entrance, walks the bays, inspects the main intake and distribution boards, maps cable routes and records bay dimensions. Back at the office the written report and fixed quote are prepared within 5 working days. Fitting teams then work bay by bay, typically 1 to 3 days, and finish with a handover and staff briefing.

## Process (4 steps)
1. Request (form or email)
2. Site visit, 90 minutes on site
3. Written report and fixed quote within 5 working days, quote valid 60 days
4. Fitting, typically 1–3 days, then handover and staff briefing
