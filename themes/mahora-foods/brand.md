# Mahora Foods - brand brief

A contract caterer that runs staff lunchrooms inside offices, factories, campuses and depots, based in the harbour city of Eastmere. Sample content in English (stage 1: home page), later Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Mahora Foods. Head office at 14 Mill Quay, Eastmere (fictional).
- **Founded:** 2009. Office hours Mon-Fri 8:30-17:30. Lunch service 11:30-13:30. Phone +1 (555) 014-0162, hello@mahorafoods.example. USD in every language.
- **Voice:** calm, plain and practical, like a good site manager: it says who cooks, at what time, for how many, and what it costs. No promises about outcomes, no superlatives.
- **Reference structure:** a calm account of how a workplace lunchroom is run, who it feeds, how a service day is staffed and how a contract begins.

## History
Mahora Foods began in 2009 with one kitchen in a harbour-side office block and a simple rule: lunch at work should be cooked on site, by people the diners know by name. Ingrid Halvorsen, a chef who had run hotel kitchens, took the first contract with 60 lunches a day and a team of three.

By 2016 the company had moved into factories and a technical college, and wrote the four-week menu cycle it still uses: two mains and one plant-based dish every day, with every allergen labelled. In 2024 it opened the fourth district, Westfields, and added insulated hot boxes for night shifts.

Today Mahora Foods runs 38 lunchrooms in four districts: Northshore 12, Harbour 11, Central 9 and Westfields 6. Each has its own team and a cook in charge who stays.

## Flow (how a contract begins)
Week 1 site visit - Weeks 2-3 menu and staffing plan - Week 6 trial week - Week 8 first service. Minimum term 12 months, notice 90 days.

## Fixed numbers
38 lunchrooms, 4 districts (Northshore 12, Harbour 11, Central 9, Westfields 6), since 2009. Service day: Prep 06:30, Pass 11:15, Floor 11:30, Close 14:30. Staffing rule: 1 team member per 40 lunches. Menu: 4-week cycle, 2 mains plus 1 plant-based dish daily.
