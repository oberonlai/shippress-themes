Hobi Journeys is a corporate incentive travel agency WordPress theme designed for agencies planning reward trips, team summits, and sales retreats for groups of 20 to 120 guests. Built with a results-first case wall, transparent pricing tables, and structured planning workflows, it communicates corporate travel with operational clarity. Featuring calm documentary photography styling and clean sans typography, the theme works seamlessly with or without WooCommerce. The home page opens on a heavy two-line white headline over three crossfading documentary photographs with a hairline ticket card, then the delivered-trip figures, a staggered case wall, three narrative paragraphs, five ruled planning stages beside a sticky photograph, a parallax band, a framed destinations panel, the navy brief band, the journal and The Itinerary newsletter. Plus Jakarta Sans for headings, Public Sans for reading. Works without WooCommerce. Sample content in English; Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download hobi-journeys.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/hobi-journeys.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Hobi Journeys.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with their text and images, journal entries with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. Languages: the sample content is in demo-content.json (English); other languages use the `shippress_demo_lang` option.

Forms: The Itinerary section uses a dynamic form block (inc/shippress-forms.php). Without a form plugin it shows an email field and a submit button that opens your mail program with the address filled in (the visitor is not subscribed until the message is sent). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP it shows that form.

The agency, its trips, clients, people, address and prices are fictional.
