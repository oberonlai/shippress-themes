# Hobi Journeys - brand brief

A corporate incentive travel agency that turns a sales year into a reward trip or a sales-team retreat for groups of 20 to 120 guests. One action everywhere: "Brief us". Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hobi Journeys (wordmark "Hobi Journeys" with a three-dot route glyph; *hobi* - the everyday made into a day out). The site answers results first: the trips already delivered, with headcounts, before any "about us".
- **Location:** Harbour House, 9 Wharf Lane, Port Arden (fictional harbour town). Phone +44 1632 960 418. Email brief@hobi-journeys.example (briefs) and hello@hobi-journeys.example (general).
- **Founded:** 2012 by Maren Holt and Daniel Okoro, after planning the sales trip of a former employer. First programme: 38 guests to Lisbon. Own tour-director team from 2016, first long-haul programme (Cape Town) in 2019, sales-team retreats line in 2023, 41st programme in 2026.
- **People (seven):** Maren Holt (founder and managing director); Daniel Okoro (co-founder, head of operations); Lucía Ferrer (senior programme planner); Arjun Mehta (costing and finance); Sofie Lindqvist (lead tour director); Kwame Asante (tour director); Hana Novak (client briefs).
- **Audience:** sales directors, HR and people teams and founders of 50 to 2,000-person companies who buy reward trips, retreats and kick-off summits for their teams.
- **Tone:** Calm, exact, and quietly warm, relying on specific operational numbers rather than adjectives. The copy avoids awards, external endorsements, and superlatives, speaking plainly about corporate reward travel and on-the-ground delivery. No guarantees, no awards, no "best", no real clients, brands or people; clients are described by industry only.

## A day on a programme (the daily routine)
07:30 the tour director at the breakfast desk with the day sheet - 09:00 coaches leave - 13:00 long lunch - 16:00 free time - 19:30 group dinner - 23:00 last check-in with the duty line.

## Hours (identical everywhere)
- Office: Monday to Friday 08:30-18:00; closed weekends and public holidays.
- Duty line: 24 hours a day for every programme while a group is travelling.
- Briefs are answered within two working days; a costed proposal of three destinations follows in three weeks.

## Prices (EUR per guest unless stated)
1 Short-haul reward trip, 3 nights from €1,850 per guest - 2 Long-haul reward trip, 5 nights from €3,900 per guest - 3 Sales-team retreat, 2 nights from €980 per guest - 4 Kick-off summit, 3 nights from €1,450 per guest - 5 Costed proposal (three destinations, one site visit) €1,200 flat, credited when you book - 6 Extra tour director €420 per day.
Included in every programme: flights or rail, 4-star hotel, transfers, two group dinners, one signature day, tour director(s) (one per 30 guests), 24 h duty line, debrief within 10 working days. Ideal lead time 6-12 months, minimum 10 weeks.

## How a programme is planned (five stages, budget to return flight)
01 Budget and rule - 02 Three destinations, costed - 03 Site visit and final programme - 04 Announce to the team and register guests - 05 Travel, return flight and debrief. Timings: week 1 - weeks 2-3 - weeks 4-8 - from 6 months out - on the trip plus 10 working days.

## The Itinerary
First Tuesday of each month: one delivered trip, one planning note, next season's dates. Subscribe on /the-itinerary/ and on the home page.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Heavy tight white headline (8.75rem, 800, -0.05em) bottom-left on a scrimmed full-bleed photograph, with a transparent hairline ticket card (label row, 40px title, three-cell footer with a route glyph), three faint column hairlines, three slide bars and a three-column navy wipe at load.
2. The case wall: photographic ticket cards in two staggered columns, the right column dropped 120px, under a giant section word.
3. The single way to brief: a mid-navy bar over a light-grey panel of four underlined cells and one red button.
Supporting: a framed centre panel between two full-height photographs, page-wide faint column hairlines, count-up figures, one parallax band.

## Photography
High-key, desaturated documentary photographs (soft daylight, cool grey-blue undertones): hotel lobbies, coaches, airports and destinations with small groups of adult colleagues; no text, logos or signage; no cut-outs.

## Inner pages
Delivered trips, Programmes (with the six-line price table), How we plan, Destinations, About, People, Brief us, Contact, The Itinerary (newsletter), Journal and Privacy, plus the story "Why we still hand out boarding passes". Each page head repeats the home hero (heavy headline over a scrimmed photograph with a hairline ticket card); case walls, the navy brief band and the column hairlines continue on the inner pages.
