# Hobi Journeys

A corporate incentive travel agency block theme: a heavy white headline over three crossfading documentary photographs with a hairline ticket card, a results-first case wall of photographic ticket cards in two staggered columns, and one navy "Brief us" band.

## Design notes
- Palette: white, light grey (#F5F5F5), navy (#192F60) with deep navy (#13203B) and mid navy (#2C3E6F); one red (#E22632) for the single call to action per view.
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans headings, Public Sans body (bundled WOFF2, loaded through theme.json).
- Motion (sp-scroll): rising cards with stagger, count-up figures, parallax on the hero photographs and the full-bleed band; the hero has a CSS-only three-column wipe at load and a three-slide crossfade.
- Forms: The Itinerary slot works without plugins (mailto with a clear notice) and uses MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when present.

## Patterns
`front-hero`, `front-figures`, `front-wall`, `front-narrative`, `front-planning`, `front-band`, `front-destinations`, `front-brief`, `front-journal`, `front-itinerary`, plus `header-main` and `footer-main`.
