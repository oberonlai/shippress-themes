# Seiwa Furniture — brand

Founded 1994. Werkhuislaan 40, 5652 AX Eindhoven, Netherlands. Hours: Mon–Fri 8:30–17:30; Sat 10:00–14:00 by appointment; closed Sun and public holidays.

## Story
Seiwa Furniture began in 1994, when Marieke Vos opened a 400 m² upholstery shop and started making task and meeting chairs to order. The work was the same idea the factory still follows: a chair is specified with an architect, cut and upholstered where the client can see it, and finished only after the fabric has been handled. Architects and fit-out contractors came for seating first, then for desks and tables that had to match a drawing rather than a catalogue page.

In 2008 the workshop moved to Werkhuislaan 40, 5652 AX Eindhoven, and a steel line was added beside the upholstery benches. Bench desk system B6, height-adjustable desk H1, and the frames under task chair T2 and meeting chair M4 are made on that line. Tomás Herrera has run production since 2006; Lukas Brandt has led the upholstery workshop since 2009. Priya Raman, design engineer since 2018, turns incoming drawings into cutting lists and, when a project needs it, a full-size mock-up of one workstation.

Since 2019 every new order has carried a 10-year warranty on steel and timber frames and a 5-year warranty on mechanisms and gas lifts. In 2025 the factory delivered 1,860 seats across 41 contracts for 23 architecture practices and fit-out contractors, and 40 of 41 installations were handed over on the agreed date. Steel frames that year used 68% recycled content, with supplier certificates available on request. Standard lead time remains 6 weeks from signed drawings to delivery. Chloé Martin, in aftercare since 2020, keeps spare parts for the frame warranty and visits once, three months after handover.

## A day at the factory
The day starts at 7:30 when the line leads walk the floor. Frames are welded and powder-coated in the morning, upholstery and assembly run after lunch, and the evening crew loads vans for next-day installs. Visitors on the Thursday 14:00 tour follow the same route a chair takes through the day.
