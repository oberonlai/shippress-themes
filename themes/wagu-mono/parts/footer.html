Colophon

This website, like everything we make, was put together slowly, from a few good materials, to be used every day and mended when it needs it.

Made by

Four makers at the workshop in Alderwick, one piece at a time, each signed under the seat or the top.

Woods

White oak and black walnut from two sawmills within sixty miles, air-dried for a year; cherry when a tree comes down nearby.

Joints

Wedged through-tenons, hand-cut dovetails, half-laps and drawbored pegs. No screws you would ever see, no metal brackets.

Finish

Hardwax oil, rubbed in by hand in three coats. Soap for the pale pieces. Nothing that cannot be renewed at home.

Set in

Libre Caslon Display and Libre Caslon Text for the headings, Karla for everything else.

Photographs

Made for this site in the workshop and the showroom. The diagrams are drawn in plain lines, like the ones pinned over the bench.
