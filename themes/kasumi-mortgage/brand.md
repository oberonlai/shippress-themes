# Kasumi Mortgage — brand brief

Design plan row: b6-4 (mortgage broker, business). Built in English first and localised into zh-TW, ja, fr and de at the client's request (decision of 2026-10-20, the same mechanism as Hokuto Driving School 1.1.0). The brand world below stays the same in every language; products, fees, rules and the buying process follow each country.
All names, places, people, rates and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kasumi Mortgage. "Kasumi" was the founder's grandmother's name; it means mist. The founder's line: "A mortgage arrives wrapped in mist. Our job is to clear it."
- **Location:** 9 Linden Row, Ashcombe, a fictional small river city of Victorian brick terraces, a covered market and new estates on the hill. The office is a former bookshop: one front room with two desks and a round table by the window, a quiet back room for long conversations.
- **Founded:** 2011, by **Naomi Hale** after fourteen years as an underwriter at a building society. She started at her own kitchen table, which is why most first meetings still happen at the client's.
- **Today:** four people, around 300 households a year, a panel of more than 90 lenders (high-street banks, building societies and specialist lenders).
- **Audience:** first-time buyers; couples moving for a bigger kitchen or a school; homeowners whose fixed rate is ending; self-employed people and contractors; landlords with one or two flats; people in their sixties planning the last mortgage.
- **Tone:** plain English, calm and specific. Numbers before adjectives. Never "guaranteed approval" or "best rate in town". We would rather say "wait six months" than push an application that will fail.

## People
- **Naomi Hale** — founder and adviser. Ex-underwriter: she reads a file the way a lender will. First-time buyers and moving home.
- **Tom Ellery** — adviser since 2015. Self-employed, contractors, buy-to-let and anything with an unusual payslip.
- **Elif Demir** — case manager. Chases lenders, valuers and solicitors so clients do not have to; sends the Friday update.
- **Owen Marsh** — protection adviser. Life cover, income protection, the conversation nobody wants and everybody needs.

## A day at Linden Row
- 08:30 Elif opens up, reads lender updates, sets the day's chase list.
- 09:00–12:00 Kitchen-table visits and video calls; the front room table is kept free for walk-ins.
- 12:30 Rate check: Naomi and Tom compare the week's products and update the rates table.
- 14:00–17:30 Applications, case notes, calls with solicitors.
- Thursday until 20:00 for people who cannot call during work. Friday afternoon: every client in progress gets an update, even when nothing has moved.

## Signature
"From the first call to the keys", told as six photographed steps (first call, paperwork, the search, agreement in principle and offer, survey and solicitors, keys). One documentary photograph of a couple at their kitchen table with an adviser leads the first screen. The week's rates sit in one quiet table well below the first screen, beside a worked example.

## Photography
One STYLE for every image: documentary 35mm film photograph, candid, available natural light, muted warm greys, oatmeal and faded slate blue, fine grain, shallow depth of field, ordinary European homes. No readable text, no logos. People as hands, backs or calm three-quarter portraits. No cut-outs.

## User aesthetic
Restrained and real: no poster look, no saturated colour fields, no cut-outs, no side rail, no giant centred slogan, no table, form or calculator on the first screen. Generous whitespace; photographs and narrative paragraphs do the work; one top menu.
