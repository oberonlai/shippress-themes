# Noki Surveyors

Noki Surveyors is a block theme for a small building surveying practice that reports on houses and small commercial buildings before a buyer commits. The home page is one long landing page: a diagonal two-pane photo hero, a numbered condition report, visit times and a fixed EUR price list, with a single call to action, Request a survey. Copy and photography are the practice's own; the visual language is a quiet grey ground with an Edo purple accent.

Reference: Kajima (https://www.kajima.co.jp/): the two-pane photograph hero joined by a seam, the dark sentence-builder band with a white and a grey select box, and the enlarged-centre project strip with round arrows, translated into a building surveying practice. See `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md` and `brand/reference-tokens.md`.

## Home blocks
Header with a centred anchor menu; diagonal two-pane hero with a three-card row; notice strip; the scattered photo field; why survey first; the sentence-builder band; the numbered report ledger; the finding strip; the visit-length parallax band; the price table; case notes; people; questions; the request band; footer.
