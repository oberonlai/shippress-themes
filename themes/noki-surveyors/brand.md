## Story
Noki Surveyors is a small building surveying practice that reports on the condition of houses and small commercial buildings before a buyer commits. Ines Kovač founded the practice in 2011 in Elsmere Quay, and the office is still at 3 Ropewalk Yard. The survey area is within 40 km of Elsmere Quay. Five people share one van: Ines, Tomasz Wren, Adaeze Nwosu, Henrik Saari and Lucía Pardo. They look at ordinary brick and rendered houses, flats and small shops, and they write down what they see in plain words, with a photograph for every finding.

A visit is a close reading of the fabric. Tomasz spends time on ladders at cracked lintels and slipped slates. Adaeze reads damp meters at skirtings and checks air bricks. Henrik looks at shopfronts, felt flat roofs and the separation between a shop and a flat above. Ines leads the practice and surveys commercial buildings and older houses. Lucía runs the request desk: every request is answered within one working day with a fixed price and a visit slot. On site, a flat or small house takes about 90 minutes, a house of 90–180 m² takes 2 to 3 hours, a large house takes 3 to 4 hours, and a small commercial building takes about 4 hours. A single-defect inspection or a re-visit takes about 45 minutes. The full report arrives within 5 working days of the visit, then a 30-minute call. Single-defect and re-visit letters take 2 working days.

The practice does not sell or arrange repairs, and it does not work for the seller or the agent. A survey is not a valuation. Prices include VAT and travel within 40 km of Elsmere Quay, and the client pays after the report is sent, within 14 days. The work is the same each week: the van leaves loaded at 07:45, the first visit starts at 08:30, photographs are uploaded at 12:30, and by 17:00 Lucía has confirmed the next day's slots. About twelve buildings are surveyed in a week. The point of the report is simple. Once a buyer commits, repairs are theirs. Before they sign, the findings are something to talk about with the seller.

## Audience
People buying a flat, a house or a small shop with a flat above: first-time buyers, families, landlords and small business owners who want the condition of the fabric written down in plain words before they sign.

## Tone
Plain, specific and unhurried, like a good field report. Short sentences, concrete nouns (lintel, air brick, valley, felt), numbers for times and prices. No guarantees, no invented statistics, no awards, no claims to find every defect. A survey is a condition report, never a valuation.

## Request flow
1. Send the request form (or email) with the address, the type of building and the date you need the report by.
2. Within one working day Lucía replies with a fixed price and a visit slot.
3. The surveyor visits; someone lets us in, you are welcome to join for the last 20 minutes.
4. The report arrives within five working days, followed by a 30-minute call.

## Inside the report (eight numbered sections)
01 Structure and movement; 02 Roof and rainwater; 03 Damp and ventilation; 04 Walls, windows and doors; 05 Services we can see (visual only); 06 Inside: ceilings, stairs, fire escape routes; 07 Outside: boundaries, paths, outbuildings, trees near walls; 08 Priorities in three columns: now / within two years / keep an eye on, each with a rough cost range.
