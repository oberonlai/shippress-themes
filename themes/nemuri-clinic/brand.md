An evening sleep-medicine clinic in Seattle for working adults who cannot stay asleep. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. ## Story
Dr. Hana Ellery founded Nemuri Clinic in Seattle in 2016 after years of working night shifts and observing how difficult it was for working adults to access sleep medicine during standard daytime office hours. Most patients struggling to stay asleep faced an impossible choice between taking time off work or letting exhaustion compound. Nemuri Clinic was created to resolve that tension by offering evening consultations that begin after the workday ends, allowing individuals to receive clinical care without disrupting their professional responsibilities.

Rather than operating a high-volume diagnostic facility, Dr. Ellery designed the practice around a quiet, unhurried model centered on a single dedicated overnight study room. A calm and private setting ensures that patients undergoing polysomnography or picking up take-home monitoring kits feel respected and at ease. The clinic deliberately limits its overnight capacity to one person per night, with an experienced sleep technologist remaining awake and attentive throughout the entire study.

Care at Nemuri Clinic is defined by a collaborative results visit where the sleep physician and patient sit down together to review the nocturnal recording line by line. Rather than mailing out automated summaries or impersonal reports, the clinical team explains the sleep architecture and oxygen data in plain English. Together, physician and patient establish a clear and sustainable path forward, whether that involves cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia, CPAP coaching, or customized schedule adjustments.

## Process
1. Book by phone or online
2. First consultation (60 min) and two-week sleep diary handed out
3. One night recorded via home sleep test or our clinic study room
4. Results visit within 10 days to read the recording together with the physician
5. Treatment plan and follow-up (CBT-I, CPAP, timing plan, or referral)
