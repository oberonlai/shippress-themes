# Nemuri Clinic

Nemuri Clinic is a calm, low-key WordPress theme designed for an evening sleep-medicine clinic for working adults. It features documentary photography layouts, sticky-media diagnostic panels, transparent fee schedules, and distinctive architectural curves. Built with clear typography and responsive structures, it offers an unhurried digital experience tailored to specialized healthcare practices.

## Signature techniques
- **Offset photo hero:** one photograph on the right 80% of the page with a single 120px rounded top-left corner, a text panel with a white bubble overlapping its lower-left and a white info bar fused inside its bottom-right.
- **Half-disc heading:** `nc-dome`, a circle segment in the colour of the section above that bites into the band or photograph below, carrying the title and a tracked label.
- **Photo rows:** a 520px photograph on the left, a dotted title, short copy and an underlined long-arrow button (`nc-more`), ruled with dotted lines.

## Home page patterns
`front-hero`, `front-statement`, `front-symptoms` (photo rows), `front-method` (sticky photograph beside four stage panels), `front-room` (parallax band), `front-people` (2:1 photograph pair), `front-stories` (1.5-slide scroll-snap row with counter cards), `front-fees` (dotted fee list), `front-strip` (endless photo strip), `front-visit` (hours, address, large telephone).

## Motion
sp-scroll: `sp-parallax` on the hero photograph and the study-room band, `sp-reveal--up` with `sp-stagger` on the symptom rows and the people pair, `sp-marquee` on the photo strip. The hero fades in and its lines rise in on load (theme CSS, no scroll script).

## Photography
All photographs are generated documentary interiors (no text, no logos) in one low-key evening style.

## Inner pages
`page-about`, `page-symptoms`, `page-night-study`, `page-treatment`, `page-team`, `page-fees`, `page-first-visit`, `page-stories` (English; the header menu and home links all point to them). They reuse the photo rows, half-disc headings and fee list.
