# Yukimi Kakigori

Yukimi Kakigori — a kakigori shaved-ice parlour theme built on sticky media: on wide screens the picture column holds still while the text column scrolls past it (the menu, the four bowl rows, the story band and the parlour page). A formal top header, centred: the parlour name and tagline in the middle of the first row, the menu centred on the second row and the Reserve a seat button at the right end; an overlay menu on phones and tablets. Sora-iro, the clear pale sky blue of traditional Japanese colour names (#A9D3E8), is the main colour: the hero, the story band, every page head and the overlay menu are solid ice-blue fields set in deep blue ink, on an ice-white ground with pale ice-mist panels and deep lake-blue links, cool and fresh like a bowl of shaved ice; the red of the strawberry bowl and the peach of the white-peach bowl are the only warm notes. The hero is a product grid: the title, lead, buttons and today's ice on the left, four paper-cut bowls as a taped, slightly tilted 2 x 2 collage on the right, and a cut-out bowl of strawberry ice (transparent WebP) standing in front of the grid and breaking over the hero bottom edge; a cut-out plover flag breaks over the story band and every page head. Every picture is a layered paper-cut illustration in a torn, taped paper collage. Darumadrop One for display, Rubik for reading, Noto Sans TC for Traditional Chinese headings. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German; vertical labels only in Chinese and Japanese. WCAG AA colours; no entrance animations.

- Design plan row b4-8: sticky-media, product-grid-hero, centered nav, collage, paper-cut, sora-iro #A9D3E8 (recoloured in v1.0.1 and again in v1.0.2), Darumadrop One + Rubik.
- Sample content languages: en, zh-TW, ja, fr, de.
- Contrast: deep blue ink #12263A on sora ice blue #A9D3E8 9.7:1 (all text on the hero, the story band including the giant opening year, every page head, the overlay menu and the ice-blue buttons); ink on ice white #F6FAFB 14.7:1, on ice mist #E3F0F6 13.2:1 and on white 15.4:1; deep lake blue #1F4E6E on ice white 8.4:1, on mist 7.6:1, on white 8.9:1 and on sora 5.6:1 (links, labels, eyebrows, pull quote); grey blue #4A6275 on ice white 6.1:1, on mist 5.5:1 and on white 6.4:1 (secondary text); ice white on ink 14.7:1 and pale ice blue #BFDDEC on ink 10.8:1 (footer); sora on ink 9.7:1; sora on ice white only 1.5:1 and white on sora only 1.6:1, used for rules, tape and number badges, never for text.
- Break-frame: the cut-out bowl of strawberry ice (transparent WebP) stands in front of the paper-cut product grid and breaks over the hero bottom edge; the cut-out plover flag breaks over the top edge of the story band and the bottom edge of every page head.
