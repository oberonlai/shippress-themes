# Kasane Venue - brand brief

Wedding venue in a daylit former wool-exchange hall. Sample content in English (stage 1: home); zh-TW, ja, fr and de follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Audience:** engaged couples (and their families) planning a wedding for 20 to 140 guests, who want an unhurried, honest-materials venue and one point of contact.
- **Name:** Kasane Venue (kasane = layers: rooms, light and days laid one over another).
- **Location:** Weavers' Hall, 22 Calder Wharf, Halbrook (fictional riverside town). **Email** hello@kasanevenue.example. No phone number. EUR prices in every language.
- **Hours:** Viewings: Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00 by appointment; Open day: first Saturday of every month 11:00–15:00; Office: Tue–Fri 9:30–17:30; Wedding hire: 11:00–00:30. Music ends 23:30.
- **Founded:** Spring 2016 (hall built 1908, restored 2014–2016). One wedding a day.
- **Tone:** A calm, understated editorial tone that highlights honest materials, natural daylight, and unhurried hospitality. The voice remains grounded and precise, offering clear factual details without emotional hyperbole or marketing jargon.

## History
Weavers' Hall was constructed in 1908 on the riverside quayside of Calder Wharf in Halbrook, originally commissioned as a bustling regional wool exchange. Throughout the early decades of the twentieth century, local merchants and shearers gathered beneath its lofty ceilings, trading fleece under the abundant natural light that poured through fourteen arched windows. In 1971, the building transitioned to storage for textiles before falling entirely vacant between 2003 and 2013, holding a decade of quiet stillness along the water.

Between 2014 and 2016, co-founders Ines Varga and Tomas Achterberg undertook a comprehensive two-year architectural restoration of the historic structure. They stripped away later partitions to reveal the exchange's original solid oak flooring and textured brickwork. Finishing the interior perimeter walls with traditional lime-wash, they preserved the hall's generous scale, soft acoustics, and bright daylight while introducing modern heating, private dressing rooms, and a purpose-built kitchen.

In spring 2016, Kasane Venue opened its doors as a dedicated wedding venue committed to hosting exactly one wedding a day. By granting exclusive private access to the Weavers' Hall, the North Room, the Dressing Room, and the gravel Garden Court from eleven in the morning until past midnight, Ines and Tomas established a sanctuary where couples and their families celebrate without haste, surrounded by authentic historic materials and unhurried riverside tranquility.

## People (seven, all fictional)
- **Ines Varga**, Co-founder, venue director. Co-founder and venue director who established Kasane Venue in spring 2016 following the two-year restoration of Weavers' Hall. She oversees daily venue operations and ensures every couple enjoys exclusive access to the building.
- **Tomas Achterberg**, Co-founder, keeper of the building. Co-founder who directed the restoration of the 1908 wool exchange between 2014 and 2016. He maintains the fourteen arched windows, original oak floors, and lime-washed brickwork throughout the hall.
- **Amara Okonjo**, Wedding coordinator. Wedding coordinator providing a single point of guidance from the initial viewing through to the final song. She leads three structured planning meetings six months, eight weeks, and ten days before each wedding.
- **Priya Nair**, Bookings and viewings. Guides couples on sixty-minute walk-throughs of the hall and grounds from Tuesday to Sunday. She manages availability schedules, seven-day date holds, and monthly open days.
- **Lucien Marchetti**, Head of the kitchen. Leads the house kitchen, preparing three-course seasonal dinners and next-morning brunches. He designs menus centered on fresh regional ingredients cooked directly on site.
- **Hana Kowalczyk**, Florist in residence. Resident florist crafting seasonal floral arrangements and tabletop foliage tailored to the hall's natural light. She also prepares dried petal confetti for ceremonies in the Garden Court.
- **Rafael Duarte**, Evening host and bar. Manages reception hospitality, dinner drink service, and the evening bar through the conclusion of music at 23:30. He coordinates courtyard transitions and ensures an unhurried atmosphere for guests.
