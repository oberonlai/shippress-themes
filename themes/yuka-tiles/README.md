# Yuka Tiles

Yuka Tiles is a calm, photographic WordPress theme designed for tile and stone showrooms, material libraries, and surface merchants. Built with a card-mosaic archetype and clean sans-serif typography, it presents materials in natural daylight using flush photo ledgers, spec-sheet definition tables, and sample-borrowing workflows. Fully responsive and WooCommerce compatible, the theme features subtle parallax movement and a shrinking two-weight wordmark.

Reference: Artedomus (https://www.artedomus.com/): the sun-edge photograph, the flush 1-up and 2-up panel ledger with scrim caption plates and paired outline boxes, and the two-weight wordmark that shrinks on scroll, translated into a tile and stone showroom. See `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md` and `brand/reference-tokens.md`.

## Home blocks
Header with the two-weight wordmark and one centred menu; hero (three flush photo columns with one continuous shadow wedge, headline and outline boxes beside them) with a stats row; the flush range ledger; why the window; the loan card; the parallax showroom band; the spec-sheet table; the price band; project cards; visit hours; the new-arrivals letter; a six-column footer.

## Shop (optional)
The theme declares WooCommerce support but does not require it. With WooCommerce active it ships `archive-product` and `single-product` templates and five sample products (standard sample loan, large board deposit, layout plan, site measure, pallet delivery) matching the Prices page. Without WooCommerce the Prices, Borrow and Contact pages carry the same information.

## Patterns
`front-hero`, `front-ranges`, `front-daylight`, `front-borrow`, `front-band`, `front-spec`, `front-prices`, `front-projects`, `front-visit`, `front-letter`, `tpl-home` (posts list), `header-main`, `footer-main`.

## Fonts
Schibsted Grotesk and Albert Sans (OFL-1.1), Noto Sans TC and Noto Sans JP for Chinese and Japanese, loaded through theme.json.
