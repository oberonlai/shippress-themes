# Yuka Tiles: brand

## Name
Yuka Tiles, an independent tile and stone showroom (fictional) where architects, builders and homeowners compare materials in daylight at a window before they order. Industry: tile and stone showroom, store type; WooCommerce is declared compatible but never required.

## Location and founding
Saltgate Works, Unit 3, 21 Weaver Street, Eastmere (fictional, international-neutral town). Founded in 2009 by Yuka Brandt. Email hello@yuka-tiles.example, phone +00 31 555 0148.

## Story
Yuka Tiles was founded in 2009 by Yuka Brandt at Saltgate Works in Eastmere as an independent tile and stone showroom designed around natural daylight. Having observed how electric showroom lighting distorts glazes and stone surfaces, Yuka established Unit 3 on Weaver Street so architects, builders, and homeowners could inspect materials honestly before placing an order.

The showroom is organized along a south-east-facing window wall where a low pale-oak bench receives direct sunlight throughout the morning and early afternoon. Between 10:00 and 14:00, visitors compare large slabs and glazed tiles in true daylight, watching the sun edge cross the bench at 13:00 to reveal subtle shade variations, surface textures, and joint relationships.

Today, the showroom maintains five curated ranges, a library of 640 reference samples available for seven-day home loans, and a strict commitment to technical clarity. Staff specifiers issue comprehensive eight-field specification sheets within 48 hours, ensuring every domestic and commercial surface choice is grounded in verified physical facts.

## Audience
Architects and specifiers, builders and installers, and homeowners planning a floor, a wall or a stair who want to see porcelain, stone and glaze in real light before ordering.

## Tone
A calm, precise, and understated voice that speaks through technical clarity and physical material facts. It avoids marketing hyperbole and sales jargon, reflecting the quiet confidence of a dedicated architectural showroom. Short sentences, concrete nouns, formats and hours instead of slogans. No guarantees, no invented figures, no awards.

## Hours
Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 18:00, Thursday until 20:00; closed Sunday and Monday. Window appointments (60 minutes with a specifier) at 10:00, 11:30 and 13:00. Replies within one working day; spec sheets within 48 hours.

## A day at the showroom (daily routine)
Daily routine: 09:30 Tomasz restocks the window wall from yesterday's returns; 10:00 doors open and the first window appointment begins as the light reaches the bench; 11:30 architects' appointment while Amira prints spec sheets; 13:00 the sun edge crosses the bench, the best hour for comparing; 15:00 returns are checked, cleaned and re-shelved; 18:00 close (Thursday 20:00, evening comparisons under the window's last light and warm lamps).

2. Natural stone: limestone, travertine, slate; honed, brushed, split face.

## Stats row
5 ranges on the window wall, 640 samples to borrow, 7 days per loan, 48 h to send a spec sheet.

## Borrowing a sample
1. Choose at the window wall — with a specifier, or from the range pages.
2. Take up to five samples — standard samples 30 × 30 cm are free; you sign one loan card; large-format boards 60 × 120 cm carry a refundable EUR 40 deposit each; every sample goes with its spec sheet.
3. Live with them for seven days — look at them on your own floor in morning, midday and evening light, next to your cabinet doors and paint.
4. Return and order — bring them back to the counter (or extend once by seven days by email); order, and we book the layout drawing, site measure and delivery.

## What a spec sheet records (eight fields)
Range and code; format and thickness; surface finish; slip rating; edge (rectified or cushion); shade variation (V1 to V4); joint width and grout colour; stock and lead time.
