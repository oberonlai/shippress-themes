# Yuka Tiles: design spec (stage 1 spec, binding for implementation)

Slug `yuka-tiles`. A tile and stone showroom where architects, builders and homeowners compare materials **in daylight at a window** before they order, borrow samples for a week, and leave with a specification sheet. Row b39-4: archetype **card-mosaic**, hero **product-grid-hero**, nav **centered**, page structure **spec-catalogue**, first screen **three-equal-columns**, type **store** (WooCommerce declared compatible, not required; the site works fully without it, no shop page in the demo), font direction **sans** (Schibsted Grotesk / Albert Sans, CJK Noto Sans TC / Noto Sans JP, gothic/hei), palette "copper patina" (dosei) copper-grey `#7A6758`, category shops-hobbies. Five languages: en, zh-TW, ja, fr, de.

Primary reference (ref-check passed, not in `used-refs.txt`): **Artedomus** https://www.artedomus.com/ (S5-Style entry; tile, stone and surface merchant). No secondary reference. Measurements in `brand/reference-tokens.md`, quoted below as **RT(a)…RT(g)**. Screens: `brand/ref/artedomus-first.png` (first screen: giant two-weight wordmark, nav row, full-bleed glazed tile in a sun beam), `scroll-artedomus-1000.png` (2-up panels with bottom scrim, caption, pairs of outline link boxes), `scroll-artedomus-1900.png`, `scroll-artedomus-2700.png` (1-up caption band, six-column footer).

**Imagery: photo** (stage-a IMAGERY = photo; every Artedomus panel is a photograph). All photos are generated by us with one STYLE string (§6). We never copy Artedomus photos, copy, logo or wordmark, or its full-page layout. The shadow wedge overlay, outline boxes and wordmark are our own CSS/markup.

## 1. Signature techniques (three, from Artedomus; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

### T1. Sun-edge triptych (Artedomus first screen, https://www.artedomus.com/, `ref/artedomus-first.png`, RT(a)(e))
- **Reference:** under the white header, one full-bleed close-up of muted sage glazed tile where two wall planes meet; a hard sun beam lights one plane and a dark **shadow wedge** with a razor-sharp diagonal edge cuts across the floor tiles; grout lines and bevelled corners are visible; nothing else on the picture.
- **Ours (home hero, §3):** three **equal, flush** portrait photo columns (porcelain | client at the bench | glazed tile), **2 px white seams** (RT(c) seam), radius 0, each lit by low daylight from the upper left. Over the three columns lies one decorative overlay `.yt-sun` (own CSS, `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events:none`): a **shadow wedge with one hard straight edge running continuously across all three columns and both seams** — `linear-gradient(112deg, rgba(0,0,0,0) 0 54%, rgba(38,34,30,.22) 54% 100%)` (the hard stop at 54% is the sun edge; lower-right triangle is shadow). The photos themselves are prompted with the same diagonal light so the edge reads as real light.
- **Not to be diluted:** columns stay equal and flush (no gutters other than the 2 px white seam, no rounding, no cards); the wedge has a **hard** edge (no blur, no feather; opacity 0.20–0.24), and it must cross all three columns at one continuous angle; the photos stay high-key and low-chroma, never dimmed overall.
- **Inner pages:** every major inner page head (Ranges, Borrow a sample, Spec sheets, Projects, The showroom, Visit) repeats the triptych at a smaller size (§4 "T1 head"): three flush columns 60% wide × 380 px with the same `.yt-sun` wedge, page title beside on white.

### T2. Bottom scrim caption plate with a pair of outline link boxes (Artedomus panels, `ref/scroll-artedomus-1000.png`, `ref/scroll-artedomus-2700.png`, RT(a)(c)(d))
- **Reference:** each photo panel ends in a bottom gradient scrim `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(0,0,0,0) 0, rgba(0,0,0,.33) 100%)`; on it, bottom-left, a white caption ~32 px / 400, and under it **two outline link boxes side by side**, each half the panel width minus the gap: 2 px solid white border, translucent fill rgba(0,0,0,.2), 44 px tall, 14 px side padding, 16 px white text, 24 px gap; padding 23.4 px 24 px.
- **Ours:** every range panel in the home range ledger (block 2) and the showroom parallax band (block 5) carry exactly this plate: scrim (RT(a) exact; strong `.66` variant on the 2-up panels so white text passes 4.5:1), caption Schibsted Grotesk 400 2rem white, one-line descriptor Albert Sans 300 16 px white, then the **pair** of boxes: "Borrow a sample" + "Read the spec sheet" (ranges) / "Book a window appointment" + "Plan your visit" (band). On white grounds (hero text column, inner heads) the same pair appears as **2 px copper `#7A6758` outline boxes**, transparent fill, copper text, hover fill `#7A6758` + white text (.2 s ease).
- **Not to be diluted:** always two boxes, side by side, equal width, 2 px border, square corners, 44 px tall; never replaced by a single pill button or a text link; caption stays bottom-left inside the photo on the scrim (below the first screen only).
- **Inner pages:** Ranges (each of the 5 range panels), Projects (each project panel: "Read the project" + "See the range"), Borrow a sample (closing plate on photo D1: "Email your list" + "Visit hours"), Spec sheets (plate on R3: "Request a sheet" + "Borrow this sample"), The showroom (plates on its ledger panels H3, M1, R1). Copper variant in every T1 inner head.

### T3. Alternating 1-up / 2-up flush photo ledger (Artedomus home body, `ref/scroll-artedomus-1000.png`, `ref/scroll-artedomus-1900.png`, RT(c)(e))

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion
Artedomus opens with a white band holding a **huge two-weight wordmark** (thin "arte", black "domus") and a single row of quiet grey nav links; beneath it, **one full-bleed close-up of muted sage glazed tile in a hard sun beam**: a wall plane and a floor plane meet, a sharp-edged **shadow wedge** slices diagonally across the floor tiles, grout lines and bevelled corners catch the light. No headline, no button on the picture. **Story:** a surface is judged in real light — the material speaks; the merchant stays quiet and grey. **Signature motion:** the giant wordmark **scales down as you scroll** (CSS scroll-driven, linear, over the first ≈130 px) until it sits small in a compact sticky header; photo panels **fade in** as they load (≈300–400 ms, ease, no movement).

### (2) Our translation (same idea "judge the surface in real daylight", same motion family, image type photo; composition forced to product-grid / beside-photo / stats-row)
Our first screen is the showroom's window bench at 10:00. The single sun-beam close-up becomes **three equal flush columns of the same daylight**: **column 1** a large warm-grey porcelain floor tile with the sun edge across it; **column 2** the scene from the brief — **Nadia, a homeowner, kneeling beside a low pale-oak bench where two large porcelain tiles lie side by side, a tray of grout sticks beside them**, the light falling across both tiles; **column 3** muted sage glazed wall tile at an inside corner with a shadow wedge (the closest material to the reference picture, as homage to its colour, not a copy). One continuous hard shadow edge (`.yt-sun`) crosses all three columns: it is the same sun, three materials. The quiet text — headline, short lead and the two outline boxes — stands **beside** the photographs on white, as Artedomus keeps its words off the picture; under everything a **stats row** states the facts of the loan. Motion keeps the reference's family: the two-weight wordmark shrinks on scroll; the three photographs drift slowly at different parallax speeds as the page leaves the hero (light moving over material); the stats count up once. The concept is kept, not dropped: a surface judged in real daylight before anything is ordered.
