# Koe Interpreters - brand brief

Conference interpreting agency that staffs booths for bilingual meetings, with a single way to request a team. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Koe Interpreters. "Koe" is the Japanese word for voice; the agency is otherwise international and plain-spoken.
- **Location:** Havenkade 14, 3011 AB Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional). EUR prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2011, by Ines Okonkwo-Bauer, who still works as chief interpreter.
- **Audience:** meeting organisers (boards, associations, press offices, engineering and medical teams) who need interpreters for a bilingual or multilingual meeting and want one calm account of how it will be briefed and staffed.
- **Tone:** The tone is calm, precise and understated, reflecting the quiet discipline of professional conference interpreting. It addresses organisers with clarity and natural authority, avoiding marketing hype and unsubstantiated claims.
- **No claims of** guaranteed quality, awards, real clients, real media, real people or real brands. The three stories are anonymised fixed cases (a shipping group, a medical-device association, a consumer-electronics maker).

## History
Koe Interpreters was founded in 2011 at Havenkade 14 in Rotterdam, with a focused commitment to bilingual conference rooms and international assemblies. Rather than operating as an impersonal booking exchange, it began as a small agency whose calm, prepared voices bridge speakers and listeners.

From the earliest assignments the operating principle has been that the agency only accepts briefs where its interpreters can be prepared with full background documentation and verified terminology. Every simultaneous meeting follows the booth rule: two conference interpreters per language pair, turns every thirty minutes.

Today the roster holds thirty-eight conference interpreters covering English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German. The team is kept compact and is coordinated directly from the Rotterdam desk, so that every interpreter is known personally, briefed rigorously and ready for direct interpretation and English relay.

## A day at the booth (the daily routine)
The request desk is open Monday to Friday, 08:30-18:00 CET. A meeting day follows the same routine: 07:45 the team arrives and reads the glossary through; 08:15 sound check with the technician; 09:00 the plenary opens and the turns change every 30 minutes; 12:30 lunch and handover notes between booths; 17:30 close, and the chief interpreter files the glossary notes. Three languages? A booth is added and the third language works through English relay.

- Worked example: 2 interpreters x 1,180 + 2 x 240 preparation = 2,840.
- Cancellation: more than 14 days before, free; 7 to 14 days, 50%; under 7 days, 100%. Rates valid for meetings booked from 1 January 2027.
- Process: (1) send the brief, reply and fixed quote within one business day; (2) documents five working days before, twenty-minute briefing call, glossary shared two days before; (3) team arrives seventy-five minutes before opening, glossary notes returned within two business days.
