# Koe Interpreters

A calm, photographic landing theme for a conference interpreting agency. A pinned film-grain booth photograph sits behind a slanted navy scrim with three slanted photo columns bleeding off the right. A phrase marquee with photo capsules and a white sheet with rounded top corners ride over the hero.

Further down: nine tilted language-pair cards on a teal-to-amber band, an hour ruler for the meeting day, a plain rates table (EUR), a three-step band, and one repeated "Request a team" button.

- Fonts: Fraunces (headings) and Newsreader (reading), bundled under the OFL.
- Main menu links to the Briefs, Meeting day, Rates and Notes pages; inner pages reuse the gradient page head.
- Sample content: English now; Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow in a later release.
- Motion: parallax, marquee and reveal-up from the bundled sp-scroll files; all of it is off under reduced motion and in the editor.
