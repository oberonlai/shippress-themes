# Yuzuriha Retreat

Editorial theme for a residential retreat lodge. Home: full-bleed first-light photograph under a frosted 67px header with a day-line, tall portrait with an outline circle label, three square calendar cards, one retreat day on two hairline rows, two offset lodge photographs, price table, people rows, the one way to reserve, journal grid and a ridgeline-cut closing band. Fonts: Newsreader and Figtree. Brand and design notes in `brand.md` and `brand/`.
