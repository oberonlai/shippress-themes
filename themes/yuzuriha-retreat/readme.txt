Yuzuriha Retreat is an editorial theme for residential retreat lodges, yoga and walking retreats and small hospitality businesses. A full-bleed first-light photograph sits under a frosted header with a day-line along its foot, followed by square calendar cards, a hairline retreat day, offset lodge photographs, a price table and a ridgeline-cut closing band. Built with block patterns, theme.json tokens and full site editing support.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The home page opens on a full-bleed photograph under a frosted 67px header, with the headline lower left on a dark forest glow and a hairline day-line along the foot. It then runs through a tall portrait with an outline circle label, three square calendar cards, one retreat day on two hairline rows, two offset lodge photographs, a price table, seven people as hairline rows, the one way to reserve, a journal grid and a ridgeline-cut closing band. See README.md for the design notes.

== Installation ==

1. Download yuzuriha-retreat.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/yuzuriha-retreat.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Yuzuriha Retreat.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, journal entries with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The scroll effects (assets/sp-scroll.css and sp-scroll.js) respect prefers-reduced-motion and show the final state without JavaScript. The newsletter and contact slots use Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when active; without a plugin they fall back to an email link.

The lodge, its people, address, telephone number, email and prices are fictional.
