## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://earthboat.jp/)
- **What it draws:** a full-bleed documentary film of a dark timber sauna cabin half buried in snow, a chimney pipe, a tan forest slope in late light behind; **two guests in down jackets walk toward the cabin** in the lower middle. Over it: a frosted 67px header strip with the dark serif wordmark left and a single dark pill "Search Availability" right; "Nature Escape" in a soft serif, white at 60%, lower left on a blurred dark glow. No other text, no buttons in the picture.
- **Story:** step out of daily life into a small private cabin in nature, quietly, with someone you like; the place is shown through people arriving, not through facilities.
- **Signature motion:** the film loops slowly (muted autoplay); the header stays a frosted strip over moving imagery; as the page moves the photograph gives way to the pale olive ground.

### (2) Our translation (same full-bleed people-arriving composition, same slow movement, same image type: documentary film-grain photograph)
**First light on the lodge deck: Ines, our teacher, kneels at the left third of a long timber deck above the forest, unrolling the last of twelve mats in a row; behind her, in the lower middle-right, two guests (diverse adults, wool layers, faces soft or turned) walk from the kitchen door carrying cups of tea; mist in the larch forest below, a pale first-light sky band across the top fifth.** Same structure as the reference: one quiet building edge (deck rail and kitchen door instead of the cabin), two people walking into it, forest behind, warm timber against cool air. Story: "three to seven quiet days, nothing to decide" — the day has not started yet; the bell is at 05:45 and the mats are going down. Motion: instead of a looping film, the photograph drifts slowly against the page with `sp-parallax` (same "the picture moves under a still header" effect), and a thin **day-line** across the foot of the photo shows where this moment sits in a retreat day (05:45 → 21:00), echoing RT's 2px progress line.
