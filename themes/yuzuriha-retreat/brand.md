# Yuzuriha Retreat - brand brief

Residential retreat lodge for yoga, forest walks and plain meals. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Yuzuriha Retreat. A small forest lodge that hosts groups of twelve for three to seven quiet days.
- **Location:** Larch Hill Lane 9, Fernhollow Valley (fictional, international-neutral). Phone +00 44 555 0186. Email stay@yuzuriha.example. Address order follows each language's local convention.
- **History:** opened in 2014 in a former forestry hostel. 2018: the old storage barn became the practice hall (14 x 9 m, timber floor). The lodge never grew past twelve guests, so that every guest can sit at one long table.
- **Today:** eight rooms (4 single, 4 twin), one practice hall, one covered deck over the forest slope, one long table for every meal. No televisions; phones rest in a basket by the door during practice (asked, never enforced). Open March to November, closed December to February.
- **Audience:** tired rather than ill adults, first-timers welcome, no experience needed; retreat organisers who want to hire the whole lodge with their own teacher.
- **Tone:** quiet, plain, concrete. Short sentences, times and numbers rather than promises. No detox, healing or "transformation" claims, no awards, press, ratings or guest counts beyond the facts below.

## The day (a retreat day, the daily routine that runs the site)
05:45 wake bell - 06:15 morning practice (90 min, deck or hall) - 08:00 breakfast at the long table - 10:00 forest walk (2-3 hours; the 9 km larch ridge loop or the 5 km stream path) - 12:30 lunch - 14:00 rest, reading, an optional workshop - 16:30 evening practice (60 min, slow and restorative) - 18:30 supper - 21:00 quiet hours until the bell.
Arrival day: lodge van from Fernhollow station at 14:30 (25 minutes), welcome tea 16:00, first supper 18:30. Departure day: last practice 06:15, brunch 10:00, van to the station 14:00.
