Michi Motors, a block theme for a family car dealership in Sacramento: new cars and approved used cars as WooCommerce vehicles, a service bay and three sites. Full-bleed dimmed front-on car photographs sit under a transparent, wide-tracked uppercase menu; a big light headline is staggered across the left and right halves; a short crimson rule with a lead and a 66 px outline ring arrow end every section. Graphite, steel and mist with one signal crimson used only for tiny marks. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German. Libre Franklin and Gelasio. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; no entrance animations.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes).

== Installation ==

1. Download michi-motors.zip from the theme releases.
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Michi Motors.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates the home page and ten inner pages (New Cars, Approved Used, Service, Locations, Our Team, Finance, Test Drive, Contact, About and the Journal) with all their text and images, six journal entries with two categories and the menu, and sets the front page. With WooCommerce active it also adds the eight vehicles as products (inc/woo-import.php) and uses the New Cars page as the shop page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The dealership, its cars, people, addresses, telephone numbers and prices are fictional.
