Michi Motors, a block theme for a family car dealership in Sacramento: new cars, approved used, a service bay and three sites. The home page opens on a full-bleed dimmed front-on car photograph under a transparent, wide-tracked uppercase menu with a divider before TEST DRIVE; a big light four-line headline is staggered across the left and right halves, followed by a short crimson rule with a three-line lead and a 66 px outline ring arrow. The same marks run through every section: the family story, a feature car with a four-row ledger, approved-used statements, a dimmed service band, three location rows, a steel band with a pull quote, the journal and a test-drive close. Graphite, steel and mist with one signal crimson used only for tiny marks. Libre Franklin and Gelasio. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; no entrance animations. This is stage 1 (home page, English) for owner review.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes).

== Installation ==

1. Download michi-motors.zip from the theme releases.
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Michi Motors.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates the home page with all its text and images, three journal entries with two categories, the menu, and sets the front page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The dealership, its cars, people, addresses, telephone numbers and prices are fictional.
