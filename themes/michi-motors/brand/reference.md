## Signature technique candidates
Renoca
- R1: full-bleed first-screen photo of a vehicle straight-on and centred, whole image dimmed with a dark navy-grey overlay; nav sits transparent on the photo.
- R2: nav = small uppercase Latin labels (LINE-UP / LIFE STYLE / JOURNAL / SUPPORT ...) in one row at the right, wide letter-spacing, a thin vertical divider before the last utility items, logo script mark at far left.
- R3: photo-first rhythm: lifestyle scene shots (people, outdoors) carry the story instead of boxes.
Nissan Creative Services
- N1: huge light-weight grotesque headline (about 11-12vw cap height block) in 3 staggered lines broken across the left and right halves ("Empowering Mobility," / "Ensuring" right-shifted / "Quality." under), overlapping the photo.
- N2: short crimson horizontal rule (about 28px) before a 3-line small lead paragraph, left, under the headline.
- N3: bottom-left "↓ Explore" label with tiny crimson arrow; bottom-right circular outline button (about 66px ring) as a motion control.
- N4: crimson used only as tiny accent (logo, rule, arrow, ring icon); everything else monochrome blue-grey.
