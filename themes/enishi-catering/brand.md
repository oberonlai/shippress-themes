# Enishi Catering - brand brief

Catering company for offices and family celebrations, Harbourside (fictional). Sample content in English first; zh-TW, ja, fr and de follow in stage 2, with the same figures in every language. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Enishi Catering. "Enishi" is our word for the ties made at a shared table.
- **Location:** 14 Wharf Lane, Harbourside (fictional); service area within 40 km.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Mireille Duval.
- **Hours:** Tue–Sat 09:00–18:00; tastings Thu & Fri 14:00–17:00; events any day until 23:30.
- **Phone / email:** +1 555 0142 (fictional), hello@enishi-catering.example.
- **Voice:** The voice is calm and concrete, the way a manager would talk a client through a booking: dates, guest counts, and what happens in the room. It stays with this kitchen's times and prices, and it does not reach for slogans, awards, or a promise the list does not already state.

## Story
Mireille Duval founded Enishi Catering in 2014. The kitchen is still at 14 Wharf Lane, Harbourside, and every booking we take stays within 40 km of that door. Enishi is our word for the ties made at a shared table: the lunch that carries a team through a working day, and the long table a family sits down to when the room is already set.

The work has kept the same shape since that first year. We cook at Wharf Lane and carry a finished table to a room the client has chosen, whether that is a meeting room, a hired hall, a glasshouse, a rowing-club room, or a studio. Office lunches need 48 hours' notice and at least 12 guests. Celebrations and banquets need three weeks, and final numbers are due five days before service. A deposit of 30 percent holds the date. The crew arrives three hours before the guests.

The kitchen is small, and the people who cook are the people who serve. Mireille still heads the pass. Tomasz Wren is sous chef, Ayo Okafor makes the pastry, Lena Hartmann runs the engagement, Rafael Costa leads the floor, and Priya Raman looks after office lunches. We do not sell alcohol. If a client brings it, the crew will serve it. An event menu starts at a tasting for two, on a Thursday or Friday afternoon, and that fee is credited when you book.

## Services and prices (USD, identical in every language)

- Canapé reception, 8 pieces: $34. Per guest. Minimum 20.
- Family celebration buffet: $46. Per guest. Minimum 20.
- Seated banquet, three courses: $72. Per guest. Minimum 30.

- Service staff: $38. Per staff hour.

## The engagement
1. Enquiry — day 0. Name the date, place, and guests. We reply within one working day.
2. Tasting — about 3 weeks before. Taste the menu with us and agree what the table should serve.
3. Menu & floor plan — 2 weeks before. We draw the table plan and lock the menu for your room.
4. Final numbers — 5 days before. Confirm the guest count so the kitchen and crew match your booking.
5. Night of service — crew in 3 hours before. The crew arrives, sets the room, and serves until the evening ends.
