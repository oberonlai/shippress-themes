Enishi Catering is a block theme for a Harbourside kitchen that caters offices and family celebrations within 40 km. Mireille Duval founded the company in 2014, and the kitchen is at 14 Wharf Lane. The copy follows a booking from a Thursday or Friday tasting through to the night of service. It ships as a block theme with EB Garamond and Karla set in theme.json.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes).
