# Enishi Catering

A WordPress block theme for a catering company serving offices and family celebrations in Harbourside (founded 2014). The company is fictional; people, places and prices are in [`brand.md`](brand.md). Type: business. Sample content is English; zh-TW, ja, fr and de follow in stage 2.

## Concept: a table set before the guests arrive

- **Palette.** Ground `#F5F3EE`, white `#FFFFFF`, ink `#1F1B1B`, body `#333333`, accent `#7A5E5E` (the one muted accent, small areas), chip `#ECE9E3`, hairline `#DDDDDD`.
- **Type.** EB Garamond for headings, Karla for reading (OFL-1.1, loaded through theme.json).
- **Signature.** Full-bleed film-grain photo hero with a centred title, outlined translucent pill and an info bar on the photo; vertical-caps course labels beside 2.31:1 photographs; hairline STEP boxes in a zig-zag row joined by diagonal lines.
- **Motion.** sp-scroll: slow parallax on the hero and night band, reveal-down on titles, staggered STEP boxes and portraits. Reduced motion and the editor always show the final state.

## Status

Stage 2: home and ten inner pages (The Engagement, Office, Celebrations, Banquets, Menus & Prices, About, Team, Stories, FAQ, Contact), a Journal page with one article, and the vertical-caps rows, STEP timelines and price lists carried through them. zh-TW, ja, fr and de are added in a later step.
