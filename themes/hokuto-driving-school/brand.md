# Hokuto Driving School — brand brief

Design plan row: b6-3 (driving school, education). English only. Quality gate 2.
All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hokuto Driving School ("Hokuto" is the family name of the founders).
- **Location:** Fenwick Hill, a small fictional market town of stone terraces, one ring road and a long climb up to Harrow Moor. The school runs from the old tram depot on Mill Lane: a brick shed with a booking office on the ground floor, a theory room upstairs and a fenced practice yard behind it with painted bays.
- **Founded:** 1986, by Ellen and Tomas Hokuto. Tomas trained bus drivers for the county; Ellen ran the booking desk and taught evening theory. They started with one car and a borrowed corner of the depot yard.
- **Today:** run by their daughter **Mara Hokuto** (owner and instructor since 2009). Four dual-control cars (two manual, two automatic), four instructors, about 160 learners a year.
- **Audience:** seventeen-year-olds getting their first licence; adults who "left it late"; nervous drivers; people coming back after years away; newcomers converting a licence from abroad.
- **Tone:** calm, patient, plain-spoken and specific. Short honest sentences. Never "pass fast, guaranteed". The school would rather a learner take two more lessons than fail a test.

## People
- **Mara Hokuto** — owner, instructor. Grew up in the yard; does the first lesson with most new learners and plans every route card.
- **Daniel Okafor** — head instructor since 2007. Known for nervous learners; quiet voice, long pauses, never touches the dual brake unless he has to.
- **Priya Raman** — automatic cars, refresher courses and licence conversions. Former paramedic, very good at motorway confidence.
- **Sam Whitlock** — evenings, night driving and the motorway module. Youngest instructor, passed his own test at Hokuto in 2012.
- **Joan Pryce** — booking desk since 1998. Knows every learner's name and every test-centre slot.

## A day at the school
- 07:30 Joan opens the depot, puts the kettle on, checks the diary. Instructors check tyres, lights and mirrors on all four cars.
- 08:00 First lessons, timed to leave before the school run.
- 10:00–14:30 Quiet-road lessons on the ring road and the moor road; the best time for first-timers.
- 15:00–16:00 No new learners on the road — school traffic. Instructors write route cards and progress notes.
- 16:30–21:00 Evening lessons; Thursday 19:00 free theory evening upstairs (tea, hazard-perception clips, questions).
- Saturday mornings: yard sessions for complete beginners and mock tests.

## Signature
"Calm first. Then the road." Learning is told as **the route** — six photographed stages from the yard to test day — rather than as a sales pitch. One full-bleed photograph of an empty early-morning road sets the tone on the first screen.

The route (used on home and on The Route page):
1. **The yard** — controls, moving off, stopping, inside the fence. No traffic.
2. **Quiet roads** — the ring road at 10am, mirrors, signals, speed.
3. **Town** — junctions, crossings, parking on Market Street.
4. **Roundabouts and dual carriageways** — planning ahead, lane choice.
5. **Night, rain and the motorway** — with Sam or Priya.
6. **Test day** — a warm-up drive on the test routes, then the test.

## Pages and contents
1. **Home** — full-bleed road photo with headline "Calm first. *Then the road.*" and a short paragraph; a welcome letter from Mara (since 1986, the depot, who we teach); the route in six photographed stages; courses and fees summary (four courses, plain prices); the instructors (four photos, one line each); a nervous-drivers note; the yard and a day at the school (photo pair + narrative); latest journal (3); visit/booking footer.
2. **About** — the family story 1986–today, the depot, the four cars, what we promise (honest test advice, same instructor, no pressure).
3. **Courses & fees** — Standard manual (approx. 40 hours), Automatic, Two-week intensive, Refresher, Licence conversion, Motorway module; what's included; pay-as-you-go vs blocks; cancellation policy.
4. **The Route** — the six stages in detail, each with a photo, what you'll do, typical hours, how you'll know you're ready.
5. **Instructors** — the four instructors and Joan, each with a paragraph.
6. **Nervous drivers** — how the first lessons work, the yard, small steps, stories.
7. **Test day** — the morning routine, the warm-up drive, what examiners look for, what happens if it goes wrong.
8. **Journal** — posts page.
9. **Newsletter** — the monthly "Route card" letter: test-centre news, new evening slots.
10. **Contact** — depot address, booking desk hours, phone, email, first-lesson enquiry form, getting here.
Posts (6): Why every learner starts in the yard · Three mirror habits that stick · What examiners look for at a roundabout · Driving in the first frost · Coming back to driving after ten years · A morning on the Harrow Moor road. Categories: Learning, Road notes, School news.

## Photography
One STYLE for every image: quiet editorial photography, soft overcast daylight, muted stone and grey-green tones, shallow depth of field, subtle 35mm film grain. Neutral international scenes, small modern hatchbacks, no readable signs or number plates, no text. People shown as hands, backs or calm three-quarter portraits. No cut-outs.

## User aesthetic
Elegant, restrained, a real website — NO poster look, NO saturated colour fields, NO cut-out objects, NO side rail, NO giant centred slogan. Generous whitespace, photographs and narrative paragraphs do the work; one top menu.
