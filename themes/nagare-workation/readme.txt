Nagare Workation Hotel is a calm, horizontal-strip WordPress theme designed for weekly and monthly lakeside workations. Built with documentary photography, split navigation, and dedicated pricing boards, it balances focused remote work with natural silence. Crafted for hotels and retreats offering long-stay desk space, fast connectivity, and quiet hospitality.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The home page opens on three unequal full-height panes: a documentary photograph of a guest on a video call at a lake window that wipes to the next moment of the week, a pale text pane with the title, a short lede, an outline pill and a row of four figures, and a narrow lake sliver at the right edge. It continues with a sideways strip of the week in photographs, a label-cell price board with a very light 96 px numeral and a plus joiner, a seam portrait that crosses a full-width lake band with four words split to both sides, the coworking floor with dark icon tiles beside three photographs flush to the right edge, one day described as a routine, three guest stories, the one way to book and the journal. See README.md for the design notes.

== Installation ==

1. Download nagare-workation.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/nagare-workation.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Nagare Workation Hotel.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, journal entries with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The scroll effects (assets/sp-scroll.css and sp-scroll.js) respect prefers-reduced-motion and show the final state without JavaScript. The newsletter and contact slots use Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when active; without a plugin they fall back to an email link.

The hotel, its people, address, telephone number, email and prices are fictional.
