# Reference research (draft; "Our implementation" column filled in at the build step)

Reference: https://www.sa-nu.com/weekday (SANU 2nd Home Weekday, a subscription for weekday stays in nature cabins; the same audience as weekly/monthly workation stays). Category on the gallery: hotels / lodging. Imagery: **photo** (documentary).
No secondary reference (the one site covers hero, price board, workspace and CTA).

## Structure (top to bottom)
firstview (two-pane photo, 100vh) / "what is" statement on pale ground / "think" band (mountain photo, portrait straddling the seam, words spread apart) / area (map + numerals) / nature (series cards) / architecture / CTA band (aerial photo) / experience: workspace, dining, wellness (full-bleed photo + title panel, icon grid) / support (4 small perks) / price (board, how to use, FAQ) / CTA again / voice / comparison table. Pricing-led: the price board is a full section with the giant numeral.

## Signature candidates
1. **Two-pane first screen**: unequal wide photo pane + narrow pane, slides revealed by an inset clip-path wipe (2.4 s), tagline set vertically on the narrow pane, caption bottom-left, outline pill.
2. **Label-cell price board**: beige label cells + white value cells with 20 px gaps, "+" joiner between rows, a giant light numeral with a tiny unit on its baseline, outline pill toggle.
3. **Seam portrait**: tall portrait photo crossing the border between a landscape photo band and the pale ground, with a four-word line split apart on both sides.
4. Icon-tile facility grid: 70 px dark squares + title + two lines (3 x 2).
5. Photo strip flush to the right edge: three abutting photos next to a text column.
