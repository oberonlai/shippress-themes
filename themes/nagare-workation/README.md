# Nagare Workation Hotel

A block theme for lakeside hotels that rent rooms by the week and the month to people who bring their work.

- Home: three-pane desk-window hero (60 / 30 / 10) with a wipe between three photographs, a sideways strip of the week, a label-cell price board, a seam portrait band, the coworking floor, one day as a routine, guest stories, the one way to book, journal.
- Fonts: Red Hat Display and Nunito Sans (bundled, OFL).
- Scroll effects: sp-reveal (wipe, up + stagger, zoom, left/right) and sp-parallax on the lake sliver and the seam band.
- Forms: `shippress_form_slot` newsletter in the footer and on the Newsletter page; works without plugins (mailto fallback with a visible notice).
- Design notes: `brand.md`, `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md`.
