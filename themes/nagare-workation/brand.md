## Story
Nagare Workation Hotel opened in 2018 inside a restored 1960s boat-builders' lodge on the quiet shores of Lake Ostra. Founder Ilse Varga recognized that remote knowledge workers needed more than temporary holiday lodgings or noisy city cafes. She envisioned a dedicated residential retreat built deliberately around the rhythm of deep work, offering quiet rooms, lake light, and reliable utilities for people staying weeks or months at a time.

The lodge was carefully retrofitted with modern infrastructure while preserving its original timber warmth and generous lake-facing windows. Today, the property houses twenty-four private bedrooms and a light-filled coworking floor featuring thirty-eight dedicated desks, two private acoustic call booths, an eight-person meeting room, and redundant 1 Gbps wired fibre. Everything from ergonomic task chairs to secondary monitors was chosen to ensure professional work proceeds without friction.

Life at Nagare balances sustained professional focus with the calm presence of nature. Stays are structured around minimum bookings of seven nights, encouraging guests to establish an unhurried daily routine of morning lake swims, steady working sessions, and communal evening dinners at the kitchen table. By keeping operations intentionally grounded at 12 Shore Road, the hotel offers a lasting sanctuary where guests can bring serious work and let the water handle the rest.
