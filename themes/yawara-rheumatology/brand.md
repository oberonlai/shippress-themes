# Yawara Rheumatology - brand brief

A small rheumatology clinic in a fictional harbour town. Sample content is in English (stage 1) and is localised later for Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Yawara Rheumatology. A clinic that treats swollen joints before they set the shape of a day.
- **Location:** 12 Linden Row, Harrow Quay (fictional), second floor, lift access. Bus 14, stop "Linden Row", three minutes on foot.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Dr. Miriam Achterberg after eleven years in a hospital rheumatology unit.
- **Audience:** adults who wake with swollen, stiff joints, their families, and family doctors who refer them.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, precise, and reassuring, communicating clinical clarity without medical jargon, hype, or false promises. Every statement focuses on observable daily symptoms, transparent procedures, and dependable collaborative care.
- **Contact:** phone +1 555 0142 300. Hours: Mon–Fri 8:30–17:30, Sat 8:30–12:30, closed Sun & public holidays. Flare-day slots: weekdays 8:30–9:30 and Saturday 8:30–9:00, for registered patients, booked by phone the same morning.

## Story
Yawara Rheumatology was founded in 2014 by Dr. Miriam Achterberg at 12 Linden Row in Harrow Quay. Having spent eleven years working in a hospital rheumatology unit, Dr. Achterberg recognized that patients living with inflammatory joint conditions needed care designed specifically around how symptoms appear in daily life. She established the clinic on the second floor of a quiet harbour-town building with lift access, creating a clinical environment organized entirely around morning symptoms, early diagnosis, and unhurried consultations.

From the beginning, the clinic's clinical method has centred on thorough objectivity and shared understanding. Every initial assessment includes a comprehensive 28-joint examination, same-morning musculoskeletal ultrasound, and targeted blood tests. Rather than leaving treatment open-ended, the clinic structured a rigorous four-step pathway: patients track a seven-day morning-stiffness diary, complete a sixty-minute first consultation, and return within ten days for an agreed one-page written treatment plan signed by both doctor and patient.

Over the years, the practice has grown to bring together a dedicated clinical team spanning rheumatologists, a rheumatology nurse specialist, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and pharmacy liaison. Care continues systematically through scheduled reviews at four, twelve, and twenty-four weeks, supported by a direct named-nurse phone line and same-morning flare-day slots. The clinic remains committed to its core founding principle: treating joint inflammation early and methodically so patients can preserve the steady shape of their day.

## Process (four visits, one page)
1. Call & 7-day morning-stiffness diary
2. First visit, 60 min (history, 28-joint exam, ultrasound, bloods same morning)
3. Plan meeting within 10 days (one-page written plan agreed and signed by both)
4. Reviews at 4, 12 and 24 weeks, then every 3 months; one named nurse line
