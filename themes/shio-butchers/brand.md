# Shio Butchers - brand brief

Independent butcher shop at 14 Market Row, Saltgate. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shio Butchers. "Shio" is salt: the block is scrubbed with coarse salt every evening.
- **Location:** 14 Market Row, Saltgate (fictional). Phone +00 0000 2009, email orders@shio-butchers.example. Prices in euro, including VAT, by actual weight.
- **Founded:** 2009 by Mateo Varga (owner and head butcher) and Hanna Sørlie (co-owner, counter and orders).
- **Hours:** Tue–Fri 07:30–18:30, Sat 07:00–15:00, closed Sun–Mon. Orders close 18:00 for pickup from 07:30 the next opening day (Friday 18:00 to Saturday 07:00); parcels are held until close of that day. Monday: beef sides arrive and are broken for the week.
- **Audience:** neighbours who cook at home and want to know where a cut came from, and who order the day before and collect in the morning.
- **Tone:** The tone is calm, unhurried, and grounded in the daily physical routines of a working butchery counter. Every description relies on precise craft facts, exact weights, and specific farm origins rather than superlatives or marketing claims.

## History
Shio Butchers opened in 2009 at 14 Market Row in Saltgate as an independent butcher shop dedicated to whole-carcass butchery. Founded by Mateo Varga and Hanna Sørlie, the shop was established on a simple principle: to source animals directly from regional family farms and break every side down by hand across the central end-grain timber block. From the first morning, every cut on the counter was portioned to order and weighed honestly for the local neighborhood.

In 2014, the shop expanded its working space to install a dedicated cold room and hanging rail, allowing beef sides to undergo twenty-one days of undisturbed dry-ageing on the hook. Five years later, in 2019, a dedicated sausage room was built to prepare daily coarse pork sausages seasoned with fresh sage and to grind fresh mince from morning chuck and brisket trim. In 2025, the shop welcomed Lucía Ferraz as its first apprentice butcher, continuing the transmission of traditional knife work and seam butchery.

Today, our supply rail operates on a strict weekly rhythm tied to three family farms situated within forty-one kilometers of Saltgate. Sides of grass-fed beef arrive from Harrow Lane Farm every Monday, woodland pork halves come from Millbrook Pastures on Wednesdays, and pasture lamb arrives from Oaken Ridge on Tuesdays between March and November. Each evening after orders close at 18:00, the timber block is scrubbed down with coarse dry salt, ready for dawn delivery.

## Order process (4)
- 01 Order by 18:00: Submit your cuts and preferred weights using the pickup form, telephone (+00 0000 2009), or email before 18:00.
- 02 Confirmation by 19:00: Receive an order confirmation by 19:00 with estimated weights and total calculation.
- 03 Cut and wrapped at 07:00: Cuts are portioned fresh at the block at 07:00, weighed to ten grams, and wrapped in waxed butcher paper with cotton string.
- 04 Pick up from 07:30: Collect your parcel from the chilled shelf from 07:30 onwards (07:00 Saturday) and pay at the counter by card or cash.
