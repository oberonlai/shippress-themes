# Azukari Stays - brand brief

A holiday-let management company that runs guesthouses and holiday homes for owners who live elsewhere. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Azukari Stays. "Azukari" is the idea of something held in trust for someone else; the company is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** office at 14 Corn Exchange Yard, Lenning, a fictional market town in the fictional Ostervale hills. Prices in euros in every language.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Maren Holt (ran housekeeping for a country hotel for 12 years) and Idris Calloway (architectural photographer). They began by looking after two stone farmhouses for neighbours who had moved abroad.
- **Today:** 41 homes in 6 regions: Ostervale hills (12), Lenning town (8), Marsh coast (7), Brennan valley (6), Upper Fells (5), Saltmoor lakes (3). At most 8 new homes per season, hence an owner waiting list.
- **Audience:** owners of holiday homes, farmhouses and guesthouses who live elsewhere (abroad, another city, inherited and shared between siblings) and want one person who answers, a house that is the same each time they return, and numbers they can read.
- **Tone:** calm, candid and operationally specific, speaking as experienced caretakers rather than hospitality marketers. Plain numbers, no promises, no forecasts, no awards, no real media, no real booking platforms ("the big booking sites" only).
- **Line:** "The house stays looked after." The one repeated call to action: "Book an owner consultation" (short form in the header: "Book a consultation").

## The five stages (the header stepper and the home chapters, always in this order)
Listing, Turnover, Guests, Fees, Payout.
- Listing: one visit, photographs, a measured floor plan, house manual, key safe fitted; listing live in five languages within 14 days of signing.
- Turnover: every changeover between 11:00 and 16:00: clean, linen, a 40-point check, restock, photos of the finished rooms sent to the owner the same day.
- Guests: messages answered within 15 minutes, 08:00-22:00 daily; self check-in from 16:00, check-out by 10:00; minimum stay 2 nights.
- Fees: 18% of nightly revenue, nothing on the cleaning fee the guest pays.
- Payout: statement on the 2nd, money in the owner's account on the 5th of each month for the previous month's stays.

## A day at the house (the daily routine)
A changeover day: guests leave by 10:00; Lucía's team arrives between 11:00 and 16:00, strips and washes the linen in our own laundry, runs the 40-point check, restocks, and photographs the finished rooms for the owner. Tomasz checks the key safe and anything the check found. Amara's desk answers guest messages within 15 minutes between 08:00 and 22:00, and self check-in opens at 16:00.
