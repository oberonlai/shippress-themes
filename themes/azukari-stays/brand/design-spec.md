## 1. Signature techniques (three; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

### T1. Five-stage stepper as the header menu (tokitoki day-flow stepper, `scroll-tt-flow-checkin.png`, `scroll-tt-flow-rest.png`, RT(f))
- **Reference:** centred in the header, five 11px labels sit over five **10px dots joined by a 1px #CCCCCC line**, dots **105px apart**, at y 38–62. Dots and line segments before the active stage turn the accent (#EB5A3E), and the active label turns accent too. It tracks the pinned horizontal day (Check-in, Rest, Dinner, Sleep, Breakfast).
- **Ours:** the landing page's single anchor menu **is** the stepper: **Listing · Turnover · Guests · Fees · Payout**, linking `#listing #turnover #guests #fees #payout` (on inner pages `/#listing` …). Header centre x 460–980 at 1440 (dots 105px apart, first dot centre x 510, last x 930), labels Inter Tight 500 **0.6875rem (11px)** ls .14em uppercase 12px above the dots, dots 10px, track 1px. Colours: on the pale ground inactive dot/track `#CCCCCC`, labels `#14181B` at 60%; active and passed: dot fill and track `#9F563A`, label `#9F563A`. Over the hero (dark panel and photo) the header is white: inactive dots/track `rgba(255,255,255,.45)`, labels `#FFFFFF`, active `#C98C72`.
- **Build:** one `core/navigation` (the only top menu) with five links; class `az-stepper`; dots and track are CSS `::before`/`::after` with **no `content` text** (`content: ""` only). Active state: (a) static, always works: the link with `aria-current` or class `is-stage` (set per page, see §4) and all links before it are lit; on home the static state is **Listing** lit. (b) Enhancement on home, CSS only: each chapter section has `view-timeline-name: --st-listing` … `--st-payout`, `body{timeline-scope: --st-listing, …}`, and each link runs a step animation on its chapter's timeline that lights its dot and the track segment before it (`animation-range: entry 40% exit 60%`, `animation-fill-mode: both`). Wrap in `@supports (timeline-scope: --a)` and in `html:not(.sp-no-motion)`; no JS, no scroll listener. Track fill transition .3s cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1) (RT(g)).
- **Must not be diluted:** five dots with labels, centred in the top header, hairline track, accent only on passed/active. Never moved to the page foot, never turned into a numbered step row in the content, never fewer than five.

### T2. Line floor plan with underlined room tabs and one accent dot (tokitoki "about" plan, `scroll-tt-plan-tabs.png`, RT(f))
- **Reference:** a **1px black line plan** of the house sits right (x 790–1250), a **tab row** of room names with a **1px underline under the active tab** sits left above a **400px text column**, and **one 14px accent dot** on/under the plan marks the room being read.

One **14px `#9F563A` dot** is placed on the active room (absolutely positioned inside the plan box via `--x/--y` percentages per room; it is decoration, not text).
- **Interaction (no JS):** tabs are links `#room-entry` … `#room-yard`; panels are `core/group` with those ids. CSS shows the `:target` panel and its dot; when no panel is targeted the first panel (Entry) is shown: `.az-plan:not(:has(.az-room:target)) .az-room:first-of-type{display:block}`; hover/focus on a tab also previews its panel via `:has()`. ## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://tokitoki-patina.jp/)
- **What it draws:** a nearly black room interior (`#1C262E`-toned) with a large **round opening** (≈860px circle) in the middle that shows a sunlit garden with a tree; the brand mark at the left (x 108–282), a thin **vertical tagline** right of the opening (x 1180–1230), a transparent header with two icons, a **1px white outline "Book" button** (190 × 55 at x 1124) and a two-line menu icon. A thin vertical scroll-cue line at the lower right.
- **Story:** step out of a hurried day into a 190-year-old house; from the dark room you look out into the light; time slows down.
- **Signature motion:** after the splash, the opening photo fades in (opacity 2s) and settles (transform 2.4s) after a 1.4s delay on **cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)**; the scroll cue runs 2.4s on the same curve, infinite. Further down, the **five-dot day stepper** in the header tracks a pinned horizontal day.
- loof-inn.com (secondary) contributes: a **thin light headline (72px, weight 100) lying across the photo at the left** and a **pale grey info bar (108px, #E1E1E1) directly under the photo** with one dark button.

### (2) Our translation (same composition idea, same motion idea, same image type: documentary photograph)
**From a dark hall into a lit room, between guests.** One documentary photograph of a renovated farmhouse bedroom at late morning: **Lucía (housekeeper) shakes out a white duvet over a made-up double bed by the window, while Idris (manager) stands at the foot of the bed photographing the room on a phone held landscape**, green garden light through two small deep-set windows, lime-washed walls, an oak floor, a folded stack of towels on a chair. The left of the screen is the dark ink-blue "hall" panel `#1C262E`; its right edge is the **diagonal** cut through which you see the lit room, the same "dark interior opening onto light" as tokitoki's round window, turned into the moment this company sells: **the house is being looked after while the owner is away**. The thin light headline lies across the diagonal as loof's does: **"The house stays looked after."** The pale info bar under the photo states the facts an owner wants first. Motion: tokitoki's slow settle (scale 1.04 → 1, 2.4s, cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)) on load and the photo drifting slower than the page (`sp-parallax`); the stepper in the header, set at "Listing", lights stage by stage as the chapters scroll past.
