# Aozora Primary School - design references (S5-Style, 2026-10-20)

Researched on S5-Style (s5-style.com, a Japanese web design gallery), school / nursery / education entries. Only structure and techniques were borrowed: no copy, photographs, logos or whole-page layouts. Colours and type follow design-plan row b6-9 (fujinezu #6E75A4 as the one small accent, Alegreya / Alegreya Sans); the techniques themselves keep the shape, composition and contrast of the originals.

## Signature techniques used

1. **Notched rounded hero photo on a pale band, headline as stacked caption-line boxes, dashed note card**
   - Source: Yamanomi Nursery (https://yamanomi-hoiku.com/).
   - There: the hero photograph is a large rounded rectangle inset from the page, a white block bites a rounded notch out of one corner, a flat band runs behind the lower half of the photo, the headline sits on the photo as three separate cream boxes (one per line, staggered, small offset shadow) and a dashed-border card overlaps the photo's foot.
   - Here: Home, first block (front-cover). The morning classroom photograph has 2.5 rem corners and a notched top-right corner with a concave fillet; a chalk-stone band runs behind its lower half and the gateways; the headline is three paper caption boxes with an offset pale-mist shadow, staggered and overlapping the photograph's right edge; a dashed "Next open morning" card overlaps its lower-left corner. The three equal gateway cards (visit / apply / families) sit on the band.

2. **Slanted wavy section edge with an offset colour strip**
   - Source: Ushizu Kodomo no Mori (https://seigaku.jp/ushizu/).
   - There: a section meets the next along a slanted, gently wavy edge, and a thin strip of a second colour peeks out under it, so the seam reads as two stacked paper layers.
   - Here: Home, "What children learn" (front-learning). The chalk band's top edge falls left to right as a soft wave (clip-path), with a mist strip offset about 1.6 rem above it following the same wave.

3. **Overlapping photo cluster joined to a one-side-round pill bar with a round arrow and a ring-text badge**
   - Source: Ushizu Kodomo no Mori (https://seigaku.jp/ushizu/).
   - There: three square-cornered, white-bordered photographs overlap loosely; the largest butts into a long white bar that is round only on its right end, holding a round arrow button and a two-line link; a round badge with text running around its ring overlaps the bar's end.
   - Here: Home, "Visit us" (front-close). The woods walk, the playground and the school building overlap with 4 px paper borders; the building photograph joins a paper pill bar (round on the right) with a round wisteria arrow, "Tuesdays at 09:30" and the tour link; a ring badge with "AOZORA PRIMARY SCHOOL - SINCE 1979 - WESTBURY VALE" set on a circle path overlaps its end.

Mixed with our own brand content: a welcome from the head, a horizontal strip of school-day photographs, teachers as ruled index rows, and admissions steps with fees and open days as plain lists. Side-by-side proof: cc-runs reference-compare.png (attached to the pull request).
