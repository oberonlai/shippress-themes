# Aozora Primary School - brand brief

Small private primary school sample site. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market.
All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Aozora Primary School ("aozora" means an open blue sky).
- **Place:** 12 Linden Lane, Westbury Vale. A timber-and-brick school building (since 2008) with a library, a music room, a dining room with long tables, a playground with a wooden climbing frame and raised garden beds.
- **History:** founded in 1979 by two teachers, Ruth Ellison and Peter Aldane, in a former linen mill office. The first raised beds were dug in 1988. The school moved to the present building in 2008. Eleanor Marsh became head teacher in 2016.
- **Today:** independent, co-educational, ages 5 to 11, Years 1 to 6, one class per year of at most 20 pupils, 112 pupils in total, 14 teachers and staff.
- **Audience (who it serves):** parents of children aged 4 to 10 choosing a primary school; local families who want a small school where every child is known, many visiting for the first time.
- **Tone:** calm, warm, plain-spoken. Short honest sentences. No marketing superlatives, no claims about exam results, rankings, accreditation or statistics beyond the facts above.

## People
- **Eleanor Marsh** - head teacher since 2016; taught Year 2 for twelve years before. Reads with a different child every week.
- **Tom Avery** - Year 3 class teacher, outdoor learning lead; looks after the garden.
- **Mei Lin Hart** - teaches Years 5 and 6, runs the library.
- **Clara Brandt** - music, choir and piano.
- **Samuel Reyes** - school office and admissions.

## Year groups and class size
Years 1 to 6 (ages 5 to 11). One class per year, never more than 20 children. Small groups for reading and mathematics. Written reports every term; two parent meetings a year.

## A school day
- 08:15 gates open; coats on named hooks, quiet reading.
- 08:40 morning circle, then reading, writing and mathematics.
- 12:10 lunch together in the dining room at long tables.
- Afternoon: garden, field, art, science, music; outdoors in nearly every weather.
- 15:20 home time with a story. Optional after-school club until 17:30.
- School office open Mon-Fri 08:00-16:30 in term time.

## Curriculum
Reading and writing; mathematics with hands-on materials; science and the school garden; art; music (every child learns the recorder from Year 2 and sings in the choir); languages (French from Year 3); physical education; outdoor afternoons.

## Signature
"A small school under an open sky." Learning is shown through quiet photographs of hands and rooms rather than slogans.
