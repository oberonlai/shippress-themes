# Hozen Facilities

A block theme (type business) for a facilities management company serving office campuses and civic buildings. Home page (English) with the menu linked to inner pages that hold the home sections until the inner-page stage; the other languages follow.

## Signature
- A floating white header card with a dark and a blue capsule pill; heading "Buildings that quietly work." beside a plant-room photograph that comes into focus on load, over a full-bleed look-up band with a parallax photograph and a vertical Scroll label.
- A brand statement with a photograph that comes into focus and two blurred ghost photographs at the margins.
- The four featured sites as a staggered two-column photo pair (right column dropped 100 px) with caption rows, arrow chips and 100 px ghost numerals; section lockups with a vertical hairline.

## Patterns
- `front-hero`, `front-band`, `front-statement`, `front-sites`, `front-network`, `front-roof`, `front-callout`, `front-services`, `front-prices`, `front-notes`, `front-contact`, `header-main`, `footer-main`

## Fonts
Proza Libre and Figtree (SIL OFL 1.1, licences in assets/fonts).

## Images
AI-generated for this theme, no text or logos (assets/images/, listed in readme.txt).

## Scroll effects
sp-scroll (assets/sp-scroll.css and .js): photo focus-in, fade-up rows and parallax; all stop under reduced motion and stay visible without JavaScript.
