# Nodo Clinic — brand brief

Design plan row: b22-2 (adult ear, nose and throat clinic, type health). The home page and eleven inner pages are in English; the other languages follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional. Medical copy never promises a cure or an outcome.

## Concept
- **Name:** Nodo Clinic, an adult ear, nose and throat practice seen by appointment only.
- **Location:** 18 Veldstraat, 9000 Ghent, Belgium. Appointment line +32 9 555 01 40, hello@nodoclinic.example. A pale brick building with white posts and a small tree at the door.
- **Founded:** 2011 by Dr. Ines Vandermeer; eight people today.
- **Services:** ears and hearing, nose and sinuses, throat and voice, dizziness and balance; hearing tests, nasal endoscopy added to a visit, balance and voice assessments, earwax removal.
- **Audience:** adults who have carried a slow complaint for a while and want one room, one person and a plan said out loud.
- **Tone:** Calm, plain and adult, with the detail of a real appointment book. State the time, the fee and the limit, and leave the outcome unstated.

## Story
Nodo Clinic opened in 2011 at 18 Veldstraat in Ghent, and it has stayed an adult ear, nose and throat practice, seen only by appointment. Dr. Ines Vandermeer began the book with ears and hearing. Dr. Samir Haddad, Dr. Lucia Ferreira and Dr. Tomasz Wrona joined it for nose and sinuses, throat and voice, and dizziness and balance. Amara Nwosu, Helen Park, Joris Claes and Noor El Amrani carry the hearing tests, the voice work, the nursing and the appointment line. One list serves all of them, and the next part of a visit stays in these rooms.

There is no walk-in hour. The line, +32 9 555 01 40, and hello@nodoclinic.example are answered while the clinic is open. A first visit is 45 minutes, in three parts of 15: listen, look and plan. One patient is in the room. That is why the hour is not cut to fit another person in behind them. The first consultation is €95. A follow-up is 20 minutes and €55. A hearing test, a nasal endoscopy added to a visit, a balance assessment, a voice assessment and earwax removal each have their own time and fee. The time on each line is the length of the appointment. It does not say what the examination will find.

The day has a fixed shape. Rooms are set at 8:00. The first patient is at 8:30. Hours run Monday to Thursday from 8:30 to 17:30, and Friday from 8:30 to 14:00. The practice closes from 12:30 to 13:15, and it is closed on weekends. At the end of the visit, the patient is walked to the door. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before. Reimbursement depends on the insurer. The clinic states the room, the length of the visit, and a plan said out loud before anyone stands up. What follows from that plan differs from person to person.

- Joris Claes, Lead nurse: He leads the nursing of the rooms from the 8:00 setup and walks with patients to the door. He works Monday to Friday.
- Noor El Amrani, Practice coordinator: She keeps the appointment book and answers +32 9 555 01 40 during opening hours. She is at the desk Monday to Friday.
