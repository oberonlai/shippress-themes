# Ueki Gardens — brand

## History
Elena Marsh founded Ueki Gardens in 1998 on Alder Lane in Fernhill, beginning with a modest glasshouse and a clear working principle: to cultivate plants that an ordinary household can keep alive. The name came from the Japanese word ueki, describing a plant grown specifically for a pot or garden. Elena admired the practical discipline behind that definition, and though nothing else across our nursery is Japanese, that singular focus on domestic resilience has anchored our work on Alder Lane for twenty-eight years.

Over nearly three decades, the grounds have grown into six working glasshouse bays and forty-two sheltered benches. We propagate and test stock through seasonal shifts, ensuring that every shrub, perennial, herb, and indoor specimen spends adequate time adjusting to real conditions before it leaves our care. Rather than stocking tender novelties that wither after purchase, head grower Tomás Reyes and our nursery team look after varieties proven to establish reliable root systems in regional gardens and homes.

Today, Ueki Gardens remains a working, independent garden centre rooted at 14 Alder Lane. We maintain quiet benches open daily to our community, offering steady counter repotting, weekend plant clinics, and unhurried guidance for balconies, borders, and bright kitchen windowsills. Everything we tend here is raised to endure, grow quietly, and settle into daily domestic routines without complicated demands or fragile expectations.

## How a plant is chosen
- Light: Assessing the aspect, window exposure, and direct hours of sun where the plant will stand.
- Space: Considering mature container height, canopy spread, and clearance along domestic walkways.
- Time: Gauging realistic watering routines, seasonal attention, and personal care capacity.
- Company: Checking whether pets, small children, or neighbouring plants share the living space.
- Follow-up: A thirty-day check-in call following planting visits to review how the specimen has settled.
