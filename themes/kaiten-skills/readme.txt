Kaiten Skills is an online career-skills school for business writing, presenting and spreadsheets, founded in 2017 and taught from a studio in Utrecht. Courses are short video lessons you can buy in euros, with lesson 1 free to watch, 24 months of access and a certificate of completion. This block theme presents the courses, tutors, stories and checkout in a calm stone-and-ink layout.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download kaiten-skills.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kaiten-skills.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kaiten Skills.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The school, its tutors, courses, address and prices are fictional.
