# Kaiten Skills - brand brief

Online career-skills school for business writing, presenting and spreadsheets, with short video courses bought through WooCommerce checkout and watched on the site (Tutor LMS, stage 2). Sample content in English (stage 1) and, from stage 2, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kaiten Skills. Founded **2017** by Ines Carvalho and Jonah Ellery.
- **Location:** studio at **Kanaalstraat 21, 3531 Utrecht, Netherlands** (fictional unit number; no walk-in classes). EUR prices (€) in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** Kaiten Skills was founded in 2017 by Ines Carvalho and Jonah Ellery. Ines teaches writing. Jonah builds the platform and looks after learner support. They opened a studio at Kanaalstraat 21, 3531 Utrecht, in the Netherlands, and kept it as a place for recording lessons and answering learners. The school does not run walk-in classes. From the start, the idea was simple: career skills taught in short video lessons that a working adult can watch at their own table and use again the same week.

The courses cover three kinds of everyday work. Clear Business Writing and Reports People Actually Read sit with Ines Carvalho and Sofia Marín. Presenting with Confidence is taught by Tomasz Wierzba. Spreadsheets from Zero is taught by Amara Osei, and Spreadsheet Models for Decisions by Rahul Menon. A Career Starter Bundle joins writing, presenting and Spreadsheets from Zero. Each lesson runs between 8 and 14 minutes. Lesson 1 of every course is free to watch. After purchase, access lasts 24 months. A refund is available within 14 days if fewer than 3 lessons were watched. Tutors reply to assignments within 5 working days.

Finishing a course brings a certificate of completion. It is a record that the lessons were finished, not an accredited qualification. Lena Hoffmann leads learner support from the studio. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 CET, and Saturday, 10:00–14:00 CET. The lessons themselves are available at any hour. Write to hello@kaiten-skills.example when you need a person. Prices are in euros and stay the same number in every language. The school stays small on purpose: the two founders, a tutor for each single course, and one learner support lead.
- **Audience:** working adults who want one practical skill (a clear email, a readable report, a talk, a spreadsheet) taught in short lessons they can fit around a job.
- **Tone:** Speak in plain sentences, as a small Utrecht studio talking to a working adult, and name the course, the price and the rule rather than a mood. Leave out press mentions, salary figures and any number that is not already fixed for the school.

## Process (5)
1. Choose a course. Watch lesson 1 free before you buy.
2. Buy and enrol. WooCommerce checkout gives instant access.
3. Learn at your table. Lessons are 8–14 minutes and available at any hour.
4. Practise with feedback. A tutor replies within 5 working days.
5. Finish with a certificate of completion, which is not an accredited qualification.

## Signature techniques (carried to inner pages)
1. **T1 hand-cut photo frame with a blob plate** (Yanokuchi, https://yanokuchi.ed.jp/): the hero photo is inset 36 px with a 36 px wobbly hand-cut edge; a 394x104 #8A4B3A blob plate carrying the wordmark hangs from the top-left and bites the frame corner.
2. **T2 leaf-shaped CTA** (Yanokuchi): a 132x120 clay-tint leaf with one sharp corner, book icon, dotted-underlined label and arrow, fixed at the photo's bottom-right corner (in flow under the frame on mobile).
3. **T3 wave-edged ink band with 112 px numerals** (Yanokuchi wavy bands + ECC-iCAP, https://www.ecc.kyoto-unicap.co.jp/): a full-bleed #2A2525 band with hand-drawn wave edges holds the horizontal five-step timeline and a slanted stone panel with the price table.
