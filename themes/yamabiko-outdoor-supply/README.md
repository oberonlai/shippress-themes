# Yamabiko Outdoor Supply

A WooCommerce block theme (type store) for an independent outdoor and camping gear shop, built as a spec catalogue. Works without WooCommerce. Home page (English) in this release; inner pages and other languages follow.

## Signature
- Inset photo band under a dark veil with three underlined shelf links.
- Fixed bottom-left dash index with a short accent dash on the active section.
- Dark capsule "field pick" chip, bottom right (thumbnail and arrow only on small screens).
- Low-left light Bitter headline over a full-bleed documentary photograph with a white four-cell info bar.

## Patterns
- `front-hero`, `front-catalogue`, `front-shelves`, `front-compare`, `front-tests`, `front-workshop`, `front-stories`, `front-visit`
- `chrome` (field-pick chip, loaded from the footer part)

## Fonts
Bitter and Karla (SIL OFL 1.1, licences in assets/fonts).

## Without WooCommerce
Catalogue links to /product/... lead to the Shop page; the header cart link (0) goes to /shop/. With WooCommerce active the mini cart replaces it.
