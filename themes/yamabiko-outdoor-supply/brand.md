# Yamabiko Outdoor Supply

Independent outdoor and camping gear store in the fictional town of Harrow Gap, founded 2011 by Ines Calder. Real shop at 14 Quarry Lane, a rental shelf and a repair bench; online it is a spec catalogue of field-tested tents, packs and layers with WooCommerce checkout.

## Voice
Plain, measured, first-person plural. Numbers before adjectives. No claims we cannot weigh.
