# Hanamizuki Dental

A dental clinic theme in jade green: a split first screen with a two-line headline and trust marks beside a booking card (7-day date strip, time-slot chips, treatment select) over stacked high-key clinic photographs, with a cut-out instrument tray breaking over its corner; then treatments with from-prices, dentists with cut-out portraits, the first visit, insurance and fees, and clinic hours. English sample content.

- Design plan row b6-1: split-screen, stacked-collage (two overlapping high-key clinic photographs stacked behind the booking card), top-bar nav (clinic name left, menu and Book a visit right), cutout-break-frame (a transparent cut-out instrument tray breaks over the booking card corner; cut-out dentist portraits rise out of jade blocks), high-key photography (85mm f/2.8, softbox and window light, near-white ground, graded toward jade), jade #38B48B, Zen Kaku Gothic New + Newsreader; first screen booking-panel, page structure booking-led.
- Front page blocks: hero-booking-card, treatments, dentists, first-visit, insurance-fees, clinic-info.
- Sample content language: en (English-only theme).
- Contrast: ink #10261F on jade #38B48B 6.1:1 (all text on jade fields; white on jade is 2.6:1 and never used for text); white on deep jade #1A6B51 6.4:1 (buttons); deep jade on white 6.4:1 (labels, links); quiet #4A5A54 on white 7.3:1.
