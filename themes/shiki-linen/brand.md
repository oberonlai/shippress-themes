# Shiki Linen - brand brief

Bed-linen shop and workroom in Galway, Ireland. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shiki Linen. "Shiki" is a Japanese word for laying out a cloth; the shop itself is plain, international and Irish.
- **Location:** a shop and workroom at 14 Ropewalk Lane, Galway, Ireland (fictional address). hello@shikilinen.example. EUR prices in every language, no conversions.
- **History:** founded in 2014 by Aoife Brennan, who set a counter in a small shop with a workroom behind it. The range was wide at first and was narrowed, cloth by cloth, until six remained.
- **Today:** an independent shop of seven people, six cloths in three weaves, pieces cut and made up to order, washed once at 40°C and posted folded and tied.
- **Audience:** people buying bedding slowly: hot sleepers, first-time linen buyers, small guest houses.
- **Tone:** plain, exact, unhurried. Names the cloth, the weight, the size and the price. No health claims, no cures, no better-sleep promises, no awards or press.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Wash-gradation strip: six contiguous macro photographs of one linen in six tones, no gap, edge to edge, caption under each (Hadawa's contiguous photo strip and its gradation concept).
2. Opposite-edge staircase with moss spec tags: bare photographs pinned 14 px from alternating viewport edges at 0.70, 1:1 and 1.5:1, a short text block in the white void (Hadawa's Concept staircase).
3. Running-stitch thread line: one 1.5 px moss dashed line through the white voids that ends in a needle-eye ring (Terada Knit's thread line).
First screen: three equal framed film-grain photographs under a centred header, statement below.
