# Sazanami Hotel — brand

## Name and place
Sazanami Hotel ("sazanami" means small ripples on water) is a fictional seven-room lakeside hotel at 14 Shore Lane, Lake Mirren. Phone +00 555 0140. Email stay@sazanami-hotel.example. Check-in 15:00–20:00; check-out 11:00; front desk 7:00–22:00; breakfast 7:30–10:00 (hamper from 6:30); quiet hours 22:00–7:00. Closed 6–31 January for repairs.

## Founded
Boathouse built 1934. Opened as a hotel in 2011. The 1934 boathouse was rebuilt in 2011 by co-owner Tomas Reyes into seven rooms in one row, every room facing the water.

## Audience
Travellers who want one quiet room on the water and the answers before they book: which rooms face the lake, how breakfast is served and how a stay is booked. Buyers of the theme: small hotels, inns and guest houses with a handful of rooms and a strong view.

## Tone
Write as the front desk would speak: calm, specific, and close to the hours and rooms of this house. Use named places and plain sentences a guest can act on. Short lines, plain facts, hours and distances; no superlatives, no guarantees, no awards.

## Story
Sazanami Hotel is at 14 Shore Lane, Lake Mirren. The house began as a timber boathouse in 1934, built on the shore and turned toward the water. In 2011 the boathouse was rebuilt and opened as a hotel. Seven rooms stand in one row, and every room faces the lake. A timber corridor runs the length of that row. A jetty leaves the shore and runs out over the water, which is the view the rooms were given.

Ilse Varga is owner and host. Tomas Reyes, co-owner, is the carpenter who rebuilt the rooms. Amara Okafor cooks breakfast. Jun Park keeps the front desk. Léa Moreau looks after the rooms. Henrik Dahl, a neighbour, keeps the boats at Mirren Boat House. Priya Nair, a neighbour, bakes at Harbour Lane Bakery. The house is seven rooms and no more: two Shore Rooms at 24 m², two Window-Seat Rooms at 28 m², two Corner Lake Rooms at 34 m², and one Jetty Suite at 46 m².

The day keeps the same hours. Lake light is at 05:30. An early breakfast hamper is ready from 6:30. Breakfast is served at the window from 7:30 to 10:00. Check-out is at 11:00. Check-in runs from 15:00 to 20:00. The front desk is open from 7:00 to 22:00, and quiet hours are from 22:00 to 7:00. The house closes for repairs from 6 to 31 January. Outside those dates the stay is the same thing each time: a room on the water, a window seat, and a morning that begins before breakfast.

## Process (how a stay is booked)
1. Choose dates and a room.
2. Send the request by form or by email to stay@sazanami-hotel.example.
3. We reply within 24 hours with a confirmation and a 30% deposit link.
4. Arrive from 15:00; the rest is settled at departure. Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival; after that the deposit is kept.

## Design notes
- Reference: kei-kusatsu.com (a small inn): pale ground stepping to a dark ground, centred glyph-and-word chapter openers, photo pairs flush to the viewport edge beside a narrow tracked verse, two-column hairline neighbour rows, a dot before the current menu word. Nothing from it is copied; see brand/reference.md.
- Palette (navy-grey kon-nezumi family, cooler than the reference): mist #EEF0F1, mist-2 #E3E7E9, fog #C9CFD2, slate #4A5560, lake-night #242B31, ink #1B2126, sub #6B757D, glint #A9BCC6 (tiny accents only).
- Type: Fraunces (display) and Source Serif 4 (text); Noto Serif TC and Noto Serif JP for zh-TW and ja.
- First screen: strip / beside-photo / logo-row. A film strip of three frames with the text beside it and a logo row at the foot; no full-bleed overlay.
- Motion: sp-scroll v1 (fade and 6 px rise at 1.2 s, list stagger, two parallax layers: the middle strip frame and the water band); the sticky hero (pale chapters slide over it) is CSS only.
- Photographs: generated film-grain documentary stills, cool light on warm timber; no cut-outs.
- Forms: the shared form slot. The letter is a real email field and submit; without a form plugin it opens the visitor's mail app with the address and says plainly that the visitor is not subscribed yet.
