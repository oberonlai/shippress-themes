Sazanami Hotel is a WordPress theme for a seven-room lakeside hotel at 14 Shore Lane, Lake Mirren. The timber boathouse was built in 1934 and opened as a hotel in 2011, with every room facing the water. The theme presents the rooms and rates, breakfast, the shore, booking and a letter from the lake in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See brand.md for the design notes. Fonts: Fraunces (display), Source Serif 4 (text), and Noto Serif TC and Noto Serif JP (zh-TW and ja subsets), all under the SIL Open Font License.

== Installation ==

1. Download sazanami-hotel.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/sazanami-hotel.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Sazanami Hotel.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The hotel, its people, village, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.

== Forms ==

The letter section on the home page, the footer and the Newsletter page use the shared form slot (inc/shippress-forms.php). Without a form plugin they show a real email field and a submit button that opens the visitor's email app with the address; the notice says plainly that the visitor is not subscribed until that message is sent. MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP are used when active. The Contact and Booking pages show the first Contact Form 7 or WPForms form when one exists, and a mailto button otherwise. Recommended booking form fields: full name*, email*, phone, arrival date*, nights* (1-14), guests* (1-4), room type (Shore Room, Window-Seat Room, Corner Lake Room, Jetty Suite, any lake room), early breakfast hamper (checkbox), message, consent* linking to the privacy page. Contact form fields: name*, email*, subject, message*, consent*.
