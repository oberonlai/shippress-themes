# Kisha Rail Holidays

Kisha Rail Holidays — a block theme for an escorted rail-tour company that serves older travellers. A rounded film-grain photograph window sits under a white sticky top bar, with a thin italic headline, drifting rail lines, a docked "Next departure" card and three tab cells that are the tops of three white cards with corner-bracket titles. It continues with a day schedule beside a route scale, a slow-parallax viaduct band, a dark grained band with four tour cards and a wide pill button, and one way to request a brochure. Libre Baskerville for headings, Mulish for reading. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; scroll effects respect reduced motion.

## Structure
- `patterns/front-*.php`: the home sections (hero window, three cards, welcome, day schedule, viaduct, carry, lines, manager, brochure, journal).
- `patterns/header-main.php`, `footer-main.php`, `tpl-*.php`: header, footer, single post, notes and 404.
- `inc/i18n/<lang>.php`: theme strings; `demo-content*.json`: sample content.
- Photos: `assets/images/` (AI-generated film-grain photographs). See `readme.txt`.

Design notes: `brand.md`, `brand/`.
