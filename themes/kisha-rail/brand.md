## Audience
Travellers over 60 who love scenic railways but would rather not manage tickets, connections and suitcases: couples, friends and people travelling alone. Buyers of the theme: escorted-tour and rail-holiday operators selling to older travellers.

## Tone
The voice is calm, unhurried, and precise, speaking directly to mature travellers with clear practical detail rather than marketing hyperbole. It relies on quiet reassurance, step-free access, and dependable personal care without exaggerated claims or invented accolades.

## Story
Kisha Rail Holidays was founded in 2009 by Helen Marsh. Having spent years travelling by train across Europe, she recognized that scenic railway journeys often became exhausting rather than restful when travellers had to haul heavy suitcases across footbridges, navigate tight connections, or manage long days on crowded platforms. Before any journey was accepted into our first brochure, Helen rode every single line herself, inspecting every platform transition, timing every connection, and keeping each travel day under three hours aboard a train.

From our office at Station House, 3 Signal Row in Larchmere, we have kept our operations intentionally small and personal. We design escorted rail holidays specifically for travellers over sixty, maintaining a strict limit of sixteen guests on any departure. A dedicated tour manager accompanies each group from the first platform to the very last, keeping travel calm, unhurried, and step-free throughout the entire route.

Today our principles remain completely unchanged. Your heavy luggage is transported separately from hotel door to hotel door by van, platform seats and dining tables are reserved in advance, and every morning begins with a clear, spoken day sheet. By removing the friction and physical burdens of independent travel, we give our guests the freedom to simply watch the landscape unfold through the carriage window.
