Kisha Rail Holidays is an elegant WordPress block theme designed for escorted scenic rail tour operators and small travel companies catering to mature travellers. Built with warm film-grain documentary imagery, framed window heroes, and day-by-day itinerary schedules, it offers calm typography and accessible layouts that prioritise clarity and ease of reading. Fully responsive and block-native, it includes custom card mosaics, luggage and service highlights, and seamless brochure enquiry integration.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The home page opens on a rounded 16 px photograph window under a white top bar, with a thin italic headline in the sky, a bundle of drifting rail lines, a Next departure card docked on the window edge and three tab cells on its bottom edge that grow into three white cards with corner-bracket titles. It continues with the story since 2009, one day on tour as eight time rows beside a route scale and a kilometre numeral, a full-width viaduct photograph with slow parallax, what is carried for you, four tours on a dark grained band with a wide white pill button, the tour manager, the one way to request a brochure and a journal list. Scroll effects (rise, count, parallax) use the small sp-scroll script in assets/ and respect prefers-reduced-motion. The optional newsletter and contact slots work without any plugin and use Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when present.

== Installation ==

1. Download kisha-rail.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kisha-rail.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kisha Rail Holidays. On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): the home page, the inner pages, the journal with sample posts and the menu. Use Tools > Import demo content to run it again.
4. Edit everything in the block editor; the sections are block patterns in the "Kisha Rail Holidays" category.

The company, its people, addresses, telephone number and prices are fictional.

== Forms ==

Request a brochure and Contact: without a plugin the page shows a button that opens your email app. The departures letter (footer and newsletter page) shows an email field and a button that opens your email app with the request; you are not subscribed until the request is sent.
