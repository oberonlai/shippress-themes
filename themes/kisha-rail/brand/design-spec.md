## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://www.jcomm.co.jp/)
- **What it draws:** a wide, washed blue-grey aerial photograph of a big station roof and its platforms, framed in a rounded window under a white header. A giant thin script word ("ideas!") crosses the upper-left of the window in white, with a tracked one-line sub-copy under it. A fine field of ~120 blue hairlines (a ribbon mesh) sweeps from centre-left to bottom-right across the lower half. A white "PICK UP WORK" card with a blue tab header docks at the right edge. Three white tab cells sit on the bottom edge; the first is raised and active.
- **Story:** "a great piece of railway infrastructure, with ideas travelling through it" — the company's work moves through stations the way people do.
- **Signature motion:** the hairline ribbon drifts continuously (three.js, paused off screen); the photo moves slower than the page (yPercent parallax, scrub); the header turns white with a rounded bottom after the first screen; the tab cells grow into three full white cards as you scroll; cards below reveal once (0.3s, per-item delay).

### (2) Our translation (same composition, same motion idea, same image type: documentary photograph, ours film-grain)
**One film-grain photograph on a rural station platform under a soft overcast sky: on the right half a two-carriage local train stands with its door open; Amara, our tour manager (woman, 40s, navy coat, folder under one arm), holds the forearm of an older man with a walking stick as he steps up onto the carriage step; his wife waits behind him with a small handbag; further down the platform, a conductor in a dark uniform holds a small flag down at his side and waits, looking back toward them. The upper-left 40% is pale sky over the platform canopy edge and low hills.** It is jcomm's "the railway with something moving through it", turned from an aerial of infrastructure into the one moment this company sells: someone makes sure you are on the train before it goes. The giant word becomes our headline "Every step, with you." in the sky area; the hairline ribbon becomes a bundle of thin parallel rail lines drifting across the lower half (the line the train is about to take); the "pick up" card becomes the **Next departure** card; the three cells are the three things the site does: **A day on tour / What we carry / Request a brochure**. Scroll: the cells grow into the three cards; the window photo moves slower than the page.
