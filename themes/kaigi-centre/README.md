# Kaigi Centre

A block theme (type business) for a conference centre serving associations and corporate offsites. Stage 1 ships the English home page; inner pages and four more languages follow.

## Signature
- A framed hall photograph on the left 68% with a diagonal right edge: the pen contour of the hall is on white first, then the photograph wipes in left to right, with a slow zoom (parallax). Notched logo tab and a notice pill overlap the photograph.
- A plum panel on the right 29% (20 px top-left radius, 67 px rows with 1 px rules and chevrons) listing the five rooms; the same split returns for the room network (floor-plan line drawing + who each room is for) and the visit block.
- A marquee strip of the five rooms, stacked gatherings with wipe-in photographs, a ruled rate table.

## Patterns
`front-hero`, `front-strip`, `front-intro`, `front-network`, `front-cases`, `front-band`, `front-process`, `front-rates`, `front-team`, `front-visit`, `header-main`, `footer-main`

## Fonts
Fraunces and Source Sans 3 (SIL OFL 1.1, licences in assets/fonts); Noto Serif TC and JP for Chinese and Japanese sample content.

## Scroll effects
sp-scroll: hero parallax and the parallax band, wipe on gathering photographs, stagger on the booking steps and team list, marquee on the room strip; all stop under reduced motion and stay visible without JavaScript.
