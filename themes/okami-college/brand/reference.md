## Block structure
Header (logo left, two-line nav) / hero photo inset with white tagline box + gradient strip / pick-up tiles / Department cards 2x2 + night-class banner / Event band on a partial-width pale panel / Category links / audience link grid on a second pale panel / News rows / deep-blue footer band.

## Signature candidates
1. White tagline box flush on the photo's top edge plus a gradient caption strip crossing its lower right.
2. Partial-width pale panel (stops 15-22% short of the right edge) holding white event rows whose thumbnails poke out of the top-left corner; ruled list at right.
3. Wide white cards with a 2.55:1 photo and widely tracked serif titles, a night-class banner of 11.6:1 with centred tracked text.
