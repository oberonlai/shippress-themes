# Okami College

Okami College: a classic editorial WordPress theme for a hotel management college with its own teaching hotel. An inset photo window with a white tagline box and a teal-to-slate caption strip opens the home page, followed by staggered pick-up tiles, a results-first graduate case wall, ruled term rows, tracked-title cards with a banner strip, a parallax teaching-hotel band, partial-width pale panels with poke-out thumbnails, a price table and a navy offer band. Fraunces for headings, Source Serif 4 for reading. Sample content in English (this release covers the home page; the inner pages are short placeholders until the next release).

## Structure
- `patterns/front-*.php`: the eleven home sections; `head-main.php` and `foot-main.php`: header and footer.
- `templates/`: block templates (front page, posts, archive, category, tag, search, 404, page, single).
- `inc/demo-import.php`: sample pages, posts and menu; `demo-content.json`: the sample content.
- Photos: `assets/images/` (AI-generated, GPL-2.0-or-later / CC0). Scroll effects: `assets/sp-scroll.*`.

Design notes: `brand.md`, `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md`.
