## Story
Okami College was founded in Lisbon in 2009 by experienced hospitality professionals who believed hotel management education required active operational immersion rather than classroom simulation. Established at Rua do Farol 22, the college integrated its academic facilities with Okami House, a purpose-built teaching hotel designed to welcome commercial guests from its first day of operation. Principal Helena Varga joined the institution following nineteen years running luxury hotel floors, establishing an educational philosophy centered on daily floor discipline, personal responsibility, and high service standards.

The institution structured its vocational program around the daily realities of Okami House, which features forty-two guest rooms, a sixty-cover restaurant open to the public for weekday lunch, and a dedicated banquet room. Rather than observing hospitality from afar, students spend three full days each week working active service shifts under the guidance of seasoned instructors and two days in seminar rooms examining operational management. Every September, forty-eight students enter the two-year Diploma in Hotel Operations, divided into four desk groups of twelve to ensure close practical supervision.

Over subsequent years, the college established an international placement network comprising thirty-eight partner hotels across Portugal and wider Europe to host second-year students during their sixteen-week placements. Every academic term methodically rotates students through front office, banquet service, teaching kitchens, and rooms division before they enter the industry. Today, Okami House continues to operate as an authentic Lisbon hotel, preparing disciplined graduates who understand that genuine hospitality leadership begins with mastering every detail of the floor.

## Audience
School leavers and career changers aged 18 to 30 who want to run hotels, and their families; applicants from Portugal and the rest of Europe; hotel employers looking for supervisors who have already worked a floor.

## Tone of voice
The voice is calm, precise, and understated, reflecting the daily discipline of an operational hotel floor. All statements prioritize factual operational specifics and measured expectations over promotional exaggeration. Plain verbs, concrete numbers, no promises of jobs or salaries, no awards, no real hotel brands.

## A day at Okami House (the daily routine)
Students work three days a week on the floor and two in class. A floor day starts with a morning briefing; front-desk groups take the morning arrivals, the kitchen brigade starts mise en place, the banquet group sets the room, and the rooms group inspects its floor. Lunch service runs Tue–Fri 12:00–14:30 for real guests. Afternoons are for check-outs, banquet set-down, a short debrief with the instructor and the next day's rota.

## Programme
Two-year Diploma in Hotel Operations, one intake in September, 48 places, four desk groups of 12. Four terms of 18 weeks: Term 1 front desk and reservations (Sep–Jan, year one); Term 2 banquet and restaurant floor (Feb–Jun, year one); Term 3 teaching kitchen and rooms division (Sep–Jan, year two); Term 4 a 16-week placement at one of 38 partner hotels plus a 2-week leadership seminar (Feb–Jun, year two). Short courses: Evening Certificate in Banquet Service (10 weeks, two evenings a week) and Front Desk Weekend Course (2 days).
