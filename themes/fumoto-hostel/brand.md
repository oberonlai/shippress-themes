# Fumoto Hostel — brand brief

Design plan row: b14-3 (urban hostel, type business). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other languages follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Fumoto Hostel, a small urban hostel for people travelling on a small budget.
- **Location:** 14 Quay Lane, Harbour Ward (fictional city, no country named). Founded 2016 by Ines Marlow.
- **Size:** 38 beds: four dormitories and six private rooms.
- **Audience:** independent travellers, cyclists and rail travellers who want a clean bed, a key and a calm house.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, unhurried, and grounded in quiet documentary realism. It speaks plainly and warmly to independent travelers without marketing hype or decorative excess.

## Story
Fumoto Hostel opened its doors in 2016 at 14 Quay Lane in Harbour Ward. Founded by Ines Marlow, the house was established with a quiet and grounded purpose: to give independent travelers an unhurried place to stay, where a clean bed, a solid brass key, and a warm personal welcome come without fuss or excess.

The property accommodates thirty-eight beds in total, divided between four shared dormitories and six private rooms. From the very beginning, every room was arranged to invite natural daylight, utilizing pale oak woodwork and crisp linen. The shared kitchen and lounge were opened to guests daily from seven in the morning until eleven at night, establishing a peaceful rhythm for the entire house.

Across the years, Fumoto has remained devoted to these simple everyday standards. Whether guests are arriving off the late train to collect a key left at the desk, gathering on Wednesday evenings at nineteen hundred hours for shared supper, or resting before an early departure, the house provides a calm sanctuary kept with quiet pride.
