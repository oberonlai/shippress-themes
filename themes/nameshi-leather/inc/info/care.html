Conditioning

Wipe with a dry cloth each week. Twice a year, rub in a little of our beeswax balm (or any plain neutral balm), let it rest overnight and buff. Keep it away from radiators; let rain dry slowly.

Covered for life

Every stitch we sew. If a seam we made opens, we restitch it free for as long as the piece is in use, with the same waxed linen thread.

Repairs

Restitching a seam $15–40 · new edge burnish $20 · replacing a strap or belt keeper $30–60 · new buckle or hardware from $25 · pieces from other makers quoted first.

Turnaround

Two to three weeks at the bench. We send a photograph and a quote before we start, and post it back insured, or keep it for you to collect.
