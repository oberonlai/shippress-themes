# Asaoka Screening — brand brief

Design plan row: b20-1 (corporate health screening centre, type business). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other languages follow. The centre, its people, address and figures are fictional. Copy never promises an outcome, a cure or an award.

## Concept
- **Name:** Asaoka Screening, a health screening centre that books employee medicals for small companies. Named for its founder.
- **Location:** 14 Linden Row, Harbour District (no country named); phone +00 0 1234 5678 (placeholder pattern). A low white building with young trees and a gravel path.
- **Founded:** 2009 by Dr Hana Asaoka.
- **Service:** one calm morning of five checks for a whole team, results explained by a physician afterwards.
- **Audience:** owners and office managers of companies with 5 to 40 staff who want medicals done without losing days of work.
- **Tone:** calm, plain, specific. Times, steps and names before adjectives. Never "best", "guaranteed" or "cure".

## Hours
Checks Mon–Sat 7:30–11:30. Result explanations Mon–Fri 14:00–17:00. Closed Sundays and public holidays.

## Fixed facts (identical on every page and language)
- Groups of 5 to 40 staff per morning; reserve at least 3 weeks ahead; we confirm a request within 2 working days.
- Fasting from 21:00 the night before; bring last year's booklet if you have one.
- Printed booklet within 10 working days, posted to the person; 20-minute explanation per person; referral letter when needed; the company receives only a fit-for-work summary.
- 140 companies a year.
- Five checks and their times: Body measurements & vision about 15 min; Blood & urine about 10 min; Chest X-ray about 10 min; ECG about 10 min; Physician consultation about 15 min. About an hour per person.
- Prices per person, VAT included: Essential check €145; Standard employee medical €210; Extended (adds abdominal ultrasound) €320; Over-40 comprehensive €410; add-ons eye pressure €25, hearing test €20.
- Packages: Essential = measurements & vision, blood & urine, physician consultation; Standard = all five checks; Extended = standard + abdominal ultrasound; Over-40 = extended + longer consultation + cardiology review of the ECG.
- Booking process: Request a date → Confirm roster → Screening morning → Results & explanation.
- Sample open dates on the home page: Tue 24 Nov (6 places left), Thu 3 Dec (12), Tue 15 Dec (3), Sat 19 Dec (9); morning slot 7:30–11:30.
- Company stories (3): Fernhill Design Studio (12 staff), Corran Bakery Wholesale (28 staff), Oduya & Pell Accountants (8 staff).
