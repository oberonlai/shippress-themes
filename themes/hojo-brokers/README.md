# Hojo Brokers

A block theme for an independent commercial insurance broker: a landing-style home with an anchor menu and one repeated call to action ("Ask for a cover review").

## Signature moves
- **Wedge head + photo-first hero:** a pale hatched page head with two slate wedges bleeding off the right edge, then a full-bleed documentary photograph whose right side fades to paper behind the statement (slow parallax on the photo).
- **Two-tab index:** "By cover / By trade" in a slate frame, switched with radio inputs (no script), cells with a small corner notch.
- **Three flush steps:** 01 / 02 / 03 in stepping slate tones with chevron joins and 5rem numerals.
- Supporting: the ochre call to action with a round arrow disc and a darker bottom edge; the yard strip of four flush portraits.

## Notes
- Fonts: Inter Tight and Public Sans, loaded through theme.json `fontFace`.
- Scroll effects: `assets/sp-scroll.css|js` (parallax, reveal, stagger). `?sp-static` shows the final state.
- Forms: `inc/shippress-forms.php` (dynamic `shippress/form` block with a mailto fallback).
- Palette: slate `#5E6C7B`, deep ink `#243241`, mist `#E4E9EE`, paper `#F4F6F8`, one ochre `#D8A93E` for the call to action.
- Inner pages, posts and the five language files are added in the next stage.
- Reference notes: `brand/design-spec.md`, `brand/reference.md`, `brand/reference-tokens.md`.
