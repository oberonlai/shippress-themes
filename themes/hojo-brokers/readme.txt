Hojo Brokers is a calm, precise WordPress theme designed for independent commercial insurance brokers and risk advisors. Built for clear readability and high conversion, it features structured cover schedules, yard visit timelines, transparent fee tables, and documentary photography. The theme provides complete responsive layouts for commercial landing pages and technical business services.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download hojo-brokers.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/hojo-brokers.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Hojo Brokers.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Forms: the renewal-notes sections use a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP configured it shows that form; without a plugin it shows a real email field and a button that opens your email app with the address (nothing is subscribed until the email is sent).

The firm, its people, address, telephone number, cases and fees are fictional.
