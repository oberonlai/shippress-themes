# Megumi Pharmacy — brand brief

Design plan row: b8-6 (community pharmacy, type store). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other four languages follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Megumi Pharmacy, an independent community pharmacy with a small counter shop.
- **Location:** 1184 Queen Street East, Toronto, ON M4M 1L4, Canada (fictional); phone (416) 555-0148; email counter@megumipharmacy.example.
- **Founded:** 2006 by pharmacist Megumi Ward; six people on the team today.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 8:30–19:00 · Sat 9:00–17:00 · Sun 10:00–14:00 · statutory holidays closed
- **Audience:** neighbours on and around Queen Street East: families, older customers on long-term medicines, and commuters who stop on the way home.
- **A day:** the dispensary opens at 8:30, repeat prescriptions are made up in the morning, counter talks and reviews fill the day, and blister packs are checked before closing.
- **Tone:** Write the way the counter speaks: calm, specific, and plain, with these people, prices, hours, and years and no others. Do not add urgency, awards, press mentions, brand names, or any promise about a health result.

## Story
Megumi Ward opened Megumi Pharmacy in 2006 at 1184 Queen Street East, Toronto, ON M4M 1L4, Canada. It is an independent community pharmacy with a small counter shop, and the address has not changed. Medicines are explained in person at the counter. The phone is (416) 555-0148 and the email is counter@megumipharmacy.example. The door opens onto the neighbourhood high street, near the streetcar stop.

The shop added one practical thing at a time. In 2011 a consultation room was opened so a medicine review or a vaccination could take place off the main counter. In 2015 the weekly blister-pack service began, prepared each week. In 2019 home delivery started for addresses within 3 km. By 2024 there were six people on the team, and there are still six. Hours are Mon–Fri 8:30–19:00, Sat 9:00–17:00, and Sun 10:00–14:00. Statutory holidays are closed.

Those six are Megumi Ward, founder and pharmacist; Daniel Osei, pharmacist manager; Leila Haddad, pharmacist; Ana Kovač, pharmacist; Samuel Brandt, pharmacy technician; and Grace Liu, pharmacy assistant. The same faces are at the counter each week. Every new medicine is explained before it is handed over, and every new long-term medicine is followed by a call two weeks later. New prescriptions are usually ready in 20 minutes. Repeat prescriptions are ready within 24 hours. The pharmacy does not use pressure sales, and it does not promise results.

## People
- Megumi Ward, founder & pharmacist: Megumi Ward opened the pharmacy in 2006 and still works at the counter. She explains a new medicine by name before it is handed over.
- Daniel Osei, pharmacist manager (inhalers, new medicines): Daniel Osei is pharmacist manager, looking after inhalers and new medicines. He shows inhaler technique at the counter, including how to use a spacer.
- Leila Haddad, pharmacist (medicine reviews): Leila Haddad leads medicine reviews in the consultation room. She sits with the medicines a person actually takes and turns them into one list.
- Ana Kovač, pharmacist (vaccinations & travel health): Ana Kovač gives flu vaccinations and travel health consultations in the consultation room. Travel vaccines are charged at cost in addition to the consultation.
- Samuel Brandt, pharmacy technician (dispensing, blister packs): Samuel Brandt prepares prescriptions in the dispensary and checks the printed label against the box. He also makes up the weekly blister packs.
- Grace Liu, pharmacy assistant (front counter, deliveries): Grace Liu works the front counter and hands finished prescriptions across. She also looks after home deliveries within 3 km.

## Fees (Canadian dollars)
- Prescription dispensing: $9.95 (per item)
- Weekly blister packs: $6.00 (prepared each week, per week)
- Medicine review: $35.00 (30 minutes, by appointment)
- Blood pressure check: Free (10 minutes, no appointment)
- Flu vaccination: $25.00 (in the consultation room)
- Travel health consultation: $45.00 (30 minutes; vaccines at cost)
- Home delivery: $4.00 (within 3 km)

## How a prescription is talked through
1. Hand it in (paper, e-prescription or repeat request)
2. We check it against your record (allergies, other medicines; about 10 minutes)
3. Prepared and double-checked (technician prepares, a pharmacist checks)
4. Talked through at the counter (or in the consultation room)
5. A call two weeks later (for every new long-term medicine)
