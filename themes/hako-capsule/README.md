# Hako Capsule

A WordPress block theme for a station-front capsule hotel that sells a clean pod and a hot shower.

- **Look:** white page ruled in 1px ink lines, a thin tracked wordmark over a framed sticky nav, a hanging ink "Book tonight" slab, a full-bleed stage photograph with a speech-bubble Tonight card.
- **Home:** narrow three-paragraph column, five-cell pod-bay wall, comparison table (pods, cabin, day use), parallax shower band, notched numeral cards, ruled house rules, night-desk stories, newsletter slot and an ink booking band.
- **Fonts:** Geist (headings) and Inter Tight (reading), both OFL-1.1, bundled in `assets/fonts`.
- **Motion:** `assets/sp-scroll.css` and `assets/sp-scroll.js` (reveal, wipe, stagger, parallax); all of it respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Forms:** optional newsletter and contact slots in `inc/shippress-forms.php`; without a form plugin they fall back to a mailto action.
- **Photography:** 14 generated documentary photographs in `assets/images` (credits in `readme.txt`).

See `brand.md` for the fictional brand, copy and page plan.
