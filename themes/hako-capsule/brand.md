# Hako Capsule - brand brief

Station-front capsule hotel in Easthaven. It sells a clean pod and a hot shower, one minute from the platform. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hako Capsule. Wordmark "HAKO CAPSULE" in tracked capitals, Latin in every language. "Hako" means box in Japanese; the hotel is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** 3 Platform Lane, Easthaven (fictional city). East Exit of Easthaven Central Station, 80 m / 1 minute on foot.
- **Founded:** 2019 by Mara Okafor, who wanted a calm, staffed place to sleep for people stepping off the last trains.
- **Audience:** travellers on late connections, long layovers and short city stays; commuters who missed the last train; conference visitors who want a quiet floor.
- **Tone:** calm, specific and unhurried. Exact times, plain rules, no hyperbole. No awards, press, ratings, guarantees or customer counts.

## A day at the desk (the daily routine)
The desk is staffed 24 hours. Check-in runs 15:00-02:00, check-out is by 10:00, quiet hours are 22:00-07:00. Housekeeping works between check-out and check-in. Day-use pods run 10:00-18:00, day-use showers 06:00-23:00. Late trains arrive past midnight; Jonah hands over a brass key and a locker number, shoes come off at the step, and the corridor stays silent until morning.

## Capacity and facts (identical on every page and in every language)
- 84 places: 48 Standard Pods, 16 Wide Pods, 12 Quiet-Floor Pods, 8 Single Cabins. 10 private shower rooms. 84 lockers and a staffed luggage room.
- Prices in EUR, per person, towel set included: Standard Pod €42, Wide Pod €54, Quiet-Floor Pod €48, Single Cabin (lockable door) €72 per night; Day-use Pod €24 for 4 hours (10:00-18:00); Shower only €9 for 45 minutes.
- Free cancellation until 18:00 on the day of arrival.
- Recipient for requests and the letter: stay@hako-capsule.example.

## Stories (fictional, first names only, no claims of results)
"The 00:52 last train" (a commuter, Standard Pod), "A shower between flights" (day-use shower on a nine-hour layover), "Four quiet nights" (a conference visitor on the Quiet Floor).

## Booking flow
1 Choose a pod or cabin. 2 Book by email or form, or walk in at the desk. 3 Check in, receive key and locker. 4 Shoes off at the step, towel set, sleep; out by 10:00.

## House rules (8)
Shoes off at the step - quiet hours 22:00-07:00 - calls in the lounge only - no food in pod rooms - one guest per pod - luggage in lockers or the luggage room - check-out 10:00 - photo ID at check-in.

## Signature techniques (carried to inner pages)
1. **Pod-bay wall** (from the ruled news wall of tokyonode.jp): full-bleed cells in 1px ink lines, square photo, label pill, name, price and a 48px square arrow box.
2. **Notched numeral cards** (from the date-tab cards of ise-seaparadise.com): 8px white frame, a white notch with a 3rem numeral and a 36px silver circle, middle card lowered 60px.
3. **Tonight card and hanging slab** (from the same site): a speech-bubble card at the lower right of the stage photograph and an ink slab hanging from the header with the one Book tonight link.
Supporting: wordmark band over a 1px-framed sticky nav, full-width section rules with a centred tracked-caps title, outline pills.
