Kuki Climate is a calm, single-column WordPress landing theme designed for commercial HVAC maintenance contractors and building services engineers. Built around an iron palette with sans typography, it features ledger rows, toggleable service schedules and structured survey workflows. The theme delivers clear contract pricing, equipment logging layouts and responsive documentary visual frames. The home page opens on a dimmed rooftop photograph inside an iron frame with a twelve-month year line, then runs through buildings, a plant room, what a year covers, the survey hour, a service-log photo strip, prices, questions and one request band. Manrope for headings, Source Sans 3 for reading. Sample content in English; works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; motion is limited to the opening veil, the year line and gentle scroll reveals, and stops for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download kuki-climate.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kuki-climate.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kuki Climate.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates the home page with all its text and images, a posts page with three building stories, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The company, its people, address, telephone numbers and prices are fictional.
