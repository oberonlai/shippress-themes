# Kuki Climate

A WordPress block theme for a commercial HVAC maintenance contractor (landing type). Iron palette (`#2E3A36`), Manrope and Source Sans 3.

Home page techniques: an iron-framed window with a veil that lifts and a twelve-month year line, pinned while an iron card rides over it; numbered ledger rows with flat colour panels and double chevrons; a CSS-only quarterly / monthly pill toggle. Scroll effects use `assets/sp-scroll.css` and `assets/sp-scroll.js` (reveal, stagger, parallax, marquee) and stop for reduced motion.

Patterns: `front-kv`, `front-buildings`, `front-plant`, `front-covers`, `front-survey`, `front-log`, `front-prices`, `front-questions`, `front-request`. Sample content: `demo-content.json`. Brand notes: `brand.md`, `brand/design-spec.md`.

Inner pages (first versions): About, Contracts, Buildings, Survey, Prices, Contact, Seasonal checklist, Privacy, Terms and the Field notes posts page. The header menu links to these pages; the home rows link to Buildings.
