# Kuki Climate - brand brief

Commercial HVAC maintenance contractor in Rotterdam, Netherlands, selling planned maintenance contracts to offices, shops, hotels, clinics and schools. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kuki Climate B.V. "Kuki" is the Japanese word for air, brought back by a co-founder from his training years in Osaka; the company is otherwise Dutch and international.
- **Location:** depot and office at Koelhuisweg 14, 3089 KN Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional street). Service area: within 60 km of Rotterdam. EUR in every language, amounts excluding VAT (21% is added); no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2009 by Anneke Visser (managing engineer) and Daichi Mori (controls engineer). From the first year the company did only scheduled commercial maintenance, choosing planned visits over emergency call-outs.
- **People (seven):** Anneke Visser, co-founder, managing engineer; Daichi Mori, co-founder, controls engineer; Leila Haddad, contract manager, leads every survey hour; Tomás Ribeiro, lead service technician, rooftop plant; Samuel Mensah, refrigeration technician, F-gas certified; Marta Kowalska, service desk coordinator; Jonas Becker, second-year apprentice technician.
- **Audience:** facilities managers, property managers and owners of commercial buildings of four to twelve floors, small hotels, clinics and schools, who want the plant on the roof kept in service without surprises.
- **Tone:** a calm, practical engineering voice. Say what is checked and when, in short sentences. No guaranteed results, awards, savings percentages, real clients or media. The response target is a target, never a guarantee.

## A day in the year (the daily routine)
- 07:00 depot briefing at Koelhuisweg 14; 07:30 vans out; roofs before the heat; controls readings after 11:00; the log is written before the technician leaves each building; 16:30 hand-over to the service desk. Office and service desk Mon-Fri 07:30-17:30; survey hours are booked Tue-Thu with starts between 08:00 and 15:00.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Contracts run 12 months with 60 days' notice. Quarterly = 4 visits (March, June, September, November; March is the cooling start-up, November the heating changeover). Monthly = 12 visits.
- Survey hour: 60 minutes, EUR 190 excl. VAT, credited to year one; written report within 3 working days; proposal valid 30 days; we call back to book within 2 working days.
- Survey plan: 0-10 walk the building and read the log; 10-30 roof and plant room, every unit and nameplate; 30-45 controls, filters, drains, refrigerant; 45-55 notes at the table; 55-60 agree the next step.
- Prices (EUR, excl. VAT): survey hour and written report 190; quarterly up to 4 units 2,400 / year; quarterly 5-12 units 4,800 / year; monthly up to 12 units 8,400 / year; monthly 13-30 units 14,800 / year; each extra unit 320 (quarterly) / 560 (monthly) per year; out-of-hours call-out for contract clients 95 / hour, parts extra.
- Response target for contract clients: on site within 4 hours on weekdays 07:00-19:00, next morning otherwise. Out-of-hours line for contract clients +31 10 555 0149, 24/7. Phone +31 10 555 0140; emails survey@, desk@, accounts@kukiclimate.example.
- Stories (category Building stories): Halvorsen House (six floors, 9 rooftop units, quarterly since 2019, monthly June-August); Noordpoort Health Centre (two floors, 2 AHUs + 6 cassettes, monthly since 2021); Maasweg Primary School (3 rooftop units + classroom cassettes, quarterly since 2022). Seasonal checks (inner pages): Drains before summer, What goes in the log, The November changeover.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
- **Ledger rows:** a light numeral (Manrope 200, 3rem), three lines of text, a thin double chevron and a flat, unbordered colour panel (cool grey or sand-grey, 42% of the row), on 1 px full-width rules. Whole row is one link.
- **Pill toggle:** a 372 x 42 capsule on a full-bleed grey band that really swaps Quarterly and Monthly (CSS only, no script).
- **Framed window:** a dimmed documentary photograph inside an iron frame, the huge light title bottom-left, a twelve-month year line with visit dots under it; on load an iron veil lifts, on scroll the window stays pinned while an iron card rides over it.

## Pages and contents
Home (stage 1): framed window and year line, the ride-over card, buildings rows, parallax plant room, covers toggle, survey hour list, service-log photo strip, price table, questions, request band. Inner pages (en, built): About (founded 2009; the first contract visit fell in March), Contracts (pill toggle, month-by-month schedules), Buildings (three expanded ledger rows), Survey (toggle between the hour on site and the report, parallax photo band), Prices and terms (term rows, quarterly or monthly price table), Field notes (ledger list of six posts), a single article (Halvorsen House), Contact, Seasonal checklist (the waitlist page), Thanks, Privacy, Terms. Every inner page opens on the small framed window with a year line.

## Photography
Documentary photographs in natural daylight, high-key and soft, a slightly cool grade with muted greys and grey-greens; real working rooftops, plant rooms and ducts; people in plain unbranded work clothes; no text, no logos, no brands.
