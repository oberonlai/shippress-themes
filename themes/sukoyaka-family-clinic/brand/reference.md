# Sukoyaka Family Clinic - S5-Style references

Screenshots: /tmp/theme1020l-kit/ref/ (1-hero = Kuwakuwa, 2-hero = Koba Clinic, 4-hero = Inui Dental, 5-hero = Suzuki Ladies). Note: several sites use viewport-height heroes, so the 5000px captures mostly show a stretched first screen; section order below is from the Inui full capture plus the first screens.

## Kuwakuwa (community clinic, Hikone)
- Real URL: https://kuwa-kuwa.jp/
- S5-Style entry: https://www.s5-style.com/11071/
- First screen: one full-bleed photograph of the building with people in front, almost no chrome. Photo takes about 95 percent, text about 5 percent. A soft yellow wave edge closes the screen at the bottom, hinting at the next section.
- Home section order: full-bleed building photo, wave divider, soft-coloured content bands (clinic as a place to drop in, not a medical menu).
- Image/text ratio: very photo-heavy at the top, text arrives only after the wave.
- Whitespace: very generous, pale washed overlay lets the photo breathe.
- Type hierarchy: minimal; copy is small and quiet, no big headline on first screen.
- Navigation: lightweight, hidden or minimal at the top.
- Borrowed for Sukoyaka: a wavy section divider that closes the first screen and leads into a pastel band; a pale wash over the photo so text and hours can sit above it calmly.

## Koba Clinic (ENT clinic, Kikuchi)
- Real URL: https://koba-clinic.jp/
- S5-Style entry: https://www.s5-style.com/11401/
- First screen: grey-blue field, three organic blob-shaped photos (one per treatment area) along the bottom half, logo top left. Photo about 50 percent, flat colour and nav about 50 percent.
- Home section order: blob trio hero, large exterior photograph, then clinic information sections.
- Image/text ratio: images dominate; text limited to short pill labels on the blobs.
- Whitespace: big empty colour field above the images; the nav is small relative to it.
- Type hierarchy: two nav tiers (larger primary links, smaller secondary links), tiny pill labels, one phone number in a dark pill.
- Navigation: top nav in two tiers plus a vertical side rail with hours, access and reception (this is the side-rail pattern Sukoyaka must NOT copy), plus a floating dark phone pill bottom right.
- Borrowed for Sukoyaka: organic rounded image masks (blob or arch) with a small white label chip per service; a single floating phone or hours pill instead of a rail.

## Inui Dental Clinic (family dental, Shizuoka)
- Real URL: https://inuidental.com/
- S5-Style entry: https://www.s5-style.com/10922/
- First screen: warm light-grey header with logo, nav and two pill buttons (phone, web booking); a spaced-out headline in light weight; wide photo band below with a news strip overlapping its lower right. Text about 35 percent, photo about 65 percent.
- Home section order: header with CTA pills, hero photo with news strip, intro text left with offset portrait photo right and a small info list, pick-up block with arch-cropped photo collage plus text, brown menu band with five white cards and a kids banner, light-blue "three reasons" band with circular photos.
- Image/text ratio: roughly 55/45, alternating photo-left/text-right and the reverse.
- Whitespace: generous vertical padding between bands, narrow text columns.
- Type hierarchy: wide-tracked light headline, small body, tiny English eyebrow labels (MENU, PICK UP), numbered badges on circles.
- Navigation: single-row top nav with plus-marked dropdowns, two pill CTAs, a small floating tab pair (hours, phone) at the right edge.
- Borrowed for Sukoyaka: intro band with narrow text column left and offset photo right plus a short fact list under the copy; alternating coloured bands (cream, warm brown, pale blue) with equal-width white cards and circular photo reasons.

## Suzuki Ladies Clinic (women and children, Koriyama)
- Real URL: https://suzuki-ladies-clinic.jp/
- S5-Style entry: https://www.s5-style.com/10868/
- First screen: full-bleed interior photo or video, small left-aligned serif headline in the lower left, a pale floating pill at bottom centre with three quick links (online booking, delivery booking, hours). Photo about 90 percent.
- Home section order: full-screen hero, then information sections (not captured in full; the tall capture shows only the stretched hero).
- Image/text ratio: photo first, text kept to a three-line headline and nav.
- Whitespace: large uninterrupted image, small elements pushed to edges.
- Type hierarchy: elegant light serif headline, small nav, thin serif phone number, large ghosted brand wordmark across the bottom.
- Navigation: transparent top bar with logo left, nav centre, utility links and phone right.
- Borrowed for Sukoyaka: a small bottom pill that carries three quick links including hours; a transparent slim header with phone number at the far right.

## Mix
A Sukoyaka Family Clinic home page can take Inui's structure as the spine and borrow quiet touches from the others.
- First screen: warm off-white background, slim header (logo, five nav links, phone pill and one booking pill, in the Inui manner). Below it a two-column split: left column with a modest headline in light weight, one sentence and one button; right column with one arch- or blob-masked photo (Koba mask idea). Roughly 45 percent text, 55 percent photo.
- Directly beneath the split, or overlapping its bottom edge, a slim opening-hours strip as a single pale pill or band (Suzuki pill idea) showing days and times in one line plus a phone number. No tables, forms or search in the first screen.
- Close the first screen with a gentle wave edge (Kuwakuwa) into the next band.
- Following sections: short intro with narrow text column left and offset photo right plus three-item fact list; a coloured band with five or six equal white service cards; a pale-blue or sage band with three numbered reasons using circular photos; then access and news.
- Keep generous vertical whitespace, two-tier type (small English eyebrow plus Japanese-style sized headline, never giant centred type), no side rails, no vertical text bars, no centred oversized headline; one floating phone or hours pill is the only fixed element.

## What Sukoyaka actually took (structure only)
- From Inui Dental: the quiet first screen with a light headline on the left and one photograph on the right, under a slim top bar with one booking button; and the narrow text column beside offset photographs (used for the founder's letter).
- From Suzuki Ladies Clinic: opening hours always in sight. Sukoyaka keeps them as one slim ruled line under the first screen (not a floating pill, nothing fixed on screen).
- From Koba Clinic: small labels over each section heading and named people introduced with a portrait each (two tall doctor portraits, set at different heights, then one wide nurses photograph).
- From Kuwakuwa: a calm photo share with long pale bands between sections; the hours, vaccinations and health checks sit on one stone band as plain lists.
- Not taken: wave edges, blob masks, rounded pills, side rails, vertical text, any copy, photograph or logo. The visit path (four photographed stops on one vertical line) is Sukoyaka's own.
