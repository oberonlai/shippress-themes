# Nagisa Grill - brand brief

A one-boat seafood restaurant in a fictional harbour town. Sample content is in English (stage 1) and is localised later for Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Nagisa Grill. A restaurant that cooks only what one day boat lands, in one dinner service a night.
- **Location:** 7 Harbour Row, Port Ellery (fictional coastal town). Harbour tram stop "Harbour Row", a four-minute walk from the quay; bicycle racks on the quay.
- **Founded:** 2019 by chef Inès Calder and skipper Tomas Varga.
- **Audience:** adults who want a long unhurried dinner built around one catch, local regulars, visitors to the harbour, and small parties of one to eight.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, clear and grounded in the daily rhythm of the boat and the kitchen. Sentences are plain and specific, avoiding promotional embellishment, awards or empty claims of freshness.
- **Contact:** table@nagisagrill.example (no telephone). Open Wednesday to Sunday: bar from 17:30, seatings at 18:00 and 20:30, last order 22:00, doors close 23:00. Closed Monday and Tuesday (the boat's maintenance days).
- **The boat:** the day boat Wren, wooden-hulled, moored at Berth 14. Skipper Tomas Varga takes her out at 05:30 and is back at the quay by 10:30.

## Story
Nagisa Grill was founded in 2019 by chef Inès Calder and skipper Tomas Varga at 7 Harbour Row in Port Ellery. Their partnership began with a simple agreement along the stone quay: Tomas would fish the coastal waters aboard his wooden day boat, and Inès would cook whatever came ashore that morning over a charcoal hearth.

Working with one boat, the Wren moored at Berth 14, defines every choice the kitchen makes. There are no secondary suppliers and no stored surplus from previous days. When the weather keeps the Wren at the quay on her maintenance days or during rough weather, the dining room serves shore plates and broth from the simmering stock-pot rather than turning elsewhere.

The dining room opens for only one dinner service from Wednesday to Sunday, with seatings at 18:00 and 20:30. Setting aside mornings and afternoons gives the kitchen the unhurried time required to break down each fish, simmer the bones for six hours, and bake the bread at 14:00 before writing the day's slate board.

## A day at the restaurant (daily routine)
05:30 the Wren leaves Berth 14 · 10:30 the catch lands at the quay · 11:00 the fish is broken down, bones go to the stock · 15:00 the board is written · 17:30 the bar opens · 18:00 first seating · 20:30 second seating (last order 22:00) · 23:00 the board is wiped. The daily routine is fixed on purpose: the catch decides the menu, the clock decides everything else.

## People
- **Inès Calder** — Chef and co-founder. Founded Nagisa Grill in 2019 and directs the kitchen at 7 Harbour Row. She cooks over charcoal and plans each evening's service around what lands at the quay.
- **Tomas Varga** — Skipper of the Wren, co-founder. Takes the day boat out from Berth 14 each morning at 05:30 and brings line and pot catch back to Port Ellery by 10:30.
- **Amara Osei** — Front of house. Manages the dining room and keeps the table book for both seatings; holds reserved tables for fifteen minutes.
- **Jun Park** — Grill cook. Tends the low charcoal grill during service and cooks whole fish over embers; every fish rests before it leaves the pass.
- **Noor Haddad** — Breaks down the fish. Receives the morning catch at the quay and breaks down each fish at 11:00; bones and trimmings go to the six-hour stock.
- **Lucía Ferraz** — Bread and desserts. Bakes the bread every afternoon at 14:00 and prepares the desserts; her work finishes before the doors open at 17:30.
- **Henrik Dahl** — Wine and the bar. Oversees the zinc bar and selects the wines poured during service; opens the counter at 17:30 for early arrivals and shore plates.

## Process (how a table is held)
1. Parties of up to 8 sit at one table; larger groups write first.
2. A deposit of €20 per guest holds the table and comes off the bill.
3. We hold your table for 15 minutes after the seating time.
4. Cancel by 15:00 the day before and the deposit is returned in full.
