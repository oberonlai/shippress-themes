# Soroe Clinic: brand

## Name, place, founding
- **Name:** Soroe Clinic ("soroe": to bring into line, to make even). A small orthodontic-only practice for teenagers and adults.
- **Location:** 21 Linden Row, Wrenfield, a fictional riverside town. Four treatment chairs, one records room, one conversation room.
- **Founded:** 2011 by Dr. Ilse Varga.
- **Line:** Every visit plans the next.

## Story
Soroe Clinic was founded in 2011 by Dr. Ilse Varga on Linden Row in the riverside town of Wrenfield. The practice takes its name from the Scandinavian word 'soroe', meaning to bring into line and to make even. Dr. Varga established the clinic as an orthodontic-only practice dedicated exclusively to teenagers and adults, purposefully focusing on precise tooth movement rather than general dentistry.

Operating from a quiet two-storey building with large windows and four treatment chairs, the practice was designed around unhurried, predictable appointments. In addition to the main clinical space, the clinic houses a dedicated records and imaging room and a private conversation room where patients can discuss options without sitting in a dental chair. Dr. Kwame Mensah joined the practice to collaborate on clinical planning, ensuring every patient case benefits from the joint review of two experienced specialist orthodontists.

From the beginning, Soroe Clinic has operated under a single guiding method: before you leave each visit, the next one is already planned and booked. Patients know what will be checked, which adjustments or aligners come next, and how long the visit will take. This quiet, structured approach removes uncertainty, allowing teenagers and adults to complete their orthodontic courses with clarity and confidence.

## Audience
Teenagers from age 11 and their parents, who worry about metal braces, long visits and school days lost; and adults at any age, many of them in their thirties to fifties, who were told years ago that treatment could wait, and who want clear facts before they decide anything.

## Tone
The clinic voice is calm, plain, and specific, relying on exact numbers, appointment lengths, and concrete procedures rather than promotional adjectives. It never promises a perfect smile, offering realistic expectations while strictly avoiding before-and-after claims, awards, and media mentions.

## Courses, steps and fees
Typical lengths (never promised): teenagers with fixed braces 18-24 months; adults with clear aligners 12-18 months; review visits every 6-8 weeks, 20 minutes each; retainers afterwards with checks at 3, 6 and 12 months.

The first visit, in four steps:
1. First conversation: 30 minutes, free. We listen, look, take photos. No X-ray, no decision on the day.
2. Records appointment: 45 minutes, €190. 3D scan, two X-rays, photographs.
3. Plan conversation: 40 minutes, included. Your written plan, options, length and cost; you take it home.
4. First fitting: 60 minutes. Braces or first aligner set fitted; the first review visit booked before you leave.

Fees (euros, no decimals; review visits are always included in the course price; every course can be paid in monthly instalments at no extra charge; a replacement retainer after the first year is EUR 140):
