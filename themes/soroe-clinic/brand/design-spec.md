## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://yaizu-ortho.com/)
- **What it draws:** a quiet, pale cool-grey page (`#F3F4F0`). A two-line Japanese statement left of centre, light and widely tracked. **One long hand-drawn blue line** enters from the left, sweeps up through the headline area, crosses the **top-left edge of a wide rounded documentary photo** (an orthodontist in slate scrubs leaning over a patient in the chair, soft white blinds behind) and ends top-right in a small wave carrying a **rotated Latin name** on the stroke. Vertical white-boxed caption lines on the photo's right. Blue wave-edged header with phone and a white reserve pill. Below the fold: dated news rows with "+" pills.
- **Story:** orthodontic care starts with a **conversation**; the first step is small and friendly – "just come and talk, adults and children alike".
- **Signature motion:** the line is drawn in; blocks fade up 30px over 0.9s `ease` with hand-set delays (0.1–2.0s); left/right variants slide 30px; band fades 1.5s. Secondary (j-ortho): hero photo settles 1.08 → 1 (1.2s ease-out) and stays fixed while white framed cells scroll over it.

### (2) Our translation (same composition idea, same motion idea, same image type: documentary photograph)
**Every visit plans the next.** One high-key documentary photograph, in the reference's place and spirit (clinician + patient in the chair, soft white room): **Dr. Ilse Varga sits beside the chair holding up the next clear aligner tray between two fingers, showing it to a teenage patient in the chair; behind them, Nadia (coordinator) opens the following visit on a wall screen.** Where yaizu's line says "the first step is just a conversation", our line is the **path of the course**: it starts on the photo (this visit), crosses the photo edge and the headline, and ends in a loop tagged **"next visit"** – the same drawn line, same crossing of photo and text, same rotated Latin tag at its end. The composition is turned into the row's split: photo **left** (bleeding to the cell's left edge, rounded), narrow text column **right**, and the reference's under-hero information (news rows) becomes the **logo-row** of four service marks at the hero foot. The j-ortho chips sit on the photo's lower-left exactly as in the secondary. Motion: the line draws itself once on load (1.4s ease), the photo drifts slower than the page (`sp-parallax` 6%, standing in for j-ortho's fixed photo/settle), and everything below the hero fades up 30px / 0.9s `ease` like yaizu.
