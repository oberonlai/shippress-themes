# Ushio Line

Ushio Line is a harbour ferry theme for a single commuter line in Calder Bay, pier to pier. The front page follows a sailing day across six piers, with fares, boats, crew and notices. Serif headings, a slate and mist palette, and sine-wave links carry the same calm harbour voice through every page.

Signature techniques: three portrait harbour photographs of unequal width (418 / 546 / 416) in one 165 px-radius frame inset 30 px with the wordmark on the seam and panels that drift sideways; every call to action is text, an arrow and a 1 px sine-wave underline; a radius-240 slate band inset 11 px on a mist ground holds the sailing day and a ghost word; a flush, square-cut photograph beside a serif column. Scroll effects: sp-parallax (hero panels, the water photograph), sp-stagger + sp-reveal--zoom (pier photographs), sp-reveal--up / --left / --right (text columns), sp-marquee (tick row).

See brand.md and brand/design-spec.md for the copy and the measured reference values.

## Pages

Home plus short pages for the menu items (Piers, Timetable, Fares, The Boats, Crew, About), On Board, Contact and the Notices page. Full inner pages and the zh-TW, ja, fr and de translations follow in the next release.
