# Ushio Line — brand brief

Design plan row: b21-3 (harbour passenger ferry, type business). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other languages follow. The company, its people, piers, boats, address and figures are fictional. Copy never claims punctuality rates, safety records, awards, passenger totals or green credentials.

## Concept
- **Name:** Ushio Line, the harbour ferry of Calder Bay (a fictional harbour city, no country named). Tagline: Harbour ferry, pier to pier. Story line: the pier is the platform, the water is the line.
- **Location:** Ushio Line Harbour Office, 1 Customs Stairs, Calder Bay. hello@ushioline.example, +1 555 0142 (fictional).
- **Founded:** 1974 with two boats and four piers. Ropewalk was added in 1996, Eastmole in 2008.
- **Service:** one line, 6 piers, 24 minutes end to end, five boats, every day of the year except when weather stops service.
- **Audience:** commuters, students, market sellers and people who simply cross the bay; riders with bicycles and step-free needs.
- **Tone:** short sentences, real times, names of piers, boats and crew. Weather stoppages are stated plainly.

## The day
A sailing day runs from the crew coming aboard at 05:15, through the first boat at 05:40, the morning peak from 07:00, midday from 10:00 and the evening peak from 16:00, to the last boat leaving Ropewalk at 23:10.

## Fixed facts (identical on every page and language)
- Piers and minutes from Eastmole: Eastmole 0, Shipyard Steps 5, Lantern Point 9, Customs Stairs 14, Fishmarket Quay 19, Ropewalk 24.
- Boats: Morning Tern, Petrel, Kittiwake, Sandpiper in service; Slack Water as relief boat. Each carries 180 passengers and 24 bicycles and has a step-free ramp. Four crew to a sailing: master, engineer, two deckhands.
- Hours: office Monday to Saturday 08:00–18:00; ticket windows (Customs Stairs and Eastmole) every day 05:15–23:30; the other four piers sell on board.
- Day stops: 05:15 crew aboard; 05:40 first boat, then every 20 minutes; 07:00 morning peak every 15 minutes until 10:00; 10:00 midday every 20 until 16:00; 16:00 evening peak every 15 until 19:00, then every 20; 23:10 last boat, arrives Eastmole 23:34.
- Fares (USD): single $3.20; child single $1.60 (ages 5–15, under 5 free); day pass $8.50; ten-ride book $27.00 (no expiry); monthly pass $74.00; bicycle $1.00.
