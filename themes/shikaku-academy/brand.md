# Shikaku Academy - brand brief

Fully online certification academy for project-management and IT credentials, Rotterdam, Netherlands. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shikaku Academy ("shikaku" is the Japanese word for a qualification; the school is otherwise international and modern).
- **Location:** recording studio and learner support office at Kade 14, 3072 Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional). Fully online. Prices in USD in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2017.
- **Audience:** working adults who need a credential the next role asks for and study after the workday.
- **Tone:** Speak in calm, concrete sentences, as a small academy talking to a working adult. Name the course, the price, the hour, and the person, and do not add pass rates, salary claims, awards, or outside certification brands.

## History
Shikaku Academy was founded in 2017 in Rotterdam for adults who already hold a job and need a credential the next role asks for. Helena Marsh set it up as a fully online school with a recording studio, not a campus timetable. The studio and the learner support office are still at Kade 14, 3072 Rotterdam, Netherlands. Lessons are recorded there and published to the lesson player. Learners watch them later, at a kitchen table, on a commuter train, or at a desk after the workday.

The school teaches six courses on two pathways. Project management is Project Delivery Foundations, Agile Practitioner Track, and Advanced Project Leadership. IT is Cloud Fundamentals, IT Service Management Essentials, and Network & Security Basics. A single course starts from $149, a pathway bundle of three courses on one track is $429, and all-access to all six courses is $690. Access runs for 12, 18, or 24 months, depending on the plan. The courses prepare learners for widely recognised project-management and IT credentials. The document the academy itself issues is a certificate of completion.

A team of seven runs the school. Tomasz Wierzbicki, Amara Osei, Rafael Quintero, Ingrid Solberg, and Daniel Mwangi teach the courses. Helena Marsh directs studies. Priya Raman leads learner support, Monday to Friday 08:00-18:00 CET and Saturday 09:00-13:00 CET. Live Q&A is on Wednesdays at 19:00 CET. A learner chooses a course or a plan, pays by card at checkout, studies at their own pace, then takes the mock exam and downloads the certificate of completion. A refund is available for 14 days if less than 20% of the course has been watched.
