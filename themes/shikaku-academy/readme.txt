Shikaku Academy is a fully online certification academy founded in 2017, with a recording studio and support office at Kade 14, 3072 Rotterdam, Netherlands. Six project-management and IT courses prepare learners for widely recognised credentials, with single courses, a pathway bundle, and all-access priced in USD. Learner support answers on weekdays and Saturday morning CET, and the academy issues a certificate of completion. A cool grey ground with paper strips in hanada blue carries the headlines; numbered-tab course cards step down the page and a fan of three rotated photo cards sits beside the lesson player. Libre Baskerville for headings, Work Sans for reading. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; scroll reveals respect reduced motion.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes.

== Installation ==

1. Download shikaku-academy.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/shikaku-academy.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Shikaku Academy.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the menu, and sets the front page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Languages: the sample content comes in English (demo-content.json); further languages follow the same mechanism (demo-content.<lang>.json). The importer uses the `shippress_demo_lang` option, else the site language.

The academy, its courses, people, address and prices are fictional.
