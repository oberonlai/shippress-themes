Shikaku Academy prepares working adults for widely recognised project-management and IT credentials. Six online courses sit on two pathways, with a lesson player you can open after work. Study from anywhere. Learner support stays in Rotterdam.

Shikaku Academy opened in 2017 for people who already have a job and need a credential the next role asks for. The recording studio and learner support office are at Kade 14, 3072 Rotterdam. Lessons are filmed there and studied later, from a kitchen table, a train seat, or a desk after hours.

Two pathways share the catalogue. Project management runs from delivery foundations through agile practice to advanced leadership. IT runs from cloud fundamentals through service management to network and security basics. Each course prepares you for a widely recognised credential. When you finish, the academy issues a certificate of completion.

Helena Marsh, founder and director of studies, works from the Rotterdam office with the course outlines. The team of seven teaches, records, and answers questions. You study in the lesson player at your own pace, take the mock exam, and download your certificate of completion. Live Q&A is on Wednesdays at 19:00 CET.

An IT pathway course on cloud services for working teams. Twenty-two lessons and sixteen hours, with one mock exam and a certificate of completion included.

The lesson player puts the video on one side and the lesson list on the other. You pause, resume, and open the next lesson when the day allows. A progress bar shows what is left in the course, and there is no class hour to keep.

Lessons are recorded at the Rotterdam studio. A study planner comes with every plan. Live Q&A is there when you want a person in the room: monthly on a single course, weekly on a pathway bundle or all-access.

Five course instructors and the director of studies record from our Rotterdam studio. Helena Marsh founded the academy in 2017 and still directs studies. On the pathway bundle and all-access plans, instructors give feedback on two assignments each.
