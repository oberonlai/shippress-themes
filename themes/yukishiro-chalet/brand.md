## Tone
The voice is calm, precise, and practical, relying on specific numbers and operational facts rather than promotional adjectives. Descriptions focus on clear, grounded alpine details and authentic daily routines without superlatives or ungrounded promises.

## Story
In 2009, Anna Brenner and Luc Morel founded Yukishiro Chalet within her grandparents' guesthouse in the alpine village of Valmont-Haut, situated at an altitude of 1,480 metres. The property originally opened with eleven guest rooms, providing straightforward hospitality and direct access to the snow right where the mountain village meets the slopes, just ninety metres from the primary gondola station.

Over subsequent years, the owners expanded the chalet's facilities in response to the practical routines of visiting skiers and walkers. In 2013, they introduced the dedicated heated boot and drying room so guests could depend on dry equipment every morning. Summer operations began in 2016 to welcome hikers to the valley's high trails, followed by an expansion in 2020 that brought the property to its present scale of eighteen rooms across four layouts.

Today, Yukishiro Chalet operates across two distinct mountain seasons, welcoming winter ski guests from 5 December to 12 April and summer hiking stays from 20 June to 20 September. In 2025, the chalet launched its four-night hut-to-hut itinerary, connecting valley lodging with alpine mountain refuges. Guests continue to step out each morning onto sixty-four kilometres of pistes served by fourteen lifts, returning each evening to warm hearths and dependable family hosting.
