# Makiba Farmstay

A block theme for a working dairy farm with guest rooms. Design notes: `brand/design-spec.md`, brand brief: `brand.md`.

- **Signatures:** a fixed hill-ridge strip carrying the five farm-day times with a travelling swallow; a fixed right-edge stack of round discs with curved labels; a hanging walnut-leaf canopy over a two-line statement.
- **Farm day:** a single horizontal row of five white rounded cards from the 05:30 milking to the 18:30 barbecue, anchored from the ridge labels.
- **Fonts:** Fraunces (display) and Mulish (text), bundled under `assets/fonts` with their OFL licences.
- **Status:** home page and menu-linked inner pages in English (stage 2 in progress). Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow.
- **Home sections (patterns):** `front-hero`, `front-intro`, `front-day`, `front-canopy`, `front-rooms`, `front-band`, `front-help`, `front-stay`, `front-journal`, `front-letter`.
- **Parts:** `header` (one centred menu), `footer` (ridge-topped, with the fixed disc stack and ridge strip).
- **Scroll effects:** `assets/sp-scroll.css|js` (reveal, stagger, count, parallax).
- **Forms:** `inc/shippress-forms.php` (shared form slot, works without plugins).
