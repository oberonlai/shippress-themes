# Makiba Farmstay - brand brief

A working dairy farm in Larkfield Valley with four family rooms above the old hay barn. Guests help with the 05:30 milking, eat the farm breakfast, do the chores with the family and stay for the evening barbecue. "Makiba" is the Japanese word for a pasture; the farm itself is international and contemporary. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Makiba Farmstay. "Makiba" means pasture.
- **Location:** Larkfield Hollow Farm, 14 Weald Lane, Larkfield Valley (fictional). Prices in euros (EUR) in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** a dairy farm since 1962, run today by the third generation. In 2014 the family opened four guest rooms in the loft of the old hay barn. 48 milking cows (Holstein and Brown Swiss crosses), 12 calves at any time, hens and two goats, on seventy hectares of hill pasture.
- **Audience:** families with children who want a real working farm for a few days. Theme buyers: farm-stay hosts and agritourism businesses selling family stays.
- **Tone:** calm, grounded and specific. Say what happens when, what children may do and what it costs. No awards, no guaranteed sightings or births, no "best", no quoted reviews.

## The rooms and rates (identical on every page and in every language)

- Evening barbecue EUR 22 per adult and EUR 11 per child (4-12), Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. The milking morning, farm chores and butter session are included for every guest.
- Minimum stay 2 nights. 30% deposit to confirm; free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival.
- Rooms open 1 February - 20 December (closed to guests 21 December - 31 January). Check-in 15:00-18:00, check-out 11:00. Booking desk daily 08:00-19:00.

## People (fictional)

## A day on the farm (daily routine)
05:30 milking with Tomas (children from age 5 help bring the cows in and hold the gate; optional) - 07:00 farm breakfast in the barn kitchen - 10:00 chores with Mateo and Rosa: bottle-feed the calves, collect eggs, fork hay - 13:00 shake butter in jars with Lukas, then a picnic basket for the meadow - 18:30 evening barbecue under the walnut tree (Tue, Thu, Sat). The same daily routine runs all season.
