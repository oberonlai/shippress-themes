# Shisei Clinic - brand brief

Chiropractic clinic for people who sit all week, in Vancouver (fictional). Sample content in English first; zh-TW, ja, fr and de follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Shisei Clinic. "Shisei" (posture) stays Latin in every language; the wordmark "BACK AT EASE" also stays English.
- **Location:** Suite 204, 1180 Linden Row, Vancouver, BC V6E 2S2, Canada (fictional). Five minutes on foot from the Linden Row SkyTrain stop. Prices in CAD, tax included, in every language.
- **History:** Shisei Clinic was founded in 2015 by Dr. Naomi Hartley, DC, in Suite 204 at 1180 Linden Row, Vancouver. After observing countless patients arrive with physical strain tied directly to prolonged computer work, Dr. Hartley envisioned a calm clinical space specifically tailored to desk workers. Most individuals spend forty hours sitting every week before anyone asks how their body actually feels or where joint restriction has begun to settle. Located five minutes on foot from the Linden Row SkyTrain stop, the clinic opened to offer a quiet environment where spinal health receives unhurried attention.
- **Today:** three treatment rooms and one movement room, seven people, founded 2015.
- **Audience:** office workers, designers, accountants, translators and anyone who sits at a screen for most of the week and arrives with neck, back, headache or jaw tension.
- **Tone:** Our tone is calm, unhurried, and grounded in specific clinical observations rather than exaggerated claims. We speak to desk workers in natural, clear English that emphasizes practical posture relief, transparent fees, and thoughtful guidance.

## A typical day
The clinic opens at 8:00. Mornings hold first visits (60 minutes) and follow-ups (30 minutes); the movement room runs 45-minute plan sessions between them; the afternoon block from 14:00 to 19:30 suits people coming from work. Saturday is 9:00 to 13:00.

## First visit (60 minutes, identical on every page and language)
1. 01 Talk (15 min): An unhurried consultation to understand your daily desk habits, symptoms, and health history.
2. 02 Look (15 min): A thorough posture and movement check to assess spinal alignment and joint mobility.
3. 03 First adjustment (20 min): A gentle, precise chiropractic adjustment performed only when clinically appropriate.
4. 04 Plan (10 min): Clear recommendations for follow-up care, workstation ergonomics, and home stretches.
Then weekly 30-minute follow-ups for three to four weeks, spaced out as mobility settles.
