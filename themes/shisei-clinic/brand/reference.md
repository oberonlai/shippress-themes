# Reference (draft, stage 1)
Source: https://sakurano-megumi.com/ (S5-Style, medical/beauty category: acupuncture + osteopathic clinic). Not used by any earlier theme. Numbers in reference-tokens.md.
Screenshots: brand/ref/sm-fold.png, sm-full.png, scroll-fade-before/mid/after.png.

## Signature techniques (candidates)
1. Arch photo bleeding right: tall photo at the right half of first screen, top radius 9999px, from header to hero bottom, with a giant white Latin wordmark straddling its lower edge.
2. Oval sub-photo with two blurred ghost ovals stepping down-right on the left, soft-focus echo.
3. Fixed pill bar at bottom centre (hours / access / menu) plus floating contact pill.
4. Bottom-to-top mask fade-in of every block (1.2 s ease-out).
5. Arch-cropped portraits (arch photo left of the concept text, round photo overlapping its corner).

## Colour relationships
Ground pastel hue gradient (~75%), white veil sections (0.6), brown text #504742, heading #75645A, accent red pill #B8615E, white type on photos. Saturated share ~1%.
