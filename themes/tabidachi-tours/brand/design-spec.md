# Tabidachi Study Tours — design spec (stage 1, design director)

Type: **business** (WooCommerce compatible, not required). Industry: a **school excursion and study-trip organiser** (day field trips, residentials, overseas study tours, language weeks). Theme buyers: school-trip and educational travel agencies selling to schools, teachers and parents.
Primary reference: **https://trippin.world/** (S5-Style, editorial travel magazine: white page, near-black ink, 20px gutters, a 72 / 28 split first screen with a large documentary photograph left and a five-entry story rail right, outlined place / topic chip pairs, tall 0.75 photo cards, a rounded black band, an inset rounded duotone "quote" band with an email line, an outlined ticker pill + black pill in the header).
Secondary: **https://studyinshizuoka.jp/** (study-abroad destination site; structure only: the three tinted "For …" audience gateway cards with a greeting line, 1px ink border and a filled arrow disc; the speech bubble anchored on the hero photo; the docked "LIVE" mini card with a tab). Its flat doodle illustrations and saturated sky ground are **not** adopted.
Tokens: `brand/reference-tokens.md`, cited as **T(a)…T(g)**. Screens: `ref/trippin-first.png`, `ref/scroll-trippin-900(-before).png`, `ref/scroll-trippin-1800(-before).png`, `ref/scroll-trippin-2700(-before).png`, `ref/shizuoka-first.png`, `ref/scroll-shizuoka-900/1800/2700(-before).png`.
**Imagery: photo** (stage-a IMAGERY = photo). One real-photography STYLE string, **high-key documentary, bright natural daylight** (row `photo_direction: documentary`; T(e)). 0 cut-outs.
Font direction: **serif** (row `font_direction`). Latin display **Source Serif 4**, Latin body / UI **Nunito Sans** (row fonts). zh-TW / ja headings **Noto Serif TC / Noto Serif JP** (Ming / Mincho); CJK body uses system sans (repo gate). Details §6.
Similarity target: about **80%** to trippin (layout rhythm, palette relationship, image treatment, chips, motion) with shizuoka's gateway cards, bubble and docked card. Photographs, copy, trip names, people, the wordmark and the quote are ours; no asset, text, logo or illustration of either reference is reused.
Row constraints honoured: archetype **editorial-magazine**, hero **photo-left-text-right** (photo 72% left, timed text rail right), nav **top-bar** (exactly one top menu), first_screen **timeline-line** (one horizontal hairline day-line under the hero), page_structure **audience-gateway** (schools / teachers / parents each get a door on home and their own page), first-screen saturated share ≤ 12% target (common cap 15%, row cap 30%).
Class prefix: `tt-`. Pattern slugs `tabidachi-tours/<name>`.

## Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion
- **What it draws (trippin)**: a slim white header (wordmark, three links with an outlined count box, an outlined pill with a scrolling line of text, a round black "Nearby" pill); under it, filling 72% of the width, **one large documentary photograph** of a real person at work in a real place (low-key, smoky, grainy), with a white headline and a short standfirst lying on its lower-left over a soft dark gradient; to its right a **rail of five story entries**, each a small portrait photograph with an outlined place chip + topic chip and a two-line title. Everything sits on white with 20px gutters and 8px-rounded photos.
- **What it draws (shizuoka, secondary)**: one framed photograph (two young people by the bay), a **speech bubble** anchored on the photo with a reassuring line, a **docked "LIVE" mini card** at the photo's lower-left corner with a tab, and a date strip at the photo's top-right ("TODAY'S Shizuoka 11/17 TUE").
- **Story**: trippin: real places told through the people in them, as editorial stories you can step into, several at once (the rail is "what else is happening now"). Shizuoka: "here is what life here is like today, and someone is speaking to you" (bubble, live card).
- **Signature motion**: trippin: image fades in on decode (`opacity .25s ease-in-out`), hover .15s ease-in-out, the header pill's line **scrolls continuously** (CSS keyframes `scroll var(--duration) linear`), carousel rows. Shizuoka: hero and sections fade in **1000ms `cubic-bezier(.25,1,.5,1)`**, a looping slider with peeking cards (T(g)).

### (2) Our scene (same composition, same motion idea, same image type: documentary photographs)
A study-tour organiser **moves a whole year group and brings every pupil back on time**. Under a slim white top bar: one large high-key documentary photograph **P01** — early morning at a roadside lay-by by a white coach, a **tour leader in a plain navy gilet and lanyard counting a line of students onto the coach** (one hand lightly raised, lips mid-count), a **teacher beside him checking a clipboard**, students with backpacks queuing at the coach door. Our serif headline **"Every pupil on. / Every pupil back."** lies on the photo's lower-left. The **speech bubble** "42 on. 42 counted." points at the leader's hand (shizuoka's voice on the photo, made specific to the headcount). The **docked mini card** "● On the road · Day 3 · Coach 2" overlaps the photo's lower-right corner (shizuoka's LIVE card = this trip, live). To the right, the trippin rail becomes **"Today on the road"**: five timed moments of the same trip day (07:30 breakfast headcount → 08:15 42 on Coach 2 → 10:00 museum workshop → 13:00 picnic on the sea wall → 17:40 back at the hostel), each with its own small portrait photograph and chip pair (place box + topic pill). Under everything one **day-line** hairline 06:00 → 22:00 with the five ticks; a gold dot travels to "now" (13:00).
Same composition (big documentary photo left with headline on it, five-photo story rail right, outlined chips, bubble + docked card on the photo); same motion idea (photo fades in on decode, header pill line scrolls continuously, rail entries fade in one after another, a soft 1s `cubic-bezier(.25,1,.5,1)`); same image type (real documentary photographs, people at work in real places).
