# Tabidachi Study Tours - brand brief

A school excursion and study-trip organiser: day field trips, residentials, overseas study tours and language weeks, planned term by term for schools, teachers and parents. One action everywhere: "Request a plan". Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German.

## Concept
- **Name:** Tabidachi Study Tours (wordmark "Tabidachi / Study Tours" with a round day-line glyph; *tabidachi* - setting out on a journey). The site answers "will every pupil come back on time?" with times and numbers before adjectives.
- **Location:** 18 Platform Lane, Ashby Vale (fictional market town). Phone +44 1632 960 418. Duty line during trips +44 1632 960 499 (24 h). Email plans@tabidachi-tours.example (plan requests) and hello@tabidachi-tours.example (general, The Register).
- **Founded:** 2009 by Mara Lindqvist, a geography teacher who led her own Year 9 field trips for eleven years and wanted every school to have one planner from the first brief to the debrief. First trip: one coach, 46 students. First study tour abroad 2012, duty line 2015, language weeks with host families 2019, 612th trip in 2026.
- **People (seven):** Mara Lindqvist (founder and director, since 2009); Daniel Okafor (head of tour leaders, 2011); Sofia Ferreira (school partnerships, 2014); Ana Kowalska (safety and risk assessments, 2015); Tomas Ibarra (operations, coaches and flights, 2013); Priya Shah (parent liaison, 2017); Elias Brandt (curriculum workshops, 2019).
- **Audience:** heads and trip coordinators who approve trips, teachers who lead groups, and parents who pay for and worry about them. Three doors on the home page, one per audience.
- **Tone:** calm, exact, teacher-to-teacher; times and numbers instead of adjectives. No guarantees, no awards, no accreditation logos, no real schools, coach companies or media.

## A day on a trip (the daily routine)
07:00 wake-up - 07:30 breakfast headcount - 08:15 departure headcount - 12:30 lunch - 17:40 return and evening headcount - 21:00 message to parents - 22:30 lights out. The home first screen shows one such day: Year 8, Normandy coast, Day 3, Coach 2, 42 students and 4 leaders.

## A day at the office (daily routine)
08:30 duty line handover from the night leader - 09:00 proposals and calls with schools - 11:00 risk assessments - 14:00 coach and flight bookings - 16:00 parents' questions - 17:30 office closes, the duty line stays open.

## Hours (identical everywhere)
- Office: Monday to Friday 08:30-17:30; closed weekends and public holidays. Duty line open 24 h during every trip.
- A plan request is answered within 3 working days. A planning visit is free for confirmed trips.

## Prices (EUR per student, from-prices)
1 Day field trip by coach from 42 - 2 Two-night residential, coach from 265 - 3 Three-night history trip abroad by coach and ferry from 410 - 4 Four-night study tour abroad by air from 690 - 5 Five-night science and geography tour by air from 840 - 6 Seven-night language week with host families from 1,190.
Included: transport, beds, meals, entries and workshops, travel insurance, leaders, duty line. Not included: spending money, passports or ID. One free leader place per 10 paying students. Paying in three parts: 20% at booking, 40% by the end of the spring term, balance 6 weeks before departure.

## How a trip is planned (five terms)
Autumn term (Sept-Oct) brief and proposal, a costed proposal within 3 working days - Winter (Nov-Dec) planning visit and costed plan, governors' approvals pack - Spring term (Jan-Mar) deposits, parents' evening, risk assessment signed - Summer term (Apr-May) final itinerary, packing list, leader briefing - Trip week three headcounts a day, 21:00 message, debrief within 10 days of return.

## Figures
612 trips since 2009 - 1 leader per 10 students (teachers counted as leaders) - 24 h duty line on every trip - at least 3 headcounts a day.

## Trips this year (four stories)
Porto river and coast (Year 10 geography, 4 nights by air, 84 students, 9 leaders) - Normandy coast (Year 8 history, 3 nights by coach and ferry, 42 students, 4 leaders) - Bay of Naples (sixth-form classics and geology, 5 nights by air, 36 students, 4 leaders) - Seville language week (Year 9 Spanish, 7 nights with host families, 28 students, 3 leaders).

## The Register
One letter each half-term (six a year): one trip told by its leader, one planning date, one note for parents. Subscribe on /the-register/ and in the home page quote band.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Editorial lead: one large documentary photograph (1.56:1, r8) with the serif headline on its lower left, beside a rail of five timed entries with portrait thumbs and outlined place / topic chips, and a hairline day-line with ticks and a gold "now" dot.
2. Tinted audience gateway cards: For schools / teachers / parents in pale gold, celadon and rose-grey, a greeting line in another language, a notched photo foot and a gold arrow disc.
3. Headcount bubble and docked card on the photo: "42 on. 42 counted." pointing at the leader, and a docked "On the road" card overlapping the photo corner.
Supporting: tall chip cards, an inset gold duotone parallax quote band with the newsletter line, an inset black request band, a horizontal term-by-term line.

## Photography
High-key documentary photographs in natural daylight: coaches, hostels, museums, beaches and school gates with students aged 12 to 17, teachers and tour leaders; no text, logos or signage; no cut-outs.

## Inner pages
For schools, For teachers, For parents, Trips, How we plan, Safety and care, Prices (the six-line price table), About, Request a plan, Contact, The Register (newsletter), Journal and Privacy, plus three stories. Each page head repeats the editorial lead with a rail; the gateway cards, bubble and docked card continue on the inner pages.
