Tsubaki Flowers — a WooCommerce store theme for a small florist shop that makes flowers for households and small ceremonies. A cool off-white ground, green-black hairlines and a celadon accent (#6E7F74). The home page opens on a white intro screen that fades away to a full-bleed film-grain photograph of a florist's hand on a bundle of stems, with the headline at the bottom left and a slow parallax drift; it continues with an asymmetric about block (tall portrait, a small photograph floating top right), a full-bleed hairline three-column grid of mixed portrait and landscape photographs, a market photograph band, a week in the shop told in three day rows, the people at the bench, a full-width price list, a small-ceremony story and a visit block. Inner pages: our story, people, a week in the shop, flowers and prices, subscriptions, ceremonies, stories, flower care and FAQ, visit and order, a shop with six products and one journal article. Hanken Grotesk for headings, Onest for reading. Quiet fade and mask reveals, parallax. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes.

== Installation ==

1. Download tsubaki-flowers.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/tsubaki-flowers.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Tsubaki Flowers.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates the pages with their text and images and the menu, and sets the front page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The shop, its people, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
