# Tsubaki Flowers: brand

## Story
Name: Tsubaki Flowers. Location: Aldmere. Audience: customers who are households and small ceremonies.
A florist shop and workroom at 14 Linden Row, Aldmere (fictional harbour town), founded in 2011 by Mara Ellison and named after the camellia (tsubaki) bush by the door. Flowers for households and small ceremonies. Seven people.

## Voice
Quiet, practical, specific. Short sentences, named people, honest numbers. No guarantees, awards, media or statistics.

## Fixed facts
- Phone (020) 555 0148, hello@tsubakiflowers.example. Currency EUR.
- Hours: Tue–Sat 9:00–18:00, Sun 10:00–15:00, Mon closed (market and workroom day).
- Delivery within 8 km, €8; same day if ordered before 12:00 Tue–Sat.
- Honest vase life: usually 5–7 days.
