# Sumibi Yakiniku — brand

## Name and location
Sumibi Yakiniku ("sumibi" is charcoal fire) is a fictional charcoal yakiniku grill of 64 seats at Ostrand Street 22, Harlow Quarter, Vellmar. Phone +00 31 555 0172. Email table@sumibi.example. Open Tuesday to Sunday 17:30–23:00, closed Monday; last seating 21:30; the reservations line answers daily 12:00–17:00. The room has 18 table grills in the main room, a 10-seat grill counter, two private rooms (Hearth Room, 8 guests; Long Room, 12 guests) and the Upper Floor (whole-floor booking, up to 24 guests).

## Founded
Opened in 2012 by Mara Lindqvist, who still cuts the first loins herself every morning.

## Audience
Diners who want a table of their own to cook at: couples, friends, families and groups of 8 to 24 for birthdays, farewells and company dinners. Buyers of the theme: yakiniku, barbecue and grill restaurants that sell set courses and take group and private-room bookings directly.

## Tone
Calm, grounded and specific. It names times, cuts, rooms and prices instead of hospitality hype, and lets the daily routine tell the story. No guarantees, no invented awards, no superlatives.

## Story
Sumibi Yakiniku opened in 2012 on the principle that charcoal grilling begins long before the first guest arrives. Every morning whole loins are cut by hand at 09:00, sliced and plated by 16:00, and nothing is frozen or held over. At 17:00 the coals of every table are lit, and the doors open at 17:30.

Three stories are told on the home page and in the journal: the morning cut (Mara and Priya, 09:00), coals at five (Tomás and Sam, 18 grills and the counter in forty minutes) and the long table (a 24-guest retirement dinner on the Upper Floor, booked by Amara three weeks ahead).

## Services and prices
EUR per guest, no "from" prices:
- Evening Course: €48 (9 plates, 90 minutes, from 2 guests)
- Butcher's Course: €68 (11 plates including three morning cuts, 105 minutes, from 2 guests)
- Whole Coal Course: €92 (13 plates, tongue to wagyu-style ribeye, 120 minutes, from 2 guests)
- Group Table Course: €58 (10 shared plates, 120 minutes, 8–24 guests, private room included)
- Drinks pairing: €28 (4 glasses chosen for the course)
- Children's grill plate: €18 (under 12, 5 plates)
Table held 48 hours; cancellation free until 48 hours before; groups of 8 or more book at least 7 days ahead and choose one course for the table; no deposit.

## Process (the single way to reserve)
1. Write to table@sumibi.example or call the reservations line (daily 12:00–17:00) with date, time, guests and course.
2. Amara replies the same day and holds the table for 48 hours.
3. Reply to confirm.
There is no booking engine and no third-party widget. Every "Reserve" goes to /reserve/.

## A day at the restaurant (daily routine)
09:00 the loins arrive and are cut · 12:00 the reservations line opens · 16:00 every cut is sliced and plated · 17:00 the coals of every table are lit (forty minutes for 18 grills and the counter) · 17:00 the reservations line closes · 17:30 doors open · 21:30 last seating · 23:00 close. Monday the kitchen rests.
