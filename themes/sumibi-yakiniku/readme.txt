Sumibi Yakiniku is a WordPress theme for a charcoal yakiniku grill with set courses and group bookings. A blush gutter frames a low-key photograph window cut at six degrees with a docked white price plate, a dark photo band bleeds from the gutter, pale tan tabs sit over three course cards, and one price table sits side by side with the group rooms and the single way to reserve.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See brand.md for the design notes. Fonts: Albert Sans (display), Onest (text), and Noto Sans TC / Noto Sans JP (zh-TW and ja subsets), all under the SIL Open Font License.

== Installation ==

1. Download sumibi-yakiniku.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/sumibi-yakiniku.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Sumibi Yakiniku.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The restaurant, its people, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.

== Forms ==

The letter in the footer and the Newsletter page use the shared form slot (inc/shippress-forms.php). Without a form plugin they show a real email field and a submit button that opens the visitor's email app with the address; the notice says plainly that the visitor is not subscribed until that message is sent. MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP are used when active. The Contact and Reserve pages show the first Contact Form 7 or WPForms form when one exists, and a mailto button otherwise.
