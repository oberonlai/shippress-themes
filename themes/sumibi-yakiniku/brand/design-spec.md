- Room: **64 seats** — **18 table grills** in the main room, a **10-seat grill counter**, two private rooms (**Hearth Room, 8 guests**; **Long Room, 12 guests**) and the **Upper Floor, up to 24 guests** (whole-floor booking).

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://kintan.restaurant/)
- **What it draws:** a bright candid photograph seen at table height — a round timber table with a built-in steel grill grate in the middle; one guest's arm (denim sleeve) reaches in with chopsticks, another hand lifts a slice of beef with steel tongs toward the empty grate; plates of cut meat, a wine bottle, a small sauce dish. The photo is a window (1181×900) between a 180px blush margin on the left (wordmark + nav) and a 79px blush strip on the right that carries a rotated tan label. A white square logo plate with a swoosh sits flush at the photo's lower-left corner; four white dots at the lower right.
- **Story:** yakiniku is shared and hands-on: friends around one grill in the city, each cooking for the others. The restaurant does not show itself — it shows the table.
- **Signature motion:** the window cross-fades through four table scenes (hold 5000ms, change 850ms, `cubic-bezier(.5,0,.14,1)`), dots follow; then on scroll the dark message band appears from the blush ground and section labels rise in the gutters.

### (2) Our translation (same table-height composition, same cross-fade, same image type: candid photograph — low-key per our row)
The same window at table height, but evening and low-key: **Rafael, our server, lays thin slices of morning-cut beef over a grill of glowing charcoal set into a dark timber table, while a guest across the table lifts a slice with steel tongs; small plates of marbled cuts, a sauce dish and two glasses catch the orange coal light; faces out of frame or soft.** Same grill-in-the-centre, hands-from-both-sides staging as the reference; the light comes from the coals instead of a window. The window cross-fades through four moments of the same evening (P01 server and tongs → P02 tongs turning a slice over the coals → P03 a group of six raising glasses over two grills → P04 Tomás setting a basket of glowing coals into a table at 17:00). The plate at the corner says **17:00 · coals lit** (our story: the beef was cut at nine, the fire starts at five). The row asks for `diagonal-split` + `top-bottom-halves`: the window's bottom edge is cut at 6°, and the blush lower half holds the H1. On scroll: the blush ground leads into the dark "five o'clock" band, exactly as the reference leads into its message band.
