Nohara Holiday Park is a calm, booking-led WordPress theme designed for touring caravan sites, campsites, and timber holiday lodges. Built around Newsreader serif typography, documentary film-grain photography, and an olive-toned palette, it presents touring pitches, lodge breaks, and seasonal wardens' work with understated clarity. Features full-bleed parallax bands, accessible booking rows, and responsive layouts that work seamlessly with or without WooCommerce.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download nohara-park.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/nohara-park.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Nohara Holiday Park.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the journal posts with their categories and tags, the menu, and sets the home page and the posts page. Pages you have already edited are left alone.

Forms: the newsletter sections use a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active it shows your form; without a plugin it shows an email field and a button that opens your email app, with a notice that you are not subscribed until you send the message.

The park, its people, its address, telephone numbers and prices are fictional.
