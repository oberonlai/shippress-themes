# Nohara Holiday Park

A booking-led block theme for a holiday park and touring caravan site: a film-grain photograph left of the first screen, a three-column stay ledger under a straddling title, a sticky narrative, caption-panel cards, ruled booking rows and one olive booking pill with a "book direct" disc.

- Fonts: Newsreader (headings), Nunito Sans (text).
- Home patterns: `front-hero`, `front-ledger`, `front-story`, `front-stays`, `front-reserve`, `front-grounds`, `front-season`, `front-prices`, `front-journal`, `front-book`.
- Scroll effects: `assets/sp-scroll.css` and `assets/sp-scroll.js` (reveal, stagger, parallax). Forms: `inc/shippress-forms.php`.
- Works without WooCommerce. Sample content, people, prices and address are fictional.
