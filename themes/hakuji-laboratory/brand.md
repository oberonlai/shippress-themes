# Hakuji Laboratory: brand notes

## Name and place
Hakuji Laboratory is a small dental laboratory in Westmere (fictional). Address: Unit 3, 18 Alder Yard, Westmere. Phone (010) 555 0142. Email cases@hakuji-lab.example.

## Founded
Opened in 2009 by Ilse Marchetti, a ceramist who wanted a lab small enough that the technician who approves a design is the one who finishes the crown.

## Audience
Dentists and practice managers within courier reach who send scans and prescriptions and want a quiet, predictable lab; their patients meet us only in the shade room.

## Services
Six case types: single crowns, bridges up to six units, veneers, inlays and onlays, implant crowns (screw-retained or cemented) and night guards. Not accepted: full-arch implant bars, orthodontic appliances, full dentures. Scan formats: STL, PLY, OBJ, DCM; impressions come by courier.

## Process
01 Open an account (confirmed within one working day). 02 Export the scan and fill the prescription. 03 Upload via the case link or email, or book a pick-up. 04 Approve the design preview; we mill, finish and send the case back by courier. Design preview within 2 working days.

## Turnaround (working days)
Single crown 5, bridge 7, veneers 6, implant crown 8, night guard 4.

## Fees (per unit, before tax, EUR)
Monolithic zirconia crown 95; layered zirconia crown 145; lithium disilicate crown or veneer 125; zirconia bridge per unit 90; inlay or onlay 110; implant crown 210; night guard 70; shade visit 35. Remakes for fit within 30 days at no charge (a policy, not a guarantee). Monthly invoicing.

## Hours and a day at the lab
Mon to Fri 07:30 to 18:00; Sat 09:00 to 13:00 (shade visits by appointment); Sun closed. The daily routine: 07:30 the bench opens, 10:30 courier pick-up, 13:00 design previews are sent, 16:00 second pick-up, 18:00 close.

## Tone
Quiet, precise, practice-to-lab. Short sentences, no hype. No guaranteed results, no awards, no media mentions, no real brands.
