# Kouhi Service — brand

## Name
The business is called Kouhi Service (kouhi is the Japanese word for coffee); the wordmark is set in two words, with a capital on each.

## History
Kouhi Service was founded in 2014 by roaster Mara Lindqvist and service lead Tomas Okafor at 14 Harbour Row, Northgate. Having spent years working around commercial espresso equipment, they noticed that local workplaces were consistently frustrated by complicated machine leases, wasteful capsule systems, and poor office coffee. They established a simpler model focused entirely on placing dependable countertop bean-to-cup machines and delivering freshly roasted whole beans.

From our workshop in Northgate, the operation expanded across neighbouring business communities, providing regular service routes throughout Northgate, Riverside, Old Mill, and Eastbank. Rather than treating maintenance as an afterthought, Mara and Tomas built the service around routine monthly care. Technicians visit every office counter to clean brew groups, descale water lines, test temperatures, and recalibrate grinders so equipment runs quietly and consistently.

Today, our team of seven manages equipment installations, bean roasting, and prompt customer support across all our service areas. Every workplace begins with a free forty-five-minute tasting visit before committing, and every installation is backed by our two working day repair or machine swap standard. We remain dedicated to providing honest, dependable coffee that lives seamlessly at work.

## Audience
Facilities managers, studio leads, clinic managers and office managers in Northgate, Riverside, Old Mill and Eastbank who want dependable coffee at work without a long equipment lease, and the colleagues who book and meet the technician.
