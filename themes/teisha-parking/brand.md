# Teisha Parking - brand brief

Commercial car-park operator leasing monthly bays in six town car parks. Landing theme: one long home page, an anchor menu and one repeated call to action, "Ask for a bay". Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Teisha Parking ("teisha" is the Japanese word for stopping a vehicle; the firm is otherwise international and modern).
- **Location:** office at 18 Canal Street, Northgate (fictional town; only the address format is localised).
- **Founded:** 2009 by Mara Lindqvist, who still walks every site with each new driver.
- **Audience:** people who park in town every day: commuters, shift workers, market traders, small businesses with vans, and electric-car drivers who want a fixed bay.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, unhurried, and precise, speaking like an experienced local car park operator who values quiet dependability. It avoids marketing hyperbole and technical jargon, presenting pricing, terms, and site details in clear, natural English. Say what the contract covers and what it does not, give every price, and do not add awards, press or promises the car park has not earned.
- **Hours:** Office: Mon–Sat 9:00–18:00 (closed Sun & holidays); Phone line: Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00; Sites: 24 hours.

## Story
Founded in 2009 at 18 Canal Street in Northgate, Teisha Parking began with a simple observation: daily parking in town was noisy, uncertain, and needlessly stressful. Rather than managing transient hourly meters or ticket barriers, we focused entirely on reserved monthly bays for people living and working in the community. Starting from our original Canal Street lot, we set out to create clean, well-lit spaces where drivers would always know their vehicle had an undisturbed home waiting for them.

Over the years, our operations expanded steadily across Northgate to encompass six distinct locations: Canal Street, Market Hall, Northgate Station, Orchard Yard, Riverside Depot, and Library Square. Today, our network includes 412 monthly bays ranging from covered commuter bays and compact motorcycle spaces to dedicated electric vehicle charging points. Every site remains open twenty-four hours a day, maintained with regular lighting inspections, barrier upkeep, and quiet on-site stewardship.

Despite our growth, our guiding philosophy remains unchanged. Founder Mara Lindqvist walks every site on foot with prospective drivers before agreements are signed, ensuring the space fits their vehicle, sightlines, and daily schedule. With clear monthly fees, a refundable thirty euro key fob deposit, thirty-day cancellation terms, and replies to every inquiry within two working days, we provide dependable parking built on quiet clarity and mutual trust.

## One day at the car park (the daily routine of a bay)
A driver arrives at dusk, lifts the barrier with the fob and drives to bay 14. The evening lights are already on; the night attendant at the Canal Street booth nods through the glass. In the morning the same bay is empty and waiting. That daily routine, the same bay every day, is what a monthly contract buys. Before it starts, the driver and Mara Lindqvist walk the site for twenty minutes and agree the exact bay.

## Process (4 steps)
1. Ask for a bay: Submit the single application form with your preferred site, vehicle details, and start month.
2. Reply within 2 working days: Receive a clear response outlining currently available bays matching your vehicle.
3. Walk the site together: Meet us for a twenty-minute walk through the car park to inspect and choose your exact bay.
4. Sign and start: Complete the agreement, pay the first month and thirty euro fob deposit, and begin parking on the 1st or 15th.
