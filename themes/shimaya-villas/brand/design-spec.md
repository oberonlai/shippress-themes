## 0. Fixed brand facts (identical on every page and in all five languages)

- **Shimaya Villas**: nine holiday houses on **Lune Cay**, a small fictional island 40 minutes by boat from the fictional harbour town of **Port Avel**. Founded **2011** by the siblings **Ana and Theo Shimaya**, who started by letting their grandmother's two houses and built the rest themselves. **They sell every night themselves, with no booking site in between. The people who clean, stock and keep each house are the people you write to.**
- Guest desk: **The Jetty House, Lune Cay** (fictional; local address order per language in brand.md). Phone **+00 30 555 0142** (fictional). Email `stay@shimayavillas.example`. This is the single address used in every mailto, form fallback and footer.
- Hours: **Guest desk daily 08:00–20:00 (island time)**. Check-in from **14:00**. Check-out **11:00**, or **16:00 when booked direct**. Season **1 April – 31 October 2027**. The houses are closed November–March for repairs ("the reef rests").
- Boats (Kwame's boat, 40 minutes): **Port Avel → Lune Cay 10:00, 13:00, 16:00**; **Lune Cay → Port Avel 09:15, 12:15, 16:15**, daily in season.
- **People (7, fictional):**

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://soneva.com/soneva-fushi/)
- **What it draws:** a cream header with a centred thin wordmark and place label, a centred row of links, a rust ribbon above it and a rust "Book" button at the right. Under it is one **film-grain photograph** (a looping film: dappled jungle canopy, then a woman's face in leaf light, then barefoot paths) filling the width. A **dark translucent booking bar** (Dates | Guests | square tile) is docked at the bottom centre of the photograph.
- **Story:** "the island sells its own stay". You arrive barefoot and unhurried, and the booking is one bar away, from the island itself, not through someone else.
- **Signature motion:** the film loops slowly, and the header slides in (.9s cubic-bezier(.33,1,.45,1)). On scroll the ribbon and top padding collapse, and the photograph gives way to a centred statement that fades in, followed by villa cards that rise and stagger (1.25s, 240ms).
- Aman (secondary) contributes the frame: the same kind of single place photograph, inset 25px inside a warm page with hairline header rows.

### (2) Our translation (same composition idea, same motion idea, same image type: documentary photograph, ours film-grain)
**One film-grain photograph, late morning, taken from inside the villa's shaded living room looking out. On the right half, Rosa (head housekeeper, woman in her 40s, sage-grey linen uniform, dark hair tied back) slides open a tall glass-and-timber door with both hands. Through the opening is a terrace with a small plunge pool and, beyond it, a calm grey-teal sea under a pale sky. On the terrace a couple in their 30s (one carrying a canvas holdall, one setting a soft suitcase down) has just arrived, half-turned to the sea. The left 45% of the frame is the shaded room interior (lime-washed wall, a low timber bench) and the pool's edge, dim and quiet: that is where our solid panel sits.** This is Soneva's "the island opens itself to you" turned into the moment this company sells: the person who keeps the house opens it to you, with no one in between. The headline says it: **"Book the house from the people who keep it."** Soneva's dark booking bar becomes the **Hold dates** bar on the window's foot. The rust ribbon becomes the sage-green "Direct only" ribbon naming what direct adds. Motion: Soneva's slow loop becomes a slow **parallax of the photograph inside the fixed frame** (sp-parallax); the header collapses as Soneva's does; the statement after the first screen unfades; the house cards rise and stagger.

## 7. Motion and scroll effects (sp-scroll v1, assets copied unchanged)

Theme overrides in style.css `:root`: `--sp-dur: 1.25s; --sp-ease: cubic-bezier(.33,1,.45,1); --sp-dist: 24px;` (RT(g) card rise 1.25s with the same curve; RT's 20% rise is capped to 24px by the ≤30px rule). The stagger step is set to RT's **240ms** with a theme rule that only sets the variable: `.sv-stagger-240 > .sp-reveal:nth-child(2){--sp-delay:.24s} … (3) .48s, (4) .72s, (5) .96s, (n+6) 1.2s` on the same parent that has `sp-stagger`. Three effects, no more:

| Effect | sp-* class | Where | Reference source and its trigger / timing / easing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parallax | `sp-parallax` | **Home H1 framed window** (cover with P01; panel and bar are foreground, outside the moving layer) `--sp-par: 8%` ≥782px / **5% ≤781px (kept on mobile)**; **Home H3 morning band** (cover with P02, caption foreground) `--sp-par: 10%` / 6%; inner windows `--sp-par: 6%` / 4% | Soneva's hero is a slow looping film of the place and the photo bands drift under Lenis smooth scroll (1.2s); ours keeps that "the place moves slower than the page" feeling with CSS scroll-driven `view()` (sp-scroll), IO + translate3d fallback, no scroll listener |
| Reveal once (rise + fade) | `sp-reveal sp-reveal--up` | H2 statement (`--sp-dist: 8px`, RT "unfade") and paragraphs; H4 cards; H6 rows; H7 cards; H8 quote; H9 heading group; H10 query group; inner pages: house sections, people rows, story text, journal cards | Soneva block-inview.js: IntersectionObserver, class added at 25% in view, CSS animation 1.25s cubic-bezier(.33,1,.45,1), rise 20% → ours 1.25s, same curve, 24px; statement: IO rootMargin −40px, threshold .1 → sp-reveal |
