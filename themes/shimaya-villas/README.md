# Shimaya Villas

Landing theme for an island villa company that sells its own nights without a booking site in between. Home: a centred two-row header under a coloured promo ribbon, a framed photograph window with the headline on a solid panel and a dark glass hold-dates bar docked on its foot, a centred statement, a full-width morning band, three house cards, a grey-out season list that swaps a small portrait and a large photograph, hairline rows of what direct adds, arrival cards, hosts, one way to hold dates and the journal.

- Header menu: The houses, Season & rates, Arrival, Journal, About, Contact (real pages); the Hold dates button jumps to the hold block.
- Fonts: Manrope (display) and Figtree (text), both OFL-1.1, bundled as Latin variable subsets.
- Scroll effects: sp-parallax on the hero window and the morning band, sp-reveal with a 240 ms stagger; all off under reduced motion.
- Forms: contact and newsletter use the shared `shippress_form_slot` block with a mailto fallback when no form plugin is active.
- Brand and design notes: `brand.md` and `brand/`.

## Status
The English home page and the starter inner pages are in place. The zh-TW, ja, fr and de localisations and the fuller inner pages (framed openers, rate tables, contact form) follow.
