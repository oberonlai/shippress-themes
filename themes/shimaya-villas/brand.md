# Shimaya Villas: brand

## Name, place, founding
- **Name:** Shimaya Villas. Nine holiday houses on Lune Cay, a small fictional island 40 minutes by boat from the fictional harbour town of Port Avel, sold directly by the family that keeps them.
- **Location:** The Jetty House, Lune Cay (fictional, international-neutral). Phone +00 30 555 0142 (fictional). Email stay@shimayavillas.example.
- **Founded:** 2011 by the siblings Ana and Theo Shimaya, who started by letting their grandmother's two houses and built the rest themselves. Seven people keep the houses. There is no booking site in between: the people who clean, stock and keep each house are the people you write to.
- **Hours:** Guest desk daily 08:00-20:00 (island time). Check-in from 14:00. Check-out 11:00, or 16:00 when booked direct. Season 1 April - 31 October 2027; the houses are closed November-March for repairs ("the reef rests").
- **Boats (Kwame's boat, 40 minutes):** Port Avel to Lune Cay 10:00, 13:00, 16:00; Lune Cay to Port Avel 09:15, 12:15, 16:15, daily in season.

## Audience
Couples, families and small groups who want an island holiday arranged by the people who run it, and who would rather write to one person than compare booking sites. For the theme: island and resort villa operators running direct-booking campaigns against OTA commission.

## Voice
Calm, honest and unhurried, like an island house kept by a family. Every note rests on a specific, practical fact: a boat time, a price, a name. No superlatives, no invented awards, no urgency tricks, no "best price" claims.

## Story
In 2011 the siblings Ana and Theo Shimaya began welcoming guests to Lune Cay by letting their grandmother's two houses, preparing the rooms and meeting every boat themselves. Theo then built the rest by hand, including the two Hill Houses over the north point, until there were nine. Every winter, from November to March, the houses close for timber and garden repairs while the reef rests.

From the start they chose to let every night directly, refusing booking sites and commissions. The money that would have gone to an agency pays for the boat both ways, a stocked kitchen, a 16:00 check-out and wages through the closed months.

## A day on Lune Cay
The daily routine: bread comes out of the island oven at 07:00 and Rosa walks it round to the Hill Houses at 07:40; the desk at the Jetty House opens at 08:00; the boats leave Port Avel at 10:00, 13:00 and 16:00 and return from Lune Cay at 09:15, 12:15 and 16:15; check-in is from 14:00; the reef is at its lowest in the late afternoon; the desk closes at 20:00 and Rosa is on the island all night.

## How a hold works
1. Send a hold request (dates, house, guests): the single way in. 2. Ana replies within 12 hours and holds the dates for 72 hours, with no card. 3. Pay the 30% deposit by bank transfer or a card link; the balance is due 30 days before arrival. 4. Rosa writes 7 days before you arrive to ask for your boat time and your kitchen list.
