# Asayake Lodge

A calm block theme for a small guest house that hosts families and carers of hospital patients.

- **Home:** a dusk-rose field around an inset, rounded film-grain photograph with a docked four-cell bar and one slow shaft of dawn light; the headline sits below the photo with a day-line and three cards hung from its ticks. Then a notes card, a sticky photo beside five hour rows, three room cards, a parallax night corridor, who the stay is for, a rate list, a tilted key-card band and house letters.
- **Type:** Fraunces (headings, hours, prices) and Karla (body, navigation, labels), both OFL-1.1 and bundled.
- **Motion:** quiet sp-scroll effects (reveal, count-up, parallax); all of it respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and works without JavaScript.
- **Photography:** generated film-grain stills of the house, bright morning light, no cut-outs.
- **Pages:** home, about, rooms, a day, rates, getting here, questions, who the stay is for, house rules, book, house letter and the letters archive.
