# Asayake Lodge: brand

## Name, place, founding
- **Name:** Asayake Lodge ("asayake": the glow of the sky at sunrise). A small, non-clinical guest house for families and carers of patients at Calder Teaching Hospital (fictional).
- **Location:** 11 Linden Row, Calder, Harrowfield, a fictional, international-neutral address. 9 rooms and 22 beds, a 6-minute walk (450 m) from the hospital's North Ward Gate.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Ines Moreau-Achebe, whose father spent eleven weeks on a cardiac ward.
- **Line:** A bed six minutes from the ward.

## Story
In 2014, Ines Moreau-Achebe founded Asayake Lodge following an eleven-week stay on a cardiac ward at Calder Teaching Hospital with her father. Throughout that difficult period, she experienced firsthand the exhausting reality faced by carers: trying to rest in hospital chairs, sleeping in car parks, and having nowhere quiet to make a simple hot drink or regroup before morning ward rounds began.

Determined to create a sanctuary for others in similar circumstances, she opened Asayake Lodge just 450 metres from the hospital's North Ward Gate. Taking its name from the Japanese word for the gentle glow of the sky at sunrise, the lodge was established as a non-clinical guest house designed entirely around the emotional and practical needs of visiting families and carers.

Today, the lodge provides 9 rooms and 22 beds at 11 Linden Row in Calder, offering a warm, quiet haven a mere six-minute walk from the wards. With a shared kitchen opening at 04:50 and dedicated quiet hours throughout the day, the house remains a dependable, comforting presence where relatives can rest, recharge, and stay close to those they love.

## Audience
Parents of children on the ward, partners and carers on long stays, and families arriving from far away. They are tired, often arriving early or late, and need a clean bed, a kitchen and quiet, not a sales pitch. Hospital social workers also send referral letters.

## Tone
The tone is calm, dependable, and understated, speaking with practical warmth to families facing demanding circumstances. It prioritises clarity, everyday routine, and quiet dignity without resorting to clinical jargon, emotional excess, or commercial slogans. The house never promises outcomes, never makes medical claims and never claims guaranteed availability.

## Rooms, steps and rates

- **Family room (up to 4: double + bunk, own bathroom)**: €78 (Per night. Includes VAT, bedding, towels, kitchen use, and Wi-Fi.)
- **Accessible room (step-free, roll-in shower, 2 beds)**: €62 (Per night. Step-free with roll-in shower. Includes VAT, bedding, towels, kitchen use, and Wi-Fi.)
- **Long stay — 7 nights or more, any room**: 20 % off every night (Discount applied across any room type for stays of 7 nights or longer.)
- **Laundry token (wash + dry)**: €3 (Includes one complete wash and dry cycle.)
