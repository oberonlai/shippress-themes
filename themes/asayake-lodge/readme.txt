== Description ==

Asayake Lodge is a calm, accessible WordPress theme designed for hospital guest houses and patient carer accommodation. Built around a day-schedule structure with sticky media, serif typography, and muted dawn tones, it offers clear booking flows, room overviews, and transparent rate schedules. Crafted to provide a dependable, non-clinical web presence for families supporting loved ones during hospital stays.

The home page opens on a dusk-rose field around an inset rounded photograph with a docked bar of four facts and one slow shaft of dawn light, with the headline below the photo and a day-line whose ticks carry three cards. It continues with a bordered notes card, a sticky photo stack beside five hour rows, three room cards, a parallax night corridor, who the stay is for, a full-width rate list, a tilted key-card band, house letters and a footer with the house-letter sign-up. Contact and newsletter forms use the optional ShipPress form slot and need no plugin.
