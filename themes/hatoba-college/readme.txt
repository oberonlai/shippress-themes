Hatoba College is a serif editorial theme for a maritime training college founded in 1987 at Pier 3, Port Ellery. The front page opens with a framed harbour photograph, then follows a twelve-week course from shore school to a berth confirmed in writing. It is set in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

Fonts: Newsreader (headings), Source Serif 4 (reading text), Noto Serif TC and Noto Serif JP (Traditional Chinese and Japanese headings); all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

== Installation ==

1. Download hatoba-college.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/hatoba-college.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Hatoba College.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with their text and images, three journal entries with two categories and three tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Optional forms: the newsletter section uses a small dynamic block. With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active, their form is shown; without a plugin it shows an email field that opens your mail app (you are not subscribed until you send the request).

Scroll effects use the bundled sp-scroll files (assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js); they respect prefers-reduced-motion.

The college, its people, places and prices are fictional.
