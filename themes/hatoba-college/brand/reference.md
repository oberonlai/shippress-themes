# Reference - Hatoba College

- Reference: https://web.aiu.ac.jp/ (S5-Style 11591) - quiet university top page: photograph-only slider window, serif Latin section words, numbered feature bars.
- Secondary: https://boat-osaka.com/osaka-river-loop/ (S5-Style 11084) - harbour boat service: photo window beside a large date block with a pill tag.
- IMAGERY: photo (film-grain, documentary). The drawn boat stickers of the secondary are not carried over.
- Dropped earlier candidates (colour or fit): CG-blob lab site, neon WebGL academy site.

## Hand candidates
1. T1 Docked caption bar + pager notch on a framed slider window (AIU first screen). Hard-edged window under the header with a solid caption bar on its lower-left and a white pager notch (dots, arrows, pause) at the lower-right.
2. T2 Enlarged-initial serif section words over a hairline with a short underline segment (AIU AIU TOPICS / FEATURE / NEWS).
3. T3 Stacked numbered "Feature" bars with a coloured serif label and soft shadow (AIU FEATURE) - ours: "Stage 1..5" bars with weeks, cadet action, college action.
4. T4 (secondary) Pill tag + big stacked date lines beside the photo (ORL "Flight date") - ours: "Berth confirmed" tag + dates.

## Colour relation (AIU)
Cool grey base + near-black bars + one small accent; ours: base #EEF1F3, navy #3F5260 as the main, mist #8FA3AE, ink #172028, brass accent #A9803F (<3%).
