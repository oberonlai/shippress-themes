The Weight of Pale Light

12 September – 9 November 2026 Daichi Morimoto Paintings, 2024–2026

Eleven new paintings by Daichi Morimoto, made over two winters in a north-facing studio. Each canvas is built from thin layers of grey, bone and dust-blue until the colour seems to sit just behind the surface, like light on a wall at four in the afternoon. Stand close, then step back: the paintings change as the room does.
