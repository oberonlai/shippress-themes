Covered

Every umbrella we make: bent or broken ribs, loose stitching at the tips, a stiff runner or a worn ferrule cap. Free in the first two years, at cost after that, for as long as the frame is sound.

Turnaround

Most repairs take five to ten days. Paper umbrellas and full re-covers take three to four weeks; we lend you an umbrella in the meantime.

Prices

New rib or tip $12–18 · runner or spring $20–30 · new ferrule $18 · re-oiling a paper canopy $40 · full re-cover from $60 · umbrellas by other makers quoted first.

Bring it in

Come by the shop with the umbrella dry and furled, or post it in its sleeve with a note of what happened. We write back with a price before we touch it.
