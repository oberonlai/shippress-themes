# Kanjo College - brand brief

Accountancy training college for adults in Halden. Sample content in English first; Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kanjo College ("kanjo" is the Japanese word for an account or a count; the college itself is international and modern).
- **Location:** 14 Corder Street, Northgate, Halden (fictional city). USD prices in every language.
- **Founded:** 2009, by Principal Helena Marsh.
- **Audience:** working adults who want to learn bookkeeping and accountancy papers one at a time: career changers, bookkeepers moving up, people returning to work.
- **Tone:** The voice of Kanjo College is calm, precise, and practical, addressing adult learners with professional respect and unvarnished clarity. It avoids academic jargon and marketing exaggeration, grounding every statement in working routines, ledger mechanics, and transparent commitments.
- **Office hours:** Mon–Fri 09:00–20:30, Sat 09:00–13:00, closed Sun.

## History
Kanjo College was founded in 2009 at 14 Corder Street in Northgate, Halden, by Principal Helena Marsh. Beginning with two evening classes in foundational bookkeeping, the college was established to offer working adults structured, disciplined accounting tuition paced around employment commitments. From its first term, teaching emphasized manual ledger mechanics, physical exercise sheets, and clear arithmetic principles.

In 2014, the college expanded its academic timetable to introduce daytime classes on Mondays and Wednesdays alongside its established Tuesday and Thursday evening terms. This expansion allowed career changers, parents, and self-employed professionals to complete the thirty-six-hour paper syllabus in cohorts strictly capped at sixteen learners, preserving individual attention and direct dialogue with lecturers.

By 2021, Kanjo College instituted a structured mock paper framework across all four core papers, establishing a week-six practice sitting and a week-nine full rehearsal for every twelve-week term. Today, the college continues to deliver calm, practical accountancy education across spring, summer, and autumn terms, preparing adult students for professional financial papers without marketing hype or inflated claims.

## A day at the college (daily routine)
Day classes meet Monday and Wednesday 09:30-15:30; evening classes Tuesday and Thursday 18:30-21:00. A daily routine in class: a short ledger warm-up on paper, the lecturer's worked example on the whiteboard, a printed exercise in pairs, then questions. Study rooms stay open until 20:30 on weekdays. Classes never exceed 16 learners.

## Taking a place
1. Placement chat: Free 30-minute consultation at our Northgate college or online to discuss your background and goals.
2. Placement exercise: 45-minute diagnostic exercise completed at college or online to evaluate numerical and bookkeeping skills.
3. Choose papers and timetable: Select your paper and choose between day classes on Mondays and Wednesdays or evening classes on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
4. Pay deposit and enrol: Secure your seat in a class capped at 16 with a $150 deposit that counts toward your tuition.

## Term route (bar, home pacing row, term page)
Enrol, Weeks 1-4, Mock paper, Exam week, Results. Two mock papers (week 6 practice, week 9 full mock); exam week is week 12; results six weeks later. Terms: Spring from 12 January, Summer from 20 April, Autumn from 14 September.
