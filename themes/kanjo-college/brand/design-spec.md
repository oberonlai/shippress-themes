# Kanjo College: design spec (stage B, binding for implementation)

Slug `kanjo-college`, accountancy training college for adults (type `education`, no WooCommerce needed; declare compatibility only). Design row b23-3: archetype single-column-longform, hero framed-window, nav top-bar, first screen timeline-line, page structure process-timeline, palette slate (ai-nezumi, indigo mouse grey #5C6A72), font direction **sans**.

References (see `reference.md`, `reference-tokens.md`, `ref/*.png`):
- Main: Mejiro "Mejinavi 2026" admissions microsite, https://www.mejiro.ac.jp/univ/mejinavi2026/
- Secondary: Mito / Hachimonji school group 70th anniversary site, https://www.mito.ac.jp/70th-anniversary/

Borrow structure, technique, colour relationships, motion and the key visual concept. Never copy their images, illustrations, copy, characters or logos. All photos are generated by us; all line-art is drawn by us as SVG.

Fixed facts (every page, all five languages; see section 8): Kanjo College, founded **2009**, **14 Corder Street, Northgate, Halden** (fictional city). Principal **Helena Marsh**. Currency **USD** in every language.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen, story, signature motion
Mejiro's first screen is a flat line-art landscape: outline mountains and clouds on cream, green hills with pines, a winding path leading down to a signpost reading "another path" (with the university name), a hiker in a sun hat reading a map at the path start, a student lying with a laptop, a kneeling photographer, a deer. Over it sits a giant rounded two-line wordmark + year. Story: "choose your own path through the university". The path returns as the fixed route bar whose marker walks to a goal flag as you scroll. Motion: on load the wordmark and figures rise in (GSAP, ~1s, circ.out), the winding path leads the eye down, and while scrolling the marker walks along the bottom bar. Mito adds the framed rounded window with drifting stickers (Lenis parallax, cubic-bezier(0.2,0.6,0.35,1)).

### (2) Our translated scene (accountancy college, same composition, same image type: real photo + flat line-art)
"Rule your own line." A slate-mist ground carries our line-art: thin horizontal ledger rules run across the whole first screen behind everything; below the window one rule leaves the grid, bends and winds down to a small outline signpost with three arms (Papers / Pace / Places). The rounded window (T1) holds the documentary photograph that plays the "hiker with a map": a lecturer ruling a ledger line across a whiteboard while two adult students at a table compare a printed exercise (P01). Left of the window: the two-line H1 with a chip, the subtitle and two links. At the bottom the route bar (T3) starts at "Enrol"; as you scroll, the pencil marker walks to "Results" and the flag. Same composition: figure scene in the middle, path leading down to a signpost, title large above-left, bar at the foot.

## 3. Colour

Source: Mito secondary ground/sky values (#EFF6F8 / #E4F1F4) are within 5% lightness of our ground/band; Mejiro's structure (pale ground ~60%, one band ~25%, one display colour ~5%, dark sub-pill) kept, but Mejiro green #5EB473 and red-orange #E8543C are swapped to the design row's required slate #5C6A72 and a muted ledger red. Reason: the row fixes palette ai-nezumi slate and quality gate caps saturated share at 15–30%; Mejiro's first screen is ~35% saturated, which fails the gate, and a grass-green college reads as a nature brand, not accountancy.

| role | hex | share | source token |
|---|---|---|---|
| ground (base) | `#EEF2F3` | ~60% | mito html `#EFF6F8` (−1% L) |
| band (cards panel, pacing) | `#DDE4E7` | ~15% | mito sky `#E4F1F4` (−3% L), mejiro band role |
| main slate (fills, route fill, close band) | `#5C6A72` | ~15% | design row hex; mejiro band role |
| ink (text, outlines, chips) | `#1F2A30` | ~7% | mejiro text #000 / mito #353535 → slate-ink |
| accent ledger red (margin line, flag, pencil tip, price rule) | `#B4533C` | ≤ 3% | mejiro display #E8543C, darkened for quiet palette |
| card tints | `#DDE4E7` `#E6E1DA` `#D9E3E0` `#E3E6EC` | in cards | mejiro pink/teal/peach/lime role |
| white panel | `#F7F9F9` | small | mito card white 92% |

First-screen saturated share: target ≤ 8%, hard cap 15% (`scripts/check-first-screen.py`). Only the photo, the red margin line and the flag carry saturation. No slate fill blocks larger than the header button on the first screen. Contrast: ink on ground 13:1; white on slate 5.6:1 (body text OK).

theme.json palette slugs: `base #EEF2F3`, `band #DDE4E7`, `slate #5C6A72`, `ink #1F2A30`, `accent #B4533C`, `panel #F7F9F9`, `tint-1..4`.
