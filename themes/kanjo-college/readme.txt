Kanjo College is an accountancy training college WordPress theme designed for adult education and professional studies. Built around structured process timelines, framed hero windows, and ledger-inspired editorial typography, it presents course papers, schedules, and tuition fees with clarity and restraint. It supports day and evening term timetables, full-width pricing ledgers, and responsive course progression bars.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download kanjo-college.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kanjo-college.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kanjo College.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, three news posts with categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. Scroll effects (assets/sp-scroll.css and sp-scroll.js) switch themselves off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

The college, its people, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
