Yunagi Jazz is an open-air jazz festival WordPress theme crafted for summer weekend events on the water. Featuring low-key dusk photography, paper lanterns, and an unhurried day-schedule layout, it presents artist lineups, hour-by-hour schedules, and direct box office ticketing with calm typography and warm restraint.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The home page opens on three equal rounded low-key photographs on an ink mat with the headline laid over their bottom edge, a row of set cards (a small photograph, a time chip and the act) that drifts left and then stays pinned behind the page, and a plum quarter-disc date badge fixed in the bottom-right corner. White rounded sheets rise over the pinned strip: the weekend in three paragraphs, Saturday and Sunday as hour-by-hour rows with a lantern row, what every ticket includes, a full-width parallax photograph of the lawn at dusk, the crew as ruled rows, one dark sheet with the single way to buy tickets, and the journal. Scroll effects (rise, stagger, parallax, drift) use the small sp-scroll script in assets/ and respect prefers-reduced-motion. The optional newsletter and contact slots work without any plugin and use Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when present.

== Installation ==

1. Download yunagi-jazz.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/yunagi-jazz.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Yunagi Jazz. On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): the home page, the journal with sample posts and the menu. Use Tools > Import demo content to run it again.
4. Edit everything in the block editor; the sections are block patterns in the "Yunagi Jazz" category.

The festival, its people, addresses, telephone number, dates and prices are fictional.

== Forms ==

Contact and ticket requests: without a plugin the page shows a button that opens your email app. The lineup letter (footer and newsletter page) shows an email field and a button that opens your email app with the request; you are not subscribed until the request is sent.
