## 1. Signature techniques (three plus one support; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

### T1. Three rounded photo columns on a stage, headline over their bottom edge (Commons first screen, RT(c)(d)(e))
- Reference: https://fes.common-s.org/ — under a thin white header, three big rounded slides (r48) fill the width from y96 to y544 (448 high); the centre one is the largest, the neighbours run to the viewport edges; the page ground around them is a cool pale grey (#EDEFF0) with a white-to-grey gradient under the header. The slides are the whole first impression.
- Ours (home H1): an **ink stage mat** `#1B1220`, radius **48px** (RT slides r48), x 40–1400 at 1440 (1360 wide), **y 128–648 (520 high)**, padding 12px, holding **three EQUAL columns (each 437px wide), gap 12px**, each photo **radius 40px** (48 − 8 nesting). The centre column is "raised": it starts at the mat top (y 140) while the two side columns start **32px lower** (y 172), so the dark mat shows above them like a stage silhouette (our translation of "centre larger"). All three end at y 636. A dusk gradient overlay (non-moving, `linear-gradient(to top, rgba(27,18,32,.88) 0%, rgba(27,18,32,0) 55%)`) covers the lower 55% of the mat; the H1 sits on it across the bottom of columns 1–2 (§2, §3).
- Not to dilute: three columns stay exactly equal in width; radius stays 40/48 (never squared); the photos stay low-key and dark; the H1 stays **overlaid at the bottom** of the photos (overlay-bottom), never moved above them on desktop; the mat never becomes full-bleed (40px pale ground stays visible left and right).
- Continues to inner pages (all 9 inner pages + journal + archives): **T1-mini** page header: same mat (r48, padding 12, gap 12, 1360 wide), **320px high**, three equal r40 columns (side columns 24px lower), page H1 Bodoni 3.5rem white overlaid at the bottom of columns 1–2 with the same gradient. Photos per page in §4.

### T2. Fixed quarter-disc date badge, bottom right (Commons badge, RT(f))
- Reference: a fixed red shape at the bottom-right corner, 213px wide, top-left corner rounded as a quarter circle, holding big white date numerals (40px), a thin dotted divider and a white pill "venue" chip; it never moves while everything scrolls under it.
- Ours: `position: fixed; right: 0; bottom: 0;` **216 × 272px**, background **plum #460E44**, `border-top-left-radius: 216px 216px` (quarter disc on top of a short rectangle), z-index 50. Content right-aligned, padding 0 24px 24px: "2027" Karla 600 0.875rem ls .04em #FAF9FB; **"21–22"** Bodoni Moda 500 2.75rem/1 #FAF9FB; "August" Bodoni italic 1.5rem; a 1px dotted rule rgba(250,249,251,.5) 120px wide; a white pill chip (#FAF9FB, ink text, radius 999, 0.75rem Karla 600 ls .04em, padding 4px 12px) "Harbour Lawn". The whole badge is one link to /tickets/ (focus ring brass 2px offset 3px). It is the **only saturated area** of the first screen.
- Not to dilute: stays fixed, bottom-right, quarter-disc silhouette, plum, white numerals. Never a circle sticker, never top-right, never scrolling away.
- Continues to inner pages: **every page** (desktop ≥782px). Under 782px the badge is hidden and the dates appear in the hero eyebrow line instead (no fixed element over mobile text). Footer inner padding-right 240px at ≥782px so the badge never covers footer text.

### T3. Pinned photo strip with white rounded sheets rising over it, strip peeking at both edges (Commons scroll, RT(c)(g), `scroll-commons-900/1800.png`)
- Reference: the thumbnail strip (212×140 tiles, r20, gap 30) drifts left and then stays fixed at mid-screen; white sheets (r56–64, x 76–1364 at 1440) slide up over it, so the strip is visible only in the 76px margins left and right of each sheet, a constant reminder of the festival photos behind the page.

Wrapper uses no `overflow: hidden` (use `clip` only on the marquee itself). 80px vertical gap between sheets (pale ground shows between them).
- Not to dilute: the strip must actually stay pinned behind the sheets (CSS sticky, no JS), the sheets must be narrower than the strip so it peeks at both edges, sheets stay white with r56 and no shadow (RT(d): flat, separated by colour only).
- Continues to inner pages: **Lineup, Schedule, Tickets, The lawn** each have a `.yj-pin` wrapper: a strip of 4–6 r20 photos (sp-marquee) sticky at top 360px, with the page's main sheet(s) rising over it. Under 1100px: sticky off, strip in flow above the sheet.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion (https://fes.common-s.org/)
- **What it draws:** a pale cool page under a thin white header with a right-hand menu; three huge rounded slides across the screen (centre big, neighbours cut by the viewport edges); under them one row of small rounded photographs of the festival at night (lanterns, raised glasses, a girl in summer clothes, festival goods); a red quarter-disc date badge fixed in the bottom-right corner with the dates and the venue.
- **Story:** a neighbourhood night festival that you can step into: it has a date, a place and a crowd under lanterns; the photographs show people from behind or in profile, warm lantern light on near-black.
- **Signature motion:** the thumbnail strip drifts left forever (~20px/s, linear) and then holds still behind the page as white rounded sheets slide up over it; the date badge never moves; every box answers hover with a 0.3s `cubic-bezier(0.4,0.4,0,1)` transition.

### (2) Our translation (same three-slide composition turned into product-grid / overlay-bottom / card-row, same drift-and-pin motion, same image type: low-key documentary photography with people in profile or from behind)
An open-air jazz weekend on a harbour lawn at dusk. **Three equal rounded low-key photographs on an ink stage**: left, **a double bassist tuning on the lantern-lit stage** (P01, Søren Vik before the closing set); centre and raised, **the sound engineer checking a monitor at the mixing desk at dusk** (P02, Daniel Osei); right, **the lantern string over the lawn with the crowd on blankets in silhouette** (P03). The H1 is laid over the bottom of the photos on a dusk gradient. Under the stage, the **set cards**: six small rounded photographs of musicians each with its time chip and act name (the reference's thumbnail strip, now the weekend's hour-by-hour); they drift left and pin behind the white sheets as the page scrolls. The plum quarter-disc badge carries "21–22 August, Harbour Lawn". The story moves from the reference's "a festival has a date, a place and a crowd" to ours: **"one summer weekend, played out hour by hour on the harbour lawn, and one way to get in"**.
