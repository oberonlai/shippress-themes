# Kaimon Fishmongers

Kaimon Fishmongers is a calm, block-based WordPress theme built for independent fishmongers, coastal markets, and food shops. Designed with an editorial aesthetic of film-grain imagery, speckled ground tones, and ruled landing tables, it showcases daily harbour catches, cut-to-order services, and counter schedules. Fully responsive and WooCommerce-compatible, it offers structured patterns for boat rosters, price boards, and kitchen trim stories.

Home (stage 1): the hanging catch (five dark fish photographs at different heights and parallax speeds over an empty speckled page), a dark counter photograph with a white tab, the landing index with thumbnails flush to the right edge, cut-to-order steps, a horizontal day strip, kitchens, price board, journal and the Landing List form slot.

Patterns: `front-catch`, `front-dock`, `front-landings`, `front-cut`, `front-day`, `front-kitchens`, `front-board`, `front-journal`, `front-letter`.

Shop: eight sample fish and shellfish products (WooCommerce optional) with `archive-product` and `single-product` templates; without WooCommerce the shop pages are unused and orders go by phone, email or the order form.

Photography: the five hanging fish (P01-P05) are single-subject film-grain photographs on a plain slate ground.
