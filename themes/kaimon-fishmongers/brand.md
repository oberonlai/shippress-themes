# Kaimon Fishmongers - brand brief

Independent fishmonger at 4 Quay Row, Harrowmouth, a fictional harbour town. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kaimon Fishmongers. "Kaimon" is the old sea gate at the end of Quay Row, after which the shop is named.
- **Location:** 4 Quay Row, Harrowmouth (fictional). Telephone 0100 555 0409, email counter@kaimonfish.example. Prices in euro. The harbour bus stop is 3 minutes on foot, there is a bike rack, two loading bays for kitchens 11:30-12:30 and a step-free counter.
- **Founded:** 2009 by Maren Holt in a former net store.
- **Hours:** Tue-Fri 07:30-18:00, Sat 07:00-14:00, Sun and Mon closed (no boats land). Phone orders 07:30-10:00 for same-day cuts, pickup from 12:00. Last cut 17:30 (Saturday 13:30). We close early if the ice is bare.
- **Audience:** neighbours who cook at home and want to know which boat a fish came from, and the cooks of small local kitchens who take the trim.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, unhurried, and precise, speaking with the practical authority of a working harbour fishmonger. It focuses entirely on morning landings, counter knife craft, and daily kitchen cooking without promotional hype or embellishment.

## History
Kaimon Fishmongers was founded in 2009 by Maren Holt in a former net store at 4 Quay Row in Harrowmouth. Named after the historic sea gate standing at the end of the row, the shop was established with a singular focus: buying directly from local morning day boats and selling through every catch by evening.

In 2014, Kaimon began its regular kitchen trim runs to ensure that nothing from the quay went to waste, delivering fresh bones, heads, and trimming to local pots like the Linnet bistro. The counter's network of independent skippers expanded further in 2021 when the Saturday shell boat Kestrel joined the weekly landings, supplying hand-dived scallops and rope mussels.

Today, the rhythm of the shop remains tied to the tides and the five boats landing at Quay 2 from dawn. Whole fish rest on crushed ice and are filleted, scaled, or steaked while customers wait at the counter, with every remaining frame bagged for stock or shared with neighbourhood kitchens before the day is out.

## A day at the counter (the daily routine)
04:40 the first boat ties up at Quay 2. 05:15 buying on the quay. 06:30 the ice board is laid and the day's board written. 07:30 doors open. 10:00 phone orders close for same-day cuts. 12:00 the trim run leaves for three kitchens. 15:00 cut-to-order pickups. 18:00 sold through, the ice washed down (Saturday 14:00). The daily routine repeats Tuesday to Saturday; on Sunday and Monday no boats land and the shop is closed.

## Cut to order
1. Choose a whole fish on the ice
2. Tell us how you will cook it
3. We cut while you wait — about 6 minutes a fish
4. Wrapped in waxed paper; heads and frames bagged for stock if you want them (free)
