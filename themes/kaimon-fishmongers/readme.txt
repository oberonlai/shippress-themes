Kaimon Fishmongers is a calm, block-based WordPress theme built for independent fishmongers, coastal markets, and food shops. Designed with an editorial aesthetic of film-grain imagery, speckled ground tones, and ruled landing tables, it showcases daily harbour catches, cut-to-order services, and counter schedules. Fully responsive and WooCommerce-compatible, it offers structured patterns for boat rosters, price boards, and kitchen trim stories.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download kaimon-fishmongers.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kaimon-fishmongers.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kaimon Fishmongers.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, journal entries with a category and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Forms: the Landing List section uses a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP configured it shows that form; without a plugin it shows a real email field and a button that opens your email app with the address (nothing is subscribed until the email is sent).

The shop, its people, address, telephone number, boats and prices are fictional.
