# Neiro Music — brand

Neiro Music is a private music school at Lindenhof 14, 9000 Ghent, Belgium. Pianist Ilse Marchetti founded it in 2011 in a converted print works. The house has two floors of plaster walls and dark oak. Teaching here is one pupil with one teacher, and the hour is given as much to listening as to the notes on the page.

Six teaching rooms fill the working floors. In 2016 the school opened the Long Room, a recital space with sixty seats and a grand piano on a low stage under warm lamps. Pupils play there twice in the teaching year, once in December and once in June. The room is kept for those recitals and for the rehearsal that leads up to them.

The year holds thirty teaching weeks, from September to June. Lessons run Monday to Friday from 14:00 to 21:30, and Saturday from 09:00 to 18:00. Sunday is closed. The office phone is answered Monday to Friday from 13:00 to 18:00. Piano and violin begin at age five. Adult beginners start at any age. Eight teachers work with two hundred and forty pupils. Fees are in euro.

## Process
1. Book a free trial lesson.
2. Meet a teacher for 30 minutes.
3. Choose an instrument and a weekly slot.
4. Start the term. The registration fee is €35, once per year.
