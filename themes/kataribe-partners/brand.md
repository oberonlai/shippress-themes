# Kataribe Partners - brand brief

Independent public-relations studio in Rotterdam. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kataribe Partners. "Kataribe" was a storyteller who carried news from place to place; the studio carries a client's news by hand to the reporters who cover the sector.
- **Location:** Westersingel 48, 3014 GT Rotterdam, Netherlands (fictional address). Prices in EUR, excluding VAT, in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** founded in 2014 by Mireille Dufort, formerly an editor at a trade weekly for the energy sector. She had seen thousands of mass-distributed releases overwhelm editorial inboxes and built the studio on named lists and personal pitches instead.
- **Today:** twelve people, five sector desks (climate and energy, health and life sciences, food and consumer, mobility and cities, software and B2B). In 2025 the studio ran 46 coverage weeks for 18 retainer clients and kept lists of 312 specialist reporters.
- **Audience:** communications and marketing leads, founders and research directors with concrete news to share with specialist trade and national reporters: product launches, trial results, openings, research reports, founder profiles.
- **Tone:** calm, precise, candid. Verified facts before promotional language. We never promise coverage, placements or outlets, and we name no real media outlets, people or brands.

## A day in a coverage week (the daily routine)
Mon List: agree the angle; Lucas builds the named press list (about 25 reporters). Tue Release: Hannah finalises the release and a one-page background note; embargo time set. Wed Pitch: personal emails and calls to each reporter, never a blast. Thu Interviews: spokesperson interviews and follow-ups; Samir sits in. Fri Log: the coverage log (who wrote, who will, who passed) and a 30-minute debrief call. The press desk is staffed Mon-Fri 08:00-18:30 CET during a coverage week; the office is open Mon-Fri 09:00-17:30 CET.

## Retainers (6 rows, EUR excluding VAT)
Brief session: free (60-minute call, written brief and proposal within 5 working days) - Quarterly retainer: 2,400 / month (one coverage week per quarter, coverage log, 3-month minimum) - Monthly retainer: 4,600 / month (one coverage week every month, one sector-briefing pitch per quarter) - Sector programme: 7,900 / month (two coverage weeks a month, spokesperson preparation, monthly reporter round-table) - Launch week: 6,200 one-off - Media training: 1,800 per session (half day, up to 3 spokespeople, recorded practice interviews). Terms run 3 months; either side ends one with 30 days' notice before a term closes.
