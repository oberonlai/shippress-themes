# Kama Cookware — brand

Name: Kama Cookware. Cookware shop with a repair and seasoning workshop, Harbour Street 14, Northbay. Fictional; all names, figures and addresses are samples.

## History
Founded in **2016** by **Ines Valdés** on the premise that everyday kitchenware should be kept, repaired and cooked with for decades. The Harbour Street workshop soon became a service bench for cooks across the region: a 40-year-old family cast-iron skillet revived by rust removal and re-seasoning, a café's six carbon-steel pans serviced every winter, antique copper saucepans re-tinned as wedding gifts.

## Contact
Location: Harbour Street 14, Northbay. Hours Tue–Sat 10:00–18:00, Sun 11:00–16:00, Monday closed. Service counter drop-off Tue–Fri 10:00–17:00.

## Audience
Home cooks and small kitchens who want pans to use daily and keep for years; people with a worn pan that deserves repair.

## Voice
Calm, practical, grounded in the daily routines of cooking and upkeep. No exaggerated claims, no guarantees, no awards or press; materials, honest care and long-term use. Prices in EUR.
