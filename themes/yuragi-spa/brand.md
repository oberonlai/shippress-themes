# Yuragi Spa - brand brief

A day spa with a short list of treatments and quiet rooms, in Bristol, United Kingdom. Sample content in English first; Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German follow in stage 2, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Yuragi Spa. "Yuragi" is the Japanese word for a gentle sway or flicker; the spa itself is international and contemporary.
- **Location:** 3 Linden Quay, Bristol BS1 4RN, United Kingdom (fictional address). +44 117 496 0418, hello@yuragi.example. GBP (£) prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2017.
- **Audience:** Customers who want one unhurried hour of treatment, adults 18+ living in or visiting Bristol.
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 10:00–20:00 (last booking 18:30), closed Mondays.
- **Rooms:** 4 treatment rooms, 1 private soaking room and a tea lounge. Guests 18+; one guest per room.
- **Voice:** The voice of Yuragi Spa is serene, understated, and quietly observational, speaking with natural warmth without marketing exaggeration or clinical claims. Sentences remain spare and grounded, focusing on tangible physical details like folded linen, warm river stones, and unhurried time.

## History
Ines Kovač established Yuragi Spa in 2017 within a quiet former linen warehouse along Linden Quay in Bristol. Overlooking the water, the stone building offered solid walls, generous natural window light, and sufficient space for four private treatment rooms, a dedicated soaking bath, and a tranquil tea lounge. From its inception, the day spa was created to offer an unhurried sanctuary where visitors could step away from the city and set the weight of the week down.

Rather than introducing an expansive or complex spa menu, Yuragi deliberately maintains a focused collection of six treatments, conducted one guest at a time. The rooms operate with an intentional rhythm, reserving a full twelve-minute reset between appointments. During this transition, crisp cotton linens are arranged fresh and every single towel is folded by hand on pale ash timber, ensuring uninterrupted quiet and restorative stillness for whoever enters next.

Today, seven therapists and hosts care for the treatment rooms and tea lounge beneath the gentle Bristol sky. There are no couples suites, no crowded corridors, and no hurried changeovers. Guests are invited to arrive fifteen minutes early for hot tea in linen slippers, experience their treatment in quiet solitude, and rest by the lounge window as long as they wish before walking back along the quay.

## How a time is held
1. Choose a treatment from our short seasonal list.
2. Hold a time online, by telephone, or by email without a deposit.
3. Arrive fifteen minutes early for warm tea and linen slippers.
4. Rest in the tea lounge as long as you like after your treatment.

Cancellation is free until 24 hours before; within 24 hours, 50% of the treatment price. Gift vouchers are valid for 12 months. Rooms reset for 12 minutes between guests.
