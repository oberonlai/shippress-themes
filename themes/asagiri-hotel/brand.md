# Asagiri Hotel - brand brief

Airport hotel beside Aldmere International Airport (fictional). Sample content in English first; Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Asagiri Hotel. "Asagiri" is the Japanese word for morning mist; the hotel is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** 14 Perimeter Road, beside Aldmere International Airport (fictional). USD prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2009. 64 rooms on three floors.
- **Audience:** delayed and connecting passengers, airline crews, and anyone who lands late or leaves early.
- **Tone:** The voice of Asagiri Hotel is calm, specific, and quietly observational, reflecting the steady atmosphere of an airport hotel operating through the night. Copy relies on plain, natural English to convey precise practical details without promotional exaggeration, decorative flourishes, or fabricated claims.
- **Hours:** Front desk open 24 hours, every day; check-in from 14:00, check-out 11:00; night pantry 22:00–03:00; early breakfast 04:30–10:00.

## History
Asagiri Hotel opened in 2009 along Perimeter Road, situated directly beside Aldmere International Airport to serve travellers during the quietest hours of transit. Built with sixty-four rooms arranged over three low floors, the property was planned from the beginning as a dedicated shelter between late flight touchdowns and morning departures. Every corridor, room, and window was engineered around acoustic isolation, blackout conditions, and absolute stillness.

The premise of the hotel is that travel does not pause when terminal concourses close. For over a decade, our night desk has remained continually staffed and awake between the evening's final scheduled arrival and the dawn's earliest departures. Guests landing after midnight are not met by locked doors or dark lobbies, but by an attentive team prepared for delayed itineraries and unexpected diversions.

Today, Asagiri Hotel maintains the disciplined overnight routine established in its founding year. Operating twenty-four hours every day, the hotel provides reliable shuttle connections, a night pantry with warm food, and breakfast ready before the first flights depart. We keep our doors open and our counter attended all night so that every arriving guest finds immediate rest and quiet care.

## A day at the hotel (the daily routine)
The hotel runs one night in clock order: 23:40 last arrival and rooms ready; 00:30 late check-in held and the last scheduled shuttle; 02:00 shuttle on call, ready in 10 minutes; 05:10 first shuttle to the terminals, early breakfast from 04:30. By day, housekeeping works between 11:00 and 14:00 only, so day sleepers stay undisturbed.

## Late check-in (4 steps)
1. Book your room and provide your scheduled flight number during reservation.
2. The front desk monitors your flight status and tracks any arrival delays.
3. Your room key envelope is prepared and held waiting under the desk lamp.
4. Call the hotel from Arrivals Door 4 for immediate shuttle collection.
Hold: until 04:00 without a flight number, all night with one. Cancellation is free until 18:00 on the arrival day.

## Shuttle
