# Hibiki Speech - brand brief

Speech and language therapy clinic for children (from age 2) and adults. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people, prices and contact details are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hibiki Speech. "Hibiki" is the Japanese word for an echo or resonance; the clinic is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** 2F, 18 Linden Row, Westmere (fictional, no real city). EUR prices in every language; no converted prices. Phone +00 31 4072 5180, email hello@hibikispeech.example.
- **History:** Hibiki Speech was founded in 2014 by Ines Halvorsen on the second floor of 18 Linden Row in Westmere. Having worked as a speech and language therapist supporting early language acquisition and late talkers, Ines envisioned an independent clinic designed around calm, unhurried attention. Rather than rushing families through crowded schedules, she established a practice where children from age two and adults could find their words in quiet rooms filled with natural window light and warm oak furniture.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 08:30–18:30, Sat 09:00–13:00, closed Sun and public holidays.
- **Audience:** families of late talkers and children with speech difficulties; adults after a stroke, people who stammer, and people who use their voice all day.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, unhurried, and specific, speaking directly to families and adults without clinical jargon, pretense, or exaggerated claims. Every statement reflects practical everyday observation, quiet patience, and respect for each individual's natural pace.

## Story
Over the following years, the clinic brought together seven dedicated therapists and coordinators, expanding care to encompass speech sounds, stammering in teenagers and adults, aphasia and word-finding after stroke, voice care, and picture-based communication (AAC). Every person works in a dedicated room equipped with low tables, a wall mirror, or a quiet sofa corner. A single therapist remains paired with each client through their entire eight-session block, ensuring continuity and trust.

Today, Hibiki Speech continues to welcome families and adult clients from across Westmere with clear scheduling: new clients are seen for an assessment within two weeks of their initial enquiry. Sessions are set at forty-five minutes, paired with practical ten-minute daily home practice routines that bridge clinical progress with everyday life. Without relying on hurried routines or impersonal tests, the clinic remains committed to listening closely before practice begins.

## First visit (5 parts)
1. Call: A free 15-minute phone conversation with the family coordinator to discuss what you notice and answer questions.
2. Assessment: A comprehensive 90-minute evaluation at the clinic, scheduled within 2 weeks of your call.
3. Written plan: A clear summary report and therapy outline delivered within 5 working days.
4. Block of 8 sessions: Weekly 45-minute therapy sessions with the same dedicated therapist throughout.
5. Review: A collaborative review after session 8 to decide whether to continue, pause, or finish.

## Method (3 parts)
Listen · Practise · Carry it home. Fixed numbers: 45 minutes per session, 8 sessions per block, 10 minutes of home practice a day, first assessment within 2 weeks.
