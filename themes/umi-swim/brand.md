# Umi Swim School — brand

## Story
In 2014, Lena Moreau founded Umi Swim School at 12 Quay Road, Larkfield, following twelve years spent coaching adults who carried deep hesitation and fear of the water. She envisioned a dedicated teaching school where progress was measured by genuine comfort rather than hurried athletic performance, beginning with a calm, purpose-built space designed to ease learners into the pool.

The school was built around two distinct pools to serve different learning stages: a warmer 12 by 6 metre teaching pool heated to 32 °C at a shallow 0.9-metre depth, and a 25-metre six-lane main pool kept at 29 °C. To protect the quality of instruction, class sizes were strictly limited to a maximum of four children per coach and five adult beginners, ensuring every swimmer receives calm, attentive guidance from their first moment at the rail.

Over the years, the coaching staff expanded to seven specialist instructors across four ten-week terms each year. From infants experiencing their first gentle water acclimation to adult learners swimming full lengths of the main pool for the first time, Umi Swim School remains dedicated to quiet decks, patient instruction, and the simple principle that composure must always come before distance.

### Calm first, then the lane
Learning to swim begins before the first stroke. We keep our teaching pool heated to 32 °C at a steady 0.9-metre depth, creating a quiet environment where the water feels warm, predictable, and supportive.

With small classes capped strictly at four children or five adults, your coach remains right beside you in the water. We build balance, quiet breath control, and effortless glides before asking anyone to swim a full lane.

### A day at Umi
Throughout the day, our two pools support swimmers at every stage of experience. Mornings begin with quiet laps in the main pool, followed by gentle infant water discovery in our teaching pool. In the afternoon, children progress from their first supported floats to steady kickboard glides, while evening groups offer adult beginners the unhurried space to master breathing and swim their first full lengths.

### Programmes
Our structured courses follow four ten-week terms across the year. Kids Stages 1 to 3 guide children aged 4 to 12 through water confidence and stroke mechanics with a maximum of four learners per coach. Adult Beginner groups keep class sizes to five for attentive instruction, and one-to-one private lessons provide tailored sessions for nervous swimmers.

### Beside you at the edge
Our seven coaches bring steady, patient instruction to every lesson. Founded by Lena Moreau in 2014, our teaching team works directly in the water and at the poolside rail so that learners of every age feel unhurried, secure, and understood.
