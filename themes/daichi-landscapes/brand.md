# Daichi Landscapes — brand brief

Design plan row: b8-4 (commercial landscaping company; type business). The home page and the inner pages are in English; the other four languages follow. All names, places and people are fictional.

## Concept
Daichi Landscapes Ltd. was founded in 2009 in Vancouver. The yard and office have stayed at Unit 4, 210 Mill Creek Road, Vancouver, BC V5Y 1Z3. From the start the company planted and maintained grounds for campuses, hospitals and civic streets across Metro Vancouver: trees in pits, paving edges, planters and the watering that follows a new planting. The work is commercial landscaping for sites that stay open to students, patients and people walking through.

The same six-stage sequence still organises a contract. A site survey in weeks 1–2 leads to a planting plan in weeks 3–5, ground preparation in weeks 6–8, planting in weeks 9–11, a handover walk in week 12, and seasonal aftercare for 24 months. Planting stock is grown so that 80% comes from within 150 km. Each planting plan uses 60% native or adapted species. Street tree planting includes a guard and two seasons of watering.

By 2023 the crew had completed three courtyards and a sensory garden at Riverside General Hospital. In 2024 Northshore College’s forecourt took 64 trees and 1,800 m² of planting. In 2025 the Fraser Street renewal covered 1.2 km and 112 street trees. After 24 months, 62 of those 64 college trees, all 3 hospital courtyards and 109 of the 112 street trees were established. The company now has a crew of 28, led by six named people, and winter planting slots for 2027 are open.

## Process (six stages)
1. Site survey (week 1–2): measure tree pits, paving edges, access and existing trees before any plan is drawn.
2. Planting plan (week 3–5): set species, sizes and locations so that 60% of the plan is native or adapted, with 80% of stock grown within 150 km.
3. Ground preparation (week 6–8): open pits and beds, set soil and irrigation, and keep the campus, hospital or street usable.
4. Planting (week 9–11): plant trees, guards and planters, including street trees from $680 each with a guard and two seasons of watering.
5. Handover walk (week 12): walk the finished site with the client and record what enters the 24-month aftercare.
6. Seasonal aftercare (24 months): return each season, with visits from $420, until the establishment period ends.
