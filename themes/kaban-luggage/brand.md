# Kaban Luggage - brand brief

Luggage and travel-goods shop with a back-room workshop in Antwerp, Belgium. Sample content in English first; Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German follow, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kaban Luggage. A shop that fits a case to the trip a person is actually taking.
- **Location:** one shop with a workshop behind the counter at Veerkaai 14, 2000 Antwerp, Belgium (fictional). EUR prices in every language; no converted prices. Phone +32 3 000 14 14, email hello@kabanluggage.example.
- **History / founded:** 2009, by Ines Vandamme, who still runs every fitting session. The first year was one counter, one scale and three kinds of shell; the workshop grew behind it.
- **People:** Ines Vandamme (founder and head fitter), Tomasz Wierzbicki (workshop lead), Amara Osei (fitter), Lucas Ferreira (fitter and special orders), Hana Lindgren (canvas and leather repairs), Priya Raman (stock buyer), Jonas Becker (online orders and delivery).
- **Audience:** people who travel by train, plane and car and want one case that suits their route: commuters between cities, long-trip travellers, families, musicians and field workers.
- **Tone:** calm, precise, practical, in the voice of an experienced fitter at the counter. Short sentences, specifications before adjectives. No awards, press, ratings, customer counts or guarantees.

## A day at the shop (routine)
Doors open at 10:00. Fittings run on the hour (forty minutes, free, no purchase needed); between them the counter takes walk-ins. Behind the counter Tomasz works through the day's repair tickets: wheels, handles, locks. In the afternoon Jonas packs the parcels that leave with the carrier at 16:00, and the shop closes at 18:30 (17:00 on Saturday).

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Fitting: 40 minutes, free, no purchase needed. Four parts: the trip, the packing list, the handle height, the case.
- Workshop turnaround 5 working days; cases from other makers are repaired too.
- Workshop prices: spinner wheel pair €38, telescopic handle €64, leather top handle re-stitch €34, zip slider €24, TSA lock reset or replacement €29, aluminium dent and scuff treatment €45.
- Special orders 10-14 days, 20% deposit.
- Delivery: Belgium €9, Netherlands and Luxembourg €12, rest of EU €25; free in the EU over €300; collection in the shop is free.
- Returns: 30 days, unused, with the fitting tag attached.
- Hours: Tuesday-Friday 10:00-18:30, Saturday 10:00-17:00, closed Sunday and Monday.
- Founded 2009.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Dark low-key macro first screen: radial vignette into near-black, blur-zoom resolve on load, tiny caps label and wordmark, a strip of stocked cases peeking at the bottom right.
2. Chapter rows: two abutting photographs (65/35, 2.14:1 together) flush to the left edge and a very large vertical title at the right edge, with a numeral, an oxblood vertical sub-label and a hairline "next" index.
3. Dark-to-paper hand-off: warm grey paper with grain and scratch lines; photographs dissolve in from the top.
Supporting: a footer revealed from under the last section. Reference for the mood: a leather-goods site's dark macro opening and chapter layout (borrowed structure and motion only; no photographs, copy or code).

## Pages and contents
1. **Home** - front-hero, front-trip, front-counter, front-parts, front-fitting, front-workshop, front-stories, front-visit.
2. **Shop** (/shop/) - intro line, grid of the five cases (WooCommerce).
3. **Single product** - gallery, spec list, add to cart.
4. **Parts guide** (/parts/) - three chapter rows (Shell, Handle, Wheels) and the shell comparison table.
5. **Fitting** (/fitting/) - chapter row, four ruled steps, what to bring, booking.
6. **Workshop** (/workshop/) - chapter row, full price list (7 rows), repairs on cases from any maker.
7. **Ordering and delivery** (/ordering/) - special orders, delivery fee table, returns.
8. **About** (/about/) - chapter row "Since 2009", story, seven people as a ruled list.
9. **Stories** (/journal/) - posts page; four entries (Trips, Workshop); one long article on wheels.
10. **Newsletter** (/newsletter/) and **Contact** (/contact/) - chapter row, address, hours, getting there.
Every inner page opens with a dark head band (T1), uses the abutting photo-pair chapter row (T2) on six pages, and sits on grained paper (T3).
Cart, checkout, account, order confirmation, search and 404 share the dark head band.
