# Kaban Luggage

A quiet block theme (type store, WooCommerce) for a luggage fitting and travel-goods shop. English home page and inner pages in this release; other languages follow.

## Signature
- Dark macro hero: a low-key photograph resolving out of black under a radial vignette, a tiny caps label and a peeking strip of stocked cases.
- Chapter rows: abutting photo pairs flush left with a large vertical title, "(0n)" numerals and spec lists.
- Dark-to-paper hand-off onto a grainy paper ground with fine scratch lines.

## Fonts
Outfit and Albert Sans (SIL OFL 1.1, Latin); Noto Sans TC and Noto Sans JP (SIL OFL 1.1, Traditional Chinese and Japanese subsets). Licences in assets/fonts/.
