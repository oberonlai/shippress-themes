Mise Studio is a commercial kitchen design theme crafted for planning studios, hospitality consultants, and technical design practices. Built around a calm, documentary aesthetic, it features structured room sheets, transparent fee schedules, and full-scale layout test workflows. Designed with responsive sans typography and clear booking paths to turn kitchen visits into completed commissions.

== Description ==
A block theme for a commercial kitchen design studio: one long landing page with an anchor menu and a single booking path. The home page opens on a 72/28 framed-window hero (a documentary kitchen photograph with a thin white stepped outline that draws itself over the cooking line, and a flat pale panel with the headline), then a narrow narrative column on a ring-titled white sheet, a grey-bar accordion whose bars open sheets of three photographs, a full-bleed parallax service band, an asymmetric masonry of the on-site layout test with three flush cells, a dated five-row process sheet, a six-row fee sheet, three flush kitchen cells and the visit sheet with the waitlist form, closed by a four-column footer.

= Fonts =
* Libre Franklin (headings, wordmark, navigation, prices), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
* Public Sans (body text, labels, forms), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
* Noto Sans TC and Noto Sans JP (headings on the Traditional Chinese and Japanese sample content, subset), SIL Open Font License 1.1.

= Images =
The sample photographs were generated for this theme. They contain no text, logos or real people.

= Forms =
The newsletter and contact forms use a dynamic block that shows a Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP form when one is active. Without a plugin the waitlist form still has an email field and a submit button that opens your email app with the address filled in; nothing is stored by the theme.
