# Mise Studio - brand brief

A commercial kitchen design studio that draws restaurant, hotel, school and bakery kitchens around the brigade that will cook in them: a site visit in the quiet hours, a written brief, a layout drawing, a full-scale floor-tape test with the cooks, then drawings and an equipment schedule for the contractor. Landing-page site (one long home page, one booking path). Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Mise Studio. Sub-line wherever the brand is introduced: "Commercial kitchen design · since 2014".
- **Location:** Unit 4, Ferrier Yard, 22 Canal Street, Oldmere (a fictional address). Email hello@mise-studio.example, phone +00 41 2207 5530. Fees in euros (€) in every language, excluding VAT.
- **History:** founded in 2014 by Renata Solberg after twelve years cooking on hot lines; the first kitchen the studio drew was marked out in chalk on a cafe floor, which is why every layout is still tested at full scale before anything is ordered. 118 kitchens drawn since 2014 (restaurants, hotels, schools, bakeries).
- **Audience:** owners, head chefs and project managers opening, rebuilding or extending a professional kitchen within 80 km of the studio, who want the layout decided by how the brigade really works rather than by an equipment catalogue.
- **Tone:** calm, factual and unhurried, grounded in the daily realities of working kitchens. Specific measurements and plain working facts; no superlatives, no promotion, no guaranteed savings, no awards, rankings or real brands. The studio does not build kitchens or supply equipment.

## A day at Mise Studio (daily routine)
08:30 the studio opens and drawings are reviewed; 09:30-11:30 a site visit between breakfast and lunch service; 13:00 drawing reviews; 15:00-17:00 a second visit or a floor test after the lunch service; 17:30 the studio closes. The routine is the same every working day, so visits are never rushed into a service.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page; reference: lixil.co.jp)
1. Framed-window hero: a 72/28 split under a 120px white header; a documentary kitchen photograph with a 1.5px white stepped hairline drawn over the cooking line (it draws itself once) and a flat pale plum-grey panel with the three-line headline and an arrow link; thin chevrons at both edges; slow cross-fade between two photographs. Inner pages: the same outline on a 948x420 head photograph with a two-line caption.
2. Hollow-ring section titles and titled white sheets: a 32px ring with a 7px plum stroke before every H2, a light 1.75rem title, content on white 948px sheets with 1px borders, dated rows under a 1px rule, filled-dot links.
3. Grey-bar accordion with a photo sheet: flat 48px grey bars with white disc chevrons, each opening a white sheet of three photographs; the first open on load.
Supporting: flush photo-top cells with 1px borders, a grey page-top square, a parallax full-bleed service photo with caption bottom-left and the one pill bottom-right, slow reveal on scroll.
