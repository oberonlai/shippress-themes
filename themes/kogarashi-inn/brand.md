# Kogarashi Inn - brand brief

A six-room country inn on a fell above a fictional Cumbrian village. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kogarashi Inn. "Kogarashi" is the first cold wind of winter; it is used as a name only. The inn itself is English and modern.
- **Location:** Fell Lane, Hollin Moor, Cumbria, England (a fictional fell village; no postcode). Train to the fictional station Ashby Cross, about 3 hours from London; free collection at 15:10 and 17:10, a 25-minute drive. Parking for 6 cars. GBP prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** 2016, by Ines Calloway and Rafael Oduya, in a restored 1890s farmhouse and barn.
- **Audience:** adults who have left the city for a week or a few days: couples, writers, walkers, people who want a house with its own pace.
- **Tone:** The voice of Kogarashi Inn is calm, specific, and quietly attentive, reflecting the unhurried daily schedule of a rural fell house. Sentences remain clear and grounded in physical routines, avoiding marketing exaggeration, hospitality clichés, and decorative praise.

## Story
In 2016, Ines Calloway and Rafael Oduya restored an 1890s stone farmhouse and barn on Fell Lane in Hollin Moor, Cumbria. Their intention was simple: to create a quiet country inn with just six rooms, accommodating twelve guests at most. Rather than expanding, they preserved the original proportions of the stone buildings, framing views across the fell and establishing a kitchen garden behind the stone walls.

The house operates to a shared and unhurried rhythm. Each morning begins at 06:40 as Ines sets the breakfast tables beside the east window, followed by fresh bread from Maren Holt and eggs from down the lane. Days are spent on the high paths or resting by the stove, before Kofi Mensah-Lyle lights the wood-fired bath house in the afternoon and Rafael cooks the evening meal for one unhurried sitting at 19:00.

Kogarashi Inn welcomes guests from 1 February through 22 December, closing for the depths of winter between 23 December and 31 January. With a minimum stay of two nights and dedicated Quiet Weeks from Sunday to Sunday, the inn offers a deliberate shelter from urban pace, anchored by the fells, seasonal cooking, and the restorative calm of the valley.

## People (7, fictional)
1. **Ines Calloway** — Innkeeper and co-founder. Co-founded the inn in 2016 and oversees the running of the house each day. She sets the breakfast tables by the east window every morning at 06:40.
2. **Rafael Oduya** — Innkeeper, co-founder and cook. Co-founded Kogarashi Inn in 2016 with Ines and prepares the evening meal. He cooks the single-sitting dinner served at 19:00 using ingredients from the garden and valley.
3. **Maren Holt** — Bread and breakfast. Bakes fresh bread early each morning and prepares breakfast from 07:30 to 09:00. Her morning table features fresh loaves, lane eggs, porridge, and tea.
4. **Kofi Mensah-Lyle** — Kitchen garden, wood and bath house fire. Tends the kitchen garden beds and manages firewood supplies across the farm. He lights and maintains the wood-fired bath house for guests every afternoon from 16:00.
5. **Saoirse Dunn** — Rooms and linen. Cares for the inn's six guest rooms and manages all laundry and linens. She readies each room before guest arrivals commence at 15:00.
6. **Yuna Park** — Evening kitchen and afternoon tea. Prepares afternoon tea served at 15:30 and works alongside Rafael in the evening kitchen. She assists in creating the four-course communal dinner at 19:00.
7. **Arthur Penhale** — Walking guide. Guides fell walks departing directly from the inn's front door on Tuesdays and Thursdays. He leads guests along routes across Hollin Moor, including the ridge and larch trails.
