# Kazoe Books App

A calm WordPress **block theme** for a **bookkeeping app for small shops** (SaaS landing page). Ink type on white, a steel-blue band (#7FA3B5) running to deep blue (#2F4B5C) with a halftone dot ramp, and blush and sky pastels for the lower half of the first screen. **Plus Jakarta Sans** headings over **Noto Sans**. All copy is English and every name is fictional (Kazoe Books, Inês Carvalho, Tomás Weber, the Lisbon address and the prices).

## Signature

- **Stepped label-box headings:** white boxes with a 1px ink border, flush left, stacked line by line (hero, section heads, closing).
- **Top and bottom halves first screen:** a steel-blue band with a three-zone halftone dot gradient above, a blush-to-sky band holding the photograph below.
- **Numbered rows:** an 80px number column, edge-to-edge 1px rules and an ink "+" box that turns into a cross when a row opens (features, FAQ).
- **Fixed bottom-right pair** of buttons (a bottom bar on phones) and an 80px outlined header box.

## Who it is for

- **Industry:** bookkeeping and accounting apps, small SaaS products.
- **Site type:** landing page. No WooCommerce needed; the theme is compatible with it.

## Pages

Home (nine sections: hero, lead and stats, features, cards, three-step setup, stories, pricing table, FAQ, closing), Features, Pricing, About, News (3 posts, 2 categories, 3 tags) with article, category and tag archives, Newsletter, Contact, Privacy, Search and 404.

## Motion

Scroll effects use the bundled sp-scroll files: parallax on the hero and the shop-street band, mask reveals, staggered rows and counters. Everything shows its final state with reduced motion or without JavaScript.

## Photography

Twelve distinct, bright documentary photographs of independent shops in `assets/images`, with raster copies in `assets/images/demo` for the Media Library.
