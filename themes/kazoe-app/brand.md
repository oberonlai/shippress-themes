# Kazoe Books App - brand brief

## Concept
Name: Kazoe Books (the app is called "Kazoe"). A bookkeeping app for small shops, sold from one long landing page. Founded 2022. Location: Rua da Prata 112, 1100-420 Lisboa, Portugal (fictional address). Remote support, hours Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 (Lisbon time), email hello@kazoe.example, replies within one business day.

## Story
Kazoe Books was founded in 2022 by Inês Carvalho and Tomás Weber in Lisbon, Portugal. Operating from their studio at Rua da Prata 112, they set out to create a calm, focused bookkeeping application tailored to the practical needs of independent retail counters. Having watched neighborhood shopkeepers spend late evenings balancing cash registers and sorting paper receipts, the founders built Kazoe around a single core promise: allowing owners to close their business day in one tap.

From the start, the software was developed alongside independent business owners, including corner cafés, florists, and bike repair shops. Inês led product design while Tomás directed engineering, building a responsive application that runs reliably across iOS, Android, and the web. They were joined by support lead Amara Okoye and certified accountant Lena Fischer, ensuring that daily sales tallies translate into compliant, structured tax exports requiring no accounting background.

Today, Kazoe provides remote customer support to merchants who want straightforward bookkeeping without complex training or bloated feature sets. Independent shops across retail and hospitality use the system to log sales, separate tax rates, and share CSV and PDF summaries directly with their accountants. The team remains based in Lisbon, committed to transparent pricing, dependable software, and responsive merchant assistance.

## Voice and tone
The voice is calm, clear, and grounded in the daily routines of small business owners. It avoids marketing hyperbole and technical jargon, explaining counter workflows, pricing, and exports with direct simplicity. Promise line: "Close the day in one tap."

## Audience
Customers are owners of independent shops: corner cafés, florists, bike repair shops. They want daily sales tallied, tax-period exports for their accountant, and no training.

## The daily routine and setup (about 10 minutes, iOS, Android and web)
1. Download & name your shop (2 min)
2. Add your payment methods and tax rate (5 min)
3. Close your first day with one tap (3 min)

Every day ends the same way: ring up sales, tap once, the closing report is done.

## Signature techniques

2. Top/bottom halves first screen: steel-blue to deep-blue gradient with 3px dot dither above, blush-to-sky band holding the photo below.
3. Numbered rows with an 80px number column, full-width 1px rules and an ink plus box (features, FAQ).
