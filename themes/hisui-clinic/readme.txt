Hisui Clinic is a calm, accessible WordPress theme designed for endocrinology practices, diabetes centers, and long-term outpatient healthcare clinics. Built around a single-column longform editorial flow with distinct split-window heroes and structured treatment ledger accordions, it presents conditions, clinical teams, and transparent fee schedules with quiet clarity. Featuring responsive typography and bilingual CJK typographic support, the theme provides dependable, patient-focused digital communication without visual clutter.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download hisui-clinic.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/hisui-clinic.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Hisui Clinic.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, the news posts with their categories and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. The theme works as a plain site without WooCommerce.

Forms: the newsletter sections use a small dynamic block (inc/shippress-forms.php). With MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP active it shows your form; without a plugin it shows an email field and a button that opens your email app with the address filled in (you are not on the list until you send that email). The theme sends nothing itself.

The clinic, its people, its address, telephone numbers and prices are fictional. The clinic gives no medical advice on this site.
