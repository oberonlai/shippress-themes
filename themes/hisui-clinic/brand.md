# Hisui Clinic - brand brief

An endocrinology and diabetes clinic for adults (from 16) that keeps diabetes, thyroid and hormone care on one long plan. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hisui Clinic.
- **Location:** 3F, 42 Alder Quay, Harwell Green (a fictional, international-neutral address). Telephone +00 21 5840 3317. Email hello@hisui-clinic.example. Prices in EUR (€) in every language.

- **Tone:** The voice is calm, clear, and reassuringly precise, speaking directly about medical routines and long-term care without clinical jargon or marketing hype. Every statement remains grounded in factual clinic processes, transparent fees, and collaborative treatment plans followed steadily over years.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00, Sat 08:30–12:30, closed Sun and public holidays. Fasting blood tests 08:00–10:00 only. Between-visits line (nurse educators): messages and calls Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00, reply within 1 working day. Not an emergency service: in an emergency call your local emergency number.

## History
Founded in 2011 by endocrinologist Helena Varga, Hisui Clinic was established at 42 Alder Quay in Harwell Green to provide dedicated outpatient endocrinology and diabetes care for adults from sixteen years of age. Having observed how frequently patients with chronic conditions experienced fragmented appointments with rotating clinicians, she established an independent medical practice centered on continuity of care, thorough evaluations, and long-term therapeutic partnerships.

The clinic is built around the philosophy of one plan followed over years with the same doctor. Rather than offering hurried consultations that deliver isolated laboratory figures without clear direction, every treatment path centers on a collaborative one-page written regimen finalized within seven days of the initial consultation. Morning laboratory slots between 08:00 and 10:00 enable fasting blood samples to be drawn and discussed on the same day, anchoring clinical care in steady, predictable routines.

Operating from the third floor of an accessible building on Alder Quay, Hisui Clinic unites physician consultations, nutritional guidance, and dedicated between-visits nurse communication under one roof. Regular three-month plan reviews maintain close oversight of diabetes, thyroid function, and hormone health, while our direct weekday nurse educator line ensures that non-emergency patient questions are resolved quickly and calmly throughout the year.

## Your plan (five parts)
1. First call: A free 15-minute phone conversation with patient coordinator Claire Mbeki to discuss your needs, explain clinic procedures, and schedule appointments.
2. First consultation: A 60-minute in-depth consultation with your endocrinologist, following morning fasting blood tests taken between 08:00 and 10:00.
3. Regimen agreed: A clear one-page written care plan agreed upon and signed by doctor and patient together within 7 days.
4. Plan reviews: Structured 40-minute follow-up appointments every 3 months with the same doctor to review laboratory tests and maintain steady progress.
5. Between visits: Ongoing phone and messaging support with nurse educators Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00, with replies to non-emergency questions within 1 working day.

Fixed figures (identical everywhere): 60 minutes first consultation, 7 days to a written plan, 3 months between reviews, 1 working day to a reply. These describe how the clinic works; they are not outcome statistics.

## A day at the clinic (daily routine)
The daily routine starts at 08:00 when the laboratory corner opens for fasting blood tests, which run until 10:00 so results can be read at the desk the same day. Consultations fill the morning and afternoon; Rosa and Priya answer the between-visits line from 09:00 to 17:00 and reply within one working day; Claire books first calls and lab slots. The doors close at 18:00 on weekdays (Saturday 12:30).
