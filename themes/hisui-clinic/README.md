# Hisui Clinic

A block theme for an endocrinology and diabetes clinic (ShipPress). See `brand.md` for the fictional clinic and `brand/design-spec.md` for the design notes.

- **Patterns:** `front-hero` (rule-split window), `front-narrative`, `front-conditions` (department band and tabs), `front-interlude` (parallax), `front-method` (ledger rows), `front-figures`, `front-between`, `front-stories`, `front-fees`, `front-news` (audience-tagged rows), `front-visit`; template patterns `tpl-*`; `header-main`, `footer-main`.
- **Motion:** `assets/sp-scroll.css` and `assets/sp-scroll.js` (reveal, stagger, count, parallax); reduced motion and `?sp-static` show the final state.
- **Forms:** `inc/shippress-forms.php` (dynamic newsletter / contact slot with a mailto fallback).
- **Fonts:** Manrope and Figtree, loaded by `theme.json`.
