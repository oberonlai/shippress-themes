# Yoake Patisserie - brand brief

A patisserie that bakes one morning batch and keeps a small salon for tea in the afternoon: counter pastries, whole cakes ordered two days ahead, and one way to book afternoon tea. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Yoake Patisserie. "Yoake" means daybreak; the shop is otherwise a plain, international, modern room with a white-marble bench.
- **Location:** 14 Quarry Lane, Halden Cross (fictional). Halden Cross station is six minutes' walk; bus 14, stop "Quarry Lane". Step-free entrance. Dogs welcome at the counter, not in the salon. Phone +44 20 7946 0532 (calls Wed-Sun 10:00-16:00). Email hello@yoake-patisserie.example.
- **Founded:** 2017 by Mara Lindqvist (head pâtissière) and Daniel Osei (tea and the salon) in a former print shop. In 2019 the back room became the tea salon (14 seats). In 2023 whole-cake orders moved to two days ahead.
- **People (seven):** Mara Lindqvist (co-founder, head pâtissière, starts 06:00), Daniel Osei (co-founder, tea and the salon, 13:00), Inès Moreau (viennoiserie, the first oven, 05:30), Tomasz Nowak (whole cakes and orders, 07:00), Amara Nwosu (the counter, 08:00), Lucía Herrera (salon host, 14:00), Felix Brandt (apprentice baker, 10:00).
- **Audience:** neighbours on the way to work, people ordering a birthday cake, friends booking tea.
- **Tone:** calm, precise, warm, never boastful. Specific about hours, sizes, prices and who does what. No awards, no "best", no health claims.

## A day at the bench (the daily routine)
05:30 the bench is lit and the oven heats - 07:00 the first trays come out (croissants, then tarts) - 08:00 the counter opens - 11:00 whole-cake collection opens (until 17:00) - 15:00 afternoon tea is seated - 17:30 doors close. The routine is the same shape every Wednesday to Sunday. The home page shows 07 / 11 / 15.

## Hours (identical everywhere)
- Wed-Sun: counter 08:00-17:00 (or until sold out); afternoon tea one sitting 15:00-17:00; doors close 17:30.
- Mon-Tue closed (the oven is cleaned on Monday; Tuesday is ordering and recipe day).

## Prices (EUR, identical everywhere)
Butter croissant 3.60 - Fruit tart of the day, slice 6.50 - Vanilla choux 4.80 - Whole cake 15 cm, serves 6: 38 - Whole cake 18 cm, serves 8-10: 52 - Afternoon tea in the salon, per person (with a pot of tea) 42 - Pot of tea 6. A message of up to 20 characters on a whole cake is free.

## Whole cake order (four steps)
1 choose a size and one of five flavours (strawberry and vanilla, dark chocolate and hazelnut, lemon and almond, apricot and pistachio, coffee and walnut) - 2 send the order by 11:00 two days before (form on Whole Cakes, or email) - 3 we confirm by email the same day - 4 collect from 11:00 to 17:00 and pay at collection. Free cancellation until 24 hours before.

## Afternoon tea (the single way to book)
The booking form on the Afternoon Tea page (or the email address). 14 seats, tables of 1-6, Wed-Sun at 15:00, bookable up to 14 days ahead; tables held 15 minutes; not walk-in.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Split dawn: a blush 110-degree wash on top with a vertical two-line lead, and a photograph flush to the left edge at 90% width (reference: Allée Omotesando first screen).
2. Wash and drift: diagonal pink wash bands with a tracked kicker, an em dash and a narrow text column, and a photograph flush to the opposite viewport edge, dropped 110px.
3. Hour ledger: ruled 3-up cells edge to edge, a tinted tile with an inset photograph, a pill, and a giant serif hour numeral at the bottom-right (reference: Nittoh 100th).
Supporting: a one-stroke whisk-and-bloom outline mark, a full-bleed film still with one tracked line, slow 1.6 s fades and parallax.

## Photography
High-key bright documentary photographs: white marble, pale plaster, porcelain, blush and copper, soft window light, slight motion blur on moving hands, one quiet moment per frame, no text, no logos, no cut-outs.
