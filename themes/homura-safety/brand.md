# Homura Safety - brand brief

Independent fire-safety compliance consultancy that reviews whether the fire precautions of a working premises would hold. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Homura Safety.
- **Location:** Unit 4, 18 Harbour Row, Port Ellery (a fictional address). Email hello@homura-safety.example, phone +00 21 4460 2011. EUR (€) prices in every language, excluding VAT.
- **History:** founded in 2011 by Mara Lindqvist, who wanted fire-safety reviews written by people who sell no equipment and therefore report only what they saw. It began as one consultant with a clipboard and is now a team of seven that has completed 1,460 reviews over 14 years.
- **Audience:** owners and managers of working premises (commercial kitchens and food halls, warehouses and workshops, offices and clinics) who want to know whether their doors, nozzles, pull stations and escape routes would work on the night they are needed.
- **Tone:** calm, precise and understated. Plain sentences, no alarm, no marketing jargon. No guarantees, no invented statistics, no awards, no press, no real brands or real people.

## People (7, named the same everywhere)
Mara Lindqvist (founder, lead consultant) - Tobias Achebe (senior consultant, kitchens) - Yuna Park (consultant, warehouses) - Rafael Duarte (consultant, offices and clinics) - Ines Moreau (report editor) - Samir Haddad (follow-up visits) - Keiko Brandt (bookings and office).

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Hours: Mon-Fri 08:00-18:00, Sat 09:00-13:00; site visits also after service 21:00-23:00 by arrangement.
- Process (4): 01 Call (15-minute scoping call) - 02 Walk-through visit (2 to 4 hours) - 03 Written findings (within 7 working days) - 04 Follow-up visit (optional, within 90 days).
- Fees (EUR, excl. VAT): Small premises review (up to 250 m²) €420 - Standard review (250-1,000 m²) €780 - Large site review (1,000-4,000 m²) €1,450 - Kitchen suppression and hood check (add-on) €190 - Follow-up visit €260.
- Facts: 1,460 reviews, 14 years, 7 people.
- Cases (outcomes described qualitatively only): Linden Hall food court (kitchen, six outlets, grease around a hood nozzle), Northgate cold store (racking stacked under sprinkler heads), Ashby Street dental clinic (escape route through a storeroom).

## A day at Homura Safety (daily routine)
08:00 the office opens and Keiko confirms the day's visits; mid-morning a consultant starts a daytime walk-through (two to four hours) with the person who runs the premises, reading pull-station tags, measuring sprinkler clearance and walking each escape route as a stranger would; in the afternoon Ines edits the notes of earlier visits into the written findings, sent within seven working days; on some evenings between 21:00 and 23:00 a consultant visits a kitchen after service, when the hood and the line can be checked with nobody cooking. Saturday 09:00-13:00 is bookings and follow-up visits.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Slanted bar word: thick white parallelogram bars (skew -8deg) spelling a short word across a low-key photograph, passing behind and in front of the people (hochiki.co.jp hero). Home: CHECK; inner pages: a short word per page.
2. Docked quick-link strip: a white four-cell strip with square thumbnails and hairline dividers overlapping the foot of the photograph.
3. Notch pair: a chestnut photo tile with a 24px triangular notch pointing into a white text panel, mirrored on the second row; section titles with a 20x2 ember rule and an uppercase sub-label.
Supporting: a white header slab with two hanging square cells (chestnut Book, black language); a crossfading pair of stills with a thin progress bar in the hero; parallax on the hero and the corridor band.

## Photography
Low-key documentary photographs (AI-generated with Grok Build), one style: warm-dark interior lit by a single practical light, shallow depth of field, muted chestnut and graphite grade, fine film grain, real equipment and diverse adults in plain work clothes; no text, logos or brand marks, no cut-outs.
