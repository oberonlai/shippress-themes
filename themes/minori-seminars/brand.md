# Minori Seminars — brand

## History
Founded in 2011, Minori Seminars began as the dedicated in-house management-training desk for a consortium of mid-sized firms seeking practical leadership development. Rather than relying on multi-day theoretical lectures or abstract corporate simulations, the desk established a disciplined single-day format centered on structured conversations, authentic workplace dilemmas, and clear peer exchange.

In 2016, Minori opened three of its core one-day seminars to the public from its permanent seminar room on the 4th floor of Lindehuis, located at Kade 41 in Rotterdam. The founding structure was preserved entirely: strictly sixteen seats arranged across four tables of four, led by one facilitator and one co-facilitator to ensure personal guidance and focused participation.

Today, the desk continues to hold its open public sessions exclusively on Thursdays, giving participants one full day away from the desk to think about the people they lead. Every seminar includes lunch and comprehensive printed seminar notes, maintaining an unhurried, quiet environment dedicated to practical communication frameworks and actionable workplace decisions.

## Audience
Our audience is managers and owners of mid-sized organisations: people in their first two years of leading a team (3–12 people), heads of department taking a team through a reorganisation, merger or new system, and owners and managing directors of firms with 10–80 staff who want to delegate more. Teams reach us through their organisation's reservation.

## Voice
The tone is calm, specific, and quietly confident, speaking in clear, natural business English without corporate jargon or hyperbole. It addresses experienced managers directly, focusing on practical workplace conversations, unhurried reflection, and realistic professional candour.

## Name and a typical day
Minori (a Japanese word for ripening) names a desk that believes learning needs time and a table. A typical Thursday: doors at 09:00, opening at the tables at 09:30, the morning idea at the flipchart, lunch at the same tables, a case clinic in pairs, and a written next conversation read to the table before the close at 17:00.
