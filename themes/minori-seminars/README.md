# Minori Seminars

An open-seminar landing theme for a corporate management training desk: one long conversion page with a centred anchor menu and one repeated call to action, "Reserve a seat". The English home page, one sample story and the anchor menu (Who, The day, Dates, Facilitators, Fees) are in place; inner pages and four more languages follow.

Signature techniques (from the reference study in brand/): a photo stage inset 35 px from both edges with a white notch card bitten out of its bottom-left corner and a statement that fades in word by word; a giant serif word cut at its middle by a diagonal tint band, over three white cards with plum and brick corner ticks and bracket-cornered titles; a two-tone rule before every label, square outline serif buttons with a long arrow, and a vertical DATES word beside a bordered panel of dated rows.

Scroll effects: sp-parallax (stage and enrol banner), sp-reveal--up with sp-stagger (cards, schedule rows), sp-count (the numerals). The header bar collapses from 80 to 44 px with a CSS scroll-driven animation.

The hero is a soft-focus close shot of a facilitator and two participants; the "who" and session photos use different framings (hands over a notebook, two people at a whiteboard, a side portrait).

All photographs in assets/images were AI-generated for this theme (CC0). The desk, its people, address, email address and prices are fictional.
