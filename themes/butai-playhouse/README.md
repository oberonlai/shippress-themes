# Butai Playhouse

A block theme for a repertory playhouse that sells seasons. Design notes: `brand/design-spec.md`, brand brief: `brand.md`.

- **Signatures:** a twin-pane stage hero (two full-height photographs abutting, a pulsing cue dot in the seam, a 74px caps ticker docked underneath); a black season board with a dim marquee line and five narrow poster cards; three abutting colour-flood ticket tiles with outline pills.
- **Season rail:** the season as a horizontal dated rail with seven milestones, a vertical list on small screens.
- **Fonts:** Playfair Display (display) and Source Sans 3 (text), bundled under `assets/fonts` with their OFL licences.
- **Status:** home page complete and polished (stage 2 fix); the menu links to the season, tickets, seats, visit, house, company, about, journal and contact pages. Inner-page copy and extra languages follow.
- **Home sections (patterns):** `front-hero`, `front-statement`, `front-board`, `front-rail`, `front-tiles`, `front-band`, `front-prices`, `front-company`, `front-journal`, `front-callsheet`, `front-visit`.
- **Parts:** `header` (one menu, corner furniture, text inverts over dark sections), `footer`.
- **Scroll effects:** `assets/sp-scroll.css|js` (marquee, reveal, stagger, parallax).
- **Forms:** `inc/shippress-forms.php` (shared form slot, works without plugins).
