## Concept

- **Location:** 2nd floor, 210 Alder Row, Victoria, BC V8W 1A1, Canada (fictional address): one daylight studio building with two rooms, the Window Studio (three tall arched windows, sprung maple floor, upright piano) and the Garden Studio (smaller, for pre-ballet and rehearsals).
- **Founded:** 2012, by Mira Ostrowska after fourteen years dancing with touring companies.
- **Audience:** families in Victoria looking for a calm first ballet class for a child, and adults who want to begin or return at any age.
- **Idea:** every class begins at the barre; live piano in every adult and Juniors class; small classes (12 children, 16 adults); children and adults in one building.
- **Hours:** front desk Mon–Fri 06:45–21:45, Sat 08:30–13:00, Sun closed.
- **Voice:** The voice is calm, clear, and unhurried, reflecting the steady focus of a daylight working studio. It communicates with practical precision and warmth, focusing on disciplined technique and daily studio practice.

## Story
Mira Ostrowska founded Suzu Ballet in 2012 after fourteen years dancing with touring companies across various stages. Seeking to create a quiet, permanent home for deliberate classical dance, she established the school on the second floor of 210 Alder Row in Victoria, British Columbia. From its inception, the school was designed to provide students of all backgrounds with patient instruction, anatomically sound corrections, and an atmosphere of calm dedication.

The facility comprises two specialized studios arranged for daily practice. The primary Window Studio features three tall arched windows admitting natural daylight, an upright piano, and a pale sprung maple floor engineered to protect the joints of developing and mature dancers. Down the hall, the Garden Studio offers an intimate setting for pre-ballet classes and ensemble rehearsals. To ensure musical sensitivity and genuine rhythm, live piano accompaniment is maintained for every adult class and every Juniors class.

Today, Suzu Ballet serves a diverse community spanning ages four through seventy and older. Whether guiding young children taking their first skips, training teenagers preparing for pointe work, or welcoming adults back to the barre after decades away, the teaching remains grounded in individual care. Each class emphasizes posture, alignment, and musicality, ensuring that every dancer leaves the studio carrying themselves a little taller.

## Process (trial class)
1. Book online or call our front desk to reserve your trial class spot.
2. Arrive 15 minutes early so we can show you the changing room and lend you a spot at the barre.
3. Take the class and experience the sprung maple floor and live piano accompaniment.
4. Talk with the teacher after class about the right level, schedule, and term enrolment.
