Taira Clinic is a vestibular and balance theme for a single clinic: a split hero, a symptom path, and a clear route to a 60-minute first assessment. The palette is slate, steel blue and white, set in Figtree and Manrope, with documentary photographs of treatment, testing and the street outside. It suits a small clinical practice that measures, retrains and returns people to ordinary walking.

== Description ==

Taira Clinic is a block theme for a vestibular and balance clinic, a physiotherapy practice or a falls-prevention service.

* A split hero: a photograph on the left that slowly crossfades between two moments of one session, a stepped four-line headline and two flat booking blocks beside it, a finder box docked on the corner of the photograph and a four-figure stats row.
* A row of plate cards for the symptoms people arrive with, cut off at the right edge.
* A narrative section with a tall photograph that drifts slower than the page, a method told as three hairline rows, one case with counted figures, dated notes with category chips, and a visiting box.
* A newsletter and a contact form that work without a plugin (they open the visitor's email app) and use Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when one is active.
* Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German.
* WooCommerce compatibility is declared; the theme works without it.
