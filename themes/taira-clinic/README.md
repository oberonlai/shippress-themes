# Taira Clinic

A block theme for a vestibular and balance clinic. Split hero with a docked finder box, a peeking row of plate cards, a narrative with a drifting photograph, a three-row method, one case and two flat booking blocks. Figtree and Manrope; slate, steel blue and white.

Sample content ships in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German. See `brand.md` for the fictional clinic's facts and `brand/design-spec.md` for the design.

The symptom card row has previous/next round buttons (assets/peek.js, click only, no scroll listener). Inner pages are short stubs until stage 2 inner; only English dictionaries exist so far.
