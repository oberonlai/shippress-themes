# Ibuki Cardiology - brand brief

Independent outpatient cardiology clinic in Northbridge. Sample content in English first; Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Ibuki Cardiology. "Ibuki" is the Japanese word for breath; the clinic is otherwise international and modern.
- **Location:** 14 Harbour Lane, 2nd floor, Northbridge (fictional). USD prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **Founded:** 2011 by Dr. Elena Varga.
- **Audience:** working adults with palpitations, high blood pressure, breathlessness or a family history of heart disease, and the people who care for them.
- **Tone:** The voice of Ibuki Cardiology is calm, precise, and reassuring, communicating clinical competence without medical jargon or dramatization. Every statement is grounded in specific timings, practical guidance, and predictable scheduling to foster quiet confidence and trust.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 08:00–17:30, Sat 09:00–12:30 (follow-ups only), closed Sunday and public holidays. Follow-up desk 13:00–16:00 weekdays.

## History
Dr. Elena Varga founded Ibuki Cardiology in 2011 at 14 Harbour Lane in Northbridge to establish an independent outpatient clinic centered on predictable, unhurried patient care. Having observed that traditional cardiology appointments often subjected patients to prolonged waiting times and delayed test reporting, she structured the practice around a guiding conviction: a heart keeps working hours, and so do we. The clinic was built from the beginning to combine comprehensive clinical cardiology with an orderly, transparent schedule that respects patient time.

To bring focused expertise to each condition, the clinic organizes its consultations across five dedicated weekday clinics. Monday is dedicated to heart rhythm disorders and Holter monitoring; Tuesday addresses blood pressure management; Wednesday focuses on heart failure and medication reviews; Thursday provides specialized echocardiography and exercise imaging; and Friday is dedicated to cardiovascular prevention and return to activity. This daily structure ensures that the appropriate clinical team, diagnostic equipment, and testing rooms are fully prepared for every patient.

Every new evaluation begins with a comprehensive ninety-minute first visit timed to the quarter hour, ensuring thorough testing and direct discussion with the cardiologist without rushing. Diagnostic results are reviewed within fourteen days, and our team directly contacts patients the moment reports are ready rather than leaving questions unanswered. Ongoing management continues through regular follow-up consultations scheduled at three, six, or twelve-month intervals, providing continuous and reliable cardiac oversight.

## A day at the clinic (the daily routine)
A heart keeps working hours, so the clinic runs one clinic a day: Monday Rhythm, Tuesday Blood Pressure, Wednesday Heart Failure, Thursday Imaging, Friday Prevention, consultations 08:30-17:00. The routine of a first visit, 90 minutes from 08:30: check-in 10, nurse, blood pressure and ECG 15, echocardiogram 30, cardiologist 25, plan and booking 10. Results are reviewed within 14 days; follow-ups every 3, 6 or 12 months.
