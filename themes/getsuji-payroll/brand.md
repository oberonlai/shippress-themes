# Getsuji Payroll - brand brief

Payroll bureau for small firms in Ghent, Belgium. Sample content in English (Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German follow). All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Getsuji Payroll. "Getsuji" is the Japanese word for monthly; the bureau is otherwise European and modern.
- **Location:** Weverslaan 14, 2nd floor, 9000 Ghent, Belgium (fictional). Phone +32 9 000 14 25, hello@getsuji-payroll.example.
- **History:** founded in 2014 by Hana Morel to solve the stressful monthly scramble between collecting hours and paying staff. It grew by working only with small firms that want a named clerk rather than a dashboard.
- **Audience:** owners of small firms (3 to 60 staff): bakeries, studios, cleaning contractors, professional practices.
- **Tone:** calm, clear, unhurried, first person plural. Factual, no jargon, no guarantees, no awards, no real brands.
- **Month:** timecards close on the 20th; the 21st-22nd are checks and questions; the 23rd the owner approves; the 25th everyone is paid; on the last working day contributions are filed. If the 25th falls on a weekend, pay on the Friday before.
- **Hours:** Mon-Fri 08:30-17:30; during close (20th-25th) the phone line is open until 19:00. Closed weekends and Belgian public holidays.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- About 60 client firms of 3 to 60 staff, about 1,100 payslips a month. Two-person check on every run. First consultation free, 45 minutes.
- Fees (EUR, excl. VAT): Monthly base 120 / month (up to 5 staff) - each further employee 9 / month - switching 350 one-off - starter or leaver 25 / change - extra pay run 60 / run - year-end statements 15 / employee. Invoiced on the 1st, payable in 14 days; monthly contract, one month's notice.

## Signature techniques (from OGINO Tax Accountant Office)
1. Framed full-bleed photo: flush navy logo panel, white message panel and white info bar cut into it, vertical MENU and credit line, round navy button.
2. Offset dot grid behind photos (1px dots every 8px, blue, partly covered by the photo).
3. Hairline label system: 80x1 orange rule under labels, 4px orange pole on index heads, 1px outline buttons, boxed tags.
Supporting: oversized thin numerals overlapping photos ("pick up 01", "Steady.").
