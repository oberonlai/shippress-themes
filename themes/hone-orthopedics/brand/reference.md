# Reference (draft, stage A)

Source: S5-Style gallery entry 10000 -> https://www.izumiiin.com/ (gastro/surgery clinic, Japan). Only one site used: the second candidate (an orthodontic site) stayed on its loading screen in headless Chrome and was dropped.
Screenshots: brand/ref/izumi-first.png (1440x900), brand/ref/izumi-full.png (1440x5000).

## Technique candidates (shape / composition / position)
- T-a Rounded-corner portrait strip: first screen is a row of tall photos with ~24px radius (photo 1 bleeding off the left edge, centre portrait ~420x690, photo 3 bleeding off the right edge), with a text column between them. Row sits under a slim header, with a gap above and below.
- T-b Thick rounded ribbon strokes: a fat (~50px) arc/stroke in accent colour sweeps from the lower right across the photo edge, with a large pale disc and a dark band behind it. Decorative, bottom-right of the first screen and again at section edges.
- T-c Vertical text: headline (about 5 columns of tall, weight-bold type) and a small paragraph set vertical beside the portrait. Cannot be copied as Japanese; for English use a rotated caption or a vertical label only.
- T-d Big light label + small caption pair: "News" / "Medical Care" in a large thin green face with a small caption under it, and an orange dot at its top-left.
- T-e Three tall photo cards with an arrow tab bottom-right of each image (corner notch), title and short text under it.
- T-f Hours table as a green header-row grid with dot marks inside a white rounded card over a pale-green band at the foot; map on the left.
