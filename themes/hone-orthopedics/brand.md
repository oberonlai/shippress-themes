# Hone Orthopedics: brand

## Who we are
Name: Hone Orthopedics. Hone Orthopedics is an orthopedic and sports injury clinic at 2180 Main Street, Vancouver, BC (Mount Pleasant), founded in 2011 by two surgeons and one physiotherapist who wanted joint specialists and rehabilitation in the same building. Phone (604) 555-0142, hello@hone-ortho.example. The clinic, its people and prices are fictional sample content.

## Location
Mount Pleasant, Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. Step-free entrance, street parking, a bus stop at the door. Hours: Mon–Fri 7:30–19:00, Sat 8:00–13:00, Sun and statutory holidays closed. Currency CAD.

## Audience
Adults with a painful or injured joint (knee, shoulder, hip, spine, hand, foot or ankle): recreational and competitive athletes, people whose work is physical, people living with arthritis who are weighing up surgery, and people recovering from an operation. Many are self-referred; a referral is welcome but not required.

## Booking
Request online or call by Wednesday 17:00, reply within one business day by 18:00, first visit the same week (Mon–Sat).

## Voice
