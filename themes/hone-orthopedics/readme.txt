Hone Orthopedics: a block theme for an orthopedic and sports injury clinic. A cool off-white ground with a slate primary, a first screen of tall rounded portraits bleeding off both edges, a thick round-capped ribbon stroke over a pale disc and a dark arc, and tall photo cards with a notched arrow tab. Works without any plugin. WCAG AA colours; no entrance animations.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The sample content is in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German (set by the ShipPress interface language or the site language; the theme's own words follow it):  a home page, a specialist directory with eight clinician profiles, conditions, sports injury, physiotherapy, first visit, fees, patient stories, about, newsletter, contact, booking and a journal.

== Installation ==

1. Download hone-orthopedics.zip from the releases page.
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Hone Orthopedics.

On activation the theme creates its pages with their text and images, six journal entries, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The clinic, its clinicians, addresses and prices are fictional. All photographs were generated with Grok for this theme and are released under GPL-2.0-or-later / CC0. They show no real people, places or brands.
