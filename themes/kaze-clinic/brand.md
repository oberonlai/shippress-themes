# Kaze Clinic - brand brief

An independent respiratory medicine clinic that treats breath as something you can measure and steady: an unhurried 45-minute first consultation, spirometry the same day, a written plan in plain language, and a named nurse who stays with the case. Breathlessness, persistent cough, wheeze and asthma, sleep breathing, COPD follow-up, post-viral cough and allergic airway disease.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kaze Clinic - respiratory medicine. Sub-line wherever the brand is introduced: "Respiratory medicine, since 2014".
- **Location:** 18 Linden Walk, Northmere (a fictional address). Email hello@kazeclinic.example, phone 020 5550 1420. Step-free entrance, 2 accessible parking bays, bus stop "Linden Walk" 80 m from the door. Fees in euros (EUR) in every language.
- **Founded:** 2014 by Dr. Ines Halvorsen, a respiratory physician who left hospital departments to build a quiet independent practice where consultations are unhurried and lung function is measured on the same day.
- **Audience:** adults with a cough that will not leave, breathlessness on stairs, wheeze at night, an inhaler they are unsure about, or suspected sleep-disordered breathing, who want a clear explanation and a written plan rather than another prescription.
- **Tone:** calm, clear and unhurried; care as a conversation at eye level. Plain words first, the clinical term second (every service, test, fee item and person is a plain title with a small clinical subtitle). No guaranteed outcomes, no invented statistics, no awards, no real media or brands.

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Hours: Mon-Fri 08:30-18:00; Thursday evening clinic to 20:00; Sat 09:00-12:30; Sunday and public holidays closed. Follow-up calls 17:30-18:00 on weekdays.
- Process (a first plan, 4 steps): 01 Listen - 45-minute first consultation; 02 Measure - spirometry the same day, other tests if needed; 03 Agree - a written plan you take home, in plain language; 04 Stay with it - a named nurse, a call at 2 weeks, a review at 6 weeks.
- Fees (EUR, self-pay): first consultation, 45 min, includes spirometry (new patient assessment) EUR 160; follow-up consultation, 25 min (review appointment) EUR 85; full lung function test (spirometry with reversibility and FeNO) EUR 140; home sleep study, one night with report (respiratory polygraphy) EUR 220; inhaler technique review with a nurse, 20 min (device and adherence check) EUR 45; breathing rehabilitation, 6 weekly sessions (pulmonary rehabilitation course) EUR 360. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before. Many insurers reimburse; we give an itemised receipt and never promise cover.
- Composite stories (names changed, labelled as composites): "The cough that outlasted a winter" (post-viral recovery), "Stairs again" (COPD management), "Nights without waking" (sleep breathing). No outcome guarantees or statistics.
- Newsletter: The Breathing Letter, monthly, first Tuesday, about 4 minutes to read.

## A day at Kaze Clinic (daily routine)
08:00 the team huddles over the day's notes; 08:30 the first tests and consultations begin; Thursday evening clinic runs to 20:00; 17:30-18:00 the named nurses make the follow-up calls. The routine is the same every working day, so no consultation is rushed and no result waits.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page; reference: evidencecare.jp)
- **Washed full-bleed photo that settles, cross-fades and insets:** the first screen is a pale high-key photograph that resolves from bright to normal, cross-fades between three slides and shrinks into a 40px frame over the first 400px of scroll; inner pages repeat it as a 60svh page head.
- **Colour-flip lead statement:** a grey statement on the pale ground turns white as a wide photograph fades in behind it.
- **Plain title plus clinical subtitle:** every pair is a bold plain-language title with a small grey clinical term, on photo columns with round green arrow discs.
- Supporting: a floating white header slab inset from the edges, two faint vertical hairlines behind the hero, a white card with a photograph on a mist band.
