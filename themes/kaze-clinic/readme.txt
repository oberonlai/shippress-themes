Kaze Clinic is a calm, accessible WordPress theme designed for independent respiratory medicine clinics and diagnostic practices. Built with a high-key documentary visual direction, clean Figtree and Manrope typography, and clear service layouts, it presents patient care and clinical measurements with quiet precision. The theme includes structured block patterns for consultations, lung-function testing, transparent fee lists, and multi-language support.

== Description ==
A block theme for an independent respiratory clinic. The home page opens on a washed, high-key full-bleed photograph that settles from bright to normal, cross-fades between three slides and shrinks into a framed window as you scroll, under a floating white header slab and above a thin info bar. It continues with a colour-flip lead statement (grey on the pale ground, turning white as a wide photograph fades in), plain-language titles paired with small clinical subtitles over three photo columns with round arrow discs, four method rows, a white proof card on a mist band with three composite stories, a four-row fee snapshot, clinic hours, the latest posts and a newsletter slot, closed by a deep patina footer. Ten inner pages (about, conditions, tests, first visit, clinicians, fees, stories, visit, book and a newsletter page) reuse the washed page head, the colour-flip lead and the title-plus-subtitle pairs.

= Images =
The sample photographs were generated for this theme. They contain no text, logos or real people.

= Forms =
The newsletter and contact forms use a dynamic block that shows a Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP form when one is active. Without a plugin the newsletter form still has an email field and a submit button that opens your email app with the address filled in; nothing is stored by the theme.
