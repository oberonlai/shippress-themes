# Kaze Clinic

Kaze Clinic is a calm, accessible WordPress theme designed for independent respiratory medicine clinics and diagnostic practices. Built with a high-key documentary visual direction, clean Figtree and Manrope typography, and clear service layouts, it presents patient care and clinical measurements with quiet precision.

Part of [ShipPress themes](https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). Design brief: `brand.md`; design notes: `brand/`.

## Features

- Washed full-bleed hero: three high-key photographs that settle from bright to normal, cross-fade, and inset into a 40px frame over the first 400px of scroll, with a floating white header slab and an info bar.
- Colour-flip lead statement: grey on the pale ground, white as a wide photograph fades in behind it; the statement sits on the empty wall at the right so no face is covered.
- Plain-language titles with a small clinical subtitle, photo columns and round arrow discs.
- Newsletter and contact forms use the shared form slot (MailPoet, Mailchimp for WP, Contact Form 7 or WPForms when active; a mailto fallback otherwise).
- Fonts: Figtree (display) and Manrope (body), loaded through theme.json; the block editor shows the same fonts as the front end.
- Languages: en, with zh-TW, ja, fr and de dictionaries to follow in the next build step.
