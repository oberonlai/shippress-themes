# Tayori Nursing — brand brief

Design plan row: b23-1 (home-visit nursing service, type business). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other languages follow. All names, places, people and figures are fictional. Nursing copy never promises an outcome.

## Concept
- **Name:** Tayori Nursing. "Tayori" means someone to rely on; the service is meant to be exactly that person at your door.
- **Location:** 14 Orchard Row, Linden Vale (a fictional town). Homes within 8 km of the office, about 20 minutes by bicycle or car.
- **Founded:** 2017 by registered nurse Mira Castell.
- **Services:** nursing visits (blood pressure, medicines, injections, wound care), rehabilitation at home (physiotherapy and occupational therapy), palliative care at home, a 24-hour on-call line for registered patients.
- **Audience:** older adults, people home after a hospital stay, and the families who look after them.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, unhurried, and grounded in practical clinical routines. It speaks directly and respectfully without jargon, dramatic claims, or false promises.

## Story
Founded in 2017 by registered nurse Mira Castell, Tayori Nursing began with the conviction that clinical care belongs in familiar surroundings where everyday life unfolds. Operating from an office at 14 Orchard Row in Linden Vale, the service was created to support individuals within an 8 km radius who require steady medical attention at home. The name Tayori reflects this purpose, signifying someone dependable to turn to during times of illness, recovery, or physical change.

Over the years, the practice expanded to assemble a dedicated multidisciplinary team comprising registered nurses, a physiotherapist, an occupational therapist, and a care-plan coordinator. Rather than offering hurried or impersonal appointments, the team structured visits around unhurried kitchen-table assessments and regular daytime rounds. By introducing a 24-hour on-call support line for registered patients, Tayori Nursing established a continuous, dependable bridge between hospital discharge and daily home living.

Today, Tayori Nursing continues to serve Linden Vale households seven days a week between 8:00 and 20:00. Whether helping an older adult regain independent mobility after surgery, administering ongoing wound therapies, or providing gentle palliative care, the team remains committed to the same founding standard established in 2017: delivering calm, professional nursing right to your living room.
