# Reference (draft)
Reference: https://www.yamatoen.or.jp/ (S5-Style entry; a care-welfare organisation site)
Type: home-visit nursing. Imagery: photo (high-key) with small line-art doodles.

## Signature candidates
1. Scattered "pebble" photo field over a warm grey ground with a wide green wave foot; small line-art doodle figures perch on the photo edges.
2. Sticky left heading with pill index tabs (01 / 02 / 03) beside tall white rounded cards that scroll past; card head has "01" left and a label right.
3. Grey-to-ink statement lines that fill while scrolling, laid out in two staggered blocks (left upper, right lower).
4. Green rectangular CTA with a white round arrow disc, beside a white rounded-square menu button.

## Colour
Ground #F3F1EF, accent #62B850 (we shift to celadon #5E7A70 family), ink #313131, white cards.
