# Tayori Nursing: design spec (stage 1, binding for implementation)

Slug `tayori-nursing`, home-visit nursing service, type `business` (WooCommerce declared compatible, not required). Design-plan row b23-1: archetype sticky-media, nav split, hero text-left-photo-right, first screen top-bottom-halves, page structure narrative-problem-method-proof, font direction **sans**.

Main reference: **yamatoen.or.jp** (https://www.yamatoen.or.jp/, S5-Style, care and welfare). Measurements: `brand/reference-tokens.md` (cited below as tok(a)…tok(g)). Screenshots: `brand/ref/koyukai-first.png` (first screen), `scroll-koyukai-before/after.png` (statement fill), `koyukai-sticky.png` (sticky cards). We borrow structure, techniques, motion ideas, colour relationships and the key-visual concept. We do **not** copy its photos, illustrations, characters, text or logo; every photo and doodle is generated by us.

Imagery: **photo** (stage-a IMAGERY: photo). High-key documentary photographs carry every section; small thick-outline flat-pastel doodles are accents only (style reference yamatoen.or.jp, drawn by us, different figures, never copied).

Similarity target: about 80% in palette relationships, image treatment, motion, decoration and layout rhythm. Copy, photos and doodles are our own.

## 1. Signature techniques (three, obvious at a glance, never diluted)

### T1. Scattered pebble-masked photos on warm grey with a celadon wave foot and own doodles (yamatoen first screen)
- Reference (koyukai-first.png): on ground `#F3F1EF` four to five photographs masked in soft irregular "pebble" shapes of different sizes are scattered across the screen; the biggest one bleeds off the left edge, another bleeds off the right edge; small thick-outline doodle figures sit on photo edges; a wide green wave hill (crest about 150 px) covers the lower part of the photos across the full width. tok(e), tok(f).
- Ours: home hero (section 1, geometry in §3) and every inner page head (small version: two pebbles + one doodle + wave foot 120 px).
- Pebble mask: **8-value border-radius on the figure, overflow: clip** (no clip-path on any observed element). Four fixed shapes, reuse by class:
  - `.tn-pebble--a` `border-radius: 46% 54% 42% 58% / 52% 44% 56% 48%`
  - `.tn-pebble--b` `border-radius: 58% 42% 55% 45% / 45% 56% 44% 55%`
  - `.tn-pebble--c` `border-radius: 40% 60% 50% 50% / 58% 48% 52% 42%`
  - `.tn-pebble--d` `border-radius: 52% 48% 60% 40% / 42% 58% 42% 58%`
- Wave foot: inline SVG path, full-bleed, fill celadon `#5E7A70`, crest height **150 px** desktop / **90 px** mobile (tok(f) "about 150px"), one long asymmetric swell (low at 45% width, high at 80% width), no stroke, no second layer. The wave sits **in front of** the photos (z-index above figures) so it bites the bottom 15–25% of the big-left and lower-right pebbles, exactly as on the reference.
- Must not be diluted: at least one pebble must bleed off the left edge and one off the right edge at 1440; pebbles must be 4 different sizes (largest ≥ 2× the smallest); the wave must be one saturated-looking celadon mass, not a pale tint or a thin line; the wave must overlap photos.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen, story and motion
yamatoen.or.jp opens on warm grey with four or five pebble-masked photographs of everyday care (a carer and an elderly woman laughing, a carer leaning in to talk, hands held across a table, a child at play), little line-art figures sitting beside them, and a big green wave hill across the foot. The story: care lives in ordinary daily moments, and the organisation is "a place to rely on" in the middle of life. The motion: photos and doodles drift in softly on load, the photos float at different depths while scrolling, and the statement below fills from grey to ink.

### (2) Our scene (home-visit nursing, same composition, same motion idea, same image type)
A bright living room on a weekday morning, as a visit. Top half: four pebble photographs of one home visit scattered on warm grey: a nurse fastening a blood-pressure cuff while the patient rests an arm on a side table (big, bleeding off left), the nurse and patient talking at the window (centre, drifting with parallax), hands sorting a weekly pill organiser (small, upper right, bleeding off right), and the nurse's bag by the front door as she arrives (lower right, bitten by the wave). Our own doodles of a nurse waving and a patient in an armchair with tea sit at the photo edges. Bottom half: the celadon wave hill carrying the headline "Nursing that comes / to your living room." with the CTA. Story: the nurse comes to the place where life already is. Motion: the pebbles move at different parallax speeds (drift), the doodles bob 4 px, and the statement below fills grey to ink.
