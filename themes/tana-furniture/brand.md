# Tana Furniture - brand brief

Independent furniture shop and workshop in a market town. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Tana Furniture.
- **Location:** a showroom and workshop at 22 Weir Lane, Aldmoor (a fictional market town). Email hello@tanafurniture.example, phone +44 1632 960 214 (fictional range). GBP (£) prices in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** founded in 2009 by Ines Haldane (upholsterer) and Tomas Reyes (joiner). They began with a single sofa frame built for a neighbour's narrow living room and have fitted pieces to the rooms households actually live in ever since.
- **Today:** a team of seven under one roof; the showroom shows twenty rooms' worth of pieces; everything is made to order in the workshop in eight to ten weeks.
- **Audience:** adults furnishing a real home with real constraints (sloping ceilings, box rooms, narrow stairs, a family of five at a kitchen corner) who want a few well-made pieces that fit.
- **Tone:** calm, factual and unhurried. Specific measurements and honest materials; no decorative sales language, no awards, press, ratings, customer counts or guarantees.

## People (7, named the same everywhere)
Ines Haldane (co-founder, upholstery and sofas) - Tomas Reyes (co-founder, joinery and tables) - Amara Okoye (room measuring visits) - Lukas Brandt (workshop lead) - Mei-Lin Chou (fabrics, leathers and finishes) - Samir Haddad (delivery and placement lead) - Clara Novak (showroom and orders).

## Fixed numbers (identical on every page and in every language)
- Opening hours: Tue-Sat 10:00-18:00, Sun 11:00-16:00, closed Mon. Home measuring visits Tue-Fri, slots 09:00 / 11:30 / 14:30, about 90 minutes, within 25 km.
- Process: 1 visit the showroom, 2 measure the room (home visit, 90 min), 3 make (8-10 weeks in our workshop), 4 deliver and place (two-person team, old piece taken away on request).
- Prices (GBP, VAT included): home measuring visit £45 (deducted from any order over £1,000); Weir Sofa, three-seat, from £2,850; Kiln Dining Table, solid oak, cut to 160-260 cm, from £1,980; Loft Dining Chair £395; Ash Bed Frame, double, £1,450; delivery and placement £85 within 40 km (beyond by quote); old piece taken away £30. Made-to-order pieces: 30% deposit, balance on delivery.
- WooCommerce demo products: Weir Sofa, Kiln Dining Table, Loft Dining Chair, Ash Bed Frame, Reading Desk £1,120, Hall Bench £640.

## Signature techniques (carried to every page)
1. Three-pane photo stage opening from black: two stacked panes left, one tall pane right, zero gap, a stepped white headline crossing the seam (shiodakagu.com first screen).
2. Grey band `#DBDBDB` with white photo-top cards and a 5 x 2 tile index of teal line icons under a bracketed centred label (shiodakagu.com products band).
3. Pentagon house-outline stamp as the single call to action, centred on a seam, scale 1.1 on hover.
4. Supporting: hairline-ruled cell strip with a photo on top and "View the room" (betchu-kagu.co.jp workshop strip).
