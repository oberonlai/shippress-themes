# Kikai Fair - brand brief

Trade fair for woodworking machinery, held in Hall 4 of a fictional riverside exhibition centre. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kikai Fair 2027, the 9th edition of a biennial fair.
- **Location:** Hall 4, Riverside Exhibition Centre, 12 Harbour Road, Lindmark (fictional, international-neutral). Organiser: Kikai Fair Office, 3rd floor, 40 Quay Street, Lindmark, hello@kikaifair.example, Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00.
- **Founded:** 2011 by the organiser team; fair director Mira Halvorsen.
- **Dates and hours:** Tue 9 - Thu 11 March 2027. Day 1–2: 09:00–17:30 · Day 3: 09:00–16:00 · Exhibitor build-up: Mon 8 March 07:00–20:00. Online pass requests close Sunday 21 February 2027.
- **Floor:** 22,000 m2, 180 exhibitors, 62 machines running under power, 4 zones (A Panel & CNC, B Edge & solid wood, C Finishing, D Extraction & tooling).
- **Audience:** joinery and cabinet workshops, production foremen, apprentices and teachers who want to see machines cut before they buy; exhibitors who sell woodworking machinery and tooling.
- **Tone:** The voice is calm, precise, and practical, speaking in the direct language of working joiners and production engineers. It prioritizes concrete technical specifications and documentary facts over marketing excitement or speculative claims.

## History
Kikai Fair was founded in 2011 as a dedicated biennial trade fair for woodworking machinery, created to serve production workshops seeking uncompromised mechanical demonstrations. From its first edition, the fair focused on presenting working equipment under live load, covering panel processing, CNC routing, edgebanding, solid-wood machining, finishing lines, dust extraction, and tooling. Rather than displaying static machinery on silent stands, the exhibition established a working hall standard that allowed joiners and cabinet makers to evaluate mechanical tolerances in real operating conditions.

Over subsequent editions leading to the 2025 event, the exhibition expanded its operational scope while maintaining its rigorous working-floor principles. At the 2025 fair, CNC maker Rafael Ortega demonstrated nesting efficiency on stand A-14 by processing the same production job six times across a single afternoon for visiting workshop owners. In zone B, custom cabinet maker Priya Nair evaluated and compared three competing edgebanders before placing an equipment order, while joinery teacher Jonas Lindqvist guided twelve apprentices equipped with dust meters across zone D to verify ambient extraction efficiency.

Returning for its 9th edition from 9 to 11 March 2027, Kikai Fair occupies 22,000 square metres across Hall 4 at Riverside Exhibition Centre, located at 12 Harbour Road in Lindmark. Managed from the Kikai Fair Office at 40 Quay Street under fair director Mira Halvorsen, the 2027 fair brings together 180 specialist exhibitors and 62 machines running under active power across four organized zones. The event continues its biennial mission to connect commercial workshops, skilled operators, and machine manufacturers through verifiable mechanical demonstration.
