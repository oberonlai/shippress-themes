## Audience
School leavers, career changers in their twenties to forties, parents choosing a trade for a child, and employers (electrical contractors, facilities teams, a lift company) who take on a trainee.

## Voice
The voice is calm, sober, and grounded in the quiet discipline of craft trades and real workshop standards. It uses specific, unadorned language that prioritises verified workmanship and physical evidence over promotional claims. Plain sentences, the work first and the course second; no promises about jobs or pass rates.

## Story
Ines Varga founded Akiha College in 1998 inside a converted brick foundry shed at Foundry Row 9, Calder Quay. Having spent years working as a site electrician, she established the workshop with four bays and twenty-four training boards, built specifically so trainees could learn through direct practice on real distribution gear and containment rather than textbook simulations.

From its earliest days, the college insisted on a clear division of learning: four days on commercial and industrial sites followed by one dedicated day back at the college workshop each week. Over nearly three decades, Akiha College developed close ties with forty-one partner employers throughout the region, including local electrical contractors, facilities teams, and specialist lift engineering companies who take on trainees following a structured trial week.

Today, the college continues to operate from the original foundry building, holding two intakes of twenty-four apprentices each year. The core philosophy remains unaltered: every skill is proven through physical execution, and no instructor or assessor signs off work they have not directly inspected with their own eyes.

## How a year is judged
Six signed pieces, always in this order: the board, the tray run, the gland, the test sheet, the site visit (three assessor visits a year) and the logbook.

## When an employer takes a trainee on
Three conditions: the twelve-week foundation term is complete; the first two signed pieces (the board and the gland); a trial week on the employer's site with a visit from Paloma Iturbe on day three. Forty-one partner employers.
