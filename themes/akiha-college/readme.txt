Akiha College is an editorial WordPress theme built for an electrical apprenticeship college, featuring a framed hero window and docked navigation. Grounded in quiet monochrome tones and robust serif typography, it structures apprentice portfolio cases, practical assessment milestones, and employer routes with calm clarity. Designed for rigorous vocational presentation, it reflects authentic workshop standards and documented craftsmanship established in Calder Quay since 1998.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

Fonts: Newsreader (headings), Source Serif 4 (reading text), Noto Serif TC and Noto Serif JP (Traditional Chinese and Japanese headings); all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

== Installation ==

1. Download akiha-college.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/akiha-college.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Akiha College.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, three journal entries with two categories and five tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Optional forms: the contact and newsletter sections use a small dynamic block. With Contact Form 7 or WPForms (contact) or MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP (newsletter) active, their form is shown; without a plugin the newsletter shows an email field that opens your mail app, and the contact page shows a mailto button.

Scroll effects use the bundled sp-scroll files (assets/sp-scroll.css and assets/sp-scroll.js); they respect prefers-reduced-motion.

The college, its people, sites, prices and photographs are fictional.
