Mochi Pet Supply is a neighbourhood pet-food and supplies shop in Leith, Edinburgh, founded in 2017 and selling online across the UK in pounds. The site starts with three questions — species, age, and one thing to watch — then shows the food and a subscribe-and-save price at 10% off repeat deliveries. Shop, product, cart, checkout, account and order-received pages are set up for WooCommerce and still read as ordinary pages without it.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download mochi-pet-supply.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/mochi-pet-supply.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Mochi Pet Supply.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, sample stories, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice. Without WooCommerce the theme works as a plain site and the home page shows the product strip as static cards.

The shop, its products, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
