# Mochi Pet Supply - brand brief

Neighbourhood pet-food and supplies shop with an online store in Leith, Edinburgh, selling across the UK. Sample content in English (stage 1) and, from stage 2, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Mochi Pet Supply. Founded **2017** by Isla Morrow and Tomasz Kowal.
- **Location:** shop and packing room at **18 Brunton Lane, Leith, Edinburgh EH6 6QT** (fictional). GBP prices (£) in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** Mochi Pet Supply opened in 2017 on Brunton Lane in Leith, when Isla Morrow and Tomasz Kowal decided a neighbourhood shop could sell pet food the way a household buys food for itself. Isla had been a veterinary nurse and wanted the shop floor to be a place where someone could ask a plain question and leave with a bag that matched the animal at home. Tomasz took on buying, reading ingredient lists and keeping the suppliers few enough that the packing room could still name what was in each sack. The shop and the packing room share 18 Brunton Lane, Edinburgh EH6 6QT. Orders placed by 14:00 on a weekday are packed that day and sent across the UK, arriving in one or two working days. Delivery is free over £40 and £3.95 below that. On Tuesday and Friday, Kofi Mensah drives a local van round through Leith, Portobello and Stockbridge at no charge. Lucas Ferreira runs the packing room, and Hannah Lindgren looks after the small-pet shelves, from timothy hay to cotton beds and ceramic bowls. A subscription takes 10% off every repeat delivery. Customers choose every 2, 4, 6 or 8 weeks, and they can skip, change or cancel up to 48 hours before dispatch. Priya Nair runs the three-question food finder at the counter for anyone who would rather talk it through in the shop. On Thursdays from 15:00 to 19:00, Dr Amara Osei holds free 20-minute chats at the nutrition desk. Those chats are general guidance only. They are not a diagnosis, and illness still belongs with a vet. Unopened food, and unused beds and bowls, can come back within 30 days.
- **Audience:** households choosing food for a dog, cat or small pet who want a plain answer and a bag that matches the animal at home.
- **Tone:** Sound like a shopkeeper on Brunton Lane: calm, specific, and glad to name the bag, the hour and the price. Keep every figure exactly as the shop states it, and never promise a health result.
