# Kyukyu College

Editorial block theme for a paramedic training college. Navy ink on a cool grey ground, a hairline page frame with a centre split, a giant section word with a red subtitle, a three-line name lockup beside a framed photograph under crossed translucent polygons, sticky term chapters with red numerals, a ruled ride-along week and a six-row price list.

## Included
- English home page, nine inner pages (About, Paramedic Diploma, Ride-along, Admissions, Fees, Faculty, Campus, Stories, Contact) and six posts; the main menu links to them.
- Page-head patterns `page-course`, `page-ride-along`, `page-admissions`, `page-fees` (use the "Page with its own head" template).
- Fonts: Newsreader and Source Sans 3 (Latin), Noto Serif TC and Noto Serif JP (headings, subset), all OFL.
- Scroll effects: reveal, stagger, count-up and a parallax hero and band (`sp-scroll`), static under reduced motion.

Further languages (zh-TW, ja, fr, de) follow in a later release.

See brand.md and brand/design-spec.md.
