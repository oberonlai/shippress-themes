# Kikin Advisors — brand

## History
Kikin Advisors was founded in Bristol in 2014 by Ruth Halloran to solve a persistent imbalance in workplace pensions. Employers across the United Kingdom were facing complex automatic enrollment obligations, yet the market offered almost no independent guidance prior to contract execution. Most businesses were forced to rely on sales representatives and brokers who were remunerated through provider commissions or asset charges. Ruth established Kikin Advisors on an uncompromising principle: independent, fee-only briefings that provide company directors, trustees, and finance leads with objective clarity before committing to any pension scheme.

From our office at 27 Wharf Lane in Bristol, we developed our signature sixty-minute briefing model. Designed specifically for organisations employing between 5 and 500 staff, each briefing breaks down scheme mechanics into three twenty-minute parts: plan assembly, workforce communications, and the critical next conversation. We operate without commission, volume kickbacks, or commercial ties to financial institutions. Because our advisers never sell financial products or manage fund assets, our contribution curves and fee analyses remain strictly objective illustrations rather than speculative sales projections.

Over the past decade, our practice has grown into a dedicated team of seven pension professionals, including specialists in contribution design, scheme analysis, and multilingual member communications. We deliver briefings online nationwide and in person for employers within 40 kilometres of Bristol. Whether guiding an independent bakery through initial compliance or assisting an established logistics firm with cross-shift consultations in English and Mandarin, our mission remains unchanged: ensuring employers choose and manage workplace pensions with confidence, transparency, and total commercial independence.

## Audience
Our audience is employers with 5 to 500 staff who are choosing, or re-tendering, a workplace pension scheme: owners, finance leads, HR managers, directors and trustees. Staff and members reach us only through their employer's briefing.
