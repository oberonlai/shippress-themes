== Description ==

Kanade International School is a WordPress block theme for an IB World School in Lisbon, founded in 1994, teaching children aged 4 to 18 in English. The templates present the Primary Years, Middle Years and Diploma Programmes, admissions, the 2026–27 fees in euros, campus life and the people who meet families. The same facts are set down for English, French, German, Japanese and Traditional Chinese. The home page opens on a photograph at the left with a curved bite of the page ground at its lower right, carrying an open-morning card, and three gateways for prospective families, current parents and staff. A tinted programme mosaic, calendar cards with a large corner and a date badge, an edge-to-edge day strip, the full fee list, the people roster, family stories and a visit block follow. Literata for headings, Fira Sans for reading. Works without WooCommerce.
