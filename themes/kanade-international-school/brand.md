# Kanade International School — brand brief

Design plan row: b7-6 (international school, K-12, IB; type education). Stage one: the home page in English. Inner pages and the other four languages follow. All names, places and people are fictional. "IB World School" appears as a programme framework only; nothing promises a score, a university place or a ranking.

## Concept
Kanade International School was founded in 1994 in Lisbon. The campus is at Rua do Jardim Alto 18, 1400-150 Lisboa, Portugal: a low white building with pale oak, large windows and a courtyard that takes the morning light. Teaching is in English from Kindergarten to Grade 12. Children keep a home language beside it. Portuguese begins in Kindergarten, French or German may be added from Grade 6, and mother-tongue clubs run alongside the taught courses. The Primary Years, Middle Years and Diploma Programmes are all offered here, as one IB World School, so a student need not change campus to move from the early years to the Diploma.

Six hundred and forty students, ages 4 to 18, come from about 40 nationalities. Kindergarten is for ages 4 and 5. Primary Years continues through Grade 5, Middle Years covers Grades 6 to 10, and the Diploma Programme is Grades 11 and 12. The school day starts with arrival at 8:20. Lessons run from 8:30 to 15:20, and activities continue until 16:30. The office is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 16:30. Library, laboratories, music rooms, dining hall and outdoor courts are all on this address, and younger and older students use them on the same day.

Families may enter in August or in January, and at other points in the year when a place is free. An enquiry by email or phone is answered within two school days. A visit is an open morning on a published Tuesday, 9:30 to 11:00, or a private tour. An application includes previous school reports and a one-time application fee of €250. Assessment and a family meeting follow within ten school days of a complete application. A place is confirmed with an enrolment deposit of €2,000, credited to the final term. Tuition for 2026–27 is quoted in euros and may be paid in three instalments, in August, January and April.

## Admissions process
1. Enquire by email or phone. The admissions office replies within two school days.
2. Visit an open morning, Tuesday 9:30–11:00, or arrange a private tour.
3. Apply online with previous school reports and the €250 application fee, which is one-time and non-refundable.
4. Attend an assessment and a family meeting within ten school days of a complete application.
5. Accept the offer and pay the €2,000 enrolment deposit, credited to the final term, to confirm the place.
