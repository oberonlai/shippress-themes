Kyugo Training — a block theme for a workplace first-aid training centre or vocational school. Two fixed white side rails frame a 1200px photo stage; the hero is a three-photo crossfade under an olive corner wash with a three-line white statement; a stacked-paper card stack tells the training day; dated notes, a wide parallax photo, a counted stat band, instructor portraits flush to the edges and a horizontal four-step booking line complete the home page. Hanken Grotesk and Schibsted Grotesk. Sample content in English. Works without WooCommerce. WCAG AA colours; scroll effects respect reduced motion.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See brand.md and brand/design-spec.md for the design notes.

== Installation ==

1. Download kyugo-training.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/kyugo-training.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Kyugo Training.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates the home page with all its text and images, six notes with three categories and two tags and the menu, and sets the front page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The centre, its people, addresses, telephone number, e-mail and prices are fictional.
