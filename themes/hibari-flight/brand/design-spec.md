# Hibari Flight: design spec (stage B, binding for implementation)

Slug `hibari-flight`, private pilot flight school on a regional airfield (type `education`, no WooCommerce needed; declare compatibility only). Design row b24-2: archetype split-screen, nav **overlay**, first screen **top-bottom-halves**, page structure **process-timeline** (horizontal, never a vertical line), palette family steel (row hex `#4E6874`, Masuhana), font direction **sans**, imagery **photo** (documentary).

References (measurements in `reference-tokens.md`, screenshots in `ref/`):
- Main: Kids Nature School, https://kids-ns.goldwin.co.jp/ (outdoor nature school for children).
- Secondary: Honda careers, https://www.honda-jobs.com/ (centred statement over drawn route arcs with travelling vehicles, three tall photo strips at the fold).

Borrow structure, techniques, colour relationships, motion and the **key visual concept**. Never copy their photos, illustrations, characters, copy or logos. All photos are generated by us (Grok, one STYLE); all stickers and the mark are SVG line-art drawn by us.

Fixed facts (every page, all five languages; section 9 is the master list): **Hibari Flight School**, founded **2011** by **Margit Halvorsen** and **Ruth Abernathy**, based in **Hangar 2, Brackmoor Airfield, Field Road, Alder Vale** (fictional, internationally neutral). Currency **USD** in every language; no converted prices.

## 1. Signature techniques (three, all obvious at a glance)

### T1. One-big-corner photo window on a white page, headline lying on the photo (Kids Nature School first screen, https://kids-ns.goldwin.co.jp/, `ref/kidsns-first.png`)
- Reference: under a 72px white header (crest left on a navy organic blob, a small centred wordmark, a round navy menu button right) sits ONE large photograph window, square on three corners and with **one huge rounded corner at the top right**; a heavy white two-line headline lies directly on the photo at the left, a white pill link with a navy arrow disc sits near the window's bottom right, drawn animals straddle the window edges, and a quiet news line runs on white under the window.
- Hibari (home block 1, desktop 1440×900): window `left: 72px; right: 72px` (= `5vw` each side, token (c) / screenshot), `top: 72px`, height **588px** (y 72–660), `border-radius: 0 clamp(96px, 16vw, 240px) 0 0` (230px at 1440; token (d) "one very large corner, ~15% of width"). Photo H01 `object-fit: cover; object-position: 60% 50%`. Left-to-right scrim on the photo only behind the text: `linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(0,22,80,.38) 0%, rgba(0,22,80,.12) 45%, rgba(0,22,80,0) 62%)`.
- Headline: H1 white, Schibsted Grotesk 800, `clamp(2.75rem, 5.6vw, 5.0625rem)` (81px at 1440 = token (b) section word 80.9px), line-height 0.98, letter-spacing -0.03em, two lines forced with `<br>` in en; left edge at window left + 84px (ref x 155 − 72), top of H1 at 44% of window height. Under it the sub line Schibsted 700 1.5rem white, margin-top 24px.
- Pill link on the photo: white `#FFFFFF` pill 46px tall, padding 0 14px 0 20px, radius 100px, text "Book a trial flight" Schibsted 700 0.96875rem (15.5px, token links) ink, followed by a 22px navy `#001650` disc with a white → arrow; position: right edge 240px inside the window's right edge, bottom 28px. Hover: overshoot scale 1.04, `0.5s cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1)` (token (g)).
- Inner pages: every inner page head is a smaller T1 window: `left/right 72px`, height 420px (≥ 960px) / 4:3 (mobile), same single top-right corner `clamp(64px, 11vw, 160px)`, page title (Schibsted 800 `clamp(2.5rem, 4.6vw, 4.25rem)`) white on the photo bottom-left with the same scrim, a one-line English label above it (0.8125rem 700 ls .08em uppercase). Photo per page is in section 4. Applies to all 9 inner pages and the posts index (posts index uses H13).
- Must not be diluted: only ONE corner is rounded and it is the top right and ≥ 200px at 1440 (not 24, not all four); the window is inset 72px from both edges on white (not full-bleed); the headline sits ON the photo (no panel, no card behind it); window height ≥ 560px at 1440.

### T2. Drawn-creature stickers straddling the edges of staggered photos of three ratios, over pale organic blobs (Kids Nature School CONCEPT, `ref/scroll-kidsns-before-concept.png`, `ref/scroll-kidsns-concept-photos.png`)
- Reference: a huge navy uppercase section word (81px) on white with pale organic blobs bleeding off the page edges; photographs with **square corners** in three ratios (0.78 portrait, 0.72 small portrait, 1.13 landscape) placed at staggered heights, two of them bleeding off the left or right page edge; painted animals (deer, squirrel, wolf, leaf; 80–160px) sit **half on the photo, half off it**, swaying slowly.
- Hibari: our own SVG stickers (section 6): `lark-perched`, `lark-flying`, `windsock`, `headset`, `cloud-puff`. Ink outline `#1B2433` 2px with a slightly wobbly hand line (paths drawn freehand-style, not geometric), flat fill ochre `#C9923E` (larks) / windsock orange `#D9632B` with white bands / slate `#4E6874` (headset), no gradients, no shadows. Sizes 80–160px (desktop), 56–96px (mobile).
- Placement rule: each sticker's centre is within ±12px of a photo edge, so 40–60% of the sticker lies over the photo. `position: absolute` on the figure wrapper (`.hf-sticker--top-left` etc.), `z-index: 2`, `pointer-events: none`, `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Motion: own CSS keyframes `hf-sway` (`rotate(-4deg) → rotate(4deg)`, transform-origin bottom centre), **5s ease-in-out infinite alternate** (token (g) `obj_shake` 5s), off under `prefers-reduced-motion`, `html.sp-no-motion` and in the editor.
- Blobs: cloud grey `#ECF0F2` organic shapes (SVG, 600–900px, token (f)), bleeding off the left or right page edge behind section words and photos; never more than 25% of a screen.
- Home: blocks 2 (approach), 4 (licences), 7 (crew); first screen (3 stickers on the window). Inner pages: About, Licences, Crew, The Field, Contact (section 4).
- Must not be diluted: photos keep **square corners** (only T1 windows are rounded); at least one photo per T2 block bleeds off the page edge; stickers must straddle edges (never float in white space, never fully inside a photo); stickers are filled ochre/orange, not grey outlines; section word ≥ 4.25rem navy.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen, story, signature motion
Kids Nature School's first screen is one big photograph of a wide landscape (a mountain ridge overlooking a plain under a soft blue-grey sky) set in a window with one huge rounded corner, with a heavy white headline about learning from nature and a short Japanese sub line lying on the photo. A navy organic blob holds the school crest at the top left; a round navy menu button sits top right with a small centred wordmark between them. Hand-painted characters stand on the window edges: a hiker in a sun hat straddling the bottom edge beside a white "see events" pill, a deer and a wolf on the right edge, a backpack on the bottom-left corner. Under the window, on white, a quiet dated news line. Story: **"we can learn from nature"**: real children outdoors, made warm and playful by the drawn creatures that live around the photos. Signature motion: long soft reveals (opacity + translate, 3s, `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`), the creatures' idle sway (5s loop), and the peeking slide strip in the navy band.

### (2) Our translated scene (private pilot school, same composition, same image types: documentary photo + drawn creatures)
**"Read the wind. Then fly."** The window holds a high-key documentary photograph of a regional airfield under a soft blue-grey overcast: on the right half a white two-seat low-wing trainer, canopy open; the instructor stands on the wing walk at the wing root and points out across the grass toward the orange windsock on the left horizon, while the student in the cockpit fastens a headset (H01). The left 45% is open grass and big sky, where the headline lies. The school's drawn creature is the **lark** (hibari): a lark perched on the window's bottom edge plays the hiker, a windsock drawing on the right edge plays the deer, a headset drawing on the bottom-left corner plays the backpack. The navy blob holds our lark roundel mark. Under the window the "field board" replaces the news line: the next trial-flight morning, the next ground-school intake and today's field hours. Story: learning to fly by reading the real sky with a person beside you, warm rather than technical. Motion: the photo drifts inside the window (parallax), the stickers sway, and further down the lesson strip peeks in the navy band.

## 3. Colour

Source: Kids Nature School relationships (white ~60%, photo ~35%, navy as ink + menu disc + blob on the first screen, a deep navy band ~44% of one later screen, tiny orange accents on drawings). Navy values kept within ±5% lightness of the reference tokens. Two swaps, with reasons:
1. Reference warm pale shape `#F8F8EC` → cool cloud grey `#ECF0F2`: the pipeline forbids a beige/cream default and the sky story wants a cool pale; same role, same lightness (L 94%).
2. The row's Masuhana slate `#4E6874` is **not** the main colour: Kanjo College (recent) used slate `#5C6A72` as its main/band; the reference's navy also suits aviation (uniforms, charts). `#4E6874` stays as the secondary (meta text, headset sticker, rules on dark, inactive UI).
