Hozue Food Festival is a refined, documentary-styled WordPress theme crafted for regional food and wine weekends, maker gatherings, and seasonal riverbank events. Built with an editorial serif aesthetic, floating navigation cards, and structured producer panels, it delivers an unhurried longform landing experience focused on early-bird ticket reservations. The design pairs warm, natural tones with clear logistics to celebrate local growers, cooks, and winemakers.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). The home page is one long early-bird ticket page: an olive header strip with the dates and venue, a framed photograph window that cross-fades three moments of the weekend with the title set on the ground below it, a floating card-row of quick links that ends in the one ticket call, a rounded olive-grey panel of programme cells for Saturday and Sunday, a full-width price table, a full-width riverbank photograph that moves slower than the page, seven people as text rows, the way in, six questions, one journal post and a closing call with a waitlist email field. See README.md for the design notes. Inner pages (About, Programme, Stallholders, Tickets, Getting here, FAQ, Newsletter, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Thanks and the journal) share a smaller framed photo window with a card-row of in-page links, the rounded panel cells and the caps section words with corner chips.

== Installation ==

1. Download hozue-foodfest.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/hozue-foodfest.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Hozue Food Festival.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, a journal entry with category and tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

The scroll effects (assets/sp-scroll.css and sp-scroll.js) respect prefers-reduced-motion and show the final state without JavaScript. The newsletter and contact slots use Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Mailchimp for WP when active; without a plugin they fall back to an email link with a visible notice.

The festival, its people, address, telephone number, email, dates and prices are fictional.
