# Hozue Food Festival: design spec (stage 1 spec, binding for implementation)

Slug `hozue-foodfest`, regional food and wine festival, early-bird ticket landing page. Row b37-3: archetype horizontal-strip, hero **framed-window**, nav **overlay** (header strip sitting over the page top, sticky), page structure **landing-longform** (one long conversion page, anchor menu, one repeated CTA), font direction **serif**, type **landing**.

Reference (single, unused by earlier themes): **Fuji Rock Festival** https://fujirockfestival.com/ . Measurements: `brand/reference-tokens.md`, quoted as **RT(a)…RT(g)**. Screens: `brand/ref/fuji-first.png`, `brand/ref/fuji-full.png`.

**Imagery: photo** (stage-a IMAGERY = photo; the reference leads with documentary crowd photography). All photos are generated by us with one STYLE string (§6); we never copy the reference's photos, icons, copy, logo or page layout.

## 1. Signature techniques (three; obvious at a glance; not to be diluted)

### T1. Framed photo window + cross-fade + floating rounded card-row (RT(e), RT(c), RT(d), RT(f), RT(g))
- **Reference (fujirockfestival.com first screen):** under the 68 px orange bar, a stage-wide photo slideshow (≈1440 × 720, 2:1) with a double-exposure fade between slides (Splide fade 800 ms, 3600 ms autoplay), a row of round dots near the photo bottom, and a **floating 72 px strip** of icon + caption tiles below it, 16–20 px gap, r24 end caps, soft shadow 4px 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.15), 1 px dividers between tiles.
- **Ours (home hero):** framed window inset 20 px each side, r24, 3 moments (H1 → H2 → H3) cross-fading, 3 round dots; H1 below the photo; a floating card-row of 5 tiles under the text (geometry in §3). Last tile is the single CTA, filled wine `#5B2A38`, white text (the reference's blue "Buy Tickets" tab moved into the strip end).
- **Inner pages:** every inner page head = the same framed window, smaller (1400 × 360, ≈3.9:1, r24, single photo, no fade), page title below it, and a **card-row of 3–5 in-page anchors** (e.g. Programme: Saturday / Sunday / Wine Tent / Families) with the same r24 strip, dividers and shadow; the last tile is the wine "Early-bird tickets" CTA. On About, Programme, Stallholders, Tickets, Getting here, FAQ, Newsletter, Contact.
- **Not to be diluted:** window must have r24 corners and visible 20 px side inset (not full-bleed); text never sits on the photo; strip must float (shadow + r24 ends), height 72 px desktop; tiles have a line icon (24 px, 1.5 px stroke, olive) + caption; the CTA tile is the only filled tile.

### T2. Big r24 panel holding a two-column feed of nested r12 cells (RT(d) news panel)
- **Reference:** NEWS block = a taupe panel `#D9CFC5` r24, padding ≈40 px, holding two columns of lighter `#E3DBD4` r12 cells; each cell = 3:2 thumbnail r8 + date stamp + title.
- **Ours (home #programme):** panel `#DDDDCB` r24, padding 48 px (desktop) / 20 px (mobile), max-width 1400; inside, two columns (gap 20 px) of 3 cells each (left = Saturday, right = Sunday), cell `#EBEBDE` r12 padding 16 px, grid `160px 1fr` (thumbnail left 3:2 r8, 160 × 107; text right: stamp "SAT 11:00" in wine 13px caps letter-spacing .12em, title Fraunces 22px, one line of description 15px).
- **Inner pages:** Programme (full Sat/Sun listing, 6 cells + text-only cells), Journal/news index (post list styled as cells), Stallholders (people as text cells, no portraits), FAQ (questions grouped in two panel columns), Getting here (three travel cells).
- **Not to be diluted:** panel colour must be visibly darker than ground (#DDDDCB on #F7F6F5), cells lighter than panel; both radii (24 outer, 12 inner) visible; two columns at ≥1024.

### T3. Centred caps section words with flat corner chips (RT(b), RT(f))
- **Reference:** FEATURED / NEWS headings centred, uppercase, bold ≈32 px, coloured; flat colour shapes at section corners (row of half-discs, short gradient bar).
- **Ours:** each home section opens with a centred Work Sans 600 caps word, 2rem (32 px), letter-spacing .08em, olive `#7A7339` (darkened olive for text contrast 4.8:1 on ground), with the Fraunces section title under it. At the section's top-left corner (desktop) a **chip cluster**: three overlapping solid discs 28 px (olive #938B4B, wine #5B2A38, olive-grey #DDDDCB; each overlaps the previous by 10 px — "wine-glass bubbles") and a 64 × 6 px bar with a linear gradient #938B4B → #5B2A38, r3, 12 px under the discs. Inline SVG in the pattern markup (decorative, aria-hidden), not CSS `content`.
- **Inner pages:** every inner page's main sections use the same caps word + chip cluster (at least once per page).
- **Not to be diluted:** caps word ≥ 2rem, centred; discs solid (no outline), 3 discs, overlap visible.

### Supporting (required)
- Olive header strip 68 px (RT(c)), dates + venue in caps with letter-spacing .12em next to the wordmark (RT(b)), language pill r999 1 px ink border.
- Round dot pager (RT(f)) under every slideshow.

## 2. Key visual concept

### (1) Reference first screen: concept, story, signature motion
fujirockfestival.com opens with a framed, stage-wide documentary photograph of festival-goers walking in under the wooden festival gate at night, the slides double-exposing into each other. Above it the orange bar states the dates and the venue; below it a floating strip of icon tiles (timetable, area guide, festival food, FAQ, tickets, access); a fixed "Buy Tickets" tab sits at the right edge. **Story:** the festival is a place you walk into with a crowd, and the dates and the ticket are always one tap away. **Signature motion:** 800 ms cross-fade every 3.6 s with an overlap (double exposure); dot pager follows; strip scrolls sideways with arrows.

### (2) Our translation (same composition, same motion idea, same image type)
A high-key documentary photograph in a framed window: **winemaker Tomás pouring a tasting glass across a stall counter while cook Priya plates a skewer behind him** (H1), cross-fading to **Priya's hands plating skewers over the grill** (H2) and to **visitors walking in under bunting at the riverside entrance** (H3 — the direct translation of the reference's crowd-under-the-gate moment). The olive bar states dates and venue; the floating card-row under the title holds the quick links and ends in the one ticket call. Story: "you walk into a riverbank of growers, cooks and winemakers; the dates and the early-bird ticket are always one tap away."
