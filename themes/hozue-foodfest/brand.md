## Story
Hozue Food Festival was first held in 2016 in Hozue Riverside Park along Weir Lane in Alderford. Marguerite Ostby founded the gathering with a small cluster of regional growers, cooks and winemakers who wanted to share their harvest directly with the community. What began as a riverbank picnic has grown steadily over the decade into a two-day weekend.

The 2027 festival marks the 10th edition, bringing together 64 independent market stalls and 18 regional winemakers across Saturday 11 and Sunday 12 September. From Sven Lindahl's wood-fired sourdough to Tomás Achterberg's hillside wines, every participant brings their own produce and craftsmanship to the water's edge. Long communal tables and open-air tasting tents line the river and keep the unhurried spirit of the founding morning.

Throughout its history the festival has kept a quiet commitment to regional stewardship, seasonal cooking and waste reduction. Glass return stations, a compost tent and direct relationships with local growers keep the park clean after pack-down on Sunday evening.

## A day at the festival (the daily routine)
The daily routine: gates open for ticket holders at 10:30, the morning market at 11:00 with vegetables, bread and cheese, a long-table lunch at 13:00, Wine Tent tastings at 16:00 and music on the lawn through the evening on Saturday. Sunday starts with Bread & fire at 11:00, a family picnic lawn at 12:30 and the last pour on the river terrace at 17:00, before the quiet clear-up by the river and the compost tent.
