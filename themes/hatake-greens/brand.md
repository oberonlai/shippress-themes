# Hatake Greens - brand brief

Weekly vegetable membership for city households (landing-page theme). Sample content in English first; zh-TW, ja, fr, de follow in stage 2. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hatake Greens (hatake = field). International, quiet, documentary.
- **Location:** packing barn at 14 Orchard Lane, Wren Valley (fictional). USD prices in every language.
- **History:** founded in 2019 by Ines Calder (grower relations) and Tomas Achebe (packing). Hatake Greens was founded in 2019 by Ines Calder and Tomas Achebe at a packing barn on 14 Orchard Lane in Wren Valley. The project began with a straightforward purpose: to bring morning-harvested vegetables directly into city households without the storage delays of conventional grocery supply chains. Working from the barn, the team established a weekly vegetable membership focused on seasonal freshness, steady farm partnerships, and transparent neighbourhood delivery.

From the start, the membership relied on close collaboration with six partner farms located within 60 kilometres of Wren Valley. Growers including Hana Moreau at Moreau Field, Rafael Ortiz at Ortiz Roots, Priya Nair at Nair Herb Garden, and Kwame Mensah at Mensah Hill harvest produce early on delivery mornings. The standard crate was designed to hold nine varieties of field vegetables and greens, weighing approximately 4.5 kilograms, reflecting what actually thrives in the soil each week.

Today, Hatake Greens delivers to twelve city neighbourhoods within 40 kilometres of the Wren Valley barn every Tuesday and Thursday. The schedule remains strictly timed: crops are harvested from 5:00, packed into reusable wooden crates by 9:00, and brought to doorsteps between 14:00 and 19:00 on the same afternoon. By keeping transit distances short and orders predictable, the service ensures that food gathered at dawn arrives on member counters in time for dinner.
- **Today:** 6 partner farms within 60 km, 12 city neighbourhoods, crates packed by 9:00 and delivered the same day 14:00-19:00 on Tuesday and Thursday.
- **Hours:** Member desk Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00; deliveries Tuesday and Thursday 14:00–19:00
- **Tone:** The tone is calm, direct, and grounded in the daily realities of field work and home cooking. It avoids exaggerated marketing claims, speaking with quiet specificity about seasonal produce, harvest times, and community partnerships.

## How to join
1. Choose a crate size suited to your household: Small, Standard, or Large.
2. Choose your preferred delivery day: Tuesday or Thursday.
3. Receive your crate delivered to your door between 14:00 and 19:00 on harvest day.
4. Skip, swap, or pause your membership anytime before Sunday at 20:00.
