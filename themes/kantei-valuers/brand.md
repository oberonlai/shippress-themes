# Kantei Valuers - brand brief

Independent commercial property valuation practice in Dublin. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kantei Valuers. "Kantei" means an appraisal; the practice values commercial buildings for lenders, owners and sellers.
- **Location:** 27 Merchants Row, Dublin 2, Ireland (fictional address). Fees in EUR, excluding VAT, in every language; no converted prices.
- **History:** founded in 2011 by Orla Brennan, formerly a panel valuer for a lender's commercial loan book. She started the practice so that one valuer would stay with an instruction from the site hour to the signature.
- **Today:** nine people, seven of whom meet the instruction. Coverage is within 150 km of Dublin with no travel charge; further by quote.
- **Audience:** banks and lenders that need the market value of a commercial property offered as loan security; owners, buyers and sellers who need a value before a sale, a purchase, a lease renewal, a rent review or for company accounts.
- **Tone:** calm, plain and specific, as if spoken on the pavement with the lease plan open. Only the fixed times, fees and limits are stated; the figure is left to the signed report. No promised figure, loan approval, sale price or timeline beyond the two fixed ones. No real lenders, bodies, accreditations, people or brands.

## The site hour (60 minutes, four parts)
00-10 min The plan (boundaries, entrances, what is let to whom); 10-35 min The tape (every floor to net internal area, frontage and depth for shops); 35-50 min The condition (roof, services, fire escapes, accessibility, visible defects, photographed for the appendix); 50-60 min The documents (leases, rents, running costs; the document list and report date). A building over 5,000 m² has a second hour at no extra fee.

## Instructions
Two kinds: a lender instruction (loan security: purchase, refinance, loan review) and an owner or seller instruction (sale, purchase, lease renewal or rent review, company accounts). We value shops and high-street retail units, offices, industrial units and warehouses, mixed-use blocks, small hotels and leisure buildings. We do not value private homes, development land without planning permission, petrol stations and specialist trading property, or buildings outside Ireland.

## Fees (7 rows, EUR excluding VAT)
Shop or retail unit up to 500 m2: 1,450 - Office building or floor up to 2,000 m2: 2,400 - Industrial unit or warehouse up to 5,000 m2: 2,650 - Mixed-use block up to 12 units: 3,200 - Portfolio of 2-5 properties, one owner: from 2,100 per property - Lease renewal or rent review opinion: 950 - Update of our own report within 12 months: 650. The fee is fixed in the written quote before the site hour; travel within 150 km is included; the invoice is sent with the signed report and is payable in 30 days; the fee never depends on the figure.
