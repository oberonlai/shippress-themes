# Fumi Press Launch: brand

## Story
Fumi Press was founded in 2014 as an independent essay publisher with its studio in Da'an District, Taipei. That same year, author Clara Hsu published her first essay in an independent small-press zine before establishing a long-term collaboration with editor Tomas Reyes. Working alongside book designer Noor Haddad and photographer Jun Park, the press dedicated its Da'an studio to publishing carefully crafted essay editions that honor the quiet details of solitary observation and city life.

In 2018, Fumi Press published Clara Hsu's debut collection, Window Seat, marking the beginning of a shared commitment to unhurried, reflective non-fiction. By 2022, Hsu established a daily morning writing practice at the same café table in Da'an District, arriving at dawn before the streets grew crowded. Over the next four years, supported by Reyes's steady editorial guidance, those morning sessions yielded twenty-one linked essays spanning 248 pages.

In 2027, Fumi Press announced Small Hours: Essays from a Corner Table, scheduled for simultaneous publication in English and Traditional Chinese on 18 March 2027. Coordinated internationally with Berlin-based publicity director Elena Brandt, the release features a signed and numbered first printing of 1,500 hardcover copies. From its Da'an District studio, Fumi Press continues to foster contemplative literature through intimate readings and dedicated reading groups across Taipei, Tokyo, Paris, and Berlin.

## Voice
The voice of Fumi Press is calm, observant, and quietly documentary, reflecting the patience of early morning writing at a neighborhood table. It prioritizes concrete material details and understated clarity over promotional urgency, commercial slogans, or hyperbolic claims.

## People
- **Clara Hsu** (Author): Born in 1986 in Kaohsiung and currently living in Taipei, Clara Hsu is the author of Window Seat and Small Hours: Essays from a Corner Table. She composed her twenty-one essays at the same corner café table every morning between 2022 and 2026.
- **Tomas Reyes** (Editor, Fumi Press): Tomas Reyes is the editor at Fumi Press who guided the development of Window Seat and Small Hours. He joins Clara Hsu for the launch conversation at Annex Reading Room in Tokyo.

- **Jun Park** (Photographer): Jun Park photographed the imagery for Small Hours, capturing documentary scenes on 35mm film with soft window light and muted tones. His quiet photographs chronicle the Da'an café setting where the essays were written.
