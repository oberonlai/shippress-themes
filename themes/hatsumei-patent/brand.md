# Hatsumei Patent Attorneys - brand brief

Patent and trademark attorney firm sample site for inventors and growing companies. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market.
All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Hatsumei Patent Attorneys ("hatsumei" means invention).
- **Location:** located on the third floor, 22 Foundry Row, Halden (fictional town). A converted foundry building with a reception, a meeting room, a small library of specifications and the attorneys' desks.
- **History:** founded in 2011 by Ines Varga, a European patent attorney who trained as a mechanical engineer. Tomas Brandt joined in 2014, Amara Okoye in 2019.
- **Today:** independent, nine people: four attorneys, two trainees and three formalities and records staff. Clients are individual inventors, university spin-outs, makers, and software and hardware companies of 5 to 200 staff.
- **Audience (who it serves):** people who have made something new and want plain answers about what protection they can expect and what it costs.
- **Tone:** calm, plain, precise, friendly. Short honest sentences. Explain what a patent can and cannot do; say plainly that nobody can promise a grant. No grant rates, success rates, guarantees, client names or awards.

## People
- **Ines Varga** - founder, European patent attorney, trained as a mechanical engineer, in her forties, short dark hair.
- **Tomas Brandt** - patent attorney, electronics and physics, in his fifties, grey beard, round glasses; joined 2014.
- **Amara Okoye** - trademark attorney, in her thirties, curly black hair; joined 2019.
- Nine people in total: four attorneys, two trainees, three formalities and records staff.

## What we do
Patents; trademarks; registered designs; patentability searches, trademark clearance searches and freedom-to-operate opinions. We do not run court cases: litigation is referred to partner litigators. Offices mentioned: USPTO, EPO, UK IPO; WIPO PCT for international filings.

## From idea to patent (process, shown as photographed chapters)
1. **The first idea** - a confidential talk; keep it secret until filed.
2. **Search** - what already exists.
3. **Drafting** - claims and drawings, written with the engineer.
4. **Filing** - the filing date fixes priority.
5. **Examination** - dialogue with the examiner, responses to reports.
6. **The decision** - grant or not; renewal reminders; trademarks registered.

## Fees (EUR, fixed)
- First conversation: free, 45 minutes, confidential.
- Patentability search and written opinion: EUR 1,400.
- Patent application, drafting and filing (one invention, up to 15 claims, drawings included): EUR 5,200.
- Response to an examination report: EUR 980 each.
- Trademark clearance search: EUR 380.
- Trademark application: EUR 620 for one class, EUR 180 for each further class.
- Freedom-to-operate opinion: EUR 3,900.
- Official office fees are NOT included and are passed on at cost with the receipt. Translations for foreign filings are quoted in writing before work starts. Invoices due in 30 days.

## A day at the firm
- 08:30 records staff open the mail and office portals and note every running date.
- 10:00 attorneys read what arrived overnight; new examination reports go to the drafting attorney.
- 11:30 first conversations of the day, free and confidential.
- 14:00 drafting time with drawings and the engineer's questions.
- 17:00 filings go out before the offices close; receipts saved; a short note to the inventor.
- Office open Mon-Fri 08:30-17:30.
