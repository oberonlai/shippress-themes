# Kogu Hardware - brand brief

A neighbourhood hardware store that cuts keys while you wait and walks a customer through a repair. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market. All names, places, people, prices and contact details are fictional. Home page (stage 1) is English only.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kogu Hardware. "Kogu" is the family name of the owners; no Japanese elements anywhere.
- **Location:** 14 Alder Row, Wrenfield (fictional town). USD ($) prices in every language, never converted. Phone (555) 014-1987, email counter@koguhardware.example.
- **History:** Opened in 1987 by Mart Kogu on 14 Alder Row, Wrenfield; run since 2014 by his daughter Liis Kogu. 1,248 drawers behind one counter; repair visits within 3 km.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 7:30–18:30 · Sat 8:00–17:00 · Sun closed. The trade counter opens with the shop at 7:30.
- **Audience:** home fixers, tradespeople on a trade account, and makers and schools who need things cut to size.
- **Tone:** The tone is calm, practical, and clear, reflecting the unhurried authority of an experienced neighbourhood counter. It prioritises precise dimensions, materials, and direct advice over promotional adjectives or marketing promises.

## Story
Kogu Hardware was founded in 1987 by Mart Kogu on 14 Alder Row in Wrenfield. Mart built the long timber counter and fitted the wall behind it with small wooden drawers so that every nail, screw, washer, and fitting could be sold individually rather than in wasteful plastic packs. From the beginning, the shop served local trades and neighbourhood households who needed a reliable part and practical guidance on how to make things hold.

In 2014, Mart’s daughter Liis Kogu took over daily ownership and operation of the counter, maintaining the same careful organisation across 1,248 drawers. Under her direction, the business introduced dedicated trade accounts for early-morning collections and launched home repair visits within 3 km to assist neighbours with plumbing, carpentry, and fixture repairs. Mart continues to spend his Saturdays at the counter key bench, cutting house and padlock keys on original machines.

The shop operates on the principle that we would rather show you how to fit a component than sell you something unsuitable. When customers bring in a worn hinge, a stripped bolt, or a mysterious plumbing washer, our team matches the exact replacement from the drawers and explains the fitting process step by step before anyone leaves the shop.

## How to ask
- 01 Bring the old part, or a photo with a coin beside it for scale.
- 02 Tell us what it does and where it lives.
- 03 We match it from the drawers and show you how to fit it at the counter.
- 04 If it doesn't fit, bring it back within 30 days.

## Visual identity
- Reference: Good2Garden (https://www.good2garden.com/). A full-bleed documentary photograph with the text bottom-left and a transparent header; a hairline-ruled three-column grid of photographs in mixed ratios; an asymmetric tall portrait with a small floating photograph and an offset text column; split dark and photograph service panels.
- Colour: ground #F7F6F0, ink #141811, charcoal #4E4B4A (panels), hover #EEEEE7, muted label #858781, meta text #6B6D67, one brass dot #A9823A. No cream fields, no filled colour blocks beyond ink and charcoal.
- Type: Instrument Sans (headings, 400/500) and Manrope (body). Traditional Chinese and Japanese in Noto Sans TC / JP (gothic), never a serif.
- Radius 0 on photographs, cells and buttons; the phone pill is 9999px. No shadows. Rules are always 1px ink.
- Photography: documentary, 50mm, natural window side light, muted grade of charcoal, warm wood and worn brass; no text, no logos.
- Motion: slow parallax on the hero and the shop-floor band, 24px rise on section titles, left-to-right clip wipe on grid photographs, a counting number.
- Inner pages (stage 2): About, Home fixers, Trade, Makers & schools, Services & prices, The drawers (shop), Stories, How to ask, Visit.
