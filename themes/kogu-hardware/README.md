# Kogu Hardware

Kogu Hardware is a calm, documentary-styled WordPress store theme designed for neighbourhood merchants, craftspeople, and independent shops. Rooted in the story of an Alder Row hardware shop established in 1987, it features hairline-ruled ledger grids, full-bleed photography, and thoughtful WooCommerce drawer templates. The theme balances practical utility with quiet craftsmanship to showcase products, counter services, and repair workflows.

## Home page patterns
`front-hero` (full-height photograph + ruled info bar), `front-gateway` (hairline ruled three-column grid), `front-counter` (asymmetric portrait + floating small photograph), `front-services` (three sticky split panels), `front-prices` (ruled price list), `front-drawers` (ruled product grid), `front-floor` (parallax band + count), `front-stories` (ruled rows), `front-ask`.

## Motion
sp-scroll: `sp-parallax` on the hero and the shop floor, `sp-reveal--up` on section titles, `sp-reveal--wipe` on the grid photographs, `sp-count` on the drawer number.

## Pages and menu
The demo import creates About, Home fixers, Trade counter, Makers & schools, Services & prices, How to ask and Visit, and a menu that links to them ("Who comes in" is a dropdown of the three audience pages). Stories link to the stories category.

## Photography
All photographs are generated documentary images (no people's likeness, no text). Product photos sit on a warm stone ground matching the page ground.
