# Isso Relocation — brand

## Story

Isso Relocation was founded in 2014 at Dokweg 12 in Antwerp by Lena Vos and Samir Haddad to address a persistent operational vulnerability in commercial moving: the loss of productive business days during office transitions. Having observed organizations struggle with disordered packing, misplaced equipment, and fragmented IT infrastructure, Vos and Haddad created a structured relocation model built around a single, weekend-only execution window. They established their crate depot and fleet operations in Antwerp's port district, assembling an in-house crew dedicated entirely to scheduled corporate relocations.

Operating across a 150-kilometre service area encompassing Antwerp, Ghent, Brussels, Mechelen, Leuven, and Rotterdam, the company defined its reputation through strict adherence to temporal boundaries. Relocations are contained within a dedicated 61-hour operating window starting Friday at 18:00 and concluding Monday at 07:00. By restricting all physical movement to weekends, Isso Relocation ensures corporate clients close operations on Friday afternoon and resume regular business in their new premises on Monday morning without experiencing commercial downtime.

Over more than a decade of corporate moves, the firm established and refined its five-phase methodology: rigorous preliminary surveys, desk-level color coding, advance delivery of reusable crates ten working days ahead of moving day, and disciplined IT reconnects. By relying exclusively on employed, permanent personnel led by dedicated crew leads and technical specialists, Isso Relocation maintains verified chains of custody, clean physical handovers, and quiet operational continuity across Belgium and the southern Netherlands.

## Process

1. Survey — week −6: free walk-through of both floors with Samir, measurements, photos.
2. Plan & label — week −3: labelled floor plan, one colour zone per team, one number per desk; every crate and cable bag gets the destination number.
3. Pack — crates delivered 10 working days before the move; staff pack their own desk (6 crates per desk on average, 60 × 40 × 35 cm, reusable, rental included for 3 weeks).
4. The weekend — Fri 18:00 lift and load · Sat 07:00 first floor emptied, new floor set out · Sun 12:00 desks built, IT reconnected · Sun 20:00 walk-through with the client · Mon 07:00 keys back.
5. Monday — two crew members on site 07:00–12:00 for small fixes; crates collected within the 3 weeks.
