# Kagi Security - brand brief

Managed cybersecurity firm sample site for small and mid-sized companies. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, French and German, localised for each market.
All names, places, people and figures are fictional.

## Concept
- **Name:** Kagi Security (a kagi is a key; the firm keeps a quiet watch over the doors).
- **Place:** 41 Canal Wharf, Millbridge. A converted canal-side warehouse with a day office, a meeting room and a night room where the desk sits.
- **History:** founded in 2014 by Daniel Okafor, a former network engineer at a regional hospital trust, after a friend's accountancy lost a week to ransomware.
- **Today:** independent, 11 analysts, two people on the desk every night, about 140 client companies. Clients have 20 to 250 staff: clinics, accountancies, law offices, small manufacturers, logistics firms and school trusts. No client is ever named.
- **Audience (who it serves):** owners and office managers of small companies who do not have a security team and want to know that someone competent is awake; IT contacts at firms of 20 to 250 people.
- **Tone:** calm, plain, human. Short honest sentences. No fear-selling, no hacker imagery, no certifications, awards or guarantees, no claim of zero incidents.

## People
- **Daniel Okafor** - founder and managing director, in his fifties; ex-network engineer at a regional hospital trust.
- **Ruth Halvorsen** - head of the night desk, in her forties, short grey hair, 19 years in operations.
- **Tomas Lindqvist** - senior analyst and incident response lead, in his thirties, beard.
- **Priya Nair** - client security engineer; does onboarding and site visits; young, glasses.
- Eleven analysts in total, two always on shift overnight.

## What we watch
Laptops and servers (endpoint detection); Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace sign-ins and mail; firewalls and VPN; backups.

## How we respond
Watch, triage, respond (contain: isolate a laptop, disable an account, block a sender) and report in plain words the next morning. Median time from alert to a person looking at it last year: 11 minutes. Most alerts are closed as harmless, and we say so.

## Plans (EUR, per device per month)
- **Watch** - EUR 18, minimum 25 devices. 24/7 monitoring of devices, sign-ins and mail; alert triage by a person; monthly plain-language report.
- **Watch & Respond** - EUR 29. Everything in Watch, plus analysts contain incidents at any hour; up to 20 incident-response hours a year; quarterly review call.
- **Partner** - EUR 42. Everything in Watch & Respond, plus a named analyst, two on-site visits a year, one tabletop exercise a year and a staff awareness session.
- **Onboarding** - one-off EUR 900, two to three weeks. Contract: 12 months, then month to month with 60 days' notice.

## A night on the desk
- 18:00 hand-over from the day shift in person; open alerts and planned changes.
- 22:00 quiet triage; most alerts closed as harmless with a short note.
- 02:00 the hour phishing and odd sign-ins tend to arrive; one analyst contains, the other calls the client.
- 06:00 backup checks and a last look at every open alert.
- 07:30 the morning report is written in plain words and sent before clients reach their desks.
- Office open Mon-Fri 08:30-17:30; 24-hour incident line for clients.
