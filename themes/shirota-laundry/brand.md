# Shirota Laundry — brand

Name: Shirota Laundry

## Story
Shirota Laundry was founded in 1987 by Kenji Shirota at 14 Kiln Lane within Northgate Works, Lindmoor. Established to provide dependable linen care for local establishments, the plant began with a single flatwork ironer and a commitment to meticulous handling. Over the decades, the facility expanded its equipment and capacity, building steady partnerships with regional hospitality providers and medical practices who depend on spotless, reliable textile care each morning.

Today, the company is managed by Kenji’s daughter, Ines Shirota, leading a dedicated team of thirty-eight staff members. The Northgate Works plant processes nine point six tonnes of commercial linen every day, supporting forty-two hotels and healthcare clinics operating within a forty-kilometre route radius. Modern continuous batch tunnel washers and precise temperature monitoring work alongside experienced finishing teams who inspect every item by hand before packaging.

The company’s operations center on a dependable twenty-four-hour cycle, moving textiles smoothly from morning collection between 06:00 and 09:00 to packed shelf delivery by 07:00 the following day. With specialized procedures including thermal disinfection held at 71 °C for three minutes, dedicated mending stations, and customized folding rhythms, Shirota Laundry remains rooted in quiet craft, operational discipline, and lasting professional service.
