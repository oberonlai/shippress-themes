Waku Exhibits is a calm, documentary-style WordPress landing theme engineered for exhibition stand builders and structural design workshops. Featuring photo-filled statement typography, edge-flush hall showcases, and transparent pricing structures, it focuses visitor attention on hall planning and craftsmanship. A single repeated quote inquiry workflow connects exhibitors directly with workshop planners.

== Description ==

A WordPress block theme from ShipPress (https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes). See README.md for the design notes, templates and patterns.

== Installation ==

1. Download waku-exhibits.zip from https://github.com/oberonlai/shippress-themes/releases/download/themes/waku-exhibits.zip
2. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme, choose the zip and click Install Now.
3. Activate Waku Exhibits.

On activation the theme sets itself up as a ready-to-use website (inc/demo-import.php): it creates its pages with all their text and images, three stories with four categories and six tags, the menu, and sets the front page and posts page. It never changes or deletes anything you wrote, and running it again creates nothing twice.

Languages: the sample content comes in English (demo-content.json), with Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German following. The importer uses the `shippress_demo_lang` option (set by ShipPress from its interface language), else the site language.

The company, its people, address, telephone number and prices are fictional.
