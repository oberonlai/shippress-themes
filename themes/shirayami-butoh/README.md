# Shirayami Butoh

Shirayami Butoh — a butoh dance company theme laid out as one long centred column, with a formal top header: the company name in the centre, the menu split in two around it (two entries left, two right) and the Book a seat button at the right end; an overlay menu on phones and tablets. Sumire violet (#7058A3) is the main colour: the hero, the company band, every page head and the overlay menu are solid violet fields set in white. The hero is centred giant type: the company line, then the title across the whole width, with a cut-out white-painted dancer standing in front of the letters and breaking out over the hero bottom edge into a white card with the lead, the buttons and this season. Everything below runs in one centred reading column: the studio board, the repertoire as pieces one below the other, the company band with the cut-out silk breaking over its top edge, the four class steps and the notebook. Every picture is a photorealistic high-key photograph (85mm, softbox light, near-white ground, faint shadows, fine grain, colour graded toward sumire). Yuji Mai for display, Sora for reading, Noto Serif TC for Traditional Chinese headings. Sample content in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French and German; vertical labels only in Chinese and Japanese. WCAG AA colours; no entrance animations.

- Design plan row b4-7: single-column-longform, centered-giant-type hero, split nav, real-photography, high-key, sumire #7058A3, Yuji Mai + Sora.
- Sample content languages: en, zh-TW, ja, fr, de.
- Contrast: white #FFFFFF on sumire #7058A3 5.8:1 (all text on the hero, the company band, every page head, the overlay menu and the violet buttons); paper #FBFAFD on sumire 5.6:1; ink #1E1828 on paper 16.6:1 and on mist #EEEAF5 14.6:1; sumire on paper 5.6:1 (pull quote, hover links); deep violet #4E3B7A on paper 9.1:1 and on mist 8.0:1 (links, labels, eyebrows); ash violet #5A5466 on paper 7.0:1 and on mist 6.1:1 (secondary text); paper on ink 16.6:1 and pale sumire #C9BEE0 on ink 9.8:1 (footer); pale sumire on sumire 3.3:1 only for the giant founding year (large text); ink on sumire 3.0:1, never used for text.
- Break-frame: the cut-out white-painted dancer (transparent WebP) stands in front of the giant hero title and breaks out over the hero bottom edge into the white card; the cut-out silk breaks over the top edge of the company band and the bottom edge of every page head.

## Templates and patterns

Every template is the top header (company name centred, menu split around it, Book a seat button), the page column and the footer. Page bodies come from patterns (home-hero with the giant centred title and the cut-out dancer, home-board with the synced studio board, home-works with the repertoire in one column, home-serial as the violet company band, home-process with the four class steps, home-journal, and a violet page head per page). The studio board, studio details and ticket guide are synced patterns.
