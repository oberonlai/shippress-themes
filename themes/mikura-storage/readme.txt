== Description ==

Mikura Storage is a landing theme for a household self-storage building in Lindenport, opened in 2014. It presents five unit sizes, plain monthly prices, a twenty-minute staffed visit, and a single way to book. Desk hours, key-card access and the people who run the building are written into the page.

The home page is one long conversion page: a hero with a drifting strip of touching square photographs and a blue three-fact bar, five numbered photo cards with an orange round-disc panel, sizes beside a sticky photograph, four visit steps, a plain price table, a parallax corridor band, three household stories, a six-row FAQ and one booking panel. A card with the phone number and a Book a visit button slides in from the right edge after the first screen.
