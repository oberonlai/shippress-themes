# Mikura Storage: brand

The name of the operator is Mikura Storage. Its audience is city households with more than a flat can hold: people between two leases, in a small flat, or clearing space for a new arrival.

## Story
Mikura Storage opened in 2014 at 48 Alder Row, Lindenport, in one dedicated building of six floors. Ines Varga still manages the building she started. The idea was simple and has stayed simple: a city household needs somewhere staffed for the things a flat cannot hold, close enough that a visit is a short walk along a clean corridor, not a drive to an edge-of-town yard.

The building holds 412 units in five sizes, from a Shelf of 0.8 m² to a Garage Bay of 8 m². Rooms are held between 18 and 24 C and kept below 60% relative humidity. Desk hours run 09:00 to 19:00, seven days. Building access runs 06:00 to 23:00, seven days, by key card. There is no admin fee, no charge for the key card, no charge for a hand-truck loan, and no deposit. The minimum term is one month, with 30 days' notice.

A visit takes 20 minutes, is free, and carries no obligation. An attendant walks the corridor with you. Same-day move-in is available when the visit is booked before 15:00, with photo ID and the first month paid. Optional contents cover is $6 a month on any size, up to $5,000, for fire, water and theft. Daniel Achebe leads the morning desk, Mei Lin Ho advises on size, Rafael Souza runs the loading bay, Priya Nair handles contracts and accounts, Jonas Weber looks after climate and fire checks, and Amara Diallo covers the weekend desk.

## Voice
Calm, concrete and practical, as if an attendant were explaining the building in person. State sizes, prices, hours and conditions plainly, and leave out awards, rankings and claims about safety.

## A day at the building
The daily routine starts at 09:00 when the desk opens. Daniel Achebe walks the first visits along the corridor, Rafael Souza opens the loading bay for morning moves, and Jonas Weber reads the climate gauges and runs the fire checks before the building fills. Households come and go on key cards between 06:00 and 23:00, and the weekend desk keeps the same 09:00-19:00 hours.
