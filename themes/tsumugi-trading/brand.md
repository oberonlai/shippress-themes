# Tsumugi Trading — brand brief

Design plan row: b6-8 (mid-sized trading company, corporate site). Built in English first and localised into zh-TW, ja, fr and de (the mechanism of Hokuto Driving School 1.1.0, Hagane Machinery and Sukoyaka Family Clinic). The brand world is the same in every language; the corporate wording follows each market (a Taiwanese import-export company with a company registration number, a Japanese trading house with a company profile page, a French international trading company, a German trading house in foreign trade).
All names, places, people, partners and figures are fictional. The copy never claims certifications, rankings, awards or market shares: it says what the company buys, ships and checks, from where, and who to call.

## Concept
- **Name:** Tsumugi Trading Co. *Tsumugi* means spun thread: the company's first cargo was linen yarn, bought from one mill and sold to three weavers. The founders kept the word because a trading company does the same thing as a spinner: it twists many short fibres into one strong line.
- **Head office:** 4 Quay Street, Port Ellery, a fictional harbour city with a container terminal, an old customs house and a ferry pier. Third and fourth floors of a 1970s office block facing the water; the bonded warehouse is two kilometres away at Pier 9.
- **Founded:** 1964, by **Martin Hale** and **Sumi Aoki**, as a two-desk office on the quay buying linen yarn. First container shipment 1978; Pier 9 warehouse 1991; tableware line 2004; solar roof and packaging review 2015; sixtieth year 2024.
- **Today:** about 140 people in Port Ellery and two buying offices, 38 partner factories in eleven countries, around 2,600 containers a year in and out.
- **Audience:** buyers at retailers, hotel groups and manufacturers who need a dependable supply of textiles, tableware and industrial materials; partner factories looking for a long-term buyer; graduates and experienced staff considering a career; local press and the harbour community.
- **Tone:** steady, exact and courteous. Places, years, names and quantities before adjectives. Never "world-class", "leading", "best", "guaranteed" or "number one". We would rather say "every first order from a new factory is inspected in person" than "uncompromising quality".

## People
- **Margaret Hale** — chair and managing director, 63, the founder's daughter. Joined in 1986 in the shipping office; ran the textile line for twelve years. Visits every new partner factory before the first order.
- **Daniel Reyes** — director of logistics and the Pier 9 warehouse, 54. Joined in 1994 as a forklift driver; rebuilt the warehouse routines and the customs team.
- **Ines Varga** — director of sustainability and sourcing, 46. Former mill engineer; runs the factory visit programme and the packaging review.
- **Teams** — the textile desk led by Oliver Brandt, the tableware desk led by Clara Moretti, the industrial materials desk led by Yusuf Demir, and the graduate programme run by Hanna Lind.

## A day at Quay Street
- 06:30 First container moves at the terminal; the Pier 9 team checks the overnight arrivals list.
- 08:30 Desk meetings: textile, tableware and industrial materials review orders, samples and vessel schedules.
- 10:00 Customs documents filed; the quality team photographs incoming samples on the light table.
- 13:00 Calls to partner factories in Asia close; calls to Europe and the Americas begin.
- 15:00 Buyers visit the sample room; trainees shadow a desk for the afternoon.
- 18:00 The next morning's loading plan is sent to the warehouse and the hauliers.

## Signature
A mid-sized trading company with sixty years behind it: a full-bleed documentary photograph of the container port at dawn, inset under a soft rounded corner, with a slim line of milestone years along its foot; sixty years as photographed milestones in a horizontal row, each with a large year numeral; the four business lines beside one sticky photograph; the three named directors; sustainability, careers and news in plain, well-spaced sections.

## Palette (low chroma; photographs carry the colour)
- harbour #F5F3F0 (ground), stone #EAE5E0 (panels), paper #FBFAF8, ink #24262A (text), quiet #615C59 (secondary text), kurumi #947A6D (the one muted accent: links, rules, labels, year numerals, one button), pale-kurumi #E3D9D3, mist #BDB6B1.

## Type
- Spectral (display: headings, the company name, years and figures) over Hanken Grotesk (body, labels, lists, navigation). Largest size 5rem for the year numerals and the first-screen line, never centred.
- Traditional Chinese and Japanese headings in Noto Serif TC / Noto Serif JP at about 0.75 of the Latin size; CJK body line height 1.85. French and German use hyphens:auto.

## Pages and contents
- **Home** — inset port photograph with a milestone-year line and a floating news card; who we are with two photographs; the four business lines beside a sticky photograph; sixty years in photographed milestones (horizontal); the three directors; sustainability; careers; news; contact.
- **Business** (/business/) — textiles and yarn, tableware and homeware, industrial materials, logistics and warehousing: what we buy, from where, for whom.
- **How we trade** (/how-we-trade/) — from first enquiry to delivery: sampling, factory visit, order, inspection, shipping, after-sales.
- **Leadership** (/leadership/) — the three directors and the desk leads; how decisions are made.
- **Sustainability** (/sustainability/) — factory visits and working conditions, packaging review, the solar roof, shipping consolidation; what we have not solved yet.
- **Careers** (/careers/) — the graduate programme, desk roles, warehouse roles, what a first year looks like, how to apply.
- **Company** (/about/) — the story since 1964, company facts, a day at Quay Street, our commitments.
- **Contact** (/contact/) — head office, Pier 9 warehouse, buying offices, phone and email; the company details are one synced pattern.
- **Newsletter** (/newsletter/) and **News** (/news/, the posts page).
