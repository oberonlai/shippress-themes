# Tsumugi Trading: S5-Style design references

Researched 2026-10-20 on https://www.s5-style.com/ (Japanese web design gallery), corporate and maker entries. No trading-house or logistics entry was in the recent list, so the references are corporate sites of makers and builders whose structure suits a calm trading-company site. Only structure and techniques are borrowed: no copy, photographs, logos or whole-page layouts. Side-by-side proof: cc-runs/theme1020m/reference-compare.png (one row per technique, reference crop left, this theme right).

## Reference sites
1. Tanica, https://tanica.jp/ (home appliance maker): beige low-chroma ground; an inset hero photograph with large rounded top corners and side margins, white headline at its lower left, an outlined round call-to-action at its lower right; small English eyebrow over each section title.
2. COC, https://companycoc.com/ (confectionery company): "Our brand" section as full-width cards that stick and stack over one another while scrolling, each with an oversized serif title over a hairline, the photograph left and a short text right.
3. Kanazawa Komuten, https://kanazawa-c.co.jp/ (construction): works shown as a two-column photo grid whose right column starts lower than the left, mixed photo proportions, small caption rows and generous gaps.
4. DeltaX, https://delta-x.co.jp/ (web media): large light numerals in hairline-ruled cells for key figures; a dark recruit band.
5. Jillion, https://jillion.co.jp/ (food service): business lines as wide rounded cards with a photo half and a text half.

## Signature techniques used
| Technique | Source | On the reference site | In this theme |
|---|---|---|---|

| Stacked sticky cards with oversized serif titles over a hairline | COC (https://companycoc.com/) | Brand cards in flat colours that stick and slide over one another, huge serif title, photo left about 58 %, text right | Home, front-lines (four business lines): four cards in quiet stone, pale walnut and mist tones plus one deep ink card (no saturated colour), each sticky with a 1rem step so the stack is visible, big Spectral title over a hairline, photo left 7/12, text right |
| Staggered two-column photo grid with small captions | Kanazawa Komuten (https://kanazawa-c.co.jp/) | Two columns, the right column offset about 100 px lower, mixed landscape proportions, a caption row under each photo | Home, front-years (sixty years in photographs): six archive and documentary photos in two columns, the even column offset by 8rem, each photo carrying a large light year numeral over its lower left corner (the DeltaX numeral style) and a two-part caption row over a hairline; replaces a vertical timeline |

Also borrowed in a smaller way: Tanica's small eyebrow label over each section title (every home section); DeltaX's ruled figure cells (front-sustain, three figures) and dark recruit band (front-careers, deep ink, not indigo).
