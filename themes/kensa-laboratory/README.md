# Kensa Laboratory

Kensa Laboratory is a block theme for a materials testing laboratory working in concrete, rebar, cores and chemistry. The home page is a single-column landing: a documentary photo strip, eight open tests, a sample log and a fee table, with a booking path for submissions. Founded-in-copy is 2009, at Unit 4, 18 Harbour Works Road, Northgate, with reports in two working days.

Signature techniques: a peeking-neighbour photo strip (the previous and next photographs cut by the viewport edges, a tracked white headline lower-left, five ticks), a four by two grid of 2 px outline test tiles directly beneath it, and dated rows with 80x24 colour chips in white radius-8 panels on a full-width steel band. Scroll effects: sp-parallax (the centre slide and the receiving-bay band), sp-stagger + sp-reveal--up (tiles, rows), sp-count (the facts line). The sideways drift of the strip is CSS only and stops under reduced motion.

See brand.md and design-spec.md for the copy and the measured reference values.

## Pages

Home, Sample log, Privacy and Terms of testing are included in the demo content. The header menu is an anchor menu for the home page plus a link to the Sample log page.
